## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> CFLOW-AD 在 ImageNet 预训练 CNN 编码器的多尺度特征上,为每个尺度训练一个以**位置编码为条件的 normalizing flow 解码器**,直接估计每个 patch 特征的精确对数似然作为异常分数;作者证明在多元高斯先验下 flow 目标退化为 Mahalanobis 距离(即 PaDiM 是其特例),而 CFLOW-AD 在 MVTec 上达到 98.26% 检测 AUROC、98.62% 定位 AUROC、94.60% AUPRO,并比同设置的 PaDiM/SPADE 快且小约 10 倍。
<!-- EN --> CFLOW-AD trains, for each scale of an ImageNet-pretrained CNN encoder, a **normalizing-flow decoder conditioned on positional encoding** that estimates the exact log-likelihood of each patch feature as the anomaly score. The authors show that under a multivariate-Gaussian prior the flow objective reduces to the Mahalanobis distance (so PaDiM is a special case), and CFLOW-AD reaches 98.26% detection AUROC, 98.62% localization AUROC and 94.60% AUPRO on MVTec while being ~10× faster and smaller than PaDiM/SPADE in the same setting.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 当时的 SOTA 无监督 AD+定位方法(SPADE、PaDiM)精度高,但后处理代价大:SPADE 要对训练特征库做 kNN,内存和速度都不可接受;PaDiM 要为每个空间位置存一个 D×D 协方差逆矩阵,内存随分辨率增长,作者推断这也是其只能在 CPU 上测速的原因。另一方面,全生成式模型(GAN、VAE)无法给出精确似然,在 MVTec 上也不比传统方法好。问题是:能否既有**精确的密度估计**、又是**全卷积、低内存、可实时**的结构?
<!-- EN --> State-of-the-art unsupervised AD + localization (SPADE, PaDiM) was accurate but costly in post-processing: SPADE runs kNN over a training feature gallery with prohibitive memory and speed; PaDiM stores a D×D inverse covariance per spatial position, so memory grows with resolution (the authors infer this is why it was only timed on CPU). Meanwhile fully generative models (GANs, VAEs) cannot give exact likelihoods and do not beat traditional methods on MVTec. The question: can one have **exact density estimation** with a **fully convolutional, low-memory, real-time** architecture?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **理论**:用 L2 weight decay 训练的 CNN 在中间特征上隐含多元高斯(MVG)先验 —— 这为 Lee 等 / Rippel 等 / PaDiM 的 Mahalanobis 打分提供了依据;作者进一步证明,在 MVG 假设下 normalizing flow 的反向 KL 损失可写成 Mahalanobis 距离与 Euclidean 距离之差加 log-Jacobian 项,因此 flow 框架收敛到与 Mahalanobis 类似的解,但能拟合**任意**分布而不限于 MVG。**编码器**:ImageNet 预训练 CNN(ResNet-18、WideResNet-50 或 MobileNetV3L),与 PaDiM 相同的多尺度特征金字塔 pooling(K 个尺度,默认 3 个,第 1–3 层)。**解码器**:每个尺度 k 一个独立的 conditional normalizing flow g_k;条件向量 c_i 是 2D 正余弦**位置编码**(C_k=128),在每个 coupling layer 中与中间向量拼接;coupling layer = 全连接 + softplus + 置换;因为解码器作用在单个特征向量上并跨位置共享参数,整体是平移等变、全卷积的,与特征图尺寸无关。**训练**:最大似然,即最小化 ‖u‖²/2 − log|det J|,每个 mini-batch 从 32 张特征图中随机采样 8192 个特征向量;Adam lr 2e-4,100 epoch,cosine 退火。**推理**:每尺度估计对数似然 → 转成概率并归一化到 [0,1] → 双线性上采样到输入尺寸 → 跨尺度聚合为异常图 S。每个 MVTec 类、每个 STC 场景单独训练一个模型。
<!-- EN --> **Theory**: CNNs trained with L2 weight decay impose an implicit multivariate-Gaussian (MVG) prior on intermediate features — justifying the Mahalanobis scoring of Lee et al. / Rippel et al. / PaDiM; the authors further show that under MVG the normalizing flow's reverse-KL loss can be written as a Mahalanobis-minus-Euclidean distance term plus a log-Jacobian term, so the flow framework converges to Mahalanobis-like solutions while fitting **arbitrary** distributions, not just MVG. **Encoder**: ImageNet-pretrained CNN (ResNet-18, WideResNet-50 or MobileNetV3L) with the same multi-scale pyramid pooling as PaDiM (K scales, default 3, layers 1–3). **Decoders**: an independent conditional normalizing flow g_k per scale; the condition c_i is a 2D sinusoidal **positional encoding** (C_k = 128), concatenated with intermediate vectors in every coupling layer; a coupling layer = fully connected + softplus + permutation; because decoders act on single feature vectors with parameters shared across positions, the whole model is translation-equivariant and fully convolutional, independent of feature-map size. **Training**: maximum likelihood, i.e. minimizing ‖u‖²/2 − log|det J|, sampling 8,192 feature vectors from 32 feature maps per mini-batch; Adam lr 2e-4, 100 epochs, cosine annealing. **Inference**: per-scale log-likelihoods → probabilities normalized to [0,1] → bilinear upsampling to input size → aggregated across scales into the anomaly map S. One model per MVTec class and per STC scene.

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> **范式:density / one-class**(在预训练特征空间上做精确密度估计;与 feature-embedding 家族共享编码器)。**承接**:RealNVP(Dinh 等)与 conditional INN(Ardizzone 等)提供 flow 结构;DifferNet(Rudolph 等 WACV 2021)首先把 flow 用于图像级 AD;Lee 等 2018 → Rippel 等 2020 → PaDiM 这条 "MVG + Mahalanobis" 线被作者在理论上统一为 flow 的特例;PaDiM 的金字塔 pooling 设置被原样采用。**影响**:与 FastFlow、CS-Flow 一起构成 AD 中的 normalizing-flow 家族,在 anomalib 中是标准基线;pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 把 CFLOW-AD 列入 normalizing flow 家族在病理数据上评测,并指出该家族"计算代价高、对结构选择敏感"。
<!-- EN --> **Paradigm: density / one-class** (exact density estimation in a pretrained feature space; shares the encoder with the feature-embedding family). **Builds on**: RealNVP (Dinh et al.) and conditional INNs (Ardizzone et al.) for the flow architecture; DifferNet (Rudolph et al., WACV 2021) as the first flow for image-level AD; the "MVG + Mahalanobis" line Lee et al. 2018 → Rippel et al. 2020 → PaDiM, which the authors theoretically unify as a special case of the flow; PaDiM's pyramid-pooling setup is adopted verbatim. **Led to**: together with FastFlow and CS-Flow it forms the normalizing-flow family in AD and is a standard anomalib baseline; pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 benchmarks CFLOW-AD within that family on pathology and notes the family's "high computational cost and sensitivity to architectural choices".

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **MVTec AD**:15 类,3,629 张训练(全为无缺陷)/ 1,725 张测试;不裁剪直接缩放到指定分辨率(128/256/512),训练时 ±5° 旋转增强。**STC**:274,515 训练帧 / 42,883 测试帧,13 个校园场景,固定 256×256。
<!-- EN --> **MVTec AD**: 15 classes, 3,629 training (all defect-free) / 1,725 test images; resized without cropping to the chosen resolution (128/256/512), ±5° rotation augmentation in training. **STC**: 274,515 training / 42,883 test frames over 13 campus scenes, fixed 256×256.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **MVTec 平均(WRN-50)**:检测 AUROC **98.26%**(PaDiM 97.9、CutPaste 97.1、DifferNet 94.9、Patch SVDD 92.1、SPADE 85.5);定位 AUROC **98.62%**(PaDiM 97.5、SPADE/CutPaste 96.0、Patch SVDD 95.7、DFR 95.0);AUPRO **94.60%**(PaDiM 92.1、SPADE 91.7、DFR 91.0)。即检测 +0.36、定位 +1.12 AUROC / +2.5 AUPRO。每个实验重复 4 次报均值。 <!-- EN --> **MVTec average (WRN-50)**: detection AUROC **98.26%** (PaDiM 97.9, CutPaste 97.1, DifferNet 94.9, Patch SVDD 92.1, SPADE 85.5); localization AUROC **98.62%** (PaDiM 97.5, SPADE/CutPaste 96.0, Patch SVDD 95.7, DFR 95.0); AUPRO **94.60%** (PaDiM 92.1, SPADE 91.7, DFR 91.0) — i.e. +0.36 detection, +1.12 localization AUROC / +2.5 AUPRO; each run repeated 4× and averaged.
- <!-- ZH --> **STC**:检测 AUROC 72.63%(CAVGA 71.9),定位 94.48%(PaDiM 91.2、SPADE 89.9、CAVGA 85.0)。 <!-- EN --> **STC**: detection AUROC 72.63% (CAVGA 71.9); localization 94.48% (PaDiM 91.2, SPADE 89.9, CAVGA 85.0).
- <!-- ZH --> **消融(定位 AUROC)**:coupling layer 4→8 平均 +0.15%;3 尺度比 2 尺度 +1.4%(加入 32×32 的第 1 层);条件 flow 比无条件 flow +0.5%(位置先验有用);WRN-50 比 R18 +0.81%;**最优输入分辨率因类别而异**(多数类 512 最好,cable/pill 256,transistor 128)。 <!-- EN --> **Ablations (localization AUROC)**: 4→8 coupling layers +0.15% on average; 3 scales vs 2 +1.4% (adding the 32×32 layer 1); conditional vs unconditional flow +0.5% (the positional prior helps); WRN-50 vs R18 +0.81%; **optimal input resolution varies by class** (512 for most, 256 for cable/pill, 128 for transistor).
- <!-- ZH --> **速度**:GPU(1080 8GB)上 CFLOW-AD-WRN50 为 27 / 9 fps(256 / 512 输入),对比 PaDiM-WRN50 1.1 fps、SPADE-WRN50 0.1 fps(后两者取自 PaDiM 论文的 CPU 测量);R18 版本 34 / 12 fps vs PaDiM-R18 4.4 fps;作者总结为快 8–25 倍。不计编码器时模型比 SPADE 小 1.7–50 倍、比 PaDiM 小 2–7 倍。 <!-- EN --> **Speed**: on a GPU (1080 8GB) CFLOW-AD-WRN50 runs at 27 / 9 fps (256 / 512 input) vs PaDiM-WRN50 1.1 fps and SPADE-WRN50 0.1 fps (the latter two from the PaDiM paper's CPU measurements); R18 version 34 / 12 fps vs PaDiM-R18 4.4 fps — summarized as 8–25× faster. Excluding the encoder, the model is 1.7–50× smaller than SPADE and 2–7× smaller than PaDiM.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **理论统一**:说明为何 MVG/Mahalanobis 是合理先验,并证明 flow 目标在该先验下收敛到相似解 —— 把 Lee 等、Rippel 等、PaDiM 放进同一个似然框架。 <!-- EN --> **Theoretical unification**: explains why MVG/Mahalanobis is a justified prior and shows the flow objective converges to similar solutions under it — placing Lee et al., Rippel et al. and PaDiM in one likelihood framework.
- <!-- ZH --> **位置条件 flow**:用位置编码作条件,把空间先验放进密度模型,同时保持参数跨位置共享(不像 PaDiM 每个位置一套参数)。 <!-- EN --> **Position-conditioned flow**: puts a spatial prior into the density model via positional-encoding conditioning while sharing parameters across positions (unlike PaDiM's per-position parameters).
- <!-- ZH --> 全卷积、与特征图尺寸无关、内存小、可在 GPU 上实时运行;代码开源且实验可复现。 <!-- EN --> Fully convolutional, feature-map-size agnostic, small and real-time on GPU; open code with reproducible experiments.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **按类别选输入分辨率**:主表结果使用每类最优分辨率(128/256/512),而论文未说明如何在不看测试异常的情况下选出 —— 有测试集调参之嫌,与 PatchCore/PaDiM 的统一设置不完全可比。 <!-- EN --> **Per-class input resolution**: headline results use the best resolution per class (128/256/512), and the paper does not explain how it was chosen without seeing test anomalies — a risk of test-set tuning that makes comparison with uniformly configured PaDiM/PatchCore imperfect.
- <!-- ZH --> 速度对比不对等:CFLOW-AD 在 GPU 上测,PaDiM/SPADE 数字取自 CPU 测量。 <!-- EN --> Unequal speed comparison: CFLOW-AD timed on GPU, PaDiM/SPADE numbers taken from CPU measurements.
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自己引用了"normalizing flow 在 OOD 检测上会失效"(Kirichenko 等)以及"深度生成模型不知道自己不知道什么"(Nalisnick 等),但没有在 AD 设定下系统检验这一风险;似然仍依赖 ImageNet 特征。 <!-- EN --> The authors cite "why normalizing flows fail to detect OOD data" (Kirichenko et al.) and "do deep generative models know what they don't know?" (Nalisnick et al.) but do not systematically test that risk in the AD setting; likelihoods still depend on ImageNet features.
- <!-- ZH --> 需要训练(每类 100 epoch),且仍是每类一个模型。 <!-- EN --> Requires training (100 epochs per class), still one model per class.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> CFLOW-AD 给伪影注入课题的是**"条件密度"这个建模角度**:异常分数 = 在给定上下文下的负对数似然。**映射到肾脏组学**:样本 = 细胞 / spot;正常训练集 = 健康参考;特征 = scVI 潜变量或基础模型 embedding;最关键的是**条件 c**:原文条件是像素位置编码,组学里可换成**细胞类型、解剖区域(皮质/髓质、肾小球/小管/间质)、空间 niche 组成、供体协变量(年龄、性别)**—— 于是模型学的是 p(细胞状态 | 细胞类型, 所处 niche, 供体),一个"在髓质里看起来正常、但出现在皮质肾小球旁就异常"的细胞能被识别出来。这比 PaDiM 的"每类一个高斯"更灵活(可多峰),也比 PatchCore 的 kNN 距离更有概率含义,原则上可接 conformal 校准(参照 conformal-scrna-bioinformatics-2025 用重建误差做非一致性分数的做法,把负对数似然换成非一致性分数即可)。**哪些假设会破**:(1) **似然 ≠ 异常**:flow 已知会给简单/低复杂度样本过高似然 —— 在 snRNA 里,低 UMI、高 ambient 比例的"空滴状"细胞信息量低,可能拿到高似然而被判为正常;反之高质量、表达丰富的健康细胞反而似然低;(2) batch effect 会直接移动密度,条件里必须显式包含批次或先做整合;(3) 连续疾病态下,似然只给"多罕见",不给方向;(4) 原文按类别挑分辨率的做法,对应到组学就是"按样本调 hyperparameter 看结果" —— 在 KidneyAnomalyBench 中必须禁止,只能用无异常验证集选参(参照 pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 的无偏 epoch 选择协议)。**仓库里已有的同类思想**:pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 在 Camelyon16、Crohn 结肠与小鼠肾小球上直接评测 CFLOW-AD;sardine-bioinformatics-2025 在表达流形 kNN 图上做局部**密度**比较(跨条件),是"密度视角"在空间转录组中的近亲,但它是非参数、跨条件的,不是在健康参考上学条件似然。
<!-- EN --> CFLOW-AD gives the artifact-injection topic the **"conditional density" modelling angle**: anomaly score = negative log-likelihood given context. **Mapping to kidney omics**: samples = cells/spots; normal training set = healthy reference; features = scVI latents or foundation-model embeddings; and crucially the **condition c** — positional encoding of pixels in the paper, which in omics becomes **cell type, anatomical region (cortex/medulla, glomerulus/tubule/interstitium), spatial niche composition, donor covariates (age, sex)**. The model then learns p(cell state | cell type, niche, donor), so a cell that looks normal in the medulla but is out of place next to a cortical glomerulus can be flagged. This is more flexible than PaDiM's one Gaussian per group (it can be multimodal) and more probabilistic than PatchCore's kNN distance, and it can in principle feed conformal calibration (as conformal-scrna-bioinformatics-2025 does with reconstruction error as non-conformity score — swap in the negative log-likelihood). **What breaks**: (1) **likelihood ≠ anomaly** — flows are known to assign high likelihood to simple/low-complexity samples; in snRNA, low-UMI, high-ambient "empty-droplet-like" cells carry little information and may get high likelihood and pass as normal, while rich, high-quality healthy cells may score low; (2) batch effects shift the density directly, so batch must be in the condition or integrated out first; (3) under continuous disease states the likelihood says "how rare", not in which direction; (4) the paper's per-class resolution picking is, in omics terms, "tuning hyperparameters per sample while looking at results" — it must be disallowed in KidneyAnomalyBench, with parameters chosen on anomaly-free validation data only (cf. pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025's unbiased epoch-selection protocol). **Existing repo papers with the same idea**: pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 benchmarks CFLOW-AD on Camelyon16, Crohn's colon and mouse glomeruli; sardine-bioinformatics-2025 compares local **densities** on an expression-manifold kNN graph across conditions — a spatial-transcriptomics relative of the density view, though non-parametric and cross-condition rather than a conditional likelihood learned on a healthy reference.
