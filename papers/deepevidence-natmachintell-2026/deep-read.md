## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> DeepEvidence 是一个面向生物医学的 deep research agent:一个 orchestrator 调度两类子 agent —— 广度优先的 BFRS 和深度优先的 DFRS —— 通过写代码去查 17 个异构知识库与文献,同时**增量构建一张带出处(provenance)的 evidence graph**;在同一底座 LLM(GPT-5)下,它在 4 个公开基准和一组新建的药物发现、临床前、临床试验、循证医学任务上都超过 Biomni 与 ToolUniverse(Abstract;Fig. 1–5)。
<!-- EN --> DeepEvidence is a biomedical deep-research agent: an orchestrator dispatches two kinds of subagent — breadth-first BFRS and depth-first DFRS — that write code to query 17 heterogeneous knowledge bases and the literature, while **incrementally building an evidence graph with provenance**. With the same base LLM (GPT-5) for every agent, it beats Biomni and ToolUniverse on four open benchmarks and on new drug-discovery, preclinical, clinical-trial and evidence-based-medicine tasks (Abstract; Figs. 1–5).

## 要点 / Takeaways
- <!-- ZH --> **公平对照**:所有 agent(含 DeepEvidence、Biomni、ToolUniverse)都跑在 GPT-5 上,所以差距来自 agent 设计而非底座模型(Results §"DeepEvidence excels on biomedical research benchmarks")。 <!-- EN --> **Controlled comparison**: every agent (DeepEvidence, Biomni, ToolUniverse) runs on GPT-5, so gaps reflect agent design rather than the base model (Results §"DeepEvidence excels on biomedical research benchmarks").
- <!-- ZH --> **Evidence graph 本身被审计过**:10 张图、316 个节点/关系,provenance 有效 100%,实体规范化 99.7%,但 claim alignment(记录的观察是否忠实于被引来源)只有 93.3%,节点级 89.2% —— 即约十分之一的节点观察与原文不符(Fig. 5g)。 <!-- EN --> **The evidence graph itself is audited**: 10 graphs, 316 nodes/relations; provenance validity 100%, normalization 99.7%, but claim alignment (does the recorded observation match the cited source) is only 93.3% overall and 89.2% at node level — roughly one node observation in ten misstates its source (Fig. 5g).
- <!-- ZH --> **消融说明"怎么接工具"比"多 agent"更重要**:把所有工具定义塞进上下文(ReAct+MCP)在循证任务上 gap detection 只有 25.0%,加代码执行升到 80.0%;朴素的 Orchestrator+ReAct 反而降到 70.0%;BFRS+DFRS 合起来最好(89.5%)(Fig. 5f)。 <!-- EN --> **Ablation: how tools are exposed matters more than "multi-agent" per se**: dumping all tool definitions in context (ReAct+MCP) gives 25.0% gap detection on the EBM task, adding code execution gives 80.0%, a naive Orchestrator+ReAct drops to 70.0%, and combining BFRS+DFRS is best (89.5%) (Fig. 5f).
- <!-- ZH --> **多个基准的答案是"代理真值"**:靶点识别、体内代谢通量、替代终点等任务的正确选项由 KEGG 通路数据按规则生成;对靶点识别和代谢通量两项,作者明说这是可控的代理真值、不代表临床/体内真相,目的是避免 LLM 凭记忆答题(Methods §"Curating target identification tasks"、§"Curating in vivo metabolic flux response tasks");替代终点一项只描述了构造方法,没有同样的声明。 <!-- EN --> **Several benchmarks use proxy truths**: correct options for target identification, in vivo flux and surrogate endpoints are generated from KEGG pathway data by rules; for target identification and flux the authors state this is a controlled proxy, not clinical/in vivo truth, chosen to avoid LLM recall (Methods §"Curating target identification tasks", §"Curating in vivo metabolic flux response tasks"); for surrogate endpoints only the construction is described, with no such statement.
- <!-- ZH --> **作者自认的空白正是课题 2 的入口**:没有显式的概率/不确定性机制去调和冲突证据,只靠底座 LLM 的参数化推理;且只处理文本(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> **The gap the authors admit is Topic 2's entry point**: no explicit probabilistic or uncertainty-aware mechanism to reconcile conflicting evidence — it relies on the base LLM's parametric reasoning — and it is text-only (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 样本量很小(HLE-Medicine 30 题,多数自建任务 n = 14–25),很多差距落在宽置信区间内。我们的理解:读数字时要看 CI,不能只看点估计。 <!-- EN --> Sample sizes are small (HLE-Medicine 30 questions; most built tasks n = 14–25), so many gaps sit inside wide confidence intervals. Our reading: read the CIs, not just the point estimates.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 生物医学证据散落在结构不同、持续变化、彼此难以对齐的资源里:文献衍生知识(PubTator、PKG)、通路与功能注释(KEGG、Gene Ontology)、基因–疾病关联(OpenTargets)、统一化学/基因注释(BioThings)等,跨资源推理在实践中很难(Introduction)。通用 deep research 系统(OpenAI DeepResearch、Gemini Deep Research、Perplexity Deep Research)主要处理非结构化网页文本,不适合关键证据分布在文献、结构化数据库、本体(ontology)和知识图谱里的生物医学场景(Introduction)。作者还观察到,Biomni、ToolUniverse 这类通用生物医学 agent 虽然接了很多知识库 API,但往往"只查几次就回答",庞大的工具集还会造成 tool confusion(Results)。
<!-- EN --> Biomedical evidence is spread across resources that differ in structure, evolve continuously and rarely align: literature-derived knowledge (PubTator, PKG), pathways and function (KEGG, Gene Ontology), gene–disease associations (OpenTargets), unified chemical/gene annotation (BioThings), so cross-resource reasoning is hard in practice (Introduction). Generic deep-research systems (OpenAI DeepResearch, Gemini Deep Research, Perplexity Deep Research) work mainly over unstructured web text and fit poorly where key evidence lives in literature, structured databases, ontologies and knowledge graphs (Introduction). The authors also observe that general biomedical agents such as Biomni and ToolUniverse, despite many knowledge-base APIs, typically "issue only a few searches before answering", and their large tool suites cause tool confusion (Results).

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **第 1 步 规划**:orchestrator 接收研究问题,先写研究计划,决定 BFRS 与 DFRS 的先后与组合,并给每一步指定研究目标(Fig. 1d)。实现上用 LangGraph 状态机,跟踪搜索预算并管理 evidence graph(Methods §"Implementing the orchestrator and research agents")。
<!-- ZH --> **第 2 步 派发子 agent**:orchestrator 通过 tool call 调用子 agent,参数包括检索目标(实体名 + 标准化 ID,如 PubTator ID 或 UMLS CUI —— UMLS 是美国国立医学图书馆的医学术语统一词表,CUI 是其中的概念编号)、要查的知识库和本次的 action 预算(Methods)。**BFRS** 做多图查询,快速扫一跳邻域(如跨 disease–drug、gene–disease 图找与某疾病相关的药物);**DFRS** 在 BFRS 找到的关键实体上做多跳深挖(如沿参考文献递归追溯,构建系统综述用的证据图)。作者不叫 BFS/DFS,因为遍历步骤由 agent 自主选择,不遵循固定图论规则;agent 还能通过共享实体或推断的跨图关系在不同知识图之间"架桥"(bridge entities,Fig. 1a)。
<!-- ZH --> **第 3 步 以代码为动作**:所有 agent 的主要动作是生成 Python 代码并在沙箱中运行 —— 例如一次基因检索会并行查多个库、合并成 dataframe、存到共享工作区;运行错误和失败的 API 调用会回传给 agent,在 ReAct 循环里自我修正;沙箱强制 action 预算,防止死循环(Methods)。
<!-- ZH --> **第 4 步 工具库**:论文称工具库可查询 17 个知识库/数据库,但列出的只有 16 个(BioThings、ChEMBL、ClinicalTrials.gov、Gene Ontology、Human Phenotype Ontology、KEGG、NCBI Datasets、OpenFDA、Open Genes、ProteinAtlas、PubChem、PubTator、PubMed、Reactome、UMLS、UniProt);Open Targets 也被查询(有 GraphQL client,并出现在统一检索工具中)。我们的理解:Open Targets 应是第 17 个,但论文没有明说。每个库两层封装:底层 client(认证、限流、错误处理、解析)+ 高层工具函数,统一返回 (DataFrame, 格式化字符串)。另有"统一检索"工具,如 `search_genes_unified` 并行查 MyGene.info、KEGG、Open Targets(可选 MyVariant.info)并合并跨库 ID;PubTator3 支持按语义关系检索(TREAT、CAUSE、INTERACT、INHIBIT、ASSOCIATE)。作者强调**不会**即时构建统一知识图,而是对外部源做并行定向查询(Methods §"Implementing the knowledge graph tools")。
<!-- ZH --> **第 5 步 evidence graph 作为记忆**:图中只存高价值、规范化的实体(基因、疾病、药物、通路、论文、具体发现),用规范 ID 并全局去重;边来自受控的关系集合(机制、成员、关联、证据等级),每个节点和关系都带出处。agent 把简短的事实性观察(实验设置、定量或机制结果)挂在实体上;添加前先查图、合并近似重复、更新观察而非新建节点,**冲突的发现用不同的证据链接分别记录**(Methods)。
<!-- ZH --> **第 6 步 汇总**:orchestrator 汇总各子 agent 的发现给出最终答案(Fig. 1d)。作者明确说这**不是 GraphRAG**,也不是在预先统一的生物医学知识图上做 BFS/DFS(Discussion)。
<!-- EN --> **Step 1 Plan**: the orchestrator receives the research question, writes a plan, chooses the order and interplay of BFRS and DFRS, and sets the target of each step (Fig. 1d). It is a LangGraph state machine that tracks search budgets and manages the evidence graph (Methods §"Implementing the orchestrator and research agents").
<!-- EN --> **Step 2 Dispatch subagents**: the orchestrator calls a subagent through a tool call specifying the search target (entity names plus standard IDs such as PubTator IDs or UMLS CUIs — UMLS is the US National Library of Medicine's unified medical vocabulary, a CUI is a concept ID in it), the knowledge bases to use and an action budget (Methods). **BFRS** issues multigraph queries to sweep first-hop neighbourhoods (e.g. drugs linked to a disease across disease–drug and gene–disease graphs); **DFRS** goes multihop deep from the key entities BFRS surfaced (e.g. recursively tracing references to build a systematic-review evidence graph). They are not called BFS/DFS because the agent chooses traversal steps itself rather than following fixed graph rules; agents can also bridge graphs through shared entities or inferred cross-graph relations ("bridge entities", Fig. 1a).
<!-- EN --> **Step 3 Code as action**: every agent's main action is generated Python run in a sandbox — e.g. one gene search queries several databases in parallel, merges a dataframe and saves it to a shared workspace; runtime errors and failed API calls go back to the agent for self-correction in a ReAct loop; the sandbox enforces action budgets against infinite loops (Methods).
<!-- EN --> **Step 4 Tool library**: the paper says the tool library queries 17 knowledge bases/databases but lists only 16 (BioThings, ChEMBL, ClinicalTrials.gov, Gene Ontology, Human Phenotype Ontology, KEGG, NCBI Datasets, OpenFDA, Open Genes, ProteinAtlas, PubChem, PubTator, PubMed, Reactome, UMLS, UniProt); Open Targets is also queried (it has a GraphQL client and appears in the unified search tools). Our reading: Open Targets is probably the 17th, which the paper does not state. Each has a low-level client (auth, rate limits, errors, parsing) and high-level tool functions that return (DataFrame, formatted string). "Unified" tools such as `search_genes_unified` query MyGene.info, KEGG and Open Targets (optionally MyVariant.info) in parallel and merge cross-database IDs; PubTator3 supports relation search (TREAT, CAUSE, INTERACT, INHIBIT, ASSOCIATE). The system does **not** build a unified knowledge graph on the fly; it issues parallel targeted queries to external sources (Methods §"Implementing the knowledge graph tools").
<!-- EN --> **Step 5 Evidence graph as memory**: only high-value normalized entities (genes, diseases, drugs, pathways, papers, concrete findings) with canonical IDs, globally de-duplicated; edges from a controlled set (mechanistic, membership, association, evidence level); every node and relation carries provenance. The agent attaches short factual observations (setting, quantitative or mechanistic outcome) to entities, queries the graph before adding, merges near-duplicates, updates observations instead of creating nodes, and **records conflicting findings as separate evidence links** (Methods).
<!-- EN --> **Step 6 Conclude**: the orchestrator aggregates the subagents' findings into the answer (Fig. 1d). The authors stress this is **not GraphRAG** and not BFS/DFS over a pre-unified biomedical knowledge graph (Discussion).

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把 deep research 从网页文本搬到异构生物医学知识源上,用统一检索接口 + 代码沙箱一次并行查多个库。 <!-- EN --> Moves deep research from web text to heterogeneous biomedical sources, with unified search interfaces and a code sandbox that queries many databases in parallel.
- <!-- ZH --> BFRS / DFRS 两种研究策略由 orchestrator 编排;消融显示二者互补,单独任何一个都不如组合(Fig. 5f)。 <!-- EN --> Two research strategies, BFRS and DFRS, orchestrated together; the ablation shows they are complementary and neither alone matches the combination (Fig. 5f).
- <!-- ZH --> **显式 evidence graph 作为一等记忆结构**:规范 ID、受控关系、逐项出处、冲突证据分开记录,并对图本身做了人工质量审计(Fig. 5g)。 <!-- EN --> **An explicit evidence graph as a first-class memory**: canonical IDs, controlled relations, per-item provenance, conflicting evidence kept as separate links — and a manual quality audit of the graph itself (Fig. 5g).
- <!-- ZH --> 一组覆盖药物发现 → 临床前 → 临床试验 → 循证医学的新任务,其中"证据冲突下的靶点验证"和"Cochrane 版本差分找证据缺口"两种构造方式可复用。 <!-- EN --> A task suite spanning drug discovery → preclinical → clinical trials → EBM; the "target validation under conflicting evidence" and "Cochrane version-diff evidence gap" constructions are reusable designs.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自述:知识源覆盖不完整,基准只代表一部分研究任务(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> Authors: knowledge-source coverage is incomplete and the benchmarks reflect only a subset of research tasks (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自述:目前依赖 KEGG、Gene Ontology 这类 schema 稳定、API 质量高的库;私有或内部数据源(缺失值、结构不一致、接口不全)需要 agent 自己造工具(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> Authors: it currently relies on well-curated sources like KEGG and Gene Ontology with stable schemas and good APIs; proprietary or in-house sources (missing values, inconsistent structure, incomplete interfaces) would require the agent to build new tools (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自述:**没有显式概率建模或不确定性机制来调和冲突证据**,靠底座 LLM 的参数化推理来权衡;未来可加入概率推理或校准置信度(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> Authors: **no explicit probabilistic modelling or uncertainty-aware mechanism to reconcile conflicting findings**; it relies on the base LLM's parametric reasoning, and calibrated confidence or probabilistic reasoning is future work (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自述:只有 BFRS/DFRS 两种策略,不太可能最优;很多任务未达专家水平;只处理文本,影像、结构生物学、蛋白组未纳入(Discussion)。完全复现受 LLM 不确定性和外部 API 变化限制(Methods)。 <!-- EN --> Authors: only two strategies, unlikely optimal; not expert-level on many tasks; text-only — imaging, structural biology and proteomics are out of scope (Discussion). Full reproducibility is limited by LLM non-determinism and evolving APIs (Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:靶点识别、通量、替代终点等选择题的真值由 KEGG 拓扑规则生成,而 DeepEvidence 本身就能查 KEGG;作者说 KEGG 只给局部结构、不能直接给出答案,但这种"出题源 = 可查工具"的关系仍值得警惕。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: truths for target identification, flux and surrogate-endpoint MCQs are generated from KEGG topology rules while DeepEvidence can itself query KEGG; the authors say KEGG gives only partial structure and does not yield answers directly, but the "question source = available tool" overlap deserves caution.
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:样本量小(n = 14–30),HLE 正文与图数字不一致,任务数量表述前后不一;专家量表只与 Biomni 对比,且未报告评审人数和一致性。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: small n (14–30), text–figure mismatch on HLE, inconsistent task counts; expert rubrics compare only with Biomni, and the number of raters and their agreement are not reported in the main text.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇最直接对应 **课题 2(生物医学分析 agent 的结论核查)**—— 它是"结论 ↔ 证据"这一环最贴切的参照。
<!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:(1) **evidence graph 是"结论 → 证据"追溯的一个可执行原型**:每个节点有规范 ID 和出处,冲突证据分开存。课题 2 要做的是把 agent 的每条结论追溯到代码、数据或文献,DeepEvidence 已经给出了"文献 / 知识库"这一侧的数据结构,我们缺的是"agent 自己跑出的数据与统计"这一侧。(2) **它对自己的 evidence graph 做的审计(Fig. 5g)几乎就是课题 2 的评测指标原型**:provenance 是否真实、实体是否规范、记录的观察是否忠实于来源(claim alignment)。即使是这个专门为证据设计的系统,节点级 claim alignment 也只有 89.2% —— 这正说明"有引用 ≠ 被支撑",需要独立的核查器。(3) **它自认缺失的不确定性机制**正是课题 2 想补的:把证据分级(代码跑出的数字 / 可复现的结果 / 文献确实写了的话),再给结论一个校准过的置信度。(4) 它只处理文本;课题 1 的肾脏单细胞/空间组学"已知答案"(哪些是疾病细胞、哪些是 doublet 或低质量细胞)可以给"数据侧证据"提供真值。(5) 同底座 LLM 的对照设计和"Cochrane 版本差分"这种自动构造真值的思路,都可以直接搬到我们的基准设计里。
<!-- ZH --> 在 detect → model → revert 链条上,我们的理解:它位于链尾之后 —— 当 virtual tissue 提名"哪些基因/扰动能把异常拉回正常"时,用类似的 BFRS/DFRS + evidence graph 去查文献与试验证据,给湿实验排序;但证据可信度必须加上数据层的置信度,这一步论文没有做。
<!-- EN --> This maps most directly onto **Topic 2 (verifying the claims of biomedical analysis agents)** — it is the closest reference for the claim-to-evidence step.
<!-- EN --> Our reading: (1) **the evidence graph is a working prototype of claim → evidence tracing**: canonical IDs, provenance per item, conflicting evidence stored separately. Topic 2 wants to trace each agent claim to code, data or literature; DeepEvidence supplies the literature/knowledge-base side of that data structure, and what is missing is the side for "data and statistics the agent itself computed". (2) **Its audit of its own graph (Fig. 5g) is nearly a template for Topic 2's metrics**: is the provenance real, are entities normalized, does the recorded observation faithfully reflect the source (claim alignment). Even in a system built for evidence, node-level claim alignment is only 89.2% — evidence that "cited ≠ supported" and that an independent verifier is needed. (3) **The uncertainty mechanism it admits lacking** is what Topic 2 aims to add: grade evidence (number the code produced / reproducible result / statement the cited paper really makes) and give each claim a calibrated confidence. (4) It is text-only; Topic 1's kidney single-cell/spatial "known answers" (which cells are diseased, which are doublets or low-quality) can supply ground truth for data-side evidence. (5) The same-base-LLM control and the "Cochrane version diff" trick for building ground truth automatically both transfer to our benchmark design.
<!-- EN --> On the detect → model → revert chain, our reading: it sits after the last step — when the virtual tissue nominates genes/perturbations to revert an anomaly, a BFRS/DFRS + evidence-graph search could collect literature and trial evidence to rank wet-lab candidates; but evidence credibility must be combined with data-level confidence, which the paper does not do.
