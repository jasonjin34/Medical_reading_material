## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> DeepEvidence 是一个面向生物医学的 deep research agent:用 orchestrator + 子 agent 的多智能体架构,把「广度优先(BFRS)」与「深度优先(DFRS)」两种检索策略编排起来,跨异构知识库与文献做长程探索,并**增量构建一张 evidence graph** 记录实体、关系与出处,使整个研究过程可追踪、可归因、可验证;在四个公开基准与七个新建任务上全面超过通用 agent。
<!-- EN --> DeepEvidence is a biomedical deep-research agent: an orchestrator plus specialized subagents coordinate breadth-first (BFRS) and depth-first (DFRS) research strategies to explore heterogeneous knowledge bases and literature over long horizons, while **incrementally constructing an evidence graph** of entities, relations and provenance that makes the research process trackable, attributable and verifiable. It outperforms generic agents across four public benchmarks and seven newly built tasks.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 生物医学证据散落在结构极不相同、且持续演化的资源里 —— PubTator/PKG(文献衍生知识)、KEGG/Gene Ontology(通路与功能注释)、OpenTargets(基因-疾病)、BioThings、ChEMBL、OpenFDA、ClinicalTrials.gov —— 彼此难以互操作,跨资源推理在实践中极难。通用 deep research 系统(OpenAI DeepResearch、Gemini、Perplexity)主要在非结构化网页文本上工作,不适配这种场景;而已有的生物医学 agent(Biomni、ToolUniverse)虽然接了大量知识库 API,却**只做浅层探索**——问几次就回答,且庞大的工具集反而引发 tool confusion,选错工具、走错推理路径。
<!-- EN --> Biomedical evidence is scattered across structurally divergent, continuously evolving resources — PubTator/PKG (literature-derived knowledge), KEGG/Gene Ontology (pathways and function), OpenTargets (gene–disease), BioThings, ChEMBL, OpenFDA, ClinicalTrials.gov — with poor interoperability, making cross-resource reasoning hard in practice. Generic deep-research systems (OpenAI DeepResearch, Gemini, Perplexity) operate mainly over unstructured web text and fit poorly; existing biomedical agents (Biomni, ToolUniverse) wire in many knowledge-base APIs but **explore shallowly** — a few searches before answering — while their large tool suites induce tool confusion, wrong tool picks and wrong reasoning paths.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **架构**:一个 orchestrator agent 维护研究计划与内部 evidence graph,把任务分派给两类 research 子 agent —— **BFRS** 做多图查询,快速铺开一跳邻域(例如跨 disease–drug、gene–disease 图找某疾病相关的药物);**DFRS** 在 BFRS 浮出的关键实体上做多跳深挖(例如递归追溯文献引用,构建系统综述所需的证据图)。作者刻意不叫 BFS/DFS:agent 是自主选择遍历步骤,而非遵循固定图论规则,并且能通过识别共享实体或推断跨图关系,在异构知识图之间架 **bridge entities**。**环境**:为异构 Web API 实现统一的实体检索与取数接口,一次药物查询即可并行触发 KEGG、ChEMBL、OpenFDA、OpenTargets 等多源查询;并配一个**执行沙箱**,让 agent 写代码大规模调用 API、把结果落成结构化文件再做下游抽取与分析。**关键设计**:evidence graph 持续更新,保存出处(provenance),支撑透明推理与人工验证。作者强调这**不是 GraphRAG**,也不是把生物医学数据预处理成统一知识图后再遍历 —— 因为底层资源本就碎片化、异构且不断变化。
<!-- EN --> **Architecture**: an orchestrator agent maintains the research plan and an internal evidence graph, dispatching to two kinds of research subagent — **BFRS** issues multigraph queries to sweep first-hop neighbourhoods fast (e.g. drugs linked to a disease across disease–drug and gene–disease graphs); **DFRS** takes the key entities BFRS surfaces and goes multihop deep (e.g. recursively tracing references to build the evidence graph for a systematic review). The authors deliberately avoid the names BFS/DFS: agents choose traversal steps autonomously rather than following fixed graph-theoretic rules, and can bridge heterogeneous graphs by recognizing shared entities or inferring cross-graph relations (**bridge entities**). **Environment**: unified entity-search and data-fetch interfaces over heterogeneous web APIs — one drug query fans out to KEGG, ChEMBL, OpenFDA, OpenTargets in parallel — plus an **execution sandbox** where agents write code to query APIs at scale, persist structured files and run downstream extraction. **Key design**: the continually updated evidence graph preserves provenance, enabling transparent reasoning and human validation. The authors stress this is **not GraphRAG**, and not traversal over a pre-unified knowledge graph — because the underlying sources are fragmented, heterogeneous and constantly evolving.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 评测分两层。**四个公开基准**:HLE-Medicine(Humanity's Last Exam 医学子集)、LabBench-LitQA2(需检索并综合文献证据)、SuperGPQA-Medicine-Hard(困难机制推理)、TrialPanorama-EvidenceQA(基于真实临床试验的证据推理)。**七个自建任务**:覆盖生物医学发现全生命周期的四个阶段 —— 药物发现、临床前体内实验、临床试验设计、循证医学(EBM)。知识源涵盖 PubMed、ClinicalTrials.gov、KEGG、Gene Ontology、OpenTargets、ChEMBL、OpenFDA、BioThings、PubTator 等。测试集公开在 HuggingFace。
<!-- EN --> Two evaluation layers. **Four public benchmarks**: HLE-Medicine (the medical slice of Humanity's Last Exam), LabBench-LitQA2 (retrieval and synthesis of publication evidence), SuperGPQA-Medicine-Hard (hard mechanistic reasoning) and TrialPanorama-EvidenceQA (evidence reasoning grounded in real clinical trials). **Seven purpose-built tasks** spanning four stages of the discovery lifecycle — drug discovery, preclinical in vivo experimentation, clinical trial development and evidence-based medicine (EBM). Knowledge sources include PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, KEGG, Gene Ontology, OpenTargets, ChEMBL, OpenFDA, BioThings and PubTator. Test sets are public on HuggingFace.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> 公平性设定很关键:**所有 agent 系统(含 DeepEvidence)都用同一个底座 LLM(GPT-5)**,基线为带文献检索能力的 GPT-5 与 Sonnet-4.5,以及通用生物医学 agent Biomni 与 ToolUniverse。四个公开基准全线领先:**HLE-Medicine 40.0%**(Biomni 20.0%、ToolUniverse 10.0%、Sonnet-4.5 3.3%、GPT-5 3.3%);**LabBench-LitQA2 64.7%**(Biomni 48.9%、ToolUniverse 25.0%);**SuperGPQA-Medicine-Hard 47.1%**(Biomni 40.7%、Sonnet-4.5 43.6%);**TrialPanorama-EvidenceQA 96.0%**(Biomni 84.0%、Sonnet-4.5 88.0%)。自建任务上同样领先,例如 68%(Biomni 56%、ToolUniverse 40%)、72%(两者均 44%)、80%(Biomni 68%)、52%(Biomni 36%);在八个评估维度中的五至六个上优于 Biomni。作者的解释是:基线并非缺工具,而是**探索太浅 + 工具混淆**,而 BFRS–DFRS 在 evidence graph 引导下做的是结构化、有针对性的深挖,这正是多跳推理与证据综合类任务需要的。
<!-- EN --> The fairness setup matters: **all agent systems including DeepEvidence run on the same underlying LLM (GPT-5)**, with baselines being GPT-5 and Sonnet-4.5 equipped with literature search, plus the biomedical agents Biomni and ToolUniverse. It leads on all four public benchmarks: **HLE-Medicine 40.0%** (Biomni 20.0%, ToolUniverse 10.0%, Sonnet-4.5 3.3%, GPT-5 3.3%); **LabBench-LitQA2 64.7%** (Biomni 48.9%, ToolUniverse 25.0%); **SuperGPQA-Medicine-Hard 47.1%** (Biomni 40.7%, Sonnet-4.5 43.6%); **TrialPanorama-EvidenceQA 96.0%** (Biomni 84.0%, Sonnet-4.5 88.0%). The built tasks show the same pattern — e.g. 68% (Biomni 56%, ToolUniverse 40%), 72% (both 44%), 80% (Biomni 68%), 52% (Biomni 36%) — winning five to six of eight evaluation dimensions against Biomni. The authors attribute this not to tool availability but to baselines' **shallow exploration plus tool confusion**, whereas evidence-graph-guided BFRS–DFRS performs structured, targeted deep investigation — exactly what multihop reasoning and evidence synthesis demand.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把 deep research 范式落到生物医学的异构知识源上,而非通用网页文本。 <!-- EN --> Brings the deep-research paradigm to heterogeneous biomedical knowledge sources rather than generic web text.
- <!-- ZH --> BFRS/DFRS 双策略编排:先铺开一跳邻域,再在关键实体上多跳深挖,由 agent 自主决定遍历。 <!-- EN --> Dual BFRS/DFRS orchestration: sweep first-hop neighbourhoods, then go multihop deep on key entities, with traversal chosen autonomously.
- <!-- ZH --> **显式 evidence graph 作为 agent 记忆**:保存实体、关系与 provenance,让研究过程可追踪、可归因、可被人类专家验证 —— 这是本文最可迁移的一点。 <!-- EN --> **An explicit evidence graph as agent memory**: entities, relations and provenance preserved so the process is trackable, attributable and expert-verifiable — the most transferable idea here.
- <!-- ZH --> 执行沙箱让 agent 以写代码的方式大规模调用 API 并做结构化落盘分析。 <!-- EN --> An execution sandbox lets agents query APIs at scale programmatically and persist structured results for analysis.
- <!-- ZH --> 新建七个覆盖发现全流程(药物发现→临床前→试验设计→EBM)的基准任务。 <!-- EN --> Seven new benchmark tasks covering the full discovery lifecycle (drug discovery → preclinical → trial design → EBM).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 知识源覆盖仍不完整,现有基准只反映生物医学研究任务的一个子集。 <!-- EN --> Knowledge-source coverage remains incomplete; current benchmarks reflect only a subset of biomedical research tasks.
- <!-- ZH --> 目前依赖 KEGG、Gene Ontology 这类 schema 稳定、API 优质的**精心策划**知识库;面对私有或自建源(缺失值、结构不一致、接口残缺)需要 agent 自己造工具。 <!-- EN --> Currently relies on **well-curated** bases like KEGG and Gene Ontology with stable schemas and good APIs; proprietary or in-house sources (missing values, inconsistent structure, incomplete interfaces) would require the agent to build new tools.
- <!-- ZH --> **缺乏显式的概率建模或不确定性机制来调和冲突证据**,只能依赖底座 LLM 的参数化推理去权衡 —— 作者自陈这是未来工作(校准置信度、概率推理框架)。 <!-- EN --> **No explicit probabilistic modelling or uncertainty-aware mechanism to reconcile conflicting evidence** — it leans on the base LLM's parametric reasoning to weigh sources; the authors flag calibrated confidence and probabilistic frameworks as future work.
- <!-- ZH --> 只有 BFRS/DFRS 两种策略,作者承认不太可能最优,理想情况应让 agent 自主发现更好的研究策略。 <!-- EN --> Only two strategies (BFRS/DFRS), which the authors concede are unlikely optimal; ideally agents would discover better strategies through self-directed adaptation.
- <!-- ZH --> 尚未达到专家水平;且**只处理文本模态**,影像、结构生物学、蛋白组等多模态数据未纳入。 <!-- EN --> Not yet expert-level; and **text-only** — imaging, structural biology and proteomic modalities are out of scope.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇处在 detect → model → revert 主链**之上的证据层**:当 pipeline 末端提名「哪些基因/扰动可以把异常拉回正常」时,这个提名必须挂着一条可查的证据链,否则无法进入湿实验优先级排序。DeepEvidence 提供的正是这条链的工程形态 —— evidence graph 保存实体、关系与 provenance,使每一步都可归因、可被专家验证。三点可复用:**(1) evidence graph 作为可审计记忆的具体实现**,可直接借用来给靶点提名做出处追踪;**(2) BFRS/DFRS 的策略分工**,对应「先扫一遍候选基因的一跳关联,再对入围者深挖文献与试验证据」这种真实工作流;**(3) 同底座 LLM 的公平对照设定**(所有系统都跑 GPT-5),是评估 agent 时该抄的实验规范 —— 否则分不清赢在 agent 设计还是赢在底座模型。**最有价值的是它自陈的空白**:它没有不确定性建模来调和冲突证据,且只做文本 —— 而本方向手里恰好有两样它没有的东西:一是数据层的可信度概念(证据所依赖的数据本身有多可靠),二是影像与空间组学模态。把「数据可信度 → 证据置信度 → 结论置信度」这条链打通,正是它留下的接口。
<!-- EN --> This sits on the **evidence layer above** the detect → model → revert chain: when the pipeline nominates "which genes/perturbations would revert the anomaly," that nomination must carry a checkable evidence chain or it cannot enter wet-lab prioritization. DeepEvidence is the engineering form of that chain — an evidence graph holding entities, relations and provenance so every step is attributable and expert-verifiable. Three reusable pieces: **(1) the evidence graph as a concrete auditable-memory implementation**, directly borrowable for provenance tracking behind target nomination; **(2) the BFRS/DFRS division of labour**, matching the real workflow of "sweep first-hop associations for candidate genes, then dig deep into literature and trial evidence for the shortlist"; **(3) the same-base-LLM controlled comparison** (every system on GPT-5), the experimental discipline to copy when evaluating agents — otherwise one cannot tell agent design from base-model strength. **Most valuable is the gap it names itself**: no uncertainty modelling to reconcile conflicting evidence, and text-only. This direction happens to hold both missing pieces — a notion of data-level reliability (how trustworthy is the data an item of evidence rests on) and the imaging / spatial-omics modalities. Closing the chain "data confidence → evidence confidence → conclusion confidence" is precisely the interface it leaves open.
