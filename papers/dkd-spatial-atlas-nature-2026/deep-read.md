## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 用 CosMx(1k 基因)与 **Xenium(5k 基因)** 单细胞空间转录组对 58 名患者的 64 个 FFPE 肾样本建图,并与 150 例 snRNA-seq 参照用 scVI/scANVI 整合成 > 500 万细胞的跨平台 DKD 空间图谱;定义 11 个可复现的组织 niche 与以细胞为中心的"microenvironment",发现随病情扩张、与 eGFR 负相关的**促纤维化损伤小管微环境**,以及其中嵌入的、**B 细胞为主的 TLS 样免疫微环境**——它标志一个进展更快的 DKD 亚群(B+ DKD,约 8.4%),并据此得到组织基因签名与 14 蛋白血浆 panel(UK Biobank 外部验证 AUC 0.70 vs KFRE 0.63)。
<!-- EN --> CosMx (1k genes) and **Xenium (5k genes)** single-cell spatial transcriptomics on 64 FFPE kidney samples from 58 patients, integrated with a 150-patient snRNA-seq reference via scVI/scANVI into a >5-million-cell cross-platform DKD spatial atlas; it defines 11 reproducible tissue niches and cell-centred "microenvironments", finds a **profibrotic injured-tubule microenvironment** that expands with disease and tracks lower eGFR, and within it a **B cell-predominant, TLS-like immune microenvironment** marking a faster-progressing DKD subset (B+ DKD, ~8.4%) — yielding a tissue gene signature and a 14-protein plasma panel (UK Biobank external AUC 0.70 vs KFRE 0.63).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> DKD 是肾衰竭的首要病因,约 40% 糖尿病患者会发生,但临床与分子异质性极大(从快速下降到长期稳定),使治疗开发与患者选择都很难。年龄、性别、eGFR、白蛋白尿能预测结局却不揭示生物学;遗传风险分数价值有限;循环 TNFR1/2 与肾功能下降强相关,但肾内炎症的细胞与空间语境不明。作者认为,真正细胞分辨率、近全基因组的空间转录组可以把组织结构变成"可计算的"对象,直接从临床活检中揭示病理亚型。
<!-- EN --> DKD is the leading cause of kidney failure (develops in ~40% of people with diabetes) but is clinically and molecularly heterogeneous (rapid decline to long-term stability), hampering therapy development and patient selection. Age, sex, eGFR and albuminuria predict outcomes but reveal little biology; genetic risk scores add little; circulating TNFR1/2 track decline but the cellular/spatial context of intrarenal inflammation is unclear. The authors argue that cellular-resolution, near-genome-wide spatial transcriptomics can make tissue architecture computable and reveal pathological subtypes directly from biopsies.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **数据整合**:CosMx 1k(n = 48,含 50 个自定义探针)与 Xenium 5k(n = 16,含 100 个自定义探针),两者重叠 656 个转录本;分别与 snRNA-seq 参照(150 例)用 scVI(NB 似然、4 隐层、30 维潜空间)+ scANVI 整合(scIB 基准显示 scANVI 最佳);**在潜空间里计算每个空间细胞到 10 个 snRNA 近邻的平均距离,距离过大的细胞被丢弃后重新整合**;最终 CosMx 3,386,822 + Xenium 951,041 个细胞,用 scANVI 潜空间插补 3,000 个高变基因。Leiden 聚类 + marker 注释得 20 种细胞类型,按损伤签名区分 iPT/PT、iTAL/TAL。**Niche**:在 20–80 µm 多尺度半径聚合邻域组成,机器学习得 11 个跨平台一致的 niche;用 COVET(局部基因协方差 + 近似最优传输)与 NicheCompass 做正交验证;Hotspot 做空间基因–基因相关与差异相关。**Microenvironment**:以某一细胞类型(损伤小管 iTEC、免疫细胞)为中心,按其邻域组成聚类。**下游**:CellPhoneDB v5 配体–受体并与 eGFR/HAVCR1/COL1A1 相关;843 例肾小管间质 bulk RNA-seq 验证;IMC 蛋白层面验证;与单细胞 B 细胞图谱整合;IgA 免疫沉淀质谱;TRIDENT 队列用 10 基因 B 细胞签名做 Ward 聚类分 B+/B−,Kaplan–Meier;血浆蛋白组上用 elastic-net logistic(SMOTE、5 折 CV)选蛋白,再入 Cox 模型并在 UK Biobank 外部验证。
<!-- EN --> **Integration**: CosMx 1k (n = 48, +50 custom probes) and Xenium 5k (n = 16, +100 custom probes) share 656 transcripts; each is integrated with the 150-patient snRNA-seq reference via scVI (NB likelihood, 4 hidden layers, 30-d latent) + scANVI (best on the scIB benchmark); **the mean latent distance from each spatial cell to its 10 snRNA nearest neighbours is computed and high-distance cells are discarded before reintegration**; final 3,386,822 CosMx + 951,041 Xenium cells, with 3,000 HVGs imputed from the scANVI latent space. Leiden + marker annotation yields 20 cell types; iPT/PT and iTAL/TAL split by injury signatures. **Niches**: neighbourhood composition aggregated over 20–80 µm radii, ML clustering → 11 niches consistent across platforms; orthogonal validation with COVET (local gene covariance + approximate optimal transport) and NicheCompass; Hotspot for spatial gene–gene correlation and differential correlation. **Microenvironments**: clustering of a given centre cell type (injured tubule iTEC, immune cells) by its neighbourhood. **Downstream**: CellPhoneDB v5 correlated with eGFR/HAVCR1/COL1A1; validation in 843 tubulointerstitial bulk RNA-seq samples; IMC protein validation; integration with a single-cell B cell atlas; IgA immunoprecipitation–MS; TRIDENT B+/B− split by Ward clustering on a 10-gene B cell signature with Kaplan–Meier; elastic-net logistic (SMOTE, 5-fold CV) on plasma proteomics, then Cox models and UK Biobank external validation.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 空间:64 个 FFPE 样本 / 58 名患者(26% 女性,平均 57.3 岁,覆盖全 GFR 范围;含健康儿童肾);CosMx 配 PAS 染色、Xenium 配 H&E 染色。snRNA-seq 参照:150 份样本、720,924 个细胞。整合后 > 500 万细胞、> 200 名患者。验证:Susztaklab Biobank 843 例 bulk RNA-seq(含病理纤维化评分)、TRIDENT 队列(n = 248,B+ 17 / B− 231;血浆蛋白组 B+ 13 / B− 182)、UK Biobank 3,309 名糖尿病参与者。
<!-- EN --> Spatial: 64 FFPE samples / 58 patients (26% female, mean age 57.3, full GFR range; includes healthy paediatric kidneys); CosMx paired with PAS, Xenium with H&E. snRNA-seq reference: 150 samples, 720,924 cells. Integrated: >5M cells, >200 patients. Validation: Susztaklab Biobank 843 bulk RNA-seq (with pathologist fibrosis scores), TRIDENT (n = 248; B+ 17 / B− 231; plasma proteomics B+ 13 / B− 182), UK Biobank 3,309 participants with diabetes.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **细胞类型**:20 种,跨平台保守的损伤标记 —— iPT:SOX4、VCAM1、HAVCR1;iTAL:CLU、SLPI、ITGB6;Xenium 的更高分辨率能检出淋巴管内皮。 <!-- EN --> **Cell types**: 20, with cross-platform conserved injury markers — iPT: SOX4, VCAM1, HAVCR1; iTAL: CLU, SLPI, ITGB6; Xenium's higher resolution detects lymphatic endothelium.
- <!-- ZH --> **11 个 niche**:与 eGFR 的相关 —— DCT r = 0.61、PT r = 0.52(正),immune r = −0.59、iPT r = −0.55(负);DKD 中肾小球 niche 的系膜细胞与免疫细胞小幅增加(P = 0.047 / 0.029),两者相关 r = 0.32;NRG1–ERBB3 与肾功能正相关,PLAU–PLAUR、IL-6–IL-6R 在重症中富集。 <!-- EN --> **11 niches**: eGFR correlations — DCT r = 0.61, PT r = 0.52 (positive); immune r = −0.59, iPT r = −0.55 (negative); in DKD, glomerular-niche mesangial and immune cells rise modestly (P = 0.047 / 0.029) and correlate (r = 0.32); NRG1–ERBB3 tracks better function, PLAU–PLAUR and IL-6–IL-6R are enriched in severe disease.
- <!-- ZH --> **5 种损伤小管微环境**(proximal、distal、periglomerular、perivascular、profibrotic);**促纤维化微环境**富含免疫与成纤维邻居,中心细胞为 MMP7+/HAVCR1+/HIF1A+ iPT 与 MMP7+/ANXA2+/CLU+/ITGB6+ iTAL,在 DKD 中扩张并与 eGFR 负相关(r = −0.6,P < 0.001),Sirius Red 证实胶原沉积;HBEGF–EGFR、TNFSF10–TNFRSF10B 与 HAVCR1 相关,JAG2–NOTCH2、PDGFβ–PDGFRβ 与 COL1A1 相关(843 例 bulk 验证)。 <!-- EN --> **Five injured-tubule microenvironments** (proximal, distal, periglomerular, perivascular, profibrotic); the **profibrotic** one is rich in immune and fibroblast neighbours, centred on MMP7+/HAVCR1+/HIF1A+ iPT and MMP7+/ANXA2+/CLU+/ITGB6+ iTAL, expands in DKD and anti-correlates with eGFR (r = −0.6, P < 0.001), with Sirius Red confirming collagen; HBEGF–EGFR and TNFSF10–TNFRSF10B track HAVCR1, JAG2–NOTCH2 and PDGFβ–PDGFRβ track COL1A1 (validated in 843 bulk samples).
- <!-- ZH --> **免疫图谱**:11 类免疫细胞;DKD 中浆细胞 3.1 倍、B 细胞 2.4 倍;健康儿童肾中多数免疫细胞位于以抗炎巨噬细胞(MRC1、CD163 等)为主的"residential"微环境,在 DKD 中比例稳定。 <!-- EN --> **Immune atlas**: 11 immune types; in DKD plasma cells ×3.1 and B cells ×2.4; in healthy paediatric kidneys most immune cells sit in a "residential" microenvironment dominated by anti-inflammatory macrophages (MRC1, CD163 …), proportionally stable in DKD.
- <!-- ZH --> **B 细胞为主的 TLS 样微环境**:嵌入促纤维化小管 niche,CXCL13–CXCR5、TNFSF13B–TNFRSF13C 共表达,CXCR5+/PDCD1+ TFH 样 CD4 T 细胞,IMC 证实 CD20+ B 与 CD4+ T、Ki-67+ 增殖 B 细胞,IgD+ 初始 B 显著富集(P = 2.38 × 10⁻¹¹),Xenium 检出 AICDA、IRF4;IgA 免疫复合物中富集颗粒酶与补体(C3、C4A、C8B)。 <!-- EN --> **B cell-predominant TLS-like microenvironment**: embedded in the profibrotic tubular niche, with CXCL13–CXCR5 and TNFSF13B–TNFRSF13C co-expression, CXCR5+/PDCD1+ TFH-like CD4 T cells, IMC-confirmed CD20+ B with CD4+ T and Ki-67+ proliferating B cells, strong IgD+ naive B enrichment (P = 2.38 × 10⁻¹¹), and AICDA/IRF4 detected by Xenium; IgA immune complexes are enriched for granzymes and complement (C3, C4A, C8B).
- <!-- ZH --> **临床转化**:B 细胞签名与间质纤维化正相关;TRIDENT 中 B+ DKD(8.4%)三年内更快到达肾终点(log-rank P = 0.02);14 蛋白 B+ DKD 血浆 panel 在 Cox 模型中优于纯临床模型,UK Biobank 外部验证 AUC 0.70 vs KFRE 0.63,最高四分位风险显著更高。 <!-- EN --> **Translation**: the B cell signature correlates with interstitial fibrosis; in TRIDENT, B+ DKD (8.4%) reaches renal end-points faster over three years (log-rank P = 0.02); the 14-protein B+ DKD plasma panel beats clinical-only Cox models, with UK Biobank external AUC 0.70 vs KFRE 0.63 and markedly higher risk in the top quartile.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 迄今最大的人肾单细胞空间数据集之一,CosMx + Xenium + snRNA 跨平台、跨模态统一图谱。 <!-- EN --> One of the largest human kidney single-cell spatial datasets to date, unifying CosMx + Xenium + snRNA across platforms and modalities.
- <!-- ZH --> "niche"(多细胞)与 "microenvironment"(以某类细胞为中心的局部语境)两级空间抽象,并用 COVET / NicheCompass 正交验证。 <!-- EN --> Two-level spatial abstraction — multicellular "niches" and cell-centred "microenvironments" — with orthogonal COVET/NicheCompass validation.
- <!-- ZH --> 从空间发现(B 细胞 TLS 样 niche)→ bulk 签名 → 血浆蛋白 panel → 人群生物库验证,走通一条完整的转化链,并提出 B 细胞靶向治疗 + 生物标志物分层的试验设想。 <!-- EN --> A complete translational chain: spatial discovery (B cell TLS-like niche) → bulk signature → plasma protein panel → biobank validation, proposing biomarker-stratified B cell-targeted trials.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 空间面板非全基因组(1k / 5k),3,000 基因靠 scANVI 从 snRNA 参照**插补** —— 插补表达用于 DEG、通路与配体–受体,可能把参照的偏好带入空间结论。 <!-- EN --> Panels are not genome-wide (1k / 5k); 3,000 genes are **imputed** from the snRNA reference via scANVI, and imputed expression feeds DEGs, pathways and ligand–receptor analysis — potentially importing reference bias into spatial conclusions.
- <!-- ZH --> **整合时丢弃与 snRNA 参照距离过大的空间细胞** —— 这可能恰好删掉了真正的"新"或异常细胞。 <!-- EN --> **Spatial cells far from the snRNA reference are discarded** during integration — which may remove exactly the genuinely novel or anomalous cells.
- <!-- ZH --> B+ DKD 组样本很小(TRIDENT 17 例,血浆 13 例),关联非因果;SMOTE 过采样 + 小阳性组容易高估;CosMx 与 Xenium 样本数不均衡(48 vs 16)。 <!-- EN --> The B+ group is small (17 in TRIDENT, 13 with plasma), associations are not causal, SMOTE on a tiny positive class risks optimism; CosMx and Xenium sample counts are imbalanced (48 vs 16).
- <!-- ZH --> 完整空间数据在发表时才放出(Zenodo DOI 已预留);健康对照有限(含儿童肾),可能与成人 DKD 存在年龄混杂。 <!-- EN --> Full spatial data released only upon publication (Zenodo DOI reserved); limited healthy controls (including paediatric kidneys) may confound age with disease.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇与 **课题 1** 的数据形态几乎同构:Xenium 5k + H&E + snRNA 参照,正对应 snRNA + snATAC + Xenium + H&E 这类多模态设计,因此它的流程(scVI/scANVI 整合、20–80 µm 多尺度邻域、niche/microenvironment 两级抽象、COVET/NicheCompass 正交验证)可以直接作为我们空间分析的基线。但从 **detect** 的角度,它暴露了两个关键问题,正好是课题 1 的切入点:(1) **它把"离参照太远"的空间细胞当作整合质量问题丢弃** —— 而对异常检测而言,这些细胞正是候选异常;课题 1 应把"到参照的潜空间距离"作为显式的异常分数,并用跨模态一致性(同一位置的 H&E 形态、snATAC 可及性是否也异常)判断它是分割/探针伪影还是疾病信号,而不是直接丢掉;(2) 它依赖**插补表达**做下游结论,KidneyAnomalyBench 应包含"插补 vs 实测"不一致的检测项。它的 B 细胞 TLS 样 niche 是一个很好的**"已知稀有异常"金标准**:仅约 8.4% 患者出现、空间上聚集、有 IMC 蛋白层面证据 —— 可作为 seeded/expert-validated finding,检验我们的无监督检测器能否在不知道标签的情况下把它排进 top-k(与 NIH Kidney AI 挑战的评审指标一致)。对 **model → revert**:促纤维化微环境的配体–受体轴(HBEGF–EGFR、JAG2–NOTCH2、PDGFβ–PDGFRβ)与 B 细胞靶向是候选"回退"扰动,virtual tissue 可以模拟阻断这些轴后微环境组成是否回到 proximal/distal 等较健康的状态。对 **课题 2**,本文的结论链(空间 → bulk → 血浆 → 生物库)是构造多跳 claim-to-evidence 测试的好素材:agent 若声称"B+ DKD 进展更快",verifier 需追溯到 TRIDENT n = 17 的小样本与 log-rank P = 0.02 这一证据强度。
<!-- EN --> This is almost isomorphic to **Topic 1's** data: Xenium 5k + H&E + an snRNA reference matches a multi-modal design of this kind (snRNA + snATAC + Xenium + H&E), so its pipeline (scVI/scANVI integration, 20–80 µm multiscale neighbourhoods, niche/microenvironment abstraction, COVET/NicheCompass validation) is a direct baseline for our spatial analysis. From the **detect** perspective, though, it exposes two issues that are Topic 1's entry point: (1) **it discards spatial cells far from the reference as an integration-quality problem** — for anomaly detection these are precisely the candidates; Topic 1 should make "latent distance to reference" an explicit anomaly score and use cross-modal agreement (is the H&E morphology / snATAC accessibility at that location also abnormal?) to decide segmentation/probe artifact vs disease signal, rather than dropping them; (2) it relies on **imputed expression** downstream, so KidneyAnomalyBench should include checks for imputed-vs-measured disagreement. Its B cell TLS-like niche is an excellent **"known rare anomaly" gold standard**: present in only ~8.4% of patients, spatially clustered, with IMC protein evidence — usable as a seeded/expert-validated finding to test whether an unsupervised detector ranks it in the top-k (matching the NIH Kidney AI challenge's judging metrics). For **model → revert**: the profibrotic ligand–receptor axes (HBEGF–EGFR, JAG2–NOTCH2, PDGFβ–PDGFRβ) and B cell targeting are candidate "revert" perturbations; a virtual tissue could simulate whether blocking them shifts microenvironment composition back toward the healthier proximal/distal states. For **Topic 2**, the conclusion chain (spatial → bulk → plasma → biobank) is good material for multi-hop claim-to-evidence tests: if an agent claims "B+ DKD progresses faster", the verifier should trace it to the small TRIDENT n = 17 and log-rank P = 0.02 evidence strength.
