## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 一个独立基准,用模拟数据、三种复杂度的物种混合实验、小鼠肾脏品系(基因型)混合实验和无污染负对照,评测 7 种 ambient RNA 去污染方法(CellBender、DecontX、FastCAR、scAR、scCDC、SoupX、CellClear)「去掉多少外源 RNA、保留多少内源 RNA、对下游解读有何影响」;结论是没有通吃的方法,推荐 CellBender、DecontX(full 模式)或 SoupX(reduced 模式),并警告在无污染数据上默认去污染会过度校正。
<!-- EN --> An independent benchmark using simulations, species-mixing experiments of three complexities, a mouse-kidney strain (genotype) mixing experiment and contamination-free negative controls to evaluate 7 ambient-RNA decontamination methods (CellBender, DecontX, FastCAR, scAR, scCDC, SoupX, CellClear) on how much ambient RNA they remove, how much endogenous RNA they keep, and how they change downstream interpretation; no method wins everywhere — CellBender, DecontX (full mode) or SoupX (reduced mode) are recommended — and default decontamination of clean data risks over-correction.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 单细胞/单核 RNA-seq(sxRNA-seq)中的 ambient RNA —— 解离、剪切或去垢剂导致细胞裂解释放的 RNA,以及多路复用中的条形码交换、孔间交叉污染、试剂污染 —— 会扭曲细胞分类与基因层面推断。已有多种去污染方法,却没有覆盖所有方法、多个数据集的独立基准;而 nf-core 等标准流程已把去污染作为常规步骤,**降低了「未经数据特异评估就做概率背景校正」的门槛**,增加过度校正风险。
<!-- EN --> Ambient RNA in single-cell/single-nucleus RNA-seq (sxRNA-seq) — RNA released by lysis during dissociation, shearing or detergents, plus barcode swapping in multiplexing, cross-well contamination and reagent contamination — distorts cell classification and gene-level inference. Many decontamination tools exist but no independent benchmark covers them all across datasets; meanwhile standard pipelines such as nf-core run decontamination routinely, **lowering the threshold for applying probabilistic background correction without dataset-specific evaluation** and raising over-correction risk.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **Ground truth 构造**:物种混合实验中,次要物种基因的 UMI 记为 ambient、主要物种的为内源(只保留 ≥75% UMI 来自单一物种的细胞);小鼠品系混合中按基因型特异 SNP(≥2 计数)判定;模拟数据由三种细胞系拟合的负二项分布生成,按设定的 ambient 比例从其他细胞类型分布中抽样污染 UMI;负对照为 Smart-seq2 胰岛数据与无污染模拟数据。**两项核心分数**:ambient 去除分数(ARS)与内源保留分数(ERS),均在 0–1 之间。**下游评测**:细胞邻域质量(Milo 邻域纯度、LISI)、标志基因选择性(熵、Moran's I、Gini)、Harmony 批次整合(bASW、batch LISI、PCR、ARI)、Azimuth 标签迁移(高置信比例、ARI、cASW、RMSE)。所有方法默认参数;SoupX/DecontX 另测只用过滤矩阵的 reduced 模式;scAR full 模式因计算量过大未完成。
<!-- EN --> **Ground truth**: in species mixing, UMIs on minor-species genes are ambient and on major-species genes endogenous (keeping cells with ≥75% UMIs from one species); in mouse strain mixing, genotype-specific SNPs (≥2 counts) decide; simulations draw from negative binomials fitted to three cell lines, sampling contaminating UMIs from other types' distributions at a set ambient fraction; negative controls are Smart-seq2 islets and contamination-free simulations. **Two core scores**: the ambient removal score (ARS) and endogenous retain score (ERS), both 0–1. **Downstream evaluation**: neighbourhood quality (Milo purity, LISI), marker selectivity (entropy, Moran's I, Gini), Harmony integration (bASW, batch LISI, PCR, ARI), Azimuth label transfer (high-confidence fraction, ARI, cASW, RMSE). Default parameters throughout; SoupX/DecontX also run in a filtered-matrix-only "reduced" mode; scAR's full mode was computationally intractable.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 7 个数据集、66 个样本、135,314 个细胞:低复杂度物种混合(10x 人 293T + 鼠 3T3)、中复杂度(人胰岛 + 人 Jurkat + 鼠 32D 掺入,GSE147203)、高复杂度(人胰岛异种移植到小鼠肾),**小鼠肾脏三品系混合**(C57BL/6、129S1/SvImJ、CAST/EiJ,GSE218853),Smart-seq2 胰岛负对照(E-MTAB-5061),以及模拟数据。全部为公开数据。
<!-- EN --> 7 datasets, 66 samples, 135,314 cells: low-complexity species mixing (10x human 293T + mouse 3T3), medium complexity (human islets spiked with human Jurkat + mouse 32D, GSE147203), high complexity (human islets xenografted into mouse kidney), **a three-strain mouse-kidney mixture** (C57BL/6, 129S1/SvImJ, CAST/EiJ; GSE218853), a Smart-seq2 islet negative control (E-MTAB-5061), and simulations. All public.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **ambient RNA 普遍存在**:49 个真实样本、97,357 个细胞/核中,81.56% 含有一定 ambient RNA;**单核样本平均 10.59%,单细胞 5.21%**;细胞间、样本间、研究间差异都很大(平均 QCD 0.314 / 0.319 / 0.171)。 <!-- EN --> **Ambient RNA is pervasive**: across 49 real samples and 97,357 cells/nuclei, 81.56% carry some ambient RNA; **single-nucleus samples average 10.59% vs 5.21% for single-cell**; variation is large within samples, between samples and between studies (mean QCD 0.314 / 0.319 / 0.171).
- <!-- ZH --> **来源**:基因的 ambient 负荷与其在空液滴中的表达高度相关(平均 R = 0.857);表达最频繁的 10% 基因贡献平均 82.30% 的 ambient RNA —— 主要是普遍表达的成熟胞质 mRNA。常用 QC 指标(外显子比例、线粒体/核糖体比例)与单细胞 ambient 负荷关联很弱;即使在 UMI 最多的 10% 细胞中,也有 2.64% 受到严重影响(>10% UMI 为 ambient)—— **靠严格 QC 无法根除**。 <!-- EN --> **Sources**: a gene's ambient load correlates strongly with its empty-droplet expression (mean R = 0.857); the 10% most frequently expressed genes contribute 82.30% of ambient RNA on average — mostly ubiquitous mature cytoplasmic mRNA. Common QC metrics (exonic, mitochondrial, ribosomal fractions) relate only weakly to per-cell ambient load; even among the top-10% UMI cells, 2.64% are strongly affected (>10% ambient UMIs) — **stringent QC cannot eliminate it**.
- <!-- ZH --> **去除 vs 保留**:去除 ambient 最多的是 scAR(平均 ARS 0.679)与 CellBender(0.604),但 **scAR 同时去掉约 0.445 的内源信号**(主要是低表达基因);各方法在无污染负对照上去掉的内源信号与有污染数据相当。SoupX reduced 模式去除更多而保留相近;DecontX reduced 模式去除略少但保留更多。 <!-- EN --> **Removal vs retention**: scAR (mean ARS 0.679) and CellBender (0.604) remove the most ambient RNA, but **scAR also strips ~0.445 of endogenous signal** (mostly low-expressed genes); on contamination-free controls methods remove about as much endogenous signal as on contaminated data. SoupX reduced mode removes more with similar retention; DecontX reduced mode removes slightly less but retains more.
- <!-- ZH --> **下游解读**:在最高污染的模拟样本中,只有 CellBender 改善无监督聚类恢复真实细胞类型;DecontX 与 scAR 使标志基因模块评分接近真值(但在无污染数据上也「改善」,提示偏向细胞类型特异表达);CellBender、scAR、SoupX、DecontX 改善邻域质量且对负对照影响可忽略;**scAR 恶化批次整合,并显著影响负对照的整合和标签迁移**。scCDC 与 FastCAR 主要从高表达基因去除,未见下游收益;CellClear 去除的内源计数与 ambient 计数相当。 <!-- EN --> **Downstream interpretation**: in the most contaminated simulation, only CellBender improves unsupervised recovery of true cell types; DecontX and scAR bring marker module scores close to ground truth (but also "improve" them on clean data, indicating a bias towards cell-type-specific expression); CellBender, scAR, SoupX and DecontX improve neighbourhood quality with negligible effect on negative controls; **scAR worsens integration and markedly affects integration and label transfer on negative controls**. scCDC and FastCAR remove mainly from highly expressed genes with no downstream benefit; CellClear removes about as many endogenous as ambient counts.
- <!-- ZH --> **推荐**:算力充足 → CellBender(需 GPU、耗时长、内存高,且自带细胞调用);需扩展性或特定细胞调用 → DecontX full 模式;无污染先验或只有过滤矩阵 → SoupX reduced 模式(在无污染时最不易过度校正)。污染先验可来自实验(snRNA 通常污染更多)或计算工具(如 AmbiQuant)。 <!-- EN --> **Recommendations**: ample compute → CellBender (GPU, long runtime, high memory, does its own cell calling); scalability or a specific cell-calling method needed → DecontX full mode; no prior expectation of contamination, or only filtered matrices → SoupX reduced mode (least prone to over-correction when contamination is absent). Priors can come from experiment (snRNA is usually more contaminated) or tools such as AmbiQuant.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 首个覆盖 7 种方法、多种 ground-truth 设计的独立基准,且显式包含**无污染负对照**以测量过度校正。 <!-- EN --> The first independent benchmark across 7 methods and multiple ground-truth designs, explicitly including **contamination-free negative controls** to measure over-correction.
- <!-- ZH --> 把「去除」与「保留」拆成两个分数(ARS/ERS),并揭示方法对高/低表达基因的系统性偏向。 <!-- EN --> Separates "removal" and "retention" into two scores (ARS/ERS) and exposes systematic biases toward high- or low-expressed genes.
- <!-- ZH --> 给出可执行的选择决策(算力、可用矩阵、污染先验)。 <!-- EN --> Provides an actionable decision rule (compute, available matrices, contamination prior).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 所有方法默认参数,未做参数调优;scAR full 模式未跑完。 <!-- EN --> Default parameters only, no tuning; scAR full mode did not finish.
- <!-- ZH --> 负对照只有 3 个 Smart-seq2 样本(其余未能跑通全部方法)。 <!-- EN --> Only three Smart-seq2 negative-control samples (others could not run through all methods).
- <!-- ZH --> 物种/品系混合的 ground truth 只能观察跨物种/跨基因型的污染,同物种同基因型内的污染不可见;模拟只用三种细胞系。 <!-- EN --> Species/strain-mixing ground truth only sees cross-species/cross-genotype contamination, not within-genotype; simulations use only three cell lines.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 对 **Plan A**:实验室的 95 例肾活检是 **snRNA**(单核),本文显示单核数据的 ambient 污染约为单细胞的两倍(10.59% vs 5.21%),而且污染主要来自表达最普遍的 mRNA —— 在肾脏中这通常意味着丰富的小管细胞转录本会「渗进」所有细胞类型。这会直接制造假异常(例如免疫细胞或足细胞「表达」PT 标志),因此 ambient RNA 应作为 KidneyAnomalyBench 中「技术伪影」类的一个明确来源。可用的设计:**(1)** 本文的 ARS/ERS + 负对照框架可直接改造成「去伪影后异常是否稳定、在干净数据上是否引入假异常」的评测;**(2)** 本文的小鼠肾脏品系混合数据(GSE218853)提供了**肾脏中 ambient RNA 的真实 ground truth**,可用来标定异常检测器对 ambient 伪影的敏感度;**(3)** 跨模态一致性 —— 配对的 snATAC 不受同一 RNA 释放机制影响,若一个「异常」只在 snRNA 中出现而在同一核的 ATAC 中无对应可及性,可提示其为 ambient 伪影(这一推论是本方向的假设,本文未测 ATAC)。**过度校正同样危险**:本文显示部分方法会人为增强细胞类型特异性,这可能把真正的混合/过渡状态(如损伤 PT)「清理」掉。对 **Plan B**:「流程里默认开启去污染、未检查是否有污染」是一个非常具体的 agent 失败模式;本文的决策规则(先判断污染先验、按可用矩阵选方法)可以直接写成验证器的检查项。
<!-- EN --> For **Plan A**: the lab's 95 kidney biopsies are **snRNA** (single-nucleus), and this paper shows single-nucleus data carry about twice the ambient contamination of single-cell (10.59% vs 5.21%), dominated by the most ubiquitously expressed mRNAs — in kidney that typically means abundant tubular transcripts "leaking" into every cell type. This directly manufactures false anomalies (e.g. immune cells or podocytes "expressing" PT markers), so ambient RNA should be an explicit source in KidneyAnomalyBench's "technical artifact" class. Usable designs: **(1)** the ARS/ERS + negative-control framework converts directly into an evaluation of "are anomalies stable after de-artifacting, and does decontamination introduce false anomalies on clean data?"; **(2)** the paper's mouse-kidney strain-mixing data (GSE218853) give **real ground truth for ambient RNA in kidney**, usable to calibrate an anomaly detector's sensitivity to ambient artifacts; **(3)** cross-modal agreement — paired snATAC is not affected by the same RNA-release mechanism, so an "anomaly" present in snRNA without corresponding accessibility in the same nucleus's ATAC may flag an ambient artifact (this inference is our hypothesis; the paper does not test ATAC). **Over-correction is equally dangerous**: some methods artificially sharpen cell-type specificity, which could "clean away" genuine mixed/transitional states such as injured PT. For **Plan B**: "decontamination switched on by default in the pipeline without checking whether contamination exists" is a very concrete agent failure mode, and the paper's decision rule (establish a contamination prior, choose by available matrices) can be written directly as verifier checks.
