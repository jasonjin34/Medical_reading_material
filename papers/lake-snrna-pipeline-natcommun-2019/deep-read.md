## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> KPMP 早期的方法学论文:比较 7 种肾组织保存/处理方式后,用 snDrop-seq 对 15 人、27 次实验、47 个文库的成人肾做单核转录组,QC 后 17,659 个细胞核聚成 30 个群;结论是 **O.C.T. 冷冻切片 + RNAlater 保存(cryoR)** 的 MT 转录本与应激伪影最少、成功率 >90%,而酶解离(dissocPC/TC)诱导 FOS/FOSB/DNAJB1 应激签名并产生一个伪影簇(PC-2)。
<!-- EN --> An early KPMP methods paper: after comparing 7 kidney tissue preservation/processing methods, it profiles adult human kidney with snDrop-seq (15 individuals, 27 experiments, 47 libraries), yielding 17,659 post-QC nuclei in 30 clusters; it concludes that **O.C.T. cryosections preserved in RNAlater (cryoR)** give the fewest MT transcripts and stress artifacts with >90% success, whereas enzymatic dissociation (dissocPC/TC) induces a FOS/FOSB/DNAJB1 stress signature and an artifact cluster (PC-2).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 肾这类上皮丰富、ECM 多的实体组织做单细胞时,酶解离会"激活基因表达伪影、降低活力、造成细胞类型回收不均"。作者想建立一个可复现、适用于活检量级组织、可与组织学并行的单核流程,并系统记录**取材与处理的 preanalytical 参数**,以便在数据中区分生物学变异与技术伪影,进而构建成人肾的分子"蓝图"并定位 CKD / 高血压相关基因的细胞类型。
<!-- EN --> In epithelium- and ECM-rich solid tissues like kidney, enzymatic dissociation causes "activation of gene expression artifacts, poor viability and disproportionate cell-type recovery". The authors want a reproducible single-nucleus pipeline that works on biopsy-scale tissue with parallel histology, and that records **preanalytical procurement/processing parameters** so biological variation can be separated from technical artifacts — then use it to build a molecular "blueprint" of adult kidney and map CKD/hypertension genes to cell types.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **取材**:Washington University(KTRC)的部分/全肾切除活体患者与弃用的死亡供体肾,以及 University of Michigan(KPMP)的肿瘤肾切除;均取自无瘤区,经组织学确认。**处理方式**(Fig. 1a):整块组织冷冻;O.C.T. 包埋冷冻切片(约 40 µm,7 张)后分别直接冷冻(cryoF)、Wisconsin 冷保存液(cryoW)或 RNAlater(cryoR);新鲜组织酶部分解离:papain/collagenase(dissocPC)或 trypsin/collagenase(dissocTC),解离后存于 RNAlater。GEO 另有"RNAlater 6 Hours"整块组织标签。荧光流式分选核会降低肾核的 snDrop-seq 成功率(Supp. Fig. 1a)。**测序**:snDrop-seq(Drop-seq 改造,HiSeq 2500;流程代码 github.com/chensong611/Dropseq_pipeline)。**QC**:>400 非 MT 转录本、>400 且 <5000 基因,MT 基因剔除;PAGODA2 中 gene/UMI 比值离群过滤、按批次缩放校正;top 2000 overdispersed 基因 → PCA → kNN(k=30,150 PCs)→ infomap,两轮去除 <30 核的簇,另去除一个 120 核无 marker 簇;UMAP 用 Seurat 2.3.4。**分析**:Wilcoxon DE、SWNE、Monocle2 轨迹、CKD eQTL/血压 GWAS 基因的簇特异性评分;按处理方法、个体、来源、性别做平均表达相关性比较(Supp. Fig. 6)。
<!-- EN --> **Procurement**: living patients undergoing partial/total nephrectomy and discarded deceased-donor kidneys at Washington University (KTRC), plus tumour nephrectomies from University of Michigan (KPMP); all from tumour-free regions, histologically confirmed. **Processing** (Fig. 1a): frozen whole tissue; O.C.T.-embedded cryosections (~40 µm, 7 sections) kept frozen (cryoF), in Wisconsin cold-storage solution (cryoW) or in RNAlater (cryoR); fresh tissue partially dissociated with papain/collagenase (dissocPC) or trypsin/collagenase (dissocTC), then stored in RNAlater. GEO additionally labels a whole-tissue "RNAlater 6 Hours" condition. Fluorescent sorting of nuclei impeded snDrop-seq success on kidney (Supp. Fig. 1a). **Sequencing**: snDrop-seq (modified Drop-seq, HiSeq 2500; pipeline at github.com/chensong611/Dropseq_pipeline). **QC**: >400 non-MT transcripts, >400 and <5000 genes, MT genes removed; PAGODA2 gene/UMI-ratio outlier filter, per-batch scaling; top 2000 overdispersed genes → PCA → kNN (k=30, 150 PCs) → infomap; two rounds removing clusters <30 nuclei, plus one 120-nucleus cluster with no markers; UMAP in Seurat 2.3.4. **Analysis**: Wilcoxon DE, SWNE, Monocle2 trajectories, cluster specificity of CKD-eQTL / blood-pressure GWAS genes; average-expression correlation by processing method, individual, source and sex (Supp. Fig. 6).

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 15 名个体(WashU 10 人:PPID 3351, 3395, 3411, 3412, 3414, 3431, 3432, 3434, 3435, 3444;UMich 5 人:17-1606, 18-139, 18-142, 18-162, 18-312),27 次实验 / 47 个文库(GEO GSM3448110–GSM3448156),皮质与髓质。QC 后 17,659 个细胞核,平均每核 1,082 个 UMI(原文 "1082 unique transcripts")。GEO 每个文库有 experiment、barcode、ppid、region、tissue collection(radical / lap / robotic partial / partial nephrectomy、"donor kidney from MAT"、Nephrectomy)、tissue processing、Sex、source。公开文件:带簇注释的 raw UMI 矩阵(列名形如 `C{cluster}_NK{library}_{barcode}`,17,659 列,MT 基因已剔除)和 30 簇注释表;原始 reads 在 SRA SRP166966。**本研究没有活检样本**:"biopsy" 只出现在"兼容活检量级"(平均用 6 mm³ 组织)和与外部数据 GSE114156 的比较中。
<!-- EN --> 15 individuals (WashU 10: PPID 3351, 3395, 3411, 3412, 3414, 3431, 3432, 3434, 3435, 3444; UMich 5: 17-1606, 18-139, 18-142, 18-162, 18-312), 27 experiments / 47 libraries (GEO GSM3448110–GSM3448156), cortex and medulla. 17,659 post-QC nuclei, mean 1,082 UMIs per nucleus ("1082 unique transcripts"). Each GEO library carries experiment, barcode, ppid, region, tissue collection (radical / lap / robotic partial / partial nephrectomy, "donor kidney from MAT", Nephrectomy), tissue processing, sex and source. Public files: a cluster-annotated raw UMI matrix (columns `C{cluster}_NK{library}_{barcode}`, 17,659 columns, MT genes removed) and a 30-cluster annotation table; raw reads in SRA SRP166966. **No biopsies are in this dataset**: "biopsy" appears only as a claim of biopsy-scale compatibility (mean 6 mm³ tissue) and in a comparison with external data GSE114156.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **处理方式**:只有 O.C.T. 冷冻切片和新鲜解离组织的核与 snDrop-seq 兼容;cryoR 优于 cryoW 与 cryoF;cryoR "showed the fewest expression artifacts while also exhibiting higher experimental success rates (>90%)"。 <!-- EN --> **Processing**: only nuclei from O.C.T. cryosections and from fresh dissociated tissue were snDrop-seq-compatible; cryoR beat cryoW and cryoF; cryoR "showed the fewest expression artifacts while also exhibiting higher experimental success rates (>90%)".
- <!-- ZH --> **MT 转录本作为质量指标**:"we observed the fewest MTs and stress-induced artifacts in cryoR samples compared to cryosections processed differently (cryoW, cryoF) or fresh dissociated samples (dissocPC, dissocTC), while global gene and transcript levels appeared unaffected"。 <!-- EN --> **MT transcripts as a quality readout**: "we observed the fewest MTs and stress-induced artifacts in cryoR samples compared to cryosections processed differently (cryoW, cryoF) or fresh dissociated samples (dissocPC, dissocTC), while global gene and transcript levels appeared unaffected".
- <!-- ZH --> **热缺血**:TAL-1(簇 12,注释为"Thick ascending limb—surgery")和 PT-5(S3,簇 7)出现 MT 富集,"consistent with the nephrectomy procedure-related warm ischemia"。 <!-- EN --> **Warm ischaemia**: TAL-1 (cluster 12, annotated "Thick ascending limb—surgery") and PT-5 (S3, cluster 7) show MT enrichment "consistent with the nephrectomy procedure-related warm ischemia".
- <!-- ZH --> **解离应激**:方法间 DE 显示"a stress response signature (including FOS, FOSB, DNAJB1)",只与整细胞解离方法相关、见于多数细胞类型;PC-2(簇 17)主要来自 dissocP/T,"likely represented a dissociation artifact"。 <!-- EN --> **Dissociation stress**: DE between methods revealed "a stress response signature (including FOS, FOSB, DNAJB1)" specific to whole-cell dissociative methods and present in most cell types; PC-2 (cluster 17) came mostly from dissocP/T and "likely represented a dissociation artifact".
- <!-- ZH --> **一致性**:方法间平均表达相关性高,"cryoW showing the lowest correlation";个体内相关略高于个体间;PT-3(簇 5,炎症基因)主要来自单个个体,可能反映疾病或手术。作者把簇 5、12、17 视为可能的处理、取材或患者来源伪影。 <!-- EN --> **Concordance**: average expression correlated highly across methods, "with cryoW showing the lowest correlation"; within-individual correlation slightly exceeded between-individual; PT-3 (cluster 5, inflammatory genes) came mostly from one individual, possibly disease or procedure. The authors flag clusters 5, 12, 17 as possible processing/collection/patient artifacts.
- <!-- ZH --> **生物学**:30 个簇;111/674 个 CKD eQTL 基因、56/220 个高血压基因在簇间差异表达;POD 富集 CKD 位点,MC 富集高血压位点;MC 特异表达 PIEZO2;S2 PT 的 CFH+ 群和 S3 PT 的 FGA/FGB+ 群;髓质 IC-A2 表达 KIT 和 CALCA。 <!-- EN --> **Biology**: 30 clusters; 111/674 CKD-eQTL genes and 56/220 hypertension genes differentially expressed across clusters; POD enriched for CKD loci, MC for hypertension loci; PIEZO2 specific to MC; CFH+ S2 PT and FGA/FGB+ S3 PT populations; medullary IC-A2 expresses KIT and CALCA.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 第一批系统比较人肾保存/处理方式对单核数据影响的研究之一,确立 cryoR 作为 KPMP 的标准流程。 <!-- EN --> One of the first systematic comparisons of human kidney preservation/processing on single-nucleus data; set cryoR as the KPMP standard.
- <!-- ZH --> 把 preanalytical 参数、组织学和 QC 指标(MT 比例、应激基因)联动起来解释伪影簇。 <!-- EN --> Ties preanalytical parameters, histology and QC metrics (MT fraction, stress genes) together to explain artifact clusters.
- <!-- ZH --> 成人肾 30 个细胞群的早期 snRNA 图谱,含 CKD / 高血压基因的细胞类型定位。 <!-- EN --> An early 30-population snRNA atlas of adult kidney with cell-type mapping of CKD/hypertension genes.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 规模小(17,659 核),snDrop-seq 灵敏度低(平均约 1,082 UMI/核),免疫细胞覆盖少;髓质只来自约 3 人。 <!-- EN --> Small (17,659 nuclei), low-sensitivity snDrop-seq (~1,082 UMIs/nucleus), sparse immune coverage; medulla from ~3 individuals.
- <!-- ZH --> 方法比较不是平衡设计:多数方法只在 1–3 人身上做,且常与区域、个体、实验日期(批次)混杂;作者的方法间比较主要是平均表达相关性和 DE,没有给出每种方法的效应量表格(正文中)。 <!-- EN --> The method comparison is not balanced: most methods were run on 1–3 individuals and are often confounded with region, individual and experiment date (batch); the main text compares methods by average-expression correlation and DE without per-method effect-size tables.
- <!-- ZH --> MT 基因已从公开矩阵剔除,复算 MT 比例需从 SRA 原始 reads 重新比对。 <!-- EN --> MT genes are removed from the public matrix; recomputing MT fraction requires re-aligning SRA raw reads.
- <!-- ZH --> 作为"正常"参照受限于供体的合并症(作者自述)。 <!-- EN --> Use as a "normal" reference is limited by donor comorbidities (stated by the authors).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 处在 **detect** 一环的**负对照/干扰源**位置。Plan A 的 benchmark 要检验 anomaly detector 能否把疾病与测量/取材伪影分开;这个数据集提供了**同一患者、同一区域、不同保存/处理方式**的真实配对:18-162、18-312、3411(皮质 cryoF vs cryoR)、3414(髓质 cryoF / cryoW / cryoR)、3435(髓质 cryoW / cryoR / dissocPC)、3395、3351。每个细胞核的处理方式可以从公开文件中确定(矩阵列名含文库号 NK#,GEO 每个文库带 tissue processing、ppid、region),因此可以做"伪影标签"的 ground truth:例如在 cryoR 上训练、看 detector 给 dissoc 核(FOS/FOSB/DNAJB1 应激、PC-2 簇)和 cryoW 核打多高的异常分。热缺血(TAL-1、PT-5)给出一个取材相关、但无逐核标签的伪影轴。注意:不同处理通常在不同日期/批次上跑,处理效应与批次部分混杂;样本无疾病标签(只有组织学报告在 Supp. Data 1),所以它只能做伪影侧,疾病侧要靠 KPMP AKI/CKD(如 Lake 2023)。平台是 snDrop-seq,与 10x 数据存在平台差异,也要当作一个伪影轴。
<!-- EN --> It sits at the **detect** stage as a **negative control / nuisance source**. Plan A's benchmark asks whether anomaly detectors separate disease from measurement/procurement artifacts; this dataset gives real **same-patient, same-region, different preservation/processing** pairs: 18-162, 18-312, 3411 (cortex cryoF vs cryoR), 3414 (medulla cryoF / cryoW / cryoR), 3435 (medulla cryoW / cryoR / dissocPC), 3395, 3351. Each nucleus's processing method is recoverable from public files (matrix column names carry the library NK#; each GEO library has tissue processing, ppid and region), so it supplies artifact-label ground truth: e.g. fit on cryoR and score how anomalous dissociated nuclei (FOS/FOSB/DNAJB1 stress, PC-2 cluster) and cryoW nuclei look. Warm ischaemia (TAL-1, PT-5) gives a procurement-related artifact axis without per-nucleus labels. Caveats: methods were usually run on different dates/batches, so processing is partly confounded with batch; there are no disease labels (only histology reports in Supp. Data 1), so it covers the artifact side only; the disease side must come from KPMP AKI/CKD (e.g. Lake 2023). snDrop-seq vs 10x is itself another artifact axis.
