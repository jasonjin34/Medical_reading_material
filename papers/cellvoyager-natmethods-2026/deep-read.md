## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> CellVoyager(Stanford Zou 组)是一个在 Jupyter notebook 里自主探索 scRNA-seq 数据的 agent:输入一篇已发表论文和它的数据,先提取"已做过哪些分析",再生成**与之不同**的假设与分析计划(exploration blueprint)、自我批评、写代码执行、用视觉语言模型读图、重新规划;在 CellBench 上比底座 LLM 更会"猜作者做了什么分析",在三个真实再分析案例中由原文作者打分,提出了 CD8+ T 细胞 pyroptosis 评分升高、SVZ 衰老与转录噪声等新假设。
<!-- EN --> CellVoyager (Zou lab, Stanford) is an agent that autonomously explores scRNA-seq data inside a Jupyter notebook: given a published paper and its data, it extracts which analyses were already done, then generates **different** hypotheses and analysis plans ("exploration blueprints"), self-critiques, executes code, reads figures with a vision-language model and replans. It beats its base LLMs on CellBench at predicting the analyses authors performed, and in three re-analysis case studies scored by the original authors it surfaced new hypotheses such as elevated pyroptosis scores in CD8+ T cells in COVID-19 and transcriptional noise in the aging SVZ.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 现有单细胞 agent(CellAgent、CompBioAgent、AutoBA、BIA)主要是"执行用户指令",受限于用户自己的想象力,也不考虑研究者已经做过的分析,因此难以作为真正的合作者去补充已有工作。海量已发表 scRNA-seq 数据很可能还藏着未被分析的信号,但人工系统性再分析不可行。问题是:agent 能否**以既有分析为条件**,自主提出并执行新的、有生物学意义的分析?
<!-- EN --> Existing single-cell agents (CellAgent, CompBioAgent, AutoBA, BIA) mainly execute user commands, so they are bounded by the user's imagination and ignore analyses the researcher has already done — limiting them as collaborators. Vast published scRNA-seq data likely hide unexplored signal, but systematic manual re-analysis is infeasible. Can an agent, **conditioned on prior analyses**, autonomously propose and run new, biologically meaningful ones?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **输入**:处理好的 scRNA-seq 数据(AnnData)、一份背景报告(本文用已发表论文全文)、Python 环境说明(scanpy、scvi-tools、celltypist 等)。agent 先把论文摘要成"生物学背景 / 已做分析 / 数据细节"三部分,之后只用摘要。**探索循环**:生成 blueprint(假设 + 分步计划 + 第一步代码)→ 自我批评并改进(本文只做一轮)→ 逐步把代码写入 notebook 单元执行;报错则最多修 3 次,仍失败就改换路线;成功则把文本和图像输出交给 VLM 解读,解读写回 markdown 单元并据此重规划;每个分析最多 8 步(案例研究中典型 5–8 步)。分析之间串行,后一个分析以"已做假设列表"为条件避免重复;最后挑出最有前景的结果写报告。**CellBench**:用 gemini-2.5-pro-preview 从论文中抽取背景与全部单细胞分析,仅给背景,让模型/agent 预测作者做了哪些分析,用 LLM 评审判断是否命中(两位博士生对 47 条输出的人工复核与评审一致率 89% 与 85%);评测 CellVoyager 时只运行其构思阶段,不写代码。
<!-- EN --> **Inputs**: a processed scRNA-seq AnnData, a background report (here the full published paper) and a Python environment spec (scanpy, scvi-tools, celltypist, …). The agent first summarises the paper into "biological background / analyses performed / dataset details" and uses only that summary thereafter. **Exploration loop**: generate a blueprint (hypothesis + stepwise plan + first-step code) → self-critique and revise (one round here) → append code cells to the notebook and execute; on error, up to 3 fix attempts, else replan around it; on success, text and image outputs go to a VLM whose interpretation is written back as markdown and drives replanning; at most 8 steps per analysis (typically 5–8 in the case studies). Analyses run sequentially, each conditioned on the list of past hypotheses to avoid repeats; a final report picks the most promising results. **CellBench**: gemini-2.5-pro-preview extracts each paper's background and all single-cell analyses; given only the background, a model/agent predicts the analyses, and an LLM judge scores matches (two PhD students re-checked 47 outputs, agreeing with the judge 89% and 85%). For CellBench, only CellVoyager's ideation stage runs (no code).

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **CellBench**(预印本):50 篇主要分析 scRNA-seq 的已发表论文,共 483 项分析;正式版摘要称扩到 76 篇研究。**案例**:(1) COVID-19 PBMC 图谱(7 例重症 + 6 例健康对照);(2) 人子宫内膜月经周期图谱(73,180 个细胞、29 位供体,Fluidigm C1 + 10x);(3) 成年小鼠脑室下区(SVZ)跨年龄 scRNA-seq 与单细胞衰老时钟。每个案例生成 8 个分析,按代码成功执行比例选 5 个交给原文一位作者按量表评审。
<!-- EN --> **CellBench** (preprint): 50 published papers focused on scRNA-seq, 483 analyses; the published abstract reports 76 studies. **Cases**: (1) a COVID-19 PBMC atlas (7 severe patients + 6 healthy controls); (2) a human endometrium menstrual-cycle atlas (73,180 cells, 29 donors, Fluidigm C1 + 10x); (3) adult mouse subventricular zone (SVZ) scRNA-seq across ages with single-cell aging clocks. Each case generated 8 analyses; the 5 with the highest fraction of successfully executed cells went to one original author for rubric-based review.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **CellBench(预印本)**:以 GPT-4o 为引擎时,CellVoyager 比 GPT-4o 本身 micro 平均准确率高 **16%**(p<0.01)、macro 高 **19.33%**(p<0.001);o3-mini 引擎略低但差异不显著,案例中采用 o3-mini。正式版摘要:76 篇研究上比 GPT-4o 与 o3-mini 最多高 **23%**。**专家评审(15 个分析)**:创造性平均 **2.8/4**(COVID 2.8、子宫内膜 3.4、SVZ 2.4);**12/15** 个假设被认为值得深入研究(预印本摘要称 80%);"有生物学意义"的分别为 3/5、2/5、3/5。**具体发现**:COVID-19 中 CD8+ T 细胞 pyroptosis 基因评分显著升高(p=0.001;作者接受了原文作者"应先按供体 pseudobulk"的反馈后重做);子宫内膜中按作者反馈扩展到 40 对配体-受体相关性,TGF-β 与 FGF2–FGFR1 排前;SVZ 中 astrocyte-qNSC 转录噪声与年龄只有边际相关(r=0.047, p=0.0135),但扩展到全细胞类型的年轻/老年对比后,少突胶质、小胶质、壁细胞的转录噪声显著更高(p<0.001)。对照:Google Colab Data Science Agent 在同样输入下未产出有意义结果。每个分析约 30 分钟。
<!-- EN --> **CellBench (preprint)**: with GPT-4o as engine, CellVoyager beats GPT-4o itself by **16%** micro-averaged (p<0.01) and **19.33%** macro-averaged (p<0.001); the o3-mini engine is slightly lower but not significantly so, and o3-mini is used for the case studies. Published abstract: up to **23%** over GPT-4o and o3-mini on 76 studies. **Expert review (15 analyses)**: mean creativity **2.8/4** (COVID 2.8, endometrium 3.4, SVZ 2.4); **12/15** hypotheses judged worth further study (the preprint abstract says 80%); "biologically meaningful" 3/5, 2/5, 3/5 respectively. **Findings**: elevated pyroptosis gene scores in COVID-19 CD8+ T cells (p=0.001; redone after the original author asked for donor-level pseudobulking first); in endometrium, after author feedback, 40 ligand–receptor correlations with TGF-β and FGF2–FGFR1 on top; in SVZ, only a marginal noise–age correlation in astrocyte-qNSCs (r=0.047, p=0.0135), but a young-vs-old split across all cell types shows significantly higher transcriptional noise in oligodendrocytes, microglia and mural cells (p<0.001). Control: Google Colab's Data Science Agent produced no meaningful results on the same inputs. Each analysis takes ~30 min.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **以"已做分析"为条件的探索**:把论文当作"已覆盖区域"的地图,让 agent 去找空白处——这是自主再分析公共数据的清晰问题定义。 <!-- EN --> **Exploration conditioned on prior analyses**: the paper is a map of covered ground and the agent seeks the gaps — a clean problem definition for autonomous re-analysis of public data.
- <!-- ZH --> Notebook 原生的可审计轨迹:每一步 = 描述 + 代码 + 输出 + VLM 解读,整本 notebook 就是完整报告。 <!-- EN --> Notebook-native auditable traces: each step = description + code + output + VLM interpretation; the notebook is the full report.
- <!-- ZH --> CellBench:用"预测作者做过的分析"衡量构思质量的可扩展基准。 <!-- EN --> CellBench: a scalable benchmark of ideation quality via "predict the analyses the authors performed."
- <!-- ZH --> 由原文作者参与的评审 + 人在回路反馈(1–2 条反馈即可显著改进分析)。 <!-- EN --> Review by the original authors plus human-in-the-loop feedback (1–2 comments meaningfully improve analyses).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 评审规模小:3 篇论文 × 5 个分析、每篇仅 1 位作者评审,无盲法、无对照 agent 的同等评审;"新发现"均未实验验证,且有的结论本身很弱(SVZ r=0.047)。 <!-- EN --> Small review: 3 papers × 5 analyses, one author reviewer each, unblinded and with no comparable review of a baseline agent; "findings" are unvalidated and some are weak (SVZ r=0.047).
- <!-- ZH --> **统计严谨性依赖人类纠正**:最初的 COVID 分析在细胞层面做检验,原文作者指出应按供体 pseudobulk——这是单细胞分析里典型的伪重复(pseudo-replication)错误,agent 自己没发现。 <!-- EN --> **Statistical rigour relied on humans**: the initial COVID analysis tested at cell level until the author asked for donor-level pseudobulking — classic single-cell pseudo-replication that the agent did not catch itself.
- <!-- ZH --> CellBench 以 LLM 抽取 + LLM 评审为主,只衡量构思而不衡量执行正确性。 <!-- EN --> CellBench relies on LLM extraction plus an LLM judge and measures ideation, not execution correctness.
- <!-- ZH --> 串行运行、每个分析约 30 分钟;主要依赖 scanpy 等热门包,对冷门/内部工具适应差;只做 scRNA-seq。HeurekaBench 的独立评测中,它因固定步数与有限的修代码能力,在开放题上落后于 Biomni 与 BixBench-Agent。 <!-- EN --> Sequential, ~30 min per analysis; leans on popular packages like scanpy and adapts poorly to niche/in-house tools; scRNA-seq only. In HeurekaBench's independent evaluation it trails Biomni and BixBench-Agent on open-ended questions because of fixed step budgets and limited code-fixing.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> CellVoyager 的设定——"给定已发表论文和它的数据,找作者没做过的分析"——与 **伪影注入课题** 的一个子问题同构:在 KPMP / Lake 肾脏图谱等公共数据上,系统性地寻找原文没报告的异常细胞态或疾病相关程序。但它恰恰示范了这种"自主发现"最需要被约束的地方:它会把细胞当独立样本做检验(伪重复)、会报告 r=0.047 这样的弱信号作为"发现"、新颖性只由原作者主观打分。对 **Agent 可信度课题** 这是一个很好的**被审计对象**:它的 notebook 轨迹天然把"声明(markdown 解读)"和"证据(代码 + 输出 + 图)"并排放置,正适合做声明-证据核查——检查每条 VLM 解读是否被对应输出支持、统计单位是否正确(细胞 vs 供体)、多次重跑是否给出相同结论。这些错误类型(伪重复、效应量夸大、单模态推断、把 VLM 读图当证据)可直接成为组学失败分类的条目。对 detect → model → revert 主链,它属于"从数据到假设"那一环,其输出应先经过数据层可信度(QC、批次、环境 RNA)与 verifier 过滤,才能进入扰动/逆转候选的排序。
<!-- EN --> CellVoyager's setting — "given a published paper and its data, find analyses the authors did not run" — is isomorphic to an **artifact-injection-topic** sub-problem: systematically searching public data such as KPMP / the Lake kidney atlas for anomalous cell states or disease programmes the original papers did not report. But it also shows exactly where such "autonomous discovery" needs constraints: it tests cells as independent samples (pseudo-replication), reports weak signals like r=0.047 as findings, and novelty is scored only subjectively by the original author. For **the agent-trustworthiness topic** it is an ideal **audit target**: its notebook trace places the "claim" (markdown interpretation) next to the "evidence" (code + output + figure), which is exactly the input a claim-to-evidence checker needs — does each VLM interpretation follow from its output, is the statistical unit right (cell vs. donor), do reruns reach the same conclusion. These error types (pseudo-replication, effect-size inflation, single-modality inference, treating VLM figure-reading as evidence) can become entries in the omics failure taxonomy directly. On the detect → model → revert chain it sits at "data to hypothesis"; its outputs should pass data-level reliability filters (QC, batch, ambient RNA) and the verifier before entering perturbation/reversion ranking.
