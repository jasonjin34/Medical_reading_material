## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> SciTrace(CMU)把安全推理「织进」AI scientist 流水线的每一步:**Safety-Intrinsic Reasoning Loop (SIR)** 在 Thinker → Experimenter → Writer → Reviewer 四个阶段之间维护并传递一个**累积风险状态**,**Compositional Tool-Chain Verifier (CTV)** 在每次工具调用前结合完整调用历史判断「这一串单步无害的操作合起来是否危险」;在 SciSafetyBench 上相对 SafeScientist 把工具调用安全率平均提升 14.3 个百分点,并抓到单步监控漏掉的 78.8% 组合式风险。
<!-- EN --> SciTrace (CMU) weaves safety reasoning into every stage of an AI-scientist pipeline: a **Safety-Intrinsic Reasoning Loop (SIR)** maintains and propagates a **cumulative risk state** across the Thinker → Experimenter → Writer → Reviewer stages, and a **Compositional Tool-Chain Verifier (CTV)** checks each tool call against the full call history to ask whether a sequence of individually benign steps adds up to something dangerous. On SciSafetyBench it raises tool-call safety by 14.3 points on average over SafeScientist and catches 78.8% of the compositional risks that single-step monitors miss.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 现有 AI scientist 的安全层与核心推理是**结构上分离**的:只检查流水线输出,不参与产出它们的推理。这带来两个失败模式:(1) **风险信号是阶段局部的** —— SafeScientist 的四层防御各自独立,Thinker 阶段的 prompt monitor 标出的双重用途风险只写进本地缓冲,不会传给 Experimenter 与 Writer;(2) **单步监控看不到组合风险** —— 例如「检索病原体基因组」→「查询抗生素耐药数据库」→「请求蛋白结构预测」,每步单独看都能通过,但整条轨迹指向双重用途研究。
<!-- EN --> Safety layers in current AI scientists are **structurally divorced** from core reasoning: they inspect outputs rather than shaping the deliberation that produces them. Two failure modes follow: (1) **risk signals are stage-local** — SafeScientist's four defence layers act independently, so a dual-use warning raised by the Thinker-stage prompt monitor is written to a local buffer and never reaches the Experimenter or Writer; (2) **single-step monitors miss compositional risk** — e.g. "retrieve a pathogen genome" → "query antibiotic-resistance databases" → "request protein-structure prediction" each pass individually, yet the trajectory points at dual-use research.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> 在 SafeScientist 流水线上叠加两个组件,共享一个**累积风险状态**(SafeScientist 原有的过滤器只作为兜底)。**风险分级**:五级而非二元 —— SAFE、LOW-RISK、WARNING、HIGH-RISK、BLOCK;整体风险取所有信号的最大值,且某些类别组合(如双重用途 + 危险合成)同时出现时**再升一级**(interaction escalation)。**SIR**:每个阶段用结构化模板输入三样东西 —— 当前阶段任务内容、累积风险状态、从安全记忆中检索的历史检查项(仿 AGrail,按关键词重叠取 k 近邻);LLM 输出风险级别、自然语言描述与建议动作(proceed / modify / flag / block);各阶段提示侧重不同(Thinker 看双重用途、Experimenter 看工具与协议安全、Writer 看信息披露、Reviewer 带全量信号做伦理审查)。**CTV**:通过 verified tool proxy 拦截每次工具调用,单次 LLM 调用评三个维度 —— 请求本身是否有害、**该调用与完整历史是否构成危险轨迹(组合风险)**、该调用本身是否安全(改编自 ToolSafe 的 TS-Guard,把 attack-correlation 维度换成组合风险);合成分数映射为 allow / modify / block;被 modify 或 block 时,再用一次 LLM 调用(TS-Flow)给出**安全替代方案**(如把结核分枝杆菌换成非致病的耻垢分枝杆菌)而非硬性中止。风险分类含 9 类(S1 危险物质合成 … S9 组合危险)。SIR 与 CTV 使用与主 agent 相同的底座模型。
<!-- EN --> Two components are layered onto the SafeScientist pipeline and share one **cumulative risk state** (SafeScientist's original filters remain only as fallback). **Risk levels**: five graded levels instead of binary — SAFE, LOW-RISK, WARNING, HIGH-RISK, BLOCK; the overall level is the max over signals, with **interaction escalation** by one step when certain category pairs co-occur (e.g. dual-use + hazardous synthesis). **SIR**: at each stage a structured template supplies the stage's task content, the cumulative risk state and safety checks retrieved from a safety memory (following AGrail, k-nearest by keyword overlap); the LLM returns a risk level, a description and an action (proceed / modify / flag / block); prompts are stage-specific (dual-use at Thinker, tool and protocol safety at Experimenter, information disclosure at Writer, a full-visibility ethical review at Reviewer). **CTV**: a verified tool proxy intercepts every call and, in one LLM pass, scores request harmfulness, **compositional risk of this call given the full history**, and tool-invocation safety (adapted from ToolSafe's TS-Guard, replacing attack correlation with compositional risk); the combined score maps to allow / modify / block, and on modify/block a second LLM call (TS-Flow) proposes a **safe alternative** (e.g. substituting non-pathogenic M. smegmatis for M. tuberculosis) rather than a hard stop. The risk taxonomy has nine categories (S1 hazardous synthesis … S9 compositional danger). SIR and CTV use the same backbone as the primary agent.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> SciSafetyBench(来自 SafeScientist):240 个高风险科研任务 + 120 个工具相关风险任务,30 个专用科学工具,均匀分布于物理、化学、生物、材料、信息科学、医学六个领域(每领域 40 + 20),按四种风险类型等比例标注(故意恶意、隐蔽危害、非故意后果、多步工具轨迹)。底座模型:Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct、Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct、DeepSeek-V3(本地 vLLM、4-bit AWQ、两张 RTX A5000)与 GPT-4o(API)。指标沿用 SafeScientist:Safety Score(1–5,GPT-4o 判分)、拒绝率、工具调用安全率、Quality/Clarity/Overall(1–5),另加组合风险检出率。
<!-- EN --> SciSafetyBench (from SafeScientist): 240 high-risk research tasks + 120 tool-related risk tasks with 30 specialized scientific tools, evenly spread over physics, chemistry, biology, materials, information science and medicine (40 + 20 per domain), labelled equally by four risk types (intentional malice, concealed harm, unintentional consequences, multi-step tool trajectories). Backbones: Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, DeepSeek-V3 (local vLLM, 4-bit AWQ, two RTX A5000s) and GPT-4o (API). Metrics follow SafeScientist: Safety Score (1–5, GPT-4o judge), reject rate, tool-call safety rate, Quality/Clarity/Overall (1–5), plus compositional-risk detection rate.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **主表**(Bare LLM / SafeScientist / SciTrace,工具调用安全率):Llama-3.1-70B 38.5 / 76.3 / 91.2%;Qwen2.5-72B 40.2 / 78.1 / 92.5%;DeepSeek-V3 42.0 / 79.5 / 93.8%;GPT-4o 45.5 / 81.2 / 94.7% —— 平均 +14.3 pp;拒绝率在四个底座上为 92–95%(SafeScientist 85–90%);Safety Score 4.87–4.93;Quality/Overall 等质量指标同时略升(例如 Qwen2.5-72B 的 Overall 3.50 → 3.72)。与 AI Scientist、CycleResearcher、ResearchTown、AI Co-Scientist、Agent Laboratory 相比,这些框架的拒绝率仅 0–22%。**消融**(Qwen2.5-72B):仅 SIR 使 Safety +0.06、拒绝率 +2.5 pp;仅 CTV 使工具安全率 +11.6 pp;二者合用最好。**分领域**:生物(+17.3 pp)与化学(+17.2 pp)提升最大,物理最小(+10.7 pp);信息科学绝对值最低(86.6%)。**组合风险**:SafeScientist 单步全部放行但整条轨迹被判不安全的 80 个任务中,CTV 检出 63 个(78.8%);生物、化学、医学、材料 81–83%,物理 77.8%,信息科学仅 64.3%(数据外泄类模式与 CTV 针对的物理合成类轨迹结构不同)。**拒答分析**:直接拒绝只增加 5.9 pp,提升主要来自安全改道(+12.2 pp)与安全完成(+19.2 pp)。**对抗**:讨论攻击防御成功率平均 39.1% → 65.2%(+26.1 pp);各类攻击拒绝率平均 Llama 48.3 → 73.6%、GPT-4o 55.6 → 79.7%;对恶意讨论 agent 提升最大(+37.3 / +35.5 pp)。**代价**:每次流水线运行延迟增加 36.9–43.8%。
<!-- EN --> **Main table** (Bare LLM / SafeScientist / SciTrace, tool-call safety rate): Llama-3.1-70B 38.5 / 76.3 / 91.2%; Qwen2.5-72B 40.2 / 78.1 / 92.5%; DeepSeek-V3 42.0 / 79.5 / 93.8%; GPT-4o 45.5 / 81.2 / 94.7% — +14.3 pp on average; reject rates 92–95% across backbones (SafeScientist 85–90%); Safety Score 4.87–4.93; quality metrics rise slightly too (e.g. Qwen2.5-72B Overall 3.50 → 3.72). AI Scientist, CycleResearcher, ResearchTown, AI Co-Scientist and Agent Laboratory reject only 0–22%. **Ablation** (Qwen2.5-72B): SIR alone +0.06 Safety and +2.5 pp reject rate; CTV alone +11.6 pp tool safety; both together best. **By domain**: biology (+17.3 pp) and chemistry (+17.2 pp) gain most, physics least (+10.7 pp); information science has the lowest absolute rate (86.6%). **Compositional risk**: of 80 tasks where SafeScientist approved every single call but the trajectory was judged unsafe, CTV catches 63 (78.8%) — 81–83% in biology, chemistry, medicine and materials, 77.8% in physics, only 64.3% in information science (data-exfiltration patterns differ structurally from the physical-synthesis trajectories CTV targets). **Refusal analysis**: direct refusals rise only 5.9 pp; gains come from safe redirections (+12.2 pp) and safe completions (+19.2 pp). **Adversarial**: discussion-attack defence 39.1% → 65.2% (+26.1 pp); average attack rejection Llama 48.3 → 73.6%, GPT-4o 55.6 → 79.7%, largest against malicious discussion agents (+37.3 / +35.5 pp). **Cost**: 36.9–43.8% added latency per pipeline run.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把安全从「阶段局部过滤器」改为**跨阶段传递的累积状态**,并引入分级风险与组合升级规则。 <!-- EN --> Turns safety from stage-local filters into a **cumulative state propagated across stages**, with graded levels and combination escalation.
- <!-- ZH --> **轨迹级**工具验证:判断的是「这一步 + 之前所有步」而非单步。 <!-- EN --> **Trajectory-level** tool verification: judging "this step plus all prior steps" rather than one step.
- <!-- ZH --> 用纠正性反馈给出安全替代方案,在提升安全的同时不明显牺牲科研产出质量。 <!-- EN --> Corrective feedback with safe alternatives, improving safety without visibly sacrificing research-output quality.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **评估高度依赖 GPT-4o 作为判分者**:作者在 60 个任务上的非正式验证显示与人工标签的 Pearson r 在物理/材料约 0.62、生物/化学约 0.81;尚无领域专家一致性研究与跨 judge 稳健性检验(作者自承)。 <!-- EN --> **Evaluation leans heavily on GPT-4o as judge**: informal validation on 60 tasks gives Pearson r with human labels of ~0.62 (physics/materials) vs ~0.81 (biology/chemistry); no domain-expert agreement study or cross-judge robustness check yet (acknowledged).
- <!-- ZH --> 只在 SciSafetyBench 一个基准上评测,且建立在 SafeScientist 之上;「组合逃逸」本身也由 GPT-4o 判定,存在 judge 循环依赖。 <!-- EN --> Evaluated on a single benchmark (SciSafetyBench) on top of SafeScientist; "compositional escapes" are themselves defined by GPT-4o judgements, a circular dependence on the judge.
- <!-- ZH --> 安全记忆按关键词重叠检索,会漏掉语义相近但措辞不同的风险;S9 组合危险偏向物理合成,对数据隐私类轨迹检出低。 <!-- EN --> Safety memory retrieves by keyword overlap and misses semantically similar but lexically different risks; S9 compositional danger is tuned to physical synthesis and detects data-privacy trajectories poorly.
- <!-- ZH --> 延迟增加约 37–44%;本地底座使用 4-bit 量化,可能影响与原论文结果的可比性。 <!-- EN --> ~37–44% added latency; local backbones use 4-bit quantization, which may affect comparability with original results.
- <!-- ZH --> 主表数值在四个底座间非常整齐,但正文未报告多次运行方差(CI 在附录)。 <!-- EN --> Main-table numbers are very regular across four backbones, while run-to-run variance is not in the main text (CIs are in the appendix).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 主题是**安全**(双重用途、危险合成),与 Plan B 的**正确性**(claim 是否被证据支持)不同,但其**架构思想可以直接迁移到 Plan B 的 claim-to-evidence verifier**。(1) **累积状态跨阶段传递** ↔ 组学分析里,QC 阶段发现的问题(例如某批样本 ambient RNA 偏高、某 Xenium 切片配准差)常在下游 DE 与通路分析中被「遗忘」,最终 claim 却仍建立在这些样本之上;一个「累积可信度状态」(而非累积风险状态)在 QC → 聚类 → 注释 → DE → 结论各阶段传递,最后由 verifier 判断 claim 是否依赖了被标记的数据 —— 这正是 Plan A 的「跨模态一致性区分 artifact 与疾病」在 agent 层面的对应物。(2) **组合式检查** ↔ 组学中「每一步单看都合理、合起来得出错误结论」的典型情况:先按疾病状态做 batch correction、再在校正后数据上做疾病 DE(信号被循环利用);CTV 式「结合完整历史判断当前一步」正是检测这种问题的形式。(3) **五级风险 + 改道而非拒绝** ↔ verifier 输出不应只有「对/错」,而应有 supported / weakly supported / unsupported / contradicted 等分级,并给出「要支持这个 claim 还需要做什么分析」的建议。(4) **反面教材**:整套评估由 GPT-4o 判分、与人工 r 只有 0.62–0.81 —— 恰好说明 Plan B 需要**校准过、有专家标注验证的 verifier**,而不是又一个未经验证的 LLM judge(呼应 ABC 的 O.c.1)。对 Plan A 关系较远。
<!-- EN --> The topic is **safety** (dual use, hazardous synthesis), not Plan B's **correctness** (is a claim supported by evidence), but its **architectural ideas transfer directly to Plan B's claim-to-evidence verifier**. (1) **Cumulative state across stages** ↔ in omics analysis, problems found at QC (e.g. high ambient RNA in a batch, poor Xenium registration on a section) are often "forgotten" downstream in DE and pathway analysis while the final claim still rests on those samples; a cumulative *credibility* state (instead of risk state) carried through QC → clustering → annotation → DE → conclusion, with the verifier checking whether a claim depends on flagged data, is the agent-level counterpart of Plan A's "cross-modal agreement separates artifacts from disease". (2) **Compositional checking** ↔ the classic omics case where every step looks reasonable but the combination is wrong: batch-correcting with disease status as a covariate and then running disease DE on the corrected data (double-dipping); CTV-style "judge this step against the full history" is the right form to catch it. (3) **Five graded levels + redirection instead of refusal** ↔ a verifier should output graded verdicts (supported / weakly supported / unsupported / contradicted) and suggest "what further analysis would support this claim", not just right/wrong. (4) **Cautionary example**: the whole evaluation is GPT-4o-judged with only r ≈ 0.62–0.81 against humans — precisely why Plan B needs a **calibrated verifier validated against expert labels**, not another unvalidated LLM judge (cf. ABC's O.c.1). Relation to Plan A is distant.
