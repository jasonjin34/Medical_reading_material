## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 用「健康参照」找疾病改变的细胞状态时,参照该怎么选?作者给出系统答案:**用大规模健康 atlas 学 latent space(scVI + scArches 映射),再只拿匹配的 control 样本做差异丰度检验(Milo)**——即 ACR(atlas-to-control reference)设计;它比只用 atlas(假阳性暴涨)或只用 control(漏掉多种异常状态)都更准,且有 atlas 时可以少采 control 而不增加 FDR。
<!-- EN --> When using a "healthy reference" to find disease-altered cell states, which reference should you use? The authors give a systematic answer: **learn the latent space on a large healthy atlas (scVI + scArches mapping), then run differential-abundance testing (Milo) against matched controls only** — the ACR (atlas-to-control reference) design. It beats atlas-only (false positives explode) and control-only (misses multiple altered states), and with an atlas available, the number of controls can be cut without inflating FDR.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 标准流程是:把疾病与健康数据整合进同一 latent space,再在匹配的细胞状态上做差异分析。参照数据的选择很关键但缺乏证据:Human Cell Atlas 级别的 atlas 覆盖面广、细胞状态全,但人群与实验协议与疾病队列不匹配,可能引入混杂造成假阳性;而匹配的 control 样本难采、量少,用于整合时会漏掉稀有状态、过度解读样本噪音。问题是:atlas 能否替代 control?两者如何组合?
<!-- EN --> The standard workflow integrates disease and healthy data in one latent space and then runs differential analysis on matched cell states. Reference choice is crucial but under-studied: Human Cell Atlas-scale atlases cover many states but differ from disease cohorts in demographics and protocols, risking confounded false positives; matched controls are hard to collect, few in number, and when used for integration can miss rare states and overfit sample noise. Can an atlas replace controls, and how should the two be combined?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> 三步流程:(1) 在「embedding 参照」上训练降维模型(scVI,5,000 HVG,n_latent=30);(2) 用 scArches 把 query(含疾病样本)迁移映射到同一 latent space;(3) 用 Milo 在 kNN 邻域层面做差异丰度(DA)检验(10% spatial FDR)。对比三种参照设计:**AR**(atlas 既做 embedding 又做 DA 参照)、**CR**(control 两步都做;又分 query mapping 的 CR scArches 与 control+disease 拼接联合训练的 CR scVI)、**ACR**(atlas 做 embedding、control 做 DA)。用 **out-of-reference (OOR)** 模拟构建 ground truth:从 13 个研究、1,248 个供者的健康 PBMC 中取一个研究随机拆成伪疾病(16 供者)与 control(13 供者),其余 12 个研究(1,219 供者,513,565 细胞)作为 atlas;把某一细胞类型从 control 与 atlas 中删除,使其成为疾病特有的 OOR 状态。还模拟了「多个 OOR 状态」(固定删除经典单核细胞 + 一个按 PCA 拆半的 shifted OOR 状态)。另外比较了 latent 距离、label-transfer 不确定性等替代判据(Supplementary Note 2.1)。
<!-- EN --> Three-step workflow: (1) train a dimensionality-reduction model (scVI, 5,000 HVGs, n_latent=30) on an "embedding reference"; (2) map the query (incl. disease samples) into that latent space with scArches transfer learning; (3) run neighbourhood-level differential abundance (DA) with Milo (10% spatial FDR). Three reference designs are compared: **AR** (atlas for both embedding and DA), **CR** (control for both; split into CR scArches with query mapping and CR scVI trained jointly on concatenated control+disease), and **ACR** (atlas for embedding, control for DA). Ground truth comes from **out-of-reference (OOR)** simulations: from healthy PBMCs of 13 studies / 1,248 donors, one study is split at random into pseudo-disease (16 donors) and control (13 donors), the other 12 studies (1,219 donors, 513,565 cells) form the atlas; one cell type is removed from control and atlas so it becomes a disease-specific OOR state. A "mixed OOR" scenario removes classical monocytes plus a PCA-split "shifted" OOR state. Alternative criteria (latent distance, label-transfer uncertainty) are compared in Supplementary Note 2.1.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 模拟:CELLxGENE 上 13 个健康 PBMC 10x 研究,下采样到每样本 500 细胞,共约 59.7 万高质量细胞、16 种细胞类型(atlas 513,565 / control 5,671 / 伪疾病 7,505 细胞),测试 15 种 OOR 状态。真实数据一:COVID-19 PBMC 队列(90 名患者、23 名健康志愿者;疾病 48,083、control 14,426 细胞)+ 12 研究 1,219 人的 PBMC atlas。真实数据二:特发性肺纤维化(IPF)肺实质 scRNA-seq(32 名 IPF、28 名 control、18 名 COPD;IPF 144,404、control 95,303 细胞)+ 核心 Human Lung Cell Atlas(HLCA,584,844 细胞)。
<!-- EN --> Simulation: 13 healthy-PBMC 10x studies from CELLxGENE, downsampled to 500 cells/sample, ~597k high-quality cells, 16 cell types (atlas 513,565 / control 5,671 / pseudo-disease 7,505 cells), 15 OOR states tested. Real data 1: COVID-19 PBMC cohort (90 patients, 23 healthy volunteers; 48,083 disease, 14,426 control cells) plus a 12-study, 1,219-donor PBMC atlas. Real data 2: idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) lung parenchyma scRNA-seq (32 IPF, 28 control, 18 COPD; 144,404 IPF, 95,303 control cells) plus the core Human Lung Cell Atlas (HLCA, 584,844 cells).

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **单一 OOR 状态**:ACR 灵敏检测高 OOR 比例邻域;AR 假阳性大量膨胀(OOR 比例为 0 的邻域也被判显著);CR scArches 的 FDR 高于 ACR;CR scVI 与 ACR 相当。各设计的整合质量差异很小;改用其他 DA 方法结论一致;任何方法都需要每种细胞类型约 ≥250 个细胞才能检出 OOR。 <!-- EN --> **Single OOR state**: ACR sensitively detects neighbourhoods with high OOR fraction; AR inflates false positives (significant even where the OOR fraction is 0); CR scArches has higher FDR than ACR; CR scVI is comparable to ACR. Integration quality barely differs across designs; conclusions hold with other DA methods; all methods need roughly ≥250 cells per cell type to detect an OOR state.
- <!-- ZH --> **CR scVI 的好表现来自特征选择**:在 control+disease 上选 HVG 会把 OOR 标记基因选进来;控制 HVG 来源后只有 CR scVI 明显掉点(control+disease 的 HVG 与仅 control 的平均重叠 81%,与仅 atlas 的重叠 68%)。在**多个 OOR 状态**的模拟里 ACR 最好。 <!-- EN --> **CR scVI's good showing is driven by feature selection**: HVGs chosen on control+disease include OOR markers; when HVG source is controlled, only CR scVI drops substantially (control+disease HVGs overlap 81% with control-only and 68% with atlas-only on average). In the **multiple-OOR** simulations ACR is best.
- <!-- ZH --> **稳健性**:control 队列变小时 ACR 始终优于 CR(AUPRC 分布 t 检验 P < 0.01);atlas 至少约 10,000 个细胞时 ACR 灵敏度稳定;无 control 时性能更依赖 atlas 规模(AUPRC 与规模相关 R² = 0.69 对 AR,0.4 对 ACR),且 atlas 变小时 AR 的 FPR 上升。 <!-- EN --> **Robustness**: ACR beats CR as the control cohort shrinks (t-test on AUPRC distributions P < 0.01); ACR sensitivity is stable once the atlas has ≥~10,000 cells; without controls performance depends more on atlas size (AUPRC–size R² = 0.69 for AR vs 0.4 for ACR), and AR's FPR rises with smaller atlases.
- <!-- ZH --> **COVID-19**:DA logFC 与 IFN 签名均值的相关性 ACR 更强(Pearson R 0.63 对 CR 0.52,Fisher z P < 2.2×10⁻¹⁶);区分出 CD14⁺ 单核细胞的 IFNhi 与 IFNlo 两种 COVID 相关状态:IFNhi 在轻症/无症状最突出(相比健康 Wilcoxon P = 1.19×10⁻⁷),IFNlo(HLA-DRlo S100Ahi 功能失调单核细胞)在中重症占优;CR 设计无法区分正常 IFNlo 与重症 IFNlo。 <!-- EN --> **COVID-19**: ACR correlates DA logFC more strongly with the mean IFN signature (Pearson R 0.63 vs CR 0.52; Fisher z P < 2.2×10⁻¹⁶); it separates IFNhi and IFNlo COVID-associated CD14⁺ monocyte states: IFNhi most prominent in mild/asymptomatic (vs healthy, Wilcoxon P = 1.19×10⁻⁷), IFNlo (HLA-DRlo S100Ahi dysfunctional monocytes) dominant in moderate-to-critical disease; CR cannot separate normal IFNlo from severe IFNlo.
- <!-- ZH --> **IPF**:ACR 在检出促纤维化 SPP1⁺ 巨噬细胞和诊断签名阳性的基质/上皮状态上均优于 AR/CR;CR 错误地优先选出样本数显著更少的邻域(残留 batch effect)。ACR 在肺实质中区分出 KRT5⁺KRT17hi 异常 basal 细胞与 KRT5⁻KRT17⁺ basaloid 细胞两种不同异常状态,鉴定 981 个差异基因(FDR 5%),部分是在研药物靶点(如 CSF2、CCL2)。 <!-- EN --> **IPF**: ACR beats AR/CR in detecting profibrotic SPP1⁺ macrophages and diagnostic-signature-high stromal/epithelial states; CR wrongly prioritizes neighbourhoods with significantly fewer samples (residual batch effects). ACR separates two distinct aberrant states in parenchyma — KRT5⁺KRT17hi aberrant basal cells vs KRT5⁻KRT17⁺ basaloid cells — with 981 DEGs (FDR 5%), some of which are drug targets in trials (e.g. CSF2, CCL2).

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把「健康参照怎么用」拆成 embedding 参照与 DA 参照两个独立选择,并给出明确的 ACR 推荐。 <!-- EN --> Splits "how to use a healthy reference" into two independent choices — embedding reference vs DA reference — and gives a clear ACR recommendation.
- <!-- ZH --> 基于真实数据切分的 OOR 模拟框架,保留真实供者/文库效应,可作为「疾病状态检测」的 ground-truth 基准。 <!-- EN --> An OOR simulation framework built by splitting real data, preserving real donor/library effects — a ground-truth benchmark for disease-state detection.
- <!-- ZH --> 量化 atlas 规模、control 数量、HVG 选择对假阳性的影响,给出队列设计指南(有 atlas 时可减少 control)。 <!-- EN --> Quantifies how atlas size, control count and HVG selection drive false positives, giving cohort-design guidelines (fewer controls needed when an atlas exists).
- <!-- ZH --> 倡导 atlas 构建者共享训练好的 embedding 模型以便 scArches 映射。 <!-- EN --> Advocates that atlas builders share trained embedding models for scArches-style mapping.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 依赖两条假设:control 中的所有状态在 atlas 中都存在;疾病只改变部分细胞类型。 <!-- EN --> Relies on two assumptions: every state in the controls is present in the atlas, and disease alters only a fraction of cell types.
- <!-- ZH --> 模拟的 OOR 是「删除细胞类型」或「拆半」,比真实的连续性疾病漂移更离散;检出需约 ≥250 细胞,稀有状态受限。 <!-- EN --> Simulated OOR states are "removed types" or "split halves" — more discrete than real continuous disease shifts; detection needs ~≥250 cells, limiting rare states.
- <!-- ZH --> 仅 scRNA-seq、仅 PBMC 与肺;不涉及空间、多模态或 ATAC。 <!-- EN --> scRNA-seq only, PBMC and lung only; no spatial, multimodal or ATAC data.
- <!-- ZH --> 仍需匹配 control;完全没有 control 时的替代方案只在补充材料中讨论。 <!-- EN --> Matched controls are still required; options without controls are only discussed in the supplement.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是 detect 阶段的**方法学基石**:我们把「疾病改变的细胞状态」当作异常,而本文告诉我们异常检测的「正常」该如何定义——**atlas 定义坐标系,匹配 control 定义统计零假设**。对 **Plan A**(肾脏组学异常检测)直接可用:用 KPMP / Lake 肾脏 atlas / HuBMAP 做 embedding 参照,把院内 95 例活检的 snRNA 映射进去,再用院内或 KPMP 的匹配健康样本(如肿瘤肾切除旁正常组织)做 DA 对照;本文的 OOR 模拟(从健康数据里删掉一类细胞)也可以直接改造成 **KidneyAnomalyBench** 的合成 ground-truth 部分,与专家标注互补。本文也解释了一种重要失败模式——「AR 设计下批次/协议差异被误判为疾病」——这正是 Plan A 需要用**跨模态一致性**(snRNA vs snATAC vs Xenium)去区分 artifact 与疾病的原因:单模态的 DA 显著性不足以定罪。对 **Plan B**(可信生物医学 agent),本文给出了一个可执行的「声明→证据」核查规则:当 agent 声称「发现某疾病特异细胞状态」时,verifier 应检查其参照设计(是否有匹配 control?HVG 是否在疾病数据上选择?邻域样本数是否异常少?),这些都可以写进 omics-specific failure taxonomy。
<!-- EN --> This is a **methodological cornerstone** for the detect stage: we treat disease-altered cell states as anomalies, and this paper tells us how to define "normal" — **the atlas defines the coordinate system, matched controls define the statistical null**. For **Plan A** (kidney-omics anomaly detection) it is directly usable: use KPMP / the Lake kidney atlas / HuBMAP as the embedding reference, map the in-house 95-biopsy snRNA into it, and use matched healthy samples (in-house or KPMP, e.g. tumour-nephrectomy normal tissue) as the DA controls; the OOR simulation (remove one cell type from healthy data) can be repurposed as the synthetic ground-truth arm of **KidneyAnomalyBench**, complementing expert labels. It also documents a key failure mode — batch/protocol differences mistaken for disease under the AR design — which is exactly why Plan A uses **cross-modal agreement** (snRNA vs snATAC vs Xenium) to separate artifacts from disease: single-modality DA significance is not enough. For **Plan B** (trustworthy biomedical agents), it yields concrete claim-to-evidence checks: when an agent claims "a disease-specific cell state was found," a verifier should check the reference design (matched controls? HVGs selected on disease data? suspiciously few samples per neighbourhood?) — all entries for an omics-specific failure taxonomy.
