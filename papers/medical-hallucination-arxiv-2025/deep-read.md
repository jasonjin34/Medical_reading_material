## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 一篇「综述 + 基准实验 + 医生标注 + 临床调查」的综合性工作:把医学幻觉定义为**可能改变临床决策的**错误/不一致/无据输出,给出五大类分类法;在 Med-HALT 上测 11 个模型发现**通用模型反而比医学专用模型更少幻觉**(基线无幻觉比例中位数 76.6% vs 51.3%),CoT 是最稳定的缓解手段;医生在 NEJM 病例上的标注显示,错误更多出在**时间顺序与化验数据理解**而非诊断本身。
<!-- EN --> A combined survey + benchmark + physician annotation + clinician survey: medical hallucination is defined as erroneous, inconsistent or unsupported output **that could alter clinical decisions**, with a five-cluster taxonomy. On Med-HALT across 11 models, **general-purpose models hallucinate less than medical-specialized ones** (median hallucination-free baseline 76.6% vs 51.3%), and CoT is the most consistent mitigation; physician annotation of NEJM cases shows errors concentrate in **chronological ordering and lab-data understanding** rather than diagnosis itself.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 基础模型进入临床流程,但其「看似合理却错误」的输出在医学中风险极高:用户与 AI 之间存在巨大的知识不对称,捏造药物、禁忌用药、错误影像解读都可能直接伤害患者。医学场景还有特殊难点:知识快速更新、要求精确、概念高度互联(小错误会级联)、术语与语境专业。作者想回答:医学幻觉与一般幻觉有何不同、如何分类、成因是什么、如何检测与缓解、真实临床人员如何经历它,以及监管与法律含义。
<!-- EN --> Foundation models are entering clinical workflows, but plausible-yet-wrong outputs are especially dangerous in medicine: there is a large knowledge asymmetry between AI and users, and fabricated drugs, contraindicated recommendations or false imaging reads can directly harm patients. Medicine adds specific difficulties: rapidly evolving knowledge, required precision, densely interconnected concepts where small errors cascade, and specialised jargon. The authors ask how medical hallucination differs from general hallucination, how to classify it, what causes it, how to detect and mitigate it, how clinicians experience it, and what the regulatory/legal implications are.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **分类法**(图 2 / 表 2):五大簇 —— (a) 事实错误(非事实、事实、输入冲突型);(b) 过时引用(记忆型);(c) 虚假相关(偏见诱导、拼接、多模态整合型);(d) 捏造来源或指南(流程型、研究型);(e) 不完整推理链(推理、决策、诊断型),并类比临床医生的认知偏差(如确认偏误)。**基准实验**(第 6 节):Med-HALT 七个任务各抽 50 例共 350 例 —— 推理类(False Confidence、None of the Above、Fake Questions)与记忆类(Abstract-to-Link、PMID-to-Title、Title-to-Link、Link-to-Title);五种条件:Base、System Prompt、CoT(「Let's think step by step.」)、MedRAG(基于 KG 的医学 RAG)、Internet Search(SerpAPI 谷歌搜索);指标为 Med-HALT pointwise 分(对 +1、错 −0.25)与 UMLSBERT 语义相似度;统计用 Mann–Whitney 与 Benjamini–Hochberg FDR 校正。**医生标注**(第 7 节):20 份 NEJM 病例记录,三类任务(时间顺序、化验数据理解、诊断预测),5 个模型,7 名 MD 级标注者按幻觉类型与 0–5 级临床风险标注,用平均成对 Jaccard 指数衡量一致性。**调查**(第 8 节):31 题在线问卷,2024-09-15 至 12-18,MIT COUHES IRB 豁免。
<!-- EN --> **Taxonomy** (Fig. 2 / Table 2): five clusters — (a) factual errors (non-factual, factual, input-conflicting); (b) outdated references (memory-based); (c) spurious correlations (bias-induced, amalgamated, multimodal-integration); (d) fabricated sources or guidelines (procedural, research); (e) incomplete chains of reasoning (reasoning, decision-making, diagnostic) — with analogies to clinician cognitive biases such as confirmation bias. **Benchmark** (Section 6): 50 examples from each of seven Med-HALT tasks (350 total) — reasoning tests (False Confidence, None of the Above, Fake Questions) and memory tests (Abstract-to-Link, PMID-to-Title, Title-to-Link, Link-to-Title); five conditions: Base, System Prompt, CoT ("Let's think step by step."), MedRAG (KG-based medical RAG), Internet Search (SerpAPI Google); metrics are the Med-HALT pointwise score (+1 correct, −0.25 wrong) and UMLSBERT semantic similarity; statistics via Mann–Whitney and Benjamini–Hochberg FDR. **Physician annotation** (Section 7): 20 NEJM case records, three tasks (chronological ordering, lab-data understanding, diagnosis prediction), 5 models, 7 MD-level annotators labelling hallucination type and clinical risk on a 0–5 scale, agreement via average pairwise Jaccard index. **Survey** (Section 8): a 31-question online instrument, 15 September – 18 December 2024, MIT COUHES IRB-exempt.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> Med-HALT(350 例);NEJM 病例记录(20 份);调查 75 人参与、70 份完整回答(研究人员 29、医生 23、数据科学家 15、生物医学工程 5、其他 3)。11 个模型:通用 7 个(o3-mini、o1、GPT-4o、GPT-4o mini、GPT-5、Gemini-2.5 Pro、DeepSeek-R1),医学专用 4 个(PMC-LLaMA、AlpaCare-13B、MedAlpaca-13B、MedGemma)。医生标注的 5 个模型为 o1、gemini-2.0-flash-exp、gpt-4o、gemini-1.5-flash、claude-3.5 sonnet。
<!-- EN --> Med-HALT (350 items); NEJM case records (20); survey with 75 participants and 70 complete responses (29 researchers, 23 physicians, 15 data scientists, 5 biomedical engineers, 3 other). Eleven models: 7 general-purpose (o3-mini, o1, GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, GPT-5, Gemini-2.5 Pro, DeepSeek-R1) and 4 medical-specialized (PMC-LLaMA, AlpaCare-13B, MedAlpaca-13B, MedGemma). Physician annotation covers o1, gemini-2.0-flash-exp, gpt-4o, gemini-1.5-flash and claude-3.5 sonnet.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **Med-HALT**:gemini-2.5-pro 基线 87.6%、CoT 后 97.9%;deepseek-r1 86.6%、o3-mini 80.4%、gpt-5 71.2%、o1 64.0%、gpt-4o 54.4%、gpt-4o-mini 48.3%。医学专用:medgemma 52.6%、pmc-llama 40.8%、medalpaca 32.0%、alpacare 28.6%。通用 vs 专用基线中位数 **76.6% vs 51.3%**(差 25.2%,95% CI 18.7–31.3%,U=27.0,p=0.012,秩二列 r=−0.64)。**CoT**:在 71% 的模型中显著改善(p<0.05),FDR 校正后 64% 仍显著;46 项模型内检验中 22 项原本显著,校正后保留 19 项(86.4% 保留率)。o3-mini:基线 80.4% → System Prompt 81.4% → CoT 90.7%。**搜索增强**:gpt-5 71.2%→87.6%(+16.5%);pmc-llama 40.8%→59.9%(相对 +46.1%);但 deepseek-r1 86.6%→84.3%,强模型可能被嘈杂检索拖累 —— 建议自适应检索。语义相似度上通用强模型 0.8–0.9,医学模型 0.1–0.5。**NEJM 医生标注**:诊断预测幻觉率最低(0%–22%),时间顺序(0.25%–24.6%)与化验数据理解(0.25%–18.7%)更高;GPT-4o 在时间顺序 24.6%、化验 18.7% 最高且多被判为「显著/相当」临床风险;Claude-3.5 与 o1 在诊断预测上 0%。**调查**:摘要报告 91.8% 遇到过医学幻觉、84.7% 认为可能伤害患者;正文给出原始计数:59 人中 50 人认为所见幻觉可能影响患者健康,85%(51 人)用交叉核对外部来源应对。(注:摘要称「64–72% 的残余幻觉源于因果/时间推理失败」,正文未找到对应的完整推导;摘要「CoT 在 86.4% 的比较中显著降低幻觉」实为 FDR 保留率,解读需谨慎。)
<!-- EN --> **Med-HALT**: gemini-2.5-pro 87.6% baseline → 97.9% with CoT; deepseek-r1 86.6%, o3-mini 80.4%, gpt-5 71.2%, o1 64.0%, gpt-4o 54.4%, gpt-4o-mini 48.3%. Medical-specialized: medgemma 52.6%, pmc-llama 40.8%, medalpaca 32.0%, alpacare 28.6%. General vs specialized median baseline **76.6% vs 51.3%** (difference 25.2%, 95% CI 18.7–31.3%, U = 27.0, p = 0.012, rank-biserial r = −0.64). **CoT**: significant improvement in 71% of models (p < 0.05), 64% after FDR; of 46 within-model tests, 22 were significant and 19 survived correction (86.4% retention). o3-mini: 80.4% → 81.4% (system prompt) → 90.7% (CoT). **Search augmentation**: gpt-5 71.2% → 87.6% (+16.5%); pmc-llama 40.8% → 59.9% (+46.1% relative); but deepseek-r1 86.6% → 84.3% — noisy retrieval can hurt strong models, motivating adaptive retrieval. Semantic similarity: strong general models 0.8–0.9, medical models 0.1–0.5. **NEJM physician annotation**: diagnosis prediction has the lowest hallucination rates (0–22%), chronological ordering (0.25–24.6%) and lab-data understanding (0.25–18.7%) higher; GPT-4o is highest on chronology (24.6%) and labs (18.7%), often rated "Significant"/"Considerable" risk; Claude-3.5 and o1 reach 0% on diagnosis prediction. **Survey**: the abstract reports 91.8% had encountered medical hallucinations and 84.7% thought they could cause harm; the body gives raw counts — 50 of 59 believed observed hallucinations might affect patient health, and 85% (51) cross-reference external sources. (Caveats: the abstract's "64–72% of residual hallucinations stem from causal or temporal reasoning failures" is not fully derived in the body; its "CoT significantly reduced hallucinations in 86.4% of comparisons" is actually the FDR retention rate — interpret with care.)

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 以「是否可能改变临床决策」为核心的医学幻觉定义,以及五大簇 + 细分类型的分类法(配临床示例)。 <!-- EN --> A definition of medical hallucination centred on "could it alter clinical decisions", plus a five-cluster taxonomy with fine-grained types and clinical examples.
- <!-- ZH --> 通用 vs 医学专用模型的系统对比,挑战「领域微调更安全」的假设。 <!-- EN --> A systematic general-vs-specialized comparison challenging the "domain fine-tuning is safer" assumption.
- <!-- ZH --> 医生在真实病例上按类型 + 0–5 临床风险双维标注,揭示时间与化验信息抽取是薄弱环节。 <!-- EN --> Physician annotation on real cases by type × 0–5 clinical risk, exposing temporal and lab-data extraction as weak points.
- <!-- ZH --> 多国临床人员调查 + 监管与法律责任讨论,把技术问题连接到部署实践。 <!-- EN --> A multinational clinician survey plus regulatory/liability discussion linking the technical problem to deployment practice.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> Med-HALT 每任务仅 50 例;评分依赖 UMLSBERT 相似度与 pointwise 分,作者自己承认它们不直接反映临床安全。 <!-- EN --> Only 50 Med-HALT items per task; scoring relies on UMLSBERT similarity and the pointwise score, which the authors admit do not directly capture clinical safety.
- <!-- ZH --> 医生标注只用 20 份 NEJM 病例、且与基准实验使用的模型集不同;正文自陈存在「输出日志与图示数据不一致」需进一步核查。 <!-- EN --> Physician annotation uses only 20 NEJM cases and a different model set from the benchmark; the text itself notes a "data discrepancy between output logs and visual representation" warranting investigation.
- <!-- ZH --> 调查样本小(70 份完整回答),摘要百分比与正文计数的分母不完全一致;多个结论(「推理驱动而非知识缺失」)的论证强度高于证据。 <!-- EN --> Small survey (70 complete responses); abstract percentages and body counts use differing denominators; several claims ("reasoning-driven, not knowledge deficit") are argued more strongly than the evidence supports.
- <!-- ZH --> 医学专用模型都是较小的旧模型,与前沿通用模型的比较混杂了规模与年代因素。 <!-- EN --> The medical models are smaller and older, so the comparison confounds specialization with scale and vintage.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 对 **Agent 可信度课题** 主要提供两样东西。一是**分类法的骨架**:五大簇中「输入冲突型」(结论与给定数据矛盾)、「拼接型」(把无关信息合成一个结论)、「多模态整合型」、「捏造来源/指南」与「不完整推理链」,都能在组学 agent 中找到对应 —— 例如把 snRNA 与 Xenium 的结果错误拼接、把空间邻近误读为因果、或引用不存在的标记基因列表;Agent 可信度课题的 omics-specific taxonomy 可以以它为上层框架,再细化出组学特有的类型(批次 vs 生物学混淆、细胞注释错误、统计显著性误读)。二是**医生标注协议**:「幻觉类型 × 0–5 临床风险」的双维标注,加上多标注者的成对 Jaccard 一致性,可以直接作为 KidneyAnomalyBench / Agent 可信度课题专家标注的模板(把「临床风险」换成「若据此做湿实验验证会浪费多少/误导多严重」)。它的发现「错误集中在时间顺序与化验数值抽取」也提示:agent 处理组学结果中的**数值与元数据**(样本分组、时间点、阈值)可能比高层生物学推断更容易出错,verifier 应优先核验这些。「通用强模型优于领域微调模型」对选择 Agent 可信度课题的被评 agent 底座有参考价值,但证据存在规模混杂。与伪影注入课题无直接关系。
<!-- EN --> For **the agent-trustworthiness topic** it offers two things. First, **a taxonomy skeleton**: input-conflicting (conclusion contradicts given data), amalgamated (unrelated information merged into one conclusion), multimodal-integration, fabricated sources/guidelines and incomplete reasoning chains all have omics-agent analogues — wrongly merging snRNA and Xenium results, reading spatial proximity as causation, or citing a non-existent marker-gene list. The agent-trustworthiness topic's omics-specific taxonomy can use it as the top level and add omics-specific types (batch-vs-biology confusion, cell-annotation errors, misread significance). Second, **a physician annotation protocol**: the two-axis "hallucination type × 0–5 clinical risk" labelling with pairwise-Jaccard multi-annotator agreement can serve directly as a template for KidneyAnomalyBench / the agent-trustworthiness topic expert annotation (replacing "clinical risk" with "how much wet-lab effort would be wasted / how misleading if acted upon"). Its finding that errors concentrate in chronology and lab-value extraction also suggests agents may err more on the **numbers and metadata** in omics results (sample groups, time points, thresholds) than on high-level biological inference, so the verifier should check those first. "Strong general models beat domain-tuned ones" informs the choice of base model for the agent-trustworthiness topic's evaluated agents, though the evidence is confounded by scale. No direct bearing on the artifact-injection topic.
