## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 作者发现 AF3 / Boltz-1/2 这类扩散式 co-folding 模型**对任何分子都会生成一个看似合理的结合位姿**,于是提出两个很朴素但有效的判据:**(a) 位姿收敛性**——同一配体在多个随机种子下预测位姿彼此的平均 RMSD,真抑制剂通常 < 2 Å 且落在结合口袋 5 Å 内,无关分子则发散且远离;**(b) 两两竞争对接(pairwise competitive docking)**——一次把两个配体一起放进去共折叠,谁占住活性位点谁赢,胜率即 **CDS(Competitive Docking Score)**,据此排出抑制剂顺序。在 17 个蛋白系统(19 个结合位点)上,CDS 排序与实验活性的一致性指数 c 从 0.52(无相关)到 0.89(强相关),与 Boltz-2 自带的亲和力预测模块总体相当;把这招推广成 "All-at-Once"(一次放 25–26 个分子竞争)后,在 3,155 个 FDA 已批准药物的库里找氟喹诺酮,叠加位姿过滤后富集度达 93.5%、富集因子 62。
<!-- EN --> The authors note that diffusion-based co-folding models such as AF3 and Boltz-1/2 **produce a plausible-looking binding pose for any molecule**, and propose two plain but effective criteria: **(a) pose convergence** — the mean RMSD among poses predicted for the same ligand across several random seeds, where true inhibitors typically fall below 2 Å and within 5 Å of the pocket while unrelated molecules diverge and sit further away; and **(b) pairwise competitive docking** — co-fold two ligands at once and let whichever occupies the active site win, with the win rate as the **CDS (Competitive Docking Score)** used to rank inhibitors. Across 17 protein systems (19 binding sites), CDS rankings agree with experimental activity at concordance indices from 0.52 (no correlation) to 0.89 (strong), broadly on par with Boltz-2's own affinity-prediction module. Extended to "All-at-Once" (25–26 molecules competing in one run), screening 3,155 FDA-approved drugs for fluoroquinolones reached 93.5% enrichment with an enrichment factor of 62 after pose filtering.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 扩散式生成模型(RoseTTAFold All-Atom、AlphaFold3、Boltz-1/2 等)在预测蛋白-配体复合物结构上已经很准,往往超过传统对接程序,但有一个根本缺陷:**它们对真 binder 和无活性分子都会给出一个结合位姿**。虽然很多模型附带打分指标,但准确推断结合亲和力仍然困难。论文把问题收成两问:(i) 如何把真实结合与假阳性区分开?(ii) 如何让预测位姿的排序反映预期的结合亲和力?这两问就是全文的动机。
<!-- EN --> Diffusion-based generative models (RoseTTAFold All-Atom, AlphaFold3, Boltz-1/2) already predict protein–ligand complexes accurately, often beating classical docking programs, but carry a fundamental flaw: **they produce a binding pose for true binders and inactives alike**. Many models supply scoring metrics, yet inferring binding affinity accurately remains hard. The paper reduces this to two questions: (i) how can genuine binding be told apart from false positives? (ii) how can predicted poses be ranked so the order reflects expected binding affinity? These two questions motivate the whole study.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **(1) 位姿判据**:对每个配体用**至少 5 个随机种子**做模型推理,每次推理生成**至少 25 个模型**;把预测的蛋白叠合到参考晶体结构上(PyMOL 的 super 命令),然后看两件事——配体距参考结合位点中心的距离、以及多个预测位姿彼此的平均 RMSD(即位姿收敛性)。真抑制剂的经验阈值是距离 < 5.0 Å、收敛性 < 2.0 Å。**(2) 两两竞争对接**:把蛋白模型与**两个相互竞争的配体**一起交给 co-folding 模型推理,占住活性位点的那个算这一轮的赢家;对每一对配体做至少 n 次独立推理,胜率(百分比)即 CDS;把所有配体两两对战的结果汇成一个**配对矩阵**,按累计 CDS 排序。**(3) All-at-Once(为降低成本的简化版)**:不做两两对战,而是把整类抑制剂一次性放进一次推理里竞争。用于虚拟筛选时,把 3,155 个化合物的库随机切成 **124 组**(109 组各 25 个、15 组各 26 个——分组大小由 GPU 显存决定),每组做一次 All-at-Once 推理。**(4) 多位点系统的处理**:在有多个口袋的系统里,可以**先用已知配体把其他位点占住**,再让目标配体竞争——论文在 DNA gyrase 上就是这么把别构抑制剂逼回正确位点的(加入 gepotidacin 封 NC 位点、sparfloxacin 封 FQ 位点)。**(5) de novo 设计**:用 **STONED 算法**在前 5 名 FQ 周围的化学空间自动生成数千个分子,选出 414 个,逐个与排名最高的 FQ(STF)做竞争对接。**(6) 模型与算力**:AF3 源码 2024-11-14 从 GitHub 取得、参数直接向 Google 申请;Boltz-1/2 从官方 GitHub 本地安装,也测了施加物理势的 steering 版本;推理用 NVIDIA RTX 4080(16 GB)、RTX 3090(24 GB)或 A40(48 GB),序列与模板检索用 11 代 Intel 8 核 CPU + 128 GB 内存;为省时间,每个系统的多序列比对预先生成,DNA、离子、化合物等非蛋白组分在推理前就并入。结构相似度用 RDKit 的 ECFP4 指纹算 Tanimoto 系数,位姿合理性用 PoseBusters 检验,统计上用 c-index(秩一致性)、Pearson r 与 Spearman 检验。
<!-- EN --> **(1) Pose criteria**: each ligand is inferred with **at least five random seeds**, each inference generating **at least 25 models**; predicted proteins are superimposed on the reference crystal structure (PyMOL's `super`), then two quantities are read off — the ligand's distance from the reference binding-site centre, and the mean RMSD among the predicted poses (pose convergence). The empirical thresholds for true inhibitors are distance < 5.0 Å and convergence < 2.0 Å. **(2) Pairwise competitive docking**: the protein model is given to the co-folding model together with **two competing ligands**, and whichever occupies the active site wins that run; each ligand pair gets at least n independent runs, and the win rate as a percentage is the CDS. All pairwise outcomes are compiled into a **pairwise matrix** and ligands ranked by cumulative CDS. **(3) All-at-Once (a cheaper simplification)**: instead of pairwise duels, a whole inhibitor class competes within a single inference. For virtual screening the 3,155-compound library was randomly split into **124 sets** (109 of 25 compounds, 15 of 26 — the set size dictated by GPU memory), one All-at-Once run each. **(4) Handling multi-site systems**: where a protein has several pockets, the other sites can be **blocked with known ligands** before letting the target ligand compete — which is how the paper forced allosteric inhibitors back to their correct site on DNA gyrase (gepotidacin blocking the NC site, sparfloxacin the FQ site). **(5) De novo design**: the **STONED algorithm** generated several thousand molecules in the chemical space around the top five FQs; 414 were selected and each competitively docked against the top-ranked FQ (STF). **(6) Models and compute**: the AF3 source was retrieved from GitHub on 14 Nov 2024 with parameters obtained directly from Google; Boltz-1/2 were installed locally from their official GitHub, including the steering versions that apply physics-based potentials. Inference ran on NVIDIA RTX 4080 (16 GB), RTX 3090 (24 GB) or A40 (48 GB), with sequence and template searches on an 11th-gen 8-core Intel CPU with 128 GB RAM; to save time, multiple sequence alignments were pre-generated per system and non-protein components (DNA, ions, compounds) incorporated before inference. Structural similarity used Tanimoto coefficients on RDKit ECFP4 fingerprints, pose plausibility was checked with PoseBusters, and statistics used the c-index (rank concordance), Pearson r and a Spearman test.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **基准蛋白**:16 个功能各异的靶点,外加结构更复杂的多位点系统 Mycobacterium tuberculosis(Mtb)DNA gyrase,合计 17 个系统、19 个结合位点。文中点名的靶点包括激酶 CDK2 与 TYK2、蛋白酶 thrombin、水解酶 PDE2 与 BACE1、驱动蛋白 KIF11/EG5、磷酸酶 PTN11 与 PTP1B、氧化还原酶 DHFR 与 COX-1/COX-2、BCL-2 样蛋白 MCL1、GPCR HCAR3 与 FFA2R、凝集素 GAL3、PAS 结构域 EPAS1,以及后来追加的两个近期才解出配体结合态 cryo-EM 结构的 GPCR 系统 GPER1 与 SLC6A3。**对照分子**:每个基准靶点配 **28 个无关的"off-target"化合物**——由其他靶点各取一个抑制剂、再加上一些结合完全无关蛋白的化合物组成。**DNA gyrase 的三个位点**:FQ 位点(氟喹诺酮)、NC 位点(non-catalytic,NBTI 结合,距 FQ 位点约 10 Å,位于两个 DNA 断裂键之间)、别构位点(噻吩类抑制剂,距 FQ 位点约 30 Å)。**排序评测用的实验数据**:来自文献与 Binding Database;另外作者**自己测了** 22 个 FQ 抑制大肠杆菌生长的 IC50。**筛选库**:3,155 个 FDA 已批准分子,其中 46 个是 FQ(占 1.5%)。**抗性变体**:4 个已知使 IC50 或 MIC 升高数倍的 DNA gyrase FQ 抗性变体。
<!-- EN --> **Benchmark proteins**: 16 functionally diverse targets plus the structurally more complex multi-site system Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Mtb) DNA gyrase — 17 systems and 19 binding sites in total. Named targets include the kinases CDK2 and TYK2, the protease thrombin, the hydrolases PDE2 and BACE1, the kinesin KIF11/EG5, the phosphatases PTN11 and PTP1B, the oxidoreductases DHFR and COX-1/COX-2, the BCL-2-like protein MCL1, the GPCRs HCAR3 and FFA2R, the lectin GAL3, the PAS-domain EPAS1, and two later additions whose ligand-bound cryo-EM structures were only solved recently, GPER1 and SLC6A3. **Control molecules**: each benchmark target gets **28 unrelated "off-target" compounds** — one inhibitor from each of the other targets plus compounds binding proteins entirely unrelated to those studied. **DNA gyrase's three sites**: the FQ site (fluoroquinolones), the NC (non-catalytic) site where NBTIs bind, ~10 Å from the FQ site and between the two scissile DNA bonds, and the allosteric site of thiophene-class inhibitors, ~30 Å from the FQ site. **Experimental data for ranking**: from the literature and the Binding Database; in addition the authors **measured themselves** the IC50 values of 22 FQs inhibiting E. coli growth. **Screening library**: 3,155 FDA-approved molecules, of which 46 are FQs (1.5%). **Resistance variants**: four known DNA gyrase FQ-resistance variants that raise IC50 or MIC several-fold.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **位姿收敛性能分真假 binder**:在 16 个基准上,真抑制剂一般结合在口袋约 5 Å 内且收敛性通常 < 2 Å,off-target 分子位置更远、变化更大。**位姿收敛这个判据总体上优于 Boltz-2 的 binding-likelihood 预测**。特异性最强的是 CDK2、TYK2、EPAS1、PDE2、PTP1B、PTN11、DHFR、GAL3、MCL1;最弱的是 GPCR FFA2R 与 HCAR3、水解酶 BACE1,其次是 COX-1——这些系统里一些 off-target 分子仍然落在口袋附近且 RMSD 很低。 <!-- EN --> **Pose convergence separates true from false binders**: across the 16 benchmarks true inhibitors generally bind within ~5 Å of the pocket with convergence typically below 2 Å, while off-target molecules sit further away and vary far more. **Pose convergence generally outperformed Boltz-2's binding-likelihood prediction.** Specificity was strongest for CDK2, TYK2, EPAS1, PDE2, PTP1B, PTN11, DHFR, GAL3 and MCL1; weakest for the GPCRs FFA2R and HCAR3 and the hydrolase BACE1, then COX-1 — systems where some off-target molecules still landed near the pocket with low RMSD.
- <!-- ZH --> **多位点系统(DNA gyrase)**:FQ 平均落在已知位点 2 Å 以内;非 FQ 的 spiropyrimidinetrione 类(QPT-1、zoliflodacin)也一样,因为它们本来就作用在 FQ 位点;NBTI 稳定落在其位点 2 Å 内,**AF3 与 Boltz-1 都做到了,Boltz-2 没有**。但噻吩类**别构**抑制剂在三种模型下都很少被对接到正确位点,多数被错放到 FQ 或 NC 位点——**除非先用已知配体把那两个位点占住**,这时它们就守在正确位点。论文还指出这一现象在另一个 20 对正位/别构配体、17 个蛋白的独立数据集上也被报道过。 <!-- EN --> **A multi-site system (DNA gyrase)**: FQs clustered within a mean 2 Å of their known site, as did the non-FQ spiropyrimidinetriones (QPT-1, zoliflodacin), which act at the FQ site anyway; NBTIs sat consistently within 2 Å of their site — **with AF3 and Boltz-1 but not Boltz-2**. The thiophene-class **allosteric** inhibitors, however, were rarely docked at their correct site by any of the models, being mislocalized to the FQ or NC sites — **unless those two sites were first blocked by known ligands**, after which they stayed put. The paper notes this has also been reported on an independent dataset of 20 orthosteric/allosteric ligand pairs across 17 proteins.
- <!-- ZH --> **CDS 排序与实验活性的一致性(AF3)**:强相关的有 GAL3(c = 0.89)、TYK2(0.87)、thrombin(0.86)、CDK2(0.78)、HCAR3(0.77)、KIF11(0.76)、DNA gyrase 别构位点(0.76)、PTN11(0.75)、PTP1B(0.74)、DNA gyrase FQ 位点(0.72);中等的有 FFA2R(0.66)、EPAS1(0.67)、MCL1(0.68)、PDE2(0.67)、DHFR(0.68)、DNA gyrase NC 位点(0.62)、BACE1(0.62)、COX-2(0.67);**COX-1 只有 0.52,等于没有相关性**。注意这两个最差的系统(COX-1/COX-2)本来位姿收敛性就差,说明问题出在"配体根本没被可靠地放进正确口袋",而不是竞争机制本身。用 Boltz-1/2 替代 AF3 做竞争对接,相关性普遍更弱,COX-1、COX-2、PDE2、PTP1B、TYK2 上**没有显著相关**。 <!-- EN --> **CDS rankings vs experimental activity (AF3)**: strong agreement for GAL3 (c = 0.89), TYK2 (0.87), thrombin (0.86), CDK2 (0.78), HCAR3 (0.77), KIF11 (0.76), the DNA gyrase allosteric site (0.76), PTN11 (0.75), PTP1B (0.74) and the DNA gyrase FQ site (0.72); moderate for FFA2R (0.66), EPAS1 (0.67), MCL1 (0.68), PDE2 (0.67), DHFR (0.68), the DNA gyrase NC site (0.62), BACE1 (0.62) and COX-2 (0.67); **COX-1 only 0.52, i.e. no correlation**. Notably the two worst systems (COX-1/COX-2) also had poor pose convergence, so the failure lies in the ligands never being reliably placed in the right pocket rather than in the competition mechanism. Substituting Boltz-1/2 for AF3 generally weakened correlations, with **no significant correlation** for COX-1, COX-2, PDE2, PTP1B and TYK2.
- <!-- ZH --> **与 Boltz-2 亲和力预测模块的对比**:在 19 个系统上两者总体相当,只有细微差别——Boltz-2 在 BACE1、DHFR、EPAS1、FFA2R、PTP1B、PDE2 上略好,竞争对接在 DNA gyrase 别构位点、CDK2、GAL3、HCAR3、PTN11、TYK2、COX-2 上略好。**在两者都表现差的系统(DNA gyrase NC 位点、COX-1、COX-2)上,竞争对接仍然更好**——这是它作为"直接亲和力预测器失效时的替代方案"的价值所在。 <!-- EN --> **Versus Boltz-2's affinity module**: comparable overall across the 19 systems with only minor differences — Boltz-2 slightly better on BACE1, DHFR, EPAS1, FFA2R, PTP1B and PDE2; competitive docking slightly better on the DNA gyrase allosteric site, CDK2, GAL3, HCAR3, PTN11, TYK2 and COX-2. **Where both performed poorly (the DNA gyrase NC site, COX-1, COX-2) competitive docking still came out ahead** — the basis of its value as a fallback when direct AI affinity predictors fail.
- <!-- ZH --> **分不开高度同源的口袋**:COX-1 与 COX-2 抑制剂的 CDS 排序几乎相同(Pearson r = 0.96),说明 AF3 无法可靠区分这两个环氧化酶同工酶,原因很可能是它们结合位点高度保守。同理,4 个 FQ 抗性变体的位姿特异性与 FQ 排序相对野生型**只有微小变化**——"换一两个催化位点的氨基酸不足以显著改变 AF3 的结合预测",这与另一项关于 co-folding 方法无法响应刻意扰动的研究一致。大肠杆菌里换用第二靶点拓扑异构酶 IV 也无法细化排序,与用 DNA gyrase 得到的排序几乎相同(r = 0.99)。 <!-- EN --> **Highly homologous pockets are indistinguishable**: CDS rankings for COX-1 and COX-2 inhibitors were nearly identical (Pearson r = 0.96), so AF3 cannot reliably separate the two cyclooxygenase isoenzymes, most likely because their binding sites are highly conserved. Likewise the four FQ-resistance variants shifted pose specificity and FQ rankings only **marginally** versus wild type — substituting one or two catalytic-site residues is not enough to change AF3's binding predictions, consistent with another study showing co-folding methods cannot account for intentional perturbations. In E. coli, switching to the secondary target topoisomerase IV did not refine rankings either, giving nearly the same order as DNA gyrase (r = 0.99).
- <!-- ZH --> **自测实验数据的验证**:22 个 FQ 抑制大肠杆菌生长的 IC50 与 CDS 排序整体相关性一般,但趋势清楚——IC50 低的 FQ 普遍 CDS 排名高;剔除两个预测水溶性差的 FQ 后相关性显著改善(FQ 本来就以水溶性差著称)。 <!-- EN --> **Validation against their own measurements**: the 22 FQs' E. coli growth-inhibition IC50 values correlated modestly with CDS rankings overall but with a clear trend — lower-IC50 FQs generally rank higher; excluding two FQs predicted to be poorly soluble improved the correlation significantly (FQs are known for limited aqueous solubility).
- <!-- ZH --> **All-at-Once 用于虚拟筛选**:在 3,155 个 FDA 药物(含 46 个 FQ,占 1.5%)的库上,挑出 **147 个**排名最高的化合物,其中 **38 个是 FQ**,即 **25.9% 富集**;没被选中的 8 个 FQ 已知对 Mtb DNA gyrase 抑制活性低,且多属第一代 FQ。再叠加位姿过滤(收敛性 2.5 Å、距结合位点 2.0 Å)后,剩余 45 个化合物中 FQ 富集到 **77.8%**;把阈值收紧到 1.0 Å 进一步提高到 **93.5%,富集因子 62**。与 Boltz-2 在同一库上的 hit 识别表现接近。计算上的意义是:3,115 个化合物不必逐个做非竞争对接,只有 All-at-Once 选出的 147 个需要进一步分析。 <!-- EN --> **All-at-Once for virtual screening**: on the 3,155 FDA-approved library (46 FQs, 1.5%), **147** top-ranking compounds were selected, **38 of them FQs** — a **25.9% enrichment**; the eight FQs not selected are known to have low activity against Mtb DNA gyrase and mostly belong to the first generation. Adding pose filtering (convergence 2.5 Å, distance 2.0 Å) raised FQ enrichment to **77.8%** among the 45 remaining compounds, and tightening both thresholds to 1.0 Å to **93.5%, with an enrichment factor of 62**. This closely matched Boltz-2's hit-identification performance on the same library. The computational point: instead of docking all 3,115 compounds individually in non-competitive mode, only the 147 selected by All-at-Once needed further analysis.
- <!-- ZH --> **All-at-Once 不如两两对战精细**:整类抑制剂一次竞争时结果较粗——一些强效抑制剂没出现在领先者里,真正占住口袋的化合物数量太少,不足以做可靠的比较分析。它擅长的是"把强 FQ 从弱 FQ 和非 FQ 里挑出来",不适合做精细排序。 <!-- EN --> **All-at-Once is coarser than pairwise**: competing a whole inhibitor class at once gave less detailed results — several potent inhibitors did not appear among the leaders, and too few compounds effectively occupied the site for reliable comparative analysis. It is good at separating strong FQs from weak FQs and non-FQs, not at fine-grained ranking.
- <!-- ZH --> **de novo 设计(概念验证)**:414 个 STONED 生成的分子中,**31 个**在 100 个生成模型里有至少 70% 占住 FQ 位点,即预测结合能力强于当前最佳的 STF;它们与 STF 的 Tanimoto 相似度在 0.25–0.88 之间。由于这些分子都不在 CAS 数据库里(即很可能从未被合成),作者又按预测 ADME 性质过滤,最终只有 **8 个**同时具备良好类药性、溶解度与合成可及性。这些分子尚未合成或实验验证。 <!-- EN --> **De novo design (proof of concept)**: of 414 STONED-generated molecules, **31** occupied the FQ site in at least 70% of 100 generated models — i.e. predicted to bind more strongly than the current best, STF — with Tanimoto similarity to STF between 0.25 and 0.88. Since none appear in the CAS database (so they have likely never been synthesized), the authors filtered by predicted ADME properties, leaving only **8** with favourable drug-likeness, solubility and synthetic accessibility. These remain unsynthesized and experimentally unvalidated.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 用"**多种子下的位姿收敛性**"作为区分真假 binder 的判据,比模型自带的 binding-likelihood 更可靠——一个几乎零成本、只需多跑几个种子就能得到的信号。 <!-- EN --> Uses **pose convergence across seeds** as the criterion separating true from false binders, more reliable than the models' own binding-likelihood — a near-free signal obtained just by running more seeds.
- <!-- ZH --> 把"**让两个配体在同一个位点竞争**"变成排序方法:作者认为用机器学习工具模拟配体间的虚拟竞争是新策略(以往的竞争对接多用于在大口袋里并排放多个配体,或用于基于片段的设计)。它的可解释性很好——赢家就是占住位点的那个,不依赖外部打分函数。 <!-- EN --> Turns **letting two ligands compete for the same site** into a ranking method: the authors consider simulating virtual competition between ligands with an ML tool a novel strategy (earlier competitive docking mostly placed multiple ligands side by side in large pockets, or served fragment-based design). It is interpretable — the winner is whoever occupies the site — and needs no external scoring function.
- <!-- ZH --> **封位点的技巧**:在多位点系统里先用已知配体占住其他口袋,能把别构抑制剂逼回正确位点——这是一个可直接复用的实用操作。 <!-- EN --> **The site-blocking trick**: in multi-site systems, occupying the other pockets with known ligands forces allosteric inhibitors back to their correct site — a directly reusable practical move.
- <!-- ZH --> All-at-Once 给出了"**两步筛选**"的成本结构:先用一次多分子竞争把 3,155 个压到 147 个,再只对这 147 个做位姿精算,最终富集 93.5%。 <!-- EN --> All-at-Once gives a **two-step screening** cost structure: one multi-ligand competition cuts 3,155 compounds to 147, then pose analysis runs on those 147 alone, ending at 93.5% enrichment.
- <!-- ZH --> 诚实地把方法的**系统依赖性**作为结论的一部分报告出来(c 从 0.52 到 0.89),而不是只报平均值。 <!-- EN --> Honestly reports the method's **system dependence** as part of the conclusion (c from 0.52 to 0.89) rather than only an average.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **不普适,必须逐案评估**。作者自己把这条写成结论:方法表现强烈依赖具体蛋白系统、抑制剂类别、分子大小与复杂度。根本原因是 co-folding 模型的蛋白-配体预测**严重受训练数据中位姿记忆的影响**,对训练集里没有出现过的配体或结合环境预期表现很差;作者用两个近期才解出配体结合结构的 GPCR 系统(GPER1、SLC6A3)做了直接检验,结果相关性都弱,反倒是 Boltz-2 的 IC50 预测更好(c 约 0.6–0.7)。 <!-- EN --> **Not generalizable; must be assessed case by case.** The authors make this a conclusion: performance depends strongly on the protein system, inhibitor class, and molecular size and complexity. The root cause is that co-folding models' protein–ligand predictions are **heavily influenced by pose memorization from training data**, so performance is expected to be poor for ligands or binding environments absent from the training set. They tested this directly on two GPCR systems whose ligand-bound structures were solved recently (GPER1, SLC6A3): correlations were weak for both, while Boltz-2's IC50 predictions did better (c ≈ 0.6–0.7).
- <!-- ZH --> **大分子会崩**。深度学习 co-folding 模型容易产生非物理的瑕疵(原子碰撞、键被拉长、手性违背),所以方法在大或复杂的配体上往往失败:HIV-2 蛋白酶(HIV2P)与 tankyrase 2(TNKS2)两个系统就因为竞争抑制剂太大、碰撞过多而**无法可靠评测**。 <!-- EN --> **Large ligands break it.** Deep-learning co-folding models are prone to non-physical artefacts (steric clashes, stretched bonds, chirality violations), so the method tends to fail for large or complex ligands: two additional systems, HIV-2 protease (HIV2P) and tankyrase 2 (TNKS2), **could not be reliably evaluated** because the large competing inhibitors clashed extensively.
- <!-- ZH --> **配体-配体相互作用是一个干扰项**:在能同时容纳两个竞争分子的大口袋里,或在像 DNA gyrase 这样的多位点系统里,胜负可能由配体之间的相互作用而非配体-蛋白相互作用决定。文中一个具体例子:两个 FQ 一起对接时只有一个占住催化 FQ 位点,另一个常常贴在第二个催化位点的 DNA 上(而那个位点在所用模型里只被部分表示)。 <!-- EN --> **Ligand–ligand interaction is a confound**: in large pockets that can hold both competitors, or in multi-site systems like DNA gyrase, the outcome may be decided by ligand–ligand rather than ligand–protein interactions. A concrete case from the paper: when two FQs were docked together only one occupied the catalytic FQ site while the other often stacked against the DNA at the second catalytic site — which is only partially represented in the model used.
- <!-- ZH --> **成本随配体数平方增长**:构建完整的两两竞争矩阵是 O(n²) 次推理,这直接限制了可排序的配体数量;All-at-Once 虽便宜但明显更粗糙,而且分组大小被 GPU 显存卡住(25–26 个)。 <!-- EN --> **Cost scales quadratically**: building a full pairwise competitive matrix is O(n²) inferences, directly limiting how many ligands can be ranked; All-at-Once is cheaper but clearly coarser, and its set size is capped by GPU memory (25–26 compounds).
- <!-- ZH --> **分不开高度保守的同源口袋**(COX-1 vs COX-2,r = 0.96),也**感知不到抗性突变**(改 1–2 个催化位点残基几乎不改变预测)——这两点对"按靶点选择性做药"和"预测抗性"这两类实际需求都是硬伤。 <!-- EN --> **Cannot separate highly conserved homologous pockets** (COX-1 vs COX-2, r = 0.96) and **cannot sense resistance mutations** (changing 1–2 catalytic-site residues barely alters predictions) — both fatal for the practical needs of target selectivity and resistance prediction.
- <!-- ZH --> **de novo 设计那部分只是概念验证**:8 个候选分子都未合成、未做实验验证,作者明确说这有待后续研究。AF3 的使用还依赖向 Google 申请模型参数,复现性上有外部依赖。 <!-- EN --> **The de novo design is only a proof of concept**: all eight candidates are unsynthesized and unvalidated, which the authors explicitly leave to future work. Using AF3 also depends on obtaining model parameters from Google, an external dependency for reproducibility.
- <!-- ZH --> **与 Boltz-2 的对比样本太小**:作者自己说"更稳健的比较需要在更多系统上做"。Steering 版 Boltz 虽然消除了原子碰撞,但竞争对接表现并没有显著改善;RoseTTAFold All-Atom、Chai、Protenix、NeuralPlexer 等其他模型都还没评测。 <!-- EN --> **The comparison against Boltz-2 rests on too few systems**: the authors say a more robust comparison needs more of them. The steering Boltz versions removed atom clashes without significantly improving competitive-docking performance, and other models — RoseTTAFold All-Atom, Chai, Protenix, NeuralPlexer — remain unevaluated.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是 Sikander 给 **"Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening"**(Hayat Lab 的硕士/HiWi 课题:LLM agent + 分子基础模型 + docking + active learning,给定蛋白结构自主规划、执行、改进筛选,应用方向心脏疾病)指定的四篇阅读材料之一,在 pipeline 里的位置是**最末端的精排与判真层**:`drugclip-science-2026` 从几亿分子里秒级取候选 → `adaptiveflow-natbiotech-2026` 的 ATG-VS 做大规模对接 → 本文的 AF3 竞争对接给最后几十个候选排序并剔除假阳性。**(1) 对 agent 最有用的两件东西**:**位姿收敛性**是一个几乎免费的"**自我一致性检验**"——多跑几个种子、看预测彼此是否一致,这正是 agent 判断"这个工具的这次输出该不该信"所缺的信号,而且它在论文里**优于模型自带的 binding-likelihood**;**CDS 的胜率**天然是一个有界、可解释、可累积的量,比裸的打分值更适合做 agent 的决策依据。**(2) agent 要做的决策**:什么时候从便宜的检索/对接升级到昂贵的 co-folding(论文的两步法已经给出成本结构:3,155 → 147 → 位姿精算);排序用两两对战(贵、精细)还是 All-at-Once(便宜、粗);多位点系统里要不要先封位点、用哪个已知配体去封;位姿过滤的阈值取多紧(论文给了 2.5 Å/2.0 Å 与 1.0 Å 两档,富集从 77.8% 到 93.5%,但剩下的化合物数会变少——典型的精度/召回权衡,正是 agent 该算的账)。**(3) 一个合理的第一个里程碑**:**先把这一层做成"验真器"而不是排序器**。用论文公开的 Zenodo 数据和 3,155 个 FDA 库复现 All-at-Once 的两步筛选,把"多种子位姿收敛性"包成一个独立工具,然后在前面任意一层(DrugCLIP 的 top-k 或 ATG-VS 的 top hits)后面接上它,量化它剔除了多少假阳性。评测指标直接用富集因子,ground truth 是已知的 FQ 标注,**完全不需要湿实验**。**(4) 这篇对"agent 可信度"这条线的意义最大**:论文给出的是一个**外部的、与模型自身打分无关的**可靠性判据(位姿收敛 + 竞争胜率),而且明确展示了模型自评(Boltz-2 binding likelihood)不如这个外部判据——这与我们在 agent 方向上主张"要用外部 verifier 取代模型自评"是同一个论点,只是落在分子层面。**(5) 必须当心的坑**:(a) **记忆而非物理**——co-folding 的表现靠训练集里的相似位姿,所以 agent 在新颖骨架或新口袋上会系统性高估自己,这里**没有**校准过的不确定性可用,GPER1/SLC6A3 的失败就是警示;(b) **分不开同源口袋**意味着这一层**不能用来做选择性判断**(心脏靶点里同家族成员很多,这一点很要紧);(c) **抗性突变不敏感**,别指望用它预测耐药;(d) O(n²) 的成本必须由 agent 显式预算,不能让它自由地两两对战;(e) 大配体会因碰撞直接失效,agent 需要一个前置的分子大小/复杂度检查来决定这条路走不走。
<!-- EN --> This is one of the four papers Sikander assigned for **"Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening"** (a Hayat Lab Master's/HiWi topic: LLM agents + molecular foundation models + docking + active learning, autonomously planning, executing and improving screening campaigns from protein structures, applied to heart disease). Its place in the pipeline is the **final re-ranking and truth-checking layer**: `drugclip-science-2026` retrieves candidates from hundreds of millions in seconds → ATG-VS from `adaptiveflow-natbiotech-2026` docks at scale → this paper's AF3 competitive docking ranks the last few dozen and discards false positives. **(1) The two most useful things for an agent**: **pose convergence** is a nearly free **self-consistency check** — run more seeds, see whether the predictions agree — exactly the signal an agent lacks when judging whether to trust a tool's output, and in this paper it **beats the model's own binding-likelihood**; and **the CDS win rate** is naturally bounded, interpretable and accumulable, better suited as an agent's decision variable than a raw score. **(2) The decisions for an agent**: when to escalate from cheap retrieval/docking to expensive co-folding (the paper's two-step method already provides the cost structure: 3,155 → 147 → pose analysis); whether to rank by pairwise duels (expensive, fine-grained) or All-at-Once (cheap, coarse); whether to block sites first in a multi-site system, and with which known ligand; how tight to set pose filters (the paper gives 2.5 Å/2.0 Å and 1.0 Å, with enrichment from 77.8% to 93.5% but fewer surviving compounds — a precision/recall trade-off that is exactly the agent's accounting). **(3) A sensible first milestone**: **build this layer as a verifier rather than a ranker.** Reproduce the two-step All-at-Once screen using the paper's public Zenodo data and the 3,155-compound FDA library, wrap "multi-seed pose convergence" as a standalone tool, then attach it after any upstream layer (DrugCLIP's top-k or ATG-VS's top hits) and quantify how many false positives it removes. Score it by enrichment factor against the known FQ labels as ground truth — **no wet lab needed at all**. **(4) This paper matters most for the agent-trustworthiness line**: it supplies an **external reliability criterion independent of the model's own score** (pose convergence plus competition win rate), and explicitly shows the model's self-assessment (Boltz-2 binding likelihood) is worse than that external criterion — the same argument we make for replacing model self-scoring with an external verifier, here landed at the molecular level. **(5) Pitfalls to watch**: (a) **memorization, not physics** — co-folding performance rests on similar poses in the training set, so an agent will systematically overestimate itself on novel scaffolds or new pockets, with **no** calibrated uncertainty available; the GPER1/SLC6A3 failures are the warning; (b) **homologous pockets are indistinguishable**, so this layer **cannot be used for selectivity judgements** (which matters, since cardiac targets have many same-family members); (c) **resistance mutations are invisible to it**, so do not expect resistance prediction; (d) the O(n²) cost must be explicitly budgeted by the agent rather than left to free-running pairwise duels; (e) large ligands fail outright through clashes, so the agent needs an upfront molecular size/complexity check to decide whether this route is viable at all.
