## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> BioML-bench 把 MLE-bench 的「任务 capsule + 自动评分」范式搬到生物医学:24 个端到端 ML 任务(药物发现、医学影像、蛋白工程、单细胞),agent 需自己建 pipeline、训模型、提交预测,再映射到**以领域专家为主的公开排行榜**百分位。四个 agent(Biomni、STELLA、AIDE、MLAgentBench)平均只到 **34–39 百分位**,且**生物医学专用 agent 并无一致优势**。
<!-- EN --> BioML-bench ports MLE-bench's "task capsule + automatic grading" paradigm to biomedicine: 24 end-to-end ML tasks (drug discovery, biomedical imaging, protein engineering, single-cell) where the agent builds the pipeline, trains models and submits predictions, scored as percentiles on **largely expert-populated public leaderboards**. Four agents (Biomni, STELLA, AIDE, MLAgentBench) average only the **34th–39th percentile**, and **biomedical specialization confers no consistent advantage**.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 现有生物医学 agent 的评估多是问答(如 LAB-Bench)或窄域生信任务;没有基准检验 agent 能否完成治疗开发中核心的**有监督 ML 挑战**(扰动响应建模、毒性预测等)的完整流程。MLE-bench 证明了端到端可执行评估可行,但其 Kaggle 任务的排行榜由众包而非领域专家构成;BixBench 主要是文本答案、不涉及监督学习。
<!-- EN --> Biomedical agents have mostly been evaluated on QA (e.g. LAB-Bench) or narrow bioinformatics; no benchmark tests whether agents can complete the **supervised ML challenges** central to therapeutics (perturbation response, toxicity prediction) end to end. MLE-bench showed executable end-to-end evaluation is feasible, but its Kaggle leaderboards are crowd-sourced rather than expert-populated; BixBench is mainly text answers without supervised learning.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> 框架改自 MLE-bench,增加生物医学任务域、新 agent、新指标与 h5ad 等生信文件格式支持,并提供云部署配置与 mkdocs 文档。每个 task capsule 给出结构化任务描述、训练数据、测试特征和示例提交文件;agent 在 Docker(预建 conda 环境)中运行,提交结果自动校验并用任务原始指标(AUROC、Spearman 等)打分。**Agent**:Biomni v0.0.5(Claude Sonnet 4)、STELLA(OpenRouter 默认模型)、AIDE v6.3.3 与 MLAgentBench(均 GPT-4.1),另有输出无信息预测的 Dummy。每个 agent–任务组合 **4 次独立重复**。**指标**:排行榜百分位、跨 agent 平均排名、超过人类中位数比例、获奖牌比例(沿用 MLE-bench 定义)、完成率;分 **penalized**(失败记 0 百分位)与 **non-penalized**(失败剔除)两种口径。**算力**:影像任务单张 NVIDIA L4(16 vCPU、64 GB)限 16 小时;其他任务纯 CPU 限 8 小时。
<!-- EN --> Adapted from MLE-bench, adding biomedical domains, new agents and metrics, bioinformatics formats such as h5ad, cloud deployment configs and mkdocs documentation. Each task capsule gives a structured description, training data, test features and a sample submission; agents run in Docker with a pre-built conda environment, and submissions are auto-validated and scored with the task's original metric (AUROC, Spearman, …). **Agents**: Biomni v0.0.5 (Claude Sonnet 4), STELLA (OpenRouter default), AIDE v6.3.3 and MLAgentBench (both GPT-4.1), plus a Dummy agent with uninformative predictions. **Four independent replicates** per agent–task. **Metrics**: leaderboard percentile, mean rank across agents, % above human median, % any medal (MLE-bench definition), completion rate; reported **penalized** (failed run = 0th percentile) and **non-penalized** (failed runs dropped). **Compute**: imaging tasks on one NVIDIA L4 (16 vCPU, 64 GB) with 16 h limit; other tasks CPU-only with 8 h limit.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 共 24 个任务:**药物发现 9 个**(PolarisHub / TDC:EGFR 抑制剂分类、肝微粒体清除率、血浆蛋白结合、溶解度、CYP2D6、logP、hERG、BBB、Caco-2);**医学影像 4 个**(Kaggle:OSIC 肺纤维化 FVC 预测、PatchCamelyon 组织病理转移检测、RSNA 脑瘤 MGMT 甲基化、UW 消化道分割);**蛋白工程 6 个**(ProteinGym:SARS-CoV-2 spike RBD、Tsuboyama 2023 稳定性、indel 等);**单细胞 5 个**(OpenProblems:CITE-seq 模态预测、PBMC 小分子扰动响应、**跨糖尿病肾病(DKD)数据集的细胞类型 label projection**、Slide-seqV2 空间可变基因、乳腺癌细胞通讯)。
<!-- EN --> 24 tasks: **drug discovery 9** (PolarisHub / TDC: EGFR inhibitor classification, liver microsomal clearance, plasma protein binding, solubility, CYP2D6, logP, hERG, BBB, Caco-2); **biomedical imaging 4** (Kaggle: OSIC pulmonary fibrosis FVC, PatchCamelyon histopathology metastasis, RSNA brain-tumour MGMT methylation, UW GI-tract segmentation); **protein engineering 6** (ProteinGym: SARS-CoV-2 spike RBD, Tsuboyama 2023 stability, indels, …); **single-cell 5** (OpenProblems: CITE-seq modality prediction, PBMC small-molecule perturbation response, **cell-type label projection across diabetic kidney disease (DKD) datasets**, Slide-seqV2 spatially variable genes, breast-cancer cell–cell communication).

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **Penalized 总体**:Biomni 排行榜百分位 **34.45 ± 5.36**(最佳,平均排名 1.88,完成率 100%)、STELLA 31.84 ± 5.82(完成率 91.7%)、AIDE 28.50 ± 5.22(完成率 80.2%)、MLAgentBench 21.43 ± 4.40、Dummy 2.50。**Non-penalized**:STELLA 39.45 ± 6.52 反超,AIDE 37.41 ± 5.85,Biomni 34.45;三者差距大多在 SEM 之内。超过人类中位数比例最高约 34–43%,获奖牌比例 ≤ 21%。**按领域(penalized)**:单细胞 Biomni 55.62 最强(STELLA 54.23、AIDE 53.17 紧随);影像 MLAgentBench 21.73 最佳;蛋白与药物 Biomni 领先;去掉惩罚后 STELLA 在影像大幅领先、AIDE 在蛋白领先、Biomni 在药物领先。**策略**:94 个超中位数的运行显示,强 agent 更常尝试多样的特征工程与模型类型;AIDE 在 Tsuboyama 2023 上用 BLOSUM、保守性、理化特征拿到 **100 百分位**,但同一 agent 同一任务另一次重跑只有 **54 百分位** —— 策略在重复运行间并不稳定。**架构**:Biomni 和 MLAgentBench 约 50% 用随机森林,AIDE 常用堆叠/梯度提升;**深度学习几乎从未被选为最终模型,即便是影像任务**,而人类排行榜以 DL 为主。**失败**:41 次失败运行主要源于脚手架问题 —— 资源耗尽(复制大图像)、静默错误(try–except 回退导致脚本跑完却没存预测)、卡在无谓的环境安装、框架未捕获异常、超时。
<!-- EN --> **Penalized overall**: Biomni leaderboard percentile **34.45 ± 5.36** (best; mean rank 1.88; 100% completion), STELLA 31.84 ± 5.82 (91.7% completion), AIDE 28.50 ± 5.22 (80.2%), MLAgentBench 21.43 ± 4.40, Dummy 2.50. **Non-penalized**: STELLA overtakes at 39.45 ± 6.52, AIDE 37.41 ± 5.85, Biomni 34.45 — differences mostly within SEM. Best above-median rates ~34–43%; any-medal ≤ 21%. **By domain (penalized)**: single-cell — Biomni 55.62 (STELLA 54.23, AIDE 53.17 close); imaging — MLAgentBench best at 21.73; protein and drug — Biomni leads; without penalties STELLA leads imaging by a wide margin, AIDE leads protein, Biomni leads drug discovery. **Strategies**: across 94 above-median runs, strong agents more often tried diverse feature engineering and model types; AIDE hit the **100th percentile** on Tsuboyama 2023 with BLOSUM, conservation and physicochemical features, yet another replicate of the same agent on the same task reached only the **54th** — strategies are not stable across reruns. **Architectures**: Biomni and MLAgentBench used random forests ~50% of the time, AIDE stacked/gradient-boosting models; **deep learning was rarely tried and almost never the final model, even on imaging**, unlike DL-dominated human leaderboards. **Failures**: 41 failed runs were mostly scaffolding issues — resource exhaustion (copying large images), silent errors (try–except fallbacks: scripts ran but saved no predictions), stalls on unnecessary environment creation, uncaught framework exceptions, timeouts.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 第一个跨四个生物医学领域的**端到端可执行 ML** agent 基准,以专家主导的排行榜为人类基线。 <!-- EN --> The first **end-to-end executable ML** agent benchmark across four biomedical domains, with expert-dominated leaderboards as the human baseline.
- <!-- ZH --> 同时报告 penalized / non-penalized 两种口径,把「能力」与「脚手架可靠性」拆开。 <!-- EN --> Reports both penalized and non-penalized views, separating capability from scaffolding reliability.
- <!-- ZH --> 对成功运行的策略、选用架构和失败运行的原因做了人工日志分析。 <!-- EN --> Manual log analysis of strategies in successful runs, chosen architectures and causes of failed runs.
- <!-- ZH --> pip 可安装、带文档的开源包,降低复现门槛。 <!-- EN --> A pip-installable, documented open-source package lowering the reproduction barrier.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 每个 agent 用各自默认 LLM(GPT-4.1 / Claude Sonnet 4 / OpenRouter 默认),**agent 设计与底座模型混淆**;作者自认需系统地变换 LLM。 <!-- EN --> Each agent uses its own default LLM (GPT-4.1 / Claude Sonnet 4 / OpenRouter default), **confounding agent design with base model**; the authors concede LLMs should be varied systematically.
- <!-- ZH --> 公开排行榜混合了不同水平、投入与算力,作为人类基线并不完美;只有 24 个任务,每领域 4–9 个,误差条很宽。 <!-- EN --> Public leaderboards mix expertise, effort and compute, so they are an imperfect human baseline; only 24 tasks (4–9 per domain), so error bars are wide.
- <!-- ZH --> 只看预测分数,不检查 agent 是否有数据泄漏、是否正确使用验证集,或其方法叙述是否属实。 <!-- EN --> Scores predictions only; does not check for leakage, proper validation use, or whether the agent's account of its method is truthful.
- <!-- ZH --> 未与 AutoML(H2O、Lazy Predict)对照,无法判断 LLM agent 相对自动化 pipeline 的增益。 <!-- EN --> No AutoML (H2O, Lazy Predict) comparison, so the gain of LLM agents over automated pipelines is unknown.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 对 **Agent 可信度课题**,有三条具体证据:(1) AIDE 在同一任务两次重跑得到 100 vs 54 百分位,是「rerun stability」轴的直接案例 —— 结论/性能在重跑间可能剧烈波动;(2) 失败模式中的「**静默错误**」(try–except 回退使脚本看似成功却没有产出)正是 claim-to-evidence 验证要抓的那类问题:agent 报告「已完成」,但证据(预测文件)不存在 —— 应写进我们的 omics failure taxonomy;(3) agent 设计与底座 LLM 的混淆提示我们的评估要像 DeepEvidence 那样固定底座。对 **伪影注入课题**,它的单细胞任务里有 **跨 DKD 数据集的 label projection**(来自 OpenProblems),这是与肾脏 omics 直接相关的现成任务,可作为 KidneyAnomalyBench 中「参考映射/标签迁移」基线的对照;但 BioML-bench 只评估预测指标,不评估异常或伪影识别。对 detect → model → revert 主线,它显示 agent 倾向用经典 ML(随机森林、梯度提升)而回避深度学习,这意味着让 agent 自动搭建 virtual tissue 模型(本质上是 DL)目前不现实。
<!-- EN --> For **the agent-trustworthiness topic**, three concrete pieces of evidence: (1) AIDE's 100th vs 54th percentile on two reruns of the same task is a direct case for the rerun-stability axis — performance/conclusions can swing wildly across reruns; (2) the **silent-error** failure mode (try–except fallbacks make a script look successful while producing nothing) is exactly what claim-to-evidence verification must catch — the agent reports "done" but the evidence (prediction file) does not exist — and belongs in our omics failure taxonomy; (3) the agent/LLM confound tells us to fix the base model in our evaluations, as DeepEvidence does. For **the artifact-injection topic**, its single-cell suite contains **label projection across DKD datasets** (from OpenProblems), a ready-made kidney-omics task usable as a comparator for the reference-mapping / label-transfer baseline in KidneyAnomalyBench; but BioML-bench scores prediction metrics only, not anomaly or artifact detection. For the detect → model → revert line, agents' preference for classical ML (random forests, gradient boosting) over deep learning means having agents autonomously build virtual-tissue models (inherently DL) is not yet realistic.
