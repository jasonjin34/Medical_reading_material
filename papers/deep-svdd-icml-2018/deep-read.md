## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Deep SVDD 是第一个**直接以异常检测为目标**训练的深度方法(作者自述):训练一个神经网络 φ,让正常数据的表示尽量落进一个以固定中心 c 为圆心、体积最小的超球,测试时异常分数就是 ‖φ(x) − c‖²;论文同时证明了哪些网络设计会导致"超球坍缩"(偏置项、有界激活、可学习的中心),以及软边界版本的 ν-性质。
<!-- EN --> Deep SVDD is, per the authors, the first deep method trained **directly on an anomaly-detection objective**: a network φ is trained so that normal data's representations fall into a minimum-volume hypersphere around a fixed centre c, and the test-time anomaly score is ‖φ(x) − c‖². The paper also proves which design choices cause "hypersphere collapse" (bias terms, bounded activations, a learnable centre) and the ν-property of the soft-boundary variant.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 经典方法(OC-SVM、KDE)在高维大数据上因扩展性差与维数灾难而失效,需要大量特征工程;核方法至少随样本数平方增长,预测还要存支持向量。而当时的深度异常检测(自编码器、AnoGAN 等)都是**为别的任务训练**(生成或压缩),再借重建误差这种启发式打分 —— 并不优化异常检测本身的目标;自编码器还面临"压缩到多少维"这个在无监督下难以选择的超参。作者要问:能否像 SVDD 一样定义一个**最小体积**的异常检测目标,但让特征由深度网络端到端学出?
<!-- EN --> Classical methods (OC-SVM, KDE) fail on high-dimensional, data-rich problems due to poor scalability and the curse of dimensionality, and need heavy feature engineering; kernel methods scale at least quadratically and must store support vectors. Deep AD at the time (autoencoders, AnoGAN, etc.) was **trained for another task** (generation or compression) and scored with heuristics such as reconstruction error — not an AD objective; autoencoders also need a compression level that is hard to choose without supervision. The question: can one define a **minimum-volume** AD objective like SVDD, but with features learned end-to-end by a deep network?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **目标函数**(权重 W 的网络 φ: X → F):**软边界 Deep SVDD**——min R² + (1/νn)·Σ max{0, ‖φ(xᵢ;W) − c‖² − R²} + (λ/2)·Σ‖W^ℓ‖²_F,ν ∈ (0,1] 权衡超球体积与越界样本;**One-Class Deep SVDD**(假设训练数据绝大多数正常)——min (1/n)·Σ‖φ(xᵢ;W) − c‖² + 权重衰减,即把所有表示到 c 的平均距离压小。异常分数 s(x) = ‖φ(x;W*) − c‖²(软边界版可再减去 R*²)。推理只需前向网络,**不存任何训练数据**。**优化**:SGD/Adam;软边界版交替优化 W 与 R(每 k=5 个 epoch 用线搜索解 R)。**防坍缩的理论**:命题 1——若 c 也作为自由变量,全零权重网络 + R=0 就是最优解,所以 c 必须固定且不等于全零权重网络的输出(实践中取初始前向输出的均值);命题 2——任何隐藏层有**偏置项**都能学出常数映射,故去掉所有偏置;命题 3——**有界激活**(如 sigmoid)可被饱和来模拟偏置,故用 ReLU/leaky ReLU 这类无界激活;命题 4——软边界版的 ν 是离群样本比例的上界、边界上或外样本比例的下界(ν-性质)。**实现细节**:LeNet 式 CNN,用训练好的卷积自编码器(DCAE)编码器初始化(预训练),BatchNorm,leaky ReLU(α=0.1),两阶段学习率 10⁻⁴ → 10⁻⁵(150+100 epoch),λ=10⁻⁶。
<!-- EN --> **Objectives** (network φ: X → F with weights W): **soft-boundary Deep SVDD** — min R² + (1/νn)·Σ max{0, ‖φ(xᵢ;W) − c‖² − R²} + (λ/2)·Σ‖W^ℓ‖²_F, with ν ∈ (0,1] trading sphere volume against boundary violations; **One-Class Deep SVDD** (assuming the training data are mostly normal) — min (1/n)·Σ‖φ(xᵢ;W) − c‖² + weight decay, i.e. shrink the mean distance of all representations to c. Anomaly score s(x) = ‖φ(x;W*) − c‖² (soft-boundary: optionally minus R*²). Inference is a forward pass and **stores no training data**. **Optimisation**: SGD/Adam; for soft-boundary, alternate between W and R (solve R by line search every k=5 epochs). **Anti-collapse theory**: Proposition 1 — if c is a free variable, all-zero weights with R=0 is optimal, so c must be fixed and differ from the all-zero-weights output (in practice the mean of an initial forward pass); Proposition 2 — any hidden-layer **bias term** lets the net learn a constant map, so all biases are removed; Proposition 3 — **bounded activations** (e.g. sigmoid) can be saturated to emulate a bias, so unbounded ones (ReLU / leaky ReLU) are preferred; Proposition 4 — in soft-boundary Deep SVDD, ν upper-bounds the fraction of outliers and lower-bounds the fraction on or outside the boundary (ν-property). **Details**: LeNet-type CNNs initialised from a trained convolutional autoencoder (DCAE) encoder (pre-training), BatchNorm, leaky ReLU (α=0.1), two-phase learning rate 10⁻⁴ → 10⁻⁵ (150+100 epochs), λ=10⁻⁶.

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> **范式:密度 / 单类(one-class)**。直接把 Tax & Duin 的核 SVDD(最小包围超球)和 Schölkopf 等的 OC-SVM / ν-性质搬到深度特征上;作者把此前方法分为"混合式"(先学表示再接浅层检测器,如 DBN + OC-SVM)和"全深度式"(直接用重建误差),Deep SVDD 是全深度、且目标就是异常检测。它引出了 Deep SAD(半监督、可用少量标注异常)、多超球扩展、以及 Patch SVDD(MEATRD 的图像基线之一)等;Ruff 等 2021 统一综述把它与核 SVDD 并列为同一"超球模型",并以此说明浅层与深度方法之间可以相互迁移改进。病理与空间组学中的"在冻结/学到的特征上做 one-class"(BI 的 center-loss + OC-SVM、MEATRD 的 Deep-SVDD 式打分)都是这条线的后代。
<!-- EN --> **Paradigm: density / one-class.** It lifts Tax & Duin's kernel SVDD (minimum enclosing hypersphere) and Schölkopf et al.'s OC-SVM / ν-property onto deep features. The authors split prior work into "mixed" (learn a representation, then a shallow detector, e.g. DBN + OC-SVM) and "fully deep" (reconstruction error directly); Deep SVDD is fully deep and its objective is AD itself. It led to Deep SAD (semi-supervised, using a few labelled anomalies), multi-sphere extensions and Patch SVDD (one of MEATRD's image baselines); the 2021 unifying review by Ruff et al. lists it next to kernel SVDD as the same "hypersphere model" to show how improvements transfer between shallow and deep methods. "One-class on frozen/learned features" in pathology and spatial omics (BI's center-loss + OC-SVM, MEATRD's Deep-SVDD-style scoring) descends from this line.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **MNIST 与 CIFAR-10 单类设定**:每次取一个类为正常类、其余 9 类为异常;只用该类训练样本训练(MNIST 约 6,000、CIFAR-10 5,000),在标准测试集 10,000 张上算 AUC;预处理为 L1 全局对比度归一化 + min-max 缩放。**GTSRB 停车标志对抗样本检测**:训练 780 张正常停车标志;测试 270 张正常 + 20 张由 Boundary Attack 生成的对抗样本;裁掉 10% 边框、缩放到 32×32。对比方法:OC-SVM/SVDD(高斯核,γ 用 10% 测试样本小验证集网格搜索——作者注明这给了浅层 SVDD 一点监督优势)、KDE、Isolation Forest(三者先做保留 95% 方差的 PCA)、DCAE、AnoGAN。结果为 10 个随机种子的平均。
<!-- EN --> **One-class MNIST and CIFAR-10**: one class is normal and the other nine are anomalies; train on that class's training samples only (≈6,000 for MNIST, 5,000 for CIFAR-10), compute AUC on the standard 10,000-image test set; preprocessing is L1 global contrast normalisation + min-max scaling. **GTSRB stop-sign adversarial detection**: 780 normal stop signs for training; test 270 normal + 20 Boundary-Attack adversarial examples; 10% border cropped, resized to 32×32. Comparators: OC-SVM/SVDD (Gaussian kernel, γ grid-searched on a small hold-out of 10% of test samples — which the authors note gives shallow SVDD a small supervised advantage), KDE, Isolation Forest (all three after PCA keeping 95% variance), DCAE and AnoGAN. Results are averaged over 10 seeds.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **MNIST(表 1)**:One-Class Deep SVDD 在 10 个类中的 8 个上最好、类 4 与 OC-SVM 并列(94.9),只有类 0 低于 OC-SVM(98.0 vs 98.6);例如类 2:91.7±0.8(OC-SVM 82.5、IF 88.6、DCAE 85.4、AnoGAN 85.0);类 8:93.9±1.6(OC-SVM 88.9)。**CIFAR-10**:结果"喜忧参半"——Deep SVDD 在 AUTOMOBILE 65.9、CAT 59.1、DOG 65.7、HORSE 67.3 上最好;但 DEER(66.2 KDE vs 60.9)、FROG(74.9 KDE vs 67.7)、TRUCK(76.0 KDE vs 73.1)上**浅层 KDE / SVDD 反而更好**,AIRPLANE(67.1)与 BIRD(52.9)上 AnoGAN 最好,SHIP 上 DCAE 最好(76.8)。作者解释:这些类的正常样本有很强的**全局结构**(如卡车图像被路面/天空水平分割),局部 CNN 特征未必合适 —— 架构选择很关键。One-Class 版在两个数据集上都略好于软边界版(因训练集确实无异常)。**GTSRB(表 2)**:One-Class Deep SVDD **80.3±2.8** 最好,DCAE 79.1、软边界 77.8、IF 73.8、OC-SVM 67.5、KDE 60.5;**AnoGAN 的 DCGAN 因数据太少未收敛**,无结果。深度方法的标准差普遍更大(SGD 随机性)。
<!-- EN --> **MNIST (Table 1)**: One-Class Deep SVDD is best on 8 of 10 classes, tied with OC-SVM on class 4 (94.9), and below OC-SVM only on class 0 (98.0 vs 98.6); e.g. class 2: 91.7±0.8 (OC-SVM 82.5, IF 88.6, DCAE 85.4, AnoGAN 85.0); class 8: 93.9±1.6 (OC-SVM 88.9). **CIFAR-10**: "mixed" — Deep SVDD is best on AUTOMOBILE 65.9, CAT 59.1, DOG 65.7, HORSE 67.3; but on DEER (KDE 66.2 vs 60.9), FROG (KDE 74.9 vs 67.7) and TRUCK (KDE 76.0 vs 73.1) **shallow KDE / SVDD win**, AnoGAN is best on AIRPLANE (67.1) and BIRD (52.9), and DCAE on SHIP (76.8). The authors' explanation: these classes have strong **global structure** (e.g. trucks split horizontally into road and sky), for which local CNN features may be ill-suited — architecture choice matters. The one-class variant slightly beats soft-boundary on both datasets (the training data really are anomaly-free). **GTSRB (Table 2)**: One-Class Deep SVDD is best at **80.3±2.8**, then DCAE 79.1, soft-boundary 77.8, IF 73.8, OC-SVM 67.5, KDE 60.5; **AnoGAN's DCGAN did not converge on so little data**, so it has no result. Deep methods show larger standard deviations (SGD randomness).

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 第一个全深度的单类分类目标:特征学习与最小体积超球联合优化。 <!-- EN --> The first fully deep one-class objective: representation learning jointly optimised with a minimum-volume hypersphere.
- <!-- ZH --> 系统刻画"超球坍缩"的成因(自由中心、偏置项、有界激活)及对应的设计规则 —— 这些规则至今仍是一切"把正常压紧"类方法的必查项。 <!-- EN --> A systematic account of "hypersphere collapse" (free centre, bias terms, bounded activations) with design rules — still the checklist for any "compress the normal" method.
- <!-- ZH --> 证明软边界版的 ν-性质,可以把"训练集中可能有多少污染"作为先验写进模型。 <!-- EN --> Proves the ν-property for soft-boundary Deep SVDD, letting a prior on training-set contamination enter the model.
- <!-- ZH --> 模型完全由网络权重刻画,无需保存训练数据,内存与推理开销低。 <!-- EN --> The model is fully characterised by its weights — no stored training data, low memory and fast inference.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 只在 MNIST / CIFAR-10 / GTSRB 这类"把分类数据集改造成单类任务"的设定上评估,异常是"别的类",与真实场景的细微异常差别很大(Ruff 等 2021 综述也批评了这种评估习惯)。 <!-- EN --> Evaluated only on classification datasets repurposed as one-class tasks (MNIST / CIFAR-10 / GTSRB), where anomalies are "other classes" — far from subtle real-world anomalies (a habit the 2021 Ruff et al. review itself criticises).
- <!-- ZH --> CIFAR-10 上并不稳定优于浅层方法;对网络架构敏感,且 SGD 带来较大方差。 <!-- EN --> Not consistently better than shallow methods on CIFAR-10; sensitive to architecture, with larger SGD-induced variance.
- <!-- ZH --> 为防坍缩必须去偏置、固定中心、用 DCAE 预训练 —— 表示的好坏很大程度依赖预训练;超球只给出单一"正常模式",对多模态正常分布(多种细胞类型)天然不合适。 <!-- EN --> Anti-collapse requires removing biases, fixing the centre and DCAE pre-training — representation quality depends heavily on pre-training; a single hypersphere models one "normal mode" and fits multi-modal normal distributions (many cell types) poorly.
- <!-- ZH --> 只给样本级分数,不提供定位或解释。 <!-- EN --> Gives sample-level scores only — no localisation or explanation.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> Deep SVDD 给 **detect** 阶段提供了最干净的"正常 = 紧致区域,异常 = 到中心的距离"打分形式,而且与 Plan A 最可能的主力路线——**在冻结的基础模型嵌入上做 one-class**——一一对应。**映射到肾脏组学**:样本 = snRNA 细胞、Xenium 细胞、Visium spot 或 H&E 图块;"正常训练集" = KPMP/HuBMAP 健康参考;φ = 在 scVI latent 或 H&E 基础模型嵌入之上加的一个小网络;异常分数 = 到"正常中心"的距离。**哪些假设会断**:(1) **单一超球 vs 多细胞类型**:肾脏正常组织本身就有 PT、TAL、足细胞、内皮、免疫等数十种状态,一个中心 c 会把"稀有但正常的细胞类型"判为异常;必须**按细胞类型分别建超球**,或用多中心/条件版本(这与 healthy-ref 的"先在健康图谱上学 latent、再与匹配对照比较"思路互补)。(2) **批次效应**:新批次整体平移就会远离 c —— 需要先参考映射并用 MapQC 式的样本间零分布校正。(3) **污染**:"健康"供肾里也可能有 doublet、ambient RNA 主导的细胞、Xenium 分割/溢出错误的细胞(见 xenium-split、ovrlpy);软边界版的 ν 正好可以写入"训练集中预计有多少比例是伪影"的先验,这比 One-Class 版更适合肾脏数据。(4) **连续疾病态**:‖φ(x) − c‖² 是连续量,但没有"异常从哪开始"的定义 —— 需要 conformal 校准(conformal-scrna)把分数变成有 FPR 保证的 p 值。(5) **无金标准**:与论文的"别的类 = 异常"不同,肾脏里没有现成的异常标签,KidneyAnomalyBench 的专家判定是评测前提。**跨模态一致性**:对同一组织,分别在 H&E 嵌入与表达嵌入上建两个超球;两个距离都大 → 疾病候选,只在一个模态大 → 伪影候选(例如分割错误只在表达侧,染色/折叠只在图像侧)。**仓库里已有的同类适配**:MEATRD(meatrd-aaai-2025)在潜在多模态重建误差上训练 Deep-SVDD 式单类分类器、以到中心 c 的距离打分;BI 的 histo-anomaly-bi-repo 在 center-loss 训练的 CNN 特征上拟合 one-class SVM(浅层 SVDD 的近亲);histo-nejmai-2024 比较了基础模型特征上的 kNN 与 one-class 微调,并把 Deep SVDD 列为迁移到空间组学的待读;MedIAnomaly 发现"冻结 ImageNet ResNet + one-class 分类器"几乎在所有数据集都进前三 —— 支持把"冻结基础模型嵌入 + 单类打分"当 Plan A 的默认强基线。
<!-- EN --> Deep SVDD gives the **detect** stage its cleanest scoring form — "normal = compact region, anomaly = distance to centre" — and maps one-to-one onto Plan A's most likely workhorse: **one-class scoring on frozen foundation-model embeddings**. **Mapping to kidney omics**: samples = snRNA cells, Xenium cells, Visium spots or H&E patches; "normal training set" = KPMP/HuBMAP healthy references; φ = a small network on top of scVI latents or H&E foundation-model embeddings; anomaly score = distance to the "normal centre". **Which assumptions break**: (1) **one sphere vs many cell types**: normal kidney already contains dozens of states (PT, TAL, podocytes, endothelium, immune…), so a single centre c will flag "rare but normal" cell types; one sphere **per cell type**, or a multi-centre / conditional variant, is required (complementing healthy-ref's "learn the latent on a healthy atlas, then compare against matched controls"). (2) **batch effects**: a new batch's global shift moves away from c — reference mapping and a MapQC-style inter-sample null are needed first. (3) **contamination**: "healthy" donor tissue also contains doublets, ambient-dominated cells and Xenium segmentation/spillover errors (see xenium-split, ovrlpy); the soft-boundary ν can encode a prior on "what fraction of the training set is artifact", making it a better fit for kidney data than the one-class variant. (4) **continuous disease states**: ‖φ(x) − c‖² is continuous with no definition of where "anomalous" starts — conformal calibration (conformal-scrna) turns scores into p-values with FPR guarantees. (5) **no gold standard**: unlike the paper's "other class = anomaly", kidney data have no ready labels; KidneyAnomalyBench's expert adjudication is a precondition for evaluation. **Cross-modal agreement**: build one sphere on H&E embeddings and one on expression embeddings of the same tissue; both distances large → disease candidate, only one large → artifact candidate (e.g. segmentation errors only on the expression side, staining/folds only on the image side). **Existing repo adaptations**: MEATRD (meatrd-aaai-2025) trains a Deep-SVDD-style one-class classifier on latent multimodal reconstruction errors and scores by distance to a centre c; BI's histo-anomaly-bi-repo fits a one-class SVM (shallow SVDD's sibling) on center-loss-trained CNN features; histo-nejmai-2024 compares kNN and one-class fine-tuning on foundation-model features and lists Deep SVDD as a follow-up for spatial omics; MedIAnomaly finds "frozen ImageNet ResNet + one-class classifier" lands top-3 on nearly every dataset — supporting "frozen foundation-model embeddings + one-class scoring" as Plan A's default strong baseline.
