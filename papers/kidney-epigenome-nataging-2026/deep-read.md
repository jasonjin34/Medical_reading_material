## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 作者构建了一个跨物种(人 + 小鼠)的单细胞肾脏表观基因组图谱 —— 单细胞 DNA 甲基化(sciMET)+ 匹配的 10x Multiome(RNA+ATAC)+ Xenium 5K 空间转录组 + scMethyl-Hi-C —— 发现肾病中的**表观遗传年龄加速主要发生在小管上皮细胞**(平均快 4.8 年),病变 PT 细胞的甲基化与表达改变和正常衰老的转录轨迹重叠,集中于缺少 CpG 岛的谱系基因,并定位到未完成修复的空间 niche。
<!-- EN --> The authors build a cross-species (human + mouse) single-cell kidney epigenome atlas — single-cell DNA methylation (sciMET) + matched 10x Multiome (RNA+ATAC) + Xenium 5K spatial transcriptomics + scMethyl-Hi-C — and find that **disease-associated epigenetic age acceleration is concentrated in tubular epithelial cells** (on average 4.8 years), that altered PT cells' methylation and expression changes overlap normal-aging transcriptional trajectories, preferentially hit CpG-island-less lineage genes, and map to spatial niches of unresolved repair.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 表观遗传衰老是慢性病(包括 CKD)的标志,但 bulk 分析看不出是哪些细胞类型在「加速衰老」;已有单细胞肾脏研究覆盖了转录组和染色质可及性,却缺少更深层、更稳定的表观层 —— DNA 甲基化(长期记录细胞状态)和 3D 基因组结构(调控所需的物理拓扑)。同时,正常的年龄相关表观漂移与疾病特异改变之间的关系也不清楚。
<!-- EN --> Epigenetic aging is a hallmark of chronic diseases including CKD, but bulk analyses cannot say which cell types "age faster"; existing single-cell kidney studies cover transcriptome and chromatin accessibility but lack deeper, more stable epigenetic layers — DNA methylation (a long-term record of cell state) and 3D genome architecture (the physical topology of regulation). The relationship between normal age-related epigenetic drift and disease-specific alterations is also unclear.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **单细胞甲基化**:组合索引 sciMET;人类样本混池同批处理,再用 SNP 反卷积(全基因组测序验证,99.2% 核正确分配)。**多模态整合**:匹配 10x Multiome、Xenium 5K、以及在一位健康供者上做 scMethyl-Hi-C(同一细胞同时测 3D 构象与甲基组)。**甲基化分区**:UMR(<10% 甲基化、CpG 富集、类启动子)与 LMR(10–50%、CpG 贫乏、类增强子)。**跨物种**:每个细胞与 50 个近邻聚成 metacell,在 40,284 个一对一同源 5 kb bin 上用 Harmony 整合。**衰老时钟**:小鼠用基于全局甲基化的时钟;人类用低覆盖鲁棒的 PC 版 Horvath 多组织时钟(下采样到约 3× 仍准确),年龄加速 = 预测年龄 − 实际年龄的残差。**验证**:用单细胞图谱对 KPMP 激光显微切割的肾小管间质/肾小球 bulk WGBS 做反卷积;S-LDSC 做遗传力富集;72 例 20–90 岁非疾病肾的 snRNA 定义年龄相关基因;单分子共甲基化/甲基化熵;Xenium niche 与 TF 活性;Hi-C A/B compartment;UK Biobank Olink 血浆蛋白与 eGFR 关联。
<!-- EN --> **Single-cell methylation**: combinatorial-indexing sciMET; human samples pooled in one batch and demultiplexed by SNPs (validated with WGS, 99.2% of nuclei correctly assigned). **Multimodal integration**: matched 10x Multiome, Xenium 5K, and scMethyl-Hi-C (3D conformation + methylome in the same cell) on one healthy donor. **Methylation segmentation**: UMRs (<10% methylated, CpG-rich, promoter-like) and LMRs (10–50%, CpG-poor, enhancer-like). **Cross-species**: each cell aggregated with its 50 nearest neighbours into metacells, integrated with Harmony over 40,284 one-to-one orthologous 5-kb bins. **Aging clocks**: a global-methylation clock in mouse; in human a low-coverage-robust PC-based Horvath multi-tissue clock (accurate down to ~3× coverage), with age acceleration = predicted − chronological age residual. **Validation**: deconvolution of KPMP laser-microdissected tubulointerstitial/glomerular bulk WGBS using the single-cell atlas; S-LDSC heritability enrichment; age-associated genes from snRNA of 72 non-diseased kidneys aged 20–90; single-molecule co-methylation/methylation entropy; Xenium niches and TF activities; Hi-C A/B compartments; UK Biobank Olink plasma proteins vs eGFR.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> sciMET:57,619 个高质量核 —— 人 12 位供者(7 例肾病 + 5 例年龄匹配参照,33,835 核),小鼠 6 只(3 只 4 月龄 + 3 只 20 月龄,均为雄性,23,784 核);每细胞中位 0.91M CpG(约 3.1% 覆盖)。Multiome:人 142,459 核(9 样本)、小鼠 36,563 核(6 样本)。Xenium 5K:健康、AKI、CKD 共 4 个样本 383,771 个细胞。scMethyl-Hi-C:1 位健康供者 3,072 个细胞、>3 亿长程接触。外部:KPMP WGBS(各 15 例)、小鼠 IRI snATAC(GSE209610)、HKA 图谱。甲基化数据存 BioStudies(S-BSST2657 人 / S-BSST2647 鼠),Multiome 在 HuBMAP。
<!-- EN --> sciMET: 57,619 high-quality nuclei — human 12 donors (7 kidney-disease patients + 5 age-matched references, 33,835 nuclei), mouse 6 animals (3 at 4 months + 3 at 20 months, all male, 23,784 nuclei); median 0.91M CpGs per cell (~3.1% coverage). Multiome: 142,459 human nuclei (9 samples), 36,563 mouse nuclei (6 samples). Xenium 5K: 383,771 cells from 4 samples (healthy, AKI, CKD). scMethyl-Hi-C: 3,072 cells, >300M long-range contacts from 1 healthy donor. External: KPMP WGBS (15 each), mouse IRI snATAC (GSE209610), the HKA atlas. Methylation data in BioStudies (S-BSST2657 human / S-BSST2647 mouse); Multiome on HuBMAP.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **图谱质量**:甲基化与 Multiome 推断的细胞组成 Spearman ρ = 0.72(sciMET 检出更多髓系免疫细胞,可能因 snRNA 漏检低核 RNA 细胞);92.6% 的 UMR 与 ATAC 峰重叠,LMR 只有 54.8%。人类细胞类型 DMR 共 208,049 个,**只有 16.4% 与 Illumina 850K 芯片位点重叠** —— 芯片研究会漏掉大部分调控元件。 <!-- EN --> **Atlas quality**: methylation- vs Multiome-inferred composition Spearman ρ = 0.72 (sciMET detects more myeloid cells, likely because snRNA under-detects low-nuclear-RNA cells); 92.6% of UMRs overlap ATAC peaks vs 54.8% of LMRs. 208,049 human cell-type DMRs, **only 16.4% overlapping Illumina 850K array CpGs** — array studies miss most regulatory elements.
- <!-- ZH --> **跨物种**:34% 的人类 DMR 在小鼠中保持同一状态,但**上皮细胞的跨物种甲基化分化显著大于非上皮细胞**,与较低的序列保守性相关;保守 DMR 富集于 TSS 与 Polycomb 抑制区,物种特异 DMR 多在远端增强子;PT 中 AP-1 与 MYC motif 只富集于物种分化 DMR。 <!-- EN --> **Cross-species**: 34% of human DMRs keep the same state in mouse, but **epithelial cells diverge significantly more than non-epithelial cells**, linked to lower sequence conservation; conserved DMRs are enriched at TSSs and Polycomb-repressed chromatin, species-specific DMRs at distal enhancers; in PT, AP-1 and MYC motifs are enriched only in divergent DMRs.
- <!-- ZH --> **小鼠衰老**:细胞类型特异的 UMR/LMR 比共享元件更易发生年龄相关甲基化变化;越相近的细胞类型,其特异调控区越易受年龄影响(仅 LMR);年龄 DMR 在小鼠 IRI 损伤相关可及区显著富集(fold enrichment = 2.95,P < 0.01)。 <!-- EN --> **Mouse aging**: cell-type-specific UMRs/LMRs shift with age more than shared elements; the closer two cell types, the more vulnerable their distinguishing regulatory regions (LMRs only); age DMRs are enriched in IRI injury-associated accessible regions (fold enrichment = 2.95, P < 0.01).
- <!-- ZH --> **人类疾病**:样本级伪 bulk 显示疾病组年龄加速(P = 0.01);细胞类型级别,**上皮细胞平均加速 4.8 年(P = 2.3 × 10⁻⁴),非上皮细胞无显著差异**,且疾病样本上皮比例相近 —— 加速是细胞内在的,而非组成变化。KPMP bulk WGBS 反卷积验证:富含小管上皮的肾小管间质区年龄加速显著。PT 特异 DMR 富集 eGFR 遗传力,健康→病变态 DMR 亦然(FDR = 0.004)。 <!-- EN --> **Human disease**: sample-level pseudobulk shows age acceleration in disease (P = 0.01); at cell-type level, **epithelial cells are accelerated by 4.8 years on average (P = 2.3 × 10⁻⁴), non-epithelial cells not significantly**, with similar epithelial proportions — acceleration is cell-intrinsic, not compositional. KPMP bulk WGBS deconvolution confirms significant acceleration in tubule-rich tubulointerstitium. PT-specific DMRs are enriched for eGFR heritability, as are healthy→altered DMRs (FDR = 0.004).
- <!-- ZH --> **病变 PT**:同一供者内比较病变(adaptive/maladaptive/degenerative 合并)与健康 PT,得到 8,996 个 DMR,其中 63.1% 为高甲基化;病变 PT 局部甲基化协调性下降、熵升高,尤以 PT 特异调控区为甚;HNF、RXR、PPAR motif 可及性降低、甲基化升高;DMR 甲基化与邻近基因表达 ρ = −0.49;病变 vs 健康的表达改变与 72 例正常肾的年龄相关得分正相关(如 CXCL1、IGFBP6、KCNB2);同时差异甲基化+差异表达的基因富集于**无 CpG 岛**基因。 <!-- EN --> **Altered PT**: within-donor comparison of altered (adaptive/maladaptive/degenerative pooled) vs healthy PT yields 8,996 DMRs, 63.1% hypermethylated; altered PT shows reduced local methylation coordination and higher entropy, most at PT-specific regulatory regions; HNF, RXR and PPAR motifs lose accessibility and gain methylation; DMR methylation vs neighbouring-gene expression ρ = −0.49; altered-vs-healthy expression changes correlate positively with age-association scores from 72 normal kidneys (e.g. CXCL1, IGFBP6, KCNB2); genes both differentially methylated and expressed are enriched for **CpG-island-less** genes.
- <!-- ZH --> **空间与 3D**:Xenium 5K 中病变 PT 甲基化相关基因签名富集在早期修复 niche(AKI 富集;ATF4、JUN、BACH2、E2F)与失败修复 niche(CKD 富集;BHLHE40、MYC、MITF、WNT);PCK1 高甲基化且下调,PLAU、MYO1C、CYTH2 低甲基化且上调。Hi-C 显示上皮细胞短程/长程接触比更高;病变上调基因富集于 PT 特异 B compartment,下调基因富集于 A compartment;找到 107 个「去抑制」区与 64 个「增抑制」区,去抑制区含 CXCL1/2/3/6、ITGA2、ANTXR1、HMGA2、ETS1、BACH2;其中 DSC2、DSG2、TIMP2 编码蛋白的血浆水平在 UK Biobank(54,219 人)中与 eGFR 负相关。 <!-- EN --> **Spatial and 3D**: in Xenium 5K, altered-PT methylation-linked gene signatures are enriched in an early-repair niche (AKI-enriched; ATF4, JUN, BACH2, E2F) and a failed-repair niche (CKD-enriched; BHLHE40, MYC, MITF, WNT); PCK1 is hypermethylated and down, PLAU, MYO1C, CYTH2 hypomethylated and up. Hi-C shows higher short- to long-range contact ratios in epithelia; up-regulated altered-state genes are enriched in PT-specific B compartments, down-regulated in A; 107 "loss-of-repression" and 64 "gain-of-repression" regions, the former containing CXCL1/2/3/6, ITGA2, ANTXR1, HMGA2, ETS1, BACH2; plasma DSC2, DSG2 and TIMP2 associate negatively with eGFR in UK Biobank (54,219 participants).

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 首个跨物种、单细胞分辨率的肾脏 DNA 甲基化图谱,并与 RNA/ATAC、空间与 3D 基因组整合。 <!-- EN --> A first cross-species, single-cell-resolution kidney DNA-methylation atlas integrated with RNA/ATAC, spatial and 3D-genome data.
- <!-- ZH --> 把 bulk 层面的「疾病加速衰老」定位到**小管上皮**,并用独立 bulk WGBS 反卷积验证。 <!-- EN --> Localizes bulk-level "disease-accelerated aging" to **tubular epithelium**, validated by deconvolving independent bulk WGBS.
- <!-- ZH --> 提出「谱系特异调控元件更易受衰老和疾病侵蚀」与「缺 CpG 岛基因去抑制」的机制框架。 <!-- EN --> Proposes a mechanistic frame: lineage-specific regulatory elements are most vulnerable to aging and disease, with de-repression of CpG-island-less genes.
- <!-- ZH --> 用遗传力(S-LDSC)与人群血浆蛋白组把单细胞表观改变连到临床肾功能。 <!-- EN --> Links single-cell epigenetic changes to clinical kidney function via heritability (S-LDSC) and population plasma proteomics.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自陈:小鼠衰老/损伤模型不能完全代表人类慢性病;需纵向研究建立因果;小鼠全为雄性;人类疾病队列小且病因异质,无法分病因分析;scMethyl-Hi-C 只有一位健康供者、分辨率稀疏。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: mouse aging/injury models do not fully capture human chronic disease; longitudinal studies are needed for causality; all mice are male; the human disease cohort is small and aetiologically heterogeneous, preventing stratified analysis; scMethyl-Hi-C comes from one healthy donor at sparse resolution.
- <!-- ZH --> 「衰老样」状态的解释被作者主动限定:损伤、炎症、应激与去分化会产生重叠的分子签名,**无法确定归因于加速的生物学衰老或细胞衰老**。 <!-- EN --> The "aging-like" interpretation is explicitly hedged: injury, inflammation, stress and de-differentiation produce overlapping signatures, **precluding definitive attribution to accelerated biological aging or senescence**.
- <!-- ZH --> 病变 PT 合并了 adaptive/maladaptive/degenerative 三种状态;Xenium 只有 4 个样本;部分检验未做多重校正(作者称为预设的两组比较)。 <!-- EN --> Altered PT pools adaptive/maladaptive/degenerative states; Xenium covers only 4 samples; some tests are not multiplicity-adjusted (described as two prespecified comparisons).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 对 **伪影注入课题** 的最大提醒是**年龄是一个混杂轴**:疾病中的病变 PT 在表观与转录上都「像变老了」,而正常老年肾本身就带有部分同样的签名(CXCL1 等)。如果异常检测器用年轻或年龄不匹配的健康参照(例如部分公开图谱),它很可能把正常衰老当成异常;反之,若参照偏老,早期疾病信号可能被吸收。因此 KidneyAnomalyBench 应**按年龄匹配参照、或把年龄作为协变量**,并报告异常分数与供者年龄的相关性。第二,本文提供了一个**多层「正交模态」**的范本:甲基化(稳定、长期)、ATAC(可及性)、RNA(瞬时)、空间(niche)在同一状态上一致 —— 这正是「跨模态一致性区分伪影与疾病」的思路:一个只在 RNA 上出现、在甲基化/ATAC 上毫无痕迹的「异常」更可能是技术性的(如 ambient RNA);反过来,甲基化熵升高本身也可作为细胞级的「身份侵蚀」异常分数。第三,Xenium 5K 上的早期修复 / 失败修复 niche 与 `inflammatory-pt-natcommun-2025`、`lake-kidney-atlas-nature-2023` 的 PT 状态对应,可作为空间异常的专家标签候选。对 **revert**:「去抑制」区的基因(CXCL 家族、HMGA2、ETS1、BACH2)与 HNF4A/RXR/PPAR 程序是天然的逆转靶点候选,但作者没有做扰动验证。对 **Agent 可信度课题**:作者对「衰老」结论的主动限定,以及「未做多重校正」的明示,是 claim-to-evidence 校验中「结论措辞是否与证据强度匹配」的好样例。
<!-- EN --> The biggest lesson for **the artifact-injection topic** is that **age is a confounding axis**: altered PT in disease looks epigenetically and transcriptionally "older", and normal aged kidneys already carry part of the same signature (e.g. CXCL1). An anomaly detector trained on young or age-mismatched healthy references (as with some public atlases) may flag normal aging as anomalous; conversely, an old-skewed reference may absorb early disease signal. KidneyAnomalyBench should therefore **age-match references or model age as a covariate**, and report the correlation between anomaly score and donor age. Second, the paper is a template for **multiple orthogonal layers**: methylation (stable, long-term), ATAC (accessibility), RNA (transient) and space (niche) agree on the same state — exactly the "cross-modal agreement separates artifacts from disease" logic: an "anomaly" visible only in RNA with no trace in methylation/ATAC is more likely technical (e.g. ambient RNA), while elevated methylation entropy can itself serve as a cell-level "identity-erosion" anomaly score. Third, the Xenium 5K early-repair / failed-repair niches correspond to PT states in `inflammatory-pt-natcommun-2025` and `lake-kidney-atlas-nature-2023`, making them candidate expert labels for spatial anomalies. For **revert**: genes in loss-of-repression regions (CXCL family, HMGA2, ETS1, BACH2) and the HNF4A/RXR/PPAR programs are natural reversion-target candidates, though no perturbation was done here. For **the agent-trustworthiness topic**: the authors' explicit hedging of the "aging" conclusion and their disclosure of unadjusted tests are good examples for claim-to-evidence checks of "does the wording match the evidence strength?".
