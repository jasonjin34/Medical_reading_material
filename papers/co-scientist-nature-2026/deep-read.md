## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Google 的 Co-Scientist 是一个基于 Gemini 2.0 的多智能体「结构化科学思考引擎」:Supervisor 在异步任务队列里调度 Generation、Reflection、Ranking、Proximity、Evolution、Meta-review 六类 agent,以「生成–辩论–进化」加 Elo 锦标赛不断扩展 test-time compute 来自我改进假设;在 AML 药物重定位(KIRA6 对 KG-1a 的 IC50 10 nM,较非 AML 对照有 18 倍窗口)、肝纤维化表观靶点(人肝类器官中 2/3 有效)与细菌 cf-PICI 机制(2 天内独立重现未发表发现)三个场景做了湿实验验证。
<!-- EN --> Google's Co-Scientist is a Gemini 2.0–based multi-agent "structured scientific thinking engine": a Supervisor schedules six agent types — Generation, Reflection, Ranking, Proximity, Evolution, Meta-review — over an asynchronous task queue, self-improving hypotheses through "generate–debate–evolve" plus an Elo tournament as test-time compute scales. It is wet-lab validated in three settings: AML drug repurposing (KIRA6 IC50 10 nM in KG-1a, an 18-fold window over a non-AML control), liver-fibrosis epigenetic targets (2 of 3 active in human hepatic organoids) and the bacterial cf-PICI mechanism (an unpublished finding independently recapitulated in 2 days).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 科研面临「广度与深度」的两难:课题需要越来越深的专业知识,而突破又常来自跨学科的广博联系,文献爆炸让个人难以两者兼得。已有 LLM 工具多停留在文献总结与 deep research,而本文要回答:能否让 AI 系统**生成真正新颖、可检验的研究假设与实验方案**,并通过扩展推理时计算让假设质量持续提升,最终经得起湿实验检验?
<!-- EN --> Research faces a breadth–depth conundrum: topics demand ever deeper expertise while breakthroughs often come from broad cross-disciplinary links, and the literature explosion makes both hard for individuals. Existing LLM tools mostly stop at summarization and deep research; the question here is whether an AI system can **generate genuinely novel, testable hypotheses and protocols**, improve them steadily by scaling inference-time compute, and survive wet-lab tests.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **四个组件**:自然语言交互界面(科学家设定目标、约束、反馈,也可提交自己的假设)、异步任务框架(Supervisor 管理队列并分配资源)、专门 agent、持久化 context memory(长程推理与故障重启)。研究目标先被解析为 research plan configuration(偏好、约束、评价标准);默认标准为与目标一致、合理性、新颖性、可检验性、安全性。**六类 agent**:Generation(网页检索文献、模拟专家辩论、迭代识别可检验的中间假设、按 meta-review 反馈扩展搜索方向);Reflection(扮演同行评审——初审不用工具快速淘汰、全审带检索、**deep verification 把假设拆成假设前提与子前提逐条独立验证**、observation review 看能否解释长尾实验观察、simulation review 逐步模拟机制或实验、tournament review 吸收锦标赛中的复发问题);Ranking(Elo 锦标赛,新假设初始 1200 分,头部假设用多轮科学辩论两两比较以减轻顺序偏差,低排名用单轮比较;借助 Proximity 优先比较相似假设);Proximity(构建假设相似图,去重与聚类);Evolution(基于检索补强、改进可行性、组合、简化、发散思考,**只生成新假设不覆盖旧假设**,新假设须重新参赛);Meta-review(汇总所有评审与辩论中的共性问题,作为反馈追加进其他 agent 的提示——无需反向传播的「学习」,并最终生成研究综述)。架构与底座模型无关,本文用 Gemini 2.0。
<!-- EN --> **Four components**: a natural-language interface (scientists set goals, constraints, feedback, or submit their own hypotheses), an asynchronous task framework (the Supervisor manages the queue and allocates resources), specialized agents, and a persistent context memory (long-horizon reasoning and restart on failure). The research goal is parsed into a research-plan configuration (preferences, constraints, evaluation criteria); default criteria are alignment with the goal, plausibility, novelty, testability and safety. **Six agents**: Generation (web literature search, simulated expert debate, iterative identification of testable intermediate assumptions, research expansion guided by meta-review feedback); Reflection (a simulated peer reviewer — a tool-free initial review to discard quickly, a full review with search, a **deep verification review that decomposes a hypothesis into assumptions and sub-assumptions and evaluates each independently**, an observation review testing whether it explains long-tail observations, a simulation review stepping through the mechanism or experiment, and a tournament review absorbing recurring issues); Ranking (an Elo tournament, new hypotheses start at 1,200; top hypotheses are compared pairwise via multi-turn scientific debate to reduce ordering bias, lower-ranked ones via single-turn comparison; similar hypotheses are matched preferentially via Proximity); Proximity (a similarity graph for de-duplication and clustering); Evolution (grounding enhancement, feasibility improvement, combination, simplification, out-of-box thinking — **it creates new hypotheses rather than overwriting old ones**, which must re-enter the tournament); Meta-review (synthesizes recurring patterns across reviews and debates into feedback appended to other agents' prompts — "learning" without back-propagation — and writes the final research overview). The architecture is model-agnostic; Gemini 2.0 is used here.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **自动评测**:截至 2025-02-03 输入系统的 203 个研究目标(以生物医学为主,也有数学、物理),按生成时间分 10 个桶看 Elo 变化;其中 15 个由 7 位生物医学专家(博士后或教职)策划的困难目标,与专家「最佳猜测」及 Gemini 2.0 Pro / Flash Thinking、OpenAI o1、o3-mini-high、DeepSeek R1 同场比 Elo;11 个目标做专家盲评。**湿实验**:AML——从 2,300 个已批准药物 × 34 种癌症中检索重定位候选,4 个 AML 细胞系(MOLM-13、KG-1a、HL-60、NOMO-1)+ 非 AML 淋巴母细胞系 TK6;肝纤维化——人肝类器官 + 活细胞成像;AMR——cf-PICI 宿主范围机制。
<!-- EN --> **Automated evaluation**: 203 research goals entered by 3 Feb 2025 (mostly biomedicine, also mathematics and physics), split into 10 temporal buckets to track Elo; a subset of 15 hard goals curated by seven biomedical experts (postdocs/faculty), compared in Elo against the experts' "best guess" and Gemini 2.0 Pro / Flash Thinking, OpenAI o1, o3-mini-high and DeepSeek R1; blinded expert evaluation on 11 goals. **Wet lab**: AML — repurposing candidates from 2,300 approved drugs across 34 cancer types, tested in four AML lines (MOLM-13, KG-1a, HL-60, NOMO-1) plus the non-AML lymphoblastoid line TK6; liver fibrosis — human hepatic organoids with live-cell imaging; AMR — the host-range mechanism of cf-PICIs.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **test-time compute 扩展**:203 个目标上,最佳 Elo 与 top-10 平均 Elo 随计算时间持续上升,未见饱和;15 个困难目标上 Co-Scientist 最终明显超过其他前沿 LLM/推理模型和专家最佳猜测(o3-mini-high、DeepSeek R1 以更少计算取得有竞争力的结果);从专家最佳猜测出发迭代,也能把它改进到超过自生成解的 Elo(初步结论)。 <!-- EN --> **Test-time compute scaling**: across 203 goals, best and top-10 mean Elo rise steadily with compute, with no saturation; on the 15 hard goals Co-Scientist eventually clearly surpasses other frontier LLMs/reasoning models and the experts' best guesses (o3-mini-high and DeepSeek R1 are competitive at far lower compute); seeded with expert best guesses it improves them beyond its self-generated solutions in Elo (preliminary).
- <!-- ZH --> **专家盲评**(11 个目标):Co-Scientist 平均偏好排名 2.36(1 最好、4 最差),新颖性 3.64/5、影响力 3.09/5,均为最高;4 个 LLM 评委也都最偏好其输出。作者强调这些是主观评估、样本很小。 <!-- EN --> **Blinded expert evaluation** (11 goals): Co-Scientist has the best mean preference rank, 2.36 (1 best, 4 worst), and the highest novelty 3.64/5 and impact 3.09/5; all four LLM judges also prefer its outputs. The authors stress these are subjective and small-scale.
- <!-- ZH --> **消融**:给 Reflection 接检索工具能有效防止「看似新颖但不合理」的幻觉假设;Ranking 用科学辩论提示显著改善排序并降低位置偏差;Evolution 的迭代改进显著提升质量。 <!-- EN --> **Ablations**: giving Reflection search tools effectively prevents hallucinated "novel but implausible" hypotheses; a scientific-debate prompt in Ranking substantially improves ranking and reduces positional bias; Evolution's iterative refinement substantially boosts quality.
- <!-- ZH --> **AML 重定位(专家参与)**:30 个高排名候选交给肿瘤学家,选出 5 个(binimetinib、pacritinib、cerivastatin、pravastatin、富马酸二甲酯),其中 binimetinib、pacritinib、cerivastatin 抑制活力;binimetinib 在除 NOMO-1 外的 AML 系 IC50 低至 2 nM,在 TK6 中高得多。 <!-- EN --> **AML repurposing (expert-in-the-loop)**: 30 top-ranked candidates went to oncologists, who picked five (binimetinib, pacritinib, cerivastatin, pravastatin, dimethyl fumarate); binimetinib, pacritinib and cerivastatin inhibited viability; binimetinib reached IC50 as low as 2 nM in AML lines except NOMO-1, and was much higher in TK6.
- <!-- ZH --> **AML 自主单药(无 DepMap、无专家反馈,要求无既往临床前证据)**:专家从排名中选 3 个(nanvuranlat、KIRA6、leflunomide);IRE1α 抑制剂 KIRA6 在 KG-1a IC50 10 nM、NOMO-1 144 nM、HL-60 870 nM、MOLM-13 1,750 nM,TK6 180 nM——KG-1a 与 TK6 之间约 18 倍;另两个药效果有限。系统自评 KIRA6 新颖性为「中等」(IRE1α 在 AML 已有研究,但该药没有)。 <!-- EN --> **Autonomous AML single agents (no DepMap, no expert feedback, no prior preclinical evidence required)**: experts took three from the ranking (nanvuranlat, KIRA6, leflunomide); the IRE1α inhibitor KIRA6 had IC50 10 nM in KG-1a, 144 nM in NOMO-1, 870 nM in HL-60, 1,750 nM in MOLM-13 and 180 nM in TK6 — ~18-fold between KG-1a and TK6; the other two had limited effect. The system rated KIRA6's novelty "moderate" (IRE1α in AML is known, this drug is not).
- <!-- ZH --> **联合用药**:7 个组合在 MOLM-13 中多为协同(如 JNJ-64619178 + selinexor;JQ1 + olaparib + MSA2),在 TP53 突变的 KG-1a 中协同与拮抗混杂。 <!-- EN --> **Combinations**: seven proposed combinations were mostly synergistic in MOLM-13 (e.g. JNJ-64619178 + selinexor; JQ1 + olaparib + MSA2) but mixed synergy/antagonism in TP53-mutant KG-1a.
- <!-- ZH --> **肝纤维化**:专家从排名中选 3 个表观修饰靶点及对应药物,其中 2 个在人肝类器官中有显著抗纤维化活性且无细胞毒性;其一 vorinostat 已获 FDA 批准(另一癌症适应症)。**AMR**:仅凭少量背景,系统的最高排名假设是「cf-PICI 与多种噬菌体尾部相互作用以扩大宿主范围」,与同期未发表的实验发现一致,用时 2 天。 <!-- EN --> **Liver fibrosis**: experts picked three epigenetic-modifier targets and drugs from the ranking; two showed significant anti-fibrotic activity in human hepatic organoids without toxicity, one being vorinostat, FDA-approved for a cancer indication. **AMR**: from minimal background, the top-ranked hypothesis — cf-PICIs interact with diverse phage tails to broaden host range — matched co-timed unpublished experimental findings, in 2 days.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把 test-time compute 扩展范式系统地用于科学假设生成:异步多 agent + 自我对弈辩论 + Elo 锦标赛 + 进化,质量随计算持续上升。 <!-- EN --> Brings the test-time-compute scaling paradigm to scientific hypothesis generation: asynchronous multi-agent + self-play debate + Elo tournament + evolution, with quality rising steadily with compute.
- <!-- ZH --> Reflection 的多种评审模式,尤其 **deep verification(把假设拆成前提与子前提逐条验证)**,是 claim 分解式验证的一个具体工程实现。 <!-- EN --> Reflection's multiple review modes — especially **deep verification (decompose into assumptions and sub-assumptions and check each)** — a concrete engineering form of decomposition-based claim verification.
- <!-- ZH --> Meta-review 把评审中的共性问题追加进提示,实现无需微调的跨轮「学习」。 <!-- EN --> Meta-review appends recurring critique patterns to prompts, achieving cross-round "learning" without fine-tuning.
- <!-- ZH --> 三个复杂度递增的生物医学场景都做了端到端湿实验验证,并保持 scientist-in-the-loop。 <!-- EN --> End-to-end wet-lab validation in three biomedical settings of increasing complexity, with scientists kept in the loop.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **评价主要靠 Elo 自评**,Elo 并非客观真值;专家盲评只有 11 个目标、偏好与评分主观;作者明说需要更客观、自动化的评价指标。 <!-- EN --> **Evaluation leans on self-computed Elo**, which is not ground truth; the blinded expert evaluation covers only 11 goals with subjective ratings; the authors themselves call for more objective, automated metrics.
- <!-- ZH --> 湿实验验证是初步的:细胞系级别的可行性检查,样本少、由专家挑选候选(选择偏差),不等于体内疗效;作者也指出 binimetinib 在既往 II 期中单药疗效有限。 <!-- EN --> Wet-lab validation is preliminary: cell-line viability checks with few candidates hand-picked by experts (selection bias), not in vivo efficacy; the authors note binimetinib showed limited monotherapy efficacy in a prior phase II trial.
- <!-- ZH --> 只能访问开放获取文献,缺乏负结果;来源文献质量参差,可能传播错误或不可复现的发现;作者把「能把 claim 追溯到来源中具体图表或数据的 provenance 能力」列为关键未来方向。 <!-- EN --> Access only to open-access literature and no negative results; mixed-quality sources risk propagating erroneous or irreproducible findings; the authors list **provenance that traces claims to specific figures or data in a source** as a key future direction.
- <!-- ZH --> 继承底座模型的幻觉与事实性问题;不能直接对公共数据库与多模态数据做推理和数据分析(列为未来工作)。 <!-- EN --> Inherits base-model hallucination and factuality issues; cannot yet reason directly over public databases and multimodal data or do data analysis (future work).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 在 detect → model → revert 里,Co-Scientist 对应的是 **revert 的「提名」环节**:给定疾病与约束,提出哪些靶点/药物可能把病理表型拉回正常,并由湿实验检验——其肝纤维化案例(在人肝类器官里找抗纤维化的表观靶点)几乎就是「肾间质纤维化逆转靶点」的同构问题。但它只吃文献、不吃数据,所以无法利用我们在 detect/model 阶段得到的异常区域与虚拟组织扰动结果;一条自然的接口是把我们的空间组学异常与扰动预测作为「research goal 的上下文 + Reflection 的 observation review 证据」输入。对 **Plan B(可信 agent)** 这篇是关键对照:**(1) Elo 自评 ≠ 正确性**——系统用自己的锦标赛评价自己的假设,正是 Plan B 要用外部、校准过的 verifier 取代的环节;**(2) deep verification 的「前提分解 + 逐条验证」** 与我们的 claim-to-evidence 验证同构,但它验证的对象是文献前提,我们要验证的是「agent 对肾脏组学数据的分析结论」是否被数据与代码支撑;**(3) 作者自陈的 provenance 缺口**(把 claim 追溯到来源中的具体图表/数据)正是 Plan B 的出发点;**(4) 新颖性自评**(KIRA6「中等新颖」)是一个可检验的校准问题——系统的新颖性/置信度打分是否与专家判断一致,可纳入 calibrated verifier 的评估。另外,所有湿实验候选都经专家挑选,意味着论文报告的「命中率」混入了人类筛选,评估 agent 时要把人机贡献拆开。
<!-- EN --> Within detect → model → revert, Co-Scientist maps to the **nomination step of revert**: given a disease and constraints, propose targets/drugs that could pull the pathological phenotype back to normal, then test in the wet lab — its liver-fibrosis case (anti-fibrotic epigenetic targets in human hepatic organoids) is nearly isomorphic to "targets that reverse kidney interstitial fibrosis." But it consumes literature, not data, so it cannot use the anomalous regions and virtual-tissue perturbation results from our detect/model stages; a natural interface is to feed our spatial-omics anomalies and perturbation predictions in as research-goal context and as evidence for Reflection's observation review. For **Plan B (trustworthy agents)** it is a key comparator: **(1) self-scored Elo ≠ correctness** — the system grades its own hypotheses in its own tournament, exactly the step Plan B wants to replace with an external, calibrated verifier; **(2) deep verification's "decompose into assumptions + check each"** is isomorphic to our claim-to-evidence verification, except it checks literature premises while we check whether an agent's conclusions about kidney-omics data are supported by the data and code; **(3) the provenance gap the authors name** (tracing claims to specific figures/data) is Plan B's starting point; **(4) its novelty self-rating** (KIRA6 "moderate") is a testable calibration question — do the system's novelty/confidence scores agree with experts? — suitable for evaluating a calibrated verifier. Also, every wet-lab candidate was expert-filtered, so reported "hit rates" mix in human selection; agent evaluation must separate human and machine contributions.
