## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 用 donor/recipient 配对 WES 生成 SNV 参考(.vcf),对 5 个肾移植活检的 10x 5′ scRNA-seq(81,139 个细胞)跑 Demuxlet,逐细胞判定供体/受体来源,发现供体巨噬细胞在非排斥移植肾中可持续存在、且与受体巨噬细胞转录程序不同。
<!-- EN --> Donor/recipient paired whole-exome sequencing provides an SNV reference (.vcf); Demuxlet on 10x 5′ scRNA-seq of five kidney-transplant biopsies (81,139 cells) assigns each cell to donor or recipient, showing donor macrophages persist in non-rejecting grafts with transcriptional programs distinct from recipient macrophages.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 实体器官移植中通常假设供体来源免疫细胞术后逐渐消失,但它们是否在肾移植物中持续存在、是否参与排斥一直未知,原因之一是缺乏区分供/受体细胞的手段(以往只能靠性别错配时的 Y 染色体)。本文提出用表达的 SNV + scRNA-seq 在单细胞分辨率上界定白细胞嵌合(leukocyte chimerism)。
<!-- EN --> Donor-derived immune cells in solid-organ transplants are assumed to decline after surgery, but whether they persist in kidney allografts or contribute to rejection was unknown, partly because there was no good way to tell donor from recipient cells (previously Y-chromosome tracking in sex-mismatched pairs). The paper uses expressed SNVs + scRNA-seq to define leukocyte chimerism at single-cell resolution.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> (1) 活检新鲜解离(0.25% trypsin + DNase I,约 30 min,活率约 90%);每个活检 2 条 10x Chromium lane(目标 10,000 细胞/lane),**5′ 试剂盒** + T 细胞 VDJ 文库;测序深度 50,000 reads/cell(GEX)。(2) Cell Ranger 3.0 → Seurat v3;QC:200–2500 genes/cell、<25% 线粒体;高分辨率过聚类后,"含两种及以上 cell-type 标志基因的 cluster 作为 doublet cluster 删除",低基因数 cluster 作为 debris 删除;Seurat v3 integration。(3) 外周血 germline DNA 做 WES(目标 50×),BWA-MEM → Picard MarkDuplicates → GATK BQSR → GATK best-practice 过滤常见变异(AF>0.05);每对 donor+recipient 合并 .vcf(平均 120,896 个编码 SNV)。(4) Demuxlet(默认参数,α=0、0.5、1)输入合并 .vcf + Cell Ranger BAM,以 BEST 列标注每个细胞 donor/recipient。(5) 差异表达(Seurat FindMarkers)+ ToppFun 通路;Spearman 相关;VDJ Loupe 做 T 细胞克隆。
<!-- EN --> (1) Fresh dissociation (0.25% trypsin + DNase I, ~30 min, ~90% viability); two 10x Chromium lanes per biopsy (target 10,000 cells/lane), **5′ kits** plus T-cell VDJ libraries; 50,000 reads/cell for gene expression. (2) Cell Ranger 3.0 → Seurat v3; QC 200–2500 genes/cell, <25% mitochondrial; over-clustering, then "clusters containing two or more different cell type–defining markers were removed as doublet clusters" and low-gene clusters removed as debris; Seurat v3 integration. (3) WES of peripheral-blood germline DNA (target 50×), BWA-MEM → Picard → GATK BQSR → GATK best-practice filtering of common variants (AF > 0.05); one combined donor+recipient .vcf per pair (mean 120,896 coding SNVs). (4) Demuxlet (default parameters, α = 0, 0.5, 1) on the combined .vcf + Cell Ranger BAM; each cell labelled donor/recipient from the BEST column. (5) DE (Seurat FindMarkers) + ToppFun pathways, Spearman correlations, VDJ Loupe for T-cell clones.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 5 个肾移植指征活检(Washington University):3 例急性抗体介导排斥(ABMR,均有 DSA)+ 2 例非排斥 AKI;移植后 5 天至 7 年。另有一个"独立数据集":同一 ABMR 患者治疗前/治疗后 1 个月的连续活检(Fig 3G,GEO 中标为 **3′** 试剂盒,不在 81,139 细胞主数据集里)。GEO **GSE145927**:7 个 scRNA 样本(Biopsy1_Day28、Biopsy2_Day2542、Biopsy3_Day11、Biopsy4_Day5、Biopsy5_Day232 为 5′;Biopsy6_Pre-treatment、Biopsy7_Post-treatment 为 3′)+ 10 个 WES 样本(5 对 donor/recipient,PBMC,IDT xGen Exome v1,NovaSeq6000)。处理后文件:每个样本一个 `*rawcounts.csv.gz`(基因 × 细胞 UMI 计数,**只含 QC 后细胞**,如 Day11 文件 = 5,395 个细胞 = metadata 中 biopsy65)+ `GSE145927_MGI.metadata.csv.gz`(81,139 个细胞:biopsy、nCount/nFeature、percent.mt、cluster、`SNV_GT`(donor/recipient)、`stim`(rejn/no_rejn)、细胞类型)。原始 FASTQ(scRNA 与 WES)在 SRA **SRP250734 / PRJNA608747**,runinfo 显示为公开下载(非 dbGaP);GEO 上 WES 样本无处理后文件(无 VCF)。
<!-- EN --> Five indication kidney-transplant biopsies (Washington University): 3 acute antibody-mediated rejection (ABMR, all DSA-positive) + 2 non-rejection AKI; 5 days to 7 years post-transplant. Plus an "independent data set": serial pre-/1-month post-treatment biopsies from one ABMR patient (Fig 3G; **3′** kits per GEO; not part of the 81,139-cell set). GEO **GSE145927**: 7 scRNA samples (Biopsy1_Day28, Biopsy2_Day2542, Biopsy3_Day11, Biopsy4_Day5, Biopsy5_Day232 = 5′; Biopsy6_Pre-treatment, Biopsy7_Post-treatment = 3′) + 10 WES samples (5 donor/recipient pairs, PBMC, IDT xGen Exome v1, NovaSeq 6000). Processed files: one `*rawcounts.csv.gz` per sample (gene × cell UMI counts, **post-QC cells only**; e.g. the Day11 file has 5,395 cells = biopsy65 in the metadata) + `GSE145927_MGI.metadata.csv.gz` (81,139 cells: biopsy, nCount/nFeature, percent.mt, clusters, `SNV_GT` donor/recipient, `stim` rejn/no_rejn, cell type). Raw FASTQs (scRNA and WES) are in SRA **SRP250734 / PRJNA608747** and the run table lists public download paths (not dbGaP); the GEO WES samples carry no processed files (no VCF).

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> - 81,139 个细胞通过 QC(平均 1,124 genes / 2,497 transcripts);8 个肾细胞 cluster + 2 个免疫 cluster;巨噬细胞 5,027、淋巴细胞 3,647(T 3,067、B 190、浆细胞 231;另亚聚类出肥大细胞 111)。
- 每个细胞用到的 SNV 中位数 71–231,最少 17;作者称整体约 90% 准确、下四分位以上细胞接近 100%。
- "Despite manual filtering and removal of doublet cells … the Demuxlet tool identified 3%–10.5% of cells as doublets"(手工去 doublet 之后 Demuxlet 仍判出 3–10.5% doublet)。
- 巨噬细胞:受体 3,798 / 供体 1,229;淋巴细胞:受体 1,528 / 供体 2,119。非排斥样本供/受体巨噬细胞约各半,排斥样本受体主导(如 Day 11 排斥活检受体 97%;Day 28 非排斥活检供体 35.8%)。淋巴细胞的供体比例随时间下降(Day 232 时很少)。
- 受体巨噬细胞:IL-6、IFN、FCγR 通路,CD16/CD68;供体巨噬细胞:补体(C1QA/B/C)、抗原呈递、F13A1、CD14。受体 T 细胞与排斥 T 细胞相关(Spearman 0.8),表达 NKG7/GNLY/GZMB/PRF1;供体 T 细胞与非排斥 T 细胞相关(1.0),富集氧化磷酸化(静息)。
- VDJ:仅 120/3,115 个 T 细胞有完整 VDJ(供体 34,全部来自 Day 11 排斥样本;受体 86)。
<!-- EN --> - 81,139 cells passed QC (mean 1,124 genes / 2,497 transcripts); 8 kidney clusters + 2 immune clusters; 5,027 macrophages and 3,647 lymphocytes (T 3,067, B 190, plasma 231; plus 111 mast cells on subclustering).
- Median SNVs per cell 71–231, minimum 17; authors report ~90% accuracy overall and near 100% above the lower quartile.
- "Despite manual filtering and removal of doublet cells … the Demuxlet tool identified 3%–10.5% of cells as doublets."
- Macrophages: 3,798 recipient / 1,229 donor; lymphocytes: 1,528 recipient / 2,119 donor. Non-rejecting samples had roughly equal donor/recipient macrophages; rejecting samples were recipient-dominated (e.g. 97% recipient at day 11 rejection; 35.8% donor at day 28 non-rejection). Donor lymphocyte share fell with time (very few by day 232).
- Recipient macrophages: IL-6, IFN, FCγR pathways, CD16/CD68; donor macrophages: complement (C1QA/B/C), antigen presentation, F13A1, CD14. Recipient T cells correlated with rejecting T cells (Spearman 0.8; NKG7/GNLY/GZMB/PRF1); donor T cells with non-rejecting T cells (1.0), enriched for oxidative phosphorylation (quiescent).
- VDJ: only 120/3,115 T cells had complete VDJ (34 donor, all from the day-11 rejection sample; 86 recipient).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 样本极小(5 个活检、3 vs 2),作者自称 proof-of-concept;排斥与时间点混杂。 <!-- EN --> Very small (5 biopsies, 3 vs 2); the authors call it proof-of-concept; rejection status is confounded with time post-transplant.
- <!-- ZH --> 正文没有给出 Demuxlet 对上皮/内皮/基质细胞的来源比例,也没有讨论 ambient RNA 或 soup 污染;"所有肾实质细胞均为供体来源"只是生物学预期,文中未逐项报告(补充材料未能获取,未核实)。 <!-- EN --> The main text does not report Demuxlet origin fractions for epithelial/endothelial/stromal cells and says nothing about ambient RNA or soup contamination; "all parenchymal cells are donor-derived" is a biological expectation, not a reported result (supplement not retrieved; unverified).
- <!-- ZH --> Demuxlet 判定的 doublet 未在 GEO metadata 中提供(最终数据集只有 donor/recipient 标签);doublet 在 QC 中已被部分人工删除,因此公开矩阵不是"原始 doublet 分布"。 <!-- EN --> Demuxlet doublet calls are not in the GEO metadata (only donor/recipient labels in the final set); doublets were partly removed manually before, so the public matrices do not preserve the natural doublet distribution.
- <!-- ZH --> **公开 metadata 中的 `SNV_GT` 与正文不符**(我们自行统计):巨噬细胞 donor 4,715 / recipient 312(正文为 donor 1,229 / recipient 3,798),且每个活检内各细胞类型的 recipient 比例几乎相同(biopsy65:22.6–29.3%;biopsy62:3.6–5.6%),不像真实的细胞类型特异嵌合。可能是标签与条形码错配或含义不同——使用前必须从 SRA 重跑 demuxlet/vireo。 <!-- EN --> **The public metadata `SNV_GT` disagrees with the paper** (our own tabulation): macrophages donor 4,715 / recipient 312 (paper: donor 1,229 / recipient 3,798), and the recipient fraction is nearly flat across cell types within each biopsy (biopsy65: 22.6–29.3%; biopsy62: 3.6–5.6%), unlike genuine cell-type-specific chimerism. Possibly a barcode/label misjoin or a different meaning — re-run demuxlet/vireo from SRA before using it.
- <!-- ZH --> 正文称测序平台为 HiSeq,但 SRA runinfo 记为 NovaSeq 6000;小细节,但说明公开记录与正文不完全一致。 <!-- EN --> The text says HiSeq while the SRA run table says NovaSeq 6000 — minor, but another record/text mismatch.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 属于 Plan A 的 **detect** 环节中的"伪影 ground truth"数据源。我们的 benchmark 要检验 anomaly detector 能否把疾病(排斥/AKI)与测量伪影(ambient RNA、doublet)分开,并在 donor 层面控制误报。本数据集的价值:(1) **doublet 真值**:同一 lane 混有供体与受体两种基因型,demuxlet 能识别异基因型 doublet(donor+recipient),论文报告 3–10.5%;但同基因型 doublet(donor+donor,在以供体上皮为主的组织里应占多数)无法被基因型识别,所以只是部分真值,需要用异基因型比例按 Demuxlet 原文的公式外推。(2) **ambient 真值(我们自己的推断,论文未提及)**:肾实质上皮细胞理论上全为供体来源,其中检出的受体特异等位基因 reads 可以作为来自受体浸润免疫细胞的 ambient/soup 污染的直接估计——与 Janssen 2023(Genome Biology)用小鼠品系混合估计 background noise 的逻辑相同。论文没有提出或验证这一点。(3) 有排斥 vs 非排斥 = 真实"疾病"信号,可作为对照。限制:只有 5 个 donor 对,donor 层面误报控制统计功效很弱;要做 (1)(2) 必须从 SRA 下载 BAM/FASTQ(单个 scRNA run 约 30–65 GB)+ WES 自行 call 变异,GEO 处理后文件不够。
<!-- EN --> Sits in Plan A's **detect** stage as a source of *artifact ground truth*. Our benchmark asks whether anomaly detectors separate disease (rejection/AKI) from measurement artifacts (ambient RNA, doublets) with donor-level false-alarm control. Value here: (1) **Doublet ground truth**: each lane mixes two genotypes, so demuxlet can flag heterotypic-genotype (donor+recipient) doublets — 3–10.5% reported — but same-genotype (donor+donor) doublets, likely the majority in donor-epithelium-dominated tissue, are invisible to genotype; it is partial truth that needs extrapolation from the cross-genotype rate (as in the Demuxlet paper). (2) **Ambient ground truth (our own inference; the paper does not say this)**: kidney parenchymal epithelium should be donor-only, so recipient-specific allele reads in epithelial barcodes directly estimate ambient/soup contamination from infiltrating recipient immune cells — the same logic as Janssen 2023's mouse-strain mixing estimate of background noise. The paper neither proposes nor validates this. (3) Rejection vs non-rejection provides a real disease signal as a contrast. Caveats: only 5 donor pairs, so donor-level false-alarm control has little power; (1) and (2) require SRA BAM/FASTQ (≈30–65 GB per scRNA run) plus our own WES variant calling — the GEO processed files are not enough.
