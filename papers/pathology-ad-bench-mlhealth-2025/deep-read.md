## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Vanderbilt 的 Huo 组(HRLB)把 23 种通用 anomaly detection 方法(重建、特征分布、蒸馏、normalizing flow、合成异常五大类)放到真实病理(Camelyon16、Crohn 结肠、小鼠肾小球)+ 受控合成病理 + MVTec 工业数据上统一评测,系统考察**图像尺度、异常模式「反转」、以及没有异常样本时如何选 epoch** 三个常被忽略的因素;结论是没有通吃的方法,特征分布类整体最好,而真实病理比工业数据难得多(平均 ROC-AUC 约 0.645 对 0.888)。
<!-- EN --> Yuankai Huo's group (HRLB, Vanderbilt) benchmarks 23 generic anomaly-detection methods (reconstruction, feature-distribution, distillation, normalizing-flow and synthetic-anomaly families) under one protocol on real pathology (Camelyon16, Crohn's colon, mouse glomeruli), controlled synthetic pathology and MVTec industrial data, and systematically studies three under-examined factors — **image scale, "reversed" anomaly patterns, and how to pick a training epoch when no anomalies are available**. No method wins everywhere; feature-distribution methods are best overall; and real pathology is far harder than industrial data (mean ROC-AUC ≈ 0.645 vs 0.888).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 病理图像天然适合「只用正常数据训练」的 anomaly detection:正常组织易得、病灶小而多变、边界模糊且很少有专家标注,还可能是罕见或全新的病变;在临床前毒理里也常用来发现药物引起的组织改变。但现有 AD 算法多为工业缺陷检测设计,病理图像有三大特殊性——**多尺度**(细胞与组织两级)、**染色差异**、**重复纹理且正常/异常边界模糊**——通用方法在这里表现如何并不清楚。此外,已有病理 AD 工作基本用固定 epoch 数训练,从未讨论在验证集也没有异常样本的情况下如何做 model selection。
<!-- EN --> Pathology is a natural fit for normal-only anomaly detection: normal tissue is abundant, lesions are small, variable, ill-bounded and rarely annotated, and may be rare or entirely novel; AD is also used in preclinical toxicology to flag drug-induced tissue change. Yet most AD algorithms were built for industrial defect inspection, and pathology has three peculiarities — **multi-scale** structure (cell and tissue), **stain variability**, and **repetitive patterns with blurry normal/abnormal boundaries** — so how generic methods fare is unclear. Moreover, prior pathology-AD work trains for a fixed number of epochs and never discusses model selection when the validation set itself has no anomalies.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **方法池**:23 个方法分五族——重建类(GANomaly、SkipGanomaly、DAE、FAE、DFR、HTAES、IGD、UniAD、DRAEM 等)、特征分布类(PatchCore、PaDiM、PANDA、CFA、DFKDE、DFM)、蒸馏类(STFPM、Reverse Distillation、Revisit Distillation、ReContrast、EfficientAD)、normalizing flow(FastFlow、CFLOW-AD、CS-Flow)、合成异常(CutPaste、DRAEM)。代码分别取自 anomalib、UPD_study 及各官方仓库(注:主结果表实际列出 22 行方法)。**统一设置**:输入 256×256,200 epoch,batch 16,有预训练权重就加载,ImageNet 归一化,仅水平翻转增强,不做逐数据集调参。需要 object/patient 级结果时取**前 20% 最高 anomaly score 的均值**聚合;指标为 ROC-AUC(另报 PR-AUC)。**三组实验**:(1) **尺度**——同一区域分别以大图直接下采样和切成小图再聚合两种方式预测;(2) **无偏 epoch 选择**——四种策略:①逐样本最大比值(Reiss et al.:各 checkpoint 的分数除以验证集正常样本平均分,取最大值)、②用含异常的完整验证集选 AUC 最高 epoch(视作上界)、③验证集正常样本 loss 最小、④最后一个 epoch;(3) **正常/异常反转**——在合成密度数据上交换「正常」与「异常」的定义重跑。
<!-- EN --> **Method pool**: 23 methods in five families — reconstruction (GANomaly, SkipGanomaly, DAE, FAE, DFR, HTAES, IGD, UniAD, DRAEM…), feature distribution (PatchCore, PaDiM, PANDA, CFA, DFKDE, DFM), distillation (STFPM, Reverse Distillation, Revisit Distillation, ReContrast, EfficientAD), normalizing flow (FastFlow, CFLOW-AD, CS-Flow) and synthetic anomaly (CutPaste, DRAEM), sourced from anomalib, UPD_study and official repos (note: the main results table lists 22 method rows). **Unified setup**: 256×256 inputs, 200 epochs, batch 16, pretrained weights when available, ImageNet normalization, horizontal-flip augmentation only, no per-dataset tuning. Object/patient-level scores aggregate the **mean of the top 20% patch scores**; metric is ROC-AUC (PR-AUC also reported). **Three experiments**: (1) **scale** — predict a region either from one downsampled large patch or from tiled small patches aggregated; (2) **unbiased epoch selection** — four strategies: ① sample-wise maximal ratio (Reiss et al.: each checkpoint's score normalized by the mean normal-validation score, take the max), ② best AUC on a complete validation set with anomalies (an upper bound), ③ minimum normal-validation loss, ④ last epoch; (3) **normal/abnormal reversal** — swap the definitions on the synthetic density data and rerun.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **真实病理(3 个)**:Camelyon16 乳腺淋巴结转移(40× 切 768×768;训练 5462 正常,验证 2150 正常 + 2169 肿瘤,测试 4000 正常 + 817 肿瘤);院内 Crohn 病结肠活检(16 例 CD + 53 例健康对照,20×,1812×1812 大块由病理医生标正常/异常,再切 512×512 小块);小鼠肾小球数据(40× H&E,中心裁 3072×3072 与 512×512,只有全部肾小球都正常的块才算正常;原文称其包含 "benign and malignant glomeruli of mice")。**合成病理(2 个)**:在 Unitopatho 的 56 张 2048×2048 正常块上叠加图案——圆 vs 十字(形态差异),以及 75% vs 25% 概率的圆点密度(密度差异),各做大小两个尺度。**工业(2 个)**:MVTec 的 Hazelnut(物体)与 Tile(纹理)作参照。训练集只含正常样本。
<!-- EN --> **Real pathology (3)**: Camelyon16 breast lymph-node metastasis (768×768 patches at 40×; train 5462 normal; val 2150 normal + 2169 tumour; test 4000 normal + 817 tumour); an in-house Crohn's disease colon biopsy set (16 CD patients + 53 healthy controls, 20×, 1812×1812 patches labelled normal/abnormal by pathologists, re-tiled to 512×512); a mouse glomerulus set (40× H&E, centre crops at 3072×3072 and 512×512; a patch is normal only if every glomerulus in it is normal; the paper describes it as "benign and malignant glomeruli of mice"). **Synthetic pathology (2)**: patterns overlaid on 56 normal 2048×2048 Unitopatho patches — circles vs crosses (morphology) and 75% vs 25% circle density (density), each at two scales. **Industrial (2)**: MVTec Hazelnut (object) and Tile (texture) as reference. Training sets contain only normal data.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **没有通吃的方法**:Reverse Distillation、SkipGanomaly、UniAD 等在某些数据集明显高于平均,在另一些几乎失效。 <!-- EN --> **No universal winner**: Reverse Distillation, SkipGanomaly, UniAD and others are well above average on some datasets and nearly fail on others.
- <!-- ZH --> **按方法族**(完整验证集选 epoch,object 级平均 ROC-AUC):特征分布 **0.692** > 蒸馏 **0.600** > 重建 **0.564**;重建类常把异常也重建得很好、反而把正常图重建模糊。 <!-- EN --> **By family** (complete-validation epoch selection, object-level mean ROC-AUC): feature distribution **0.692** > distillation **0.600** > reconstruction **0.564**; reconstruction methods often reconstruct anomalies well while blurring normal images.
- <!-- ZH --> **难度差距**:按数据集平均 ROC-AUC,工业 0.888、合成病理 0.636、真实病理 0.645——在工业数据上好用的方法不足以区分病理图像的正常与异常。真实病理上 PANDA 最稳(完整验证集策略平均 0.882;Camelyon16 0.943、结肠 2048 尺度 0.990)。 <!-- EN --> **Difficulty gap**: dataset-averaged ROC-AUC is 0.888 on industrial, 0.636 on synthetic pathology and 0.645 on real pathology — methods that work on industrial data do not suffice for pathology. PANDA is the most robust on real pathology (0.882 mean under complete-validation selection; 0.943 on Camelyon16, 0.990 on colon at 2048).
- <!-- ZH --> **epoch 选择**:总体排序 完整验证集 > 逐样本 > 最小 loss > 最后 epoch;「最后 epoch」在 20 个数据集设置中有 19 个最差。两种无偏策略(逐样本、最小 loss)都不需要异常样本,逐样本略好,但要保存 N 个 checkpoint、推理成本 N 倍。 <!-- EN --> **Epoch selection**: overall ranking complete-val > sample-wise > min-loss > last; "last epoch" is worst in 19 of 20 dataset settings. Both unbiased strategies (sample-wise, min-loss) need no anomalies; sample-wise is slightly better but stores N checkpoints and costs N× inference.
- <!-- ZH --> **尺度**:肾小球小尺度更好(0.650 vs 0.611,小块能看清球内细节);结肠大尺度更好(0.767 vs 0.640,需要隐窝结构与炎症的上下文);合成数据同理——形态类异常小尺度好,密度类异常大尺度好。网络内部的多尺度特征并不能替代输入尺度的选择。 <!-- EN --> **Scale**: glomeruli favour the small scale (0.650 vs 0.611; small patches resolve intraglomerular detail); colon favours the large scale (0.767 vs 0.640; crypt architecture and inflammation need context); the synthetic data agree — morphology anomalies prefer small scale, density anomalies large scale. Internal multi-scale features do not replace choosing the input scale.
- <!-- ZH --> **反转**:同一组测试图,「高密度为正常」平均 0.792,「低密度为正常」只有 0.551——**正常模式本身的可学习性**决定了 AD 难度。 <!-- EN --> **Reversal**: on the same test images, "high density = normal" averages 0.792 while "low density = normal" reaches only 0.551 — **how learnable the normal pattern is** governs AD difficulty.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 首个专门面向病理图像、覆盖五大方法族 23 个方法的统一 AD 基准,并附病理 AD 文献综述。 <!-- EN --> The first unified AD benchmark dedicated to pathology covering 23 methods across five families, plus a review of pathology-AD literature.
- <!-- ZH --> 把「没有异常样本时如何选 epoch」作为显式实验变量——这是此前几乎没人系统讨论的实际问题。 <!-- EN --> Makes "how to choose an epoch without anomalies" an explicit experimental variable — a practical problem almost nobody had examined systematically.
- <!-- ZH --> 受控合成病理数据(形态 vs 密度、两个尺度、可反转定义),能把尺度、模式类型与正常模式难度这些因素拆开看。 <!-- EN --> Controlled synthetic pathology (morphology vs density, two scales, reversible definitions) that disentangles scale, anomaly type and normal-pattern difficulty.
- <!-- ZH --> 面向临床的使用建议:多算法 ensemble 取一致高分区域、热图辅助、随异常样本积累逐步转向监督学习。 <!-- EN --> Clinically oriented advice: ensemble several detectors and flag consistently high-scoring regions, show heatmaps, and migrate toward supervised models as anomalies accumulate.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 全部用默认超参、不逐数据集调参,作者也承认方法间差异的原因因此难以解释。 <!-- EN --> All methods use default hyperparameters with no per-dataset tuning; the authors concede this leaves the causes of between-method differences unexplained.
- <!-- ZH --> 输入统一缩到 256×256,大尺度块的训练数量受限;且不包含任何病理 foundation model 特征(UNI、Virchow 等)作为 backbone,结论主要针对 ImageNet 时代的方法。 <!-- EN --> Inputs are uniformly resized to 256×256, limiting large-scale training patches; and no pathology foundation-model features (UNI, Virchow, …) are tested as backbones, so the conclusions mainly concern ImageNet-era methods.
- <!-- ZH --> 真实数据只有三个,其中两个是院内数据(需 Vanderbilt DUA),肾小球数据是小鼠;只做 patch/object 级分类,没有像素级定位评测。 <!-- EN --> Only three real datasets, two in-house (Vanderbilt DUA required), and the glomerulus set is murine; only patch/object-level classification, no pixel-level localization evaluation.
- <!-- ZH --> 「完整验证集」策略需要与测试集同分布的异常样本,现实中拿不到,所以它只是上界。 <!-- EN --> The "complete validation" strategy needs anomalies distributed like the test set — unavailable in practice — so it is only an upper bound.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇是 detect 一环在病理图像上的**基准方法学教科书**,而且直接来自做肾脏病理的组(经费含 KPMP Glue Grant,数据含肾小球)。对 **伪影注入课题** 有四点可直接借用:**(1) 无偏 epoch 选择**——肾脏异常检测模型训练只用正常/参考组织,验证集同样没有专家确认的异常,「逐样本最大比值」与「正常样本最小 loss」是可以写进 benchmark 协议的标准做法,「最后 epoch」应明确禁止;**(2) 尺度是一等变量**——肾小球病变要小尺度、间质纤维化/炎症要大尺度,KidneyAnomalyBench 应按解剖单元(肾小球、小管、间质)分别报告,而非一个全局 AUC;**(3) 反转实验揭示「正常的可学习性」**——肾脏正常组织本身异质(皮质/髓质、不同节段小管),参考集的构造会直接决定检出率,这一点在设计 healthy reference 时必须控制;**(4) 受控合成异常**——把这种「已知真值的合成扰动」迁移到 Xenium/snRNA 上(例如注入表达偏移或细胞密度变化),可以作为 benchmark 中带 ground truth 的一层。更重要的是它**没有回答的问题**:只用了 H&E 单模态,无法区分「染色/切片伪影」与「真实病变」,而伪影注入课题的核心假设正是用 H&E 与空间组学的**跨模态一致性**来做这个区分——这篇的单模态结果可以作为跨模态方法的对照基线。对 Agent 可信度课题的启示较间接:「没有通吃的方法 + 默认超参」提醒我们,agent 在跑 AD 分析时若只用一种方法、一个尺度、最后一个 epoch,其结论应被 verifier 标为低可信。
<!-- EN --> This is the **methodological benchmark textbook** for the detect stage on pathology images, and it comes straight from a kidney-pathology group (funding includes a KPMP Glue Grant; data include glomeruli). Four pieces transfer directly to **the artifact-injection topic (kidney-omics AD + KidneyAnomalyBench)**: **(1) unbiased epoch selection** — kidney anomaly models train only on normal/reference tissue and the validation set has no expert-confirmed anomalies either, so "sample-wise maximal ratio" and "minimum normal loss" are protocol-ready choices and "last epoch" should be explicitly disallowed; **(2) scale is a first-class variable** — glomerular lesions need small scale, interstitial fibrosis/inflammation large scale, so KidneyAnomalyBench should report per anatomical unit (glomerulus, tubule, interstitium) rather than one global AUC; **(3) the reversal experiment exposes "learnability of normal"** — normal kidney is itself heterogeneous (cortex/medulla, tubular segments), so how the reference set is built directly drives detection rates and must be controlled when designing healthy references; **(4) controlled synthetic anomalies** — porting "known-ground-truth synthetic perturbations" to Xenium/snRNA (e.g. injected expression shifts or cell-density changes) gives the benchmark a layer with ground truth. Most important is what it **does not answer**: it is H&E-only and cannot separate stain/sectioning artifacts from true lesions, which is exactly the artifact-injection topic's core hypothesis — use **cross-modal agreement** between H&E and spatial omics to make that call — so these single-modality results serve as the baseline a cross-modal method must beat. For the agent-trustworthiness topic the lesson is indirect: "no universal method + default hyperparameters" means an agent that runs one AD method at one scale with the last epoch should have its conclusions flagged low-confidence by a verifier.
