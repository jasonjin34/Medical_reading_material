## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 作者用 27 位供者、41 张乳腺癌与非小细胞肺癌切片(靶向 Breast/Lung/Custom IO 面板 + 5K PRIME 面板,配匹配 snRNA-seq 与 IHC)系统刻画 Xenium 的灵敏度、特异性和**转录本溢出(spillover)污染**,并提出 SPLIT:用 RCTD doublet 模式的细胞类型权重,把每个被污染细胞的计数按参照谱拆成「主」「次」两部分,只保留主细胞类型的纯化表达。
<!-- EN --> Using 41 breast-cancer and NSCLC sections from 27 donors (targeted Breast/Lung/Custom IO panels plus the 5K PRIME panel, with matched snRNA-seq and IHC), the authors systematically characterize Xenium sensitivity, specificity and **transcript-spillover contamination**, and propose SPLIT: take RCTD doublet-mode cell-type weights and split each contaminated cell's counts into "primary" and "secondary" parts according to reference profiles, keeping the purified primary profile.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> Xenium 被广泛使用,但其数据特性缺乏大规模刻画:已有比较研究只有少数患者或组织芯片,没人直接比较过靶向面板与 5K 面板的灵敏度/特异性。更关键的是,分割误差和转录本**在 x–y 平面内**溢出到邻近细胞,会制造「人工转录组双细胞」—— 这些并非单细胞测序意义上的物理双细胞,而是信号层面的混合;z 轴重叠只能解释一部分。现有校正方法(加权 NMF+CRF、resolVI、FastReseg、ProSeg 等)要么依赖启发式,要么不可解释,且只在少量样本上验证过。
<!-- EN --> Xenium is widely adopted, yet its data properties lack large-scale characterization: prior comparisons covered few patients or tissue microarrays, and none directly compared targeted panels with the 5K panel for sensitivity/specificity. More importantly, segmentation errors and **in-plane (x–y)** spillover of transcripts into neighbours create "artificial transcriptomic doublets" — not physical doublets in the single-cell sense but signal-level mixing; z-axis overlap explains only part of it. Existing corrections (weighted NMF + CRF, resolVI, FastReseg, ProSeg, etc.) are either heuristic or hard to interpret, and validated on few samples.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **注释**:用 RCTD(doublet 模式)以匹配 snRNA-seq(或外部 scRNA-seq)为参照,为每个 Xenium 细胞估计主/次细胞类型及权重 w1、w2;作者强调 Xenium 中的 RCTD「doublet」几乎都不是真双细胞,因此称有次信号的细胞为「contaminated」,无次信号的为「pure」。**溢出量化**:比较细胞的污染程度 w2 与其二维邻域中同一(次)细胞类型的局部丰度,二者余弦相似度高 → 污染来自邻居溢出;对每对细胞类型求平均得到 **spillover index**,衡量某类细胞「向外污染」的倾向。**SPLIT**:按 RCTD 权重与参照谱,把每个被污染细胞的转录本按比例分配给主/次来源,默认保留主谱;可选**空间门控** —— 只有当邻域中确实有大量次细胞类型时才分解,避免把参照中缺失的真实表型(如增殖期肿瘤细胞)错误拆掉;**SPLIT-shift** 可依据邻域同质性把主表型改判为次表型。**正交验证**:Xenium 后做多重 IHC(CD8、肿瘤标志)。**比较**:SPLIT vs resolVI vs ovrlpy,并与默认 5 µm 扩张、纯核(0 µm)、多模态、Baysor、ProSeg、Segger 分割全组合比较,指标为 scib 生物保守/批次、保留细胞与基因数、恶性→T 细胞污染强度(邻域逻辑回归)、与 Chromium T 细胞伪 bulk 的余弦相似度。
<!-- EN --> **Annotation**: RCTD in doublet mode with matched snRNA-seq (or an external scRNA-seq) reference estimates each Xenium cell's primary/secondary cell types and weights w1, w2; the authors stress that RCTD "doublets" in Xenium are almost never true doublets, so cells with a secondary signal are called "contaminated" and those without "pure". **Spillover quantification**: compare a cell's contamination w2 with the local abundance of that same (secondary) cell type in its 2D neighbourhood; high cosine similarity → contamination comes from neighbour spillover; averaging over cell-type pairs gives a **spillover index** measuring each type's tendency to contaminate others. **SPLIT**: using RCTD weights and reference profiles, proportionally assign each contaminated cell's transcripts to primary vs secondary sources and keep the primary profile by default; optional **spatial gating** decomposes only where the secondary type is actually abundant nearby, avoiding splitting real phenotypes absent from the reference (e.g. cycling tumour cells); **SPLIT-shift** can reassign the primary phenotype to a secondary one based on neighbourhood homogeneity. **Orthogonal validation**: multiplex IHC after Xenium (CD8, tumour markers). **Comparison**: SPLIT vs resolVI vs ovrlpy, crossed with default 5-µm expansion, nucleus-only (0 µm), multimodal, Baysor, ProSeg and Segger segmentation, scored on scib bioconservation/batch, retained cells and genes, malignant→T-cell contamination strength (neighbourhood logistic regression), and cosine similarity to Chromium T-cell pseudobulk.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 自产数据:19 张乳腺癌 FFPE 切片(17 位供者)+ 22 张 NSCLC FFPE 切片(10 位供者),共 41 张;乳腺用 Breast 靶向面板,NSCLC 用 Lung 靶向面板、自定义免疫肿瘤面板(Custom IO)与 5K PRIME 面板;部分样本有技术重复(相邻切片)、匹配 snRNA-seq(Chromium)和多重 IHC。外部参照:扩展肺癌 scRNA 图谱(318 位患者、>100 万细胞)与 Wu 等乳腺癌 scRNA。另用公开 Xenium 数据验证溢出普遍性。Xenium 数据 GEO GSE311609;snRNA 参照在 cellxgene。
<!-- EN --> Generated data: 19 breast-cancer FFPE sections (17 donors) + 22 NSCLC FFPE sections (10 donors), 41 total; breast with the Breast targeted panel, NSCLC with the Lung targeted panel, a custom immuno-oncology panel (Custom IO) and the 5K PRIME panel; some samples have technical replicates (adjacent sections), matched snRNA-seq (Chromium) and multiplex IHC. External references: an extended lung-cancer scRNA atlas (318 patients, >1M cells) and the Wu et al. breast scRNA. Public Xenium data were used to confirm spillover generality. Xenium data at GEO GSE311609; snRNA references on cellxgene.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **可重复性高**:技术重复(相邻切片)几乎完全一致;仅做 Seurat log 归一化、不做批次整合,非恶性细胞即可跨患者对齐(恶性细胞按患者成簇,符合预期)。 <!-- EN --> **High reproducibility**: technical replicates (adjacent sections) agree almost perfectly; with only Seurat log-normalization and no integration, non-malignant cells align across patients (malignant cells cluster by patient, as expected).
- <!-- ZH --> **靶向面板比 5K 灵敏**:限定 194 个共有基因时,5K 与 Lung 面板的细胞类型分离相当,但 **5K 约 60% 细胞因转录本过少未通过 QC**,同基因下检测到的转录本明显更少,灵敏度接近 Chromium;两种面板的细胞类型组成仍高度一致;两个靶向面板(仅共享 87 个基因)之间一致性最高。 <!-- EN --> **Targeted panels are more sensitive than 5K**: restricted to 194 shared genes, 5K and Lung separate cell types comparably, but **~60% of 5K cells fail QC for low transcript counts**, detecting far fewer transcripts for the same genes — sensitivity close to Chromium; cell-type composition still agrees well across panels; the two targeted panels (sharing only 87 genes) agree best.
- <!-- ZH --> **溢出普遍存在**:w2 与邻域中同类细胞丰度高度余弦相似,在公开多组织 Xenium 和不同分割下都可复现;越丰富的细胞类型 spillover index 越高,**恶性细胞最高**;IHC 显示肿瘤特异转录本延伸进相邻 CD8+ T 细胞,有时强到让 RCTD 把主次类型颠倒;低 RNA 含量细胞(如 T 细胞)受害最重。 <!-- EN --> **Spillover is pervasive**: w2 is strongly cosine-similar to neighbourhood abundance of the same type, reproducibly across public multi-tissue Xenium data and segmentations; more abundant types have higher spillover indices, **malignant cells highest**; IHC shows tumour-specific transcripts extending into adjacent CD8+ T cells, sometimes strongly enough that RCTD swaps primary and secondary; low-RNA cells (e.g. T cells) are hit hardest.
- <!-- ZH --> **纯核分割(0 µm)也有污染**,校正后改善;5K 在多模态分割下污染显著更低,但作者提醒这可能只是每基因转录本少导致统计功效下降。 <!-- EN --> **Nucleus-only segmentation (0 µm) is still contaminated**, and correction helps; 5K with multimodal segmentation shows much lower contamination, though the authors caution this may just reflect lower statistical power from fewer transcripts per gene.
- <!-- ZH --> **方法比较**:所有校正方法都改善细胞类型分离并降低污染;**SPLIT 的细胞类型分离最好、与 Chromium 谱余弦相似度最高**(作者承认这部分源于 SPLIT 本身用了 Chromium 参照);**ovrlpy 与 resolVI 显著减少表达基因数、留下大量近空细胞**;resolVI 有时 iLISI 略差。分割上 ProSeg 优于 Baysor、多模态与 Segger,**ProSeg + SPLIT 整体最佳**。乳腺癌样本结论一致。 <!-- EN --> **Method comparison**: all corrections improve cell-type separation and reduce contamination; **SPLIT gives the best separation and the highest cosine similarity to Chromium profiles** (the authors concede this partly follows from SPLIT using the Chromium reference); **ovrlpy and resolVI substantially reduce detected genes, leaving many near-empty cells**; resolVI sometimes slightly worsens iLISI. Among segmenters ProSeg beats Baysor, multimodal and Segger; **ProSeg + SPLIT is best overall**. Breast samples agree.
- <!-- ZH --> **生物学**:未校正时,肿瘤邻近 T 细胞富集虚假的恶性/上皮基因签名;SPLIT 校正后,邻近肿瘤的 T 细胞显示更强的耗竭签名(HAVCR2/TIM-3、CTLA4、PDCD1、LAG3、CXCL13);resolVI、ovrlpy 的效果不稳定,resolVI 偶尔同时压掉污染与 T 细胞程序。 <!-- EN --> **Biology**: uncorrected, tumour-adjacent T cells show spurious malignant/epithelial signatures; after SPLIT, T cells near tumour show stronger exhaustion signatures (HAVCR2/TIM-3, CTLA4, PDCD1, LAG3, CXCL13); resolVI and ovrlpy are inconsistent, with resolVI occasionally suppressing both contamination and T-cell programs.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 迄今最大的 Xenium 系统评估之一,并首次直接比较靶向面板与 5K 面板的灵敏度–覆盖度权衡。 <!-- EN --> One of the largest systematic Xenium evaluations, and the first direct comparison of targeted vs 5K panels on the sensitivity–coverage trade-off.
- <!-- ZH --> 把 RCTD 的 doublet 权重重新解释为**污染度量**,并提出 cell-type 级的 **spillover index**。 <!-- EN --> Reinterprets RCTD doublet weights as a **contamination measure** and introduces a cell-type-level **spillover index**.
- <!-- ZH --> SPLIT:简单、可解释、基于参照的信号分解,可与任何分割组合,并用空间门控防止过度校正。 <!-- EN --> SPLIT: a simple, interpretable, reference-based signal decomposition that composes with any segmentation and uses spatial gating to avoid over-correction.
- <!-- ZH --> 用 IHC 蛋白信号做正交验证(蛋白染色不易溢出)。 <!-- EN --> Orthogonal validation with IHC protein signal (protein staining is less prone to spillover).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **依赖参照**:参照中没有的细胞状态可能被错拆;作者计划做无参照版本。 <!-- EN --> **Reference-dependent**: states absent from the reference can be wrongly split; a reference-free version is planned.
- <!-- ZH --> 主分析只覆盖两种癌症;每个细胞只建模主+次两种来源。 <!-- EN --> Primary analyses cover only two cancer types; each cell is modelled with only a primary plus one secondary source.
- <!-- ZH --> 「SPLIT 与 Chromium 最像」的指标对 SPLIT 有利(作者已说明);与 resolVI 的比较结论和 resolVI 论文本身的结论相反 —— 两组都在自己设计的指标上胜出。 <!-- EN --> The "most similar to Chromium" metric favours SPLIT (acknowledged); the comparison with resolVI contradicts resolVI's own paper — each group wins on metrics it designed.
- <!-- ZH --> 5K 污染较低可能只是统计功效问题,未能区分。 <!-- EN --> Lower 5K contamination may be purely a power artefact; not disentangled.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 对 **伪影注入课题** 这几乎是一份操作手册:snRNA + snATAC + Xenium + H&E 这类多模态设计**同时有 snRNA/snATAC 与 Xenium**,正好满足 SPLIT「匹配 snRNA 参照 + RCTD」的前提。三点直接可用:**(1) 「contaminated vs pure」+ spillover index 作为伪影标签** —— 肾脏中近端小管细胞数量最多,很可能扮演本文「恶性细胞」的角色,向体积小、RNA 少的免疫细胞溢出;在 KidneyAnomalyBench 里,一个「免疫细胞表达 PT 标志物」的异常若与邻域 PT 丰度一致,应优先判为溢出伪影。**(2) 跨模态一致性**:本文用 IHC 这种不易溢出的模态验证转录组信号,与伪影注入课题「用跨模态一致性区分伪影与疾病」完全同构 —— 可对应到 H&E 形态和 snATAC。**(3) 面板选择**:5K 面板灵敏度低、约 60% 细胞未过 QC,做异常检测时低计数细胞本身就是「异常」来源,需要单独分层。**关键张力**:SPLIT 是参照驱动的,参照中没有的状态会被拆回已知类型 —— 而异常检测要找的恰恰是参照中没有的东西。若用健康参照做 SPLIT,可能把疾病特异状态(如 `inflammatory-pt-natcommun-2025` 的炎症性 PT)「纯化」成普通 PT。所以应使用疾病参照或空间门控模式,并把「校正前后异常是否稳定」作为基准的一部分。对 **Agent 可信度课题**:本文与 resolVI 论文对同一组方法得出相反排名,是「评测指标由作者选择 → 自评偏差」的典型案例,适合作为 claim-to-evidence 校验中「比较性结论需要独立证据」一类的样例;作者对 5K 低污染的谨慎解读(可能是功效不足)也是 agent 应学会的推理。
<!-- EN --> For **the artifact-injection topic** this is close to an operating manual: a multi-modal design of this kind (snRNA + snATAC + Xenium + H&E) **has both snRNA/snATAC and Xenium**, exactly SPLIT's prerequisite of a matched snRNA reference + RCTD. Three directly usable pieces: **(1) "contaminated vs pure" + spillover index as artifact labels** — in kidney, proximal-tubule cells are the most abundant type and will likely play this paper's "malignant cell" role, spilling into small, RNA-poor immune cells; in KidneyAnomalyBench an "immune cell expressing PT markers" anomaly that tracks neighbourhood PT abundance should be ranked as probable spillover. **(2) Cross-modal agreement**: validating transcript signals with spillover-resistant IHC is isomorphic to the artifact-injection topic's "cross-modal agreement separates artifacts from disease" — mappable to H&E morphology and snATAC. **(3) Panel choice**: the 5K panel's low sensitivity (~60% of cells failing QC) means low-count cells are themselves an anomaly source and must be stratified. **Key tension**: SPLIT is reference-driven, so states absent from the reference get split back into known types — yet anomaly detection is precisely about what the reference lacks. Running SPLIT with a healthy reference could "purify" disease-specific states (e.g. the inflammatory PT of `inflammatory-pt-natcommun-2025`) into ordinary PT. Use a disease-inclusive reference or the spatially gated mode, and make "anomaly stability before vs after correction" part of the benchmark. For **the agent-trustworthiness topic**: this paper and the resolVI paper rank the same methods oppositely — a textbook case of "authors choose the metrics → self-evaluation bias", a good exemplar for the claim-to-evidence class "comparative claims need independent evidence"; the authors' cautious reading of 5K's low contamination (possibly low power) is also the kind of reasoning an agent should reproduce.
