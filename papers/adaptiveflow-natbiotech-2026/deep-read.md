## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> AdaptiveFlow 是一个开源(GNU GPL-2.0)的超大规模虚拟筛选(ULVS)平台:它把 Enamine REAL Space 2022q1-2 准备成 69 billion 个 ready-to-dock 分子、按 18 维分子性质切成约 1,200 万个 tranche(格子),再用 Adaptive Target-Guided Virtual Screen(ATG-VS)先用每格 1–10 个代表分子做 12 million 次 prescreen、只对最优的 tranche 做 primary screen(可选叠加一个主动学习式 ML 分类器),把穷举 docking 的成本最多降低 1,000 倍;平台集成约 1,500 个 docking protocol,在 AWS 上近线性扩展到 5.6 million vCPU,并在 FSP1 与 PARP1 上前瞻性地做出纳摩尔抑制剂(afi-FSP1-1 Ki 0.283 µM、iParp1 IC50 8.8 nM)与共晶结构。
<!-- EN --> AdaptiveFlow is an open-source (GNU GPL-2.0) ultralarge virtual screening (ULVS) platform: it prepares Enamine REAL Space 2022q1-2 into 69 billion ready-to-dock molecules partitioned by 18 molecular properties into ~12 million tranches, then runs Adaptive Target-Guided Virtual Screens (ATG-VS) — a 12-million-docking prescreen over 1–10 representative molecules per tranche, followed by a primary screen of only the best tranches (optionally with an active-learning ML classifier) — cutting exhaustive docking cost by up to 1,000-fold; it integrates ~1,500 docking protocols, scales near-linearly to 5.6 million vCPUs on AWS, and prospectively produced nanomolar inhibitors of FSP1 and PARP1 (afi-FSP1-1 Ki 0.283 µM, iParp1 IC50 8.8 nM) together with co-crystal structures.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 合成可及的类药小分子估计超过 10^60 个,而典型实验 HTS 只能筛几十万个;可购买的 on-demand 分子已从 2020 年的约 2 billion 增长到万亿量级(论文引用的估计是多家供应商合计可达 3 trillion)。ULVS 因此成为替代路线,但有三个硬约束:**成本**(穷举 docking 数十亿到数百亿分子即使在云上也不可承受)、**灵活性**(不同靶点类别需要理论基础不同、输入格式各异的 docking 算法,而平台往往绑死在少数程序上)、**可扩展性**(多数现有平台只支持 CPU,无法同时利用 GPU 与新兴架构)。瓶颈尤其出在 protein–protein interaction interface 与 allosteric site 这类浅、动态、边界不清的口袋上。论文要解决的是:在不穷举的前提下,如何**智能地把 docking 预算集中到对给定结合位点最可能出命中的那一小块化学子空间**,并把这件事做成可复用、也能服务 AI/ML 方法开发的开源基础设施。
<!-- EN --> Synthetically accessible drug-like space is estimated at >10^60 molecules while a typical experimental HTS screens only hundreds of thousands; purchasable on-demand molecules grew from ~2 billion in 2020 to trillions (the paper cites estimates reaching 3 trillion across vendors). ULVS is the alternative, but faces three hard limits: **cost** (exhaustive docking of billions to tens of billions is prohibitive even in the cloud), **flexibility** (different target classes need docking algorithms built on different theoretical foundations with distinct input formats, while platforms are locked to a few programs), and **scalability** (most existing platforms are CPU-only and cannot exploit GPUs and emerging architectures). The bottleneck is sharpest for shallow, dynamic, poorly defined pockets such as protein–protein interaction interfaces and allosteric sites. The paper asks how to **concentrate the docking budget, without exhaustive enumeration, on the small chemical subspace most likely to contain hits for a given binding site**, and how to package that as reusable open infrastructure that also serves AI/ML method development.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **三个模块**:AFLP(AdaptiveFlow Ligand Preparation,库准备)、AFVS(AdaptiveFlow for Virtual Screening,筛选引擎)、AFU(AdaptiveFlow Unity,把两者合成单一自动化流水线,既可 standalone 命令行运行,也可以 **API mode 直接被其他程序/ML 代码调用**;AFUparr 是 SLURM 上的并行版)。整个代码库从上一代的 Bash 重写为 Python。
<!-- EN --> **Three modules**: AFLP (ligand preparation), AFVS (the screening engine) and AFU (Unity, merging both into one automated pipeline, runnable standalone from the command line or in **API mode so other programs/ML code can call it directly**; AFUparr is the SLURM-parallel version). The codebase was rewritten from Bash (previous generation) to Python.

<!-- ZH --> **AFLP**:desalting、中和、立体异构体枚举、互变异构体生成、质子化态预测、最低势能 3D 构象计算、目标格式转换(支持 100 多种输出格式);输入支持 SMILES、SELFIES、氨基酸序列;外部工具逐步可换(ChemAxon JChem 18.20.0 / Open Babel 2.3.2 / RDKit,每一步都提供开源替代——上一代有些步骤必须用非开源的 ChemAxon);质量检查用 Open Babel 算 3D 势能、自动剔除势能过高(可能在准备过程中损坏)的分子,并可选用 **PoseBusters** 做构象合理性检查;为每个分子算 **28 个**理化性质/描述符(含 QED、logP),并**自动**把库重组成多维 tranche 矩阵(上一代要求输入已是 tranche 格式)。速度上用自写 Java 代码经 Nailgun 调 JChem(减少调用次数),并加了用户可调的超时参数以跳过异常慢的配体。
<!-- EN --> **AFLP**: desalting, neutralization, stereoisomer enumeration, tautomer generation, protonation-state prediction, minimum-potential-energy 3D conformer generation and target-format conversion (>100 output formats); inputs are SMILES, SELFIES or amino-acid sequences; external tools are swappable step by step (ChemAxon JChem 18.20.0 / Open Babel 2.3.2 / RDKit, with an open-source option for every step — the previous version required non-open-source ChemAxon for some); quality control computes 3D potential energy with Open Babel to drop molecules whose energy is excessive (a sign of corruption during preparation) and optionally runs **PoseBusters** conformer plausibility checks; it computes **28** physicochemical properties/descriptors per molecule (including QED and logP) and **automatically** reorganizes a library into the multidimensional tranche matrix (the previous version required tranche-formatted input). For speed it calls JChem through Nailgun from its own Java code (fewer Nailgun calls) and adds user-tunable timeouts to skip unusually slow ligands.

<!-- ZH --> **18 维 tranche 矩阵**:从 28 个性质中挑 18 个作为维度(molecular weight、logP、hydrogen-bond acceptor/donor count、rotatable bond count、TPSA、molecular refractivity、logS、aromatic ring count / aromatic proportion、formal charge、positive/negative charge count、fraction of sp3 carbons、chiral center count、(double-bond)stereoisomer count、halogen atom count、sulfur atom count、Enamine compound class),每维离散成若干区间,理论上组合出 **24.4 billion** 个 tranche,REAL Space 实际只占据 **约 1,200 万**个;每个被占据的 tranche 平均 **5,600** 个分子,但占据度跨多个数量级(从只含 1 个分子的 tranche 到 **>10 million** 分子的 tranche)。配两个交互式 web interface(不同渲染框架),把 18 维投影到 2D 显示,同时用滑块在全部 18 维上独立选区间——等价于在 18 维里选一个超矩形,所选分子数实时更新,子集可直接送 AFVS 去筛。18 这个维数是"化学相关性 vs 计算可行性"的折中:维数再高矩阵会过稀疏。
<!-- EN --> **The 18-dimensional tranche matrix**: 18 of the 28 properties become axes (molecular weight, logP, hydrogen-bond acceptor/donor counts, rotatable bond count, TPSA, molecular refractivity, logS, aromatic ring count / aromatic proportion, formal charge, positive/negative charge counts, fraction of sp3 carbons, chiral-centre count, (double-bond) stereoisomer count, halogen- and sulfur-atom counts, Enamine compound class), each discretized into intervals, giving **24.4 billion** theoretical tranches of which REAL Space occupies **~12 million**; a populated tranche holds **5,600** molecules on average, but occupancy spans orders of magnitude (from single-molecule tranches to tranches of **>10 million**). Two interactive web interfaces (different rendering frameworks) project the 18 dimensions onto 2D while sliders set the range of all 18 independently — effectively selecting a hyperrectangle in 18-D, with the selected molecule count updating live and the subset usable directly in AFVS. The choice of 18 balances chemical relevance against tractability: more dimensions would make the matrix too sparse.

<!-- ZH --> **ATG-VS 四步**:(1) *Library decomposition* — 每个 tranche 选 1–10 个代表分子(用户设定;选 >1 时代表分子自动要求彼此多样);(2) *ATG prescreen* — 只 dock 这些代表分子(1 个代表/tranche ⇒ 全库 12 million 次 docking,比穷举 69 billion 少 **>5,000 倍**),可叠加基于 18 维性质的动态过滤(例如按靶点位点的性质手工设定准则)进一步减少;(3) *Selection of active tranches* — 按代表分子的 docking score 与 ligand efficiency 自动选最优 tranche,两种选法:逐个手动选 tranche(细粒度控制特定化学子空间),或取 18 个维度上各自表现最好的 bin 做**笛卡尔积形成超矩形区域**(hyperrectangle 法,得到"化学多样但有针对性"的子集);用户指定 primary screen 的分子总数,平台自动优先选代表分子表现最好的 tranche;(4) *ATG primary screen* — 把选中的 tranche 全筛(典型 1M–1B 化合物),并可选在格内先用 ML 分类器挑最有希望的分子。另有可选的第三阶段用更高精度(如加入受体柔性)重打分;三个阶段可全自动串起来。平台同时支持传统 funnel-type 多阶段筛选(先粗筛排名,再用更高 exhaustiveness 或不同打分函数重筛 top),阶段数任意;新库/专有库也能被 AFVS 准备成带代表分子的多维 tranche 矩阵以支持 ATG-VS。
<!-- EN --> **ATG-VS in four steps**: (1) *library decomposition* — pick 1–10 representatives per tranche (user-set; with >1, representatives are automatically chosen to be diverse from each other); (2) *ATG prescreen* — dock only those representatives (1 per tranche ⇒ 12 million dockings for the whole library, **>5,000-fold** less than exhaustively docking 69 billion), optionally narrowed further by dynamic filters on the 18 properties (e.g. criteria set by hand from the properties of the targeted site); (3) *selection of active tranches* — pick the best tranches automatically from representative docking scores and ligand efficiency, either by selecting individual tranches manually (fine-grained control over specific chemical subspaces) or by taking the top-performing bin in each of the 18 dimensions and forming their **Cartesian product as a hyperrectangle** (a chemically diverse yet targeted subset); the user sets the total molecule count for the primary screen and the platform prioritizes the tranches with the best-performing representatives; (4) *ATG primary screen* — fully screen the selected tranches (typically 1M–1B compounds), optionally pre-filtering within each tranche with the ML classifier. An optional third stage rescores top hits at higher accuracy (e.g. with receptor flexibility), and all three stages can run fully automatically. Classical funnel-type multistage screening is supported too (an initial ranking, then rescreening the top with higher exhaustiveness or different scoring functions), with arbitrarily many stages; new or proprietary libraries can also be prepared by AFVS into a multidimensional tranche matrix with preassigned representatives so that ATG-VS applies.

<!-- ZH --> **可选的 ML / active learning 层**:在 prescreen 之后、primary screen 之前,用一个全连接前馈网络过滤选中 tranche 里还没 dock 的分子。输入是 RDKit 2023.03.1 生成的 Morgan fingerprint(length 1,024、radius 2);标签来自 prescreen 的 docking score——最负的 top 25%(即第 75 百分位作决策边界)记为 "high-confidence binders" 正例,其余为负例,训练时对多数类下采样;结构 1,024 → 512 → 256 → 128(ReLU)→ 1 个 sigmoid 输出;Adam(lr 0.001)、binary cross-entropy、10% 验证集、patience 5 的 early stopping、最多 50 epoch、batch 256(通常更早收敛);超参经 prescreen 数据上的交叉验证做有限调优;实现用 PyTorch 2.0.1 并支持 GPU;整个模块集成在 AFU 里,**一个配置参数即可开启**。预测结合概率 >0.5 的分子保留去做完整 docking,这一步**通常把需要 dock 的分子数再减少 30–70%**(依靶点而定),同时保持或提高命中富集。因为每一轮筛选都产生新的带标签数据,这个循环可以**重复任意轮数**,用新 dock 的(以及可得的实验测定的)化合物重训模型、再选下一批,构成逐步把采样集中到最有希望区域的闭环主动学习。
<!-- EN --> **The optional ML / active-learning layer**: between prescreen and primary screen, a fully connected feedforward network filters the not-yet-docked molecules inside the selected tranches. Inputs are Morgan fingerprints (length 1,024, radius 2) from RDKit 2023.03.1; labels come from prescreen docking scores — the top 25% most negative (a 75th-percentile decision boundary) are positives ("high-confidence binders"), the rest negatives, with the majority class undersampled during training; the architecture is 1,024 → 512 → 256 → 128 (ReLU) → one sigmoid output; Adam (lr 0.001), binary cross-entropy, a 10% validation split, early stopping with patience 5, up to 50 epochs, batch size 256 (convergence is usually earlier); hyperparameters came from limited tuning by cross-validation on the prescreen data; it is implemented in PyTorch 2.0.1 with GPU support and integrated into AFU, **activated by a single configuration parameter**. Compounds with predicted binding probability >0.5 go to full docking, which **typically cuts the number docked by a further 30–70%** depending on the target while maintaining or improving hit enrichment. Because every screening stage produces new labeled data, the cycle can be repeated for **an arbitrary number of rounds**, retraining on newly docked (and where available experimentally assayed) compounds and re-selecting the next batch — a closed loop that progressively concentrates sampling on the most promising regions.

<!-- ZH --> **docking protocol 的组合学**:论文把 "docking protocol" 定义为 *sampling algorithm(pose prediction)× scoring function* 的一个具体组合;平台支持约 40 个 docking 程序(分三类:既做 pose 预测又打分的、只做 pose 预测的、只打分的),自由组合出**约 1,500 个 protocol**,通过单一命令行接口调用——用户不必逐个配置和学习多个 docking 程序。覆盖的配体/靶点类型包括 small molecule、peptide、protein(protein–protein docking)、DNA/RNA 与 covalent binder,部分程序还有显式溶剂/金属参数化等 "special features";ML docking 含 **DiffDock、TANKBind、EquiBind**;GPU 打分含 Gnina、NNScore 2.0、DeepBindRG、DeepAffinity;GPU pose prediction 含 AutoDock-GPU、QVina2-GPU、QVina2-W-GPU、Vina-GPU;MD 类重打分/精修含 **MM/PBSA、MM/GBSA 与 binding pose metadynamics(OpenBPMD)**;Vina 系的 pose 可选 PoseBusters 校验。QuickVina 2 被重新 profile(Intel VTune)后发现执行剖面很平(最耗时函数 <20% 运行时),因此改走编译器级优化:用 Intel oneAPI DPC++/C++ Compiler 2022.2.0.20220730 开自动向量化、过程间优化与放松浮点约束重编译,最终可执行文件比默认构建**快 18%** 且无精度损失(profile-guided optimization 没有帮助)。
<!-- EN --> **Docking-protocol combinatorics**: a "docking protocol" is defined as one specific combination of *sampling algorithm (pose prediction) × scoring function*; the platform supports ~40 docking programs (three kinds: pose prediction plus scoring, pose prediction only, scoring only) freely combined into **~1,500 protocols** reachable from a single command-line interface — users need not configure and learn multiple docking programs individually. Supported ligand/target types include small molecules, peptides, proteins (protein–protein docking), DNA/RNA and covalent binders, with "special features" such as explicit solvent/metal parameterization in some programs; ML docking includes **DiffDock, TANKBind, EquiBind**; GPU scoring includes Gnina, NNScore 2.0, DeepBindRG, DeepAffinity; GPU pose prediction includes AutoDock-GPU, QVina2-GPU, QVina2-W-GPU and Vina-GPU; MD-based rescoring/refinement includes **MM/PBSA, MM/GBSA and binding pose metadynamics (OpenBPMD)**; Vina-family poses can be checked with PoseBusters. Profiling QuickVina 2 (Intel VTune) showed a flat execution profile (the costliest function was <20% of runtime), so the route taken was compiler-level: rebuilding with the Intel oneAPI DPC++/C++ Compiler 2022.2.0.20220730 using automatic vectorization, interprocedural optimization and relaxed floating-point constraints made the executable **18% faster** than default builds with no accuracy loss (profile-guided optimization did not help).

<!-- ZH --> **工程与云**:并行化分三层 —— collection(约 1,000 个配体一组,降低逐配体调度开销)→ subjob(原子计算单元,按 job 配置自动生成,约 30 分钟)→ work unit(以 job array 提交给调度器);批处理系统支持 **Slurm**(Google Cloud / Azure / AWS 皆可;用户给一个提交模板,AdaptiveFlow 运行时填入 job array、partition、memory 等变量,从而把工作流逻辑与集群配置解耦)与 **AWS Batch**(+ S3 对象存储、CloudFormation 部署、Docker 容器自动生成);使用 spot(可抢占)实例降低成本,并通过 AWS Batch 的 "spot-capacity-aware" 调度与短 subjob + 自动重跑来吸收抢占;支持 x86 与 **ARM** CPU(ARM 往往更便宜)以及 NVIDIA/AMD GPU。AFVS 输出可写成 **Apache Parquet**,从而被任何能读 Parquet 的查询系统使用(例如 S3 上的 serverless **Amazon Athena**),并有自动用 Athena 后处理 ATG prescreen、为 primary screen 准备输入的功能。
<!-- EN --> **Engineering and cloud**: parallelism has three levels — collections (~1,000 ligands, reducing per-ligand scheduling overhead) → subjobs (atomic units of work, generated automatically from the job configuration, ~30 min) → work units (submitted to the scheduler as job arrays); batch systems are **Slurm** (Google Cloud / Azure / AWS; the user supplies a submission template that AdaptiveFlow fills at runtime with job arrays, partitions and memory, decoupling workflow logic from cluster configuration) and **AWS Batch** (plus S3 object storage, CloudFormation deployment and automatic Docker-container generation); spot (preemptible) instances cut cost, with AWS Batch's "spot-capacity-aware" scheduling plus short subjobs and automatic re-running absorbing preemption; x86 and **ARM** CPUs (ARM is often cheaper) and NVIDIA/AMD GPUs are supported. AFVS output can be written as **Apache Parquet**, readable by any Parquet-capable query system (e.g. serverless **Amazon Athena** over S3), and ATG prescreens can be post-processed automatically through Athena to prepare the primary-screen input.

<!-- ZH --> **FSP1 的湿实验链路**:先用 ancestral sequence reconstruction 重建人 FSP1 的四足动物祖先蛋白(序列同一性 72.5%,保留全部活性位点残基;E. coli 表达约 100 mg l⁻¹,Tm 62 °C,保留全部酶活,衍射质量好),解出 FSP1–FAD、FSP1–FAD–NAD⁺、FSP1–FAD–NAD⁺–coenzyme Q1 三个高分辨率结构,再以 **PDB 9IFT**(FSP1 + FAD + NAD⁺)为受体筛 coenzyme Q 位点:受体用 Maestro(Schrödinger)准备(去溶剂、补氢、pH 7.4 下修正质子化态、修正键级),用 AutoDockTools 1.5.7 转成 PDBQT。
<!-- EN --> **The FSP1 wet-lab chain**: ancestral sequence reconstruction first gave the tetrapod ancestor of human FSP1 (72.5% sequence identity, all active-site residues retained; ~100 mg l⁻¹ in *E. coli*, Tm 62 °C, full enzymatic activity, well-diffracting), from which three high-resolution structures were solved (FSP1–FAD, FSP1–FAD–NAD⁺, FSP1–FAD–NAD⁺–coenzyme Q1); **PDB 9IFT** (FSP1 + FAD + NAD⁺) was then the receptor for screening the coenzyme Q site, prepared in Maestro (Schrödinger) by removing solvent, adding missing hydrogens, correcting protonation at pH 7.4 and assigning bond orders, and converted to PDBQT with AutoDockTools 1.5.7.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **化学库**:Enamine REAL Space,enumerated release **2022q1-2**(2022-11-15 获取),原始 **31,507,987,117** 个以 SMILES 表示的未处理分子,经 AFLP 准备(主要因立体异构体与互变异构体枚举)扩展到 **68.7 billion** ready-to-dock 分子(摘要作 69 billion)。组成:**137,000** 个已建立的建块 × Enamine 内部验证的 **167** 个 one-pot 合成方案;基于 2021 年 **386,000** 次实验反应报告的合成成功率 **82%**;**99%** 符合 Lipinski Rule of Five;**1,098,811,629** 个唯一 Murcko scaffold;**>62 billion(74%)** 至少含一个手性中心(所谓 "nonflat" 化学空间);**23 billion** 含卤素,其中 **17.4 billion** 含氟(适合不需同位素标记蛋白的 ¹⁹F NMR 结合实验)。作为对照,此前最大的公开 ready-to-dock 库(2018 版 REAL Database、ZINC20)各约 1.5 billion。格式:ready-to-dock 的 PDB、PDBQT、MOL2、SDF(每种格式压缩后约 **50 TB**,四种共约 **200 TB**;未压缩约 **2 PB**),外加 SMILES、**SELFIES**、Parquet。18 维矩阵本身仍很稀疏,大量未被占据的 tranche 被作者视为"值得前瞻性合成去填充的化学空间"。
<!-- EN --> **Chemical library**: Enamine REAL Space, enumerated release **2022q1-2** (accessed 15 Nov 2022), **31,507,987,117** original unprocessed SMILES, expanded by AFLP preparation (mainly stereoisomer and tautomer enumeration) to **68.7 billion** ready-to-dock molecules (69 billion in the abstract). Composition: **137,000** established building blocks × **167** internally validated Enamine one-pot synthetic protocols; a reported **82%** synthetic success rate based on **386,000** experimental reactions in 2021; **99%** comply with Lipinski's Rule of Five; **1,098,811,629** unique Murcko scaffolds; **>62 billion (74%)** contain at least one chiral centre ("nonflat" space); **23 billion** are halogenated, of which **17.4 billion** contain fluorine (suited to ¹⁹F NMR binding assays that need no isotopically labeled protein). For scale, the largest previously public ready-to-dock libraries (the 2018 REAL Database, ZINC20) held ~1.5 billion each. Formats: ready-to-dock PDB, PDBQT, MOL2 and SDF (~**50 TB** compressed per format, ~**200 TB** for all four; ~**2 PB** uncompressed) plus SMILES, **SELFIES** and Parquet. The 18-D matrix remains sparsely populated, and the authors read the empty tranches as chemical space worth populating by prospective synthesis.

<!-- ZH --> **基准靶点**:10 个结构多样的蛋白,覆盖 kinase、phosphatase、GPCR 与 protein–protein interaction interface,图 4 标注为 **KRAS-G12V、CB1、PTPRB、JNK3、TLR8、PIK3CG、NTR1、MED15-KIX、KEAP1、RPTO**(确切身份与结构来源在 Supplementary Table 7,未读)。两套设置:(a) *test-based benchmark* — 从 Enamine 空间随机抽合成反应构造约 **5 million** 化合物子集,每个靶点用**不同的**随机子集以保证评测无偏;(b) *production-scale benchmark* — 整个 69-billion REAL Space。所有基准 docking 默认用 **QuickVina 2** 默认参数;另用 **Smina(Vinardo 打分)** 与 **GWOVina(柔性侧链)** 复跑,以检验 binning 策略对 sampling 算法与打分函数的敏感性。

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **化学空间聚焦的有效性是靶点依赖的**,作者自己明说("the effectiveness of chemical space focusing is target dependent");收益最大的是口袋复杂、特征丰富的蛋白,而对浅而无特征的位点能省多少、会不会失效,论文没有给出失败案例分析。 <!-- EN --> **The effectiveness of chemical-space focusing is target dependent**, as the authors state; the gain is largest for complex, feature-rich pockets, and no failure analysis is given for shallow, featureless sites — how much is saved there, or whether the approach breaks down, is unknown.
- <!-- ZH --> **基准的评价指标是 docking score,不是实验亲和力**。三臂之间 top-50 平均 docking score 的差距多在 0.1–0.4 kcal mol⁻¹,远小于打分函数本身的误差;而"穷举最优"这条参考线只存在于 5-million 子集上,69-billion 全库没有(也不可能有)穷举基线——因此"最多省 1,000 倍"是相对一个**从未被实际运行过**的基线说的。与一个调好的传统漏斗式筛选相比到底省多少,正文没有正面回答。 <!-- EN --> **The benchmark metric is docking score, not measured affinity**. Differences in mean top-50 docking score between arms are mostly 0.1–0.4 kcal mol⁻¹, well inside the error of the scoring functions themselves; and the "brute-force best" reference exists only on the 5-million subset — there is (and can be) no exhaustive baseline for the full 69-billion library, so "up to 1,000-fold cheaper" is quoted against a baseline **nobody actually runs**. How much it saves against a well-tuned classical funnel screen is not addressed head-on.
- <!-- ZH --> **主动学习层在真正的生产规模运行中没被用**:production-scale benchmark 明确"不加 active learning 以减少计算开销",FSP1 的 primary screen 也标注 "without ML feature"。ML 增强的证据因此全部来自 5-million 子集,其在 69-billion 规模上的增益是**推断**("we expect that incorporating active learning could yield further improvements")而非实测;论文摘要强调的 "optional active learning" 与两个湿实验成功案例之间并无因果链。 <!-- EN --> **The active-learning layer was not used in the real production runs**: the production-scale benchmarks explicitly omit active learning "to minimize computational overhead", and the FSP1 primary screen is labeled "without ML feature". All evidence for the ML enhancement therefore comes from the 5-million subset, and its benefit at 69-billion scale is an **expectation** ("we expect that incorporating active learning could yield further improvements"), not a measurement; there is no causal chain between the abstract's "optional active learning" and the two wet-lab successes.
- <!-- ZH --> **分类器学的是打分函数,不是活性**:标签直接取 prescreen docking score 的最负 25%,因此这一层学到的是"复现 QuickVina 2 的偏好",打分函数的系统误差会被继承;第 75 百分位这个决策边界与 0.5 的概率阈值都是固定启发式,没有做敏感性分析。 <!-- EN --> **The classifier learns the scoring function, not activity**: labels are simply the most negative 25% of prescreen docking scores, so the layer learns to reproduce QuickVina 2's preferences and inherits that function's systematic error; both the 75th-percentile label boundary and the 0.5 probability threshold are fixed heuristics with no sensitivity analysis.
- <!-- ZH --> **tranche 决策的粒度假设未被检验**:18 维的分箱方案、"每维取最好 bin 再做笛卡尔积"的超矩形启发式、以及 ligand efficiency 的使用,都没有与别的子空间选择方法(基于 scaffold、基于相似性检索、或基于学习的 surrogate)做对照;prescreen 每个 tranche 只用 1–10 个代表,而 tranche 平均含 5,600 个分子、占据度跨多个数量级(有 >10 million 分子的 tranche),单个代表能代表多大的方差没有量化。 <!-- EN --> **The granularity assumptions behind tranche decisions are untested**: the 18-D binning scheme, the "best bin per dimension × Cartesian product" hyperrectangle heuristic and the use of ligand efficiency are never compared with alternative subspace-selection methods (scaffold-based, similarity retrieval, or learned surrogates); the prescreen uses only 1–10 representatives for tranches that average 5,600 molecules and whose occupancy spans orders of magnitude (some exceed 10 million), and how much variance one representative captures is not quantified.
- <!-- ZH --> **命中率的分母被人工筛选过**:FSP1 是 10 million 分子对接 → 42 个订购(37 cluster、SwissADME 零警示 + 多条性质与毒性过滤)→ 33 个合成 → 2 个一级命中;PARP1 是 160 个合成 → 7 个抑制剂 → 4 个 <250 nM。这些比例混入了专家的过滤与挑选,不能直接读作"虚拟筛选的命中率",也无法把 AdaptiveFlow 的贡献与"大库本身更容易出好分子"分开。 <!-- EN --> **The denominator of the hit rate has been human-filtered**: FSP1 went 10 million docked → 42 ordered (37 clusters, zero SwissADME alerts plus several property and toxicity filters) → 33 synthesized → 2 primary hits; PARP1 went 160 synthesized → 7 inhibitors → 4 below 250 nM. These ratios include expert filtering and selection, so they are not a virtual-screening hit rate, and AdaptiveFlow's contribution cannot be separated from the fact that larger libraries yield better molecules anyway.
- <!-- ZH --> **从命中到可用化合物仍有明显缺口**:iParp1 酶学 IC50 8.8 nM 与 olaparib 相当,但细胞活性弱,被归因于水解造成的膜通透性差;FSP1 的结构工作用的是序列同一性 72.5% 的祖先蛋白而非人蛋白(细胞内结合另由 CETSA 证实);细胞 ADME 的结论只是 afi-FSP-3"最适合继续优化",也就是说还没有一个成药分子。另外 REAL Space 是按需合成库,82% 的合成成功率意味着订购的候选有一部分拿不到实物(本文即 42 个订购 → 33 个合成)。 <!-- EN --> **There is still a clear gap from hit to usable compound**: iParp1's enzymatic IC50 of 8.8 nM matches olaparib but its cellular activity is weak, attributed to poor permeability from hydrolysis; the FSP1 structural work used an ancestral protein of 72.5% sequence identity rather than the human enzyme (cellular engagement shown separately by CETSA); and the cellular ADME verdict is only that afi-FSP-3 is the best starting point for further optimization — no drug-like endpoint yet. REAL Space is also an on-demand library whose 82% synthetic success rate means some ordered candidates never materialize (here: 42 ordered → 33 synthesized).
- <!-- ZH --> **资产本身的门槛与利益关系**:ready-to-dock 库压缩后约 200 TB(未压缩约 2 PB),实际使用几乎必须走 AWS S3;库是单一供应商(Enamine)的 2022 年版本,而正文自己指出 on-demand 空间已到万亿级;重现 5.6 million vCPU 级别的运行需要云额度(作者致谢 AWS 研究额度与 ChemAxon 的免费学术许可);多位作者分别任职或创办 Enamine、Onepot AI、AWS、Google、NVIDIA、Intel 与计算药物发现服务公司(Virtual Discovery、Phind Beteiligungs UG)。此外 "near-linear scaling" 的直接证据来自 AFLP,AFVS 只是"预期相同"。 <!-- EN --> **Barriers and interests around the asset itself**: the ready-to-dock library is ~200 TB compressed (~2 PB uncompressed), so practical use essentially requires AWS S3; it is a single vendor's (Enamine) 2022 release while the text itself notes on-demand space is now at trillion scale; reproducing 5.6-million-vCPU runs needs cloud credits (the authors acknowledge AWS research credits and a free ChemAxon academic licence); and several authors work for or founded Enamine, Onepot AI, AWS, Google, NVIDIA, Intel and computational drug-discovery service companies (Virtual Discovery, Phind Beteiligungs UG). The direct evidence for near-linear scaling is also AFLP's; AFVS is only "expected to be the same".

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇是 Sikander Hayat lab(Uniklinik Aachen)课题 **"Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening"**(thesis/HiWi:LLM agent + molecular foundation model + docking + active learning,给定蛋白结构自主规划、执行、改进筛选 campaign 并找出合适化合物,应用方向为心脏疾病)的 **key paper 1**,在四篇阅读材料里扮演的角色是"**agent 要调度的那台机器**"。关键在于:论文已经把一个 ULVS campaign 拆成了参数化、可配置、可脚本化的阶段,而作者明确把 AFU 的 API mode 设计给"开发药物发现新方法的 ML 社区"使用——agent 需要的 handle 基本都已存在,不必自己造筛选引擎。
<!-- EN --> This is **key paper 1** for the Sikander Hayat lab (Uniklinik Aachen) project **"Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening"** (a thesis/HiWi topic: LLM agents + molecular foundation models + docking + active learning that, given protein structures, autonomously plan, execute and improve screening campaigns and find suitable compounds; application area heart disease). Among the four readings its role is **the machine the agent has to drive**: the paper has already decomposed a ULVS campaign into parameterized, configurable, scriptable stages, and the authors explicitly designed AFU's API mode for "ML communities that develop new methods for drug discovery" — nearly every handle an agent needs already exists, so there is no need to build a screening engine.

<!-- ZH --> **可以直接包成 agent tool 的部分(都对应论文中实际存在的接口)**:(1) `prepare` = AFLP —— 受体/配体准备 + 28 个性质计算,输入可为 SMILES、SELFIES 或氨基酸序列,每一步可选工具;(2) `tranche_select` —— 在 18 维矩阵上手动选 tranche 或用 hyperrectangle 法选超矩形(交互式 web interface 的 18 个滑块语义,正是 agent 要输出的那组约束);(3) `prescreen` = ATG prescreen —— 代表分子数(1–10)× exhaustiveness,输出 Parquet;(4) `query` —— 用 Amazon Athena 对 prescreen 结果做 SQL 查询(天然的 agent 工具形态,也是"选格"决策的证据来源);(5) `primary_screen` —— 用户指定分子预算的全量对接;(6) `ml_filter` —— 一个配置参数开关的 Morgan-fingerprint 分类器(课题里 "active learning" 的现成底座,也是把 DrugCLIP 式对比学习打分器替换进来的插槽);(7) `rescore` —— 第三阶段高精度重打分(受体柔性、MM/PBSA、MM/GBSA、BPMD);(8) `pose_check` —— PoseBusters 的物理合理性检查,可作为 agent 的自动 sanity check;(9) `dock(protocol=…)` —— 约 1,500 个 protocol 的单接口选择;(10) `submit` —— 填充 Slurm / AWS Batch 提交模板(job array、partition、memory)并设定 subjob 时长与 spot 策略,即 agent 的"算力与预算"动作。
<!-- EN --> **Parts that wrap directly into agent tools (each an interface the paper actually exposes)**: (1) `prepare` = AFLP — receptor/ligand preparation plus the 28 property calculations, accepting SMILES, SELFIES or amino-acid sequences, with a choice of tool per step; (2) `tranche_select` — manual tranche picking or the hyperrectangle method on the 18-D matrix (the semantics of the web interface's 18 sliders are exactly the constraint set an agent would emit); (3) `prescreen` = the ATG prescreen — representatives per tranche (1–10) × exhaustiveness, emitting Parquet; (4) `query` — SQL over the prescreen results with Amazon Athena (a natural agent-tool shape and the evidence source for tranche decisions); (5) `primary_screen` — full docking under a user-specified molecule budget; (6) `ml_filter` — the Morgan-fingerprint classifier toggled by one configuration parameter (the ready-made substrate for the project's "active learning", and the slot where a DrugCLIP-style contrastive scorer drops in); (7) `rescore` — the high-accuracy third stage (receptor flexibility, MM/PBSA, MM/GBSA, BPMD); (8) `pose_check` — PoseBusters physical-validity checks as an automatic sanity check; (9) `dock(protocol=…)` — one interface over ~1,500 protocols; (10) `submit` — filling the Slurm / AWS Batch submission template (job arrays, partitions, memory) and setting subjob length and spot strategy, i.e. the agent's compute-and-budget action.

<!-- ZH --> **agent 实际可以做的决策(每条都有论文中的量化依据)**:**选哪个 docking protocol** —— 约 1,500 个组合是一个真实的离散动作空间,而论文证明 binning 对 protocol 不敏感(QuickVina 2 / Smina-Vinardo / GWOVina 的 speedup 与 enrichment 可比),所以 agent 可以安全地"便宜 protocol 做 prescreen、贵 protocol 做 rescoring",并按靶点类型(kinase / GPCR / PPI interface / RNA / 共价)挑程序;Extended Data Fig. 9 的单次计算耗时就是它做成本估算的查找表。**选哪块化学子空间** —— 18 维性质约束是 agent 最自然的输出形式,而论文自己示范了把靶点与下游需求翻译成约束的全过程(FSP1 用了 logP < 5、logS > −5、TPSA < 140 Å²、立体中心 ≤ 3,加 SwissADME 零警示与跨 37 个 cluster 的分散采样);心脏方向所需的约束可以挂在同一套维度上。**花多少算力** —— prescreen 规模(12 million 全库 vs 加动态过滤)、代表分子数(1 vs 10)、exhaustiveness(1 vs 10)、primary screen 预算(论文实际用过 10,000 / 100,000 / 1 million / 10 million / 100 million)全是显式旋钮。**什么时候停** —— 论文给了四类停机信号的雏形:prescreen 超参的收益已饱和(三种设置结果相近 ⇒ agent 不该在这里加钱)、top-50 平均 docking score 与"穷举最优"参考线的剩余 gap(5M 子集上 100K ATG+AL 仍差约 0.5–1.6 kcal mol⁻¹)、ML 过滤掉的比例(30–70%)、以及"再加一轮是否还能改进"——FSP1 的第二轮(用 FSP1–afi-FSP1-1 共晶结构重筛、20 个类似物 → 4 个新抑制剂)正是 agent 应该学会自己触发的 SAR 迭代。**选哪些分子去买/合成** —— cluster 分散 + 性质过滤 + 毒性/ADME 警示过滤,是一个完全可自动化但本文仍由专家执行的决策点,也是 agent 最容易展示增量价值的位置。
<!-- EN --> **Decisions an agent could actually make (each with a quantitative basis in the paper)**: **which docking protocol** — ~1,500 combinations are a real discrete action space, and the paper shows the binning is protocol-insensitive (comparable speedups and enrichment for QuickVina 2 / Smina-Vinardo / GWOVina), so an agent can safely use a cheap protocol for the prescreen and an expensive one for rescoring, and pick programs by target class (kinase / GPCR / PPI interface / RNA / covalent); the single-calculation timings in Extended Data Fig. 9 are its cost-estimation lookup table. **Which chemical subspace** — constraints over the 18 properties are the most natural agent output, and the paper itself demonstrates the full translation from target and downstream needs into them (FSP1 used logP < 5, logS > −5, TPSA < 140 Å², ≤3 stereocentres, plus zero SwissADME alerts and diverse sampling across 37 clusters); heart-disease constraints hang off the same dimensions. **How much compute to spend** — prescreen size (12 million whole-library vs dynamically filtered), representatives per tranche (1 vs 10), exhaustiveness (1 vs 10) and primary-screen budget (the paper actually used 10,000 / 100,000 / 1 million / 10 million / 100 million) are all explicit knobs. **When to stop** — the paper supplies four nascent stopping signals: the prescreen hyperparameters have saturated (three settings give similar results ⇒ don't spend there), the residual gap in mean top-50 docking score to the brute-force reference (on the 5M subset 100K ATG+AL is still ~0.5–1.6 kcal mol⁻¹ away), the fraction removed by the ML filter (30–70%), and whether another round still improves things — the FSP1 second round (re-screening from the FSP1–afi-FSP1-1 co-crystal, 20 analogs → 4 new inhibitors) is exactly the SAR iteration an agent should learn to trigger itself. **Which molecules to order or synthesize** — cluster diversity plus property filters plus toxicity/ADME alert filters is fully automatable yet still expert-driven here, and the easiest place for an agent to add measurable value.

<!-- ZH --> **一个合理的第一个 thesis milestone**:在 5-million 化合物的 test-based benchmark 设置下复现并包装论文的三臂实验(配方齐全:从 Enamine 空间随机抽合成反应构库、QuickVina 2 默认参数、1M random / 100K ATG / 100K ATG+AL 三臂、top-50 平均 docking score 为指标、全库穷举值作参考上界、10 个跨类别靶点),把它做成一个 agent 可调用的 harness;然后让 LLM agent 在**只给定受体结构与一句自然语言目标**的条件下自己产出 AFU/AFVS 配置文件(选 protocol、写 18 维约束、定 prescreen 与 primary screen 预算、决定是否开 ML 过滤、决定是否再来一轮),与论文手工设定的 100K ATG+AL 对照两个问题:**相同 docking 预算下 agent 的配置能否打平或更好**,以及**agent 能否在收益饱和处自己停下**。选这个 milestone 的理由:指标、基线、参考上界、复现所需的代码与数据全部开放(GPL-2.0 代码 + AWS Open Data 的库 + 10 个基准靶点),规模小到单机或小集群可跑(不需要 5.6 million vCPU),产物(agent trace + 配置 schema)直接可用于下一步换心脏靶点、接 DrugCLIP 打分器或接 co-folding 重打分。
<!-- EN --> **A sensible first thesis milestone**: reproduce and wrap the paper's three-arm experiment in the 5-million-compound test-based benchmark setting (the recipe is complete: build the subset from randomly selected Enamine synthetic reactions, QuickVina 2 with default parameters, the arms 1M random / 100K ATG / 100K ATG+AL, mean top-50 docking score as the metric, the brute-force value as a reference upper bound, ten targets across classes) as a harness an agent can call; then have an LLM agent — **given only the receptor structure and a one-sentence natural-language goal** — emit the AFU/AFVS configuration file itself (protocol choice, the 18-D constraints, prescreen and primary-screen budgets, whether to enable the ML filter, whether to run another round), and test two things against the paper's hand-set 100K ATG+AL: **at equal docking budget, does the agent's configuration match or beat it**, and **does the agent stop by itself once returns saturate**? The reasons to pick this milestone: metric, baseline, reference upper bound and all code and data needed are open (GPL-2.0 code + the library on AWS Open Data + ten benchmark targets); the scale fits a single machine or small cluster (no 5.6 million vCPUs needed); and the outputs (agent traces plus a configuration schema) carry straight over to swapping in cardiac targets, plugging in a DrugCLIP-style scorer, or adding co-folding rescoring.

<!-- ZH --> **与另外三篇读物的分工**:EXSCALATE 是同一问题的"**穷举 + HPC**"解(1 trillion 次评估),AdaptiveFlow 是"**自适应 + 云**"解,两者正好给 agent 的 compute-allocation 策略划出两个极端(买算力 vs 买信息);DrugCLIP 的对比学习打分器可以替换或前置于 ATG prescreen 里的 docking —— prescreen 那 12 million 次 docking 是整条流程里最大的固定成本,也是最值得被 foundation model 近似的一步;pairwise competitive docking(AF3/Boltz co-folding)则正好落在 AdaptiveFlow 第三阶段"更高精度重打分"的插槽里,并提供 docking score 之外的排序信号(这对缓解本文"ML 层只是在学打分函数"这一局限尤其重要)。**必须注意**:本文没有任何心脏相关靶点(靶点是 FSP1、PARP1 与 10 个基准蛋白,背景以肿瘤为主),所以心脏方向只能借其**流程与基础设施**,靶点、通路与 ADME 约束必须自己定义;FSP1 是 ferroptosis / 脂质过氧化的调控者、本文也提到其在癌症抗性中的角色,但**论文完全没有涉及心脏**,不应据此推断相关性。 <!-- EN --> **Division of labour with the other three readings**: EXSCALATE is the "**exhaustive + HPC**" answer to the same problem (1 trillion evaluations) and AdaptiveFlow the "**adaptive + cloud**" answer — together they bracket an agent's compute-allocation policy (buy compute vs buy information); DrugCLIP's contrastive scorer could replace or precede the docking inside the ATG prescreen, since those 12 million dockings are the pipeline's largest fixed cost and the step most worth approximating with a foundation model; pairwise competitive docking (AF3/Boltz co-folding) lands exactly in AdaptiveFlow's third-stage "higher-accuracy rescoring" slot and supplies a ranking signal beyond docking score — which matters especially for the limitation that the ML layer here only learns the scoring function. **Important caveat**: this paper contains no cardiac target (FSP1, PARP1 and ten benchmark proteins, in a mostly oncology context), so the heart-disease direction can borrow its **workflow and infrastructure** only and must define its own targets, pathways and ADME constraints; FSP1 is a regulator of ferroptosis and lipid peroxidation, with a role in cancer resistance mentioned here, but **the paper says nothing about the heart** and relevance must not be inferred from it.
