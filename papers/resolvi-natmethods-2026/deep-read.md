## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> resolVI 是 scvi-tools 里的一个 VAE 生成模型,接在**任意分割算法之后**,把每个细胞的观测计数拆成三部分 —— 细胞自身的真实表达、从邻近细胞「扩散」进来的表达、以及每张切片恒定的非特异背景 —— 从而去除分子错配导致的虚假共表达,同时给出批次校正后的概率潜在表示,支持差异表达和空间邻域丰度检验。
<!-- EN --> resolVI is a VAE-based generative model in scvi-tools that sits **downstream of any segmentation algorithm** and decomposes each cell's observed counts into three parts — the cell's true expression, expression "diffused" in from neighbouring cells, and a per-slice constant non-specific background — removing spurious co-expression caused by molecule misassignment while producing a batch-corrected probabilistic latent representation that supports differential expression and differential niche-abundance testing.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 亚细胞分辨率空间转录组(Xenium、MERSCOPE、CosMx、Stereo-seq、Visium HD)在做细胞级分析前必须**分割 + 定量**:把平面切成近似细胞的区域,再把区域内的分子计数。即使用了 Baysor、ProSeg 这类利用转录本信息的分割方法,分子仍会被分错细胞 —— 原因包括细胞密集、染色信号弱、形状不规则,以及组织处理导致的 RNA 泄漏和**第三维(z 轴)上的细胞重叠**。后果是虚假的跨谱系标志基因共表达(例如小胶质细胞「表达」神经元标志物),这让分析停留在预先定义的细胞大类上,也让新细胞状态的发现变得困难。
<!-- EN --> Subcellular-resolution spatial transcriptomics (Xenium, MERSCOPE, CosMx, Stereo-seq, Visium HD) requires **segmentation + quantification** before any cell-level analysis: partition the plane into cell-like regions and count the molecules inside each. Even with transcript-aware segmenters such as Baysor and ProSeg, molecules still land in the wrong cell — because of high cell density, weak staining, irregular shapes, RNA leakage from tissue handling, and **cell overlap along the poorly resolved z axis**. The result is spurious cross-lineage marker co-expression (e.g. microglia "expressing" neuronal markers), which confines analyses to a priori-defined cell classes and hampers the discovery of new cell states.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **输入**:任意分割/定量得到的细胞×基因计数矩阵、细胞空间坐标、切片 ID。**生成模型**:观测计数 x_ng ~ Poisson(l_n · h_ng),其中 h_ng = Gamma(真实表达 α0·f_θ(z_n,b) + 扩散 α1·Σ β_nN(n)·f_θ(z_N(n),b)) + 背景 α2·bg_sg。真实表达与扩散用负二项(Gamma-Poisson)建模,背景假设无过离散、用 Poisson;α_n(三部分比例)由一个「diffusion encoder」以 Dirichlet 先验做 MAP 估计;每个邻居的贡献 β(默认 20 个邻居)用基于欧氏距离的 RBF 核放进 Dirichlet 先验,越近的细胞越可能是错配来源;背景先验取最低表达 10% 基因的均值(作者发现在 CosMx 上它接近负对照探针计数)。**潜在空间**:用高斯混合先验(而非单峰高斯),既更好区分细胞类型,也允许半监督地接入已知细胞类型标签并训练一个分类器。**去批次**:decoder 接收批次 ID,encoder 默认不接收。**直觉**:VAE 瓶颈假设数据可被低维编码,错配分子让观测谱「更复杂」,最小化 ELBO 会把「无关」分子推给扩散或背景分量。**训练细节**:每细胞 <20 分子的细胞先过滤;对低计数细胞做多项式重采样增强;支持类似 scArches 的 network surgery 做 query 映射;下游提供基于模型的 lvm-DE 差异表达和双侧贝叶斯「差异邻域丰度」检验。
<!-- EN --> **Input**: a cell × gene count matrix from any segmentation/quantification, cell coordinates, and slice ID. **Generative model**: observed counts x_ng ~ Poisson(l_n · h_ng), with h_ng = Gamma(true expression α0·f_θ(z_n,b) + diffusion α1·Σ β_nN(n)·f_θ(z_N(n),b)) + background α2·bg_sg. True and diffused expression are negative binomial (Gamma-Poisson); background is assumed non-overdispersed and Poisson. The per-cell proportions α_n are MAP-estimated by a "diffusion encoder" under a Dirichlet prior; per-neighbour contributions β (20 neighbours by default) get a Dirichlet prior shaped by an RBF kernel on Euclidean distance, so nearer cells are likelier sources of misassignment; the background prior is set to the mean of the lowest-expressed 10% of genes (which the authors found approximates negative-control probe counts on CosMx). **Latent space**: a mixture-of-Gaussians prior instead of a unimodal Gaussian, which separates cell types better and allows semi-supervised training with known labels plus a classifier head. **Batch**: the decoder sees batch ID; the encoder does not by default. **Intuition**: the VAE bottleneck assumes a low-dimensional description; misassigned molecules make observed profiles "more complex", so minimizing the ELBO pushes "unrelated" molecules into the diffusion or background components. **Training details**: cells with <20 molecules are filtered; low-count cells are augmented by multinomial resampling; scArches-style network surgery maps query data; downstream, model-based lvm-DE differential expression and a two-sided Bayesian differential niche-abundance test are provided.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **Xenium 小鼠脑**:resolVI 潜在空间在 scib-metrics 生物保守性与「低/高错配率细胞之间的整合」上优于 PCA、scVI、scANVI(把 α1+α2>20% 定义为高错配率细胞作伪批次);下丘脑小胶质细胞中的神经元标志物 Slc17a6 假阳性被大幅去除,Trem2 保留;同时**保留了**单细胞参照里没有、但有文献支持的内皮细胞 Lyz2 共表达。resolVI 估计的虚假信号比例与(基于标志物知识的)假双阳性率单调相关,而它本身不使用标志物信息。 <!-- EN --> **Xenium mouse brain**: the resolVI latent space beats PCA, scVI and scANVI on scib-metrics bioconservation and on "integration" between low- and high-misassignment cells (α1+α2 > 20% used as a pseudo-batch); false-positive neuronal Slc17a6 in hypothalamic microglia is largely removed while Trem2 is kept; literature-supported endothelial Lyz2 co-expression absent from the scRNA reference is **preserved**. resolVI's estimated spurious fraction rises with the marker-based false double-positive rate, although it never sees marker knowledge.
- <!-- ZH --> **分割依赖**:利用表达的分割(Baysor、ProSeg)本身降低假双阳性,但残留仍在;resolVI 在每种分割之后都能进一步降低。作者报告 ovrlpy 与 ProSeg 自带的校正「未能去除大部分错误信号」。在肝癌 MERSCOPE 上,Baysor+resolVI 最好,但作者在讨论中**推荐实践中先用 ProSeg**,因为 Baysor 会产生大量少分子细胞、虚增细胞数。 <!-- EN --> **Segmentation dependence**: expression-aware segmentation (Baysor, ProSeg) lowers false double positives but leaves residue; resolVI reduces it further after every segmentation. The authors report that ovrlpy and ProSeg's own correction "fail to remove a majority of erroneous signals". On liver-cancer MERSCOPE, Baysor + resolVI performed best, yet the Discussion **recommends ProSeg in practice**, since Baysor inflates cell counts with many low-molecule cells.
- <!-- ZH --> **测序型 ST**:Stereo-seq 全基因运行 <20 分钟、内存 <30 GB;Slc17a7+ 细胞中同时 Mbp+ 的比例从 **14.8% 降到 <0.01%**;Visium HD 8 µm bin 上 20 分钟训练完,分出 VIP、SST、NPY 抑制性神经元亚群。 <!-- EN --> **Sequencing-based ST**: on Stereo-seq with all genes it runs in <20 min and <30 GB; the fraction of Slc17a7+ cells that are also Mbp+ falls from **14.8% to <0.01%**; on Visium HD 8-µm bins it trains in 20 min and resolves VIP, SST and NPY inhibitory subsets.
- <!-- ZH --> **CosMx 肝癌**:去除背景后胆管细胞标志物(SOX4、KRT13、EPCAM)回到门管周围分布;肿瘤中 SPP1–CD68 共表达在校正后**仍保留**(肿瘤相关巨噬细胞表达 SPP1,真实生物学);hotspot 找到两种肿瘤细胞空间模块 —— 低氧免疫抑制区(MIF、ENO1、SPP1、VEGFA)与干扰素促炎区(MX1、IFI6、STAT1),并与巨噬细胞、中性粒细胞、T 细胞亚群的共定位对应。 <!-- EN --> **CosMx liver cancer**: after background removal, cholangiocyte markers (SOX4, KRT13, EPCAM) return to a periportal pattern; tumour SPP1–CD68 co-expression is **retained** after correction (tumour-associated macrophages genuinely express SPP1); hotspot reveals two tumour-cell spatial modules — a hypoxic immunosuppressive region (MIF, ENO1, SPP1, VEGFA) and an interferon pro-inflammatory region (MX1, IFI6, STAT1) — matched by co-localizing macrophage, neutrophil and T-cell subsets.
- <!-- ZH --> **DSS 结肠炎(140 万细胞,RTX 3090 上训练不到 6 小时)**:day 35 被描述为「几乎完全恢复」,但 resolVI 的差异邻域分析显示隐窝顶端成纤维细胞(Fibro 2)从隐窝间迁移到黏膜下层聚集、Bmp2/5/7 表达下降,并伴随高结肠细胞密度区之外屏障基因(Cldn4、Cldn23、Tnfaip3、Il22ra1)表达降低 —— 即**宏观恢复后组织并未真正回到基线**。 <!-- EN --> **DSS colitis (1.4M cells, <6 h training on an RTX 3090)**: day 35 is described as "almost fully recovered", yet resolVI's differential niche analysis shows crypt-tip fibroblasts (Fibro 2) relocating from between crypts into submucosal aggregates with reduced Bmp2/5/7, alongside lower barrier-gene expression (Cldn4, Cldn23, Tnfaip3, Il22ra1) outside colonocyte-dense regions — i.e. **the tissue has not truly returned to baseline after macroscopic recovery**.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把「分子错配」显式建模成可估计的扩散 + 背景分量,并且与分割算法解耦,任何分割都能接。 <!-- EN --> Models molecule misassignment explicitly as estimable diffusion + background components, decoupled from segmentation so it plugs in after any segmenter.
- <!-- ZH --> **每个细胞都得到一个错配比例 α1+α2** —— 一个不依赖标志物知识、却与假双阳性率一致的「技术伪影分数」。 <!-- EN --> **Every cell gets a misassignment fraction α1+α2** — a "technical-artifact score" that needs no marker knowledge yet tracks the false double-positive rate.
- <!-- ZH --> 同一个生成模型同时做去噪、去批次、半监督注释、query 映射、差异表达和差异邻域丰度检验。 <!-- EN --> One generative model handles denoising, batch correction, semi-supervised annotation, query mapping, differential expression and differential niche abundance.
- <!-- ZH --> 跨成像型与测序型共六类平台验证,并扩展到百万级细胞。 <!-- EN --> Validated across imaging- and sequencing-based platforms (six technologies) and scales to millions of cells.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **校正质量依赖初始分割**(作者自陈);原始分割下仍残留成纤维细胞–髓系/内皮共表达。 <!-- EN --> **Correction quality depends on the initial segmentation** (stated by the authors); residual fibroblast–myeloid/endothelial co-expression remains with the original segmentation.
- <!-- ZH --> 依赖 VAE 瓶颈,对潜在空间设定敏感;作者建议用监督模式缓解。 <!-- EN --> Relies on the VAE bottleneck and is sensitive to the latent-space choice; the authors suggest the supervised mode as mitigation.
- <!-- ZH --> 「错误共表达」的基准由文献标志物和 scRNA 共表达构造,作者承认这些基因对**高度依赖情境**,真实数据中没有 ground truth。 <!-- EN --> The "erroneous co-expression" benchmark is built from literature markers and scRNA co-expression; the authors admit these gene pairs are **highly context-dependent** and real data lack ground truth.
- <!-- ZH --> 与其他工具的比较由作者自己做;另一组(Bilous 等,本仓库 `xenium-split-natmethods-2026`)的独立比较显示 resolVI 会显著减少表达基因数、产生近空细胞,iLISI 有时略差,并偶尔同时压掉污染和真实 T 细胞程序。 <!-- EN --> Comparisons to other tools are run by the authors; an independent comparison by another group (Bilous et al., `xenium-split-natmethods-2026` in this repo) finds resolVI substantially reduces detected genes, leaves near-empty cells, sometimes slightly worsens iLISI, and occasionally suppresses both contaminating and genuine T-cell programs.
- <!-- ZH --> 没有肾脏数据;部分图的底层数据仅「合理请求」提供。 <!-- EN --> No kidney data; data for some figures are available only on reasonable request.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是 **detect** 这一步之前必须做的「去伪影」层。对 **伪影注入课题**(肾脏 Xenium + snRNA/snATAC + H&E 异常检测)来说,最直接的风险是:在 Xenium 上找出的「异常细胞」其实只是分割/扩散伪影 —— 例如一个近端小管细胞因为紧贴浸润的巨噬细胞而「表达」CD68。resolVI 给每个细胞的 **α1+α2** 正好是一个与疾病无关的**技术伪影协变量**:(1) 可在 KidneyAnomalyBench 中作为「artifact」类的弱标签或分层变量,检验异常分数是否只是在追踪 α;(2) 可与 snRNA 参照、H&E 形态做**跨模态一致性**判断 —— 高异常 + 高 α + snRNA 中不存在该共表达 = 优先判为伪影;高异常 + 低 α + snRNA/snATAC 支持 = 疾病候选。**需要警惕的张力**:肾病里真正的异常状态(例如 `inflammatory-pt-natcommun-2025` 中表达趋化因子、ICAM1、贴着巨噬细胞的炎症性 PT 细胞)恰恰就是「混合样」表型,也正位于免疫细胞密集的纤维化 niche —— 过度校正会把真实异常抹掉。所以 resolVI 应作为**对照条件**(校正前后各跑一次异常检测,比较哪些异常稳定)而不是默认预处理。DSS 结肠炎的结果对 **revert** 也有启示:「宏观恢复」不等于「niche 恢复」,评估逆转时应看邻域组成而不仅是细胞比例。对 **Agent 可信度课题**:是否做了错配校正、用哪种分割,会直接改变共表达类结论;agent 分析空间组学时若不声明这一步,是一个很具体的 omics 特有失败模式;而且 VAE 训练有随机性,适合纳入 rerun stability 检验。
<!-- EN --> This is the de-artifacting layer that must precede **detect**. For **the artifact-injection topic** (anomaly detection on kidney Xenium + snRNA/snATAC + H&E) the most immediate risk is that "anomalous cells" found on Xenium are just segmentation/diffusion artifacts — e.g. a proximal-tubule cell "expressing" CD68 because it abuts an infiltrating macrophage. resolVI's per-cell **α1+α2** is exactly a disease-agnostic **technical-artifact covariate**: (1) usable in KidneyAnomalyBench as a weak label or stratifier for an "artifact" class, testing whether an anomaly score merely tracks α; (2) combinable with the snRNA reference and H&E morphology for **cross-modal agreement** — high anomaly + high α + co-expression absent in snRNA → likely artifact; high anomaly + low α + snRNA/snATAC support → disease candidate. **A tension to watch**: genuine kidney-disease states (e.g. the chemokine/ICAM1-expressing inflammatory PT cells of `inflammatory-pt-natcommun-2025`, sitting next to macrophages) are themselves "mixed-looking" phenotypes located in immune-dense fibrotic niches — over-correction could erase real anomalies. So resolVI belongs in a **controlled comparison** (run anomaly detection before and after correction, keep the anomalies that are stable), not as silent default preprocessing. The DSS-colitis result also informs **revert**: "macroscopic recovery" is not "niche recovery", so reversion should be judged on neighbourhood composition, not only cell proportions. For **the agent-trustworthiness topic**: whether misassignment correction was applied, and with which segmentation, directly changes co-expression claims; an agent that analyses spatial omics without declaring this step exhibits a concrete omics-specific failure mode, and VAE stochasticity makes it a natural target for rerun-stability checks.
