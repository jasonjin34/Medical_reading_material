## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Susztak 实验室(UPenn)的人肾多组学 + 空间图谱:来自 58 名参与者(补充表 1 实际列出 57 个 ID)的 81 份样本,做了 scRNA-seq、snRNA-seq、snATAC-seq(三者独立建库,不是 multiome)、Visium FFPE 和 CosMx。整合后的 SCVI 图谱有 338,565 个细胞/细胞核、44 种主要细胞类型和 114 种亚型/状态。Visium 经 NMF 分出四种微环境(肾小球 / 小管 / 纤维化 / 免疫)。由纤维化微环境得到的 FME 基因签名(FME-GS),在 fibrosis < 10% 的 245 例 bulk 小管样本中仍能预测肾功能下降(HR = 3.95),而病理纤维化评分做不到(HR = 1.17)。
<!-- EN --> A UPenn (Susztak lab) multi-omic and spatial atlas of the human kidney: 81 samples from 58 participants (Supplementary Table 1 actually lists 57 IDs) profiled by scRNA-seq, snRNA-seq and snATAC-seq (three separate libraries, not multiome), Visium FFPE and CosMx. The integrated SCVI atlas holds 338,565 cells/nuclei in 44 main types and 114 subtypes/states. NMF on Visium finds four microenvironments (glomerular, tubule, fibrotic, immune). A fibrotic-microenvironment gene signature (FME-GS) predicts kidney-function decline in 245 bulk tubule samples with fibrosis < 10% (HR = 3.95), where the pathologist's fibrosis score does not (HR = 1.17).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 慢性肾病(CKD)的 75% 以上由糖尿病和高血压引起,纤维化是所有进展性 CKD 的共同终点。但纤维化灶的细胞组成和空间组织还不清楚:过去的工作主要盯着 ECM 堆积。解离式单细胞数据又丢掉了位置信息,所以只按解剖位置定义的细胞(系膜细胞、PEC、各类成纤维细胞)很难给出分子身份。临床上,早期 CKD(eGFR 尚保留、纤维化 < 10%)的预后很难判断,病理纤维化评分的预测价值也不稳定。
<!-- EN --> Diabetes and hypertension cause > 75% of CKD, and fibrosis is the common end-stage of all progressive CKD. Yet the cellular make-up and spatial organisation of fibrotic lesions are poorly known: prior work focused on ECM accumulation. Dissociated single-cell data also drop location, so cells defined only by anatomy (mesangial cells, PECs, fibroblast subtypes) are hard to pin to a molecular identity. Clinically, prognosis in early CKD (preserved eGFR, fibrosis < 10%) is hard to call, and the pathologist's fibrosis score predicts outcome inconsistently.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **湿实验**:scRNA-seq 用**新鲜**肾组织(0.5 g),Miltenyi Multi Tissue Dissociation Kit 1 在 37 °C 酶解 30 min,再做 RBC lysis,活率 > 80%。snRNA-seq 用**冻存**组织(10–30 mg),NP40 裂解后 gentleMACS 匀浆。两者都用 10x Chromium Next GEM 3′ v3.1(Chip G),在 NovaSeq 6000 上测序。snATAC-seq 用同一套核提取流程,10x Chromium Single Cell ATAC(Chip E),**和 RNA 是分开建库的,不是 multiome**。Visium 是 FFPE 探针版,样本要求 DV200 > 50%,切片 5 µm。CosMx 用约 1,000 基因的 universal cell characterization panel,另加 50 个自定义探针。IMC(Hyperion)和免疫荧光做蛋白验证。
**生信**(补充材料):Cell Ranger v6.0.1 / Cell Ranger ATAC。ambient RNA 用 SoupX v1.5.0 校正,doublet 用 DoubletFinder v2.0(默认参数)去除。阈值为 nFeature 在 200–3000 之间、线粒体比例 sn < 15% 且 sc < 50%、核糖体比例 < 15%。之后先去掉同时表达多类 marker 的簇,再把注释与已发表参照的 label transfer 结果比对,不一致的细胞视为 doublet 剔除。sn 和 sc 分别用 Harmony 处理。三种模态再用 SCVI(v0.20.3,covariates 为 sample、nFeature_RNA、nCount_RNA、tech)整合成一个 atlas,并与 KPMP 合并成约 60 万个细胞的版本。Visium 用 Cell2location 反卷积、CellTrek 映射到近单细胞分辨率(149,717 个点),再用 STutility NMF 划分微环境。细胞通讯用 CellChat 和 COMMOT,iPT 轨迹用 Monocle3,FME-GS 用 bulk 层次聚类,预后用 Kaplan–Meier/Cox,另做 LASSO。
<!-- EN --> **Wet lab**: scRNA-seq uses **fresh** tissue (0.5 g) digested with Miltenyi Multi Tissue Dissociation Kit 1 at 37 °C for 30 min, then RBC lysis, giving > 80% viability. snRNA-seq uses **frozen** tissue (10–30 mg), NP40 lysis and gentleMACS homogenisation. Both run on 10x Chromium Next GEM 3′ v3.1 (Chip G) and are sequenced on NovaSeq 6000. snATAC-seq uses the same nuclei prep with 10x Chromium Single Cell ATAC (Chip E), in **libraries separate from RNA, not multiome**. Visium is the FFPE probe-based assay, with samples required to have DV200 > 50% and 5 µm sections. CosMx uses the ~1,000-gene universal cell characterization panel plus 50 custom probes. IMC (Hyperion) and immunofluorescence provide protein validation.
**Bioinformatics** (Supplementary Note): Cell Ranger v6.0.1 / Cell Ranger ATAC. Ambient RNA is corrected with SoupX v1.5.0 and doublets are removed with DoubletFinder v2.0 (default parameters). Thresholds are nFeature 200–3000, mitochondrial fraction < 15% (sn) and < 50% (sc), and ribosomal fraction < 15%. Clusters co-expressing markers of several cell types are then removed, and cells whose annotation disagrees with label transfer from a published reference are dropped as doublets. Harmony is applied to sn and sc separately. SCVI (v0.20.3, with sample, nFeature_RNA, nCount_RNA and tech as covariates) integrates the three modalities into one atlas, and a version merged with KPMP has ~600k cells. Visium is deconvolved with Cell2location and mapped to near single-cell resolution with CellTrek (149,717 points); STutility NMF then splits it into microenvironments. Cell communication uses CellChat and COMMOT, the iPT trajectory uses Monocle3, FME-GS is clustered on bulk data, prognosis uses Kaplan–Meier/Cox, and LASSO is run on top.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **图谱**:338,565 个细胞/细胞核,6 个超家族,44 种主要类型,114 种亚型/状态。图谱中有 11 种内皮细胞(包括 FBLN5+ 入/出球小动脉和 MCTP1+ 直小血管)、稀有的 EPO 产生细胞、SLC47A2-high PT,以及完整的免疫亚群;与 KPMP 整合后共 588,425 个细胞。 <!-- EN --> **Atlas**: 338,565 cells/nuclei in 6 superfamilies, 44 main types and 114 subtypes/states. It includes 11 endothelial types (FBLN5+ afferent/efferent arterioles, MCTP1+ vasa recta), rare EPO-producing cells, SLC47A2-high PT and a full set of immune subsets; integrated with KPMP it holds 588,425 cells.
- <!-- ZH --> **间质**:共 12 种亚型,找到能区分它们的 marker:系膜细胞为 ITGA8/POSTN,VSMC/周细胞为 MYH11/NOTCH3/NTRK3,成纤维细胞为 KCNK2/FAP,肌成纤维细胞为 COL1A1/SYNPO2。肌成纤维细胞的 ECM 评分最高,且在疾病样本中比例更高。 <!-- EN --> **Stroma**: 12 subtypes, with markers that separate them: mesangial ITGA8/POSTN, VSMC/pericyte MYH11/NOTCH3/NTRK3, fibroblast KCNK2/FAP, myofibroblast COL1A1/SYNPO2. Myofibroblasts have the highest ECM score and are more frequent in disease samples.
- <!-- ZH --> **FME**:NMF 分出的纤维化微环境与病理纤维化评分相关(r = 0.75,P = 0.003)。FME 里同时有成纤维细胞、肌成纤维细胞、内皮、免疫细胞(早期三级淋巴结构样聚集)和 iPT,并富集 SPP1、CXCL12、CCL19/21、PDGFB 和 TGFB1 信号。 <!-- EN --> **FME**: the NMF fibrotic microenvironment correlates with the pathologist's fibrosis score (r = 0.75, P = 0.003). It contains fibroblasts, myofibroblasts, endothelium, immune cells (early tertiary-lymphoid-like aggregates) and iPT, and is enriched for SPP1, CXCL12, CCL19/21, PDGFB and TGFB1 signalling.
- <!-- ZH --> **iPT 两种亚型**:iPT_VCAM1+ 是代谢型,"present in healthy samples";iPT_HAVCR1+(KIM1)偏黏附和基质,主要出现在 CKD 样本和 FME 中。Monocle 显示一条 PT → iPT 的连续轨迹。CosMx 进一步分出 iPT_APOE、iPT_SPP1 和 iPT_KRT7,三者的邻居组成不同。 <!-- EN --> **Two iPT subtypes**: iPT_VCAM1+ is metabolic and "present in healthy samples"; iPT_HAVCR1+ (KIM1) is adhesion/matrix-like and found mostly in CKD samples and the FME. Monocle shows a continuous PT → iPT path. CosMx further splits iPT into iPT_APOE, iPT_SPP1 and iPT_KRT7, which have different neighbours.
- <!-- ZH --> **预后**:在 292 例 bulk 样本上,按 FME-GS 聚类得到的 cluster 1 到达终点的概率约高 4 倍(终点为 eGFR 下降 40% 或 ESRD)。在 245 例 fibrosis < 10% 的样本上,FME-GS 的 HR = 3.95(95% CI 1.71–9.12),病理纤维化评分的 HR = 1.17(0.49–2.83)。只用 LASSO 选出的前 10 个基因仍然显著(P = 0.021),随机基因集则不显著。 <!-- EN --> **Prognosis**: in 292 bulk samples, the FME-GS cluster 1 is ~4× more likely to reach the endpoint (40% eGFR decline or ESRD). In 245 samples with fibrosis < 10%, FME-GS gives HR = 3.95 (95% CI 1.71–9.12) and the pathologist's fibrosis score gives HR = 1.17 (0.49–2.83). The top 10 LASSO genes alone remain significant (P = 0.021); a random gene set does not.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自己承认:HKD 未必是单一疾病;FME-GS 需要外部队列验证;iPT 和免疫细胞的功能需要机制实验。 <!-- EN --> Admitted by the authors: HKD may not be a single entity; FME-GS needs external-cohort validation; the function of iPT and immune cells needs mechanistic experiments.
- <!-- ZH --> 单细胞统计 "treat each cell as an independent observation"(伪重复),组间比例差异的 P 值偏乐观。 <!-- EN --> Single-cell statistics "treat each cell as an independent observation" (pseudo-replication), so P-values for between-group proportion differences are optimistic.
- <!-- ZH --> 设计层面有混杂:sc 用新鲜酶解,sn 用冻存组织,两者的组别构成不同。早期 HK1xxx 批次几乎全是 sc,后期 HK28xx 批次 sc 和 sn 都有。补充表 2 中,对照组 scRNA 文库的 median genes/cell 中位数约 358,疾病组约 598,有的对照低到 17(HK2662)和 70(HK2899 SC)。也就是说,**测量质量与疾病标签相关**。 <!-- EN --> Design confounds: sc uses fresh enzymatic digestion and sn uses frozen tissue, and the two have different group mixes. The early HK1xxx batch is almost all sc, while the later HK28xx batch has both sc and sn. In Supplementary Table 2, control scRNA libraries have a median of median-genes/cell of ~358 versus ~598 for disease, with some controls as low as 17 (HK2662) and 70 (HK2899 SC). In other words, **measurement quality is correlated with the disease label**.
- <!-- ZH --> 计数在正文、补充表、Reporting Summary 和 GEO 之间多处不一致(见"数据"一节)。原始 FASTQ 未公开("Raw data were not provided due to privacy concerns")。 <!-- EN --> Counts disagree across the text, supplementary tables, Reporting Summary and GEO (see Data). Raw FASTQs are not released ("Raw data were not provided due to privacy concerns").
- <!-- ZH --> "健康"对照其实是肿瘤肾切除的旁组织,而且 25 名对照中有 8 名标注了高血压;作者也观察到对照中存在 iPT_VCAM1+ 细胞以及低度纤维化。 <!-- EN --> "Healthy" controls are tumour-nephrectomy adjacent tissue, and 8 of 25 control donors are marked hypertensive; the authors also see iPT_VCAM1+ cells and low-grade fibrosis in controls.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> **位置**:属于 detect 阶段的数据基础。在课题 1(人肾单细胞基准,检验异常检测器能否把疾病和测量/取材伪影分开,并在供体层面用 conformal 控制误报)中,它是**第二队列**:用另一个实验室的健康供体来检验误报率能否迁移。
**有利之处**:(1) 对照组规模可用。按供体计,sc 有 14 人、sn 有 7 人,合计 16 人。有 5 名对照同时做了 sc 和 sn,可以作为**同一供体、不同 protocol**的天然配对,直接测量"protocol 伪影会不会触发误报"。(2) 有细胞级 QC 协变量(nCount、nFeature、percent_mt)和 tech 标签,可以用来构造伪影轴。(3) 取材是肿瘤肾切除,与 KPMP 的活体活检和 Lake 2023 的混合来源不同,正好可以作为"取材方式"这一 shift。
**风险**(必须写进 benchmark 设计):(a) 对照组 sc 文库质量系统性偏低,如果直接比较疾病和对照,检测器可能学到的是质量而不是疾病,所以应该先在对照内部做 conformal 校准。(b) GEO 上的对象已经过 SoupX 校正(metadata 里的 nCount_RNA 是非整数),也已经过 DoubletFinder、nFeature ≤ 3000 截断、按线粒体比例过滤和 marker 层面的 doublet 剔除。也就是说,**伪影已被部分清掉,而且各样本的处理方式不同**,没有 FASTQ 就无法统一重处理。(c) 每个模态的供体数很少(sn 对照只有 7 人),供体层面 conformal 的校准集太小,只能做 leave-one-donor-out,或者把 sc 和 sn 合并后按 tech 分层。(d) 有些样本细胞数极少(HK2976 SN 只有 12 个、HK2899 SC 只有 183 个、HK1635_T SC 有 491 个),应设最小细胞数门槛。(e) 对照里有高血压患者和 iPT_VCAM1+ 细胞,"健康"标签本身有噪声,需要按 eGFR/fibrosis 做敏感性分析。(f) GEO 这个 series 还被 4 篇其他 PMID 引用,同实验室的其他数据可能与本数据有供体重叠(**未核实**),合并队列前需要按 HK ID 去重。
<!-- EN --> **Stage**: the data foundation for the detect stage. In Topic 1 (a human-kidney single-cell benchmark testing whether anomaly detectors separate disease from measurement/procurement artifacts, with donor-level conformal false-alarm control), it is the **second cohort**: healthy donors from a different lab test whether the false-alarm rate transfers.
**What helps**: (1) The control arm is usable: 14 sc donors and 7 sn donors, 16 unique. Five controls have both sc and sn, giving natural **same-donor, different-protocol** pairs that directly test whether protocol artifacts trigger false alarms. (2) Cell-level QC covariates (nCount, nFeature, percent_mt) and a tech label are available for building artifact axes. (3) The tissue comes from tumour nephrectomy, unlike KPMP living-donor biopsies or Lake 2023's mixed sources, so it provides a "procurement" shift.
**Risks** (these belong in the benchmark design): (a) Control sc libraries are systematically lower quality, so a detector compared naively on disease versus control may learn quality rather than disease; calibrate the conformal step within controls first. (b) The GEO objects are already SoupX-corrected (nCount_RNA is non-integer in the metadata) and already filtered by DoubletFinder, an nFeature ≤ 3000 cap, mitochondrial-fraction thresholds and marker-based doublet removal. **Artifacts are therefore partly removed, unevenly across samples**, and without FASTQs nothing can be reprocessed uniformly. (c) Each modality has few donors (only 7 sn controls), so the calibration set for donor-level conformal is tiny; use leave-one-donor-out, or pool sc and sn and stratify by tech. (d) Some samples have very few cells (HK2976 SN 12, HK2899 SC 183, HK1635_T SC 491); set a minimum-cell threshold. (e) Controls include hypertensive donors and iPT_VCAM1+ cells, so the "healthy" label is itself noisy; run sensitivity analyses on eGFR/fibrosis. (f) The GEO series is also linked to four other PMIDs, and other datasets from the same lab may share donors (**unverified**); deduplicate by HK ID before merging cohorts.
