## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 用 DDIM 的**确定性加噪**把病人图像编码到噪声水平 L,再在去噪时加**分类器引导**把它"翻译"成健康类,输入与生成图之差就是像素级异常图 —— 一个只需图像级标签(健康/患病)的弱监督异常检测方法,作者称是首个用于异常检测的扩散模型。
<!-- EN --> DDIM's **deterministic noising** encodes a patient image to noise level L; denoising with **classifier guidance** then "translates" it to the healthy class, and the difference between input and output is a pixel-level anomaly map — a weakly supervised AD method needing only image-level (healthy/diseased) labels, described by the authors as the first diffusion model for anomaly detection.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 医学图像的像素级标注难得、常缺失且带有标注者偏差,因此只用图像级标签的弱监督异常检测很有吸引力。当时的方法主要靠自编码器(重建往往模糊、丢细节)或 GAN(训练难、要大量调参,图像翻译还需额外的循环一致性损失与架构改动)。核心要求是:**只改病变区域、其余全部保留**,这样"原图 − 翻译图"才是干净的异常图。
<!-- EN --> Pixel-wise annotation in medical imaging is hard to get, often missing and biased by annotators, so weakly supervised AD with only image-level labels is attractive. Existing methods relied on autoencoders (blurry reconstructions that lose detail) or GANs (hard to train, heavy tuning, extra cycle-consistency losses and architecture changes for image translation). The core requirement: **change only the pathological region and preserve everything else**, so that "input − translated image" is a clean anomaly map.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **训练两部分(都用健康 + 患病两类、只有图像级标签)**:(1) 按 Nichol & Dhariwal 的设置训练一个类条件 DDPM(T=1000,hybrid loss,Adam,lr 10⁻⁴,batch 10,首层 128 通道、16 分辨率处一个注意力头,共 113,681,160 参数),50,000 次迭代;(2) 在**加噪图像** x_t(所有 t)上训练一个二分类器 C(5,452,962 参数),20,000 次迭代;合计在一张 Quadro RTX 6000 上约一天。**推理(算法 1)**:给定输入 x、健康类 h、梯度尺度 s 与噪声水平 L ——先用 DDIM 的**逆向 ODE**(σ_t=0 的确定性更新)从 t=0 走到 L,把解剖信息"编码进噪声";再从 L 去噪回 0,每步用 ε̂ = ε_θ(x_t,t) − s·√(1−ᾱ_t)·∇log C(h|x_t,t) 做分类器引导,得到健康版本 x₀;**异常图** a = Σ_channels |x − x₀|。关键权衡:L 太小没有自由度去掉肿瘤,太大会破坏图像;s 太小去不掉肿瘤,太大在脑边界引入伪影。所有实验用 **L=500、s=100**。消融:把确定性编码换成 DDPM 的随机加噪(仍用分类器引导采样)。
<!-- EN --> **Two trained components (both use healthy + diseased images with image-level labels only)**: (1) a class-conditional DDPM following Nichol & Dhariwal (T=1000, hybrid loss, Adam, lr 10⁻⁴, batch 10, 128 channels in the first layer, one attention head at resolution 16; 113,681,160 parameters), 50,000 iterations; (2) a binary classifier C trained on **noisy images** x_t for all t (5,452,962 parameters), 20,000 iterations; about one day on a Quadro RTX 6000. **Inference (Algorithm 1)**: given input x, healthy class h, gradient scale s and noise level L — first run DDIM's **reversed ODE** (deterministic updates with σ_t=0) from t=0 to L to "encode the anatomy in noise"; then denoise from L to 0 with classifier guidance ε̂ = ε_θ(x_t,t) − s·√(1−ᾱ_t)·∇log C(h|x_t,t) to obtain the healthy version x₀; **anomaly map** a = Σ_channels |x − x₀|. Key trade-off: too small an L leaves no freedom to remove the tumour, too large destroys the image; too small an s fails to remove it, too large introduces artifacts at the brain border. All experiments use **L=500, s=100**. Ablation: replace deterministic encoding with DDPM stochastic noising (still sampling with classifier guidance).

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> **范式:重建 / 生成式**(更准确地说,是"弱监督的**条件翻译到健康**",带有分类器引导这一 classifier-based 成分)。建立在 DDPM(Ho 等)、改进 DDPM(Nichol & Dhariwal)、DDIM(Song 等,确定性采样即 ODE 求解、可逆编码)和 Dhariwal & Nichol 的分类器引导之上;对照方法是 Fixed-Point GAN(GAN 图像翻译)与 Chen & Konukoglu 的约束对抗自编码器 VAE。与 AnoGAN / f-AnoGAN 相比:同样是"投影到正常再取残差",但(a)需要患病样本与图像级标签来训练分类器,不再是纯"只看正常";(b)用确定性 DDIM 编码保留细节,替代 GAN 的 latent 搜索或编码器。它与同年的 AnoDDPM(纯无监督、部分加噪、simplex 噪声)一起开启了扩散异常检测;本仓库的 AnoPILaD(histo-miccai-2025,沿用 AnoDDPM 的部分加噪 + 文本条件)与 MoPaDi(counterfactual-diffusion-cancerres-2026,扩散自编码器 + 分类器方向生成 counterfactual)都处在这条"扩散 + 条件 → 正常/反事实版本"的谱系里,但两者原文并未声明以本文为基础。
<!-- EN --> **Paradigm: reconstruction / generative** (more precisely, weakly supervised **conditional translation to healthy**, with a classifier-based component via guidance). It builds on DDPM (Ho et al.), improved DDPM (Nichol & Dhariwal), DDIM (Song et al.: deterministic sampling as an ODE solve, enabling invertible encoding) and Dhariwal & Nichol's classifier guidance; comparators are Fixed-Point GAN (GAN image translation) and Chen & Konukoglu's constrained adversarial autoencoder VAE. Versus AnoGAN / f-AnoGAN: still "project to normal, take the residual", but (a) it needs diseased samples and image-level labels to train the classifier — no longer "normal-only"; (b) deterministic DDIM encoding preserves detail, replacing GAN latent search or an encoder. Together with the same-year AnoDDPM (purely unsupervised, partial noising, simplex noise) it opened diffusion-based AD; in this repo AnoPILaD (histo-miccai-2025, following AnoDDPM's partial noising plus text conditioning) and MoPaDi (counterfactual-diffusion-cancerres-2026, a diffusion autoencoder plus classifier directions for counterfactuals) sit in the same "diffusion + conditioning → normal/counterfactual version" lineage, though neither states it builds on this paper.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **CheXpert**(胸部 X 光,胸腔积液):训练 14,179 名健康对照 + 16,776 名胸腔积液患者,256×256,归一化到 [0,1];测试每类 200 张。**BRATS2020**(脑肿瘤 MRI,T1、T2、FLAIR、T1ce 四通道):只用轴向 2D 切片,补到 256×256 并归一化;去掉最低 80 张与最高 26 张切片;切片无肿瘤标注即为健康,其余为患病;训练 5,598 健康 + 10,607 患病切片,测试 1,082 含肿瘤 + 705 无肿瘤切片。像素级标签只在 BRATS 上用于评估。
<!-- EN --> **CheXpert** (chest X-ray, pleural effusion): training 14,179 healthy controls + 16,776 effusion patients, 256×256, normalised to [0,1]; test 200 images per class. **BRATS2020** (brain-tumour MRI, four channels T1, T2, FLAIR, T1ce): axial 2D slices only, padded to 256×256 and normalised; the lowest 80 and uppermost 26 slices excluded; a slice with no tumour in the label mask is healthy, otherwise diseased; training 5,598 healthy + 10,607 diseased slices, test 1,082 tumour + 705 tumour-free slices. Pixel labels are used for evaluation only, on BRATS.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **定性(图 2、3)**:与 FP-GAN、VAE 和 DDPM 随机采样消融相比,本方法生成的健康图更真实,且保留了输入的全部细节,得到更细致的异常图;其他方法要么改动了图像的其他部分,要么找不到异常;DDPM 随机加噪会改变基本解剖结构,而 DDIM 确定性编码能重建所有细节。**健康输入(图 7)**:对无肿瘤切片几乎不做改动,异常图接近零 —— 这是"只改病变区域"的关键自检。**超参敏感性(图 4)**:在 BRATS 上以平均 Otsu 阈值算 Dice、并算患病切片的 AUROC,随 s(在 L=250/500/750 下)变化,与 FP-GAN、VAE 的水平线比较;**正文没有给出数值表**,只有曲线。**速度**:一次完整的图像翻译约 **158 秒**,主要来自迭代生成;减小 L 或跳步可加速但会降低图像质量。作者也提到已有研究批评"以重建误差作异常分数"(直方图均衡化这种简单方法可超过神经网络,且重建质量与 Dice 相关性差),并计划探索基于似然的分数。
<!-- EN --> **Qualitative (Figs 2, 3)**: versus FP-GAN, VAE and the DDPM stochastic-sampling ablation, the method produces more realistic healthy images that preserve all input detail, giving very detailed anomaly maps; the others either alter other image regions or miss the anomaly; DDPM stochastic noising changes basic anatomy, whereas deterministic DDIM encoding reconstructs every detail. **Healthy input (Fig 7)**: tumour-free slices are left nearly unchanged with an anomaly map close to zero — the key self-check for "change only the lesion". **Hyper-parameter sensitivity (Fig 4)**: on BRATS, Dice (average Otsu threshold) and AUROC on diseased slices are plotted against s for L=250/500/750, with FP-GAN and VAE as horizontal bars; **no numeric table is given in the text**, only curves. **Speed**: one full translation takes about **158 s**, dominated by iterative generation; smaller L or skipped steps speed it up but degrade image quality. The authors also acknowledge prior criticism of reconstruction error as an anomaly score (simple histogram equalisation can beat neural networks, and reconstruction quality correlates poorly with Dice) and plan to explore likelihood-based scores.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 首个把扩散模型用于(医学)异常检测的工作(作者自述)。 <!-- EN --> The first diffusion model for (medical) anomaly detection, per the authors.
- <!-- ZH --> **确定性 DDIM 编码 + 分类器引导去噪**的组合,实现"细节保持"的病人→健康图像翻译。 <!-- EN --> **Deterministic DDIM encoding + classifier-guided denoising** for detail-preserving patient→healthy translation.
- <!-- ZH --> 不改损失函数和训练流程(直接用原始 DDPM 实现),换数据集几乎零成本。 <!-- EN --> No changes to the original DDPM loss or training scheme, so moving to new datasets is straightforward.
- <!-- ZH --> 把 L(编码深度)与 s(引导强度)明确为"保真 vs 翻译自由度"的两个旋钮,并给出可视化的敏感性分析。 <!-- EN --> Makes L (encoding depth) and s (guidance strength) explicit knobs for "fidelity vs translation freedom", with a visual sensitivity analysis.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 定量评估很薄:只有 BRATS 上的 Dice/AUROC 曲线,没有数值表、没有置信区间、CheXpert 只有定性结果。 <!-- EN --> Thin quantitative evaluation: only Dice/AUROC curves on BRATS, no numeric table or confidence intervals; CheXpert results are qualitative only.
- <!-- ZH --> 弱监督而非无监督:需要患病样本与图像级标签训练分类器 —— "异常"被限定为"分类器认为是患病的那一类",对未见过的异常类型没有保证。 <!-- EN --> Weakly supervised, not unsupervised: it needs diseased samples and image-level labels for the classifier — "anomaly" is defined as "what the classifier deems diseased", with no guarantee for unseen anomaly types.
- <!-- ZH --> 每张图约 158 秒,难以扩展到整张 WSI 或百万级细胞。 <!-- EN --> About 158 s per image, hard to scale to whole slides or millions of cells.
- <!-- ZH --> 只做 2D 切片;对 L 与 s 敏感,且最优值依数据而定;s 过大在脑边界产生伪影。 <!-- EN --> 2D slices only; sensitive to L and s, whose optimum is data-dependent; large s creates artifacts at the brain border.
- <!-- ZH --> 仍以"原图 − 重建"作异常分数,作者自己承认这一做法已受到质疑。 <!-- EN --> Still scores by "input − reconstruction", an approach the authors acknowledge has been criticised.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇是 detect → model → **revert** 三段里"revert"在**图像层面**最直观的演示:它显式地生成"同一组织若健康会是什么样",差值既是异常图,也是"需要被逆转的改变"。对本方向的意义在于**条件翻译**这个操作本身——把"健康"作为一个可引导的条件,而不仅仅是"在正常数据上训练"。**映射到课题 1**:H&E 层面可以直接照搬——在 KPMP/HuBMAP 健康与 CKD/AKI 活检 H&E 图块上训练扩散模型 + 带噪分类器,把患病图块翻译到健康;表达层面,对 scVI / 基础模型 latent 上的扩散模型做同样的分类器引导,就得到"每个细胞的健康反事实",差值即候选的 revert 基因方向(与 MrVI 的样本反事实思路相通)。**哪些假设会断**:(1) **图像级标签的含义**:肾活检的"患病"是患者级诊断,同一切片里绝大多数图块/细胞仍是正常的 —— 带噪分类器学到的是一个嘈杂的"患病概率",引导方向可能夹带批次或中心差异(若患者与健康样本来自不同中心,分类器会学到中心而非疾病);必须让健康与患病样本在批次上平衡,或在分类器里加批次对抗项。(2) **ambient RNA / doublet / 分割错误**:这些在表达上也会被分类器"翻译掉",使伪影与病变共用同一张差值图 —— 课题 1 的跨模态一致性可以把关:H&E 翻译差与表达翻译差在同一区域同时出现才算疾病。(3) **连续疾病态**:s 本质上控制"往健康走多远",在连续损伤轴上它反而有用 —— 可以把不同 s 下的差值看成沿损伤轴的剂量-反应;但没有标定就无法解释 s 的生物学含义。(4) **无像素/细胞级金标准**:本文的定量只依赖 BRATS 的肿瘤掩膜,而肾脏没有对应掩膜;KidneyAnomalyBench 需要专家来判定翻译出的"健康版本"是否合理。(5) **算力**:158 秒一张图,对整张 WSI 或百万级细胞必须用 latent diffusion 或少步采样。**仓库里已有的相近工作**:AnoPILaD(histo-miccai-2025)用 LoRA 微调的 Stable Diffusion + 病理 VLM 文本条件,沿用 AnoDDPM 部分加噪重建淋巴结正常形态;MoPaDi(counterfactual-diffusion-cancerres-2026)用扩散自编码器沿分类器方向生成病理 counterfactual,用于解释而非检测 —— 两者都不声明以本文为基础,但三者合起来说明"扩散 + 条件 → 健康/反事实版本"是病理图像上的可行路线;MedIAnomaly 也报告 DDPM 类方法在 Camelyon16 上明显优于 AE/GAN。
<!-- EN --> This is the most intuitive **image-level** demonstration of **revert** in detect → model → revert: it explicitly generates "what this tissue would look like if healthy", and the difference is both the anomaly map and "the change to be reverted". Its value here is the **conditional translation** operation itself — "healthy" as a steerable condition, not merely "trained on normal data". **Mapping to Topic 1**: at the H&E level it transfers almost directly — train a diffusion model and a noisy classifier on KPMP/HuBMAP healthy and CKD/AKI biopsy H&E patches and translate diseased patches to healthy; at the expression level, the same classifier guidance on a diffusion model over scVI / foundation-model latents yields "a healthy counterfactual for each cell", whose difference is a candidate revert-gene direction (akin to MrVI's sample counterfactuals). **Which assumptions break**: (1) **meaning of image-level labels**: "diseased" in a kidney biopsy is a patient-level diagnosis, and most patches/cells in the section are still normal — the noisy classifier learns a noisy "disease probability", and the guidance direction can carry batch or centre differences (if patients and healthy donors come from different centres, the classifier learns the centre, not the disease); healthy and diseased samples must be batch-balanced, or the classifier given a batch-adversarial term. (2) **ambient RNA / doublets / segmentation errors**: the classifier will also "translate away" these in expression space, so artifacts and lesions share one difference map — Topic 1's cross-modal agreement can gate this: count as disease only where the H&E translation difference and the expression translation difference co-occur. (3) **continuous disease states**: s controls "how far towards healthy", which is actually useful on a continuous injury axis — differences at several s can be read as a dose–response along the axis; without calibration, though, s has no biological meaning. (4) **no pixel/cell-level gold standard**: the paper's quantitative evaluation relies on BRATS tumour masks, which kidney data lack; KidneyAnomalyBench needs experts to judge whether the translated "healthy version" is plausible. (5) **compute**: 158 s per image means whole slides or millions of cells require latent diffusion or few-step sampling. **Related work already in the repo**: AnoPILaD (histo-miccai-2025) uses LoRA-tuned Stable Diffusion with pathology-VLM text conditioning and AnoDDPM-style partial noising to reconstruct normal lymph-node morphology; MoPaDi (counterfactual-diffusion-cancerres-2026) uses a diffusion autoencoder moved along classifier directions to produce pathology counterfactuals for explanation rather than detection — neither states it builds on this paper, but together they show "diffusion + conditioning → healthy/counterfactual version" is viable on pathology images; MedIAnomaly also reports DDPM-type methods clearly ahead of AE/GAN on Camelyon16.
