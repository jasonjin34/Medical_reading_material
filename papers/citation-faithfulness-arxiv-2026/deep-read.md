## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> OpenScholar、PaperQA2 这类文献综合 agent 及其基准都会「检查引用是否成立」,但没人审计这个**检查器本身**是否可靠。本文证明:同一批 agent 输出,仅因 verifier 严格度不同,未支持引用率就在 **约 3% 到约 18%** 之间变动;verifier 们在「哪些被支持」上一致,在「该标记哪些」上却几乎不一致(negative-specific agreement 0.27–0.30)。作者给出**以人工金标准为锚的评估协议** + 一个 **split-conformal 守卫**,对「漏过标记规则的真正未支持引用」给出无分布、有限样本的捕获率保证,并指出保证能否迁移取决于**校准负样本的难度**。
<!-- EN --> Literature-synthesis agents like OpenScholar and PaperQA2, and their benchmarks, all check whether citations hold — but nobody audits whether **the checker itself** is reliable. This paper shows that on identical agent outputs the unsupported-citation rate ranges from **~3% to ~18%** depending only on verifier strictness; verifiers agree on what is supported but barely agree on what to flag (negative-specific agreement 0.27–0.30). The authors give a **gold-anchored evaluation protocol** and a **split-conformal guard** that places a distribution-free, finite-sample guarantee on the catch rate of truly unsupported citations, and show that whether the guarantee transfers is governed by **calibration-negative difficulty**.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 三个缺口:(G1) 报告的未支持引用率依赖 verifier,单个数字无法跨研究比较;(G2) verifier 在该标记什么上分歧大,没有一组可信的部署标记集;(G3) 没有方法对「某个标记规则漏掉多少真正未支持的引用」给出无分布保证。作者强调贡献不在于「模型评审不完美」这一已知观察,而在于它对基准测试的后果,以及补救方案。
<!-- EN --> Three gaps: (G1) the reported unsupported-citation rate is verifier-dependent, so one number does not compare across studies; (G2) verifiers disagree on what to flag, so no deployment flag set can be trusted; (G3) no method gives a distribution-free guarantee on how many truly unsupported citations slip past a chosen flagging rule. The contribution is not the known observation that model graders are imperfect, but its consequence for benchmarking and a remedy.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **形式化**:把答案拆成 claim–citation 对 (c, pᵢ);verifier 给出二值判定 v(c,p) 及连续分数 s(c,p) ∈ [0,1]（支持概率）;金标签 y(c,p) 只在基准上有。**三处锚定到人工金标准**:(1) 用金标准验证 verifier,而非凭名气选;(2) 用带金标准支持段落的数据测 re-attribution;(3) 在金标准上校准保证。**Verifier 作为可替换仪器**:在**匹配的捕获率**下比较(而非各自默认阈值),若区分度相当就按成本选 —— 选用本地 3B 的 AttrScore。**守卫流程**(每个带引用的句子):verify(AttrScore-3B 检查 claim 与所引段落)→ 若不支持,re-attribute(在其余已提供段落中排序,提出替换指针,**再由 verifier 复核**后才移动指针,句子本身不改)→ 无法修复的**标记为 [N UNVERIFIED] 而非删除**。**Split-conformal 阈值**:在金标准校准集的**未支持类**分数上取 τ = s₍ₖ₎,k = ⌈(n_cal+1)(1−α)⌉;凡 s ≤ τ 即标记,则在可交换性假设下对新的未支持引用有 Pr[s_new ≤ τ] ≥ 1−α(命题 4.1,附证明;离散分数平局按保守方向处理)。保证的是**捕获率(安全轴)**,不是结论正确性。**Re-attribution 排序器**可替换:默认确定性 BM25,对比 verifier 分数与各生成模型的自我归因。
<!-- EN --> **Formalism**: answers decompose into claim–citation pairs (c, pᵢ); a verifier gives a binary v(c,p) and a continuous score s(c,p) ∈ [0,1] (probability of support); gold labels y(c,p) exist only on benchmarks. **Three gold anchors**: (1) validate the verifier against gold, not by reputation; (2) measure re-attribution against gold supporting passages; (3) calibrate the guarantee on gold. **The verifier as a swappable instrument**: compare verifiers at a **matched catch rate** rather than native thresholds; given comparable separation choose on cost — the local 3B AttrScore. **Guard** (per cited sentence): verify (AttrScore-3B, claim vs cited passage) → if unsupported, re-attribute (rank other provided passages, propose a replacement pointer, **re-verify** before moving it; the sentence is unchanged) → what cannot be repaired is **flagged [N UNVERIFIED], not deleted**. **Split-conformal threshold**: on gold calibration **unsupported-class** scores, τ = s₍ₖ₎ with k = ⌈(n_cal+1)(1−α)⌉; flag every s ≤ τ, giving Pr[s_new ≤ τ] ≥ 1−α under exchangeability (Proposition 4.1, with proof; ties broken conservatively). The guarantee is on **catch rate (the safety axis)**, not conclusion correctness. **Re-attribution ranker** is swappable: deterministic BM25 by default, compared with the verifier score and the generators' self-attribution.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **被审计系统**:四个 27–35B 开源指令模型(Gemma-4 两个、Qwen3.6 两个;稠密与 MoE 各二),单 GPU 本地运行;三种管线:bare(无检索下限)、OpenScholar、PaperQA2;**固定检索上下文**并复现各系统已发表的 prompt,只测生成阶段的引用行为;部署输出来自 ScholarQABench 多论文任务。**Verifier(5 个)**:AttrScore-3B(高召回)、OpenScholar post-hoc judge、GPT-4o、RAGAS、DeBERTa-NLI(高特异)。**金标准**:SciFact(208 对测试 + 802 对不相交划分,仅用于 prompt 选择/留出验证);QASA 全测试集 1,375 支持 + 1,375 未支持对(re-attribution 与 conformal 校准);PubMedQA 843(范围对照)。所有头条数字带 bootstrap 或 Wilson 95% CI。
<!-- EN --> **Systems audited**: four open 27–35B instruction models (two Gemma-4, two Qwen3.6; two dense, two MoE) run locally on one GPU; three pipelines: bare (no-retrieval floor), OpenScholar, PaperQA2; **retrieval context held fixed** with each system's published prompting, so only generation-time citation behaviour is measured; deployment outputs from ScholarQABench multi-paper tasks. **Five verifiers**: AttrScore-3B (high recall), OpenScholar post-hoc judge, GPT-4o, RAGAS, DeBERTa-NLI (high specificity). **Gold**: SciFact (208 test pairs + a disjoint 802 split for prompt selection / held-out validation); full QASA test set, 1,375 supported + 1,375 unsupported pairs (re-attribution and conformal calibration); PubMedQA 843 (scope control). Every headline number carries bootstrap or Wilson 95% CIs.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **Verifier 前沿(SciFact)**:支持类召回 / 特异性 —— AttrScore-3B 0.90 / 0.54、OpenScholar post-hoc 0.50 / 0.92、GPT-4o 0.46 / 0.92、RAGAS 0.30 / 0.96、DeBERTa-NLI 0.25 / 0.97;AttrScore 在留出 802 对上召回 0.94。ROC-AUC 相互重叠:AttrScore 0.88 [0.84, 0.93]、DeBERTa 0.90 [0.85, 0.94]、GPT-4o 0.87 [0.82, 0.92] —— **严格的前沿 judge(GPT-4o)并不带来区分优势**。金标准上分数均值:支持 0.54 vs 未支持 0.20。**未支持率依赖 verifier**:同一输出从高召回 verifier 下 bare 约 3% 单调升到严格 NLI 下约 14–18%;agentic 管线在每个设置下比 bare 高几个点;升高大部分来自严格 verifier **错误拒绝真正被支持的引用**。**分歧**:300 个部署句子上,三 verifier 两两 positive-specific agreement 0.90–0.96,negative-specific 仅 0.27–0.30 —— 一个 verifier 标记的引用中 **60–80%** 被另一个判为支持。**Conformal 保证(QASA)**:目标 0.90/0.94/0.98 → 留出实测 0.90/0.94/0.98;校准量 25 时朴素经验分位数欠覆盖(0.85 @ 0.90 目标)而 conformal 达 0.92,约 200 时两者趋同。**迁移条件**:QASA 校准负样本是容易的论文内干扰段(均值 0.20),而真正的错引(SciFact 人工未支持)均值 0.50 —— 用 QASA 校准、目标 90% 的 τ 只能抓住 **37%** 的难负样本(94%、98% 目标时为 44%、61%);在目标域负样本上重新校准可恢复保证,代价是更高的复核量。**复核预算(90% 保证)**:合并后标记率 bare 2.5%、OpenScholar 3.3%、PaperQA2 6.1%(单格如 PaperQA2 + Qwen3.6-35B 9.5% [6.5, 13.8]),随管线 agency 上升。**Re-attribution(QASA,recall@1)**:Gemma-4-26B 0.76、Gemma-4-31B 0.74、**BM25 0.69(CPU)**、verifier 分数 0.58、Qwen3.6 0.51–0.52、随机 0.03;高词汇重叠半数样本上 BM25 与 Gemma 持平(0.88),最低重叠四分位 BM25 0.31 vs Gemma 0.53。BM25 无法区分支持与反驳(SciFact 上答案与反驳段落的 Jaccard 0.064 vs 0.055),故必须由 verifier 复核(0.83 vs 0.50)。**范围对照**:在 PubMedQA(结论 yes/no)上 κ = 0.02 —— 守卫检验「段落是否支持 claim」,**不检验结论是否正确**。
<!-- EN --> **Verifier frontier (SciFact)**: supported-class recall / specificity — AttrScore-3B 0.90 / 0.54, OpenScholar post-hoc 0.50 / 0.92, GPT-4o 0.46 / 0.92, RAGAS 0.30 / 0.96, DeBERTa-NLI 0.25 / 0.97; AttrScore recall 0.94 on the held-out 802. ROC-AUCs overlap: AttrScore 0.88 [0.84, 0.93], DeBERTa 0.90 [0.85, 0.94], GPT-4o 0.87 [0.82, 0.92] — **a strict frontier judge (GPT-4o) buys no separation advantage**. Gold score means: supported 0.54 vs unsupported 0.20. **Rate is verifier-dependent**: identical outputs climb from ~3% (bare, high-recall verifier) to ~14–18% under strict NLI; agentic pipelines sit a few points above bare at each setting; most of the climb is strict verifiers **falsely rejecting genuinely supported citations**. **Disagreement**: on 300 deployed sentences, pairwise positive-specific agreement 0.90–0.96 but negative-specific only 0.27–0.30 — **60–80%** of citations one verifier flags another calls supported. **Conformal guarantee (QASA)**: targets 0.90/0.94/0.98 → held-out achieved 0.90/0.94/0.98; at calibration size 25 a naive empirical quantile under-covers (0.85 at a 0.90 target) while conformal holds (0.92), converging by ~200. **Transfer condition**: QASA calibration negatives are easy in-paper distractors (mean 0.20), whereas real mis-citations (SciFact human unsupported) average 0.50 — a τ calibrated on QASA for 90% catches only **37%** of the hard negatives (44% and 61% at 94% and 98% targets); recalibrating on target-domain negatives restores the guarantee at a higher review budget. **Review budget (90% guarantee)**: pooled flag rate bare 2.5%, OpenScholar 3.3%, PaperQA2 6.1% (cells up to e.g. PaperQA2 + Qwen3.6-35B 9.5% [6.5, 13.8]), rising with pipeline agency. **Re-attribution (QASA recall@1)**: Gemma-4-26B 0.76, Gemma-4-31B 0.74, **BM25 0.69 (CPU)**, verifier score 0.58, Qwen3.6 0.51–0.52, random 0.03; on the high-overlap half BM25 and Gemma tie (0.88), on the lowest-overlap quartile BM25 0.31 vs Gemma 0.53. BM25 cannot tell support from refutation (answer–refuting passage Jaccard 0.064 vs 0.055 on SciFact), hence verifier re-checking (0.83 vs 0.50). **Scope control**: PubMedQA (yes/no conclusions) κ = 0.02 — the guard checks "does the passage support the claim", **not whether the conclusion is correct**.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 定量证明引用忠实度指标**依赖 verifier 的工作点**,且 verifier 在「标记什么」上几乎不一致 —— 没有命名 verifier 与协议的跨论文比较无效。 <!-- EN --> Quantifies that citation-faithfulness metrics **depend on the verifier's operating point** and that verifiers barely agree on what to flag — cross-paper comparisons without a named verifier and protocol are invalid.
- <!-- ZH --> 以人工金标准为锚的评估协议(验证 verifier、测 re-attribution、校准保证都对照金标准而非另一个模型)。 <!-- EN --> A gold-anchored protocol (verifier validation, re-attribution and guarantee calibration all against human gold, not another model).
- <!-- ZH --> 用 split-conformal 把一个只有中等一致性的 verifier 变成**有限样本、无分布的捕获率保证**,并给出「声明容忍度 → 预先确定复核预算」的操作方式。 <!-- EN --> Split-conformal calibration turns a moderate-agreement verifier into a **finite-sample, distribution-free catch-rate guarantee**, with "declare a tolerance → fix the review budget in advance" as the operating mode.
- <!-- ZH --> 明确识别并量化保证迁移的关键条件 —— **校准负样本难度**,给出重新校准方案(作者称此前 conformal-factuality 工作未检验)。 <!-- EN --> Identifies and quantifies the condition governing transfer — **calibration-negative difficulty** — with a recalibration recipe (untested by prior conformal-factuality work, per the authors).
- <!-- ZH --> MIT 许可的单 GPU(80GB)/CPU 开源审计工具包。 <!-- EN --> An MIT-licensed single-GPU (80 GB) / CPU open audit kit.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 演示的保证在 QASA 上是**分布内**的;对真实错引偏乐观(37% vs 声明 90%),部署必须在目标域负样本上重新校准。 <!-- EN --> The demonstrated guarantee is **in-distribution** (QASA); it is optimistic for real mis-citations (37% vs a declared 90%), so deployments must recalibrate on target-domain negatives.
- <!-- ZH --> 保证是**边际**的(对校准抽样平均),单次划分可能低于目标。 <!-- EN --> Coverage is **marginal** over the calibration draw; an individual split can fall below target.
- <!-- ZH --> 固定检索,只测生成阶段,不覆盖各 agent 自身检索引入的错误。 <!-- EN --> Retrieval held fixed — only generation is audited, not errors from each agent's own retrieval.
- <!-- ZH --> 只检验「单个段落是否支持 claim」,不处理矛盾证据、选择性引用或结论正确性(作者列为下一步)。 <!-- EN --> Checks only "does one passage support the claim" — not contradiction, selective citation or conclusion correctness (named as next steps).
- <!-- ZH --> 未考虑 agent 被优化去对抗 verifier 的情形;被审计的只是 27–35B 开源模型。 <!-- EN --> Does not handle agents optimized against the verifier; only 27–35B open models are audited.
- <!-- ZH --> 作者提醒:截断段落(只看前缀)会把未支持率虚高数倍,须用滑窗读全文。 <!-- EN --> The authors warn that truncating passages to a prefix inflates the unsupported rate severalfold; sliding windows over full passages are required.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这几乎就是 **课题 2「calibrated verifier」** 的原型,而且在方法上与我们的计划高度吻合:**(1) 「verifier 是要被验证的仪器」**这一立场,正是我们在肾脏 omics 场景应采取的 —— 不能只报告「agent 的 claim 中 X% 未被证据支持」,必须同时报告用哪个 verifier、在什么金标准上验证、工作点在哪。**(2) Split-conformal 捕获率保证**可以直接移植:把 claim–citation 对换成 claim–evidence 对(evidence 可以是文献段落,也可以是 agent 实际跑出的统计表/图),在专家标注的金标准上校准 τ,给出「漏检的未支持 claim ≤ α」的保证,并预先声明复核预算。**(3) 校准负样本难度**这一发现对我们特别关键:肾脏 omics 中真正难的错误(如把 ambient RNA 污染导致的信号说成疾病特征、把 Xenium 分割伪影说成细胞状态)与「容易的干扰项」完全不同 —— 金标准负样本必须来自真实 agent 错误(这与课题 1 中「专家验证的 KidneyAnomalyBench」可以共用标注流程)。**(4) 范围对照的结论**(守卫不检验结论正确性,PubMedQA κ = 0.02)提醒我们:claim-to-evidence 验证与「结论是否为真」是两层问题,课题 2 的 omics verifier 需要第二层 —— 证据本身(数据)是否可靠,即课题 1 的跨模态一致性正好能提供。与 citation-blame-emnlp-2026 互补:那篇负责**定位**错误发生在哪个 agent,这篇负责对 verifier 的判断**给出统计保证**。
<!-- EN --> This is almost a prototype of **Topic 2's "calibrated verifier"**, and methodologically very close to our plan: **(1) the stance that the verifier is an instrument to be validated** is exactly what we should adopt for kidney omics — never report "X% of the agent's claims are unsupported" without naming the verifier, the gold it was validated on, and its operating point. **(2) The split-conformal catch-rate guarantee** ports directly: replace claim–citation pairs with claim–evidence pairs (evidence can be a paper passage or the statistics/plots the agent actually computed), calibrate τ on expert gold, and promise "missed unsupported claims ≤ α" with a review budget declared in advance. **(3) Calibration-negative difficulty** is especially important for us: the hard errors in kidney omics (calling an ambient-RNA contamination signal a disease feature, reading a Xenium segmentation artifact as a cell state) are nothing like easy distractors — gold negatives must come from real agent errors (and can share the annotation pipeline with Topic 1's expert-validated KidneyAnomalyBench). **(4) The scope control** (the guard does not check conclusion correctness; PubMedQA κ = 0.02) reminds us that claim-to-evidence verification and "is the conclusion true" are two layers; the omics verifier needs the second layer — is the evidence (the data) itself reliable — which Topic 1's cross-modal agreement can provide. Complementary to citation-blame-emnlp-2026: that paper **localizes** which agent erred; this one gives the verifier's judgements a **statistical guarantee**.
