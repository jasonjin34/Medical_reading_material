## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 在 CancerRiskNet 式的胰腺癌轨迹 Transformer 里,把随机初始化的诊断码嵌入换成**LLM 对 OMOP 概念名称的文本嵌入**(OpenAI text-embedding-3-small),在 Columbia(CUMC)与 Cedars-Sinai(CSMC)两家美国院内 EHR 上,6–12 个月预测 AUROC 分别从 0.59/0.82 提升到 0.67/0.86;再排除确诊前 0–3 个月数据,升到 0.82/0.89。
<!-- EN --> In a CancerRiskNet-style pancreatic cancer trajectory Transformer, replace randomly initialized code embeddings with **LLM text embeddings of OMOP concept names** (OpenAI text-embedding-3-small): on two US institutional EHRs — Columbia (CUMC) and Cedars-Sinai (CSMC) — 6–12-month AUROC rises from 0.59/0.82 to 0.67/0.86, and to 0.82/0.89 once data from the 0–3 months before diagnosis are excluded.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 胰腺癌 5 年生存仅 13%,早期确诊可升至 44%;但筛查只覆盖家族史/胚系变异人群(仅 5–10% 病例),MRI/CT 不可能在一般人群中普查。EHR 的纵向病史可能藏有早期信号。受 GenePT(用 GPT 对基因 NCBI 描述做嵌入)启发,作者假设:**LLM 对医学概念名称的语义嵌入**能比从头学的嵌入更好地表示诊断码,从而提升早期预测 —— 尤其在单个医院这种比国家登记小得多、更异质的数据上。另一个问题是**临近确诊数据造成的泄漏**:多时间窗共享模型里,近端强信号会「渗透」到远端窗口。
<!-- EN --> Pancreatic cancer five-year survival is 13%, rising to 44% if caught early; but screening covers only those with family history or germline variants (5–10% of cases), and MRI/CT cannot be applied population-wide. Longitudinal EHR histories may hold early signals. Inspired by GenePT (GPT embeddings of NCBI gene descriptions), the authors hypothesize that **LLM semantic embeddings of medical concept names** represent diagnosis codes better than embeddings learned from scratch, improving early prediction — especially on single-institution data far smaller and more heterogeneous than a national registry. A second issue is **leakage from near-diagnosis data**: in shared multi-horizon models, strong proximal signal "bleeds" into distal windows.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **数据格式**:OMOP CDM 的诊断(condition)序列 + 记录时年龄作为 positional encoding。**嵌入来源**(用于初始化诊断码嵌入):(a) baseline:PyTorch nn.Embedding 随机初始化;(b) RGCN:在 OMOP 祖先-后代层级构成的知识图上训练关系图卷积网络得到概念嵌入;(c) **GPT**:OpenAI text-embedding-3-small 对 OMOP 概念名称编码,32 维与 1536 维两种;(d) Mistral:SFR-Embedding-Mistral(4096 维)。每种再分冻结 vs 微调,共 12 个模型。**两种任务形式**:(1) **多标签模型**(仿 CancerRiskNet)同时预测 0–3、3–6、6–12、12–36、36–60 个月五个窗口;评估时每个窗口只用确诊恰落在该窗口的病例,避免近端病例抬高远端分数;另训练一个**排除 0–3 个月数据**的版本(这部分数据转去扩充 3–6 个月窗口)。(2) **二分类模型**(3–6、6–12、12–36 个月各一个),用**数据增强**:把近端病例的最近记录逐条删除,直到最后一条记录落入目标窗口,以扩充远端窗口的病例数。60/20/20 划分;bootstrap 95% CI;LIME 解释;并与传统危险因素(CA19-9、2 型糖尿病、胰腺炎)和已知胚系变异比较 PPV。
<!-- EN --> **Data format**: OMOP CDM condition sequences with age at recording as positional encoding. **Embedding sources** (initializing code embeddings): (a) baseline: randomly initialized PyTorch nn.Embedding; (b) RGCN: a relational graph convolutional network trained on the knowledge graph of OMOP ancestor–descendant relations; (c) **GPT**: OpenAI text-embedding-3-small on OMOP concept names, at 32 and 1536 dimensions; (d) Mistral: SFR-Embedding-Mistral (4096-d). Each frozen vs fine-tuned — 12 models. **Two task framings**: (1) a **multi-label model** (CancerRiskNet-style) predicting five windows (0–3, 3–6, 6–12, 12–36, 36–60 months), evaluated per window only on cases whose diagnosis falls in that window so proximal cases do not inflate distal scores, plus a version **excluding 0–3-month data** (moved to augment the 3–6-month window); (2) **binary models** (one each for 3–6, 6–12, 12–36 months) with **data augmentation**: proximal cases' most recent records are removed until the last remaining record falls in the target window, enlarging distal case counts. 60/20/20 split; bootstrap 95% CIs; LIME explanations; PPV compared against traditional risk factors (CA19-9, type 2 diabetes, pancreatitis) and known germline variants.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **CUMC**(哥伦比亚大学医学中心)3,300 例 / 785,335 对照;**CSMC**(Cedars-Sinai 医学中心,洛杉矶 —— 注意**不是** Mount Sinai)1,781 例 / 484,515 对照。病例为胰腺恶性肿瘤诊断码且无其他癌症史;对照排除任何癌症相关词、近 2 年内诊断、<5 条诊断记录、<18 或 >100 岁。CSMC 有遗传数据:1,500 名携带胰腺癌相关变异者,其中 130 人患胰腺癌(数据集中保留 50 人)。**留出测试集中远端窗口病例极少**:CUMC 6–12 个月仅 23 例、36–60 个月 14 例;CSMC 6–12 个月 18 例、36–60 个月 7 例。HIPAA 禁止共享 EHR,两中心通过共享代码分别运行。
<!-- EN --> **CUMC** (Columbia University Medical Center) 3,300 cases / 785,335 controls; **CSMC** (Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, Los Angeles — note: **not** Mount Sinai) 1,781 cases / 484,515 controls. Cases have a pancreatic malignancy code and no other cancer history; controls exclude any cancer-related terms, diagnoses within the last 2 years, <5 condition records, and age <18 or >100. CSMC has genetic data: 1,500 carriers of pancreatic-cancer-associated variants, 130 of whom have pancreatic cancer (50 retained in the dataset). **Held-out distal windows have very few cases**: CUMC 23 cases at 6–12 months and 14 at 36–60; CSMC 18 and 7. HIPAA prohibits EHR sharing; the two sites ran shared code independently.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **6–12 个月(多标签,含 0–3 个月数据)**:CUMC baseline 0.592 → GPT-1536 **0.673**;CSMC 0.819 → **0.858**。CUMC 上 RGCN 0.647,Mistral 0.598(但 Mistral max-F1 最高,p = 0.035)。 <!-- EN --> **6–12 months (multi-label, including 0–3-month data)**: CUMC baseline 0.592 → GPT-1536 **0.673**; CSMC 0.819 → **0.858**. On CUMC, RGCN 0.647, Mistral 0.598 (though Mistral has the best max-F1, p = 0.035).
- <!-- ZH --> **排除 0–3 个月后(GPT-1536)**:6–12 个月 CUMC **0.819**、CSMC **0.893**;但 3–6 个月反而下降(CUMC 0.856→0.763,CSMC 0.884→0.870)—— 说明晚期症状可前推到约 6 个月,但不会更早。 <!-- EN --> **After excluding 0–3 months (GPT-1536)**: 6–12 months CUMC **0.819**, CSMC **0.893**; but 3–6 months drops (CUMC 0.856→0.763, CSMC 0.884→0.870) — late symptoms extend back about 6 months, not further.
- <!-- ZH --> **嵌入维度**:GPT 32 维常与 1536 维相当甚至更好;Mistral 4096 维并不稳定占优;微调对 32 维帮助大、对 1536 维帮助小。 <!-- EN --> **Embedding size**: 32-d GPT often matches or beats 1536-d; 4096-d Mistral is not consistently better; fine-tuning helps 32-d much more than 1536-d.
- <!-- ZH --> **与 CancerRiskNet 对比(36 个月内)**:baseline AUROC 0.694(CUMC)/ 0.744(CSMC)vs CancerRiskNet 0.88;排除 0–3 个月后 0.737/0.771 vs 0.83;加 LLM 嵌入后 **0.761/0.811** —— 院内数据仍低于丹麦国家登记。 <!-- EN --> **Versus CancerRiskNet (within 36 months)**: baseline AUROC 0.694 (CUMC) / 0.744 (CSMC) vs CancerRiskNet's 0.88; with 0–3-month exclusion 0.737/0.771 vs 0.83; with LLM embeddings **0.761/0.811** — institutional data still trail the Danish national registry.
- <!-- ZH --> **临床效用(CUMC,6–12 个月二分类,338 例 / 157,067 对照)**:GPT 模型 max-F1 点 PPV **0.141**(22 TP / 134 FP)vs「任一传统危险因素」PPV **0.004**(96 TP / 24,950 FP);要检出 50% 病例需筛查 17% 人群。**但 CSMC 上 GPT 模型 max-F1 点 PPV 只有 0.012**(baseline 0.084)—— 摘要中的 0.141 只对应 CUMC。 <!-- EN --> **Clinical utility (CUMC, 6–12-month binary, 338 cases / 157,067 controls)**: GPT model PPV **0.141** at max-F1 (22 TP / 134 FP) vs **0.004** for "any traditional risk factor" (96 TP / 24,950 FP); detecting 50% of cases requires screening 17% of the population. **But on CSMC the GPT model's max-F1 PPV is only 0.012** (baseline 0.084) — the abstract's 0.141 is CUMC only.
- <!-- ZH --> **补充传统危险因素**:模型检出的病例中,CUMC 超过 50%、CSMC 超过 85% 既无传统危险因素也无已知胚系变异。 <!-- EN --> **Complementing risk factors**: among model-detected cases, over 50% (CUMC) and over 85% (CSMC) had neither traditional risk factors nor known germline variants.
- <!-- ZH --> **特征**:LIME 显示 GPT 嵌入模型更偏向临床可解释特征(baseline 常出现骨折等无关项);二分类模型在 6–12 与 12–36 个月窗口都把**肾脏/输尿管疾病**与肝病列为前列特征。 <!-- EN --> **Features**: LIME shows the GPT-embedding model favours clinically interpretable features (baseline surfaces unrelated ones such as fractures); the binary models rank **kidney/ureter disorders** and liver disease among top features in both 6–12- and 12–36-month windows.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 用**LLM 对概念名称的文本嵌入**初始化 EHR 码嵌入 —— 零额外训练成本,可直接插入任何 EHR 序列模型。 <!-- EN --> Initializes EHR code embeddings with **LLM text embeddings of concept names** — no extra training cost, pluggable into any EHR sequence model.
- <!-- ZH --> 系统比较嵌入来源(随机 / 知识图 RGCN / GPT / Mistral)、维度与冻结-微调。 <!-- EN --> A systematic comparison of embedding source (random / KG-RGCN / GPT / Mistral), dimension and frozen vs fine-tuned.
- <!-- ZH --> 明确指出并处理**多标签多时间窗模型的泄漏**:按窗口限定评估病例、排除 0–3 个月数据、二分类 + 截断式数据增强。 <!-- EN --> Explicitly identifies and handles **leakage in multi-label multi-horizon models**: per-window case restriction, 0–3-month exclusion, binary models with truncation-based augmentation.
- <!-- ZH --> 双中心、仅共享代码不共享数据的验证模式;报告 PPV 与「需要筛查多少人」的临床口径。 <!-- EN --> Two-site, code-sharing-not-data-sharing validation; reports PPV and "how many must be screened".

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **远端窗口病例极少**(留出集 6–12 个月仅 18–23 例),CI 很宽(如 CUMC 0.673 [0.559, 0.776]);部分增益未达显著。 <!-- EN --> **Very few distal-window cases** (18–23 held-out cases at 6–12 months), wide CIs (e.g. CUMC 0.673 [0.559, 0.776]); some gains are not significant.
- <!-- ZH --> **两站点结论不一致**:GPT 嵌入在 CSMC 稳定提升,在 CUMC 不一致;CSMC 上的 PPV 反而低于 baseline。 <!-- EN --> **Inconsistent across sites**: GPT embeddings help consistently at CSMC but not at CUMC; CSMC PPV is actually lower than baseline.
- <!-- ZH --> 只用诊断序列,无化验、用药、文本;家族史等关键危险因素在非结构化记录里,未利用。 <!-- EN --> Condition sequences only — no labs, medications or text; key risk factors like family history live in unstructured notes and are unused.
- <!-- ZH --> 评估队列中的患病率(215/10 万)远高于真实人群(约 32/10 万),真实 PPV 会更低。 <!-- EN --> Evaluation-cohort prevalence (215 per 100k) far exceeds the real population (~32 per 100k), so real-world PPV would be lower.
- <!-- ZH --> 依赖闭源 OpenAI 嵌入 API(概念名称虽不含 PHI,但可复现性与版本稳定性受限)。 <!-- EN --> Relies on a closed OpenAI embedding API (concept names contain no PHI, but reproducibility and version stability are limited).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> **对 Sinai EHR 项目,这篇最接近「我们自己会遇到的处境」**:单个美国学术医疗中心、OMOP 格式、几十万到百万级病人,而不是丹麦那种完整的国家登记 —— 本文显示同一任务在院内数据上 AUROC 明显低于 CancerRiskNet,而两家医院之间的结论也不一致。可直接借用的三件事:(1) **LLM 概念名称嵌入作为初始化**,成本几乎为零,Sinai 的 OMOP 概念表可以直接生成;值得同时测试开源嵌入模型以避免闭源依赖;(2) **反泄漏评估**:按窗口限定评估病例 + 排除 0–3 个月 + 截断式增强 —— 这比只报一个 AUROC 严格得多;(3) **报告口径**:PPV、「检出 50% 需要筛查多少人」。需要警惕的是:摘要只报 CUMC 的 PPV 0.141,而 CSMC 上 PPV 只有 0.012 —— 这正是 Plan B「claim-to-evidence 校验」应当抓住的选择性报告类型,可作为 omics/EHR agent 失败分类中的一个具体样例。**与心-代谢-肾方向**:二分类模型把**肾脏疾病**列为胰腺癌 6–36 个月前的早期特征,作者解读为多器官系统性信号 —— 这提示在 Sinai 上做心-代谢-肾轨迹模型时,肾相关码本身就是跨病种的强信号;反过来,这套「LLM 嵌入 + 多窗口 + 反泄漏」流程可原样用于 CKD/DKD 进展预测。与 detect → model → revert:这是 EHR 层的 detect,改进点在表示(把医学知识注入嵌入),而非因果。
<!-- EN --> **For the Sinai EHR project this is the closest to the situation we will actually face**: a single US academic centre, OMOP format, hundreds of thousands to a million patients — not a complete national registry like Denmark's. It shows the same task scores clearly lower on institutional data than CancerRiskNet, and the two hospitals disagree. Three directly borrowable pieces: (1) **LLM concept-name embeddings as initialization** — near-zero cost, generable straight from Sinai's OMOP concept table; open embedding models should be tested too to avoid a closed dependency; (2) **anti-leakage evaluation**: per-window case restriction + 0–3-month exclusion + truncation-based augmentation — far stricter than reporting one AUROC; (3) **reporting**: PPV and "how many must be screened to catch 50%". A caution: the abstract reports only CUMC's PPV of 0.141, while CSMC's is 0.012 — exactly the selective-reporting pattern Plan B's claim-to-evidence verification should catch, and a concrete example for an omics/EHR agent failure taxonomy. **For cardiometabolic-kidney disease**: the binary models rank **kidney disorders** among early features 6–36 months before pancreatic cancer, which the authors read as a multi-organ systemic signal — suggesting kidney codes are strong cross-disease signals in Sinai trajectory models; conversely, the "LLM embeddings + multi-horizon + anti-leakage" pipeline applies unchanged to CKD/DKD progression. On detect → model → revert: this is EHR-level detect, improving representation (injecting medical knowledge into embeddings) rather than causality.
