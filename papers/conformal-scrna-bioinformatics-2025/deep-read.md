## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 用 conformal prediction 给 scRNA-seq 自动注释加上分布无关的统计保证:先用**自编码器 + conformal p 值**检测参照中不存在的细胞类型(OOD),控制假阳性率 ≤ α₀;再对剩下的细胞输出**带覆盖保证的预测集合**(标准 / classwise / cluster 三种 Mondrian 分类方式,APS / RAPS / THR 三种非一致性分数),集合大小即不确定性;conformal p 值分布偏离均匀还可以用来诊断参照与 query 之间的可交换性是否被破坏。
<!-- EN --> Adds distribution-free statistical guarantees to automatic scRNA-seq annotation with conformal prediction: an **autoencoder + conformal p-values** first detects cell types absent from the reference (OOD) with false-positive rate ≤ α₀; the remaining cells get **prediction sets with coverage guarantees** (standard / classwise / cluster Mondrian taxonomies; APS / RAPS / THR non-conformity scores), where set size is the uncertainty. Departures of the conformal p-values from uniformity also diagnose broken reference–query exchangeability.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 监督式细胞注释器(scmap、CellTypist 等)通常缺乏可靠的不确定性估计;已有的「拒识」机制靠 softmax 分数阈值,而神经网络的分数常常校准不良,也没有统计保证。conformal prediction 在 i.i.d./可交换假设下能给出覆盖保证,但 query 中出现参照外的新细胞类型时假设被破坏,保证失效。此前只有一项工作把 conformal 用于单细胞注释,而且只做了标准分类方式,没处理新细胞类型。
<!-- EN --> Supervised annotators (scmap, CellTypist, etc.) usually lack reliable uncertainty; existing rejection schemes threshold softmax scores, which are often miscalibrated in neural networks and carry no guarantee. Conformal prediction gives coverage under i.i.d./exchangeability, but novel cell types in the query break that assumption and the guarantee. Only one earlier work applied conformal prediction to single-cell annotation, limited to the standard taxonomy and not handling novel types.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **归纳式 conformal**:参照集拆为训练集与校准集(校准集占可用训练样本的 40%;另留 10% 作内部测试、15% 作验证)。**模块 1:OOD 检测器**——在训练集(无离群)上训练全连接自编码器,重建误差作非一致性分数;对新细胞计算 conformal p 值(校准分数中不小于其分数的比例),在可交换时边际有效,**直接控制边际 FPR ≤ α₀**;可选用 calibration-conditional p 值(Bates et al. 2023)以概率 1−δ 保证本次校准集下 FPR ≤ α₀(功效降低);α₀ 有自动选择方法(补充 S3.1)。**模块 2:conformal 注释器**——底层分类器为 scmap、CellTypist 或前馈网络「TorchNet」,要求输出 softmax;非一致性分数用 THR、APS、RAPS(κ=1,η=0.01);对每个类别算 p 值,预测集合 = p > α 的类别;标准方式保证边际覆盖,**classwise**(Mondrian)保证每个细胞类型的条件覆盖(稀有类会不稳定),**cluster** 方式(k-means 聚合类别,Ding et al. 2023)折中。预处理:CPM + log2;TorchNet 用 Seurat 2,000 HVG + Harmony 整合。
<!-- EN --> **Inductive conformal**: the reference is split into training and calibration sets (calibration = 40% of available training samples; another 10% held out as internal test and 15% as validation). **Module 1: OOD detector** — a feed-forward autoencoder trained on the (outlier-free) training set; reconstruction error is the non-conformity score; a new cell's conformal p-value is the fraction of calibration scores at least as large, marginally valid under exchangeability, so **marginal FPR ≤ α₀ is controlled directly**; calibration-conditional p-values (Bates et al. 2023) optionally guarantee FPR ≤ α₀ for this calibration set with probability 1−δ (less power); α₀ can be chosen automatically (Supplement S3.1). **Module 2: conformal annotator** — the base classifier is scmap, CellTypist or a feed-forward net "TorchNet", which must output softmax scores; non-conformity scores are THR, APS and RAPS (κ=1, η=0.01); a p-value is computed per class and the prediction set is all classes with p > α; the standard taxonomy gives marginal coverage, **classwise** (Mondrian) gives per-cell-type conditional coverage (unstable for rare classes), and **cluster** (k-means grouping of classes, Ding et al. 2023) is a compromise. Preprocessing: CPM + log2; TorchNet uses Seurat 2,000 HVGs + Harmony integration.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 共 10 个批次化实验:**肺**——参照为跨组织免疫细胞 atlas(Domínguez Conde et al. 2022),query 为健康肺(GSE178360)和肺癌患者(GSE127465);**胰腺**——GSE84133 四名受试者轮流留一人作 query(4 个实验);**乳腺**——GSE180878,12 例预防性乳房切除作参照、4 例缩乳术作 query;**小鼠原肠胚**——Pijuan-Sala et al. 2019,按两个测序批次分参照/query。质控:排除 <60 个细胞的类型,过滤线粒体 >10%、核糖体 >60%、血红蛋白 >60% 的细胞。评估:leave-one-cell-type-out(从参照删除一类,保留在 query),α ∈ {0.01, 0.05, 0.1},每种配置 20 个随机种子。
<!-- EN --> Ten batched experiments in total: **lung** — reference is a cross-tissue immune atlas (Domínguez Conde et al. 2022), queries are healthy lung (GSE178360) and lung-cancer patients (GSE127465); **pancreas** — GSE84133, four subjects in turn left out as query (4 experiments); **breast** — GSE180878, 12 prophylactic-mastectomy samples as reference and 4 reduction-mammoplasty samples as query; **mouse gastrulation** — Pijuan-Sala et al. 2019, split by two sequencing batches. QC: types with <60 cells excluded; cells with mitochondrial >10%, ribosomal >60% or haemoglobin >60% removed. Evaluation: leave-one-cell-type-out (one type removed from the reference, kept in the query), α ∈ {0.01, 0.05, 0.1}, 20 random seeds per configuration.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **OOD 检测**:以 Naive B 细胞为 OOD(在健康肺 query 中占 1.54%、肺癌 query 中占 1.27%),在低显著性阈值下就达到高检出功效;健康 query 中满足 FPR 保证,**肺癌 query 中不满足**——因为疾病破坏了可交换性。其他细胞类型和组织趋势相似(补充图 S3–S7)。 <!-- EN --> **OOD detection**: with Naive B cells as OOD (1.54% of the healthy-lung query, 1.27% of the lung-tumour query), power is high even at low significance thresholds; the FPR guarantee holds in the healthy query but **not in the tumour query** — disease breaks exchangeability. Other cell types and tissues show similar trends (Supplementary Figs S3–S7).
- <!-- ZH --> **FDR 偏高**:OOD 细胞越稀有,FDR 越大——可交换的 ID 细胞的 p 值服从均匀分布,必然有一部分很低,在 OOD 很少时淹没真阳性。 <!-- EN --> **High FDR**: the rarer the OOD cells, the higher the FDR — exchangeable ID cells have uniform p-values, so some are low and swamp the few true positives.
- <!-- ZH --> **可交换性诊断**:p 值分布呈三种形态——含 OOD 时低 p 值尖峰;无 OOD 的健康 query 有小的低 p 值峰(轻微 batch / 生物差异);肺癌 query 明显偏离均匀(疾病造成的强协变量偏移)。 <!-- EN --> **Exchangeability diagnosis**: three p-value patterns — a low-p spike when OOD cells are present; a small low-p bump in the OOD-free healthy query (mild batch/biological differences); a clear departure from uniformity in the tumour query (disease-driven covariate shift).
- <!-- ZH --> **注释覆盖**:乳腺数据上 TorchNet 在三种分类方式下都接近理论覆盖;scmap 在 classwise/cluster 下好、标准方式下失败;CellTypist 达不到覆盖保证(作者推测是预处理未处理技术差异)。其他组织覆盖基本满足;健康肺(参照与 query 完全独立)略有偏差;肺癌 query 偏差最大。 <!-- EN --> **Annotation coverage**: on breast, TorchNet is near nominal coverage under all three taxonomies; scmap works under classwise/cluster but fails under standard; CellTypist misses the guarantee (attributed to unaddressed technical variation in preprocessing). Coverage largely holds elsewhere; healthy lung (fully independent reference/query) deviates slightly; the tumour query deviates most.
- <!-- ZH --> **取舍**:标准方式集合最小;classwise 保证每类覆盖但集合更大、极稀有类 CovGap 更高;cluster 折中,但在极严格覆盖要求下会给出完全无信息的集合;THR 高效但可能出现空集(= 拒识),APS/RAPS 更自适应。 <!-- EN --> **Trade-offs**: standard gives the smallest sets; classwise guarantees per-class coverage with larger sets and higher CovGap for very rare classes; cluster is a compromise but becomes uninformative under very strict coverage; THR is efficient but can return empty sets (= rejection); APS/RAPS are more adaptive.
- <!-- ZH --> **局限案例(胰腺)**:α₀ 很低时检测器几乎不报 OOD,经验覆盖与理论差距明显;α₀ 增大后差距收窄。实验 2 中即使 OOD 检出率高,覆盖仍偏低,直到更大 α₀ 剔除了一批**违反可交换性的 ID 细胞**后才突然闭合。 <!-- EN --> **Failure case (pancreas)**: at very low α₀ the detector rarely flags OOD and empirical coverage falls well below nominal; the gap narrows as α₀ grows. In experiment 2, coverage stays low despite good OOD power until a larger α₀ removes a set of **exchangeability-violating ID cells**, at which point the gap closes abruptly.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 用同一个 conformal 框架同时处理「新细胞类型检测」(带 FPR 保证的假设检验)和「不确定性注释」(带覆盖保证的预测集合)。 <!-- EN --> One conformal framework for both novel-type detection (hypothesis testing with FPR control) and uncertainty-aware annotation (prediction sets with coverage).
- <!-- ZH --> 系统比较三种 Mondrian 分类方式 × 三种非一致性分数 × 三种底层分类器在 10 个实验上的表现。 <!-- EN --> A systematic comparison of three Mondrian taxonomies × three non-conformity scores × three base classifiers over 10 experiments.
- <!-- ZH --> 把 conformal p 值的均匀性当作**可交换性 / 协变量偏移的诊断工具**。 <!-- EN --> Uses uniformity of conformal p-values as a **diagnostic for exchangeability / covariate shift**.
- <!-- ZH --> 预测集合支持 human-in-the-loop:单元素集合自动注释,多元素集合交给专家缩小范围。 <!-- EN --> Prediction sets support human-in-the-loop use: singletons auto-annotated, larger sets narrowed by experts.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 保证只在可交换性成立时有效;疾病 query(恰恰是我们最关心的)会打破它。 <!-- EN --> Guarantees hold only under exchangeability, which disease queries — exactly the case we care about — break.
- <!-- ZH --> OOD 稀有时 FDR 高;检出功效不足会连带破坏注释覆盖。 <!-- EN --> High FDR when OOD cells are rare; low detection power in turn breaks annotation coverage.
- <!-- ZH --> 只保证边际(或按类)覆盖,不保证单个细胞;classwise 对极稀有类不稳定。 <!-- EN --> Only marginal (or per-class) coverage, not per-cell; classwise is unstable for very rare classes.
- <!-- ZH --> OOD 检测器只是一个简单自编码器;α₀ 自动选择可能在找全 OOD 之前就停止。 <!-- EN --> The OOD detector is a plain autoencoder; automatic α₀ selection may stop before all OOD cells are found.
- <!-- ZH --> 只评估 scRNA-seq,底层模型种类有限;正文定量结果多在图中。 <!-- EN --> scRNA-seq only with a limited range of base models; most quantitative results live in figures.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇给 detect 阶段带来**统计保证**这一维度。本方向里的「异常」大多是一个连续分数(重建误差、mapQC Z 值、Sardine 似然),而本文展示了如何用 conformal p 值把任意异常分数变成**有 FPR 保证的检验**——可以直接套在 MEATRD/STANDS 类重建误差、或 Plan A 自己的 snRNA/snATAC/Xenium 异常分数上,让 KidneyAnomalyBench 报告「在 α₀ = 0.05 下的 FPR/功效/FDR」,而不只是 AUROC。更重要的是本文的**负面结果**:疾病样本会破坏可交换性,p 值整体偏离均匀——这说明「异常」可能来自真正的疾病细胞,也可能来自整体协变量偏移(batch、协议),和 mapQC(`refmap-eval-biorxiv-2025`)指出的问题相同;Plan A 的跨模态一致性正好可以作为 p 值之外的第二道判据。对 **Plan B**,本文几乎就是「calibrated verifier」的单细胞原型:预测集合大小 = 不确定性,空集 = 拒识,p 值均匀性 = 分布偏移警报;verifier 核查 agent 的细胞注释声明时,可以要求以 conformal 集合而非单一标签作为证据,并在 p 值分布异常时自动降低置信度。
<!-- EN --> This adds **statistical guarantees** to the detect stage. Most "anomalies" in this direction are continuous scores (reconstruction error, mapQC Z, Sardine likelihood); the paper shows how conformal p-values turn any anomaly score into a **test with FPR control** — directly applicable to MEATRD/STANDS-style reconstruction errors or Plan A's own snRNA/snATAC/Xenium scores, so KidneyAnomalyBench can report FPR/power/FDR at α₀ = 0.05, not just AUROC. More important is the **negative result**: disease samples break exchangeability and shift the whole p-value distribution — so an "anomaly" can be real disease cells or a global covariate shift (batch, protocol), the same issue mapQC (`refmap-eval-biorxiv-2025`) raises; Plan A's cross-modal agreement is a natural second criterion beyond p-values. For **Plan B**, this is almost a single-cell prototype of a "calibrated verifier": set size = uncertainty, empty set = abstention, p-value uniformity = distribution-shift alarm; when verifying an agent's cell-annotation claims, the verifier can require conformal sets rather than single labels as evidence and lower confidence automatically when p-values look non-uniform.
