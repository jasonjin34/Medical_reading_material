## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> f-AnoGAN 把 AnoGAN 的"每张图 latent 迭代搜索"换成**一个训练好的编码器 E**:先在健康 OCT 图块上训练 WGAN-GP,再固定 G 和 D、用"像素残差 + 判别器特征残差"(izi_f)训练 E,测试时一次前向即得重建 G(E(x)) 与异常分数;图像级 AUC 0.930,优于 AE / AdvAE / ALI 与迭代式 AnoGAN(0.911),而推理从 2 天缩到 20 秒。
<!-- EN --> f-AnoGAN replaces AnoGAN's per-image iterative latent search with **a trained encoder E**: train a WGAN-GP on healthy OCT patches, then freeze G and D and train E with a "pixel residual + discriminator-feature residual" objective (izi_f); at test time one forward pass gives the reconstruction G(E(x)) and the anomaly score. Image-level AUC is 0.930, beating AE / AdvAE / ALI and iterative AnoGAN (0.911), while inference drops from 2 days to 20 seconds.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 临床影像的专家标注昂贵,且不是所有有意义的标志物都事先已知,监督学习因此只能检测已标注病灶。AnoGAN 证明了"只学正常、按残差找异常"可行,但其迭代 latent 映射太慢,无法用于大规模筛查或实时应用;GAN 本身也没有图像→latent 的逆映射。本文要解决:如何**快速且准确**地把新图像映射到 GAN 的 latent 空间,并研究不同编码器训练方式对检测与定位的影响。
<!-- EN --> Expert labels in clinical imaging are expensive and not every relevant marker is known in advance, so supervised learning only detects annotated lesions. AnoGAN showed that "learn normal, find anomalies by residual" works, but its iterative latent mapping is too slow for large-scale screening or real-time use, and GANs have no image→latent inverse. The question: how to map new images to the GAN's latent space **fast and accurately**, and how encoder-training choices affect detection and localisation.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **两步训练,均只用正常数据**。**(1) WGAN-GP**:ResNet 式生成器与判别器各 4 个残差块(G 通道 512-256-128-64,D 128-256-512-512,3×3 卷积,D 用 layer norm),128 维正态 latent;训练 100,000 次迭代(batch 64),每次 G 更新配 5 次 D 更新。**(2) 编码器训练(G、D 固定)**,比较三种:**ziz**——从 z 采样、经 G 生成、再让 E 还原 z(L = ‖z − E(G(z))‖²),不看任何真实图像;**izi**——标准自编码器式,L = ‖x − G(E(x))‖²/n;**izi_f(提出的 f-AnoGAN)**——在 izi 基础上加判别器中间层特征残差,L = ‖x − G(E(x))‖²/n + κ·‖f(x) − f(G(E(x)))‖²/n_d。理由:只最小化像素差,可能把图像映到 latent 中训练时稀疏的区域,得到"不真实但残差很小"的重建,使残差不再是可靠的异常标志。编码器输出加 tanh,把 latent 限制在 (−1σ, +1σ)。50,000 次编码器迭代(RMSprop)。**(3) 打分**:A(x) = A_R(x) + κ·A_D(x),κ=1;像素级定位用 |x − G(E(x))|。
<!-- EN --> **Two training stages, normal data only.** **(1) WGAN-GP**: ResNet-style generator and discriminator with four residual blocks each (G channels 512-256-128-64, D 128-256-512-512, 3×3 convs, layer norm in D), 128-d normal latent; 100,000 iterations (batch 64), five D updates per G update. **(2) Encoder training (G, D frozen)**, three variants: **ziz** — sample z, generate with G, have E recover z (L = ‖z − E(G(z))‖²), never seeing a real image; **izi** — autoencoder-style, L = ‖x − G(E(x))‖²/n; **izi_f (proposed f-AnoGAN)** — izi plus an intermediate discriminator-feature residual, L = ‖x − G(E(x))‖²/n + κ·‖f(x) − f(G(E(x)))‖²/n_d. Rationale: minimising pixel differences alone can map into sparsely sampled latent regions, yielding "unrealistic but low-residual" reconstructions and making the residual an unreliable anomaly marker. A tanh on encoder outputs restricts latents to (−1σ, +1σ). 50,000 encoder iterations (RMSprop). **(3) Scoring**: A(x) = A_R(x) + κ·A_D(x), κ=1; pixel localisation uses |x − G(E(x))|.

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> **范式:重建 / 生成式**。直接继承 AnoGAN(同组 2017),作者列出的三处差别:WGAN-GP 替代 DCGAN、学习型编码器替代迭代梯度下降、更广的对照实验。ziz 来自 BiGAN 论文(Donahue 等)中的 "latent regressor" 基线;判别器特征残差源自 Salimans 等的 feature matching。对照方法 ALI 在方法上等价于 BiGAN(Zenati 等 2018 已用于异常检测)。之后它成为医学影像无监督异常检测里**最常被引用的 GAN 基线**:MedIAnomaly 的分类法把它列入图像重建类,MEATRD(空间转录组)与 AnoPILaD(淋巴结病理)都拿它作基线;扩散模型方法(AnoDDPM、Wolleb 等 2022)则在同一"重建到正常"范式里取代了它。
<!-- EN --> **Paradigm: reconstruction / generative.** A direct successor of AnoGAN (same group, 2017); the authors list three differences: WGAN-GP instead of DCGAN, a learned encoder instead of iterative gradient descent, and a wider set of comparators. ziz is the "latent regressor" baseline from the BiGAN paper (Donahue et al.); the discriminator-feature residual derives from Salimans et al.'s feature matching. The comparator ALI is methodologically equivalent to BiGAN (used for AD by Zenati et al. 2018). It then became **the most-cited GAN baseline** in unsupervised medical AD: MedIAnomaly's taxonomy files it under image reconstruction, and MEATRD (spatial transcriptomics) and AnoPILaD (lymph-node pathology) both use it as a baseline; diffusion methods (AnoDDPM, Wolleb et al. 2022) superseded it within the same "reconstruct to normal" paradigm.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 与 AnoGAN 相同的临床 SD-OCT(Heidelberg Spectralis,每体 49 张 B-scan,496×512×49)。预处理:逐体灰度归一化到 [−1,1],列数 512→256(x 方向 22 µm),用层分割定位视网膜上下边界后展平(仅在视网膜厚度超出提取高度时归一化厚度)。训练:约 **850,000 个 64×64 图块**,来自 270 个确认无积液的健康体。测试:10 个健康 + 10 个含积液病例,共 **70,000 个图块**(70% 正常 / 30% 含积液),有专家像素级积液标注(仅用于评估)。
<!-- EN --> The same clinical SD-OCT as AnoGAN (Heidelberg Spectralis, 49 B-scans per volume, 496×512×49). Preprocessing: per-volume grey-value normalisation to [−1,1], columns 512→256 (22 µm in x), retina localised by layer segmentation and flattened (thickness normalised only if it exceeds the extracted height). Training: ≈ **850,000 patches of 64×64** from 270 healthy volumes confirmed fluid-free. Test: 10 healthy + 10 fluid cases, **70,000 patches** (70% normal / 30% fluid), with expert pixel-level fluid annotations (evaluation only).

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **图像级检测(表 1,Youden 点)**:f-AnoGAN **AUC 0.9301**、precision 0.7863、sensitivity 0.8091、specificity 0.9049、F1 0.7975;迭代式(≈AnoGAN)AUC 0.9114;AE 0.8688;AdvAE 0.8649;ALI 0.7897;直接用 WGAN 判别器输出 A_D 仅 0.7504。**速度**:迭代方案每图 300 次更新、70,000 张测试图在 Titan X 上共需 **2 天**,f-AnoGAN 为 **20 秒**。**编码器对比(表 2)**:izi_f 0.9301 > ziz 0.9066 > izi 0.8874;即使给 izi/ziz 在打分时补上判别器特征项,AUC 也只到 0.8976 / 0.9209,仍低于 izi_f —— 说明判别器在**训练编码器**时就有用。**像素级定位**:积液"分割"sensitivity 0.69、specificity 0.75、AUC 0.78(作者注明只是粗略指标);AE 重建过于平滑、定位最差,ALI 与 ziz 易在视网膜-玻璃体边界产生假阳性和过度分割。**视觉图灵测试**:两位视网膜专家区分 50 张真实 / 50 张生成的正常图块,平均准确率 **0.44**(低于随机),两人一致性 0.58。
<!-- EN --> **Image-level detection (Table 1, at the Youden point)**: f-AnoGAN **AUC 0.9301**, precision 0.7863, sensitivity 0.8091, specificity 0.9049, F1 0.7975; iterative (≈AnoGAN) AUC 0.9114; AE 0.8688; AdvAE 0.8649; ALI 0.7897; the raw WGAN discriminator output A_D only 0.7504. **Speed**: the iterative scheme (300 updates per image) took **2 days** for 70,000 test images on a Titan X versus **20 seconds** for f-AnoGAN. **Encoders (Table 2)**: izi_f 0.9301 > ziz 0.9066 > izi 0.8874; even when izi/ziz get the discriminator-feature term added at scoring time they reach only 0.8976 / 0.9209, still below izi_f — the discriminator helps already when **training the encoder**. **Pixel localisation**: fluid "segmentation" sensitivity 0.69, specificity 0.75, AUC 0.78 (flagged by the authors as only a coarse indication); AE reconstructions are over-smooth with the worst localisation, while ALI and ziz produce false positives and over-segmentation at the retina–vitreous boundary. **Visual Turing test**: two retina experts separating 50 real from 50 generated normal patches reached mean accuracy **0.44** (below chance), inter-rater agreement 0.58.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 用学习型编码器实现单步图像→latent 映射,使 GAN 异常检测可用于高通量筛查。 <!-- EN --> A learned encoder for single-step image→latent mapping, making GAN-based AD usable for high-throughput screening.
- <!-- ZH --> 提出判别器引导的编码器训练 izi_f,并系统比较 ziz / izi / izi_f 三种编码器训练方式。 <!-- EN --> Discriminator-guided encoder training (izi_f) and a systematic comparison of ziz / izi / izi_f.
- <!-- ZH --> 在统一架构下与 AE、AdvAE、ALI、迭代映射、纯判别器打分做了尽量公平的对照。 <!-- EN --> A deliberately fair comparison against AE, AdvAE, ALI, iterative mapping and discriminator-only scoring under matched architectures.
- <!-- ZH --> 用专家视觉图灵测试评估生成质量,替代对医学图像不适用的 Inception Score。 <!-- EN --> An expert visual Turing test for generation quality instead of the Inception Score, which does not transfer to medical images.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自述:像素级定量只是粗略指标 —— 标注只覆盖部分异常类型,假阳性估计不可靠,"假阳性"可能是未标注的真异常。 <!-- EN --> Per the authors: pixel-level numbers are only coarse — annotations cover only part of the possible anomalies, so false-positive estimates are unreliable and "false positives" may be true unannotated anomalies.
- <!-- ZH --> 只在 64×64 小图块上工作;临床医生看的是整张 B-scan,图灵测试结果不能推到整幅图;全幅生成留待以后。 <!-- EN --> Works on 64×64 patches only; clinicians read whole B-scans, so the Turing test does not generalise to full images; full-slice generation is left for future work.
- <!-- ZH --> 作者明确前提:需要**大量正常数据**,且**测试分布等于训练分布** —— 任何域偏移都会被当成异常。 <!-- EN --> The authors state the precondition explicitly: **large amounts of normal data** and **test distribution equal to training distribution** — any domain shift is read as anomaly.
- <!-- ZH --> 单器官、单设备、单一异常类型(积液);依赖层分割预处理。 <!-- EN --> One organ, one device, one anomaly type (fluid); depends on layer-segmentation preprocessing.
- <!-- ZH --> 作者定位它主要用于正常/异常区分与**粗定位**(作为专家复核的 region proposal),而非精确分割。 <!-- EN --> The authors position it mainly for normal/abnormal separation and **coarse localisation** (region proposals for expert review), not precise segmentation.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> f-AnoGAN 是"学正常 → 摊销式投影回正常流形 → 读残差"的工程化版本,对本方向有两层意义。**第一,作为 virtual tissue 的雏形**:E 把任何观测压到"正常 latent",G 把它还原成"若健康会是什么样",G(E(x)) − x 就是 revert 方向 —— 这与 MrVI 的 counterfactual("如果这个细胞来自另一个样本会怎样")是同一种思路,只是 f-AnoGAN 没有条件变量、也不区分细胞状态与样本效应。**第二,作为伪影注入课题的必备基线**。**映射到肾脏组学**:训练集 = KPMP/HuBMAP 健康参考的 snRNA 细胞(特征可以是 scVI latent 或基础模型嵌入)或健康 H&E 图块;打分 = 表达/图块的重建残差 + 判别器特征残差。**哪些假设会断**:作者自己写明的"测试分布等于训练分布"在多中心肾活检上不成立 —— 批次、测序深度、组织保存方式都会推高残差,必须先像 MapQC(refmap-eval)那样用参考内部的样本间变异作为零分布;ambient RNA 与 doublet、Xenium 的转录本溢出与分割错误(见 xenium-split、ovrlpy)都会产生"离开正常流形"的谱,与真实病变同样得高分;PT 损伤是连续轴(见 damage-scores 的连续损伤分数),f-AnoGAN 的残差也是连续量,需要校准阈值;OCT 有像素级积液标注,肾脏数据没有对应的细胞级金标准 —— KidneyAnomalyBench 的专家判定正好补这个空。**跨模态一致性**:对同一 Xenium 切片,分别用 H&E 图块和表达谱训练两个 f-AnoGAN,只把两个残差**同时**高的区域当作疾病候选,单模态高残差当作伪影候选 —— 这是伪影注入课题把 f-AnoGAN 从"异常检测器"升级为"伪影/疾病判别器"的最直接做法。**仓库里已有的证据**:MEATRD 把 f-AnoGAN 当图像基线,在视觉上难分辨的乳腺肿瘤边缘 precision/recall 都是 0;AnoPILaD(histo-miccai-2025)中 f-AnoGAN 在淋巴结 LH 与 Camelyon16 上 AUC 仅 0.229 / 0.174(低于随机);MedIAnomaly 也指出不用 ImageNet 权重时多数 AE/GAN 方法在 Camelyon16 上基本失效。结论:在肾脏组织上它应作为**必须报告的经典基线**,而不是主力方法。
<!-- EN --> f-AnoGAN is the engineered version of "learn normal → amortised projection onto the normal manifold → read the residual", and matters here in two ways. **First, as a proto-virtual-tissue**: E compresses any observation to a "normal latent", G restores "what it would look like if healthy", and G(E(x)) − x is the revert direction — the same idea as MrVI's counterfactual ("what would this cell look like had it come from another sample"), except f-AnoGAN has no conditioning variable and does not separate cell state from sample effects. **Second, as a mandatory the artifact-injection topic baseline.** **Mapping to kidney omics**: training set = snRNA cells from KPMP/HuBMAP healthy references (features can be scVI latents or foundation-model embeddings) or healthy H&E patches; score = reconstruction residual of expression/patch plus discriminator-feature residual. **Which assumptions break**: the authors' own "test distribution equals training distribution" fails on multi-centre kidney biopsies — batch, sequencing depth and tissue preservation all inflate residuals, so the reference's own inter-sample variation must first serve as a null, as in MapQC (refmap-eval); ambient RNA and doublets, plus Xenium transcript spillover and segmentation errors (see xenium-split, ovrlpy), produce off-manifold profiles that score as high as real lesions; PT injury is a continuous axis (see the continuous damage scores in damage-scores), and f-AnoGAN's residual is continuous too, so thresholds need calibration; OCT has pixel-level fluid labels but kidney data have no matching cell-level gold standard — exactly the gap KidneyAnomalyBench's expert adjudication fills. **Cross-modal agreement**: on one Xenium section, train one f-AnoGAN on H&E patches and one on expression, treat regions where **both** residuals are high as disease candidates and single-modality highs as artifact candidates — the most direct way the artifact-injection topic could turn f-AnoGAN from an anomaly detector into an artifact-vs-disease discriminator. **Evidence already in the repo**: MEATRD uses f-AnoGAN as an image baseline and it scores precision/recall 0 / 0 on a visually subtle breast-tumour edge; in AnoPILaD (histo-miccai-2025) f-AnoGAN reaches only AUC 0.229 / 0.174 on lymph-node LH and Camelyon16 (below chance); MedIAnomaly likewise finds most AE/GAN methods essentially fail on Camelyon16 without ImageNet weights. Conclusion: on kidney tissue it is a **classic baseline that must be reported**, not the main method.
