## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> KPMP / HuBMAP / HCA 联合构建的人肾多模态单细胞 + 空间图谱:> 40 万个细胞/细胞核(snRNA、scRNA、SNARE-seq2 双组学),配合 Slide-seq2、Visium 与大规模 3D 成像,覆盖 45 名健康参照供体与 48 名 AKI/CKD 患者,定义 51 种主要细胞类型和 **28 种损伤相关的"altered states"**(cycling、adaptive/maladaptive repair、transitioning、degenerative),把它们定位到损伤 niche,并发现 TAL/PT 的 adaptive 修复签名可预测肾功能下降。
<!-- EN --> A KPMP / HuBMAP / HCA multimodal single-cell + spatial atlas of the human kidney: >400,000 cells/nuclei (snRNA, scRNA, SNARE-seq2 dual-omics) plus Slide-seq2, Visium and large-scale 3D imaging, spanning 45 healthy reference donors and 48 AKI/CKD patients; it defines 51 main cell types and **28 injury-associated "altered states"** (cycling, adaptive/maladaptive repair, transitioning, degenerative), localizes them to injury niches, and shows that TAL/PT adaptive-repair signatures predict decline of kidney function.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 肾损伤后小管与间质发生动态的急慢性改变,成功修复与 maladaptive repair 的平衡决定了 AKI → CKD → 肾衰竭的进程。要理解这个过程,需要在单细胞分辨率上界定**健康参照状态**与**损伤后的改变状态**,知道它们在组织中的位置、邻域与相互作用,并把它们与临床结局联系起来。已有图谱多聚焦健康细胞类型的整合,缺少系统的、跨技术交叉验证的损伤状态定义与空间定位。
<!-- EN --> After kidney injury, tubules and interstitium undergo dynamic acute and chronic changes; the balance between successful and maladaptive repair drives progression AKI → CKD → kidney failure. Understanding this needs single-cell definitions of **healthy reference states** and **injury-altered states**, their tissue location, neighbourhoods and interactions, and links to clinical outcomes. Earlier atlases mostly integrated healthy cell types and lacked systematic, cross-technology-validated definitions and spatial mapping of injury states.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **单细胞多组学**:10x Chromium v3 单核(snCv3)与单细胞(scCv3)RNA-seq,以及 SNARE-seq2(同一细胞核的 RNA + 染色质可及性)。snCv3 先做无监督聚类(100 个群 → 77 个 subclass),再作为锚把 scCv3 与 SNARE2 映射到同一 embedding 并转移标签。**Altered states** 依据既往研究与已知损伤特征(Supplementary Table 10)人工界定,并通过映射到小鼠 AKI(IRI)时间序列模型来确认其时间进程。**空间**:Slide-seq2(10 µm bead,6 人 67 个 puck)、Visium(55 µm spot,22 人 23 个样本)做反卷积/标签转移与共定位分析;3D 多重免疫荧光 + 无标记细胞术(3DTC,含 SHG 胶原信号)对 15 名个体做邻域分析(约 1.2M 邻域)与社区检测得到 niche。**下游**:轨迹推断与 RNA velocity、模块检测、SNARE2 的 chromVAR 转录因子活性与 GRN(含模拟 TF 扰动)、配体–受体互作、GWAS/eQTL 富集;临床关联用 NEPTUNE 队列(生存分析,ESRD 或 eGFR 下降 40% 的复合终点)与 ERCB 队列。为减少采样/技术偏差,很多样本用多于一种技术处理以做交叉验证。
<!-- EN --> **Single-cell multi-omics**: 10x Chromium v3 single-nucleus (snCv3) and single-cell (scCv3) RNA-seq, plus SNARE-seq2 (RNA + chromatin accessibility from the same nucleus). snCv3 is clustered first (100 clusters → 77 subclasses) and used as an anchor to map scCv3 and SNARE2 into one embedding with label transfer. **Altered states** are defined from prior studies and known injury features (Supplementary Table 10) and confirmed by mapping onto a mouse AKI (IRI) time-course. **Spatial**: Slide-seq2 (10 µm beads; 6 individuals, 67 pucks) and Visium (55 µm spots; 22 individuals, 23 samples) via deconvolution/label transfer and colocalization; 3D multiplexed immunofluorescence with label-free cytometry (3DTC, with SHG collagen) on 15 individuals for neighbourhood analysis (~1.2M neighbourhoods) and community detection into niches. **Downstream**: trajectory inference and RNA velocity, module detection, SNARE2 chromVAR TF activity and GRNs (including simulated TF perturbation), ligand–receptor analysis, GWAS/eQTL enrichment; clinical association in NEPTUNE (survival; composite of ESRD or 40% eGFR decline) and ERCB. Many samples were run on more than one assay for cross-validation and to reduce procurement/assay bias.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 摘要层面:45 名健康参照供体、48 名患者,> 40 万高质量细胞/细胞核;整合转录组分析覆盖 58 个参照组织(35 名供体)与 52 个疾病组织(36 名患者)。样本来源包括健康参照(部分或全部肾切除、死亡供体、活体供体活检;论文没说肾切除是否因肿瘤)与 KPMP 的 AKI/CKD 活检;单细胞实验用了 15 例 CKD、12 例 AKI、18 例活体供体活检核心。Visium 共 146,460 个 spot(22 人);3DTC 分类 1,540,563 个细胞、14 类细胞、14 个 niche。临床:NEPTUNE(正文 193 例、图注 199 例)、ERCB 131 例。
<!-- EN --> At abstract level: 45 healthy reference donors and 48 patients, >400,000 high-quality cells/nuclei; integrated transcriptomics covers 58 reference tissues (35 donors) and 52 diseased tissues (36 patients). Sources include healthy references (partial or total nephrectomies, deceased donors, living-donor biopsies; the paper does not say whether the nephrectomies were for tumours) and KPMP AKI/CKD biopsies; single-cell assays used 15 CKD, 12 AKI and 18 living-donor biopsy cores. Visium: 146,460 spots over 22 individuals; 3DTC: 1,540,563 cells, 14 cell classes, 14 niches. Clinical: NEPTUNE (193 patients in text, 199 in the figure legend) and ERCB (131).

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **细胞类型**:51 种主要(canonical)细胞类型,含稀有与此前未描述的群体,并附 marker、开放染色质与 TF motif 图谱;Slide-seq/Visium 重现肾小体、JGA、皮髓质过渡(ATL→M-TAL→C-TAL)等空间组织。 <!-- EN --> **Cell types**: 51 canonical types including rare and previously undescribed populations, with markers, open-chromatin maps and TF motifs; Slide-seq/Visium recapitulate renal corpuscle, JGA and corticomedullary transitions (ATL→M-TAL→C-TAL).
- <!-- ZH --> **28 种 altered states**:degenerative 状态高表达 SPP1、CST3、CLU、IGFBP7,共享 FOS/JUN 签名,在小鼠损伤后早期出现、恢复后消退;adaptive PT(aPT:VCAM1、DCDC2、HAVCR1)对应啮齿类 failed repair;新发现 **adaptive TAL(aTAL:PROM1/CD133、DCDC2、ITGB6)**,在小鼠 AKI 后持续 6 周;间质 altered states 包括 MyoF、cycMyoF 与 aFIB(POSTN、FAP、ACTA2、胶原)。**重要提醒**:altered states 也出现在参照组织中,可能来自取样时缺血应激或正常衰老。 <!-- EN --> **28 altered states**: degenerative states express SPP1, CST3, CLU, IGFBP7 with a shared FOS/JUN signature, appear early after mouse injury and fade on recovery; adaptive PT (aPT: VCAM1, DCDC2, HAVCR1) matches rodent failed repair; a new **adaptive TAL (aTAL: PROM1/CD133, DCDC2, ITGB6)** persists 6 weeks after mouse AKI; stromal altered states include MyoF, cycMyoF and aFIB (POSTN, FAP, ACTA2, collagens). **Key caveat**: altered states also occur in reference tissue, possibly from ischaemic stress during acquisition or normal ageing.
- <!-- ZH --> **空间损伤 niche**:Visium 中 altered 签名在 AKI/CKD 显著富集;免疫与间质细胞更常与 altered 上皮共定位;3DTC 中 CD3+ T 细胞几乎只出现在含上皮退化与纤维化的邻域(N13),髓系细胞(CD68+)出现在较健康、正在修复的区域。 <!-- EN --> **Spatial injury niches**: altered signatures are enriched in AKI/CKD Visium spots; immune and stromal cells colocalize more with altered epithelium; in 3DTC, CD3+ T cells occur almost only in neighbourhoods with epithelial degeneration and fibrosis (N13), while myeloid (CD68+) cells sit in healthier, actively repairing areas.
- <!-- ZH --> **修复轨迹与信号**:aEpi 轨迹从去分化到成熟,富集 Wnt、Notch、TGF-β、EGF、MAPK、JAK/STAT、NF-κB;GRN 提示 TFAP2B(原文写作 "TRAP2B",即 AP-2β)关键,模拟扰动 TFAP2B 会打断修复轨迹;aTAL 早期修复态先与免疫、中期与间质互作(NRG1/NRG3、NECTIN2、BMP6、IGF1)。 <!-- EN --> **Repair trajectories and signalling**: aEpi trajectories run from dedifferentiated to mature states, enriched for Wnt, Notch, TGF-β, EGF, MAPK, JAK/STAT, NF-κB; GRNs highlight TFAP2B (written "TRAP2B" in the text; AP-2β), whose simulated perturbation disrupts the repair trajectory; early aTAL repair states interact first with immune, then (mid-repair) with stromal cells (NRG1/NRG3, NECTIN2, BMP6, IGF1).
- <!-- ZH --> **临床关联**:NEPTUNE 中按状态分数分层的 Kaplan–Meier:aPT P = 0.021、**aTAL P = 0.003**、degenerative P = 0.55(log-rank trend);ERCB 中糖尿病、高血压、FSGS 患者 aPT 与 aPT–aTAL 共同签名高于活体供体(糖尿病组在校正年龄、性别、eGFR 后仍更高)。eGFR/CKD 因果变异富集于 TAL 调控区(ESRR motif)。作者提醒临床样本量小,应谨慎解读。 <!-- EN --> **Clinical association**: NEPTUNE Kaplan–Meier by state score: aPT P = 0.021, **aTAL P = 0.003**, degenerative P = 0.55 (log-rank trend); in ERCB, diabetes, hypertension and FSGS patients have higher aPT and shared aPT–aTAL signatures than living donors (diabetes remains higher after adjusting for age, sex and eGFR). eGFR/CKD causal variants are enriched in TAL regulatory regions (ESRR motifs). The authors caution the clinical sample sizes are small.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 第一个跨 snRNA/scRNA/SNARE2/Slide-seq/Visium/3D 成像**交叉验证**的人肾健康 + 损伤图谱,并统一到同一套注释。 <!-- EN --> The first human kidney healthy + injury atlas **cross-validated** across snRNA/scRNA/SNARE2/Slide-seq/Visium/3D imaging under one annotation scheme.
- <!-- ZH --> 系统定义并命名 altered states(cycling / adaptive / transitioning / degenerative),成为后续肾脏单细胞工作的通用词汇与参照。 <!-- EN --> Systematic definition of altered states (cycling / adaptive / transitioning / degenerative), now the shared vocabulary and reference for kidney single-cell work.
- <!-- ZH --> 发现 aTAL 状态并把 TAL 提升为肾病进展的核心节段。 <!-- EN --> Identification of the aTAL state, elevating TAL as a central segment in disease progression.
- <!-- ZH --> 公开 Azimuth 参照映射、cellxgene、KPMP Atlas Explorer 等可交互资源。 <!-- EN --> Public interactive resources: Azimuth reference mapping, cellxgene, KPMP Atlas Explorer.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> Altered states 由先验知识人工界定,并非无监督发现;而且也出现在"健康"参照中(取样缺血、衰老),说明健康/异常边界本身是模糊的。 <!-- EN --> Altered states are defined manually from prior knowledge, not discovered unsupervised, and they also appear in "healthy" references (acquisition ischaemia, ageing) — the healthy/abnormal boundary is itself blurry.
- <!-- ZH --> 临床关联样本量小(作者自述),且是关联非因果;部分时间进程推断依赖小鼠 IRI 模型。 <!-- EN --> Small clinical association samples (acknowledged) and associational, not causal; some time-course inferences rely on mouse IRI.
- <!-- ZH --> Visium 55 µm spot 分辨率有限,只能做共定位而非单细胞空间分析;不包含 Xenium 等亚细胞空间平台。 <!-- EN --> Visium's 55 µm spots allow colocalization, not single-cell spatial analysis; no subcellular platforms like Xenium.
- <!-- ZH --> KPMP 原始测序数据为受控访问;不同来源(肾切除、死亡供体、活检)存在采样差异。 <!-- EN --> KPMP raw sequencing data is controlled-access; tissue sources (nephrectomy, deceased donor, biopsy) differ in procurement.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是 **课题 1 的参照骨架**。detect → model → revert 中的"detect"需要一个"什么是正常"的参照流形,而 Lake 2023 正是肾脏领域事实上的健康参照 + 损伤状态标注:(1) **Azimuth 健康参照**可直接作为异常检测的 reference(把待检测样本的 snRNA 映射上去,映射置信度低/距离远即候选异常);(2) **28 个 altered states 提供"已知异常"的弱标签** —— KidneyAnomalyBench 可以用它们做第一层评测(检测器能否在不看标签的情况下把 aPT/aTAL/degenerative 排在前面),再把"Lake 标签之外"的高异常分数细胞作为待专家验证的新发现;(3) 它自陈 altered states 也出现在参照组织里(取样缺血、衰老) —— 这正是课题 1 "跨模态一致性区分伪影与疾病"要解决的混淆:单一模态上看起来"受损"的细胞,若 snATAC/Xenium/H&E 不一致,更可能是取样伪影;(4) 同一样本多技术交叉验证的设计,是多模态设计(如 snRNA + snATAC + 空间转录组 + H&E)的直接前例;(5) 它是 NIH Kidney AI 挑战所用 KPMP open 数据的主要来源之一,其细胞状态可用于挑战第 3 项评审标准的患者分层。对 "model/revert":TFAP2B 模拟扰动打断修复轨迹,是 GRN 层面"虚拟扰动"的早期示例,可与 virtual tissue 的扰动预测对照。对 **课题 2**,它是 agent 分析肾脏组学时最常被引用的"已知结论"来源,可用来构造 claim-to-evidence 的 ground truth(例如"aTAL 标记为 PROM1/DCDC2/ITGB6")与检测"复述文献 vs 从数据得出"的测试。
<!-- EN --> This is **Topic 1's reference backbone**. The "detect" stage of detect → model → revert needs a reference manifold of "normal", and Lake 2023 is the de-facto kidney healthy reference plus injury-state annotation: (1) the **Azimuth healthy reference** can serve directly as the anomaly-detection reference (map the snRNA of the samples to be tested onto it; low mapping confidence/large distance = candidate anomaly); (2) the **28 altered states give weak labels for "known anomalies"** — KidneyAnomalyBench can use them as a first tier (does a label-blind detector rank aPT/aTAL/degenerative cells highest?), then treat high-scoring cells *outside* Lake's labels as candidate discoveries for expert validation; (3) its admission that altered states also appear in reference tissue (acquisition ischaemia, ageing) is exactly the confound Topic 1's cross-modal agreement idea targets: a cell that looks "injured" in one modality but disagrees in snATAC/Xenium/H&E is more likely a procurement artifact; (4) running the same samples across technologies is the direct precedent for multi-modal designs (e.g. snRNA + snATAC + spatial transcriptomics + H&E); (5) it is a main source of the open KPMP data used by the NIH Kidney AI challenge, and its cell states support the challenge's criterion-3 patient stratification. For "model/revert": the simulated TFAP2B perturbation disrupting the repair trajectory is an early GRN-level "virtual perturbation" to compare against virtual-tissue predictions. For **Topic 2**, it is the most-cited "known result" an agent analysing kidney omics will reach for — useful for building claim-to-evidence ground truth (e.g. "aTAL markers are PROM1/DCDC2/ITGB6") and tests of "restating literature vs deriving from data".
