## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 这是一篇**软件工程 / HPC 系统**论文:作者把 Dompé 的 Exscalate 对接平台重写为「每节点一个 MPI 进程 + 节点内 reader→splitter→docker→writer 异步流水线 + CUDA/C++ 异构 worker」,并在外面套一层工作流(用决策树预测每个配体的对接耗时 → 按耗时分桶 → ~3400 个独立 SLURM 作业,每个约 5 分钟),从而在 CINECA-Marconi100 + ENI-HPC5(合计 81 PFLOPS)上于 **60 小时**内把 **700 亿**个小分子对接到 SARS-CoV-2 **12 个病毒蛋白的 15 个结合位点**上,合计约 **1 万亿次**打分,双机吞吐 **5M ligands/s**(单节点约 2.2k ligands/s)。
<!-- EN --> This is a **software-engineering / HPC systems** paper: the authors rewrote Dompé's Exscalate docking platform as "one MPI process per node + an asynchronous in-node reader→splitter→docker→writer pipeline + heterogeneous CUDA/C++ workers", wrapped in a workflow (a decision tree predicts each ligand's docking time → ligands are bucketed by predicted time → ~3,400 independent SLURM jobs of ~5 minutes each). On CINECA-Marconi100 + ENI-HPC5 (81 PFLOPS combined) this docked **70+ billion** small molecules against **15 binding sites of 12 SARS-CoV-2 proteins** in **60 hours** — about **1 trillion** scoring evaluations at **5M ligands/s** across both machines (~2.2k ligands/s per node).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 在「urgent computing」(疫情期间要尽快找到候选药物)的场景下,虚拟筛选的瓶颈不是化学而是工程:分子可以用已知化学反应在计算机里生成,所以化学库大小只受算力限制;而 TOP500 前五的机器里有四台是 GPU 异构节点,要吃满算力就必须解决一串系统问题——怎么高效用加速器、怎么把数据从存储搬到节点再喂给 GPU、怎么减少节点间通信与进程同步、怎么在节点故障时不丢掉已完成的计算。作者的目标函数很直接:**在给定时间预算内筛完尽可能多的配体**。
<!-- EN --> Under "urgent computing" (a pandemic demands candidate drugs as fast as possible) the bottleneck in virtual screening is engineering, not chemistry: because molecules can be enumerated in silico from known reactions, library size is limited only by compute — and four of the top five TOP500 machines have GPU-heterogeneous nodes, so saturating them means solving a chain of systems problems: how to use accelerators efficiently, how to move data from storage into the node and feed the GPUs, how to minimise inter-node communication and process synchronisation, and how to stay resilient so a node failure does not discard completed work. The objective is blunt: **screen as many ligands as possible within a fixed time budget**.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **(1) 打分算法(四步)**:① 配体预处理——旋转可转键把分子「摊平」,最大化所有原子间内部距离之和(与蛋白无关,可一次性预计算并跨 campaign 复用);② 用**带多次重启的贪心优化**把配体放进结合位点,驱动搜索的打分函数**只考虑几何位阻**,配体柔性被建模、口袋当刚体,实验中每个配体评估 **256 个初始位姿**;③ 以 **RMSD 3 Å** 为阈值对生成位姿聚类,每簇最高分者排前、其余按几何分排序;④ 用 **LiGen 化学打分函数**对**前 30 个位姿**重打分,配体得分 = 最佳位姿得分。复杂度 O(n·m)(n 原子数、m 可转键数);有 C++17(CPU)、OpenACC 与 CUDA 三套实现,NVIDIA GPU 上 CUDA 最快。实测最快与最慢配体类别之间**耗时相差一个数量级以上**;GPU 版耗时对原子数不敏感(硬件并行),但对可转键数敏感(必须串行处理以保持分子几何),且因为按 32 原子为一束组织计算,在 64、96 原子处有阶跃。
<!-- EN --> **(1) The dock-and-score algorithm (four steps)**: ① ligand pre-processing — rotate torsional bonds to "flatten" the molecule, maximising the sum of internal inter-atomic distances (protein-independent, so precomputed once and reused across campaigns); ② dock into the binding site with a **greedy optimiser with multiple restarts**, driven by a scoring function considering **only geometric steric effects**, with ligand flexibility modelled and the pocket rigid — **256 initial poses per ligand** in the campaign; ③ cluster the generated poses at an **RMSD threshold of 3 Å**, putting each cluster's best pose first and sorting the rest by the geometric score; ④ re-score the **top 30 poses** with the **LiGen chemical scoring function**; the ligand's score is that of its best pose. Complexity is O(n·m) in atoms n and torsional bonds m; there are C++17 (CPU), OpenACC and CUDA implementations, CUDA fastest on NVIDIA GPUs. Measured per-ligand time spans **more than an order of magnitude** between the fastest and slowest ligand classes; the GPU version is insensitive to atom count (hardware parallelism) but sensitive to torsional-bond count (bonds must be processed serially to preserve geometry), with step increases at 64 and 96 atoms because work is organised in bundles of 32 atoms.
<!-- ZH --> **(2) 高吞吐对接应用**:单体式(monolithic)MPI 应用,**每节点一个进程**,进程内用 C++11 线程开一条异步流水线,每 stage 一个专用线程 + 线程安全队列:**reader**(顺序读化学库文件块)→ **splitter**(把块切成单个配体描述)→ **docker**(构建数据结构、对接打分)→ **writer**(把 SMILES + 分数以 CSV 形式攒满缓冲区后批量写出)。只有 docker stage 可以有多个线程并在同一队列上**工作窃取**,且可以混用 CUDA worker 与 CPP worker 来吃节点异构性;一个 GPU 可绑多个 CUDA worker(round-robin)。口袋信息在启动时读一次常驻(C++ 用常量静态内存,CUDA 用 texture memory)。输入文件按**文件大小与进程数静态均分**成 slab(约定:描述起点落在本 slab 内的配体归本进程,最后一个配体的描述可以越界),读是顺序的、对 I/O 友好,但静态划分**消除了 MPI 进程间的工作窃取**,于是应用吞吐 = 最慢进程的吞吐。writer 用 **collective I/O** 合并写请求,发起 I/O 的进程数可配置以降低文件系统压力。软件栈:libdpipe(封装 MPI 与 C++ 线程的高层接口)+ LiGenDock/LiGenScore(领域逻辑)+ MPI/CUDA。**不依赖节点本地存储**(目标机器上没有)——这是与 AutoDock-GPU/Summit 路线的关键区别。
<!-- EN --> **(2) The high-throughput docking application**: a monolithic MPI application, **one process per node**, each spawning an asynchronous pipeline of C++11 threads — one dedicated thread per stage plus a thread-safe input queue: **reader** (sequentially reads chunks of the library file) → **splitter** (separates individual ligand descriptions) → **docker** (builds structures, docks and scores) → **writer** (accumulates SMILES + score in CSV-like form and flushes in batches). Only the docker stage may hold several threads **work-stealing** from the same queues, and it may mix CUDA and CPP workers to exploit node heterogeneity; several CUDA workers may be bound round-robin to one GPU. Pocket information is read once at start-up and kept resident (constant static memory for C++, texture memory for CUDA). The input file is partitioned **statically into equal slabs by file size and process count** (convention: a process handles every ligand whose description *begins* inside its slab, the last description may run past the slab end), keeping reads sequential and I/O-friendly — but static partitioning **removes work-stealing across MPI processes**, so application throughput equals that of the slowest process. The writer uses **collective I/O** to coalesce writes, with a configurable number of I/O-issuing processes to limit filesystem pressure. Stack: libdpipe (high-level MPI + C++ threads) + LiGenDock/LiGenScore (domain logic) + MPI/CUDA. It deliberately **does not rely on node-local storage** (unavailable on the target machines) — the key departure from the AutoDock-GPU/Summit route.
<!-- ZH --> **(3) Exscalate 工作流(补偿应用的弱点)**:把一个覆盖全机的巨型 MPI 作业拆成**一批可并行的小作业**(SLURM/PBS job array),解决两件事——**容错**(MPI 通信域默认在节点故障时终止所有进程,会丢掉大量算力;拆小后只需重跑小作业)与**负载均衡**(大而复杂的配体若聚在一起会严重拖慢对应进程)。流程:SMILES 化学库 → 用**决策树(最大深度 16)**按 SMILES 层面可直接得到的特征(重原子数、环数、链数及其交互项)预测对接耗时 → 按 **10 ms** 为宽度分桶 → 配体预处理(加氢、生成 3D 初始坐标、摊平;一次性、可跨 campaign 复用)→ 生成自定义二进制配体文件 → **一次一个口袋**地在所有配体文件上跑对接应用 → 每个位点一份配体排名。领域专家挑出对多个位点/蛋白都强结合的分子后,因**对接算法是确定性的**,可**按需重算 3D 位姿**,所以只需存 SMILES + 最佳分数。工作流需要两类领域输入:靶蛋白的结合位点(怎么得到被明确划在本文范围之外)和待评估的化学库。
<!-- EN --> **(3) The Exscalate workflow (compensating for the application's weak points)**: instead of one giant machine-wide MPI job, launch **many small parallel jobs** via a plain SLURM/PBS job array. This buys two things — **fault resilience** (the default MPI response to a node failure is to terminate every process in the communicator, discarding large amounts of work; small jobs mean small re-runs) and **load balance** (large, complex ligands clustered together would badly slow the owning process). The flow: SMILES library → predict docking time with a **decision tree (max depth 16)** from features directly available at the SMILES level (heavy-atom count, ring count, chain count and their interactions) → bucket ligands at **10 ms** width → ligand pre-processing (add hydrogens, generate initial 3D coordinates, unfold — done once, reusable across campaigns) → emit custom binary ligand files → run the docking application over all ligand files **one pocket at a time** → one ranking of the library per site. After experts pick molecules binding strongly across several sites or proteins, the 3D pose can be **regenerated on demand** because the docking algorithm is **deterministic** — hence only SMILES + best score need storing. The workflow takes two kinds of domain input: the target proteins' binding sites (how to obtain them is explicitly out of scope) and the chemical library to evaluate.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **靶点**:SARS-CoV-2 蛋白质组主要功能单元中的 12 个蛋白、15 个结合位点,晶体结构取自 PDB(3CL protease/NSP5 = 6LU7、N-protein = 6VYO、NSP3 = 6W02、NSP9 = 6W4B、NSP12 = 7BV2、NSP13 = 6XEZ、NSP15 = 6W01、NSP16 = 6W4H、PL protease = 6W9C、Spike-ACE2 = 6M0J);**NSP6 用 de novo 模型、NSP14 用同源模型**(无晶体结构时生成同源模型)。口袋信息总共只占 29MB,启动时读一次。
<!-- EN --> **Targets**: 12 proteins / 15 binding sites covering the main functional units of the SARS-CoV-2 proteome, with crystal structures from the PDB (3CL protease/NSP5 = 6LU7, N-protein = 6VYO, NSP3 = 6W02, NSP9 = 6W4B, NSP12 = 7BV2, NSP13 = 6XEZ, NSP15 = 6W01, NSP16 = 6W4H, PL protease = 6W9C, Spike-ACE2 = 6M0J); **NSP6 uses a de novo model and NSP14 a homology model** (homology models were generated where no crystal structure existed). Pocket information totals only 29 MB, read once at start-up.
<!-- ZH --> **化学库**:700 亿配体。SMILES 格式共 **3.3TB**;对接应用需要更丰富的描述,作者用自定义二进制格式(只存原子坐标、类型、键)代替 TRIPOS Mol2(ASCII、可读性优先),**Mol2 要多占 5–6 倍空间**,二进制全库 **59TB**。若把所有对接位姿都存下来(15 个位点 × 每配体 30 个重打分位姿)需要 **26PB**,因此只存 SMILES + 最佳分数,最终输出 **7.3TB**(结论段写作 64TB,与 §4.1 的 7.3TB 不一致,原文未解释)。**调参用的小数据集**:MEDIATE 网站公开的 "Commercial Compound MW<330",500 万个小分子。**耗时预测模型的数据**:2100 万配体的实测对接时间,80% 训练 / 20% 测试。
<!-- EN --> **Chemical library**: 70+ billion ligands. In SMILES it is **3.3 TB**; the docking application needs a richer description, so a custom binary format (atom positions, types, bonds only) replaces TRIPOS Mol2 (ASCII, readability-first), which would need **5–6× more space** — the binary library is **59 TB**. Storing every docked pose (15 sites × 30 re-scored poses per ligand) would require **26 PB**, so only SMILES + best score are kept, giving a **7.3 TB** final output (the conclusion instead says 64 TB; the discrepancy with §4.1's 7.3 TB is unexplained). **Tuning dataset**: "Commercial Compound MW<330" from the MEDIATE website, 5 million small molecules, publicly available. **Data for the time-prediction model**: measured docking times for 21 million ligands, 80% train / 20% test.
<!-- ZH --> **机器**:Marconi100 节点 = 32 核 IBM POWER9 AC922(128 硬件线程)+ 4× NVIDIA V100 + NVLINK 2.0;HPC5 节点 = Intel Xeon Gold 6252 24C(24 核 / 48 硬件线程)+ 4× V100,NVLINK 只用于 GPU 互连;InfiniBand 网络。注:§4 开头把库规模写成「70 million ligands」,与摘要/结论的 700 亿矛盾,应为笔误。
<!-- EN --> **Machines**: a Marconi100 node = 32-core IBM POWER9 AC922 (128 hardware threads) + 4× NVIDIA V100 with NVLINK 2.0; an HPC5 node = Intel Xeon Gold 6252 24C (24 cores / 48 hardware threads) + 4× V100, NVLINK only between GPUs; InfiniBand interconnect. Note: the opening of §4 states "70 million ligands", contradicting the 70+ billion of the abstract and conclusion — evidently a typo.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **规模**:60 小时、两机合计 81 PFLOPS、700 亿配体 × 15 个结合位点 ≈ 1 万亿次对接打分,作者称这是当时**最大的一次虚拟筛选 campaign**。 <!-- EN --> **Scale**: 60 hours on two machines totalling 81 PFLOPS, 70+ billion ligands × 15 binding sites ≈ 1 trillion dock-and-score evaluations — claimed as the **largest virtual-screening campaign to that date**.
- <!-- ZH --> **吞吐**:单节点平均 **2.2k ligands/s**,双机合计 **5M ligands/s**。逐位点(表 2):Marconi100 上每节点 2427–2499 ligands/s(如 PLPRO 2496、3CL 2427),整机约 1.94–2.00M ligands/s;HPC5 上每节点 1965–2010,整机约 2.95–3.02M ligands/s;NSP13ortho 在两台机器上各算了一部分(每节点 2454/1987)。机器吞吐是用「跑完整库的墙上时间 ÷ 配体数」算的,**包含所有执行开销**。 <!-- EN --> **Throughput**: **2.2k ligands/s** per node on average and **5M ligands/s** across both machines. Per site (Table 2): 2,427–2,499 ligands/s/node on Marconi100 (e.g. PLPRO 2,496; 3CL 2,427) for ~1.94–2.00M machine-wide; 1,965–2,010 on HPC5 for ~2.95–3.02M; NSP13ortho was split across both machines (2,454/1,987 per node). Machine throughput is wall-time-to-solution divided by library size, so it **includes all execution overheads**.
- <!-- ZH --> **异构 worker 扫描(Marconi100 单节点,图 7)**:吞吐在 **CUDA worker 数较多**时达到峰值(约 2.4k ligands/s),而**把 CPP worker 数加到匹配硬件线程数反而伤性能**(128 CPP worker 时掉到 664–985 ligands/s)。结论:在本案例里**CPU 最好用来支撑加速器与 I/O,而不是参与计算本身**;另外单个 CUDA worker 吃不满一块 GPU(worker 要先解析配体描述、初始化数据结构才能发 kernel),多 worker 才能把这些开销隐藏掉。 <!-- EN --> **Heterogeneous-worker sweep (one Marconi100 node, Fig. 7)**: throughput peaks with a **high CUDA-worker count** (~2.4k ligands/s), while **raising CPP workers to match the hardware-thread count hurts** (664–985 ligands/s at 128 CPP workers). The conclusion: in this case study **CPUs are best used to support the accelerators and I/O rather than to compute**; and a single CUDA worker cannot saturate a GPU (it must parse the ligand description and initialise structures before launching kernels), so several workers are needed to hide that overhead.
- <!-- ZH --> **耗时预测模型**:决策树在 2100 万配体上的**平均误差可忽略(−0.00088 ms)、标准差 3.81 ms**;因为单配体预测仍有误差,工作流按 10 ms 分桶来吸收波动——负载均衡只需要平均行为对。 <!-- EN --> **Time-prediction model**: on 21 million ligands the decision tree has a **negligible mean error (−0.00088 ms) with a 3.81 ms standard deviation**; since per-ligand predictions still err, the workflow buckets at 10 ms to absorb the variance — load balancing only needs the average behaviour to be right.
- <!-- ZH --> **作业划分**:输入被切成 **~3400 个作业**,每个 32 个 MPI 进程、约 5 分钟、只针对一个结合位点;位点被**顺序**评估。全机运行时测到的单节点吞吐与扫描调参时基本一致,说明平台确实把可用资源都用上了。 <!-- EN --> **Job decomposition**: the input was split into **~3,400 jobs**, each 32 MPI processes, ~5 minutes, targeting a single binding site; sites were evaluated **sequentially**. Per-node throughput at full-machine scale matches that measured during the worker sweep, i.e. the platform did exploit all available resources.
- <!-- ZH --> **产出公开**:每个靶点筛出的最有希望的化合物集合发布在 MEDIATE 门户,供全球研究者在缩小后的集合上开展更细致的 de novo campaign。 <!-- EN --> **Public output**: the most promising compound set filtered per target was released on the MEDIATE portal so researchers worldwide can start finer de novo campaigns from a reduced set.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **用「小作业数组 + 预测驱动的分桶」替代巨型 MPI 作业**:一个同时解容错与负载均衡的朴素但有效的设计——MPI 层面做不到的跨进程工作窃取,被搬到调度器层面用 job array 近似实现。 <!-- EN --> **Replacing one giant MPI job with "a job array of small jobs + prediction-driven bucketing"**: a plain but effective design solving fault resilience and load balance at once — the cross-process work-stealing MPI cannot do is approximated at the scheduler level.
- <!-- ZH --> **把「对接耗时」本身当成可学习的量**:用 SMILES 层面即可获取的特征(重原子/环/链)训练决策树预测耗时再据此分桶,是 campaign 规划里一个很干净、可直接搬用的 cost model。 <!-- EN --> **Treating docking time itself as a learnable quantity**: a decision tree over features available already at the SMILES level (heavy atoms, rings, chains) predicts runtime and drives bucketing — a clean, directly transferable cost model for campaign planning.
- <!-- ZH --> **节点内异步流水线 + 可混搭的 CUDA/CPP worker**:在不用节点本地存储的前提下吃满 GPU 异构节点,并给出「CPU 该当 I/O 与喂数据的仆役」这一实测结论。 <!-- EN --> **An in-node asynchronous pipeline with mixable CUDA/CPP workers**: saturates GPU-heterogeneous nodes without node-local storage, with the measured conclusion that CPUs should serve I/O and data feeding.
- <!-- ZH --> **确定性算法 ⇒ 用「SMILES + 最佳分数」替代位姿存储**:把 26PB 的存储需求压到 7.3TB,位姿按需重算。这是极大规模筛选里关键的「可复算而非可存储」思路。 <!-- EN --> **A deterministic algorithm ⇒ store "SMILES + best score" instead of poses**: shrinks a 26 PB storage requirement to 7.3 TB, regenerating poses on demand — a key "recompute rather than store" idea at extreme scale.
- <!-- ZH --> **自定义紧凑二进制配体格式**:比 TRIPOS Mol2 省 5–6 倍空间,把全库压到 59TB。 <!-- EN --> **A compact custom binary ligand format**: 5–6× smaller than TRIPOS Mol2, bringing the full library to 59 TB.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **科学产出完全未评估**:论文明说是从软件工程角度写的,通篇只有吞吐、存储、预测误差,**没有任何 enrichment、命中率、已知活性分子 recovery,也没有体外验证**;「最有希望的化合物」由领域专家在排名之后挑选,挑选标准与结果不在本文。 <!-- EN --> **The scientific output is not evaluated at all**: the paper is explicitly written from a software-engineering viewpoint and reports only throughput, storage and prediction error — **no enrichment, no hit rate, no recovery of known actives, no in vitro validation**. The "most promising compounds" were selected by domain experts after ranking, and neither the criteria nor the outcome appear here.
- <!-- ZH --> **打分函数很粗**:驱动搜索的只是几何位阻分,口袋被当作刚体,只重打分前 30 个位姿——这是为吞吐刻意做的精度妥协,论文没有量化它在精度上的代价(例如与 AutoDock 类方法的对比)。 <!-- EN --> **A very coarse scoring function**: search is driven by a purely steric geometric score, the pocket is rigid, and only the top 30 poses are re-scored — a deliberate accuracy trade-off for throughput whose cost is never quantified (e.g. against AutoDock-class methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **静态数据划分抹掉了跨进程工作窃取**,应用吞吐被最慢进程锁死;分桶只是部分缓解(单配体预测的标准差 3.81 ms 仍在)。 <!-- EN --> **Static data partitioning removes cross-process work stealing**, pinning throughput to the slowest process; bucketing only partly mitigates this (the 3.81 ms per-ligand standard deviation remains).
- <!-- ZH --> **一次只跑一个口袋、位点顺序评估**,库被完整扫过 15 遍;没有任何「先筛一部分再决定继续投多少算力」的自适应机制——所有 700 亿配体都被对接了所有 15 个位点。 <!-- EN --> **One pocket at a time, sites evaluated sequentially**, so the library is swept 15 times in full; there is no adaptive mechanism to decide where further compute should go — all 7×10¹⁰ ligands were docked against all 15 sites.
- <!-- ZH --> **可复现性门槛极高**:需要两台欧洲顶级超算、59TB 输入;Exscalate/LiGenDock/LiGenScore 是 Dompé 的专有栈,论文未给代码链接。 <!-- EN --> **Reproducibility is out of reach**: it needs two top European supercomputers and a 59 TB input; Exscalate/LiGenDock/LiGenScore are Dompé's proprietary stack and no code link is given.
- <!-- ZH --> **数字内部不一致**:输出规模 7.3TB(§4.1)vs 64TB(结论);库规模在 §4 开头写成 70 million。引用时应以摘要/结论的 700 亿与 §4.1 的逐项推算为准,并注明此不一致。 <!-- EN --> **Internal inconsistencies**: output size 7.3 TB (§4.1) vs 64 TB (conclusion); library size written as "70 million" at the start of §4. ## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 对 Hayat lab(Uniklinik Aachen)的「**Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening**」课题(LLM agent + 分子基础模型 + docking + active learning,给定蛋白结构自动规划/执行/改进筛选 campaign、找出合适化合物),这篇是**被编排的那一层的上限参考**,同时也是「为什么需要 agent」的最好反面教材。
<!-- EN --> For the Hayat-lab (Uniklinik Aachen) project "**Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening**" (LLM agents + molecular foundation models + docking + active learning, planning/executing/improving campaigns from protein structures to find suitable compounds), this paper is the **ceiling reference for the layer being orchestrated** — and the best argument for why orchestration is needed at all.
- <!-- ZH --> **它是穷举 baseline,我们要做的是自适应替代品。** EXSCALATE 把 700 亿 × 15 个位点全算了一遍,靠的是 81 PFLOPS;active-learning 式筛选的全部价值就在于用极小一部分算力拿到相近的 top-k。所以它给我们一个干净的**对照轴**:在同一个库/靶点上,「全量 docking 的 top-k」是 agent 流水线的 ground truth,而**compute-to-recall 曲线**(花多少次 docking 调用、恢复多少 top-k)就是评价指标。 <!-- EN --> **It is the exhaustive baseline our adaptive pipeline must beat.** EXSCALATE docked 7×10¹⁰ ligands against 15 sites by brute force on 81 PFLOPS; the entire value proposition of active-learning screening is reaching a comparable top-k at a small fraction of that compute. That gives a clean **comparison axis**: on a fixed library/target, "top-k from exhaustive docking" is the ground truth for an agent pipeline, and the **compute-to-recall curve** (docking calls spent vs top-k recovered) is the metric.
- <!-- ZH --> **耗时预测模型可以直接搬进 agent 的 planner。** 一个会规划的 agent 必须能回答「这批 1e6 个分子在这个口袋上要花多久、够不够预算」。本文的决策树(重原子数/环数/链数 → 对接毫秒数,平均误差 ≈0、σ=3.81 ms)就是这个 **cost model** 的现成形态,而且只需要 SMILES、不需要先做 3D 预处理——正适合 planner 在决定是否展开某个批次之前调用。按预测耗时分桶、以及「用 10 ms 粒度吸收预测误差」这个技巧,对我们做批次调度同样适用。 <!-- EN --> **The runtime-prediction model drops straight into the agent's planner.** A planning agent must answer "how long will this batch of 10⁶ molecules take on this pocket, and does it fit the budget?" The decision tree here (heavy atoms / rings / chains → docking milliseconds, mean error ≈ 0, σ = 3.81 ms) is exactly that **cost model**, and it needs only SMILES, no 3D pre-processing — ideal for a planner deciding whether to expand a batch. Bucketing by predicted time, and absorbing prediction error at 10 ms granularity, transfer directly to our batch scheduling.
- <!-- ZH --> **把 docking 当作可编排的工具,需要的正是这里的工程契约。** 一次一个口袋的作业粒度(~5 分钟 × 32 进程)天然就是 agent 可以发起、取消、重试的**动作单元**;确定性算法 +「只存 SMILES+分数、位姿按需重算」给 agent 一个极便宜的状态表示(不必在记忆里携带几十 TB 的位姿);小作业数组的容错语义让长程 campaign 中途失败不至于全盘重来。这些都是我们设计 docking 工具接口时该照抄的约定。 <!-- EN --> **Treating docking as an orchestratable tool needs precisely these engineering contracts.** The one-pocket job granularity (~5 min × 32 processes) is a natural **action unit** an agent can launch, cancel and retry; a deterministic algorithm plus "store only SMILES + score, regenerate poses on demand" gives the agent a very cheap state representation (no tens of TB of poses in its memory); and the small-job-array fault semantics mean a mid-campaign failure does not restart everything. These are conventions to copy when designing our docking tool interface.
- <!-- ZH --> **多位点 / 多蛋白是天然的 agent 决策空间。** 本文为 12 个蛋白准备了 15 个位点,其中 NSP6 用 de novo 模型、NSP14 用同源模型——这正是我们「给定蛋白结构」场景里的真实情况(结构质量不齐)。它顺序、均等地对待所有位点;而 orchestrator 该做的恰恰是**不均等**:按位点的结构置信度(de novo/同源 vs 晶体)与早期命中富集情况,决定继续投多少算力、要不要换位点、要不要先补一次结构预测。「口袋识别」在本文被明确划到范围之外,而在我们的流水线里它应当是 agent 的第一步。 <!-- EN --> **Multiple sites / proteins are a natural agent decision space.** The paper prepares 15 sites over 12 proteins, with NSP6 from a de novo model and NSP14 from a homology model — exactly our "given protein structures" setting, with uneven structural quality. It treats all sites sequentially and equally; an orchestrator's job is to be **unequal**: allocate further compute by structural confidence (de novo/homology vs crystal) and early hit enrichment, decide whether to switch sites, or to run a structure prediction first. Pocket identification is explicitly out of scope here, whereas in our pipeline it should be the agent's first step.
- <!-- ZH --> **它也给出了我们必须回答的问题。** EXSCALATE 完全没报科学指标,所以不能用它来论证「大 = 好」;反过来,如果 agent 流水线要宣称优于穷举,就必须自带它所缺的东西——enrichment/命中率、已知活性 recovery,以及对打分粗糙性(几何位阻 + 刚性口袋)的误差传播分析。与 `competitive-docking-npjdrugdiscov-2026` 对读很有用:后者用 co-folding 模型在十几个体系上量化了排序与实验的一致性(c-index 0.52–0.89),但只能跑几百个配体。两篇正好标出我们流水线里「便宜而粗」与「贵而准」两档打分的两端,而多档打分器之间的**级联决策**(先用谁、何时升级、何时放弃)正是 orchestrator 的核心职责。 <!-- EN --> **It also sets the bar we must clear.** EXSCALATE reports no scientific metric, so it cannot be cited for "bigger is better"; conversely, an agent pipeline claiming to beat brute force must supply what is missing here — enrichment/hit rate, recovery of known actives, and an error-propagation analysis for the coarse scoring (steric score + rigid pocket). Reading it against `competitive-docking-npjdrugdiscov-2026` is instructive: that work quantifies ranking–experiment agreement with co-folding models across ~17 systems (c-index 0.52–0.89) but handles only hundreds of ligands. The two mark the cheap-and-coarse and expensive-and-accurate ends of our scoring ladder, and the **cascade decision** between tiers (which scorer first, when to escalate, when to abandon) is the orchestrator's central job.
