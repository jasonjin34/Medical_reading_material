## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 把来自两个小鼠亚种(*M. m. castaneus* CAST/EiJ 与 *M. m. domesticus* C57BL/6J、129S1/SvImJ)的肾脏细胞混在同一个 10x 通道里,用亚种间纯合 SNP 区分「本细胞的分子」和「外来分子」,从而得到**每个细胞背景噪声比例 ρ_cell 的基因型真值**;发现背景噪声在重复间、细胞间差异很大(平均 3–35% 的 UMI),主要来自 ambient RNA 而非 barcode swapping,并以此真值评测 CellBender / DecontX / SoupX —— CellBender 估计最准、对 marker 基因改善最大,而聚类与分类对背景噪声相当稳健。
<!-- EN --> Kidney cells from two mouse subspecies (*M. m. castaneus* CAST/EiJ and *M. m. domesticus* C57BL/6J and 129S1/SvImJ) are pooled in one 10x channel so that subspecies-diagnostic homozygous SNPs separate endogenous from foreign molecules, giving a **genotype-based ground truth for the per-cell background-noise fraction ρ_cell**. Background noise varies widely between replicates and cells (3–35% of UMIs on average), comes mostly from ambient RNA rather than barcode swapping, and — used as ground truth to benchmark CellBender / DecontX / SoupX — CellBender gives the most precise estimates and the biggest gain for marker genes, while clustering and classification are fairly robust to background noise.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 液滴式 scRNA-seq / snRNA-seq 中,一个 cell barcode 上的 reads 并不全来自被包裹的细胞:破裂细胞释放的游离 **ambient RNA** 以及建库 PCR 中的 **barcode swapping**(嵌合分子)都会造成背景噪声。它会让 marker 基因在不该表达的细胞类型里被检出、降低 log2FC、甚至伪造「新细胞类型」,还会在条件间差异表达中制造假阳性。已有去噪方法(SoupX、DecontX、CellBender)常用的评测基准是人-鼠细胞系混合(只能看到跨物种的、特征空间不重叠的污染,细胞类型单一)或「排他性 marker 基因」,两者都无法刻画真实组织中**同一基因上的表达量偏移**。作者要回答:背景噪声有多大、多变、从哪来,对下游分析影响多大,去噪工具能否在没有基因型信息时正确估计并去除它。
<!-- EN --> In droplet-based scRNA-seq / snRNA-seq not every read on a cell barcode comes from the encapsulated cell: cell-free **ambient RNA** from broken cells and **barcode swapping** (chimeric molecules) during library PCR both add background noise. It makes marker genes appear in the wrong cell types, shrinks log2FC, can suggest spurious "novel" cell types, and can create false positives in between-condition DE. Existing decontamination tools (SoupX, DecontX, CellBender) are usually evaluated on a human–mouse cell-line mix (cross-species contamination in non-overlapping features, low cell-type diversity) or on exclusive marker genes — neither captures the **shifts in expression level of the same gene** that dominate in real tissue. The paper asks how large, variable and where-from background noise is, how much it affects downstream analysis, and whether the tools can estimate and remove it without genotype information.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **实验设计**:每个 10x 通道(Chromium Next GEM 3' v3.1,目标 10,000 个细胞/核)混合 3 只小鼠(CAST/EiJ、C57BL/6J、129S1/SvImJ,6–8 周,PBS 心内灌注)的肾脏四分之一块。scRNA-seq 用新鲜组织 Liberase TH 酶解;snRNA-seq 用 RNAlater 保存的冷冻组织做 TST 核提取(nuc3 比 nuc2 多洗两次)。Cell Ranger 3.0.2(sc 用 mm10 2020A,sn 用 pre-mRNA mm10)→ DropletUtils `barcodeRanks` 拐点 ∪ Cell Ranger 细胞调用定义细胞 → cellsnp-lite(≥20 UMI、MAF≥0.1)+ Vireo 按 Mouse Genomes Project 的 VCF 解复用,去掉 doublet/未分配 → SingleR 以 Denisenko et al. 小鼠肾脏数据为参考注释 13 种细胞类型。**背景比例估计**:只用区分两亚种的纯合 SNP,改编 souporcell(Heaton et al.)的方法:每个位点可观测污染概率 p = ρ_c · A_{l,1}/(A_{l,0}+A_{l,1}),其中 A_{l,1}/(A_{l,0}+A_{l,1}) 是全实验(细胞+空液滴)的外来等位基因频率,用来外推同基因型(看不见的)污染;以二项似然在 [0,1] 上一维优化求 ρ_c 的 MLE,profile likelihood 给 95% CI。主要分析只用 CAST 细胞(占 9–43%,其污染主要来自 domesticus,因此可见比例高),排除线粒体基因。**来源分析**:用 domesticus 等位基因计数构建 endogenous(domesticus 细胞)、contamination(CAST 细胞)、empty(UMI 曲线平台区 ~500–1000 UMI 的空液滴)三种 pseudobulk 谱,比较 Spearman 相关、SCDC 反卷积细胞组成、内含子比例;用「同一 CB-UMI 对应多个基因」识别 PCR 嵌合体估计 swapping。**基准**:CellBender v0.2.0(fpr 0.01,另试 0.05/0.1)、SoupX 1.6.1(autoEstCont,另试手动 0.1/0.2)、DecontX(celda 1.12.0;default 与以空液滴为 background 两种);每细胞估计 a_c = 1 − Σx/Σr,与 ρ_c 比较 RMSLE 和 Kendall τ;下游评测 marker 基因检出率/log2FC、SingleR 预测分数、silhouette、cluster purity、与「基因型清洗」矩阵的 k-NN(k=50)重叠。
<!-- EN --> **Design**: each 10x channel (Chromium Next GEM 3' v3.1, targeting 10,000 cells/nuclei) pools kidney quarters from three mice (CAST/EiJ, C57BL/6J, 129S1/SvImJ; 6–8 weeks, PBS-perfused). scRNA-seq: fresh tissue, Liberase TH dissociation; snRNA-seq: RNAlater-preserved frozen tissue, TST nuclei isolation (nuc3 had two extra washes vs nuc2). Cell Ranger 3.0.2 (mm10 2020A for sc, pre-mRNA mm10 for sn) → cells = DropletUtils `barcodeRanks` inflection ∪ Cell Ranger calls → cellsnp-lite (≥20 UMIs, MAF ≥0.1) + Vireo against the Mouse Genomes Project VCF, dropping doublets/unassigned → SingleR with Denisenko et al. mouse kidney reference (13 cell types). **Background estimate**: using only homozygous subspecies-diagnostic SNPs, an adaptation of souporcell (Heaton et al.): the observable contamination probability per locus is p = ρ_c · A_{l,1}/(A_{l,0}+A_{l,1}), where the experiment-wide (cells + empty droplets) foreign-allele fraction extrapolates to unseen same-genotype contamination; ρ_c is the binomial-likelihood MLE by 1-D optimisation on [0,1], with profile-likelihood 95% CIs. Main analyses use only CAST cells (9–43% of cells; their contamination comes mostly from domesticus and is therefore largely visible), excluding mitochondrial genes. **Source analysis**: domesticus-allele counts form endogenous (domesticus cells), contamination (CAST cells) and empty (plateau barcodes with ~500–1000 UMIs) pseudobulk profiles, compared by Spearman correlation, SCDC deconvolution and intron fraction; PCR chimeras = one CB-UMI mapping to several genes. **Benchmark**: CellBender v0.2.0 (fpr 0.01; also 0.05/0.1), SoupX 1.6.1 (autoEstCont; also fixed 0.1/0.2), DecontX (celda 1.12.0; default vs empty-droplet background). Per-cell estimate a_c = 1 − Σx/Σr vs ρ_c by RMSLE and Kendall τ; downstream: marker detection rate/log2FC, SingleR prediction score, silhouette, cluster purity, and k-NN (k=50) overlap with a "genotype-cleaned" matrix.

<!-- EN --> Five 10x channels: scRNA-seq **rep1, rep2, rep3** (fresh tissue) and snRNA-seq **nuc2, nuc3** (frozen tissue, from the same samples as rep2 and rep3 respectively). ## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> (1) **幅度与变异**:每实验外来基因型计数中位数 2–27%(可见下界);外推后的 ρ_cell 中位数:rep3 大多数细胞 <3%,rep2 约 11%,nuc2 35%,nuc3 17%(rep1 未在正文给出,见 Fig. 2A);冷冻组织 snRNA-seq 明显高于对应的 scRNA-seq(35% vs 11%,17% vs 3%);与细胞产量无关。污染 UMI 的**绝对量在细胞间近似恒定**,因此细胞越大比例越小。(2) **来源**:每细胞污染 UMI 数与空液滴 UMI 数相当;sc 中 contamination 谱与 empty(ρ=0.73–0.85)和 endogenous(ρ=0.70–0.87)都高度相关,sn 中更像 empty(~0.85 vs ~0.50),且 sn 空液滴中 PT 比例下降;内含子比例 contamination 更接近 empty;PCR 嵌合体中位比例 0.2%(rep3)–0.7%(nuc3),对背景的贡献中位 <10% → 主要是 ambient RNA。(3) **marker 基因**:PT marker Slc34a1 在 rep2 约 60% 的非 PT 细胞中被检出;背景越高 log2FC 越小。(4) **工具估计精度**:sc 中 CellBender 与 DecontX 相近,SoupX 低估且抓不住细胞间差异;sn 中 SoupX 全局水平更好但仍无细胞间相关,CellBender/DecontX 变差;CellBender 默认参数最稳健(代价约 45 CPU 小时/通道);nuc3 所有方法都最差,后续不再分析。(5) **去噪后 marker**:rep2 中 Slc34a1 非 PT 检出率 60% → SoupX 54%、CellBender 7%、DecontX_background 9%、DecontX_default 2%;nuc2 中 DecontX_default 失败(87%),DecontX_background 20%,CellBender 4%,SoupX <1%;CellBender 对已知 marker 的 log2FC 提升最大。(6) **分类与聚类**:去噪后仅 <1%(sc)/ ≤1.3%(nuc2)细胞换标签;DecontX 让 sc 簇最纯但降低 k-NN 与真值的重叠(破坏细粒度结构),SoupX 基本不动细粒度结构;nuc2 高背景下所有方法均改善。**建议**:marker 分析总是去噪;分类/聚类/拟时序只在背景高时去噪。
<!-- EN --> (1) **Size and variability**: median foreign-genotype count fraction per experiment 2–27% (visible lower bound); extrapolated median ρ_cell: rep3 mostly <3%, rep2 ~11%, nuc2 35%, nuc3 17% (rep1 not stated in the text; see Fig. 2A); frozen-tissue snRNA-seq is much higher than the matching scRNA-seq (35% vs 11%, 17% vs 3%) and unrelated to cell yield. The **absolute amount of contaminating UMIs is roughly constant across cells**, so larger cells have smaller fractions. (2) **Source**: contaminating UMIs per cell match UMIs in empty droplets; in sc the contamination profile correlates with both empty (ρ=0.73–0.85) and endogenous (ρ=0.70–0.87), in sn it resembles empty (~0.85 vs ~0.50) and empty droplets show a reduced PT share; the intron fraction of contamination is close to empty; median PCR-chimera fraction 0.2% (rep3)–0.7% (nuc3), median swapping contribution <10% of background → mostly ambient RNA. (3) **Markers**: PT marker Slc34a1 detected in ~60% of non-PT cells in rep2; higher background → smaller log2FC. (4) **Estimation accuracy**: in sc, CellBender ≈ DecontX, SoupX underestimates and misses cell-to-cell variation; in sn, SoupX gets global levels better but still no per-cell correlation, CellBender/DecontX worsen; CellBender is most robust at defaults (cost ~45 CPU h per channel); all methods worst on nuc3, which is dropped thereafter. (5) **Markers after correction**: rep2 Slc34a1 non-PT detection 60% → SoupX 54%, CellBender 7%, DecontX_background 9%, DecontX_default 2%; in nuc2 DecontX_default fails (87%), DecontX_background 20%, CellBender 4%, SoupX <1%; CellBender gives the largest log2FC gain for known markers. (6) **Classification/clustering**: <1% (sc) / up to 1.3% (nuc2) of cells change label; DecontX yields the purest sc clusters but lowers k-NN overlap with the genotype ground truth (distorts fine structure), SoupX leaves fine structure mostly unchanged; at nuc2's high background all methods improve. **Recommendation**: always decontaminate for marker analysis; for classification/clustering/pseudotime only when background is high.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 只有小鼠肾脏、一种平台(10x 3' v3.1)、5 个通道;PT 占 66–89%,细胞组成高度偏斜;nuc2 与 nuc3 实验条件不同(洗涤次数),不是严格重复。 <!-- EN --> One tissue (mouse kidney), one platform (10x 3' v3.1), five channels; PT is 66–89% so composition is skewed; nuc2 and nuc3 differ in protocol (wash steps), so they are not strict replicates.
- <!-- ZH --> 真值只对 CAST 细胞可靠;同基因型污染靠外推(假设 SNP 覆盖的计数代表全细胞),nuc2 每细胞只有 ~100 个信息 SNP,置信区间更宽。 <!-- EN --> Ground truth is reliable only for CAST cells; same-genotype contamination is extrapolated (assuming SNP-covering counts represent the whole cell), and nuc2 has only ~100 informative SNPs per cell, so CIs are wider.
- <!-- ZH --> 健康组织,没有疾病状态;无法区分「背景噪声」与「真实生物学状态变化」在疾病样本中的交互。 <!-- EN --> Healthy tissue only; no disease states, so it cannot show how background noise interacts with real disease-related state changes.
- <!-- ZH --> 只评测了三个工具的固定版本(如 CellBender v0.2.0);默认参数下的结论,SoupX 给定真实背景水平时表现明显变好(Fig. S15)。 <!-- EN --> Only three tools at fixed versions (e.g. CellBender v0.2.0); conclusions are for defaults, and SoupX improves markedly when given the true background level (Fig. S15).
- <!-- ZH --> 正文中各重复的细胞数并不完全一致(5,000–20,000 与 rep3「3900 cells」),需看补充图确认。 <!-- EN --> Per-replicate cell counts in the text are not fully consistent (5,000–20,000 vs rep3 "3900 cells"); the supplementary figures are needed to confirm.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 处在 **detect** 环节的「伪影侧」(伪影注入课题,track A2:质控与伪影)。伪影注入课题要做一个**人类肾脏单细胞基准**,测试 anomaly detector 能否把疾病相关的异常与测量伪影(ambient RNA 等)分开。本文**只用于校准我们注入的 ambient RNA 的强度与谱型**:(1) 强度 —— 每细胞背景比例的经验分布(sc 新鲜组织中位约 3–11%,sn 冷冻组织约 17–35%;每细胞 ρ_c 文件在 Zenodo 每个重复的 `perCell_noMito_CAST_binom.RDS` 中),以及「污染 UMI 绝对量近似恒定、比例随细胞大小下降」这一结构,可直接作为注入模型(固定绝对量 vs 固定比例)的依据;(2) 谱型 —— 背景谱应取自**空液滴**而非细胞平均谱(sn 中 contamination 与 empty 的相关 ~0.85 vs endogenous ~0.50),且 sn 的 ambient 谱中 PT 比例下降、内含子比例偏低;raw `feature_bc_matrix.h5` 提供了构建空液滴谱的原料。(3) 评测设计可借鉴:以「伪影真值」做 RMSLE + Kendall τ,以及用 k-NN 重叠检验「去噪是否伤害细粒度结构」—— 对应我们需要检查伪影注入/去除是否会掩盖或伪造疾病信号。注意它是**小鼠**健康肾脏,不能直接用作人类疾病基准的数据;跨物种/跨平台的强度迁移需要在人类肾脏数据(如 KPMP)的空液滴上再核对。
<!-- EN --> Sits on the **artifact side of the detect stage** (the artifact-injection topic, track A2: QC & artifacts). The artifact-injection topic builds a **human-kidney single-cell benchmark** testing whether anomaly detectors separate disease-related anomalies from measurement artifacts such as ambient RNA. This paper is used **only to calibrate the strength and profile of the ambient RNA we inject**: (1) strength — the empirical distribution of per-cell background fractions (median ~3–11% in fresh-tissue sc, ~17–35% in frozen-tissue sn; per-cell ρ_c in each replicate's `perCell_noMito_CAST_binom.RDS` on Zenodo), plus the finding that the absolute number of contaminating UMIs is roughly constant across cells (fraction falls with cell size) — a direct guide for the injection model (fixed absolute amount vs fixed fraction); (2) profile — the background profile should come from **empty droplets**, not the mean of cells (in sn, contamination correlates ~0.85 with empty vs ~0.50 with endogenous), and the sn ambient profile has fewer PT transcripts and a lower intron fraction; the raw `feature_bc_matrix.h5` files provide the empty droplets needed. (3) Evaluation design to borrow: RMSLE + Kendall τ against an artifact ground truth, and k-NN overlap to test whether decontamination harms fine structure — mirroring our need to check that artifact injection/removal does not mask or fake disease signal. Caveat: it is **healthy mouse** kidney, not usable as human disease benchmark data; transferring the strength across species/platform should be re-checked on empty droplets from human kidney data (e.g. KPMP).
