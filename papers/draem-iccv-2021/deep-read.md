## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> DRÆM 把表面异常检测从"只学正常、看重建误差"改成**判别问题**:在正常图像上用 Perlin 噪声 mask + 外部纹理贴出"刚好越界(just-out-of-distribution)"的合成异常,同时训练一个把异常修复回正常的重建子网络和一个读取"原图 ⊕ 重建图"并输出像素级异常图的判别子网络;MVTec AD 图像级 AUROC 98.0%、像素 AP 68.4%,比此前最好方法分别高 2.5 和 13.4 个百分点。
<!-- EN --> DRÆM recasts surface anomaly detection from "learn normal only, read the reconstruction error" into a **discriminative problem**: it pastes "just-out-of-distribution" synthetic anomalies onto normal images (Perlin-noise masks + external textures) and jointly trains a reconstructive sub-network that inpaints them back to normal and a discriminative sub-network that reads "original ⊕ reconstruction" and outputs a pixel-level anomaly map — 98.0% image AUROC and 68.4% pixel AP on MVTec AD, +2.5 and +13.4 points over the previous best.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 表面异常只占图像很小一部分,且往往离正常分布很近。重建类方法(AE、GAN、AnoGAN/f-AnoGAN)只在正常数据上训练,依赖"异常重建不好"这一假设,但对接近正常的异常常常重建得很好(过度泛化),而且需要手工设计的后处理(如 SSIM 比较)来出异常图,无法为检测目标直接优化特征。反过来,直接在合成异常上训练分割网络又会**过拟合合成外观**,对真实缺陷泛化差。DRÆM 要解决的是:不依赖真实异常、又能端到端判别式训练且泛化到真实异常。
<!-- EN --> Surface anomalies occupy a small fraction of pixels and are often close to the normal distribution. Reconstruction methods (AEs, GANs, AnoGAN/f-AnoGAN) train on normal data only and rely on "anomalies reconstruct badly", yet near-normal anomalies are often reconstructed well (over-generalization), and anomaly maps need hand-crafted post-processing (e.g., SSIM comparison), so features cannot be optimized for detection. Conversely, training a segmenter directly on synthetic anomalies **over-fits the synthetic appearance** and generalizes poorly to real defects. DRÆM asks how to train discriminatively end-to-end, without real anomalies, and still generalize to them.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **重建子网络**:encoder–decoder,输入是被模拟器破坏的图像 I_a,目标是还原原始正常图像 I,损失为 λ·SSIM 损失 + L2(SSIM 用来考虑邻域像素相关)。它学会"把局部异常模式改写成正常模式、正常区域保持不变"。**判别子网络**:U-Net 式结构,输入为 I_a 与重建 I_r 的通道拼接;因为重建子网络会把异常处"修正常",二者的联合外观在异常处差异显著,判别网络因此**学习一个与局部外观相关的距离函数**而非手工 SSIM;用 Focal Loss 监督像素 mask。总损失 = L_rec + L_seg。**异常模拟器**:Perlin 噪声生成随机形状并以随机阈值二值化为 mask M_a;纹理来源是与目标域无关的 DTD 纹理数据集,经 RandAugment 风格的 3 个随机增强(posterize、sharpness、solarize、equalize、亮度、颜色、auto-contrast 中抽取);按 I_a = M̄_a⊙I + (1−β)(M_a⊙I) + β(M_a⊙A) 混合,不透明度 β∈[0.1,1.0] 随机——低 β 产生"轻微扰动",用于收紧决策边界。模拟器同时给出像素完美的 mask。**推理**:异常图 M_o 经 s_f×s_f 均值滤波后取最大值为图像分数 η。训练 700 epoch,lr 1e-4(400/600 epoch 各乘 0.1),正常图像做 (−45°,45°) 旋转增强。
<!-- EN --> **Reconstructive sub-network**: an encoder–decoder taking the simulator-corrupted image I_a and restoring the original normal image I, with loss λ·SSIM loss + L2 (SSIM accounting for neighbouring-pixel dependence). It learns to rewrite local anomalous patterns as normal while leaving normal regions untouched. **Discriminative sub-network**: a U-Net-like net taking the channel-wise concatenation of I_a and the reconstruction I_r; since the reconstruction "normalizes" anomalies, their joint appearance differs strongly there, so the network **learns a local-appearance-conditioned distance function** instead of a hand-crafted SSIM; supervised with Focal Loss on the pixel mask. Total loss = L_rec + L_seg. **Anomaly simulator**: Perlin noise gives random shapes, binarized at a random threshold into mask M_a; textures come from the domain-unrelated DTD texture dataset, with 3 random RandAugment-style ops (from posterize, sharpness, solarize, equalize, brightness, colour, auto-contrast); blending I_a = M̄_a⊙I + (1−β)(M_a⊙I) + β(M_a⊙A) with opacity β ∈ [0.1, 1.0] — low β yields subtle perturbations that tighten the decision boundary. The simulator also yields a pixel-perfect mask. **Inference**: the map M_o is mean-filtered (s_f×s_f) and its maximum is the image score η. Trained 700 epochs, lr 1e-4 (×0.1 at epochs 400 and 600), with (−45°, 45°) rotation augmentation on normal images.

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> **范式:synthetic anomalies / self-supervised**(以重建子网络为辅助的混合体,但核心判别信号来自合成异常)。**承接**:重建路线(Bergmann 等 SSIM-AE、GANomaly、AnoGAN、f-AnoGAN —— 论文在相关工作中引用并作为对照)以及作者自己的 RIAD(inpainting 重建);同时期的 CutPaste 也是合成异常 + 自监督。DRÆM 的关键差异是:合成异常**不需要逼真**,只需"刚好越界",判别器学的是"偏离正常的程度"而非异常外观。**影响**:成为 synthetic-anomaly 家族的标准基线(pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 把 DRAEM 与 CutPaste 一起列为 synthetic anomaly 方法评测;medianomaly-media-2025 的一阶段 SSL 家族 FPI/NSA/PII/AnatPaste 与它同一思路);后续工业 AD 的许多合成异常方法沿用了它的 Perlin-mask + 外部纹理模拟器。
<!-- EN --> **Paradigm: synthetic anomalies / self-supervised** (a hybrid with a reconstructive helper, but the core discriminative signal comes from synthetic anomalies). **Builds on**: the reconstruction line (Bergmann et al.'s SSIM-AE, GANomaly, AnoGAN, f-AnoGAN — cited and used as foils) and the authors' own RIAD (inpainting reconstruction); contemporaneous CutPaste is also synthetic-anomaly self-supervision. DRÆM's key difference: synthetic anomalies **need not be realistic**, only just-out-of-distribution, and the discriminator learns "extent of deviation from normal" rather than anomaly appearance. **Led to**: a standard synthetic-anomaly baseline (pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 benchmarks DRAEM alongside CutPaste as synthetic-anomaly methods; the one-stage SSL family in medianomaly-media-2025 — FPI/NSA/PII/AnatPaste — follows the same idea); many later industrial methods reuse its Perlin-mask + external-texture simulator.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **MVTec AD**(15 类,只用正常图训练);**DAGM 2007**(10 类人工生成纹理,缺陷用椭圆粗标,用于和监督方法比较,只评图像分类)。合成异常的纹理源:**DTD**(Describable Textures Dataset);消融中替换为 ImageNet。
<!-- EN --> **MVTec AD** (15 classes, normal-only training); **DAGM 2007** (10 synthetic texture classes with coarse ellipse defect labels, used to compare against supervised methods; image classification only). Texture source for simulation: **DTD** (Describable Textures Dataset), swapped for ImageNet in an ablation.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **图像级检测(MVTec AUROC)**:DRÆM 平均 **98.0%**,15 类中 9 类最高;对比 PaDiM 95.5%、Rippel 等 (MAD) 94.4%、RIAD 91.7%、Uninformed Students 87.7%、GANomaly 78.2%。较弱类:transistor 93.1%、cable 91.8%(缺失部件类异常最难)。 <!-- EN --> **Image detection (MVTec AUROC)**: DRÆM averages **98.0%**, best on 9 of 15 classes, vs PaDiM 95.5%, Rippel et al. (MAD) 94.4%, RIAD 91.7%, Uninformed Students 87.7%, GANomaly 78.2%. Weaker classes: transistor 93.1%, cable 91.8% (missing-part anomalies are hardest).
- <!-- ZH --> **定位(像素 AUROC / AP)**:DRÆM 97.3 / **68.4**,PaDiM 97.4 / 55.0,RIAD 94.2 / 48.2,US 93.9 / 45.5 —— AUROC 持平,AP 高 13.4 个百分点;作者强调在异常像素极少时 AUROC 被大量正常像素主导,AP 更合适。 <!-- EN --> **Localization (pixel AUROC / AP)**: DRÆM 97.3 / **68.4** vs PaDiM 97.4 / 55.0, RIAD 94.2 / 48.2, US 93.9 / 45.5 — AUROC on par, AP +13.4 points; the authors stress that with few anomalous pixels AUROC is dominated by normal pixels and AP is more appropriate.
- <!-- ZH --> **消融(检测 / 定位 AUROC / AP)**:只有判别网络 93.9 / 92.7 / 62.5(过拟合合成异常);只有重建网络 + SSIM 83.9 / 89.7 / 47.5;换成监督缺陷网络 Božič 等 92.8 / 93.9 / 60.7;纹理源换 ImageNet 97.9 / 97.0 / 67.9;**纯色块替代纹理**仍有 96.2 / 92.6 / 56.5;矩形 mask 替代 Perlin 96.9 / 96.8 / 65.1;去掉增强与不透明度随机化 97.4 / 94.5 / 64.3;只保留 β 随机化 97.9 / 97.1 / 68.4。少于 10 张纹理图即可达到高性能。 <!-- EN --> **Ablations (det. / loc. AUROC / AP)**: discriminative net alone 93.9 / 92.7 / 62.5 (over-fits synthetic anomalies); reconstructive net + SSIM alone 83.9 / 89.7 / 47.5; swapping in the supervised defect network of Božič et al. 92.8 / 93.9 / 60.7; ImageNet as texture source 97.9 / 97.0 / 67.9; **plain colour patches** instead of textures still 96.2 / 92.6 / 56.5; rectangular masks instead of Perlin 96.9 / 96.8 / 65.1; no augmentation or opacity randomization 97.4 / 94.5 / 64.3; β randomization only 97.9 / 97.1 / 68.4. Fewer than 10 texture images suffice for high performance.
- <!-- ZH --> **DAGM**:DRÆM 图像 AUROC 99.0%、分类准确率 98.5%,无监督方法中最好(PaDiM 95.0% / 95.7%),接近全监督方法(Božič 等 100%),且定位优于用粗标训练的监督方法。 <!-- EN --> **DAGM**: DRÆM image AUROC 99.0%, classification accuracy 98.5% — best unsupervised (PaDiM 95.0% / 95.7%), close to fully supervised (Božič et al. 100%), and better-localized than supervised methods trained on coarse labels.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把表面 AD 主要表述为**判别问题**,用"原图 + 重建图"的联合嵌入学习距离函数,无需手工后处理。 <!-- EN --> Casts surface AD primarily as a **discriminative problem**, learning a distance function on the joint "original + reconstruction" embedding without hand-crafted post-processing.
- <!-- ZH --> **"刚好越界"合成异常**原则:模拟器不需逼真,Perlin mask + 无关纹理 + 随机不透明度足矣 —— 消融显示纯色块也能达到 SOTA 级别。 <!-- EN --> The **"just-out-of-distribution" synthetic-anomaly** principle: simulations need not be realistic — Perlin masks + unrelated textures + random opacity suffice; even plain colour patches reach near-SOTA in ablation.
- <!-- ZH --> 像素级 AP 作为极度不平衡定位任务的更合适指标。 <!-- EN --> Pixel AP as the better metric for highly imbalanced localization.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 缺失部件 / 与背景相似的异常难以检测(如 transistor 断脚);部分误差来自真值 mask 本身模糊。 <!-- EN --> Missing parts and background-like anomalies are hard (e.g., cut transistor leads); some errors stem from ambiguous ground-truth masks.
- <!-- ZH --> 需要从头训练两个网络 700 epoch,每类一个模型;比 PaDiM/PatchCore 这类免训练方法昂贵得多。 <!-- EN --> Requires training two networks from scratch for 700 epochs, one model per class — far costlier than training-free PaDiM/PatchCore.
- <!-- ZH --> 模拟器是为**纹理/外观**异常设计的(局部贴图),对逻辑/结构异常(缺件、错位)或语义层面的异常没有对应机制。 <!-- EN --> The simulator targets **texture/appearance** anomalies (local pastes); it has no mechanism for logical/structural (missing, misplaced) or semantic anomalies.
- <!-- ZH --> "合成异常不需逼真"只在工业表面数据上验证;是否能迁移到复杂组织形态未知。 <!-- EN --> "Simulations need not be realistic" is shown only on industrial surfaces; transfer to complex tissue morphology is untested.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> DRÆM 对课题 1 最有价值的不是网络,而是**"用已知真值的合成扰动来训练和评测"这一思路**,它直接对应 KidneyAnomalyBench 的"有真值层"。**映射到肾脏组学**:样本 = 细胞 / spot;正常训练集 = 健康参考;"Perlin mask" = 在 Xenium/Visium 切片上随机选取的空间连通区域;"外部纹理贴图" = 在该区域内注入表达偏移(例如把一组基因按损伤方向缩放、混入另一个细胞类型的表达、改变细胞密度或细胞组成);"不透明度 β" = 扰动强度,低 β 对应亚临床的轻度改变;H&E 端可原样使用 Perlin + 纹理模拟器做 patch 级合成异常。重建子网络对应"把一个细胞/邻域还原到健康状态"的模型 —— 这恰好也是 revert 阶段想要的东西:**重建出的"健康版本"与原始观测之差,就是需要被逆转的变化**。**哪些假设会破**:(1) DRÆM 的模拟器不需逼真,是因为工业缺陷就是局部外观变化;而肾脏疾病(纤维化、PT 损伤)是**连续、弥散**的状态变化,"刚好越界"的边界很难定义,判别器可能学到的是合成扰动的特征签名而非疾病;(2) 合成扰动若与技术伪影(ambient RNA 的"混入别的细胞类型表达"、doublet 的"两种细胞混合"、分割误差导致的转录本错配)形态相同,模型会把伪影与疾病混为一类 —— 反过来,这也是机会:**可以有意把 ambient/doublet/分割错误作为一类合成"伪影"、把损伤方向扰动作为另一类,训练模型区分二者**,这与课题 1 的跨模态伪影 vs 疾病区分直接对应;(3) 组学无像素 mask,但合成扰动天然自带 mask,正好补上 PRO/AP 类指标。**仓库里已有的同类思想**:pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 直接评测 DRAEM,并用"在正常 Unitopatho 块上叠加图案"构造了已知真值的合成病理数据集;medianomaly-media-2025 系统比较了 CutPaste、FPI、NSA、PII、AnatPaste 这类合成异常 SSL 方法,发现分割任务上重建类明显优于 SSL 类(朴素 AE 在 BraTS2021 上胜过所有一阶段 SSL 分割法),且**合成异常越逼真越好**(两阶段 AnatPaste 比 CutPaste 在 RSNA 上 AUC 高 20.2%)—— 这与 DRÆM「不需逼真」的结论相反,是对 DRÆM 式方法在生物医学上的重要警示;ambient-rna-bench-biorxiv-2026 / xenium-split-natmethods-2026 / ovrlpy-natbiotech-2026 提供的伪影模型可作为"伪影型合成扰动"的生成器。
<!-- EN --> DRÆM's main value for Topic 1 is not the network but the idea of **training and evaluating with synthetic perturbations of known ground truth** — which maps directly onto KidneyAnomalyBench's ground-truth layer. **Mapping to kidney omics**: samples = cells/spots; normal training set = healthy reference; "Perlin mask" = a randomly chosen spatially connected region on a Xenium/Visium section; "external texture paste" = injected expression shifts inside that region (scaling a gene set in the injury direction, mixing in another cell type's profile, changing cell density or composition); "opacity β" = perturbation strength, low β being subclinical change; on H&E the Perlin + texture simulator ports unchanged for patch-level synthetic anomalies. The reconstructive sub-network corresponds to a model that "restores a cell/neighbourhood to its healthy state" — which is also what the revert stage wants: **the difference between the reconstructed healthy version and the observation is the change to be reverted**. **What breaks**: (1) DRÆM's simulations need not be realistic because industrial defects are local appearance changes; kidney disease (fibrosis, PT injury) is a **continuous, diffuse** state change, so "just out of distribution" is ill-defined and the discriminator may learn the signature of the synthetic perturbation rather than disease; (2) if synthetic perturbations look like technical artifacts (ambient RNA = "mixed-in expression of another type", doublets = "two cells blended", segmentation errors = misassigned transcripts), the model conflates artifact with disease — but this is also an opportunity: **deliberately simulate ambient/doublet/segmentation errors as one synthetic "artifact" class and injury-direction shifts as another, and train the model to tell them apart**, mirroring Topic 1's artifact-vs-disease separation; (3) omics has no pixel masks, but synthetic perturbations come with masks, enabling PRO/AP-type metrics. **Existing repo papers with the same idea**: pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 benchmarks DRAEM directly and builds known-ground-truth synthetic pathology by overlaying patterns on normal Unitopatho patches; medianomaly-media-2025 systematically compares synthetic-anomaly SSL methods (CutPaste, FPI, NSA, PII, AnatPaste) and finds reconstruction clearly beats SSL for segmentation (a plain AE beats every one-stage SSL segmenter on BraTS2021) and that **more realistic synthetic anomalies help** (two-stage AnatPaste over CutPaste: +20.2% AUC on RSNA) — the opposite of DRÆM's "need not be realistic", and an important caution for DRÆM-style methods in biomedicine; the artifact models in ambient-rna-bench-biorxiv-2026 / xenium-split-natmethods-2026 / ovrlpy-natbiotech-2026 could serve as generators for "artifact-type" synthetic perturbations.
