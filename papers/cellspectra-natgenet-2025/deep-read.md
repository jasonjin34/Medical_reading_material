## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 两件事:(1) **SISKA 1.0** —— 人、小鼠、大鼠 > 100 万细胞、> 140 个样本的跨物种单细胞(snRNA)肾脏图谱,21 个主要细胞类型、近 80 个跨物种保守的细胞状态;(2) **CellSpectra** —— 一个**单样本**统计工具:对每个细胞类型 × 通路,用参照样本伪 bulk 矩阵的 SVD 第一主轴作为"协调模式",把待测样本回归上去,R² 降低 + χ² 检验即判为该样本该通路"协调性丢失";据此生成**患者级单细胞功能画像报告**、为患者选择最匹配的啮齿类模型,并在叶酸肾损伤模型中由 CellSpectra 提名的 TNF 通路用 etanercept 干预改善了肾功能。
<!-- EN --> Two things: (1) **SISKA 1.0** — a cross-species single-nucleus kidney atlas (human, mouse, rat; >1M cells, >140 samples) with 21 main cell types and ~80 cross-species-conserved cell states; (2) **CellSpectra** — a **single-sample** statistic: for each cell type × pathway, the first SVD axis of the reference samples' pseudobulk matrix defines the "coordination pattern"; a query sample is regressed onto it, and lowered R² plus a χ² test flags loss of coordination in that sample. This powers a **patient-level single-cell functional profiling report**, rodent-model selection for a patient, and — in a folic-acid kidney-injury model — the CellSpectra-nominated TNF pathway was targeted with etanercept, improving kidney function.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 单细胞数据要进入临床,需要能在**单个患者**层面找出细胞类型特异、可干预的分子改变,并知道哪个动物模型最像这个患者。现有通路分析多基于单基因均值/方差变化再汇总(如 GSEA),受单细胞稀疏性、技术变异和单样本统计困难所限;而动物模型与患者的对应关系缺乏量化。
<!-- EN --> Bringing single-cell data into the clinic requires finding cell-type-specific, targetable changes in **individual patients** and knowing which animal model best mirrors a given patient. Existing pathway analyses aggregate single-gene mean/variance changes (e.g. GSEA) and suffer from sparsity, technical variability and single-sample statistics; the correspondence between rodent models and patients is not quantified.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **SISKA 整合**:基于约 12k 一对一直系同源基因,用 scVI 整合人(H-CKD、DKD、AKI、健康)、小鼠(AKI 时间序列、DKD、叶酸)与大鼠(ZSF1 糖尿病、DOCA 高血压)snRNA-seq;MetaMarker 找跨物种保守 marker,MetaNeighbor 做相关性独立验证;以"至少在两个物种中出现"来决定细胞状态分辨率。**CellSpectra**:对给定细胞类型与基因集,构建样本 × 基因的伪 bulk 矩阵(每样本该类型 ≥ 10 个细胞,归一化 + log);在参照组上做 SVD,第一个基因载荷向量 v* 视为"eigensample";把每个查询样本回归到 v* 得 R²;参照分布用**留一法**得到(避免 double-dipping);显著性用参数化检验 —— 假设残差 ε_sg ~ N(0, σ_g²),σ_g 由参照残差估计,查询样本的标准化残差平方和服从自由度 G−1 的 χ²,每个基因贡献上限 CHISQ.MAX = 4 以抗离群;FDR 校正。**On-the-fly 参照**:为每个查询样本按其总 counts 对参照细胞下采样(3 个伪重复),消除细胞数/捕获率造成的假阳性。**驱动基因**:按标准化残差排序(report_genes())。**质量报告**:LOO R² 均值/中位数/标准差与 v* 解释方差。
<!-- EN --> **SISKA integration**: ~12k one-to-one orthologues; scVI integration of human (H-CKD, DKD, AKI, healthy), mouse (AKI time course, DKD, folic acid) and rat (ZSF1 diabetic, DOCA hypertensive) snRNA-seq; MetaMarker for conserved markers, MetaNeighbor for independent validation; cell-state resolution chosen by requiring presence in ≥2 species. **CellSpectra**: for a cell type and gene set, a sample × gene pseudobulk matrix (≥10 cells per sample, normalized + log); SVD on the reference, first gene-loading vector v* as an "eigensample"; each query sample is regressed on v* to get R²; the reference R² distribution comes from **leave-one-out** (to avoid double-dipping); significance is parametric — residuals ε_sg ~ N(0, σ_g²) with σ_g estimated from reference residuals, the query's standardized squared residual sum follows χ² with G−1 df, per-gene contribution capped at CHISQ.MAX = 4 for robustness; FDR correction. **On-the-fly reference**: for each query, reference cells are subsampled to match its total counts (3 pseudo-replicates) to stop cell-number/capture-rate inflation. **Driver genes**: ranked by standardized residuals (report_genes()). **Quality report**: LOO R² mean/median/SD and variance explained by v*.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> SISKA:> 100 万细胞、> 140 个样本、三物种;扩展人图谱:150 个健康与疾病样本、> 70 万细胞(含 Penn 肾内科活检与 KPMP 数据),用 scANVI 转移标签;功能画像比较 > 100 个患者样本与 47 个健康参照。公开数据:KPMP、GSE211785、GSE209821(ZSF1 大鼠)、GSE183839(DOCA 大鼠)、GSE184652(DKD 小鼠)、GSE139107(小鼠 AKI 时间序列)、新数据 GSE291551;NSCLC 图谱(CELLxGENE)。
<!-- EN --> SISKA: >1M cells, >140 samples, three species; extended human atlas: 150 healthy and diseased samples, >700k cells (including Penn nephrology biopsies and KPMP), labels transferred with scANVI; profiling compares >100 patient samples to 47 healthy references. Public data: KPMP, GSE211785, GSE209821 (ZSF1 rat), GSE183839 (DOCA rat), GSE184652 (DKD mouse), GSE139107 (mouse AKI time course), new GSE291551; an NSCLC atlas (CELLxGENE).

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **图谱**:21 个主要细胞类型,> 900 个跨物种保守 marker(852 个仅属于单一细胞类型),近 80 个跨物种保守细胞状态;人肾 PTS3 细胞明显更少。 <!-- EN --> **Atlas**: 21 main cell types, >900 conserved markers (852 unique to one type), ~80 conserved states; humans have markedly fewer PTS3 cells.
- <!-- ZH --> **协调性**:多数功能基因集中 v* 解释方差 > 75%;"Regulation of glomerular filtration" 在啮齿类足细胞上与人高度一致(R² 高),其他细胞类型则低;PEC 中 "Regulation of protein kinase C signaling" 大鼠比小鼠更像人;**没有一个啮齿类模型在所有功能上都最优**。 <!-- EN --> **Coordination**: for most functional gene sets v* explains >75% of variance; "Regulation of glomerular filtration" is highly concordant in rodent vs human podocytes but not other cell types; PEC "Regulation of protein kinase C signaling" is more human-like in rat than mouse; **no single rodent model is best across all functions**.
- <!-- ZH --> **单样本检测**:小鼠 AKI 时间序列中 PT "Response to ischemia" 在再灌注后 4 h 与 12 h 协调性显著下降,后期与糖尿病小鼠无变化;更重的 db/db + AAV-Renin 模型受影响通路数多于 db/db。 <!-- EN --> **Single-sample detection**: in the mouse AKI time course, PT "Response to ischemia" loses coordination at 4 h and 12 h after reperfusion but not at later times or in diabetic mice; the more severe db/db + AAV-Renin model has more affected pathways than db/db.
- <!-- ZH --> **治疗效应**:糖尿病大鼠足细胞的协调性改变几乎被 sGC 激活剂完全逆转,sGC 刺激剂则不能(与原研究的表型结果一致);足细胞 "SMAD protein signal transduction" 仅被激活剂恢复。 <!-- EN --> **Treatment effects**: podocyte coordination changes in diabetic rats are almost fully reversed by an sGC activator but not a stimulator (matching phenotypes in the original study); podocyte "SMAD protein signal transduction" is restored only by the activator.
- <!-- ZH --> **实验验证**:叶酸模型中显著改变的基因集数量与 PT 组织学损伤一致;CellSpectra 在多数样本检出 mTOR 与 TNF 信号改变,而 GSEA 几乎检不出;**etanercept(TNF 抑制剂)显著降低叶酸损伤小鼠的 BUN**(每组 n = 5)。作者强调 CellSpectra 本身不给出变化方向。 <!-- EN --> **Experimental validation**: in folic-acid mice the number of significant gene sets tracks PT histological damage; CellSpectra detects mTOR and TNF changes in most samples where GSEA finds few; **etanercept (TNF inhibitor) significantly lowered BUN** in folic-acid injury (n = 5 per group). The authors note CellSpectra itself does not give the direction of change.
- <!-- ZH --> **患者画像**:PT "Vasculature Development" 在 > 70% 疾病样本中改变;高血压患者 DCT_CNT_CD "Sodium Ion Transport" 协调性改变(SCN3A、SLC23A2、SLC24A1、SCNN1B;NEDD4L),非高血压者无;ADPKD 中 TNF 与 mTOR 改变最常见,"Water Transport" 与 CFTR/水通道蛋白改变相符(与 tolvaptan 疗效一致);肺癌中恶性细胞协调性改变远多于非恶性上皮,LUSC 多于 LUAD。 <!-- EN --> **Patient profiles**: PT "Vasculature Development" is altered in >70% of diseased samples; hypertensive patients show DCT_CNT_CD "Sodium Ion Transport" changes (SCN3A, SLC23A2, SLC24A1, SCNN1B; NEDD4L) absent in non-hypertensives; ADPKD has the highest prevalence of TNF and mTOR changes and "Water Transport" changes with CFTR/aquaporins (consistent with tolvaptan efficacy); in lung cancer, malignant cells show far more coordination changes than non-malignant epithelium, LUSC more than LUAD.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **单样本、带统计置信度**的通路级偏离检验:不是"上调/富集",而是"基因间相对表达模式是否偏离参照"。 <!-- EN --> A **single-sample, statistically calibrated** pathway-level deviation test: not "up/enriched" but "does the relative expression pattern among genes deviate from the reference".
- <!-- ZH --> 用跨物种保守性来决定细胞状态分辨率,并用协调性 R² 量化动物模型与患者的功能相似度。 <!-- EN --> Uses cross-species conservation to set cell-state resolution, and coordination R² to quantify functional similarity between rodent models and patients.
- <!-- ZH --> On-the-fly 参照下采样控制细胞数/捕获深度这一技术混杂;每次查询自带质量报告。 <!-- EN --> On-the-fly reference subsampling controls cell-number/depth confounding; every query ships with a quality report.
- <!-- ZH --> 从计算提名到动物干预(etanercept)闭环验证。 <!-- EN --> Closes the loop from computational nomination to animal intervention (etanercept).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 线性假设(SVD + 回归),可能漏掉非线性关系;多层计算有过拟合风险(作者自述)。 <!-- EN --> Linear assumptions (SVD + regression) may miss non-linear structure; multiple computational layers risk overfitting (acknowledged).
- <!-- ZH --> 只检测"协调性丢失",**不给方向**;依赖 GO/KEGG 等并非为协调性设计的基因集;稀有细胞类型受稀疏性影响。 <!-- EN --> Detects loss of coordination but **not direction**; relies on GO/KEGG gene sets not designed for coordination; rare cell types suffer from sparsity.
- <!-- ZH --> 跨物种只用约 12k 直系同源基因;人类预测大多尚无干预验证;缺少用药等临床元数据,参照尚未按年龄/性别匹配。 <!-- EN --> Cross-species limited to ~12k orthologues; most human predictions lack interventional validation; missing clinical metadata such as medication; references not yet age/sex-matched.
- <!-- ZH --> 一个 sample × 通路 × 细胞类型的检验在成千上万组合上进行,多重检验与依赖结构使"显著通路数"作为严重度指标需谨慎。 <!-- EN --> Thousands of sample × pathway × cell-type tests: multiple testing and dependence make "number of significant pathways" a severity proxy to use with care.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> CellSpectra 本质上是一个**单样本、细胞类型 × 通路粒度的异常检测器**:参照 = 健康样本的协调模式,异常 = 查询样本相对参照的标准化残差(χ² 检验),再加上驱动基因解释 —— 这几乎就是 **伪影注入课题** 想要的"可解释、带置信度的患者级异常报告"在单细胞层面的现成版本。可直接借用:(1) **作为伪影注入课题的强基线**:在待测肾样本(或 KPMP / Lake 2023 疾病样本)的 snRNA 上跑 CellSpectra(以 KPMP/Lake 健康样本为参照),我们的方法应在专家验证的异常上优于它,或至少提供它没有的空间/跨模态维度;(2) **on-the-fly 参照下采样**直接回应"细胞数/捕获率差异造成假异常"这一技术伪影 —— 应纳入 KidneyAnomalyBench 的必测混杂;(3) 它的 χ² 单样本检验 + 质量报告,是"校准的异常分数"的一个可比较实现;(4) **跨模态扩展**是我们的增量:CellSpectra 只看 snRNA,而同一患者的其他模态(如染色质可及性或空间组学)若一致偏离,才更可能是疾病而非伪影。对 **detect → model → revert**,它完整走了一遍:检测协调性丢失(detect)→ 跨物种选择匹配模型(model)→ 在模型中用 etanercept 阻断 TNF 并改善 BUN(revert 的湿实验验证);sGC 激活剂"逆转"足细胞协调性则示范了**用协调性恢复作为 revert 读数**。对 **Agent 可信度课题**:Functional Profiling Report 是 agent 很可能被要求生成/解释的产物;它自陈"不给方向",正是 claim-to-evidence verifier 需要拦截的一类过度声明(例如 agent 把"协调性改变"说成"通路激活")。
<!-- EN --> CellSpectra is essentially a **single-sample anomaly detector at cell-type × pathway granularity**: reference = healthy coordination pattern, anomaly = standardized residuals of a query relative to it (χ² test), plus driver-gene explanations — nearly an off-the-shelf single-cell version of the "interpretable, calibrated patient-level anomaly report" **the artifact-injection topic** wants. Directly reusable: (1) **a strong the artifact-injection topic baseline** — run CellSpectra on snRNA from the kidney samples to be tested (or KPMP / Lake 2023 disease samples) with KPMP/Lake healthy samples as reference; our method should beat it on expert-validated anomalies or at least add spatial/cross-modal dimensions it lacks; (2) **on-the-fly reference subsampling** addresses "false anomalies from cell-number/capture differences" — a confound KidneyAnomalyBench must test; (3) its χ² single-sample test and quality report are a comparable implementation of a "calibrated anomaly score"; (4) **cross-modal extension** is our increment: CellSpectra sees only snRNA, whereas consistent deviation in other modalities from the same patient (e.g. chromatin accessibility or spatial omics) is what makes disease more likely than artifact. For **detect → model → revert** it walks the whole chain: detect loss of coordination → pick the matching model cross-species → block TNF with etanercept and improve BUN (wet-lab revert validation); the sGC activator "reversing" podocyte coordination shows **coordination recovery as a revert readout**. For **the agent-trustworthiness topic**: the Functional Profiling Report is exactly what an agent might be asked to produce or interpret; its admission that it gives no direction is a classic overclaim a claim-to-evidence verifier must catch (e.g. an agent calling a "coordination change" a "pathway activation").
