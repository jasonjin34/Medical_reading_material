## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 作者在 19 位健康供体的肾皮质上同时做 3' scRNA-seq、5' scRNA-seq 与 10x multiome(snRNA + snATAC),得到 119,744 个细胞/核的基准数据集 mBDRC,并开发可解释的监督式分类器 scOMM,系统比较「水平(sc↔sn RNA)」「垂直(RNA↔ATAC 同一细胞)」「全局(mosaic/diagonal)」三类整合策略对肾细胞类型/状态分辨率的贡献;全局整合找回了单一模态里看不到的 WFDC2⁺ aTAL 亚群与产 EPO 的 Norn 细胞。
<!-- EN --> On renal cortex from 19 healthy donors the authors run 3' scRNA-seq, 5' scRNA-seq and 10x multiome (snRNA + snATAC) side by side, yielding the 119,744-cell/nucleus benchmark mBDRC, and build scOMM, an interpretable supervised classifier, to systematically compare "horizontal" (sc↔sn RNA), "vertical" (RNA↔ATAC in the same nuclei) and "global" (mosaic/diagonal) integration for resolving kidney cell types and states. Global integration recovers a WFDC2⁺ adaptive-TAL subpopulation and EPO-producing Norn cells that single modalities miss.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 大型器官图谱(HCA、KPMP 等)越来越多地混用 scRNA、snRNA、snATAC 等不同协议与模态,但「每种模态对识别哪些细胞类型/状态有独特贡献、整合到底带来多少增益」缺少系统评估。以往的 benchmark 多是在同一类 assay 内比较实验方法,或只比较批次校正算法;对肾这种细胞脆弱、上皮亚型呈连续梯度(DCT/CNT/PC)、存在 adaptive/injury 状态的复杂组织,缺乏有配对供体、多协议的真实基准数据。
<!-- EN --> Large organ atlases (HCA, KPMP, etc.) increasingly mix protocols and modalities — scRNA, snRNA, snATAC — yet there is no systematic assessment of what each modality uniquely contributes to identifying cell types/states, or how much integration actually helps. Previous benchmarks compared experimental methods within one assay class or batch-correction algorithms alone; for a complex tissue like the kidney — fragile cells, gradient-like epithelial continua (DCT/CNT/PC), adaptive/injury states — a real, donor-matched, multi-protocol benchmark was missing.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **实验设计**:多中心;33 份肾皮质样本(25 份来自有多份样本的供体 = paired,8 份 unpaired),来源于肾切除术中的正常组织,分装冷冻后分别做 3'/5' scRNA-seq 和 multiome。另有少量 Smart-seq2、scNMT-seq(数据不足,仅作补充);MERFISH 多次优化仍未得到可用数据。**注释**:锚定已有人肾参考(HCA kidney 等),两级注释 L1(12–14 个大类)与 L2(39 个类型/状态)。**scOMM**:基于前馈神经网络的参考映射分类器(R 包),直接在表达值(或 peaks / gene activity)上训练;用「把某基因置零后准确率下降 ≥10 个相对点」定义 feature importance(FI),从而可解释地比较各模态的 marker 贡献。在肾、PBMC、心脏 multiome 上对比 Seurat、CHETAH、scmap,与 Seurat label transfer 精度接近。**整合策略**:水平整合用 scVI(benchmark 最高分);ATAC 横向整合比较 SnapATAC2 Spectral-MNN、Signac LSI-Harmony、PeakVI、PoissonVI(Spectral-MNN 最好);垂直整合比较 SnapATAC2 multi-spectral 与优化后的 Seurat WNN;全局整合比较 MultiVI(mosaic,利用配对细胞)与 GLUE(diagonal,非配对)。**指标**:AUC(无监督聚类 vs scOMM 标签,specificity > 0.9)、Mahalanobis 距离 / 重叠比、binary cLISI(b-cLISI)、平均轮廓宽度(AWS)、marker 检出率(对照 HCA 参考 marker)。另做经验功效分析(方差分解、per-donor 均值/方差/检出率、purity、silhouette),以及用 LDSC 在细胞类型特异 CRE 上做高血压 / eGFR 遗传力富集。
<!-- EN --> **Design**: multicentre; 33 renal-cortex samples (25 from donors with multiple samples = paired, 8 unpaired) from normal tissue at nephrectomy, aliquoted and frozen for 3'/5' scRNA-seq and multiome. A few Smart-seq2 and scNMT-seq runs are reported separately (too little data); MERFISH never produced usable data despite optimisation. **Annotation**: anchored on existing human kidney references (HCA kidney etc.), two levels — L1 (12–14 broad types) and L2 (39 types/states). **scOMM**: a feedforward-network reference-mapping classifier (R package) trained directly on expression values (or peaks / gene activity); feature importance (FI) = relative accuracy drop ≥10 points when a gene is zeroed, giving an interpretable per-modality marker comparison. Benchmarked against Seurat, CHETAH and scmap on kidney, PBMC and heart multiome; closely matches Seurat label transfer. **Integration strategies**: horizontal with scVI (top benchmark score); ATAC harmonisation comparing SnapATAC2 Spectral-MNN, Signac LSI-Harmony, PeakVI and PoissonVI (Spectral-MNN best); vertical comparing SnapATAC2 multi-spectral vs optimised Seurat WNN; global comparing MultiVI (mosaic, uses paired cells) vs GLUE (diagonal, unpaired). **Metrics**: AUC (unsupervised clusters vs scOMM labels at specificity > 0.9), Mahalanobis distance / overlap ratio, binary cLISI (b-cLISI), average silhouette width (AWS), marker detection rate against HCA reference markers. Plus an empirical power analysis (variance decomposition, per-donor mean/variance/detection, purity, silhouette) and LDSC heritability enrichment for hypertension and eGFR in cell-type-specific CREs.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> mBDRC:119,744 个高质量细胞/核,19 位健康供体(性别、年龄、BMI 多样),33 份样本;水平整合 97,125 个细胞/核;multiome 11 份样本 37,717 个配对核。外部验证:一个独立的公开肾 multiome + CosMx 空间转录组数据集(论文引用 [23]),以及一个人心脏图谱(3′ sc/snRNA + multiome)。原始数据 dbGaP phs004459;整合数据将通过 CELLxGENE 发布。
<!-- EN --> mBDRC: 119,744 high-quality cells/nuclei from 19 healthy donors (diverse sex, age, BMI) across 33 samples; 97,125 cells/nuclei in the horizontal integration; 11 multiome samples with 37,717 paired nuclei. External validation: an independent public kidney multiome + CosMx spatial dataset (ref. [23] in the paper) and a human heart atlas (3′ sc/snRNA + multiome). Raw data on dbGaP phs004459; integrated data to be released via CELLxGENE.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **sn vs sc 功效分析**:表达方差中 assay 解释中位 24%、细胞类型 13%、assay×细胞类型交互 6%、供体仅 3% —— 协议差异远大于供体差异。snRNA-seq 测到更多基因(dropout 更低)、更多细胞、簇纯度更高,在肾这类脆弱组织里多数细胞类型上信息量更大;但交互项说明两者互补。 <!-- EN --> **sn vs sc power analysis**: assay explains a median 24% of expression variance, cell type 13%, assay×cell-type interaction 6%, donor only 3% — protocol differences dwarf donor differences. snRNA-seq measures more genes (lower dropout), detects more cells and gives purer clusters, so for most cell types in fragile kidney tissue it carries more information; the interaction term shows the two are complementary.
- <!-- ZH --> **水平整合**:PT 亚型(PT-S1、aPT、PT-S3)在 snRNA 中最准;TAL(C-TAL、aTAL)在 3' scRNA 中最准;MD、DCT1、CNT 等连续/低丰度群在 5' scRNA 中最好。整合后 Mahalanobis 距离多数升高,b-cLISI 在 CNT/DCT/PC 上提升(snRNA 与 5' scRNA 整合后 >0.8)。snRNA 的 marker 检出率在 POD、PC、DCT 约 0.5,为各协议最高;整合模型平衡各协议,并检出独有 marker(如 CNT-PC 的 PTP4A1)。 <!-- EN --> **Horizontal integration**: PT subtypes (PT-S1, aPT, PT-S3) are best captured by snRNA; TAL (C-TAL, aTAL) by 3' scRNA; continuous/low-abundance MD, DCT1, CNT by 5' scRNA. Integration raises Mahalanobis separation for most populations and b-cLISI for CNT/DCT/PC (>0.8 after integration for snRNA and 5' scRNA). snRNA has the highest marker detection rate (~0.5 for POD, PC, DCT); the integrated model balances protocols and finds unique markers (e.g. PTP4A1 for CNT-PC).
- <!-- ZH --> **垂直整合**:WNN 几乎在所有细胞类型上都偏向 RNA 权重,稀释了 ATAC 贡献;multi-spectral 的轮廓分数更高,呈近似「加性」的互补效应(尤其 POD、PEC、C-TAL、IC-B),b-cLISI 在 aPT、CNT-PC、DCT1 上更高。scOMM 三变体中,直接用 peaks 的 Bridge 模型 AUC 最高;gene-activity 的 Cross-Modality 模型最差;genes+peaks 的 Integrated 模型并未超过 Bridge。 <!-- EN --> **Vertical integration**: WNN weights RNA in almost every cell type, diluting ATAC; multi-spectral gives higher silhouettes with a near-additive complementary effect (notably POD, PEC, C-TAL, IC-B) and higher b-cLISI in aPT, CNT-PC, DCT1. Among scOMM variants, the peaks-based Bridge model has the highest AUC; gene-activity Cross-Modality is worst; genes+peaks Integrated does not beat Bridge.
- <!-- ZH --> **全局整合**:MultiVI(mosaic)与 GLUE(diagonal)无显著差异,且总体上并不优于更简单的组合;但对 adaptive 状态有利 —— aTAL1 的 b-cLISI 从垂直整合的 0.45 升至 mosaic 的 0.61。重聚类发现以 3' scRNA 为主的 WFDC2⁺ aTAL1_0(WFDC2/HE4 曾被提为狼疮肾炎与 CKD 血清标志物),以及 aFIB 中极罕见的产 EPO 的 Norn 细胞(DCN、TIMP1、CFD),两者在 snRNA 中几乎看不到。 <!-- EN --> **Global integration**: MultiVI (mosaic) and GLUE (diagonal) do not differ significantly and overall do not beat simpler combinations, but help adaptive states — aTAL1 b-cLISI rises from 0.45 (vertical) to 0.61 (mosaic). Reclustering reveals a 3'-scRNA-dominated WFDC2⁺ aTAL1_0 (WFDC2/HE4 is a proposed lupus-nephritis/CKD serum biomarker) and very rare EPO-producing Norn cells within aFIB (DCN, TIMP1, CFD), both nearly invisible in snRNA.
- <!-- ZH --> **外部复现**:在独立数据集中,Norn 与 WFDC2⁺ aTAL1_0 签名在疾病样本中显著上调(p < 0.01 / p < 0.001),在 CosMx 中分别定位于纤维母细胞与 Loop of Henle,并富集于纤维化 / 损伤上皮 niche;scOMM 投影也找回了两群。在外部肾数据中,ATAC 嵌入更好地分开 adaptive 群(aTAL1 b-LISI 0.69 vs RNA 0.55;aPT 0.81 vs 0.66),RNA 更好地分开远端小管(CNT、DCT1)。 <!-- EN --> **External replication**: in an independent dataset, Norn and WFDC2⁺ aTAL1_0 signatures are significantly up in disease samples (p < 0.01 / p < 0.001), localise in CosMx to fibroblasts and the Loop of Henle respectively, and are enriched in fibrotic/injured epithelial niches; scOMM projection recovers both. In the external kidney data ATAC embeddings separate adaptive populations better (aTAL1 b-LISI 0.69 vs RNA 0.55; aPT 0.81 vs 0.66), while RNA better separates distal tubule (CNT, DCT1).
- <!-- ZH --> **遗传力**:MD 细胞对高血压与 eGFR 均有强富集;内皮与 VSMC/周细胞只富集高血压;在 CRE 选择中加入转录信号(Set 2/3)得到显著更高的遗传力富集。 <!-- EN --> **Heritability**: MD cells are strongly enriched for both hypertension and eGFR; endothelium and VSMC/pericytes only for hypertension; adding transcription signal to CRE selection (Sets 2/3) gives significantly higher enrichment.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 首个供体匹配、四协议(3'/5' scRNA、snRNA、snATAC)的肾皮质多模态基准数据集 mBDRC。 <!-- EN --> The first donor-matched, four-protocol (3'/5' scRNA, snRNA, snATAC) renal-cortex multimodal benchmark, mBDRC.
- <!-- ZH --> scOMM:可解释、按模态比较 marker 贡献的参考映射工具。 <!-- EN --> scOMM: an interpretable reference-mapping tool for per-modality marker contribution.
- <!-- ZH --> 针对细胞类型特性给出整合策略的实用指南:哪类细胞该用哪种协议/整合方式。 <!-- EN --> Practical, cell-type-aware guidelines on which protocol/integration resolves which populations.
- <!-- ZH --> 找回并在外部数据与空间上验证了两个疾病相关的罕见状态(WFDC2⁺ aTAL、Norn)。 <!-- EN --> Recovers two disease-associated rare states (WFDC2⁺ aTAL, Norn) and validates them externally and spatially.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 跨协议的配对样本数有限,供体异质性会干扰 GRN/TF 推断等分析。 <!-- EN --> Limited matched samples across protocols; donor heterogeneity confounds analyses such as GRN/TF inference.
- <!-- ZH --> 细胞类型识别缺少真正的 ground truth,只能依赖专家注释与模拟场景。 <!-- EN --> No true ground truth for cell identity; relies on expert annotation and simulated scenarios.
- <!-- ZH --> 仅健康组织;Smart-seq2/scNMT-seq 未针对冷冻肾优化,MERFISH 失败;对其他组织的泛化有限(虽然在心脏上做了验证)。 <!-- EN --> Healthy tissue only; Smart-seq2/scNMT-seq not optimised for frozen kidney, MERFISH failed; generalisation to other tissues is limited (despite a heart check).
- <!-- ZH --> 部分 aTAL/Norn 的检出差异可能部分来自协议敏感性,作者认为是生物学差异,但无法完全排除技术偏倚。 <!-- EN --> Some protocol-specific detection of aTAL/Norn could partly reflect sensitivity; the authors argue biology, but technical bias is not fully excluded.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是课题 1 最直接的「数据层」参考:对 snRNA + snATAC(+ Xenium + H&E)这类多模态设计,这篇用健康供体量化了「哪些肾细胞状态只在某个模态里可见」。对 detect → model → revert 中的 **detect** 阶段,它给出两条硬约束:**(1) 协议效应(中位 24%)远大于供体效应(3%)**,所以任何「异常」打分都必须先在 assay 维度上对齐,否则模型会把协议差异当作异常;**(2) 模态间不一致并不总是伪影** —— aTAL、Norn 这类 adaptive/rare 状态恰恰只在部分模态中被看见,而它们在疾病样本中上调。这正是课题 1「cross-modal agreement 区分伪影与疾病」需要细化的地方:不能简单把「跨模态不一致」判为伪影,而应结合细胞类型特异的模态灵敏度先验(本文给出了 PT→snRNA、TAL→3'、MD/DCT/CNT→5'、adaptive→ATAC 的经验表)。mBDRC 作为**健康参考**,可直接用于 KidneyAnomalyBench 的正常分布建模;WFDC2⁺ aTAL1_0 与 Norn 可作为专家可验证的「已知阳性异常状态」候选。对课题 2,scOMM 的 feature importance 与「整合并非总是提升」的结论,是 agent 在分析肾组学时常犯的过度结论的现成反例(例如声称「多模态整合必然更好」),可纳入组学特异的失败分类与 claim-to-evidence 校验用例。
<!-- EN --> This is Topic 1's most direct "data-layer" reference: for a multi-modal design of this kind (snRNA + snATAC, plus Xenium + H&E), this paper quantifies, on healthy donors, which kidney cell states are visible only in some modalities. For the **detect** stage of detect → model → revert it gives two hard constraints: **(1) protocol effects (median 24%) dwarf donor effects (3%)**, so any anomaly score must be aligned along the assay axis first or the model will flag protocol differences as anomalies; **(2) cross-modal disagreement is not always artefact** — adaptive/rare states like aTAL and Norn are seen only in some modalities, and they rise in disease. That is exactly where Topic 1's "cross-modal agreement separates artefacts from disease" needs refinement: disagreement should not be read as artefact by default but weighed against cell-type-specific modality sensitivity priors (the paper gives an empirical table: PT→snRNA, TAL→3', MD/DCT/CNT→5', adaptive→ATAC). mBDRC as a **healthy reference** can serve the normal-distribution modelling for KidneyAnomalyBench; WFDC2⁺ aTAL1_0 and Norn are candidate expert-verifiable "known-positive anomalous states". For Topic 2, scOMM's feature importance and the finding that integration does not always help supply ready counter-examples to typical agent over-claims (e.g. "multimodal integration is always better") for an omics-specific failure taxonomy and claim-to-evidence test cases.
