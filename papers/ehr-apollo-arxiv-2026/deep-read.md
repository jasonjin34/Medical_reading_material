## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> APOLLO(Mahmood 组):在 Mass General Brigham 17 家医院 33 年、**720 万病人、253 亿条医疗事件、28 种模态**(结构化码、化验、生命体征、用药、病程记录与报告文本、病理/血涂片图像)上做掩码预训练的多模态时间 Transformer,把整个就医历程压成一个「虚拟病人」向量;冻结向量 + 线性 Cox 头在 140 万人测试集的 261 个预后任务和 61 个检索任务上大幅超过年龄-性别基线。
<!-- EN --> APOLLO (Mahmood lab): a multimodal temporal Transformer pretrained with masked modelling on 33 years of records from 17 Mass General Brigham hospitals — **7.2M patients, 25.3B medical events, 28 modalities** (structured codes, labs, vitals, medications, note and report text, pathology/blood-smear images) — compressing a whole care journey into a "virtual patient" embedding. Frozen embeddings with a linear Cox head substantially beat an age–sex baseline across 261 prognostic and 61 retrieval tasks on a 1.4M-patient test set.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> EHR 是现代医学的记录系统,但数据被模态孤岛割裂,且维度极高、稀疏、不规则采样。已有纵向 EHR 基础模型大多**只用结构化数据**(诊断码、化验),因为有现成本体和离散 token 建模;而疾病进展的细节、医学推理、新型生物标志物常常只存在于自由文本和影像里。已有工作也多基于经过大量清洗的公开数据集(如 MIMIC-IV,仅 ICU)。问题:能否在一个**真实、未经精修、全科室**的医疗系统数据上,把结构化 + 文本 + 图像的完整纵向病历统一成一个时间感知的病人表示,并证明它在发病、进展、疗效、不良事件、医院运营等大量任务上通用?作者明确把这类比为「virtual cell」—— 多模态统一表示捕捉系统动态。
<!-- EN --> The EHR is medicine's system of record, but its data are fragmented across modality silos and are high-dimensional, sparse and irregularly sampled. Existing longitudinal EHR foundation models mostly use **structured data only** (codes, labs), thanks to ontologies and discrete-token modelling, whereas progression detail, clinical reasoning and novel biomarkers often live only in free text and images. Prior work also leans on heavily curated public datasets (e.g. MIMIC-IV, ICU only). The question: on **real, uncurated, all-department** health-system data, can the full longitudinal record — structured + text + images — be unified into one time-aware patient representation that generalizes across onset, progression, treatment response, adverse events and hospital operations? The authors explicitly draw the analogy to the "virtual cell": unified multimodal representations capturing system dynamics.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **Tokenization**:结构化数据 → 离散 token(每个 ICD 诊断、每个 RxNorm 成分为一个 token;数值测量为 (代码, 分位数 bin) token;分类测量为 (代码, 规范类别) token),共 **235,768 个**可学习嵌入的结构化 token;生命体征/flowsheet 名称用 Qwen-32B 与 LOINC 类别对齐。**非结构化**用冻结的现成编码器:所有临床文本用 **GatorTron-base**(超长文本分块后取平均);全切片病理图像用 **TITAN**;血涂片用 **DinoBloom**;电镜与大体图像用 **CONCHv1.5**;非病理影像只用其诊断报告文本。**架构**:各模态 embedding 经模态特定投影层映射到共享空间;前置人口学 token(就诊末年龄、性别、种族)和 CLS;每个事件加上该时刻的病人年龄作为时间编码;encoder-decoder 结构。**预训练**:**模态特定 mask 的掩码 token 建模**(mask token 提示输出头应重建哪种模态);每步随机取 1,536 个连续事件窗口;AdamW,30,000 迭代,8×A100。**推理**:在序列末尾追加最后时刻 + 某模态 mask token,其隐状态即病人嵌入;多数下游任务用「诊断 mask」作提示,检索时可换成文本或图像 mask。**下游**:冻结嵌入 → PCA → Cox 比例风险模型(time-to-event,含右删失),报告固定时长阈值下的时间依赖 AUROC;对照为**年龄+性别 Cox 模型**。**可解释性**:个体层面 leave-one-token-out(LOTO)敏感性分析;人群层面 Integrated Gradients(在 Transformer 之前的输入上计算)。
<!-- EN --> **Tokenization**: structured data → discrete tokens (each ICD diagnosis and RxNorm ingredient is a token; numeric measurements become (code, quantile-bin) tokens; categorical ones (code, canonical-category) tokens), **235,768** learnable structured tokens in total; vitals/flowsheet names are harmonized to LOINC classes with Qwen-32B. **Unstructured** data use frozen off-the-shelf encoders: all clinical text with **GatorTron-base** (long notes chunked and averaged); whole-slide images with **TITAN**; blood smears with **DinoBloom**; electron-microscopy and gross images with **CONCHv1.5**; non-pathology imaging via its report text only. **Architecture**: modality-specific projections into a shared space; prepended demographic tokens (age at last event, sex, ethnicity) and a CLS token; per-event patient age as the time encoding; encoder–decoder. **Pretraining**: **masked token modelling with modality-specific masks** (the mask token tells the head which modality to reconstruct); contiguous 1,536-event windows per step; AdamW, 30,000 iterations, 8×A100. **Inference**: append the last time step plus a modality mask token; its hidden state is the patient embedding — the diagnosis mask for most tasks, text or image masks for retrieval. **Downstream**: frozen embeddings → PCA → Cox proportional hazards (time-to-event with right-censoring), time-dependent AUROC at a fixed threshold duration; comparator is an **age+sex Cox model**. **Interpretability**: patient-level leave-one-token-out (LOTO) sensitivity; population-level Integrated Gradients on pre-Transformer inputs.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **MGB-7M**:Mass General Brigham 1992–2025 年,7,155,044 名病人、25,296,943,893 条事件、28 种模态、12 个主要专科;其中 flowsheet 229 亿条、化验 14.4 亿、生命体征 5.05 亿、病程记录 1.59 亿份、诊断报告 7,798 万份、图像 115.8 万张(含血液学与组织病理)。覆盖全部 14 个 ATC 用药大类和全部 ICD 章节。按 75:5:20 划分训练/验证/测试;**所有下游任务均来自 143 万人的测试集**。下游:95 个新发疾病任务(每任务 ≥6 万人,事件数 157–27,503)、78 个进展、59 个疗效、17 个不良事件、12 个医院运营任务、61 个检索队列。数据仅在机构审批 + MTA 下按个案共享。
<!-- EN --> **MGB-7M**: Mass General Brigham, 1992–2025; 7,155,044 patients, 25,296,943,893 events, 28 modalities, 12 major specialties — 22.9B flowsheet entries, 1.44B labs, 505M vitals, 159M progress notes, 78M diagnostic reports, 1.16M images (incl. haematology and histopathology). All 14 ATC chapters and all ICD chapters covered. 75:5:20 train/val/test split; **all downstream tasks drawn from the 1.43M-patient test set**. Downstream: 95 new-onset tasks (≥60k patients each, 157–27,503 events), 78 progression, 59 treatment-response, 17 adverse-event, 12 hospital-operations tasks, and 61 retrieval cohorts. Data shared case-by-case only, under institutional review and an MTA.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **新发疾病(95 任务)**:74/95 显著优于年龄-性别基线(p<0.05);1 年全因死亡 AUROC **0.92**;3 年心衰 0.77→**0.88**;1 年急性心梗 0.68→0.82;3 年 **2 型糖尿病 0.61→0.85**;1 年痛风 0.74→0.83;1 年精神分裂症 0.65→0.92。 <!-- EN --> **New onset (95 tasks)**: significantly better than age–sex on 74/95 (p<0.05); 1-year all-cause mortality AUROC **0.92**; 3-year heart failure 0.77→**0.88**; 1-year acute MI 0.68→0.82; 3-year **type 2 diabetes 0.61→0.85**; 1-year gout 0.74→0.83; 1-year schizophrenia 0.65→0.92.
- <!-- ZH --> **疾病进展(78 任务)**:53/78 显著;心血管类平均 0.65→0.75;**高血压→心衰 5 年 0.75→0.86**;卒中后 3 年生存 0.72→0.84;主动脉瓣狭窄后 3 年生存 0.72→0.83;哮喘急性加重 0.57→0.83;黑色素瘤 3 年生存 0.71→0.87。 <!-- EN --> **Progression (78 tasks)**: 53/78 significant; cardiovascular average 0.65→0.75; **hypertension→heart failure at 5 years 0.75→0.86**; 3-year survival after stroke 0.72→0.84; after aortic stenosis 0.72→0.83; asthma exacerbation 0.57→0.83; melanoma 3-year survival 0.71→0.87.
- <!-- ZH --> **疗效(59 任务)**:30/59 显著;内分泌系统用药平均 0.57→0.80;房颤用阿哌沙班后无卒中生存 0.69→0.81;氯沙坦/氨氯地平治疗高血压后无 MACE 生存 0.80/0.79;曲妥珠单抗 0.66→0.93。 <!-- EN --> **Treatment response (59 tasks)**: 30/59 significant; endocrine drugs average 0.57→0.80; stroke-free survival on apixaban for AF 0.69→0.81; MACE-free survival on losartan/amlodipine for hypertension 0.80/0.79; trastuzumab 0.66→0.93.
- <!-- ZH --> **不良事件(17 任务)**:12/17 显著;**NSAID 后急性肾损伤 0.80→0.91**;NSAID 后消化道出血 0.80→0.91;糖皮质激素后新发 2 型糖尿病 0.70→0.87。 <!-- EN --> **Adverse events (17 tasks)**: 12/17 significant; **AKI after NSAIDs 0.80→0.91**; GI bleeding after NSAIDs 0.80→0.91; treatment-emergent T2D after corticosteroids 0.70→0.87.
- <!-- ZH --> **医院运营(12 任务,balanced accuracy)**:9/12 显著;**住院透析依赖 0.65→0.97**;**急性肾衰 0.64→0.80**;脓毒症 0.66→0.79;30 天再入院 0.54→0.62。 <!-- EN --> **Hospital operations (12 tasks, balanced accuracy)**: 9/12 significant; **in-hospital dialysis dependence 0.65→0.97**; **acute kidney failure 0.64→0.80**; sepsis 0.66→0.79; 30-day readmission 0.54→0.62.
- <!-- ZH --> **消融(肿瘤进展任务集,正文为 28 个任务)**:APOLLO 平均 AUROC **0.735**;仅结构化数据 0.71(多模态 +0.025);同架构逐任务有监督训练 0.626;最近一份病程记录嵌入 0.615;年龄-性别 0.619 —— 预训练、多模态、时间上下文缺一不可。 <!-- EN --> **Ablation (neoplasm progression set, 28 tasks in the Results text)**: APOLLO mean AUROC **0.735**; structured-only 0.71 (multimodal +0.025); same architecture supervised per task 0.626; latest progress-note embedding 0.615; age–sex 0.619 — pretraining, multimodality and temporal context each matter.
- <!-- ZH --> **检索**:61 个队列 Acc@5 全部高于「最近病程记录嵌入」基线;可检索到缺少肾移植 ICD 码(Z94.0)但实为肾移植的病人;支持自然语言入组标准和外部 TCGA 病理切片作为查询。 <!-- EN --> **Retrieval**: Acc@5 above the latest-progress-note baseline on all 61 cohorts; retrieves a kidney-transplant patient lacking the Z94.0 code; supports natural-language inclusion criteria and an external TCGA slide as queries.
- <!-- ZH --> **可解释性**:一例 CKD 3 年风险在高血压、血脂异常、2 型糖尿病记录后上升,并在 eGFR 降至 50 出头时再跳升;IG 在心衰→急性肾衰任务上突出肥胖、术后状态与衰老相关代理特征;作者明确把归因视为关联而非因果。 <!-- EN --> **Interpretability**: one patient's 3-year CKD risk rises after hypertension, dyslipidaemia and T2D are documented and jumps again when eGFR falls to the low 50s; IG for heart failure→acute kidney failure highlights obesity, post-procedural states and ageing proxies; the authors treat attributions as associative, not causal.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 首个把**完整纵向病历(结构化 + 文本 + 图像,28 种模态)**统一成时间感知病人表示的医疗系统规模基础模型。 <!-- EN --> The first health-system-scale foundation model unifying the **full longitudinal record (structured + text + images, 28 modalities)** into a time-aware patient representation.
- <!-- ZH --> 冻结现成单模态编码器 + 只训练时间 Transformer:原始数据不进入最终模型,降低 PHI 泄漏风险,且计算可行。 <!-- EN --> Frozen off-the-shelf unimodal encoders + a trained temporal Transformer: raw data never enter the final model (lower PHI-leakage risk) and compute stays feasible.
- <!-- ZH --> **模态特定 mask 作提示**:同一模型可产出偏向诊断/文本/图像的病人嵌入,从而支持跨模态检索。 <!-- EN --> **Modality-specific masks as prompts**: one model yields diagnosis-, text- or image-tuned patient embeddings, enabling cross-modal retrieval.
- <!-- ZH --> 322 个任务的大规模评测框架(全部 time-to-event + 校准)。 <!-- EN --> A 322-task evaluation framework (all time-to-event, with calibration).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **仅单一医疗系统**(MGB,共享 Epic 平台,美国东北部):只是系统内泛化,无外部验证 —— 与 CancerRiskNet 的跨系统跌幅相比,这是最大的未知。 <!-- EN --> **Single health system** (MGB, shared Epic instance, US northeast): within-system generalization only, no external validation — given CancerRiskNet's cross-system drop, this is the biggest unknown.
- <!-- ZH --> **对照只有年龄-性别**:没有与 ASCVD、CHA₂DS₂-VASc、Framingham 等临床评分或其他 EHR 基础模型正面比较。 <!-- EN --> **Only an age–sex comparator**: no head-to-head against ASCVD, CHA₂DS₂-VASc, Framingham or other EHR foundation models.
- <!-- ZH --> 观察性数据,预测为关联;「疗效」任务是在同一用药队列内做预后分层,**不能回答哪种药对该病人最好**(作者明确指出反事实任务尚待解决)。 <!-- EN --> Observational, associational predictions; "treatment response" is prognostic stratification within a treated cohort and **cannot say which drug is best for a patient** (the authors flag the counterfactual task as open).
- <!-- ZH --> 冻结编码器、长文本简单平均;无可穿戴/生活方式数据。 <!-- EN --> Frozen encoders and naive averaging of long notes; no wearables/lifestyle.
- <!-- ZH --> 预印本;代码「发表后」才开放,数据不公开。 <!-- EN --> Preprint; code only "upon publication", data not public.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> **对 Sinai EHR 项目,APOLLO 是「如果把 Sinai 全部模态都用上会怎样」的上限参照**:规模(720 万人)与 Sinai 的「数百万病人」同量级,同样是美国学术医疗系统的真实、未精修数据,所以它的工程选择几乎可以逐条对照 —— 化验数值的分位数 token 化、用 LLM 对齐本地 flowsheet 命名到 LOINC、冻结文本/病理编码器、模态 mask 作提示、全部用 Cox time-to-event 评估。相比之下 Delphi/CancerRiskNet 只用诊断码:三者构成「仅诊断码 → 多模态」的梯度,Sinai 项目可以沿这条梯度逐步加模态并量化每步收益(APOLLO 的消融显示多模态比纯结构化只多 +0.025 AUROC,而预训练 vs 有监督差 0.109 —— **预训练规模比模态数量更重要**,这是 Sinai 早期优先级的直接依据)。**与心-代谢-肾方向高度相关**:它报告的最强结果里有大量肾/心代谢终点 —— 透析依赖(0.97 balanced acc.)、急性肾衰、NSAID 后 AKI、高血压→心衰、2 型糖尿病新发、心衰→急性肾衰、CKD 风险轨迹示例 —— 这正是实验室 Novo 资助的 cardiometabolic-kidney 基础模型/agent 要覆盖的终点集,可直接作为 Sinai 上的任务清单与目标数值。**与本方向(detect → model → revert)的联系**:作者自己把它类比为 virtual cell,并称其为「virtual patient」—— 这是病人尺度的 "model";它也用 TITAN/CONCH 嵌入病理切片,和 Plan A 的 H&E 侧编码器是同一族(Mahmood 组)。**它没做的正是 revert**:作者明确说疗效预测不是反事实,不能回答「换哪个药」—— 组织尺度的扰动/逆转建模与 EHR 尺度的虚拟病人之间,缺的就是这层因果接口。对 Plan B:LOTO + IG 的「可审计」解释,以及作者把归因标注为「关联/护理过程代理/假设生成」三类的做法,可直接转化为 agent 在 EHR 上作结论时的证据分级。
<!-- EN --> **For the Sinai EHR project APOLLO is the upper-bound reference for "what if we used every Sinai modality"**: its scale (7.2M patients) matches Sinai's millions, on equally real, uncurated US academic health-system data, so its engineering choices map almost one-to-one — quantile-bin tokens for lab values, LLM harmonization of local flowsheet names to LOINC, frozen text/pathology encoders, modality masks as prompts, Cox time-to-event evaluation throughout. Delphi and CancerRiskNet use diagnosis codes only; together the three form a "codes-only → multimodal" gradient that the Sinai project can climb step by step, quantifying each step's gain (APOLLO's ablation shows multimodality adds only +0.025 AUROC over structured-only, while pretraining vs supervised differs by 0.109 — **pretraining scale matters more than modality count**, a direct input to early Sinai priorities). **Highly relevant to cardiometabolic-kidney disease**: many of its strongest results are kidney or cardiometabolic endpoints — dialysis dependence (0.97 balanced accuracy), acute kidney failure, AKI after NSAIDs, hypertension→heart failure, new-onset T2D, heart failure→acute kidney failure, and a CKD risk-trajectory example — precisely the endpoint set the lab's Novo-funded cardiometabolic-kidney foundation models/agents should cover, usable as a Sinai task list with target numbers. **On detect → model → revert**: the authors themselves invoke the virtual cell and call this a "virtual patient" — patient-scale "model"; it also embeds slides with TITAN/CONCH, the same encoder family (Mahmood lab) as Plan A's H&E side. **What it does not do is revert**: the authors state treatment-response prediction is not counterfactual and cannot say which drug to switch to — the missing causal interface between tissue-scale perturbation/reversion modelling and the EHR-scale virtual patient. For Plan B: the auditable LOTO + IG explanations, and the authors' sorting of attributions into "association / care-process proxy / hypothesis-generating", translate directly into an evidence-grading scheme for agents drawing conclusions from EHR.
