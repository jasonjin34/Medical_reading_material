## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Delphi-2M 把 GPT-2 改造成**连续时间、多病种竞争风险的生成模型**:输入一个人的 ICD-10 首诊序列(1,256 种疾病 + 死亡)及性别/BMI/吸烟/饮酒,同时输出下一事件是什么以及何时发生;在 40 万 UK Biobank 参与者上训练、仅 220 万参数,对 >1,000 种疾病的平均年龄分层 AUC 约 0.76,不改参数迁移到 193 万丹麦人上仍有 0.67,还能采样未来 20 年的合成健康轨迹。
<!-- EN --> Delphi-2M turns GPT-2 into a **continuous-time, competing-risk generative model of multi-morbidity**: given a person's sequence of first ICD-10 diagnoses (1,256 diseases + death) plus sex/BMI/smoking/alcohol, it predicts both what the next event is and when it happens. Trained on 0.4M UK Biobank participants with only 2.2M parameters, it reaches an average age-stratified AUC of ~0.76 across >1,000 diseases, 0.67 on 1.93M Danish individuals with no parameter changes, and can sample synthetic health trajectories up to 20 years ahead.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 疾病进展是多病共存、随时间演化的,但现有风险模型多是单病种(心血管、某种癌症),极少有算法能同时预测 ICD-10 顶层 >1,000 种诊断。多病共病建模的难点:诊断种类多、事件间的时间依赖、异质数据整合、预测的统计校准。LLM 把语言建模为 token 序列并逐个生成 —— 疾病史同样是「过去事件 → 预测未来序列」,那么 GPT 式模型能否学会人类疾病的「自然史」,并做到可校准、可生成、可外部验证?
<!-- EN --> Disease progression is multimorbid and time-evolving, yet most risk models are single-disease (cardiovascular, one cancer); few algorithms predict the full spectrum of >1,000 top-level ICD-10 diagnoses. Hard parts of multimorbidity modelling: many diagnoses, temporal dependencies between events, heterogeneous data integration, and calibration. LLMs model language as token sequences generated one by one — disease histories are likewise "past events → future sequence". Can a GPT-style model learn the natural history of human disease while being calibrated, generative and externally valid?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> 基于 nanoGPT 的 GPT-2,三处关键改动:**(1) 连续年龄编码** 替代离散位置编码(sin/cos 基函数,最低频率 1/365,再经可训练线性组合)—— 支持任意长度输入;**(2) 第二个输出头预测到下一事件的时间**,采用**竞争指数等待时间模型**:每个 token 的速率 λᵢ = exp(logitᵢ),下一事件为 j 的概率 = λⱼ/Σλᵢ(恰好等于 softmax 交叉熵),到下一事件的时间服从速率 Σλᵢ 的指数分布,于是 logit 可直接解释为**发病率(每日)**;**(3) 因果注意力额外屏蔽同一时刻记录的 token**。另外:**"no event" 填充 token** 以平均每 5 年 1 个的速率随机插入,消除长时间无记录的空窗(也让推理时可在任意时刻查询风险);性别、BMI(<22/22–28/>28)、吸烟、饮酒作为输入 token 但不进损失;生活方式 token 的时间在入组点 −20 到 +40 年间随机化以打破 immortal-time 偏倚造成的虚假关联。超参扫描复现 scaling law,UK Biobank 规模下最优约 200 万参数;选用 120 维、12 层、12 头、**220 万参数**。权重绑定(输入嵌入 = 输出投影)便于解释。训练:Adam,20 万迭代,batch 128,cosine 学习率 6e-4→6e-5。**采样**:从 softmax 采下一疾病,从各指数分布采时间取最小,迭代得到完整轨迹。**可解释性**:嵌入 UMAP、SHAP、注意力图。
<!-- EN --> GPT-2 via nanoGPT with three key changes: **(1) continuous age encoding** replacing discrete positional encoding (sin/cos bases, lowest frequency 1/365, followed by a trainable linear combination) — allowing arbitrary-length input; **(2) a second output head for time-to-next-event** under a **competing-exponential waiting-time model**: each token has rate λᵢ = exp(logitᵢ); P(next = j) = λⱼ/Σλᵢ (exactly softmax cross-entropy), and time to next event is exponential with rate Σλᵢ, so logits are interpretable as **daily incidence rates**; **(3) causal attention additionally masks tokens recorded at the same time**. Also: **"no event" padding tokens** inserted at ~1 per 5 years remove long empty gaps (and allow querying risk at any time at inference); sex, BMI (<22/22–28/>28), smoking and alcohol are input tokens excluded from the loss; lifestyle token times are randomized −20 to +40 years around enrolment to break spurious associations from immortal-time bias. A hyperparameter sweep reproduces scaling laws, with ~2M parameters optimal at UK Biobank scale; the chosen model has 120-d embeddings, 12 layers, 12 heads, **2.2M parameters**. Weight tying (input embedding = output projection) aids interpretation. Training: Adam, 200k iterations, batch 128, cosine LR 6e-4→6e-5. **Sampling**: draw the next disease from the softmax and the time as the minimum over exponentials, iterating to full trajectories. **Interpretability**: embedding UMAP, SHAP, attention maps.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **UK Biobank**:约 50 万人(2006–2010 年入组,37–73 岁);疾病来自 "first occurrence"(类别 1712,整合初级保健、住院、死亡登记、自报)+ 癌症登记(自行补入),ICD-10 level-3,共 1,256 种诊断。训练 402,799 人(80%),验证 100,639 人(20%),纵向测试 471,057 名 2020-07-01 仍在世者(2021-07 至 2022-07 的发病,留 1 年空窗)。**丹麦外部验证**:国家患者登记(LPR)+ 死因登记,2016-01-01 时 50–80 岁的 **193 万人**,1978–2016 年 1,151 万个疾病 token,在 2017 年发病上评估(796 种 ICD-10 码,每种 ≥25 例);ICD-8 映射到 ICD-10,无生活方式数据(视为缺失)。
<!-- EN --> **UK Biobank**: ~500k participants (recruited 2006–2010, aged 37–73); diseases from "first occurrence" data (category 1712, integrating primary care, hospital admissions, death registry, self-report) plus cancer registry (added by the authors), ICD-10 level 3, 1,256 diagnoses in total. Training 402,799 (80%), validation 100,639 (20%), longitudinal test on 471,057 alive on 1 July 2020 (incidence July 2021–July 2022, with a 1-year gap). **Danish external validation**: National Patient Registry (LPR) + cause-of-death registry; **1.93M** people aged 50–80 on 1 January 2016, 11.51M disease tokens from 1978–2016, evaluated on 2017 incidence (796 ICD-10 codes with ≥25 cases); ICD-8 mapped to ICD-10; no lifestyle data (treated as absent).

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **全病谱预测**:内部验证下一事件平均年龄分层 AUC **≈0.76**;97% 的诊断 AUC > 0.5;死亡 AUC **0.97**;5 年年龄段校准良好。 <!-- EN --> **Full-spectrum prediction**: average age-stratified next-event AUC **≈0.76** on internal validation; AUC > 0.5 for 97% of diagnoses; death AUC **0.97**; well calibrated in 5-year age bins.
- <!-- ZH --> **远期预测**:10 年后平均 AUC 从 0.76 降到 **0.70**,仍有远期预后价值。 <!-- EN --> **Long horizon**: average AUC declines from 0.76 to **0.70** at 10 years, retaining long-term prognostic value.
- <!-- ZH --> **对比专用评分**:心血管病与痴呆接近常规临床评分(QRisk3、SCORE2、PREVENT、Framingham、UKBDRS),死亡优于 Charlson/Elixhauser;**糖尿病不如单一 HbA1c**;多数病种 AUC 高于基于 67 个血液标志物的 MILTON(可比 410 种病)。 <!-- EN --> **Versus dedicated scores**: comparable to clinical scores for CVD and dementia (QRisk3, SCORE2, PREVENT, Framingham, UKBDRS), better than Charlson/Elixhauser for death; **worse than HbA1c alone for diabetes**; generally higher AUC than MILTON (67 blood biomarkers; 410 diseases comparable).
- <!-- ZH --> **生成轨迹**:以 60 岁前病史为条件采样 63,662 条轨迹,70–75 岁时的人群发病率被很好重现;第 1 年约 17% 疾病 token 预测正确,20 年后 <14%(仅用年龄性别为 12–13%)。 <!-- EN --> **Generated trajectories**: conditioning on history up to age 60, 63,662 sampled trajectories recapitulate population incidence at ages 70–75; ~17% of disease tokens correct in year 1, <14% after 20 years (vs 12–13% from age and sex alone).
- <!-- ZH --> **纯合成数据训练**:只在合成轨迹上训练的模型在真实验证集上 AUC **0.74**,仅比原模型低 3 个百分点 —— 合成数据可作为隐私更低敏感的替代。 <!-- EN --> **Training on synthetic data only**: a model trained exclusively on synthetic trajectories reaches AUC **0.74** on real validation data, only 3 points below the original — a less privacy-sensitive alternative.
- <!-- ZH --> **外部验证**:不改参数用于丹麦,平均 AUC **0.67**(s.d. 0.09)vs UKB 纵向测试 0.69;两数据集各病种性能相关 r = 0.76(95% CI 0.72–0.80)。 <!-- EN --> **External validation**: applied to Denmark with no changes, average AUC **0.67** (s.d. 0.09) vs 0.69 for UKB longitudinal testing; per-disease performance correlates at r = 0.76 (95% CI 0.72–0.80).
- <!-- ZH --> **可解释性**:嵌入按 ICD 章节自发聚类(模型不知道章节);两型糖尿病与视网膜病、神经病变聚在一起;心梗、败血症与死亡聚在一起。SHAP 示例:一系列消化系统诊断使某人胰腺癌风险升高 19 倍。癌症对死亡率的影响半衰期数年,败血症则迅速回落。 <!-- EN --> **Interpretability**: embeddings cluster by ICD chapter without being told; both diabetes types cluster with retinal disorders and neuropathies; MI and septicaemia cluster with death. SHAP example: a run of digestive diagnoses raised one person's pancreatic cancer risk 19-fold. Cancer's effect on mortality decays with a multi-year half-life, septicaemia's much faster.
- <!-- ZH --> **偏倚**:模型学到了数据采集过程 —— 仅在住院记录出现的疾病,在已有其他住院记录者中预测速率平均高 10 倍(败血症 93% 在医院确诊,预测速率高 8 倍);UKB 的健康志愿者偏倚、入组前无死亡(immortality bias)、80 岁以上数据稀少都被模型继承。 <!-- EN --> **Biases**: the model learns the data-collection process — hospital-only diseases are predicted at ~10× higher rates in people with any other hospital record (septicaemia, 93% hospital-diagnosed, at 8×); UKB's healthy-volunteer bias, absence of pre-recruitment deaths (immortality bias) and sparse data beyond age 80 are all inherited.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **竞争指数等待时间头**:让 GPT 的 softmax 同时成为疾病发生率模型,输出可解释的绝对速率而非仅相对概率。 <!-- EN --> **Competing-exponential waiting-time head**: makes GPT's softmax double as an incidence model, yielding interpretable absolute rates rather than relative probabilities only.
- <!-- ZH --> 连续年龄编码 + 同时刻屏蔽 + no-event 填充:把「文本 GPT」改造为「连续时间事件 GPT」的一组小而必要的改动(有消融)。 <!-- EN --> Continuous age encoding + same-time masking + no-event padding: a small, ablated set of changes turning a text GPT into a continuous-time event GPT.
- <!-- ZH --> 单模型同时覆盖 >1,000 种疾病,并在另一国家数据上**零调参外部验证**。 <!-- EN --> One model covering >1,000 diseases, **externally validated with zero tuning** in another country.
- <!-- ZH --> **生成式**:采样 20 年未来轨迹做人群疾病负担预测;用合成数据训练下游模型。 <!-- EN --> **Generative**: sampling 20-year futures for population disease-burden projection; training downstream models on synthetic data.
- <!-- ZH --> 坦率的偏倚分析:展示模型如何「学到」数据来源缺失模式。 <!-- EN --> A candid bias analysis showing how the model "learns" data-source missingness patterns.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 只有首次诊断 + 少量生活方式;无化验、用药、影像、文本(作者列为扩展方向;糖尿病上明显不如 HbA1c)。 <!-- EN --> First diagnoses plus a few lifestyle factors only; no labs, medications, imaging or text (listed as extensions; clearly inferior to HbA1c for diabetes).
- <!-- ZH --> UKB 偏倚:健康志愿者、白人比例高、较富裕;入组前无死亡;80 岁以上数据不足。 <!-- EN --> UKB biases: healthy volunteers, over-representation of white and affluent participants; no pre-recruitment deaths; sparse data beyond age 80.
- <!-- ZH --> 模型利用了数据来源缺失模式(住院记录有无),部分关联是数据采集的伪迹。 <!-- EN --> The model exploits data-source missingness (presence of hospital records); some associations are collection artefacts.
- <!-- ZH --> 作者明确告诫:时间方向上的依赖**不可作为因果**来干预未来健康。 <!-- EN --> The authors explicitly caution that temporally directed dependencies **must not be read as causal** levers for changing future health.
- <!-- ZH --> 个体层面预测受随机性主导,与一系列结局都兼容;临床决策支持还需监管框架。 <!-- EN --> Individual predictions are dominated by chance and compatible with many outcomes; clinical decision support still needs a regulatory framework.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> **对 Sinai EHR 项目,这是「病人轨迹基础模型」的最清晰范式**:一个小模型(220 万参数)在 40 万人上就能学到 >1,000 种疾病的竞争风险,而 Sinai 有数百万病人 —— 按本文复现的 scaling law,Sinai 规模下最优模型应明显更大,这本身就是一个可检验的问题。可直接借鉴的设计:(1) **竞争指数头**给出可校准的绝对发生率,适合把 CKD 分期进展、透析、心衰、心梗、死亡作为**相互竞争**的终点同时建模 —— 正是心-代谢-肾(cardiometabolic-kidney)共病的本质;(2) no-event 填充让模型可以在任意时刻查询风险,适合做「风险随时间的轨迹」;(3) 外部验证规范(零调参迁移 + 各病种性能相关性)。**明确的空白也正是 Sinai 的机会**:Delphi 在糖尿病上输给 HbA1c,说明对代谢-肾病终点,**化验值(HbA1c、eGFR、UACR)和用药(SGLT2i、GLP-1RA、RAS 抑制剂)是必须加入的 token** —— Sinai EHR 恰好有这些而 UKB first-occurrence 数据没有。对实验室 Novo 资助的心-代谢-肾智能体:一个 Delphi 式模型可以作为 agent 调用的**数值预测工具**(作者在讨论中也提出 LLM 调用 Delphi 来提升数值准确性),这与 Plan B 的「agent 结论必须挂在可验证的计算证据上」一致。**偏倚部分对 Plan A 的思路是同构的**:模型学会了「数据来源缺失模式」而非疾病 —— 这与组学里把批次/伪迹当成疾病信号是同一类问题;Plan A 用跨模态一致性区分伪迹与疾病,EHR 上的对应做法是对数据来源/就诊强度做显式建模或分层检验。与 detect → model → revert:Delphi 是病人尺度的「model」(可生成、可采样的虚拟病人),但作者明确说不可做因果干预 —— 要走到 revert,需要因果/反事实层或与组织层 virtual tissue 的连接。
<!-- EN --> **For the Sinai EHR project this is the clearest paradigm of a patient-trajectory foundation model**: a small model (2.2M parameters) learns competing risks for >1,000 diseases from 0.4M people, while Sinai has millions of patients — by the scaling law reproduced here the optimal model at Sinai scale should be noticeably larger, itself a testable question. Directly borrowable design: (1) the **competing-exponential head** yields calibrated absolute incidences, ideal for modelling CKD stage progression, dialysis, heart failure, MI and death as **competing** endpoints simultaneously — the essence of cardiometabolic-kidney multimorbidity; (2) no-event padding enables risk queries at any time, i.e. risk trajectories; (3) its external-validation discipline (zero-tuning transfer plus per-disease performance correlation). **The explicit gap is Sinai's opportunity**: Delphi loses to HbA1c for diabetes, so for metabolic-kidney endpoints **lab values (HbA1c, eGFR, UACR) and medications (SGLT2i, GLP-1RA, RAS inhibitors) must become tokens** — Sinai's EHR has them, UKB first-occurrence data does not. For the lab's Novo-funded cardiometabolic-kidney agents, a Delphi-style model can be a **numerical prediction tool the agent calls** (the authors themselves propose LLMs invoking Delphi for numerical accuracy), consistent with Plan B's requirement that agent conclusions rest on verifiable computational evidence. **The bias analysis is isomorphic to Plan A's concern**: the model learns data-source missingness rather than disease — the same failure as treating batch/artefact as disease signal in omics; Plan A separates artefacts from disease via cross-modal agreement, and the EHR analogue is explicitly modelling or stratifying by data source / care intensity. On detect → model → revert: Delphi is patient-scale "model" (a generative, sampleable virtual patient), but the authors explicitly rule out causal intervention — reaching "revert" needs a causal/counterfactual layer or a link to tissue-scale virtual tissue.
