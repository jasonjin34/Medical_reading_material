## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Delphi-2M 把 GPT-2 改成**连续时间、竞争风险的疾病生成模型**:输入一个人按首诊年龄排列的 ICD-10 诊断(1,256 种疾病)以及性别、BMI、吸烟、饮酒,输出下一事件是哪种病(或死亡)**以及多久之后发生**;在 40 万 UK Biobank 参与者上训练、仅 220 万参数,内部验证中 >1,000 种疾病的平均年龄-性别分层 AUC 约 0.76,不改参数用于 193 万丹麦人平均 AUC 0.67,并能采样最长 20 年的合成未来健康轨迹(Abstract;Fig. 2b;Fig. 5a)。
<!-- EN --> Delphi-2M turns GPT-2 into a **continuous-time, competing-risk generative model of disease**: given a person's ICD-10 diagnoses ordered by age at first diagnosis (1,256 diseases) plus sex, BMI, smoking and alcohol, it predicts which disease (or death) comes next **and how long until it happens**. Trained on 0.4M UK Biobank participants with only 2.2M parameters, it reaches an average age–sex-stratified AUC of ~0.76 across >1,000 diseases in internal validation, 0.67 on 1.93M Danish individuals with no parameter changes, and can sample synthetic future health trajectories up to 20 years ahead (Abstract; Fig. 2b; Fig. 5a).

## 要点 / Takeaways
- <!-- ZH --> **一个 softmax 同时是疾病发生率模型**:把每个 logit 解释为对数速率 λ = exp(logit),「下一事件是 j」的概率就是 softmax,「到下一事件的时间」服从速率 Σλ 的指数分布;于是模型输出的是可校准的**绝对发病率(每天)**(Methods §"Exponential waiting time model")。 <!-- EN --> **One softmax doubles as an incidence model**: each logit is a log-rate λ = exp(logit); P(next = j) is the softmax and time-to-next-event is exponential with rate Σλ, so the model outputs calibratable **absolute incidence rates (per day)** (Methods §"Exponential waiting time model").
- <!-- ZH --> **单模型覆盖全病谱**:内部验证平均 AUC ≈0.76,97% 的诊断 AUC > 0.5,死亡 0.97;在心血管病、痴呆上与常规临床评分相当,糖尿病上**不如单一 HbA1c**(Fig. 2b,f;Results)。 <!-- EN --> **One model for the whole disease spectrum**: internal-validation average AUC ≈0.76, AUC > 0.5 for 97% of diagnoses, 0.97 for death; comparable to clinical scores for CVD and dementia, but **worse than HbA1c alone for diabetes** (Fig. 2b,f; Results).
- <!-- ZH --> **零调参外部验证**:直接用于丹麦登记数据,平均 AUC 0.67(UKB 纵向测试 0.69),两数据集各病种 AUC 相关 r = 0.76(Fig. 5a)。 <!-- EN --> **External validation with zero tuning**: applied directly to Danish registries, average AUC 0.67 (vs 0.69 for UKB longitudinal testing), per-disease AUC correlation r = 0.76 (Fig. 5a).
- <!-- ZH --> **能生成**:从 60 岁前病史采样未来轨迹,可重现 70–75 岁的人群发病率;只用合成数据训练的模型在真实数据上 AUC 0.74,仅低 3 个百分点(Fig. 3b,f)。 <!-- EN --> **Generative**: sampling futures from history up to age 60 reproduces population incidence at ages 70–75; a model trained only on synthetic data reaches AUC 0.74 on real data, 3 points lower (Fig. 3b,f).
- <!-- ZH --> **模型学到了数据采集过程**:只在住院记录中出现的疾病,在已有其他住院记录的人中被预测的速率平均高 10 倍 —— 这是数据来源缺失造成的伪迹,不是生物学(Fig. 5e,f)。作者明确告诫不要把这些时间依赖当作因果(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> **The model learns the data-collection process**: hospital-only diseases are predicted at ~10× higher rates in people with other hospital records — an artefact of data-source missingness, not biology (Fig. 5e,f). The authors explicitly caution against reading these temporal dependencies as causal (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:对 Sinai 项目,这是「病人轨迹基础模型」的一个清晰、小而可复现的范式(代码公开);它在糖尿病上输给 HbA1c,说明代谢-肾病类终点需要把化验值等加入输入。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: for the Sinai project this is a clear, small and reproducible template for a patient-trajectory foundation model (code is public); losing to HbA1c on diabetes suggests that metabolic/kidney endpoints need lab values and similar data as inputs.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 人的疾病进展是多病共存、随年龄演化的,但现有预测算法大多针对单一疾病(心血管病、某种癌症),很少能预测 ICD-10 顶层 >1,000 种诊断的全病谱(Introduction)。多病共病建模的方法难点包括:诊断种类多、事件之间的时间依赖、整合多种预后数据、预测的统计校准。LLM 把语言建模为 token 序列逐个生成;疾病史同样是「根据过去事件预测未来序列」。此前已有 BERT 类模型和针对单一任务(胰腺癌、自伤、卒中)的 Transformer,但全面的**生成式**多病共病建模尚未被充分评估(Introduction)。
<!-- EN --> Human disease progression is multimorbid and evolves with age, yet most prediction algorithms target single diseases (cardiovascular disease, specific cancers), and few predict the full spectrum of >1,000 top-level ICD-10 diagnoses (Introduction). Methodological difficulties include the number of diagnoses, temporal dependencies among events, integrating diverse prognostic data, and statistical calibration. LLMs model language as token sequences generated one at a time; disease histories are likewise "predict the future sequence from past events". BERT-style models and Transformers for specific tasks (pancreatic cancer, self-harm, stroke) exist, but comprehensive **generative** multimorbidity modelling had not been fully assessed (Introduction).

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> 面向 CS 读者,按步骤写(出自 Results §"A transformer model for health records"、Methods、Fig. 1):
<!-- EN --> Step by step for a CS reader (from Results §"A transformer model for health records", Methods and Fig. 1):

- <!-- ZH --> **第 1 步:序列表示**。每个数据点是 (token, 年龄[天])。token 包括:ICD-10 三位码疾病(首诊年龄)和死亡;性别(出生时给出);BMI(<22 / 22–28 / >28 kg/m²)、吸烟、饮酒各 3 级(入组时记录);"no event" 填充 token。正文说共 1,258 个状态(疾病 + 死亡 + no event),Methods 列出 1,257 个疾病 token + 9 个生活方式 + 2 个性别 + 1 个 no-event + 1 个非信息填充。 <!-- EN --> **Step 1: sequence representation**. Each datapoint is (token, age in days). Tokens: ICD-10 three-character diseases (at age of first diagnosis) and death; sex (given at birth); BMI (<22 / 22–28 / >28 kg/m²), smoking and alcohol at 3 levels each (recorded at enrolment); "no event" padding tokens. The text counts 1,258 states (diseases + death + no event); Methods lists 1,257 disease tokens + 9 lifestyle + 2 sex + 1 no-event + 1 non-informative padding token.
- <!-- ZH --> **第 2 步:no-event 填充**。按平均每 5 年 1 个的速率随机插入,消除年轻时长段无记录的空窗;推理时也可在任意时刻插入以查询该时刻的风险。另外,生活方式 token 的时间在入组点 −20 到 +40 年间随机化,因为入组时刻恰好是 immortal-time bias(入组者必须活着)的结束点,不随机化会造成死亡率在该时刻跳变的虚假关联(Methods)。 <!-- EN --> **Step 2: no-event padding**. Inserted at an average rate of 1 per 5 years to remove long empty gaps at young ages; at inference they can be inserted anywhere to query risk at that time. Lifestyle token times are randomised by −20 to +40 years around enrolment, because enrolment coincides with the end of immortal-time bias (participants had to be alive), which would otherwise create a spurious jump in mortality tied to those tokens (Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **第 3 步:三处架构改动**(基于 nanoGPT 的 GPT-2):(a) 用**连续年龄编码**替代离散位置编码 —— 年龄的 sin/cos 函数(最低频率 1/365),再经可训练线性变换,所以输入长度不受限;(b) 增加对**到下一事件时间**的建模;(c) 因果注意力额外**屏蔽同一时刻记录的 token**(同时记录的事件没有顺序)。权重绑定:输入 embedding 与输出投影共享,便于解释(Fig. 1c;Methods)。 <!-- EN --> **Step 3: three architectural changes** (GPT-2 via nanoGPT): (a) **continuous age encoding** replaces discrete positional encoding — sin/cos functions of age (lowest frequency 1/365) followed by a trainable linear map, so input length is unrestricted; (b) modelling of **time to the next event**; (c) causal attention additionally **masks tokens recorded at the same time** (simultaneous events have no order). Weight tying: input embedding and output projection are shared, aiding interpretation (Fig. 1c; Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **第 4 步:竞争指数等待时间损失**。假设到每个 token 的等待时间 T<sub>i</sub> 独立服从速率 λ<sub>i</sub> = exp(logit<sub>i</sub>) 的指数分布,下一事件是最先发生的那个:P(j) = λ<sub>j</sub>/Σλ<sub>i</sub>(就是 softmax 交叉熵);等待时间 T* 服从速率 Σλ<sub>i</sub> 的指数分布,loss<sub>T</sub> = −(logsumexp(logits) − Σexp(logits)·T*)。总损失 = loss<sub>j</sub> + loss<sub>T</sub>;性别、生活方式和非信息填充 token 只作输入,logit 设为 −Inf,不进损失。速率恒定的假设只在短时间内合理,这也是插入 no-event 的原因(期望间隔不超过 5 年)(Methods)。 <!-- EN --> **Step 4: competing-exponential waiting-time loss**. Assume waiting times T<sub>i</sub> to each token are exponential with rate λ<sub>i</sub> = exp(logit<sub>i</sub>); the next event is the first to occur: P(j) = λ<sub>j</sub>/Σλ<sub>i</sub> (exactly softmax cross-entropy); the waiting time T* is exponential with rate Σλ<sub>i</sub>, loss<sub>T</sub> = −(logsumexp(logits) − Σexp(logits)·T*). Total loss = loss<sub>j</sub> + loss<sub>T</sub>; sex, lifestyle and non-informative padding tokens are input-only (logits set to −Inf). The constant-rate assumption only holds over short periods, which is why no-event tokens are inserted (expected gaps ≤5 years) (Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **第 5 步:规模与训练**。对 embedding 维度、层数、头数做系统扫描(Extended Data Fig. 2:486 个模型),复现 scaling law:UKB 规模下最优约 200 万参数;选用 120 维、12 层、12 头,共 **220 万参数**。Adam,20 万次迭代,batch 128,1,000 次 warmup 后 cosine 学习率 6×10<sup>−4</sup> → 6×10<sup>−5</sup>(Fig. 1e;Methods)。 <!-- EN --> **Step 5: scale and training**. A systematic screen over embedding size, layers and heads (Extended Data Fig. 2: 486 models) reproduces scaling laws: ~2M parameters is optimal at UKB scale; the chosen model has 120-d embeddings, 12 layers, 12 heads, **2.2M parameters**. Adam, 200k iterations, batch 128, 1,000 warm-up iterations then cosine LR 6×10<sup>−4</sup> → 6×10<sup>−5</sup> (Fig. 1e; Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **第 6 步:采样生成**。下一疾病从 softmax 采样;时间从各指数分布采样后取最小值;非疾病 token 的 logit 被丢弃;迭代得到整条轨迹(Methods §"Sampling procedure")。 <!-- EN --> **Step 6: sampling**. The next disease is drawn from the softmax; times are drawn from all exponentials and the minimum kept; non-disease logits are discarded; iterate to a full trajectory (Methods §"Sampling procedure").
- <!-- ZH --> **第 7 步:评估**。AUC 在 50–80 岁的 5 年年龄段内、按性别分别计算后平均(年龄-性别分层 AUC),DeLong 法给 CI;纵向测试在截止日后空 1 年再评估下一年的发病;基线为按年龄性别的 Nelson–Aalen 发病率,以及 QRisk3、SCORE2、PREVENT、Framingham、UKBDRS、Charlson、Elixhauser、AutoPrognosis v2、一个普通 Transformer 编码器和 Llama3.1-8B 提示基线(Methods)。 <!-- EN --> **Step 7: evaluation**. AUC is computed within 5-year age brackets (50–80) separately by sex and averaged (age–sex-stratified AUC), with DeLong CIs; longitudinal testing leaves a 1-year gap after the cut-off and evaluates incidence in the following year; baselines are age–sex Nelson–Aalen incidence plus QRisk3, SCORE2, PREVENT, Framingham, UKBDRS, Charlson, Elixhauser, AutoPrognosis v2, a plain Transformer encoder and a Llama3.1-8B prompting baseline (Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **第 8 步:解释**。token embedding 的 UMAP;SHAP(PartitionExplainer,被遮蔽的 token 换成 no-event)在 100,639 条验证轨迹上计算,并与带惩罚的时间依赖 Cox 模型的风险比对照;Nelson–Aalen 非参数风险比;注意力图(Methods §"Model interpretation")。 <!-- EN --> **Step 8: interpretation**. UMAP of token embeddings; SHAP (PartitionExplainer, masked tokens replaced by no-event) on 100,639 validation trajectories, compared with hazard ratios from a penalised time-dependent Cox model; Nelson–Aalen nonparametric hazard ratios; attention maps (Methods §"Model interpretation").

Diseases come from UKB "first occurrence" data (category 1712: linked primary care, hospital admissions, death registry, self-report; ICD-10 level 3; neoplasms not included), with cancer-registry data (fields 40005/40006) added by the authors, giving 1,256 diagnoses. ## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **全病谱下一事件预测**(内部验证):平均年龄-性别分层 AUC **≈0.76**;97% 的诊断 AUC > 0.5;死亡 AUC **0.97**(两性);5 年年龄段内预测速率与观察病例数吻合(Fig. 2b;Extended Data Fig. 3)。 <!-- EN --> **Full-spectrum next-event prediction** (internal validation): average age–sex-stratified AUC **≈0.76**; AUC > 0.5 for 97% of diagnoses; death AUC **0.97** (both sexes); predicted rates match observed counts in 5-year age brackets (Fig. 2b; Extended Data Fig. 3).
- <!-- ZH --> **远期**:预测与诊断间隔 10 年时平均 AUC 从 0.76 降到 **0.70**(Fig. 2e)。 <!-- EN --> **Long horizon**: with a 10-year gap between prediction and diagnosis, average AUC falls from 0.76 to **0.70** (Fig. 2e).
- <!-- ZH --> **与专用模型比较**(内部纵向测试集,Fig. 2f):死亡 —— Delphi 0.81,Transformer 0.78,Charlson 0.73,Elixhauser 0.71,Llama3.1 0.69;痴呆 —— Delphi 0.82,UKBDRS 0.81,Transformer 0.79,Llama3.1 0.67;心血管病 —— QRisk3 0.71,Delphi 0.70,PREVENT/SCORE2/Framingham/Transformer 0.69,AutoPrognosis 0.68,Llama3.1 0.63。糖尿病上不如单一 HbA1c(Results;Extended Data Fig. 4a)。注意:这里死亡的 0.81 与上一条的 0.97 是不同设置(纵向测试 vs 下一事件)。 <!-- EN --> **Versus dedicated models** (internal longitudinal test set, Fig. 2f): death — Delphi 0.81, Transformer 0.78, Charlson 0.73, Elixhauser 0.71, Llama3.1 0.69; dementia — Delphi 0.82, UKBDRS 0.81, Transformer 0.79, Llama3.1 0.67; CVD — QRisk3 0.71, Delphi 0.70, PREVENT/SCORE2/Framingham/Transformer 0.69, AutoPrognosis 0.68, Llama3.1 0.63. For diabetes it is worse than HbA1c alone (Results; Extended Data Fig. 4a). Note that death's 0.81 here and 0.97 above come from different settings (longitudinal test vs next-event).
- <!-- ZH --> **与 MILTON 比较**:对可匹配的 410 种疾病,Delphi-2M 的 AUC 总体高于基于 UKB 血液标志物的 MILTON(正文:67 个标志物)预后模型(Fig. 2g)。 <!-- EN --> **Versus MILTON**: for 410 matchable diseases, Delphi-2M's AUCs are generally higher than those of MILTON's prognostic models built on UKB blood biomarkers (text: 67 biomarkers) (Fig. 2g).
- <!-- ZH --> **生成轨迹**:以 60 岁前数据为条件,每人采样一条轨迹(正文 63,662 人;Fig. 3a 图注写 63,622),70–75 岁的人群发病率被很好重现;采样第 1 年平均约 17% 的疾病 token 预测正确,20 年后降到 <14%,只用年龄和性别为 12–13%(Fig. 3b,c)。吸烟、饮酒、BMI 高低组的疾病负担差异也被重现(Fig. 3d)。 <!-- EN --> **Generated trajectories**: conditioning on data up to age 60, one trajectory per person (text: 63,662; Fig. 3a caption: 63,622) reproduces population incidence at ages 70–75; ~17% of disease tokens are correct in the first year, falling to <14% after 20 years, vs 12–13% using age and sex alone (Fig. 3b,c). Differences in disease burden between high/low smoking, alcohol and BMI groups are also reproduced (Fig. 3d).
- <!-- ZH --> **纯合成数据训练**:用 40 万条从出生采样的合成轨迹训练同样的模型,在真实验证集上平均 AUC **0.74**,比原模型低 3 个百分点;生成轨迹与训练数据的相似度不高于验证集(Fig. 3f;Supplementary Fig. 6;Methods)。 <!-- EN --> **Training on synthetic data only**: the same model trained on 400,000 synthetic trajectories sampled from birth reaches average AUC **0.74** on real validation data, 3 points below the original; generated trajectories are no more similar to the training data than the validation cohort is (Fig. 3f; Supplementary Fig. 6; Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **外部验证**:不重训、不调整用于丹麦,平均 AUC **0.67**(s.d. 0.09),UKB 纵向测试为 0.69(s.d. 0.09);各病种在两数据集上的 AUC 相关 Pearson r = **0.76**(95% CI 0.72–0.80)(Fig. 5a)。 <!-- EN --> **External validation**: applied to Denmark with no retraining or adjustment, average AUC **0.67** (s.d. 0.09) vs 0.69 (s.d. 0.09) for UKB longitudinal testing; per-disease AUCs correlate across datasets with Pearson r = **0.76** (95% CI 0.72–0.80) (Fig. 5a).
- <!-- ZH --> **可解释性**:embedding 的 UMAP 按 ICD 章节聚类(模型不知道章节);两型糖尿病与视网膜病、神经病变聚在一起;心梗、败血症与死亡聚在一起(Fig. 4a)。SHAP 示例:一串消化系统诊断使某人胰腺癌风险升高 19 倍,随后的胰腺癌诊断使死亡率升高近万倍(正文 Results §"Explaining Delphi-2M predictions";但 Fig. 4b 图中标注的死亡相对速率为 9.3 × 10<sup>4</sup>,约 9.3 万倍,其中胰腺癌 token 单项贡献 ×168)。癌症对死亡率的影响以数年半衰期衰减,败血症则迅速回落(Fig. 4d)。 <!-- EN --> **Interpretability**: the embedding UMAP clusters by ICD chapter without the model being told chapters; both diabetes types cluster with retinal disorders and neuropathies; MI and septicaemia cluster with death (Fig. 4a). SHAP example: a series of digestive diagnoses raised one person's pancreatic cancer risk 19-fold, and the subsequent pancreatic cancer diagnosis raised mortality almost ten-thousandfold (main text, Results §"Explaining Delphi-2M predictions"; the Fig. 4b panel, however, labels the mortality relative rate 9.3 × 10<sup>4</sup>, about 93,000-fold, with the pancreatic cancer token alone contributing ×168). Cancer's effect on mortality decays with a half-life of several years; septicaemia's drops sharply (Fig. 4d).
- <!-- ZH --> **偏倚**:只在住院记录中出现的疾病,在有其他住院记录的人中预测速率平均高 **10 倍**;败血症 93% 在医院确诊,在有任何其他住院数据者中被预测高 **8 倍**(Fig. 5e,f)。UKB 入组前无死亡(immortality bias)影响死亡率估计;80 岁以上数据太少,不能可靠建模(Fig. 5b;Results)。 <!-- EN --> **Biases**: diseases exclusive to hospital records are predicted at on average **10×** higher rates in people with other hospital records; septicaemia, 93% diagnosed in hospital, is predicted **8×** higher in people with any other hospital data (Fig. 5e,f). No deaths before UKB recruitment (immortality bias) distorts mortality estimates; ages above 80 are too sparse to model reliably (Fig. 5b; Results).

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **竞争指数等待时间头**:让 GPT 的 softmax 同时成为发病率模型,给出绝对速率,从而能估计事件间隔(Fig. 1g)。 <!-- EN --> **Competing-exponential waiting-time head**: makes GPT's softmax double as an incidence model with absolute rates, enabling inter-event time estimates (Fig. 1g).
- <!-- ZH --> 连续年龄编码 + 同时刻屏蔽 + no-event 填充:把文本 GPT 改成连续时间事件模型的一组小改动,有消融实验(Fig. 1f;Supplementary Table 1)。 <!-- EN --> Continuous age encoding + same-time masking + no-event padding: a small set of changes turning a text GPT into a continuous-time event model, with ablations (Fig. 1f; Supplementary Table 1).
- <!-- ZH --> 一个模型同时覆盖 >1,000 种疾病,并在另一国家的登记数据上**零调参外部验证**。 <!-- EN --> One model covering >1,000 diseases, **externally validated with zero tuning** on another country's registries.
- <!-- ZH --> **生成式**:采样最长 20 年的未来轨迹估计疾病负担;用合成数据训练下游模型。 <!-- EN --> **Generative**: sampling futures up to 20 years for disease-burden estimates; training models on synthetic data.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 作者所述:UKB 中英国白人比例高于总人口,参与者平均更富裕、受教育程度更高;白人参与者报告的疾病率较低,诊断率随贫困程度上升,这些趋势 Delphi-2M 都重现了(Results §"External validation and bias assessment";图注内容见 Extended Data Fig. 10「Effects of ethnicity and deprivation」,正文此处引作 Extended Data Fig. 9)。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: UKB has more white British participants than the general population, and participants tend to be more affluent and educated; lower disease rates are reported among white participants and diagnosis rates rise with deprivation — trends Delphi-2M reproduces (Results §"External validation and bias assessment"; shown in Extended Data Fig. 10 "Effects of ethnicity and deprivation", although the text cites it as Extended Data Fig. 9).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者所述:UKB 的健康志愿者偏倚与入组前选择偏倚;数据来源缺失模式被模型利用;不同祖源和贫困指数亚组的预测速率不同(Discussion;Supplementary Figs. 10–11)。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: UKB healthy-volunteer and pre-recruitment selection biases; data-source missingness exploited by the model; different predicted rates across ancestry and deprivation subgroups (Discussion; Supplementary Figs. 10–11).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者所述:时间方向上的依赖**不应被解读为可用来改变未来健康的因果关系**;个体预测很大程度受随机性影响,与多种结局都相容(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: temporally directed dependencies **should not be interpreted as causal relationships that could be exploited to modify future health**; individual predictions are strongly influenced by chance and compatible with a range of outcomes (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者所述:输入只有首诊 + 少量生活方式;处方、血检、影像、基因组、可穿戴等是未来扩展方向;糖尿病等疾病仍离不开生物标志物(Results;Discussion)。临床决策支持还需要尚不成熟的监管框架(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: inputs are first diagnoses plus a few lifestyle factors; prescriptions, blood tests, imaging, genomics and wearables are future extensions; biomarkers remain indispensable for diseases such as diabetes (Results; Discussion). Clinical decision support still needs an immature regulatory framework (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:只用「首次」诊断,丢掉了复发、分期进展、就诊强度等信息 —— 对 CKD 分期这类反复测量、逐级加重的病,这一表示可能不够。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: using only *first* diagnoses discards recurrences, stage progression and care intensity — possibly insufficient for repeatedly measured, stepwise conditions such as CKD stages.
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:外部验证只覆盖 50–80 岁、只评估一年(2017)的发病,且丹麦缺生活方式输入;论文内有小的数字不一致(63,662 vs 63,622;MILTON 标志物 67 vs Extended Data Fig. 5 的 57;胰腺癌诊断后死亡率「近万倍」(正文)vs Fig. 4b 标注的 9.3 × 10<sup>4</sup>)。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: external validation covers ages 50–80 and one year of incidence (2017), without lifestyle inputs in Denmark; there are small internal inconsistencies (63,662 vs 63,622; 67 MILTON biomarkers vs 57 in Extended Data Fig. 5; mortality "almost ten thousandfold" after the pancreatic cancer diagnosis in the text vs 9.3 × 10<sup>4</sup> on the Fig. 4b panel).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> **首先是 Mount Sinai EHR 项目**。我们的理解:Delphi-2M 是一个小、可复现(代码公开)的「病人轨迹基础模型」模板,和胰腺癌轨迹论文(`ehr-pancreatic-trajectory-natmed-2023`,单病种判别模型)形成对照:后者回答「某个病多早能发现」,Delphi 回答「所有病的发生率随时间如何变化」。可借鉴的设计:(1) 竞争指数头输出绝对速率,适合把 CKD 进展、透析、心衰、心梗、死亡等**相互竞争**的终点放在一个模型里;(2) no-event 填充让风险可在任意时刻查询;(3) 外部验证规范:零调参迁移 + 各病种 AUC 相关性 + 校准曲线。论文复现了 scaling law(UKB 规模最优约 200 万参数),所以「在更大的单一医疗系统 EHR 上最优模型多大」本身是一个可检验的问题。Delphi 在糖尿病上输给 HbA1c,说明对代谢-肾病终点,化验值(如 HbA1c、eGFR)和用药很可能需要作为输入 —— 前提是 Sinai 数据中确实有这些字段(待确认)。偏倚部分对 Sinai 尤其重要:单中心 EHR 里病人可能在别处就医,「数据来源/就诊强度」造成的缺失模式会被模型学进去,需要分层检验。作者在讨论中也提出,未来面向医疗的 LLM 可以调用 Delphi 类模型来提高数值准确性。
<!-- EN --> **First, the Mount Sinai EHR project.** Our reading: Delphi-2M is a small, reproducible (public code) template for a patient-trajectory foundation model, complementing the pancreatic-trajectory paper (`ehr-pancreatic-trajectory-natmed-2023`, a single-disease discriminative model): that paper asks "how early can one disease be flagged", Delphi asks "how do the rates of all diseases evolve over time". Borrowable design: (1) the competing-exponential head gives absolute rates, suited to putting **competing** endpoints such as CKD progression, dialysis, heart failure, MI and death in one model; (2) no-event padding allows risk queries at any time; (3) the external-validation discipline: zero-tuning transfer + per-disease AUC correlation + calibration curves. The paper reproduces scaling laws (~2M parameters optimal at UKB scale), so "how large is the optimal model on a larger single-system EHR" is itself a testable question. Delphi losing to HbA1c on diabetes suggests that for metabolic/kidney endpoints, lab values (e.g. HbA1c, eGFR) and medications likely need to be inputs — provided the Sinai data actually contain these fields (to be confirmed). The bias analysis matters especially for Sinai: in a single-centre EHR, patients may receive care elsewhere, and missingness driven by data source / care intensity will be learned by the model, so stratified checks are needed. The authors also suggest in the Discussion that future healthcare LLMs could invoke Delphi-type models to improve numerical accuracy.

<!-- ZH --> 我们的理解(与 detect → model → revert 的关系,简述):Delphi 是病人尺度的「model」—— 可采样的虚拟病人;但作者明确说时间依赖不可作为因果干预依据,所以要走到「revert」还需要因果/反事实层或与组织层 virtual tissue 的连接。它「学到数据来源缺失而非疾病」的问题,与组学中把批次/伪迹当作疾病信号是同一类问题,和我们在肾脏组学异常检测中用多模态一致性区分伪迹的思路对应。
<!-- EN --> Our reading (relation to detect → model → revert, briefly): Delphi is patient-scale "model" — a sampleable virtual patient; but the authors explicitly rule out using its temporal dependencies for causal intervention, so reaching "revert" needs a causal/counterfactual layer or a link to tissue-scale virtual tissue. Its "learns data-source missingness rather than disease" problem is the same class of failure as treating batch/artefacts as disease signal in omics, mirroring our use of cross-modal agreement to separate artefacts in kidney-omics anomaly detection.
