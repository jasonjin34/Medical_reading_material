## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 在同一系统(健康成年小鼠肾,10x Chromium)里、三生物学重复地系统比较 37 °C vs 冰上冷蛋白酶解离、新鲜 vs 冻存 vs 甲醇固定、scRNA-seq vs 三种 snRNA-seq 核提取流程(加 bulk RNA-seq 对照),量化每种流程带来的表达伪影(应激基因)和细胞组成偏差;全部数据公开于 GSE141115。
<!-- EN --> A single-system (healthy adult mouse kidney, 10x Chromium), triplicate comparison of 37 °C vs on-ice cold-protease dissociation, fresh vs cryopreserved vs methanol-fixed cells, and scRNA-seq vs three snRNA-seq nuclei workflows (with bulk RNA-seq controls), quantifying protocol-induced expression artifacts (stress genes) and cell-composition biases; all data public as GSE141115.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 实体组织必须解离/保存/提核后才能上 10x;每一步都可能改变转录谱(如 37 °C 酶解诱导即刻早期基因)或改变细胞类型代表性。此前的评估分散在不同组织、不同条件下,且缺少与 bulk 的系统对照。本文在同一器官里把这些偏差并排量化,帮助实验设计与数据解读。
<!-- EN --> Solid tissue must be dissociated/preserved/nuclei-isolated before 10x profiling; each step can alter transcriptional profiles (e.g., 37 °C enzymatic digestion inducing immediate-early genes) or the representation of cell types. Prior assessments were scattered across tissues and conditions and lacked bulk comparisons. This paper quantifies these biases side by side in one organ to guide design and interpretation.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **实验臂(均 n=3 生物学重复,每只小鼠一个肾):**
<!-- EN --> **Experimental arms (all n=3 biological replicates, one kidney per mouse):**

- <!-- ZH --> **批次 1(C57BL/6J 雄性,19 周,10x v2):** 37 °C 解离(Miltenyi Multi Tissue Dissociation Kit 2,gentleMACS `37C_Multi_E`)vs 冷解离(冰上 *B. licheniformis* 冷活性蛋白酶,改自 Adam et al.);每种解离后的悬液分为 新鲜 / 冻存(50% FBS + 40% RPMI-1640 + 10% DMSO,梯度降温→液氮,存 6 周)/ 甲醇固定(80% MeOH,−20 °C→−80 °C,存 3 个月)三臂;另做两种解离悬液的 bulk RNA-seq。 <!-- EN --> **Batch 1 (C57BL/6J male, 19 wk, 10x v2):** 37 °C dissociation (Miltenyi Multi Tissue Dissociation Kit 2, gentleMACS `37C_Multi_E`) vs cold dissociation (on-ice *B. licheniformis* cold-active protease, adapted from Adam et al.); each suspension split into fresh / cryopreserved (50% FBS + 40% RPMI-1640 + 10% DMSO, gradient-cooled → liquid N2, 6 weeks) / methanol-fixed (80% MeOH, −20 °C → −80 °C, 3 months); plus bulk RNA-seq of both dissociated suspensions.
- <!-- ZH --> **批次 2(Balb/c 雄性,18 周,10x v2):** 冷解离 scRNA-seq vs SN_FANS_3x500g snRNA-seq(闪冻全肾),加全肾 bulk。 <!-- EN --> **Batch 2 (Balb/c male, 18 wk, 10x v2):** cold-dissociation scRNA-seq vs SN_FANS_3x500g snRNA-seq (flash-frozen whole kidney), plus whole-kidney bulk.
- <!-- ZH --> **批次 3(Balb/c 雌性,15 周,10x v3):** 冷解离 scRNA-seq 新鲜 vs 冻存重复实验(存 2 周,1200g 离心);三种 snRNA-seq:SN_FANS_3x500g(洗 3 次、500g、FANS 分选)、SN_FANS_1x2000g(洗 1 次、2000g、FANS)、SN_sucrose(500g 后蔗糖垫、无需分选);加全肾 bulk 与冷解离悬液 bulk。 <!-- EN --> **Batch 3 (Balb/c female, 15 wk, 10x v3):** repeat of fresh vs cryopreserved cold-dissociated scRNA-seq (2-week storage, 1200g spins); three snRNA-seq workflows: SN_FANS_3x500g (3 washes, 500g, FANS), SN_FANS_1x2000g (1 wash, 2000g, FANS), SN_sucrose (sucrose cushion, no sorting); plus whole-kidney and cold-dissociated-suspension bulk.

<!-- ZH --> **分析:** Cell Ranger 2.1.1(mm10;snRNA 用 pre-mRNA 参考计入内含子)→ EmptyDrops → 过滤(基因 ≥10 个细胞;每细胞 200–3000 基因、线粒体 <50%;细胞核另需 UMI ≥450 (v2)/≥900 (v3) 且去除线粒体基因;Scater 去 UMI/基因比离群)→ Seurat v2 CCA 整合、Wilcoxon 差异表达。细胞注释:scMatch 对三套公开小鼠肾参考 + 基因签名打分两轮细化,CNT 另行亚聚类(15 种细胞类型)。应激评分:从 warm 诱导基因中人工挑选 17 个文献已知应激基因,用 AddModuleScore 计算,1000 次随机基因集 Monte-Carlo 检验。组成差异用卡方检验;bulk 用 edgeR exact test 和 BSEQ-sc 反卷积。
<!-- EN --> **Analysis:** Cell Ranger 2.1.1 (mm10; pre-mRNA reference counting introns for snRNA) → EmptyDrops → filtering (genes in ≥10 cells; 200–3000 genes/cell, <50% mito; nuclei additionally ≥450 UMIs (v2) / ≥900 (v3) with mito genes removed; Scater UMI/gene-ratio outliers removed) → Seurat v2 CCA integration, Wilcoxon DE. Annotation: scMatch against three public mouse-kidney references + two rounds of signature-score refinement, CNT resolved by sub-clustering (15 cell types). Stress score: 17 literature stress genes hand-picked from the warm-induced list, AddModuleScore, 1000-random-gene-set Monte-Carlo test. Composition via chi-square; bulk via edgeR exact test and BSEQ-sc deconvolution.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **37 °C 解离诱导应激:** bulk 中 71 个基因在 warm 中更高、5 个在 cold 中更高(logFC>2,FDR<0.05);logFC>4 的包括 *Fosb, Fos, Jun, Junb, Atf3, Egr1, Hspa1a, Hspa1b*;GO 首位 "regulation of cell death"。scRNA 中 64 个基因至少在一个细胞类型中 warm 更高;*Junb/Jund* 在 7 种细胞类型中差异,*Jun/Fos* 在 5 种。 <!-- EN --> **37 °C dissociation induces stress:** bulk shows 71 genes up in warm and 5 up in cold (logFC>2, FDR<0.05); logFC>4 includes *Fosb, Fos, Jun, Junb, Atf3, Egr1, Hspa1a, Hspa1b*; top GO term "regulation of cell death". In scRNA, 64 genes up in warm in ≥1 cell type; *Junb/Jund* DE in 7 cell types, *Jun/Fos* in 5.
- <!-- ZH --> **17 基因应激签名:** *Fosb, Fos, Jun, Junb, Jund, Atf3, Egr1, Hspa1a, Hspa1b, Hsp90ab1, Hspa8, Hspb1, Ier3, Ier2, Btg1, Btg2, Dusp1*;"significantly high stress scores were detected only in warm-dissociated samples in eight out of 14 cell types",其中 "immune and endothelial cells are particularly sensitive"。 <!-- EN --> **17-gene stress signature:** *Fosb, Fos, Jun, Junb, Jund, Atf3, Egr1, Hspa1a, Hspa1b, Hsp90ab1, Hspa8, Hspb1, Ier3, Ier2, Btg1, Btg2, Dusp1*; "significantly high stress scores were detected only in warm-dissociated samples in eight out of 14 cell types", with "immune and endothelial cells … particularly sensitive".
- <!-- ZH --> **解离造成的组成偏差:** 8 个细胞群在 warm 中更少(含足细胞、系膜细胞、内皮细胞;卡方 p<0.001),且与高应激评分重合;足细胞 warm 仅 3 个(0.03%)vs cold 330 个(2.78%)。aLOH(4.99% vs 2.52%)和 PT(71.36% vs 63.34%)在 warm 中更多。cold 样本血红蛋白转录本更高(红细胞污染)。 <!-- EN --> **Dissociation composition bias:** 8 populations depleted in warm (incl. podocytes, mesangial, endothelial; chi-square p<0.001), overlapping the high-stress types; podocytes 3 cells (0.03%) warm vs 330 (2.78%) cold. aLOH (4.99% vs 2.52%) and PT (71.36% vs 63.34%) higher in warm. Cold samples have more hemoglobin transcripts (erythrocyte contamination).
- <!-- ZH --> **冻存丢失上皮:** PT 从新鲜 63.12%/70.86%(cold/warm)降到冻存 0.31%/0.57%;可用细胞约为新鲜的 ~30%;冻存还诱导应激基因(即刻早期基因、热休克蛋白)。重复实验(Balb/c 雌性、v3、2 周、1200g)PT 55.55% → 7.65%。 <!-- EN --> **Cryopreservation loses epithelium:** PT drops from 63.12%/70.86% fresh (cold/warm) to 0.31%/0.57% cryopreserved; usable cells ~30% of fresh; cryo also induces stress genes (IEGs, HSPs). Repeat (Balb/c female, v3, 2 weeks, 1200g): PT 55.55% → 7.65%.
- <!-- ZH --> **甲醇固定保组成但有 ambient RNA:** 组成接近新鲜,巨噬细胞中度下降(5.36%→3.2% cold;4.28%→2.54% warm);多种细胞类型中出现相同的管状上皮高表达转录本与血红蛋白基因,提示细胞损伤导致的环境 RNA 污染。 <!-- EN --> **Methanol keeps composition but leaks ambient RNA:** composition close to fresh, macrophages moderately reduced (5.36%→3.2% cold; 4.28%→2.54% warm); the same tubular and hemoglobin transcripts appear across most cell types, indicating damage-driven ambient RNA contamination.
- <!-- ZH --> **sc vs sn:** 三种核提取流程结果相近,SN_FANS_1x2000g 线粒体污染更高;snRNA 每核基因中位数 1,819 vs scRNA 981。免疫细胞 snRNA 平均 0.73% vs scRNA 6.03%(BSEQ-sc 估计 Balb/c 雌 1.51%、雄 4.84%);snRNA 只回收巨噬细胞,无 T(scRNA 1.38%)、B(0.77%)、NK(0.65%);足细胞 0.7% vs 3.28%;LoH 与系膜细胞在 snRNA 中更多。snRNA 中 lncRNA 更高,scRNA 中线粒体/核糖体基因更高。 <!-- EN --> **sc vs sn:** the three nuclei workflows were comparable, SN_FANS_1x2000g with more mito contamination; median genes 1,819 per nucleus vs 981 per cell. Immune cells 0.73% in snRNA vs 6.03% in scRNA (BSEQ-sc estimates 1.51% female, 4.84% male Balb/c); snRNA recovers only macrophages, no T (1.38% in scRNA), B (0.77%), NK (0.65%); podocytes 0.7% vs 3.28%; LoH and mesangial more abundant in snRNA. lncRNAs higher in snRNA; mito/ribosomal genes higher in scRNA.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 同一器官、同一实验室、三重复下并排比较 2 种解离 × 3 种保存 + 3 种核提取 + bulk,偏差可以直接对照。 <!-- EN --> Side-by-side, same-organ, same-lab, triplicate comparison of 2 dissociations × 3 storage states + 3 nuclei workflows + bulk, so biases are directly comparable.
- <!-- ZH --> 给出一个可复用的 17 基因解离应激签名,并显示应激敏感与细胞类型丢失相耦合。 <!-- EN --> A reusable 17-gene dissociation-stress signature, and evidence that stress sensitivity couples with cell-type loss.
- <!-- ZH --> 揭示肾组织冻存悬液严重丢失 PT 上皮(跨品系、性别、10x 版本可重复),以及 snRNA-seq 丢失淋巴细胞。 <!-- EN --> Shows severe PT-epithelium loss after cryopreserving kidney suspensions (reproducible across strain, sex, 10x chemistry) and lymphocyte loss in snRNA-seq.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 仅健康小鼠肾;无疾病样本,也无人类组织。 <!-- EN --> Healthy mouse kidney only; no disease and no human tissue.
- <!-- ZH --> 批次与品系/性别/化学版本混杂(批次 1 C57 雄 v2;批次 3 Balb/c 雌 v3),sc-vs-sn 比较与冻存重复实验无法完全区分这些因素。 <!-- EN --> Batch is confounded with strain/sex/chemistry (batch 1 C57 male v2; batch 3 Balb/c female v3), so sc-vs-sn and the cryo repeat cannot fully separate these factors.
- <!-- ZH --> 没有组成"真值":BSEQ-sc 反卷积与 Clark et al. 解剖估计相互矛盾,作者自承需继续寻找 ground truth。 <!-- EN --> No composition ground truth: BSEQ-sc deconvolution contradicts the Clark et al. anatomical estimates, and the authors say they "will continue to search for approaches to better define the 'ground truth'".
- <!-- ZH --> 应激签名是从 warm 诱导基因中人工挑选的文献基因,并非独立推导;Seurat v2、Cell Ranger 2.1.1 等工具已较旧。 <!-- EN --> The stress signature was hand-picked from warm-induced genes rather than derived independently; tooling (Seurat v2, Cell Ranger 2.1.1) is dated.
- <!-- ZH --> 冷解离与 37 °C 解离还在机械步骤、离心速度等方面不同,温度不是唯一变量。 <!-- EN --> Cold and 37 °C protocols also differ in mechanical steps and spin speeds, so temperature is not the only variable.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 处在 **detect** 一环的"负对照/干扰因素"位置。Plan A 要构建人肾单细胞 benchmark,检验异常检测器能否把**疾病信号**与**测量/取材伪影**分开。本数据集提供了已知、受控的技术扰动:(1) 37 °C vs 冷解离 → 解离应激(17 基因签名,免疫与内皮细胞最敏感,足细胞丢失);(2) 新鲜 vs 冻存 vs 甲醇 → 保存伪影(上皮丢失、应激、ambient RNA);(3) sc vs sn → 平台/组分偏差。由于样本均为健康组织,任何被检测器打高分的细胞都是"技术异常"而非疾病,可作为假阳性标定集。但它是**小鼠**数据,只能作为后期的校准/迁移检验,不能作为主 benchmark;17 基因签名需映射到人类同源基因(FOS、JUN、HSPA1A 等)后再用于人肾数据的伪影标注。注意 GEO 中臂标签仅在样本层面,且批次与品系/性别/化学版本混杂。
<!-- EN --> Sits at the **detect** stage as a negative-control / nuisance-factor resource. Plan A builds a human-kidney single-cell benchmark testing whether anomaly detectors separate **disease** from **measurement/procurement artifacts**. This dataset provides known, controlled technical perturbations: (1) 37 °C vs cold → dissociation stress (17-gene signature; immune and endothelial most sensitive; podocyte loss); (2) fresh vs cryo vs methanol → storage artifacts (epithelial loss, stress, ambient RNA); (3) sc vs sn → platform/composition bias. Because all tissue is healthy, any cell a detector flags here is a *technical* anomaly, not disease, so it can serve as a false-positive calibration set. Being **mouse**, it is for later calibration / transfer checks only, not the main benchmark; the 17-gene signature needs mapping to human orthologs (FOS, JUN, HSPA1A, …) before annotating artifacts in human kidney data. Caveats: arm labels in GEO are sample-level, and batch is confounded with strain/sex/chemistry.
