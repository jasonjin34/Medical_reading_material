## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> CancerRiskNet:只用**带时间戳的 ICD 诊断码序列**(disease trajectory,疾病轨迹),在丹麦全国患者登记(DNPR;过滤后 620 万人、23,985 例胰腺癌)上训练 Transformer,同时预测评估时刻之后 3/6/12/36/60 个月内是否发生胰腺癌;36 个月窗口 AUROC 0.879,训练时去掉确诊前 3 个月的数据后摘要报告 0.83;不改参数直接用于美国退伍军人系统(US-VA)降到 0.710,在 VA 上重新训练后为 0.775(摘要:0.78)(Abstract;Results)。
<!-- EN --> CancerRiskNet: using **only timestamped ICD diagnosis-code sequences** (disease trajectories), a Transformer trained on the Danish National Patient Registry (DNPR; 6.2M patients and 23,985 pancreatic cancer cases after filtering) predicts whether pancreatic cancer occurs within 3/6/12/36/60 months after the assessment time. The 36-month AUROC is 0.879, and the abstract reports 0.83 when the last 3 months before diagnosis are removed from training. Applied unchanged to the US Veterans Affairs system (US-VA) it drops to 0.710; retrained on VA data it reaches 0.775 (abstract: 0.78) (Abstract; Results).

## 要点 / Takeaways
- <!-- ZH --> **时间顺序有信息量**:同样的诊断码,忽略顺序的 bag-of-words 为 AUROC 0.807,Transformer 为 0.879;在「100 万人中前 1,000 名」操作点,相对风险(RR)从 2.1 升到 104.7(Fig. 3a,b)。 <!-- EN --> **Time order carries signal**: on the same codes, an order-agnostic bag-of-words gets AUROC 0.807 vs 0.879 for the Transformer; at the "top 1,000 of 1M" operating point, relative risk (RR) rises from 2.1 to 104.7 (Fig. 3a,b).
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:**"数据排除"是这篇论文最该照搬的评估设计**:训练时删掉确诊前 3/6/12 个月的诊断,看模型是不是只在读黄疸、腹痛这类"已经快确诊"的码;性能下降但没崩(正文:0.843/0.829/0.827)(Results §"Performance with data exclusion")。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: **"data exclusion" is the evaluation design most worth copying**: remove diagnoses from the last 3/6/12 months before diagnosis in training, to test whether the model only reads near-diagnosis codes such as jaundice or abdominal pain; performance drops but does not collapse (text: 0.843/0.829/0.827) (Results §"Performance with data exclusion").
- <!-- ZH --> **跨医疗系统不能直接搬**:丹麦模型在 VA 上 AUROC 0.710,VA 本地重训 0.775;作者结论是系统差异大时需要本地独立训练(Results §"Application in a different healthcare system";Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> **Models do not transfer across health systems for free**: the Danish model gives AUROC 0.710 on VA, local VA retraining 0.775; the authors conclude that independent local training is needed when systems differ substantially (Results §"Application in a different healthcare system"; Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> **标签质量本身是一个大问题**:VA 的 EHR 里有胰腺癌 ICD 码的人只有 32.4% 出现在 VA 癌症登记中,所以作者把病例定义为「登记 ∩ EHR 码」(Methods §"US-VA cancer registry and data quality")。 <!-- EN --> **Label quality is a major issue in itself**: only 32.4% of VA patients with a pancreatic cancer ICD code in the EHR appear in the VA cancer registry, so cases were defined as registry ∩ EHR code (Methods §"US-VA cancer registry and data quality").
- <!-- ZH --> **用临床容量来报告性能**:除 AUROC 外,作者报告 RR@前 0.1%,并估算 100 万人中筛出前 1,000 人时约 320 人会发病(PPV 0.32,不排除数据),保守估计(PPV 0.07,排除 3 个月)至少约 70 人是新识别的高危(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> **Performance reported in terms of clinical capacity**: besides AUROC, RR at the top 0.1%, and an estimate that of the top 1,000 of 1M people about 320 would develop cancer (PPV 0.32, no exclusion), with a conservative estimate (PPV 0.07, 3-month exclusion) of at least ~70 newly identified high-risk patients (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:对 Sinai 项目,可直接复用的是这套**评估协议**(子轨迹、对照截断、数据排除、RR@N1000、跨系统检验),而不只是模型结构。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: for the Sinai project, the reusable part is this **evaluation protocol** (partial trajectories, control truncation, data exclusion, RR@N1000, cross-system test), not only the architecture.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 胰腺癌通常发现得晚:约 80% 的患者确诊时已是局部晚期或远处转移,此时 5 年生存只有 2–9%;而早期发现的患者可以通过手术、化疗与放疗的组合治愈(Introduction)。胰腺癌发病率远低于肺癌、乳腺癌、结直肠癌,按年龄做全人群筛查会带来大量假阳性和昂贵检查;已知的高外显率危险因素很少;目前只有家族史、罕见遗传致病变异或胰腺囊性病变的少数人接受影像监测,而他们只占胰腺癌患者的一小部分(Introduction)。作者的目标:从大量病人的**真实世界纵向临床记录**中挑出数量适中的高危人群,使监测项目可负担。此前用病历做胰腺癌风险预测的工作(问卷、全科记录、医院系统数据、TriNetX)只使用「是否出现过某诊断码」,不使用病人轨迹中的**时间顺序**;用丹麦登记数据做的疾病轨迹研究则是描述性的(Introduction)。
<!-- EN --> Pancreatic cancer is usually found late: ~80% of patients are diagnosed with locally advanced or distant metastatic disease, when 5-year survival is 2–9%, whereas patients with early-stage disease can be cured by surgery, chemotherapy and radiotherapy (Introduction). Incidence is far lower than for lung, breast or colorectal cancer, so age-based population screening would produce many false positives and costly tests; few high-penetrance risk factors are known; only the few people with family history, rare inherited pathogenic variants or cystic pancreatic lesions currently get imaging surveillance, and they account for a small fraction of cases (Introduction). The aim: use **real-world longitudinal clinical records** of many patients to pick out a moderate number of high-risk patients so that surveillance becomes affordable. Previous record-based pancreatic cancer models (survey data, GP records, hospital-system data, TriNetX) used only the occurrence of codes, not the **time sequence** of a patient's trajectory; Danish-registry trajectory studies were descriptive (Introduction).

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> 面向 CS 读者,按步骤写(均出自 Methods 与 Fig. 1):
<!-- EN --> Step by step for a CS reader (all from Methods and Fig. 1):

- <!-- ZH --> **输入表示**:每个病人是一串 (ICD 诊断码, 日期, 年龄) 事件。ICD(International Classification of Diseases,国际疾病分类)是分层编码;本文截到三位字符的类别(level 3,如 C25 = 胰腺恶性肿瘤)。Methods 写的是 n = 2,997 个三位码,Results 写的是「ICD level-3, 2,000 disease codes」—— 论文内部两个数字不一致。丹麦数据中 ICD-8(使用至 1994 年)与 ICD-10 **不做语义映射**、作为不同 token 共用。去掉标记为 temporary / referral 的码(8.3%)。病例定义:ICD-10 C25 或 ICD-8 157。 <!-- EN --> **Input representation**: each patient is a sequence of (ICD diagnosis code, date, age) events. ICD (International Classification of Diseases) is hierarchical; codes are truncated to the three-character category (level 3, e.g. C25 = malignant neoplasm of pancreas). Methods states n = 2,997 three-character codes while Results says "ICD level-3, 2,000 disease codes" — the paper is internally inconsistent here. In the Danish data, ICD-8 (used until 1994) and ICD-10 codes are used side by side **without semantic mapping**. Codes labelled "temporary" or "referral" (8.3%) are removed. Case definition: ICD-10 C25 or ICD-8 157.
- <!-- ZH --> **样本构造(数据增强)**:对每个病人取所有「从病史起点开始、长度 ≥5 个诊断、终点不同」的连续前缀子轨迹,终点称为评估时刻(time of assessment)。病例只用在确诊之前结束的子轨迹;非病例只用在其记录结束(死亡或数据冻结)前 ≥2 年结束的子轨迹,以避免对照里混进尚未确诊的癌症。每个 batch 正负样本约各半(balanced sampling)。 <!-- EN --> **Sample construction (augmentation)**: for each patient, all continuous prefix sub-trajectories starting at the first record, of length ≥5 diagnoses, with different endpoints; the endpoint is the time of assessment. For cases, only sub-trajectories ending before diagnosis; for non-cases, only those ending ≥2 years before the end of their records (death or data freeze), to avoid undiagnosed cancer among controls. Batches are balance-sampled to roughly equal positives and negatives.
- <!-- ZH --> **标签**:对每个子轨迹,在 t ∈ {3, 6, 12, 36, 60} 个月处给阶跃函数标签 y<sub>i,t</sub>(这段时间内发生胰腺癌 = 1)。损失是这 5 个时间点上交叉熵之和(见下),即共享表示上的 5 个二分类目标;论文把预测描述为一个从 0 单调上升的风险函数(Methods)。 <!-- EN --> **Labels**: for each sub-trajectory, a step-function label y<sub>i,t</sub> at t ∈ {3, 6, 12, 36, 60} months (1 if cancer occurs within that window). The loss sums cross-entropy over these five time points (below), i.e. five binary targets on a shared representation; the paper describes the prediction as a risk function that increases monotonically from 0 (Methods).
- <!-- ZH --> **模型三段式**:embedding(诊断码 → 低维向量,与其余部分联合训练;无 embedding 的模型用 one-hot)→ encoding(GRU、Transformer,或 bag-of-words / MLP 的简单 pooling;编码层也输入年龄)把整段轨迹压成一个 "fingerprint" 向量 → 前馈网络输出 5 个时间窗的风险。Transformer 的位置编码改编自 Vaswani 等:用 128 个频率的余弦波,在**每个诊断的年龄**和**诊断之间的时间差**处取值,作为各 token 的权重;这样同一种病在不同时间、两种病相隔远近都能区分。 <!-- EN --> **Three-part model**: embedding (codes → low-dimensional vectors trained jointly; one-hot for models without embedding) → encoding (GRU, Transformer, or simple pooling for bag-of-words / MLP; age is also input) compresses the trajectory into a "fingerprint" vector → feed-forward network outputs risk for the five windows. The Transformer's positional encoding is adapted from Vaswani et al.: cosine waves at 128 frequencies evaluated at **the age of each diagnosis** and **the time differences between diagnoses**, used as weights on each token, so the same disease at different times, and two diseases near or far apart in time, can be distinguished.
- <!-- ZH --> **损失**:5 个时间点上的交叉熵之和 + L2 正则;病例只计入确诊之前的时间点(N<sub>T</sub> ≤ 5)。大规模超参搜索(Supplementary Table 2),在开发集上用 **AUPRC** 选模型。 <!-- EN --> **Loss**: cross-entropy summed over the five time points plus L2; for cases only time points before diagnosis count (N<sub>T</sub> ≤ 5). A large hyperparameter search (Supplementary Table 2); model selection on the development set by **AUPRC**.
- <!-- ZH --> **划分与评估**:按病人随机 80/10/10 划分(同一病人的所有子轨迹只进一个集合)。性能**按子轨迹而非按病人计算**。指标:AUROC;F1 操作点的 precision/recall;以及 RR = precision / 人群发病率,在「100 万人中风险最高的 n 人」处读数(N1000 = 前 0.1%)。95% CI 来自 200 次 bootstrap。 <!-- EN --> **Split and evaluation**: random 80/10/10 split by patient (all sub-trajectories of a patient in one split). Performance is **computed over trajectories, not patients**. Metrics: AUROC; precision/recall at the F1 point; and RR = precision / population incidence, read at "the n highest-risk patients per 1M" (N1000 = top 0.1%). 95% CIs from 200 bootstrap resamples.
- <!-- ZH --> **数据排除实验**:分别重新训练,输入中删掉确诊前最后 3、6、12 个月的诊断,检验模型是否只依赖临近确诊、几乎等于标签的码(如消化道肿瘤)。 <!-- EN --> **Data-exclusion experiments**: retrain separately with diagnoses from the last 3, 6 or 12 months before diagnosis removed from the input, to test reliance on near-diagnosis codes that almost encode the label (e.g. digestive-tract neoplasms).
- <!-- ZH --> **跨系统与可解释性**:把丹麦最佳模型用于 VA,只做编码映射(ICD-9 → ICD-10,再加前缀 "D" 变成丹麦格式);再在 VA 上从头训练。用 Integrated Gradients(Captum)对 36 个月输出求归因,在 embedding 输出上计算、对维度和轨迹求和,并按距确诊 0–6/6–12/12–24/24–36 个月分层;年龄贡献在年龄 embedding 层上算。 <!-- EN --> **Cross-system and interpretability**: apply the best Danish model to VA with only code mapping (ICD-9 → ICD-10, then a "D" prefix for Danish format); then train from scratch on VA. Integrated Gradients (Captum) attributions for the 36-month output, computed on the embedding output, summed over dimensions and trajectories, stratified by 0–6/6–12/12–24/24–36 months to cancer; age contribution computed at the age-embedding layer.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把疾病轨迹的**时间序列**(而非码是否出现)作为输入,并用诊断年龄 + 诊断间隔做连续时间位置编码;与忽略顺序的对照直接比较(Fig. 3a,b)。 <!-- EN --> Uses the **time sequence** of disease trajectories (not just code occurrence) as input, with continuous-time positional encoding from age at diagnosis and inter-diagnosis intervals, directly compared with order-agnostic baselines (Fig. 3a,b).
- <!-- ZH --> **多时间窗输出**(3–60 个月),对应「能提前多久发现」的临床问题。 <!-- EN --> **Multi-horizon outputs** (3–60 months), matching the clinical question of how early cancer can be flagged.
- <!-- ZH --> **数据排除协议**:系统地去掉确诊前 3/6/12 个月的输入,区分早期信号与近确诊信号。 <!-- EN --> **Data-exclusion protocol**: systematically removing the last 3/6/12 months of input to separate early signal from near-diagnosis signal.
- <!-- ZH --> **RR@前 0.1%** 作为临床操作点,把模型性能换算成监测项目规模。 <!-- EN --> **RR at the top 0.1%** as the clinical operating point, translating performance into surveillance-programme size.
- <!-- ZH --> 在两个差异很大的国家级系统之间做**直接迁移 vs 本地重训**的对比。 <!-- EN --> A **direct transfer vs local retraining** comparison between two very different national systems.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 作者所述:只用诊断码;作者预计加入用药、化验、临床文本、腹部 CT/MRI、胚系遗传、全科记录后会更好(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: diagnosis codes only; they expect gains from medications, lab values, clinical notes, abdominal CT/MRI, germline genetics and GP records (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者所述:不存在一个全球通用模型,系统差异大时需本地训练;联邦学习只是被提议,作者指出其障碍也有社会与组织层面(Discussion)。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: no single globally robust model; local training is needed when systems differ; federated learning is only proposed, with social and organisational barriers (Discussion).
- <!-- ZH --> 作者所述:没有成本-收益的实证数据;临床影响取决于 EHR 数据质量与当地实践;本文只解决「识别高危」这一步,不涉及后续监测与治疗(Results;Discussion)。IG 特征不一定有因果关系(Results §"Predictive features")。 <!-- EN --> Stated by the authors: no empirical cost–benefit data; clinical impact depends on EHR quality and local practice; the work addresses only the first step (identifying high-risk patients), not surveillance or treatment (Results; Discussion). IG features are not necessarily causal (Results §"Predictive features").
- <!-- ZH --> 人群:丹麦无全科医生数据;VA 以男性为主(85.7%)(Methods;Table 1)。 <!-- EN --> Populations: no GP data in Denmark; VA is mostly male (85.7%) (Methods; Table 1).
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:测试集是按病人**随机**划分,不是按时间划分,所以没有检验时间漂移(如 2015 年美国 ICD-9 → ICD-10 切换);指标按子轨迹计算,轨迹多的病人权重更大;RR@N1000 是把测试集结果外推到假想的 100 万人。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: the test set is a **random** patient split, not a temporal split, so drift (e.g. the 2015 US ICD-9 → ICD-10 switch) is not tested; metrics are computed over sub-trajectories, so patients with many trajectories weigh more; RR@N1000 extrapolates test-set results to a hypothetical 1M population.
- <!-- ZH --> 我们的理解:论文内部有数字不一致(码数 2,997 vs 2,000;排除实验正文 vs Fig. 3c 图例;VA 人数四个版本),引用时要注明出处。 <!-- EN --> Our reading: the paper has internal inconsistencies (2,997 vs 2,000 codes; exclusion results in text vs Fig. 3c legend; four versions of the VA counts), so cite numbers with their location.
- <!-- ZH --> 后续文献的观点(非本文):npj Digital Medicine 2025 的 LLM-embedding 论文指出,本文 0–6 个月与 24–36 个月 top-10 特征有一半以上重叠(如不明原因黄疸),认为多标签结构可能把近端强特征「传」到远端窗口(见该论文 Discussion;本仓库 `ehr-pancreatic-llm-npjdigitmed-2025`)。 <!-- EN --> A later paper's view (not this paper): the npj Digital Medicine 2025 LLM-embedding paper notes that over 50% of this paper's top-10 predictors for 0–6 months also appear for 24–36 months (e.g. unspecified jaundice), suggesting multi-label heads may propagate strong proximal features into distal windows (that paper's Discussion; `ehr-pancreatic-llm-npjdigitmed-2025` in this repo).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> **首先是 Mount Sinai EHR 项目**。我们的理解:它给出了一个只需 ICD 码 + 时间戳就能跑的完整最小方案,以及一套可以照搬的评估协议:(1) 每个病人截取多个评估时刻的前缀子轨迹;(2) 对照组在记录结束前 2 年截断;(3) 排除确诊前 3/6/12 个月的数据检验「近确诊信号」;(4) 报告 RR@前 0.1% 这类能对上临床容量的操作点;(5) 外部模型直接迁移作为基线,对比本地重训。我们的理解:Sinai 是美国医疗系统,和 VA 一样经历 2015 年 ICD-9 → ICD-10 切换(VA 的切换日期见 Methods),编码也可能受计费影响(作者用编码频率、就医门槛、计费约束与优化、病人进出 VA 系统来解释 VA 迁移性能下降,Discussion;ICD 切换是我们补充的因素,作者未提),所以 Sinai 上应以本地训练为主,并最好加一个**按时间划分**的测试集。标签上,VA 的教训(EHR 码只有 32.4% 被癌症登记确认)提示 Sinai 需要肿瘤登记或病理确认的病例定义,而不是只看 ICD 码。终点上,同一协议可以换成心-肾-代谢类终点(如 CKD 进展、透析、心衰),这与实验室的肾脏/心脏方向更贴近;这些都是我们的推断,论文本身只研究胰腺癌。
<!-- EN --> **First, the Mount Sinai EHR project.** Our reading: it gives a complete minimal recipe that runs on ICD codes plus timestamps alone, and an evaluation protocol that can be copied: (1) multiple prefix sub-trajectories per patient at different assessment times; (2) controls truncated 2 years before record end; (3) 3/6/12-month exclusion to test near-diagnosis signal; (4) operating points such as RR at the top 0.1% that map onto clinical capacity; (5) direct transfer of an external model as a baseline vs local retraining. Our reading: Sinai is a US system that, like VA, went through the 2015 ICD-9 → ICD-10 switch (VA's switch date is in Methods), and its coding may also be shaped by billing (the authors explain the VA transfer drop by coding frequency, thresholds for seeking care, billing constraints and optimization, and patients entering and leaving VA, Discussion; the ICD switch is our addition, not named by the authors), so Sinai work should centre on local training and ideally add a **temporal** test split. For labels, the VA lesson (only 32.4% of EHR-coded patients confirmed by the cancer registry) suggests Sinai needs a tumour-registry or pathology-confirmed case definition rather than ICD codes alone. For endpoints, the same protocol can be pointed at cardio-kidney-metabolic outcomes (e.g. CKD progression, dialysis, heart failure), closer to the lab's kidney/heart focus; these are our inferences — the paper itself studies only pancreatic cancer.

<!-- ZH --> 我们的理解(与 detect → model → revert 的关系,简述):轨迹模型相当于**病人尺度的 detect**,回答「谁、何时风险高」,但作者明确说 IG 特征不一定是因果,所以它不回答「为什么、改什么」;一个可能的接口是从 EHR 高危人群中挑出有活检/组学数据的病人,再做组织层的异常检测。数据排除实验也可以作为 agent 给出 EHR 风险结论前必须跑的一项「泄漏检查」。
<!-- EN --> Our reading (relation to detect → model → revert, briefly): a trajectory model is **patient-scale detect**, answering "who is at high risk, and when", but the authors state IG features are not necessarily causal, so it does not answer "why, and what to change". One possible interface is to take EHR-flagged high-risk patients who have biopsies/omics and run tissue-level anomaly detection on them. The data-exclusion test could also be a leakage check an agent must run before stating an EHR risk claim.
