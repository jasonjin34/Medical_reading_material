# A Deep Learning-Based Pathomics Methodology for Quantifying and Characterizing Nucleated Cells in the Bone Marrow Microenvironment

- **Authors:** Spencer Krichevsky, Madhu M. Ouseph, Yuwei Zhang, Ghaith Abu-Zeinah, Joseph M. Scandura, Rajarsi Gupta
- **Venue / Year:** Blood, Vol. 142 (Supplement 1), 65th ASH Annual Meeting, Abstract 2294 · 2023

## Abstract
Background / rationale: The overall cellularity of the bone marrow (BM) microenvironment is a WHO criterion for differentiating BCR-ABL- myeloproliferative neoplasms (MPNs) such as polycythemia vera (PV) and myelofibrosis (MF). However, manual assessment by hematopathologists is subjective, not scalable, and only records a single global measure, disregarding regional differences within the bone marrow.

Methodology: Deep learning combined with classical image analysis was used to segment nucleated cells, fat cells, and other evaluable regions of hematopoietic tissue on bone marrow whole-slide images (WSIs). Shape, texture, and color features were extracted from the segmented nuclei to enhance characterization of the BM microenvironment.

Results / conclusions: This pilot study produced a practical and interpretable Pathomics pipeline with downstream analytics, as a proof of concept for hypothesis-driven research. Future directions include applying the approach to a large cohort of diagnosis WSIs from other MPN subtypes and correlating the findings with additional tools such as immune-cell detection models.
