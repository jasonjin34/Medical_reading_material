## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> COMET(Aghaeepour 组,Stanford):组学研究往往只有几十到几百个样本,而同一医院的 EHR 有几万人 —— 先在「只有 EHR」的大队列上预训练一个 GRU,再把权重迁移进 EHR + 组学的多模态网络(早融合与晚融合混合),在 61 人妊娠蛋白组和 559 人 UK Biobank 癌症蛋白组上都提升了预测,并让模型挑出的蛋白更符合已知生物学。
<!-- EN --> COMET (Aghaeepour lab, Stanford): omics studies often have tens to hundreds of samples while the same hospital's EHR covers tens of thousands — pretrain a GRU on the large EHR-only cohort, transfer its weights into a multimodal EHR + omics network that blends early and late fusion, and both prediction and the biological plausibility of selected proteins improve, in a 61-person pregnancy proteomics cohort and a 559-person UK Biobank cancer proteomics cohort.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 组学测量维度高,但队列规模受临床与预算限制,结果难以复现;机器学习中的「假阳性」表现为过拟合。现有改进(先验特征权重、迁移学习、深度 Cox)主要只用组学或元数据本身。EHR 越来越可得(MIMIC、UK Biobank、OMOP 标准化的院内数据库),但 EHR–组学整合有结构性矛盾:早/中融合需要两模态都完整(会丢掉大多数只有 EHR 的病人),晚融合又学不到跨模态交互。问题:如何利用**只有 EHR 的大量病人**来改善**小规模组学队列**的建模与生物学发现?
<!-- EN --> Omics is high-dimensional but cohort size is capped by clinical and budget constraints, hurting replication; in ML, false positives show up as overfitting. Existing remedies (feature priors, transfer learning, deep Cox) mostly learn from the omics or metadata alone. EHRs are increasingly available (MIMIC, UK Biobank, OMOP-standardized hospital databases), but EHR–omics integration has a structural tension: early/intermediate fusion needs both modalities complete (dropping the many EHR-only patients), while late fusion cannot learn cross-modal interactions. The question: how to use **many EHR-only patients** to improve modelling and biological discovery in a **small omics cohort**?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **EHR 嵌入**:从 OMOP 的 measurement、observation、drug_exposure、condition_occurrence、procedure_occurrence 表抽取概念码;把同一病人同一天的码视为「句子」(随机打乱,因很多时间戳不可靠),用 word2vec 学 400 维嵌入,同日取平均得到每天一个 summary embedding,最多取最近 32 天。**预训练**:只有 EHR 的病人(pretraining cohort)上训练 GRU-RNN 预测同一结局(妊娠:随机选末 100 天内一点预测距分娩天数;癌症:以首次 C 开头 ICD 码时间为起点预测 3 年死亡)。**多模态架构**:三支 —— EHR 支(冻结的预训练 GRU + 线性层)、组学支(线性层;癌症任务为单隐层 MLP)、joint 支(EHR 潜表示与组学拼接后的线性层),最后一个无偏置线性层把三个预测加权合成 —— 即**早融合与晚融合的自适应混合**。损失分别为 MSE 与 BCE。**对照**:EHR-only、组学-only、joint baseline(结构与 COMET 完全相同但无预训练);ridge/logistic 回归(含把预训练系数作先验 β₀ 的变体);COMET Transformer 变体。每个实验 25 次不同划分(70/15/15),验证集预测取平均。**机制分析**:EHR 潜维度与蛋白的相关数、Integrated Gradients 蛋白重要性、外部数据集验证重要蛋白、中间节点性能、函数空间 t-SNE 轨迹。
<!-- EN --> **EHR embedding**: concept codes from OMOP measurement, observation, drug_exposure, condition_occurrence and procedure_occurrence tables; codes from one patient-day form a "sentence" (shuffled, since many timestamps are unreliable); word2vec learns 400-d embeddings, averaged per day into a summary embedding, using up to the 32 most recent days. **Pretraining**: a GRU-RNN trained on EHR-only patients (pretraining cohort) to predict the same outcome (pregnancy: days to labour from a random point in the last 100 days; cancer: 3-year mortality from the first C-prefixed ICD code). **Multimodal architecture**: three branches — EHR (frozen pretrained GRU + linear), omics (linear; one-hidden-layer MLP for cancer), joint (linear on concatenated EHR latent + omics) — combined by a final bias-free linear layer: an **adaptive blend of early and late fusion**. MSE and BCE losses respectively. **Comparators**: EHR-only, omics-only, and a joint baseline identical to COMET minus pretraining; ridge/logistic regression (including variants using pretrained coefficients as priors β₀); a COMET Transformer variant. 25 random splits (70/15/15) per experiment, averaging validation predictions. **Mechanism analyses**: number of EHR-latent–protein correlations, Integrated-Gradients protein importance, external validation of important proteins, intermediate-node performance, function-space t-SNE trajectories.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **妊娠队列(Stanford)**:2013–2021 年在 Stanford 分娩的 30,904 人(STARR OMOP);其中 **61 人**(171 份血浆样本,妊娠末 100 天 2–3 次采样)有基于适配体(aptamer)平台的蛋白组(1,317 个蛋白,去掉 12 个对照后 1,305 个),其余 30,843 人为预训练队列;另用同队列代谢组验证。**癌症队列(UK Biobank)**:入组前 12 个月至入组后 5 年内诊断任意癌症的 36,901 人;其中 **559 人**在确诊后 12 个月内有入组时血样蛋白组(UKB 蛋白组覆盖 53,058 人、中位 2,894 个蛋白),其余 36,342 人为预训练队列;3 年死亡率 5.5%。外部验证分别用公开妊娠蛋白组与乳腺癌蛋白组数据。
<!-- EN --> **Pregnancy cohort (Stanford)**: 30,904 people delivering at Stanford 2013–2021 (STARR OMOP); **61** of them (171 plasma samples, 2–3 draws in the last 100 days) have aptamer-based proteomics (1,317 proteins; 1,305 after removing 12 controls), the other 30,843 form the pretraining cohort; metabolomics from the same cohort as a second omics test. **Cancer cohort (UK Biobank)**: 36,901 people diagnosed with any cancer from 12 months before to 5 years after enrolment; **559** have enrolment-blood proteomics within 12 months after diagnosis (UKB proteomics covers 53,058 participants, median 2,894 proteins), the other 36,342 form the pretraining cohort; 3-year mortality 5.5%. External validation uses public pregnancy and breast-cancer proteomics datasets.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **距分娩天数**:COMET Pearson r = **0.868**(95% CI 0.825–0.900,RMSE 16.0 天);joint baseline 0.815(18.4 天);蛋白-only 0.796;EHR-only 0.768。代谢组:COMET 0.839 vs 仅代谢物 0.758。 <!-- EN --> **Days to labour**: COMET Pearson r = **0.868** (95% CI 0.825–0.900, RMSE 16.0 days); joint baseline 0.815 (18.4 days); proteomics-only 0.796; EHR-only 0.768. Metabolomics: COMET 0.839 vs metabolites alone 0.758.
- <!-- ZH --> **3 年癌症死亡**:COMET AUROC **0.842**(0.744–0.922)、AUPRC **0.504**;joint baseline 0.786 / 0.365;EHR-only 0.749 / 0.205;蛋白-only 0.737 / 0.325。 <!-- EN --> **3-year cancer mortality**: COMET AUROC **0.842** (0.744–0.922), AUPRC **0.504**; joint baseline 0.786 / 0.365; EHR-only 0.749 / 0.205; proteomics-only 0.737 / 0.325.
- <!-- ZH --> **预训练对线性模型也有效**:ridge 的 joint 模型加入预训练先验后 r 从 0.572 升到 0.799;Transformer 变体 r = 0.848,略低于 GRU 版 COMET。 <!-- EN --> **Pretraining helps linear models too**: adding pretrained priors raises ridge joint r from 0.572 to 0.799; the Transformer variant reaches r = 0.848, slightly below the GRU COMET.
- <!-- ZH --> **EHR–蛋白对齐**:妊娠中 EHR 潜维度与蛋白的显著相关数 3,201 → **5,364**;癌症中 0 → **7,591**。COMET 最相关蛋白(IL1RL1/ST2、cystatin C、plexin-B2)已知与妊娠进程相关。 <!-- EN --> **EHR–protein alignment**: significant EHR-latent–protein correlations rise from 3,201 to **5,364** (pregnancy) and from 0 to **7,591** (cancer). COMET's most correlated proteins (IL1RL1/ST2, cystatin C, plexin-B2) are known pregnancy-progression players.
- <!-- ZH --> **外部验证**:COMET 更重视的蛋白在外部妊娠数据中与分娩时间的平均 |r| = 0.22 vs 基线重视蛋白 0.12(p = 0.0276);癌症中 18 个里 9 个与死亡显著相关(基线 8 个)。 <!-- EN --> **External validation**: proteins COMET weights more have mean |r| = 0.22 with time to labour in an external dataset vs 0.12 for baseline-favoured proteins (p = 0.0276); in cancer, 9/18 vs 8/18 are significantly associated with mortality.
- <!-- ZH --> **机制**:相同训练损失下测试损失更低,参数(函数)空间轨迹与基线分离 —— 作者称之为「通过初始化实现的正则化」。 <!-- EN --> **Mechanism**: lower test loss at matched training loss, and function-space trajectories separate from the baseline — the authors call it "regularization by initialization".

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 据作者所知,**首个用 EHR 迁移学习改善组学分析**的方法。 <!-- EN --> To the authors' knowledge, the **first approach using EHR transfer learning to improve omics analysis**.
- <!-- ZH --> 解决「EHR 多、组学少」的结构不对称:预训练利用所有 EHR 病人,多模态阶段仍能学到跨模态交互。 <!-- EN --> Resolves the "many EHR, few omics" asymmetry: pretraining uses all EHR patients while the multimodal stage still learns cross-modal interactions.
- <!-- ZH --> 以生物学一致性(而不仅是预测性能)作为评估维度:EHR 潜表示与蛋白的对齐、外部数据验证重要蛋白。 <!-- EN --> Evaluates biological consistency, not just predictive performance: EHR-latent–protein alignment and external validation of important proteins.
- <!-- ZH --> 预训练的效益跨架构成立(GRU、Transformer、ridge、logistic)。 <!-- EN --> The pretraining benefit holds across architectures (GRU, Transformer, ridge, logistic).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 组学队列极小(61 人 / 559 人),置信区间宽(癌症 AUROC CI 0.744–0.922);外部验证只是间接的(重要蛋白的相关性),且癌症中 9/18 vs 8/18 的差距很小。 <!-- EN --> Very small omics cohorts (61 / 559) with wide CIs (cancer AUROC 0.744–0.922); external validation is indirect (correlation of important proteins), and the cancer gap of 9/18 vs 8/18 is small.
- <!-- ZH --> 只在表格型组学(蛋白组、代谢组)上验证;作者明确说**不知道对需要复杂架构的模态(如空间转录组)是否有效**。 <!-- EN --> Validated only on tabular omics (proteomics, metabolomics); the authors explicitly state it is **unknown whether it helps modalities needing complex architectures (e.g. spatial transcriptomics)**.
- <!-- ZH --> 预训练需要有标签的 EHR 任务(与目标任务相同);自监督预训练留待未来。 <!-- EN --> Pretraining needs labelled EHR tasks (the same as the target); self-supervised pretraining is future work.
- <!-- ZH --> EHR 表示较粗:word2vec + 日均值,同日码被打乱,只取最近 32 天;OMOP 映射可能有误。 <!-- EN --> Coarse EHR representation: word2vec with daily means, within-day codes shuffled, only the last 32 days; OMOP mappings may contain errors.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 在 PI 转发的五篇里,这篇是**唯一把 EHR 与分子数据连起来**的,因此它是 Sinai EHR 项目与本实验室组学/组织工作之间最直接的桥。场景完全对应:Sinai 有数百万人的 EHR,而有组学或活检的只是很小的子集(例如肾活检、BioMe 生物库样本)—— COMET 的「大 EHR 预训练 → 小组学队列迁移」正是这种不对称下的标准做法。**与心-代谢-肾方向**:把妊娠/癌症换成 CKD 进展或 DKD,把 EHR 预训练任务换成 eGFR 下降或进入透析,就得到一个可在 Sinai 上直接复现的设计;实验室 Novo 资助的 cardiometabolic-kidney 基础模型若要把 EHR 病人表示和分子数据对齐,本文给出了最小可行的评估方式(潜表示–分子相关数、重要特征的外部验证)。**与 Plan A 的关系**:作者明确把空间转录组列为未知 —— 而 Plan A 恰好是肾的 snRNA/snATAC/Xenium + H&E。一个自然的延伸是:用 EHR 预训练的病人表示作为**样本级协变量/先验**,帮助区分「临床上异常」与「技术伪迹」,与 Plan A 的跨模态一致性思路互补(临床模态成为又一个独立证据源)。**与 detect → model → revert**:COMET 把「病人尺度的临床轨迹」连接到「分子尺度的特征」,是从 EHR 层的 detect 走向分子层 model/revert 的一个接口原型;但它仍是相关性的(重要蛋白≠可干预靶点)。
<!-- EN --> Among the five papers the PI sent, this is **the only one linking EHR to molecular data**, making it the most direct bridge between the Sinai EHR project and the lab's omics/tissue work. The setting matches exactly: Sinai has EHR for millions but omics or biopsies for only a small subset (e.g. kidney biopsies, BioMe biobank samples) — COMET's "pretrain on large EHR → transfer to small omics cohort" is the standard move under this asymmetry. **For cardiometabolic-kidney disease**: swap pregnancy/cancer for CKD progression or DKD and the EHR pretraining task for eGFR decline or dialysis initiation, and you have a design directly reproducible at Sinai; if the lab's Novo-funded cardiometabolic-kidney foundation models must align EHR patient representations with molecular data, this paper gives the minimal viable evaluation (latent–molecule correlation counts, external validation of important features). **For Plan A**: the authors list spatial transcriptomics as an open question — and Plan A is exactly kidney snRNA/snATAC/Xenium + H&E. A natural extension is to use EHR-pretrained patient representations as **sample-level covariates/priors** that help separate "clinically abnormal" from "technical artefact", complementing Plan A's cross-modal-agreement idea (the clinical modality becomes one more independent line of evidence). **On detect → model → revert**: COMET links patient-scale clinical trajectories to molecular features — an interface prototype from EHR-level detect towards molecular-level model/revert — but it remains correlational (an important protein is not an actionable target).
