## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> NYU(Tsirigos 组)与 JHU/UPenn 把原本为肺癌 H&E 开发的自监督框架 Histomorphological Phenotype Learning(HPL)用到 1,421 张 PAS 染色肾脏 WSI 上:不用任何病理标注,把 tile 嵌入聚成「组织形态表型簇」(HPC),每张切片表示为各 HPC 的面积占比向量;这些簇跨 5 个内外部队列视觉一致、与间质纤维化等病理定量相关(MIL 预测纤维化 AUC 0.83),并能预测 TRIDENT 糖尿病肾病队列的肾功能下降。
<!-- EN --> NYU (Tsirigos lab) with JHU/UPenn applies Histomorphological Phenotype Learning (HPL), a self-supervised framework first built for lung-cancer H&E, to 1,421 PAS-stained kidney WSIs: with no pathologist labels, tile embeddings are clustered into histomorphological phenotype clusters (HPCs) and each slide becomes a vector of HPC area fractions. The clusters are visually consistent across five internal/external sets, track pathologist quantifications such as interstitial fibrosis (MIL AUC 0.83), and predict kidney-function decline in the TRIDENT diabetic-nephropathy cohort.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 计算病理多依赖监督学习,标注昂贵且主观(例:肺癌分类需 39 位病理医生在 4,704 张 WSI 上勾画),而肾脏病理医生尤其稀缺,许多机构一个驻院肾病理医生都没有。问题是:能否在**与疾病亚型无关**的前提下,不用专家标签,得到可复现的肾组织模式,并让这些模式复现病理医生的切片级评估、关联临床结局与基因型?
<!-- EN --> Computational pathology mostly relies on supervised learning with costly, subjective annotations (e.g. 39 pathologists outlining 4,704 lung WSIs), and kidney pathologists are especially scarce — many institutions lack even one on site. The question: can reproducible kidney tissue patterns be obtained **disease-subtype-agnostically** and label-free, and can these patterns recapitulate pathologist slide-level assessment and associate with clinical outcomes and genotype?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **预处理**:DeepPATH 切 224×224 非重叠 tile,去掉背景 >50% 的 tile,Reinhard 染色归一化;在 5×、10×、20× 三个倍率分别建模。**自监督**:Barlow Twins(ResNet 层 + 一个 self-attention 层),同一 tile 两种增强视图,使交叉相关矩阵逼近单位阵,输出 128 维嵌入;按验证 loss 停在 epoch 30。**两轮 Leiden 聚类**:第一轮高分辨率,人工识别并剔除伪影簇(模糊、边缘、非组织),这些标签也用于在外部集上去除伪影 tile;第二轮对剩余组织 tile 聚类,用 disruption score(簇内到质心距离)在验证集上取 elbow 定簇数,得到 5×/10×/20× 分别 77/100/99 个 HPC。**切片表示**:每个元素 = 某 HPC 占组织面积的百分比。**下游**:与病理定量做 Spearman 相关(Bonferroni);以 HPC 组成为特征训练 CLAM(MIL)预测间质纤维化 >5%;Kaplan–Meier 与 Cox 模型(LASSO 选出 4 个 HPC)预测 ESKD/eGFR 下降 ≥40%;患者层面按欧氏距离层次聚类为 5 组,比较临床变量与 3 个 SNP(UMOD rs12917707、SLC34A1 rs6420094、SLC7A9 rs12460876)。另与 ImageNet ResNet-50 和 UNI 嵌入做对比。
<!-- EN --> **Preprocessing**: DeepPATH tiling into non-overlapping 224×224 tiles, dropping tiles with >50% background, Reinhard stain normalization; separate models at 5×, 10× and 20×. **Self-supervision**: Barlow Twins (ResNet layers plus one self-attention layer) on two augmented views of each tile, pushing the cross-correlation matrix toward identity, yielding 128-d embeddings; stopped at epoch 30 by validation loss. **Two Leiden rounds**: a high-resolution first round to visually identify and remove artifact clusters (blur, edges, non-tissue) — labels also used to strip artifact tiles from external sets; a second round on remaining tissue tiles with cluster count chosen at the elbow of a disruption score (tile-to-centroid distance) on validation, giving 77/100/99 HPCs at 5×/10×/20×. **Slide representation**: each element = percentage of tissue area in an HPC. **Downstream**: Spearman correlation with pathologist quantification (Bonferroni); CLAM (MIL) on HPC composition to predict interstitial fibrosis >5%; Kaplan–Meier and Cox models (four LASSO-selected HPCs) for ESKD or ≥40% eGFR decline; patient-level hierarchical clustering into five groups compared on clinical variables and three SNPs (UMOD rs12917707, SLC34A1 rs6420094, SLC7A9 rs12460876). Embeddings are also compared with ImageNet ResNet-50 and UNI.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 共 1,421 张 PAS WSI、21 个队列。**开发集**:UPenn 739 例肾切除楔形活检的非肿瘤组织,按糖尿病史与 eGFR 分层均分为训练 247 / 验证 246 / 测试 246(平均 61 岁,eGFR 67,35.2% 糖尿病)。**一年后获得的外部集**:独立测试集 262 张(UPenn 组织、NYU 扫描,不同扫描仪);DDKT 已故供肾移植前穿刺 181 张(106 名供者,其中 75 对左右肾);TRIDENT 糖尿病肾病临床穿刺 239 张(19 家机构,含纵向随访)。tile 数量:5× 425,807、10× 1,670,210、20× 6,748,666。
<!-- EN --> 1,421 PAS WSIs from 21 cohorts. **Development**: non-neoplastic tissue from 739 UPenn nephrectomy wedge biopsies, split evenly (stratified by diabetes and eGFR) into train 247 / val 246 / test 246 (mean age 61, eGFR 67, 35.2% diabetic). **External sets received a year later**: an independent test set of 262 slides (UPenn tissue, scanned at NYU on a different scanner); DDKT pre-implantation donor core biopsies, 181 slides (106 donors, 75 with left/right pairs); TRIDENT diabetic-nephropathy clinical biopsies, 239 slides from 19 institutions with longitudinal follow-up. Tile counts: 425,807 (5×), 1,670,210 (10×), 6,748,666 (20×).

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **跨队列一致**:tile-set 匹配测试(21 人参与),专家平均正确率 94.2%(SD 11.7%),非专家 90.0%(SD 13.1%);DDKT 中同一供者左右肾的切片向量距离显著小于不同供者(p = 4.5e-23)。 <!-- EN --> **Cross-cohort consistency**: in a tile-set matching exercise (21 participants), experts scored 94.2% (SD 11.7%) and non-experts 90.0% (SD 13.1%); in DDKT, left/right kidneys of the same donor are much closer in slide-vector space than across donors (p = 4.5e-23).
- <!-- ZH --> **复现病理定量**:与间质纤维化相关的 HPC 在验证、测试、独立测试与 TRIDENT 中保持相关,高亮区域与病理医生勾画的纤维化区一致;CLAM 预测纤维化 >5% 的 AUC 在测试集 0.79、独立测试集 0.83。HPL 簇与纤维化的关联显著强于 ResNet-50 与 UNI 嵌入的簇;UNI 簇在 TRIDENT 同一组织的 PAS 与 H&E 切片上呈现**染色特异与患者特异的批次效应**。 <!-- EN --> **Recapitulating pathology**: fibrosis-associated HPCs stay correlated across validation, test, independent test and TRIDENT, and highlighted regions match pathologist fibrosis annotations; CLAM predicting fibrosis >5% reaches AUC 0.79 (test) and 0.83 (independent test). HPL clusters associate with fibrosis significantly more strongly than ResNet-50 or UNI clusters, and UNI clusters on paired PAS/H&E slides of the same TRIDENT core show **stain-specific and patient-specific batch effects**.
- <!-- ZH --> **预后**:TRIDENT 中 HPC 49(盲评病理医生注释为 IFTA 背景下的球性硬化)高三分位者肾功能下降更多,HPC 53(形态正常的近端小管)高三分位者更少。Cox 模型:临床基线(年龄、性别、ACR、eGFR;N = 182)c-statistic 0.82 → 加 4 个 HPC 后 0.84;组织学基线(纤维化、RPS 评分、上皮增生;N = 177)0.76 → 0.80。 <!-- EN --> **Prognosis**: in TRIDENT, the top tertile of HPC 49 (blindly annotated as global sclerosis amid IFTA) shows more kidney-function decline, and HPC 53 (histologically unremarkable proximal tubules) less. Cox models: clinical baseline (age, sex, ACR, eGFR; N = 182) c-statistic 0.82 → 0.84 with four HPCs; histology baseline (fibrosis, RPS score, epithelial hyperplasia; N = 177) 0.76 → 0.80.
- <!-- ZH --> **患者分组**:5 个患者组中,第 4 组(标志簇为髓质形态的 HPC 8)eGFR 显著更低(p = 2.0e-10)、尿蛋白更高(p = 0.008);第 0 组 1 型糖尿病比例更高(p = 0.006);第 1 组 SLC34A1 rs6420094 等位基因频率不同。 <!-- EN --> **Patient groups**: of five groups, group 4 (marker HPC 8, medullary morphology) has markedly lower eGFR (p = 2.0e-10) and higher dipstick protein (p = 0.008); group 0 has more type 1 diabetes (p = 0.006); group 1 differs in SLC34A1 rs6420094 allele frequency.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把 HPL 从肺癌 H&E 迁移到异质性极高的 PAS 肾组织,并在 21 个队列、不同扫描仪与活检类型(楔形→穿刺)上外部验证。 <!-- EN --> Transfers HPL from lung-cancer H&E to highly heterogeneous PAS kidney tissue, externally validated across 21 cohorts, scanners and biopsy types (wedge → core).
- <!-- ZH --> 用「切片 = HPC 组成向量」这种可解释表示,同时打通病理定量、预后(Cox)与基因型关联三条下游。 <!-- EN --> A single interpretable representation (slide = HPC-composition vector) feeding pathology quantification, prognosis (Cox) and genotype association.
- <!-- ZH --> 显式的「伪影簇」识别与剔除步骤,以及跨队列一致性的人类匹配测试,是无监督表型可信度的两种实用检验。 <!-- EN --> An explicit artifact-cluster identification/removal step and a human matching test of cross-cohort consistency — two practical checks of unsupervised-phenotype trustworthiness.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 训练集来自肾切除楔形活检(多为常见的糖尿病/高血压改变),不含穿刺活检与罕见病因,也不含移植受者,覆盖不了真实肾病谱。 <!-- EN --> Training data are nephrectomy wedge biopsies (mostly common diabetic/hypertensive change), excluding core biopsies, rare etiologies and transplant recipients — not the full nephrology spectrum.
- <!-- ZH --> 仅 PAS 染色;倍率与簇数的取舍(可解释性 vs 覆盖度)未充分探索;HPC 解释依赖病理医生事后看图。 <!-- EN --> PAS only; trade-offs in magnification and cluster count (interpretability vs coverage) are under-explored; HPC meaning relies on post hoc pathologist inspection.
- <!-- ZH --> 预后提升幅度不大(c-statistic +0.02 / +0.04),且基于同一 TRIDENT 队列做选择与评估,未见独立外部验证;SNP 分析样本小(N = 183)且只有 3 个位点。 <!-- EN --> Prognostic gains are modest (c-statistic +0.02 / +0.04) and selection and evaluation share the TRIDENT cohort with no independent external validation; the SNP analysis is small (N = 183) with three loci.
- <!-- ZH --> 数据不公开(需向通讯作者申请)。 <!-- EN --> Data are not public (available on request from the corresponding author).

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇不是 anomaly detection,但它是伪影注入课题在**组织学侧的「正常/表型图谱」先例**:不用标注、在大规模肾组织上学出可复现的形态簇,并证明这些簇对应真实病理(纤维化、硬化)。对 detect → model → revert 有三点用处:**(1) 参考分布的构造**——「HPC 组成向量」本身就是一种组织状态的低维表示,若用健康/参考肾组织拟合其分布,偏离即可作为切片或区域级 anomaly score,这是一条简单、可解释的 baseline;**(2) 伪影 vs 疾病**——作者靠人工识别伪影簇来剔除模糊、边缘等 tile,这恰好是伪影注入课题想自动化的步骤:我们的假设是用 H&E 与 Xenium/snRNA 的**跨模态一致性**区分伪影与病变,而本文「人工挑伪影簇」可作为对照;**(3) foundation model 的批次效应**——UNI 簇在同一组织的 PAS 与 H&E 上出现染色与患者特异的批次效应,提醒我们直接用通用病理 FM 的嵌入做异常分数,很可能把染色/批次差异误报为异常,KidneyAnomalyBench 需要专门的「同组织不同染色/批次」对照集。TRIDENT 是糖尿病肾病队列,与 DKD 空间图谱(`dkd-spatial-atlas-nature-2026`)及 KPMP 中的 CKD 活检同属 CKD 范畴,其 IFTA/硬化相关簇可作为「已知异常」的弱标签。对 Agent 可信度课题的关联较弱,唯一的点是:这种 HPC → 临床结局的关联正是 agent 分析肾脏数据时常见的「统计显著但效应小」的结论类型,适合作为 claim 校验的案例。
<!-- EN --> Not an anomaly-detection paper, but it is the artifact-injection topic's **histology-side precedent for a "normal/phenotype atlas"**: label-free, reproducible morphological clusters learned at scale on kidney tissue and shown to map onto real pathology (fibrosis, sclerosis). Three uses along detect → model → revert: **(1) building the reference distribution** — the HPC-composition vector is itself a low-dimensional tissue-state representation; fitting its distribution on healthy/reference kidney makes deviation a slide- or region-level anomaly score — a simple, interpretable baseline; **(2) artifact vs disease** — the authors remove blur/edge tiles by manually spotting artifact clusters, exactly the step the artifact-injection topic wants to automate: our hypothesis is that **cross-modal agreement** between H&E and Xenium/snRNA separates artifacts from lesions, and this manual artifact-cluster step is the comparator; **(3) foundation-model batch effects** — UNI clusters show stain- and patient-specific batch effects on PAS vs H&E of the same tissue, warning that anomaly scores from generic pathology-FM embeddings may report stain/batch differences as anomalies, so KidneyAnomalyBench needs a dedicated "same tissue, different stain/batch" control set. TRIDENT is a diabetic-nephropathy cohort, in the same CKD space as the DKD spatial atlas (`dkd-spatial-atlas-nature-2026`) and KPMP's CKD biopsies; its IFTA/sclerosis-linked clusters can serve as weak labels for "known anomalies". The link to the agent-trustworthiness topic is weak; the one point is that HPC → outcome associations are the typical "significant but small-effect" claims an agent produces on kidney data — good test cases for claim verification.
