## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> MapQC(Theis 组)用健康参照 atlas 自身的**样本间差异作为零分布**,来判断一次 query→reference 映射是否成功:query 的 control 样本应与参照「正常地远」(mapQC 分数 < 2),case 样本应在部分细胞中「异常地远」(> 2)。它能检出 UMAP 看不出来的残留 batch effect,防止把 artifact 报告成疾病状态,而且比重新拿来用的整合指标(LISI、kBET 等)更可解释。
<!-- EN --> MapQC (Theis lab) uses the **healthy reference atlas's own inter-sample variation as a null distribution** to judge whether a query→reference mapping succeeded: query control samples should sit at a "normal" distance from the reference (mapQC score < 2), and case samples should be "abnormally" distant in some cells (> 2). It catches residual batch effects invisible in UMAPs, prevents artifacts from being reported as disease states, and is more interpretable than repurposed integration metrics (LISI, kBET, etc.).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 把疾病/扰动的 query 数据映射到健康参照 atlas(scArches、Symphony、Azimuth 等)已是标准做法,但映射成败事先无法预测(受解离协议、测序平台、映射参数影响)。失败有两种:**欠整合**(残留 batch effect,artifact 被当成疾病状态)与**过整合**(疾病信号被抹掉)。现有指标要么只看 query 结构是否被保留(恰恰与映射目的冲突),要么是 LISI、kBET 之类的整合指标,没有绝对阈值,而且都忽略了**健康人群本身就有样本间差异**这一事实。
<!-- EN --> Mapping disease/perturbation queries onto healthy reference atlases (scArches, Symphony, Azimuth) is standard practice, but success cannot be predicted in advance (dissociation protocol, assay, mapping parameters). Failures come in two kinds: **under-integration** (residual batch effects; artifacts read as disease states) and **over-integration** (disease signal erased). Existing metrics either check preservation of the query's own structure (at odds with the point of mapping) or are integration metrics such as LISI and kBET, which lack absolute thresholds — and all ignore that **healthy populations themselves vary between samples**.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> 两条假设:(A1) 健康参照 atlas 覆盖健康人群的大部分变异;(A2) 疾病至少在部分细胞类型上与健康不同。推出两条可检验的假设:(H1) query control 与参照样本的相似度应等同于参照样本彼此的相似度;(H2) case 样本在某些细胞类型上应比这更不相似。**计算**:在整合后的 embedding 中随机选中心细胞、取大邻域;在每个邻域内,用 **energy distance(E-distance)** 计算样本间距离;样本到参照的距离 = 到所有参照样本距离的均值(参照样本自身计算时排除同一数据集的样本,避免组内偏小);以该邻域内参照样本自身的距离分布为零分布做 **Z-score**(剔除 3.5×IQR 外的离群值);细胞的 mapQC 分数 = 其样本在该细胞所属全部邻域的归一化距离均值。**判读**:> 2 视为远离参照(残留 batch 或参照外的生物状态)。成功映射 = control 大多 < 2 且 case 有部分细胞 > 2;control 也 > 2 → 欠整合;case 与 control 一样近 → 过整合。作者试过 Wasserstein 距离(单次映射 >48 小时,不可行)和平均成对距离(受方差影响),最后选 E-distance。
<!-- EN --> Two assumptions: (A1) the healthy atlas covers most variation in the healthy population; (A2) disease differs from health in at least some cell types. These yield two testable hypotheses: (H1) query controls should be as similar to reference samples as reference samples are to each other; (H2) case samples should be less similar in some cell types. **Computation**: sample random centre cells in the integrated embedding and form large neighbourhoods; within each, compute inter-sample **energy distance (E-distance)**; a sample's distance to the reference is its mean distance to all reference samples (for reference samples, same-dataset samples are excluded to avoid within-dataset bias); **Z-score** against the neighbourhood's null distribution of reference samples' own distances (outliers beyond 3.5×IQR removed); a cell's mapQC score is its sample's mean normalized distance across all neighbourhoods containing it. **Reading**: > 2 means distant from the reference (residual batch or out-of-reference biology). Successful mapping = controls mostly < 2 while some case cells > 2; controls also > 2 → under-integration; cases as close as controls → over-integration. Wasserstein distance (>48 h per mapping, infeasible) and mean pairwise distance (variance-confounded) were tried before settling on E-distance.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> (1) IPF 数据集(5 名 IPF 患者、6 名 control)映射到 Human Lung Cell Atlas(HLCA);(2) Asherman 综合征(AS)子宫内膜数据(6 名健康、9 名 AS 供者)映射到 Human Endometrial Cell Atlas(HECA)。映射工具为 scArches。
<!-- EN --> (1) An IPF dataset (5 IPF patients, 6 controls) mapped to the Human Lung Cell Atlas (HLCA); (2) an Asherman syndrome (AS) endometrial dataset (6 healthy, 9 AS donors) mapped to the Human Endometrial Cell Atlas (HECA). Mapping via scArches.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **IPF → HLCA(默认映射)**:很多 query control 细胞 mapQC > 2,说明残留 batch effect,而 UMAP 上(如血管内皮)看不出来。对内皮细胞做常规聚类会得到一个「疾病特异」簇,但其差异基因主要是实验 artifact:环境 RNA(B 细胞来源的 IGKC、IGHA1)、解离应激(FOSB)、核糖体蛋白(RPS4Y1)。 <!-- EN --> **IPF → HLCA (default mapping)**: many query control cells score > 2, indicating residual batch effects invisible in the UMAP (e.g. blood-vessel endothelium). Standard clustering of endothelial cells yields a "disease-specific" cluster whose DEGs are mostly experimental artifacts: ambient RNA (B-cell-derived IGKC, IGHA1), dissociation stress (FOSB), ribosomal proteins (RPS4Y1).
- <!-- ZH --> **AS → HECA**:93.9% 的 query control 细胞 < 2;但两个以 query 为主的簇(94% 与 97% 来自 query)持续 > 2,其中簇 21 含原论文 74% 的「AS 上皮」细胞。进一步分析显示这些是低质量细胞(总 counts 低、无特异标记、簇 25 MALAT1 低、疾病细胞呈基质 + 上皮双系表达像 doublet)——即**原论文报告的疾病特异状态很可能是低质量细胞/doublet artifact**。 <!-- EN --> **AS → HECA**: 93.9% of query control cells score < 2, but two query-dominated clusters (94% and 97% query cells) stay > 2, and cluster 21 holds 74% of the "AS epithelium" cells reported in the original study. Further analysis shows low-quality cells (low total counts, no specific markers, low MALAT1 in cluster 25, doublet-like stroma + epithelium co-expression in disease cells) — i.e. **the originally reported disease-specific state is likely a low-quality-cell / doublet artifact**.
- <!-- ZH --> **改进映射**:query control 中 mapQC > 2 的比例:原始映射 28.4%;max KL weight 改为 2 → 100%、改为 0.1 → 49.1%(都更差);batch 协变量从 dataset 改成 sample → 17.1%;再找回 69 个因基因注释统一而丢失的输入基因 → 9.3%。此时 IPF 细胞 > 2 的比例为 23.7%(对 control 9.3%)。 <!-- EN --> **Improving the mapping**: fraction of query control cells > 2: original 28.4%; max KL weight 2 → 100%, 0.1 → 49.1% (both worse); batch covariate sample instead of dataset → 17.1%; plus recovering 69 input genes lost during gene-annotation harmonization → 9.3%. In this mapping, 23.7% of IPF cells score > 2 (vs 9.3% of controls).
- <!-- ZH --> **成功映射下的疾病状态**:平滑肌细胞 IPF 26% 对 control 2% 高分,特异基因(IGFBP2、FAM162B、CCL21 等)在其他 IPF 数据集中复现;SPP1hi 肺泡巨噬细胞被正确识别为疾病特异(UMAP 上看不出);原论文报告的 CTHRC1hi 成纤维细胞则接近参照——7 名参照健康供者的 CTHRC1 均值 ≥ IPF 供者,说明更大的健康对照群体能纠正误报。 <!-- EN --> **Disease states in the successful mapping**: smooth muscle cells score high in 26% of IPF vs 2% of control cells, with specific genes (IGFBP2, FAM162B, CCL21, etc.) replicated in other IPF datasets; SPP1hi alveolar macrophages are correctly flagged as disease-specific (invisible in UMAP); the originally reported CTHRC1hi fibroblasts map close to the reference — 7 healthy reference donors have mean CTHRC1 ≥ the IPF donors — showing that a larger healthy population corrects false calls.
- <!-- ZH --> **对比现有指标**:cluster preservation、wiq-kNN-corr、donor LISI 在最好和最差映射上几乎无差别(最差映射中 query control 在 UMAP 上已完全分离,mapQC 判 100% 远离);kBET 两种映射下几乎都是最低分(过敏感);batch LISI 对 query 下采样(1%、10%)极其敏感,mapQC 基本稳定。 <!-- EN --> **Against existing metrics**: cluster preservation, wiq-kNN-corr and donor LISI barely differ between the best and worst mappings (in the worst, query controls separate fully even in the UMAP and mapQC flags 100% as distant); kBET gives near-minimal scores for almost all cells in both (oversensitive); batch LISI shifts dramatically when query cells are downsampled to 1% or 10%, while mapQC stays largely stable.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 第一个把**健康人群自然的样本间差异**显式纳入映射质量评价的指标;有绝对、可解释的阈值(Z > 2)。 <!-- EN --> First mapping-quality metric to explicitly model **natural inter-sample variation in the healthy population**, with an absolute, interpretable threshold (Z > 2).
- <!-- ZH --> 邻域级零分布:不同细胞类型/状态的正常变异幅度不同,局部归一化。 <!-- EN --> Neighbourhood-level nulls: normal variation differs by cell type/state, so normalization is local.
- <!-- ZH --> 用 control 检验(欠整合)和 case 检验(过整合)两个方向同时判定映射成败;与映射方法无关、细胞级分辨率。 <!-- EN --> Control test (under-integration) and case test (over-integration) judge success in both directions; mapping-method-agnostic, cell-level resolution.
- <!-- ZH --> 具体展示了已发表的「疾病特异状态」可能是 artifact(AS 上皮、CTHRC1hi 成纤维细胞)。 <!-- EN --> Concretely shows that published "disease-specific states" can be artifacts (AS epithelium, CTHRC1hi fibroblasts).

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 需要 query 中有与 case 同协议的 control 样本。 <!-- EN --> Requires query control samples generated with the same protocol as the cases.
- <!-- ZH --> 假设参照覆盖了 query 人群的健康变异——只对大规模 atlas 成立;若参照只覆盖部分人群(如仅欧洲血统),会把生物学差异误判为 batch。 <!-- EN --> Assumes the reference covers the query population's healthy variation — only true for large atlases; if the reference covers a subpopulation (e.g. European ancestry only), biology is confused with batch.
- <!-- ZH --> 参照自身若有强 batch effect,会抬高零分布,掩盖同等强度的 query batch effect。 <!-- EN --> Strong batch effects inside the reference inflate the null and mask query batch effects of similar strength.
- <!-- ZH --> 假设 query 中 control 与 case 的 batch effect 程度相同;完全混杂时任何数据驱动指标都无解。 <!-- EN --> Assumes control and case batch effects in the query are of similar size; full confounding cannot be solved by any data-driven metric.
- <!-- ZH --> 只有两个案例(肺、子宫内膜),Z>2 阈值依赖近似高斯假设;预印本,未经同行评审。 <!-- EN --> Only two case studies (lung, endometrium); the Z>2 threshold relies on an approximately Gaussian null; preprint, not peer reviewed.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这篇是 **伪影注入课题最关键的「artifact vs 疾病」工具之一**,也是 Dann et al.(`healthy-ref-natgenet-2023`)ACR 设计的直接延续:mapQC 本质上是一个「以健康 atlas 为零分布的样本级异常分数」,> 2 就是异常——而本文最重要的贡献是指出**这个异常可能来自 batch / 环境 RNA / 解离应激 / doublet,而不一定是疾病**,并给出 control 检验来区分。伪影注入课题可以直接把待测肾样本的 snRNA 映射到 Lake 肾脏 atlas / KPMP 参照,用 mapQC 检查 control(如 KPMP 参照组织或其他正常肾组织)是否正常;本文 IPF 内皮簇的 artifact 基因清单(IGKC、IGHA1、FOSB、RPS4Y1)和 AS 低质量细胞特征(低 counts、低 MALAT1、双系共表达)可以直接成为 KidneyAnomalyBench 的 **artifact 类别**与负样本标签。与跨模态一致性结合:一个 snRNA 中 mapQC > 2 的群体,如果在 snATAC / Xenium 中找不到对应异常,就更可能是 artifact。对 **Agent 可信度课题**:「已发表的疾病特异状态其实是 artifact」正是 claim-to-evidence 核查要抓的东西——agent 声称发现疾病状态时,verifier 应要求 mapQC 式的 control 检验证据;mapQC 的 Z 分数也是天然可校准的置信度信号,可作为 calibrated verifier 的输入特征。
<!-- EN --> This is **one of the artifact-injection topic's key "artifact vs disease" tools** and a direct continuation of the ACR design in Dann et al. (`healthy-ref-natgenet-2023`): mapQC is essentially a sample-level anomaly score with the healthy atlas as its null (> 2 = anomalous), and its main contribution is to show that **such an anomaly can come from batch / ambient RNA / dissociation stress / doublets rather than disease**, with the control test to tell them apart. The artifact-injection topic can map the snRNA of the kidney samples to be tested onto the Lake kidney atlas / KPMP reference and use mapQC to check that controls (KPMP reference tissue or other normal kidney tissue) look normal; this paper's artifact gene list from the IPF endothelial cluster (IGKC, IGHA1, FOSB, RPS4Y1) and the low-quality-cell signatures from AS (low counts, low MALAT1, dual-lineage co-expression) can become KidneyAnomalyBench's **artifact classes** and negative labels. Combined with cross-modal agreement: a population with mapQC > 2 in snRNA but no matching anomaly in snATAC / Xenium is more likely an artifact. For **the agent-trustworthiness topic**: "a published disease-specific state was actually an artifact" is exactly what claim-to-evidence verification should catch — when an agent claims a disease state, the verifier should demand mapQC-style control-test evidence; mapQC's Z-score is also a naturally calibratable confidence signal and a candidate input feature for a calibrated verifier.
