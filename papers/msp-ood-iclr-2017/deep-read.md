## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> OOD 检测领域的起点论文:训练好的分类器给出的**最大 softmax 概率(MSP)**本身不能当作置信度(MNIST 分类器对高斯噪声也给出 91% 的「置信度」),但正确分类样本的 MSP 统计上高于错分样本和分布外样本,所以把 MSP 当阈值分数,就能在视觉、NLP、语音任务上检出错误和 OOD;作者同时定义了一套标准任务与指标(AUROC + 两种 AUPR),并用一个「辅助解码器 + 异常模块」说明这个基线可以被超越。
<!-- EN --> The founding paper of OOD detection: a trained classifier's **maximum softmax probability (MSP)** is not a usable confidence on its own (an MNIST classifier assigns 91% "confidence" to Gaussian noise), but correctly classified examples have statistically higher MSP than misclassified and out-of-distribution ones, so thresholding MSP detects errors and OOD inputs across vision, NLP and speech. The authors also define standard tasks and metrics (AUROC plus two AUPRs) and show with an "auxiliary decoder + abnormality module" that the baseline can be beaten.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 分类器在训练与测试分布不一致时往往**静默失败**:高置信度地给出错误答案。作者举的例子就是医学诊断——模型对困难样本也高置信度输出,而本应把它们交给人工。两个相关问题:(1) **错误/成功预测**:能否预测分类器在某个测试样本上会出错;(2) **分布内/分布外检测**:能否判断测试样本是否来自与训练数据不同的分布。softmax 用指数函数,logit 的小变化就会引起输出分布的大变化,所以很少对 OOD 输入给出均匀分布。
<!-- EN --> Classifiers deployed under train–test distribution mismatch often **fail silently**, producing confident wrong answers; the authors' own example is medical diagnosis, where a model confidently classifies hard cases that should be flagged for a human. Two related problems: (1) **error/success prediction** — can we predict whether the classifier will be wrong on a given test example; (2) **in-/out-of-distribution detection** — can we tell whether a test example comes from a different distribution than the training data. Because softmax uses the fast-growing exponential, small logit changes cause large output changes, so softmax rarely outputs a uniform distribution on OOD inputs.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **基线**:对每个测试样本取预测类别的 softmax 概率(即 MSP)作为分数。错误检测时把正确/错误样本分开看;OOD 检测时把分布内测试集当作正类、OOD 样本当作负类(或取负号后把异常当正类)。作者也试过 softmax 分布与均匀分布之间的 KL 散度,结果与 MSP 高度相关。**评估**:强调不能用准确率,而用阈值无关的 **AUROC**,以及 **AUPR**——并且分别报告把「成功/分布内」当正类(AUPR Succ / AUPR In)与把「错误/分布外」当正类(AUPR Err / AUPR Out)两种情况,同时列出随机检测器的基线值(Base),因为 AUPR 依赖正类比例。所有 AUROC 与随机基线的差异经 Wilcoxon 秩和检验都高度显著。语音任务(CTC)中计算 softmax 时**去掉 blank 符号的 logit**,否则大多数时间步都高置信度地预测 blank。**异常模块(abnormality module)**:在分类器上接一个重构输入的辅助解码器,与分类器在分布内数据上联合训练;然后冻结,另训练一个小网络(sigmoid 输出),用干净样本与**人工加噪样本**(白噪声、棕噪声、粉噪声;MNIST 用模糊、旋转、高斯噪声)区分「正常/异常」。
<!-- EN --> **Baseline**: for each test example take the softmax probability of the predicted class (MSP) as the score. For error detection, correct vs misclassified examples are compared; for OOD detection the in-distribution test set is the positive class and OOD examples the negative class (or scores are negated to treat anomalies as positive). KL divergence of the softmax from uniform was also tried and was highly correlated with MSP. **Evaluation**: accuracy is rejected in favour of threshold-free **AUROC** and **AUPR**, reported both with success/in-distribution as positive (AUPR Succ / AUPR In) and with error/OOD as positive (AUPR Err / AUPR Out), alongside a random-detector "Base" value because AUPR depends on the positive-class rate. All AUROCs differ from random with high significance (Wilcoxon rank-sum). For speech (CTC), softmax is computed **ignoring the blank symbol's logit**, which otherwise dominates most time steps. **Abnormality module**: an auxiliary decoder reconstructing the input is trained jointly with the classifier on in-distribution data; these layers are then frozen and a small sigmoid-output network is trained to separate clean examples from **synthetically noised** ones (white/brown/pink noise for TIMIT; blur, rotation, Gaussian noise for MNIST).

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> **范式:基于分类器的 OOD(classifier-based OOD)**。它直接建立在「softmax 置信度不可靠」这类经验观察之上(Nguyen 等),是此后几乎所有 OOD 论文的默认基线:ODIN(温度缩放 + 输入扰动)、Mahalanobis(`mahalanobis-ood-neurips-2018`,改在特征空间做密度)、energy score(`energy-ood-neurips-2020`,证明 log MSP 是被平移过的 free energy)、Outlier Exposure(Hendrycks 自己的后续工作)以及 OpenOOD 基准(`generalized-ood-survey-ijcv-2024`,把 MSP 作为领域起点)。论文第 4 节的异常模块——用人工噪声当负样本训练「正常 vs 异常」判别器——是后来**合成异常 / 自监督**一支(如 DRAEM)的早期雏形;辅助解码器则带有**重构**范式的味道。
<!-- EN --> **Paradigm: classifier-based OOD.** It builds directly on empirical observations that softmax confidence is unreliable (Nguyen et al.), and became the default baseline of nearly every later OOD paper: ODIN (temperature scaling + input perturbation), Mahalanobis (`mahalanobis-ood-neurips-2018`, moving to feature-space density), the energy score (`energy-ood-neurips-2020`, which shows log-MSP is a shifted free energy), Outlier Exposure (Hendrycks' own follow-up), and the OpenOOD benchmark (`generalized-ood-survey-ijcv-2024`, which names MSP as the field's origin). The Section-4 abnormality module — a "normal vs abnormal" discriminator trained with synthetic noise as negatives — is an early form of the later **synthetic-anomaly / self-supervised** branch (e.g. DRAEM), and the auxiliary decoder has a **reconstruction** flavour.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **视觉**:MNIST(三层 256 宽全连接网络,GELU)、CIFAR-10/100(40-4 Wide ResNet);OOD 用 SUN 场景、Omniglot、notMNIST、黑白 CIFAR-10、高斯与均匀噪声。**NLP**:IMDB 情感分类(OOD:Customer Review、Movie Review);20 Newsgroups / Reuters 8 / Reuters 52 文本分类(留出部分类别作 OOD:15/5、6/2、40/12);WSJ 与 Twitter 词性标注(OOD:推文、English Web Treebank weblog)。**语音**:TIMIT(双向 LSTM + CTC),OOD 为叠加 Aurora-2 噪声(机场、嘈杂人声、汽车等,噪声音量 30%,平均 SNR≈5)与中文语料 THCHS-30。
<!-- EN --> **Vision**: MNIST (3-layer, 256-wide MLP, GELU) and CIFAR-10/100 (40-4 Wide ResNet); OOD = SUN scenes, Omniglot, notMNIST, greyscale CIFAR-10, Gaussian and uniform noise. **NLP**: IMDB sentiment (OOD: Customer Review, Movie Review); 20 Newsgroups / Reuters 8 / Reuters 52 topic classification with held-out subjects as OOD (15/5, 6/2, 40/12); WSJ and Twitter POS tagging (OOD: tweets, English Web Treebank weblogs). **Speech**: TIMIT (bidirectional LSTM + CTC), OOD = TIMIT mixed with Aurora-2 noises (airport, babble, car, …; 30% noise volume, mean SNR ≈ 5) and the Chinese THCHS-30 corpus.

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> - **错误检测**(AUROC/随机基线 50):MNIST 97、CIFAR-10 93、CIFAR-100 87;错分样本的平均 MSP 仍高达 86% / 80% / 66%——说明 MSP 单独看是误导性的置信度。IMDB 82;15 Newsgroups 89、Reuters 6 89、Reuters 40 91;WSJ 96、Twitter 89。
- **OOD 检测**(AUROC):CIFAR-10/SUN 95、CIFAR-10/高斯 97、CIFAR-100/SUN 91、CIFAR-100/高斯 88;MNIST/Omniglot 96、/notMNIST 85、/均匀噪声 99。IMDB/All 94;15/5 Newsgroups 75(最难)、Reuters40/12 95;WSJ/Twitter 80、WSJ/Weblog 61(weblog 与新闻文体接近,几乎检不出);TIMIT 加噪 98–100、TIMIT/中文 85。
- **异常模块**:TIMIT(全连接网络,加噪后帧错误率只从 29.69% 升到 30.43%,softmax 几乎失效)平均 AUROC 从 softmax 的 80 提升到 97;MNIST 平均 AUROC 从 93 提升到 100。注意异常模块训练时用的噪声类型与测试时的失真不同,仍能泛化。
<!-- EN --> - **Error detection** (AUROC, random = 50): MNIST 97, CIFAR-10 93, CIFAR-100 87, while misclassified examples still have mean MSP of 86% / 80% / 66% — MSP alone is a misleading confidence. IMDB 82; 15 Newsgroups 89, Reuters 6 89, Reuters 40 91; WSJ 96, Twitter 89.
- **OOD detection** (AUROC): CIFAR-10/SUN 95, CIFAR-10/Gaussian 97, CIFAR-100/SUN 91, CIFAR-100/Gaussian 88; MNIST/Omniglot 96, /notMNIST 85, /Uniform 99. IMDB/All 94; 15/5 Newsgroups 75 (hardest), Reuters40/12 95; WSJ/Twitter 80, WSJ/Weblog 61 (weblogs are stylistically close to newswire and nearly undetectable); noised TIMIT 98–100, TIMIT/Chinese 85.
- **Abnormality module**: on TIMIT with a fully connected net (noise only raises frame error from 29.69% to 30.43%, so softmax barely helps) mean AUROC rises from 80 (softmax) to 97; on MNIST from 93 to 100. The module was trained on noise types different from the test distortions and still generalized.

## 创新点 / Contributions
<!-- ZH --> (1) 把「错误检测」与「OOD 检测」形式化为两个标准任务,并提出简单而跨领域有效的 MSP 基线;(2) 确立评估规范:AUROC + 正负两种 AUPR + 随机基线 + 报告底层分类器准确率(作者指出:一个总是错的分类器在以错误为正类时能拿到最大 AUPR);(3) 建议在多种任务与架构上测试,而不只在视觉上;(4) 用异常模块证明基线可被超越,开启后续研究。
<!-- EN --> (1) Formalizes error detection and OOD detection as two standard tasks with a simple, cross-domain MSP baseline; (2) sets the evaluation norm: AUROC + both AUPRs + random baselines + reporting the underlying classifier's accuracy (an always-wrong classifier gets maximal AUPR with errors as positives); (3) urges testing across tasks and architectures, not vision only; (4) shows via the abnormality module that the baseline can be beaten, opening the field.

## 局限 / Limitations
<!-- ZH --> MSP 必须有一个训练好的分类器,且只看输出层——远离决策边界的 OOD 样本也可能拿到高 MSP(后续 Mahalanobis/energy 论文正是从这里切入)。近 OOD(文体接近的 weblog、相近新闻主题)效果差(AUROC 61–75)。早期 OOD 数据集可能含有与 ID 类别重叠的图像,导致评估不准(survey 明确指出了这一点)。异常模块依赖人工噪声类型的选择,只在 TIMIT 和 MNIST 上展示。所有实验都是 2016 年左右的小模型。
<!-- EN --> MSP needs a trained classifier and only reads the output layer — OOD points far from the decision boundary can still get high MSP (the opening that the Mahalanobis and energy papers exploit). Near-OOD (stylistically similar weblogs, related news topics) is weak (AUROC 61–75). Early OOD sets may contain images of ID classes, distorting evaluation (the survey flags exactly this). The abnormality module depends on the chosen synthetic noise and is shown only on TIMIT and MNIST. All experiments use small circa-2016 models.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> **映射到肾脏组学**:样本 = 单个细胞或 Visium/Xenium spot;「分类器」= 在健康参照(KPMP / Lake 图谱 / HuBMAP 正常肾)上训练的细胞类型注释器(scANVI、CellTypist 或 scVI latent 上的 MLP),H&E 侧则是 patch 级组织分类器;MSP 低 = 「这个细胞不像参照中任何一类」,即候选异常/新状态。这是伪影注入课题里最便宜的 detect 基线,任何注释流程都免费给出。**哪些假设会断**:(1) **连续疾病状态**——肾损伤是 PT → aPT/injured PT → 失败修复的连续谱,中间态在两类之间,MSP 低是因为**类别歧义**而非新颖,MSP 把 aleatoric 歧义和真正的 OOD 混在一起;(2) **doublet** 天然是两类的混合,MSP 低——会被当成「异常」,其实是技术 artifact;(3) **环境 RNA / 质量差的细胞**同样拉低 MSP;(4) **批次效应**改变 logit 尺度,新 donor 的正常细胞也可能 MSP 偏低。因此 MSP 单独使用会把 artifact 报成疾病——这正是伪影注入课题用**跨模态一致性**(snRNA 与 snATAC、Xenium 与 H&E 是否同时报警)来区分 artifact 与疾病的动机;MSP 可作为每个模态内部的基线分数。**可直接借用的部分**:本文的评估规范——AUROC + AUPR-Out(异常为正类,因为异常稀少)+ 随机基线 + 报告注释器准确率——应原样写进 KidneyAnomalyBench 的评测协议;「错误检测」半边也直接对应「标出注释器可能注错的细胞」。**仓库里已有的相关做法**:`conformal-scrna-bioinformatics-2025` 明确批评「对 softmax 分数设阈值」没有保证,改用 conformal p 值;`histo-nejmai-2024` 的最佳方案是 Outlier Exposure 二分类器给出的异常类概率(MSP 思路的训练版);`scatac-novel-genomeres-2026`(CARA)做「参照外」新类型检测。**对 Agent 可信度课题**:MSP 是「用模型自身置信度当 verifier 信号」的原型,其核心教训——原始置信度不校准(噪声也 91%)——同样适用于 agent / LLM 的自报置信度,支持 Agent 可信度课题需要**单独校准的 verifier**,而不是相信 agent 的自信度。
<!-- EN --> **Mapping onto kidney omics**: samples = single cells or Visium/Xenium spots; the "classifier" = a cell-type annotator trained on a healthy reference (KPMP / the Lake atlas / HuBMAP normal kidney) — scANVI, CellTypist, or an MLP on the scVI latent — or a patch-level tissue classifier on H&E. Low MSP = "this cell resembles no reference class", i.e. a candidate anomaly / novel state. It is the cheapest Topic-1 detect baseline, available for free from any annotation pipeline. **Which assumptions break**: (1) **continuous disease states** — kidney injury is a continuum PT → aPT/injured PT → failed repair; intermediate cells sit between classes, so low MSP reflects **class ambiguity**, not novelty — MSP conflates aleatoric ambiguity with genuine OOD; (2) **doublets** are mixtures of two classes and get low MSP — flagged as "anomalous" although they are technical artifacts; (3) **ambient RNA / low-quality cells** also depress MSP; (4) **batch effects** rescale logits, so normal cells from a new donor can score low. Used alone, MSP will report artifacts as disease — precisely why the artifact-injection topic uses **cross-modal agreement** (do snRNA and snATAC, or Xenium and H&E, both fire?) to separate artifacts from disease; MSP serves as a per-modality baseline score. **Directly reusable**: the paper's evaluation norm — AUROC + AUPR-Out (anomaly as positive, since anomalies are rare) + random baselines + reporting annotator accuracy — should go verbatim into the KidneyAnomalyBench protocol; the error-detection half maps to "flag cells the annotator likely mislabelled". **Repo papers already adapting the idea**: `conformal-scrna-bioinformatics-2025` explicitly criticizes thresholding softmax scores as guarantee-free and uses conformal p-values instead; `histo-nejmai-2024`'s best method is the anomaly-class probability of an Outlier Exposure binary classifier (a trained version of the MSP idea); `scatac-novel-genomeres-2026` (CARA) flags out-of-reference novel types. **For the agent-trustworthiness topic**: MSP is the archetype of "use the model's own confidence as the verifier signal", and its core lesson — raw confidence is uncalibrated (91% on noise) — carries over to agents' / LLMs' self-reported confidence, supporting the agent-trustworthiness topic's need for a **separately calibrated verifier** rather than trusting agent confidence.
