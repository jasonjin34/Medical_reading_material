## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 10 个主流 LLM 生成的 4,900 份科学摘要显示,多数模型会把原文「有限定的结论」改写成**更宽泛的泛化**(去掉量词变成泛指、过去时变现在时、描述变建议);LLM 文章摘要含泛化结论的几率是 NEJM Journal Watch 人类专家摘要的 **4.85 倍**;反直觉的是,**明确要求「不要引入不准确」反而让泛化翻倍**,而且**新模型比旧模型更严重**(Claude 系列是例外)。
<!-- EN --> Across 4,900 summaries from 10 prominent LLMs, most models rewrite restricted scientific conclusions into **broader generalizations** (dropping quantifiers into generics, past into present tense, descriptions into recommendations). LLM article summaries are **4.85× as likely** as expert NEJM Journal Watch summaries to contain generalized conclusions; counter-intuitively, **explicitly asking for accuracy doubles the effect**, and **newer models are worse than older ones** (Claude being the exception).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 科研人员与公众越来越多地用 LLM 聊天机器人总结科学文献。除了常见的捏造,一个更隐蔽的风险是**省略限定语与不确定性**,导致结论范围被放大(例如把「在 26 周内、对正在用或不用二甲双胍/基础胰岛素的 2 型糖尿病青少年、每周 0.75 或 1.5 mg 度拉糖肽优于安慰剂」概括为「度拉糖肽是青少年 2 型糖尿病安全有效的治疗选择」)。这在临床场景可能导致不当处方。三个问题:(1) 算法性过度泛化是否存在?(2) 强调系统性或准确性的 prompt 能否缓解?(3) LLM 与人类科学传播者相比是否更容易过度泛化?
<!-- EN --> Researchers and the public increasingly use LLM chatbots to summarize science. Beyond outright fabrication, a subtler risk is **omitting qualifiers and uncertainty** so that conclusions are broadened — e.g. turning "dulaglutide at a once-weekly dose of 0.75 or 1.5 mg was superior to placebo in improving glycaemic control through 26 weeks among youths with type 2 diabetes treated with or without metformin or basal insulin" into "dulaglutide is an effective and safe treatment option for improving glycaemic control in youths with type 2 diabetes". In clinical settings this can drive inappropriate prescribing. Three questions: (1) do algorithmic overgeneralizations occur? (2) can prompts emphasizing systematic or accurate processing mitigate them? (3) do LLMs overgeneralize more than human science communicators?

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **三类泛化编码**:(1) **泛指(generics)**——主语无量词、用现在时把结果套到整个类别(「父母温暖具有保护作用」);(2) **现在时泛化**——把过去时的样本结论变成现在时;(3) **行动指导泛化**——把描述性结果变成建议(「应推荐 CBT」)。含任一者为「generalized」,否则为「restricted」。**过度泛化**定义:总体层面(LLM 摘要中泛化结论多于原文)与具体层面(某原文无泛化而其摘要有)。以「忠实于原文」为规范基准,不评判原文泛化本身是否有据。**设计**:预注册(OSF),三种 prompt(简单 / 「深呼吸一步步来」系统型 / 「不要引入任何不准确」准确型),温度 0 vs 0.7 vs UI 默认,部分模型重复测试 2–3 次;UI 采集用新账号或关闭记忆。**分析**:二项分布 logit 链接的 GLMM,控制温度、prompt 与测试轮次。两名语料分析与科学传播专家按预注册标准编码,第三名盲评研究者复核 100 篇,κ=0.79–0.95。
<!-- EN --> **Three generalization codes**: (1) **generics** — present-tense claims with no quantifier in the subject that extend results to whole categories ("parental warmth is protective"); (2) **present-tense generalizations** — past-tense sample findings rendered in present tense; (3) **action-guiding generalizations** — descriptive results turned into recommendations ("CBT should be recommended"). Any of these marks a text "generalized", otherwise "restricted". **Overgeneralization**: overall (more generalized LLM summaries than generalized originals) and specific (an original without generalization whose summary has one). Faithfulness to the original is the normative standard; whether the original's generalizations are warranted is not assessed. **Design**: preregistered on OSF; three prompts (simple / "take a deep breath and work step by step" systematic / "do not introduce any inaccuracies" accuracy); temperature 0 vs 0.7 vs UI default; some models retested 2–3 times; UI collection via fresh accounts or memory off. **Analysis**: binomial GLMMs with logit link controlling for temperature, prompt and test round. Two experts in corpus analysis and science communication coded with preregistered criteria; a third, source-blinded researcher recoded 100 texts (κ = 0.79–0.95).

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **原文**:200 篇摘要 —— 100 篇来自四大综合医学期刊(Lancet、NEJM、JAMA、BMJ),100 篇来自四本综合科学期刊(Nature、Science、Nature Human Behaviour、PSPI),每刊最近 25 篇;另 100 篇四大医学期刊的**前瞻性临床研究全文**及其对应的 NEJM Journal Watch 专家摘要。**模型**(10 个):GPT-3.5 Turbo、GPT-4 Turbo(API 与 UI)、LLaMA 2 70B、Claude 2(2024-01 起),ChatGPT-4o、ChatGPT-4.5、LLaMA 3.3 70B、Claude 3.5 Sonnet、Claude 3.7 Sonnet、DeepSeek(2025-03)。共 4,900 份摘要(4,300 份摘要级 + 600 份全文级)。
<!-- EN --> **Source texts**: 200 abstracts — 100 from the top general medical journals (Lancet, NEJM, JAMA, BMJ) and 100 from multidisciplinary science journals (Nature, Science, Nature Human Behaviour, PSPI), the 25 most recent per journal; plus 100 full-length **prospective clinical study** articles from the four medical journals with their NEJM Journal Watch expert summaries. **Models** (10): GPT-3.5 Turbo, GPT-4 Turbo (API and UI), LLaMA 2 70B, Claude 2 (from January 2024); ChatGPT-4o, ChatGPT-4.5, LLaMA 3.3 70B, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, DeepSeek (March 2025). 4,900 summaries total (4,300 abstract-level + 600 article-level).

## 主要结果 / Key results
<!-- ZH --> **普遍存在**:所有 LLM 合并后,摘要含泛化结论的几率约为原文摘要的 **2 倍**(B=0.693,p<0.001);10 个模型中 6 个显著高于原文。旧模型 GPT-4 Turbo、LLaMA 2 70B 约 **2.6×**,新模型 ChatGPT-4o 约 **9×**、LLaMA 3.3 70B 约 **39×**;GPT-3.5 Turbo 与 Claude 2/3.5/3.7 与原文无显著差异。总体过度泛化比例:ChatGPT-4o 45%–60%、LLaMA 3.3 70B 69%–73%、DeepSeek 26%–67%,Claude −1% 至 20%;具体过度泛化 ChatGPT-4o 最高 61%、LLaMA 3.3 70B 最高 73%;最常见的转变是**量化泛化 → 泛指**。**温度**:温度 0 比 0.7 产生泛化结论的可能性低 **76%**。**Prompt 适得其反**:系统型 prompt 无显著影响,准确型 prompt 让泛化几率翻倍(OR=1.90,95% CI [1.11, 3.26],p=0.02),除 Claude 外的模型都在准确型 prompt 下过度泛化最多(作者类比心理学「讽刺性反弹」)。**重复测试稳定**:测试轮次无显著影响(p=0.57)—— 是稳定的「算法偏差」而非随机噪声。**人机对比**:NEJM JW 摘要与原文无显著差异;LLM 文章摘要比原文高 **6.72 倍**(95% CI [4.22, 10.71]),比 NEJM JW 高 **4.85 倍**(95% CI [3.06, 7.70],p<0.001)。
<!-- EN --> **Pervasive**: all LLMs combined are about **twice** as likely as original abstracts to contain generalized conclusions (B = 0.693, p < 0.001); 6 of 10 models differ significantly from originals. Older GPT-4 Turbo and LLaMA 2 70B ~**2.6×**; newer ChatGPT-4o ~**9×** and LLaMA 3.3 70B ~**39×**; GPT-3.5 Turbo and Claude 2/3.5/3.7 do not differ significantly. Overall overgeneralization: ChatGPT-4o 45–60%, LLaMA 3.3 70B 69–73%, DeepSeek 26–67%, Claude −1% to 20%; specific overgeneralization peaks at 61% (ChatGPT-4o) and 73% (LLaMA 3.3 70B); the most frequent transition is **quantified → generic**. **Temperature**: temperature 0 makes generalized conclusions **76% less likely** than 0.7. **Prompts backfire**: the systematic prompt has no significant effect; the accuracy prompt doubles the odds (OR = 1.90, 95% CI [1.11, 3.26], p = 0.02), and every model except Claude overgeneralizes most under it (the authors liken it to psychological "ironic rebound"). **Stable on retest**: test round has no significant effect (p = 0.57) — a stable algorithmic bias rather than noise. **Human vs LLM**: NEJM JW summaries do not differ from originals; LLM article summaries are **6.72×** as likely as originals (95% CI [4.22, 10.71]) and **4.85×** as likely as NEJM JW (95% CI [3.06, 7.70], p < 0.001) to contain generalized conclusions.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 首次系统量化 LLM 科学摘要中的**范围放大**——一种不同于捏造的、更隐蔽的「幻觉」。 <!-- EN --> First systematic quantification of **scope inflation** in LLM science summaries — a subtler "hallucination" distinct from fabrication.
- <!-- ZH --> 可操作的三特征编码框架(泛指/现在时/行动指导)与总体/具体过度泛化分数,可作为任何新模型的基准方法。 <!-- EN --> An operational three-feature coding scheme (generic / present tense / action-guiding) with overall and specific overgeneralization scores, usable as a benchmark for any new model.
- <!-- ZH --> 揭示「准确型 prompt 反作用」与「新模型更差」两个反直觉现象,并提出 RLHF 偏好自信、广泛适用答案的机制假说。 <!-- EN --> Reveals two counter-intuitive effects — accuracy prompts backfire, newer models are worse — with an RLHF-prefers-confident-broad-answers hypothesis.
- <!-- ZH --> 预注册设计、盲评复核、GLMM 统计,方法学严谨。 <!-- EN --> Preregistered design, blinded recoding and GLMM statistics — methodologically rigorous.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 只测了三种 prompt;未让 LLM 扮演特定角色(专家/媒体办公室)。 <!-- EN --> Only three prompts; LLMs were not assigned roles (expert, media office).
- <!-- ZH --> 人机比较只覆盖 4 个模型与 NEJM JW 一类专家作者,不代表所有人类科学传播者。 <!-- EN --> Human–LLM comparison covers only four models and NEJM JW-style expert writers, not all human communicators.
- <!-- ZH --> 以医学文本为主;不同学科(基础 vs 应用)的泛化倾向可能不同。 <!-- EN --> Mostly medical texts; tendencies may differ by field (foundational vs applied).
- <!-- ZH --> 只关注过度泛化(也存在欠泛化,但更少);不评判原文泛化是否有据;模型快照随版本迭代而过时。 <!-- EN --> Focuses on overgeneralization (undergeneralization exists but is rarer); does not judge whether originals' generalizations are warranted; model snapshots age quickly.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 对 **Plan B** 而言,这篇定义了一种**最容易被 claim-to-evidence 核验漏掉的失效**:结论在内容上「被支持」,但**范围被放大了**。组学 agent 的典型版本是:「在 3 例 DKD 活检的 PT 细胞中观察到 HAVCR1 上调」被写成「HAVCR1 上调是 DKD 的特征」甚至「应以 HAVCR1 为干预靶点」—— NLI 式核验通常会判为蕴含。因此 Plan B 的 omics failure taxonomy 应单列 **scope inflation** 一类,并直接借用本文三特征作为检测规则:(1) 量词/样本限定消失(n、供体数、细胞类型、区域);(2) 时态/普适化;(3) 从关联跳到行动建议(这一条与 detect → model → **revert** 的最后一步直接相关 —— 把「相关」写成「可逆转的靶点」正是最需要卡住的过度泛化)。另外三点可直接用于实验设计:**温度 0 显著降低泛化**(agent 评测要固定并报告温度);**「请准确」类 prompt 可能反作用**(不能把 prompt 级劝告当作缓解措施);**偏差在重复运行中稳定** —— 说明 rerun stability 低不代表没问题,稳定的系统性偏差同样需要单独度量。对 Plan A,它提醒我们:异常检测结论在写入 KidneyAnomalyBench 标注或论文时,也要保留样本与模态限定。
<!-- EN --> For **Plan B**, this defines **the failure most likely to slip past claim-to-evidence verification**: a conclusion that is "supported" in content but **inflated in scope**. The typical omics-agent version: "HAVCR1 up-regulation observed in PT cells of 3 DKD biopsies" becomes "HAVCR1 up-regulation is a hallmark of DKD" or even "HAVCR1 should be targeted" — which NLI-style verification usually marks as entailed. Plan B's omics failure taxonomy should therefore list **scope inflation** as its own category, borrowing the three features as detection rules: (1) quantifiers/sample restrictions disappear (n, donors, cell type, region); (2) tense/universalization; (3) jumping from association to action — directly relevant to the last step of detect → model → **revert**, where writing "associated" as "a reversible target" is exactly the overgeneralization to stop. Three points apply directly to experimental design: **temperature 0 markedly reduces generalization** (fix and report temperature in agent evaluations); **"be accurate" prompts can backfire** (prompt-level exhortation is not mitigation); **the bias is stable across reruns** — high rerun stability does not mean correctness, so stable systematic bias needs its own measurement. For Plan A, it is a reminder that anomaly-detection conclusions written into KidneyAnomalyBench labels or papers must keep their sample and modality qualifiers.
