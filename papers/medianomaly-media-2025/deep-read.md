## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> HKUST 的 Cai、Chen、Cheng 等搭建了医学图像 anomaly detection 的统一基准 MedIAnomaly:7 个数据集、5 种模态(胸片、脑 MRI、眼底、皮肤镜、病理)、30 种方法,在**统一网络结构**下比较图像级分类与像素级分割,并逐个拆解关键组件(latent 大小、重建距离函数、梯度分数、DDPM 噪声类型、ImageNet 权重),给出「朴素 AE 是强基线、ImageNet 特征出奇地强、重建质量与检测性能弱相关」等结论。
<!-- EN --> Cai, Chen, Cheng et al. (HKUST) build MedIAnomaly, a unified medical-image AD benchmark — 7 datasets, 5 modalities (chest X-ray, brain MRI, fundus, dermoscopy, histopathology), 30 methods — comparing image-level classification and pixel-level segmentation under **unified architectures**, and dissecting key components one by one (latent size, reconstruction distance, gradient scores, DDPM noise type, ImageNet weights). Headline findings: a plain AE is a strong baseline, ImageNet features are surprisingly strong, and reconstruction quality correlates only weakly with detection performance.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 医学 AD 方法很多,但缺乏公平、全面的对比:(1) 数据集与划分各不相同,有的数据集太简单——作者自己实现的朴素 AE 在 Hyper-Kvasir 与 OCT2017 上就几乎满分,说明在这些数据上「超越 SOTA」并不说明问题;(2) 同一范式的方法用了不同网络结构和训练技巧(如 f-AnoGAN 用残差块、AE-Flow 用 Wide ResNet-50-2、AE-U 用普通卷积),比较的其实是工程而非方法。已有综述要么不做实验,要么只覆盖单模态或单一范式,要么没有拆解组件。
<!-- EN --> Medical AD has many methods but no fair, comprehensive comparison: (1) datasets and splits differ, and some datasets are too easy — the authors' plain AE is near-perfect on Hyper-Kvasir and OCT2017, so "beating SOTA" there means little; (2) methods of the same paradigm use different architectures and tricks (f-AnoGAN residual blocks, AE-Flow Wide ResNet-50-2, AE-U plain convs), so comparisons measure engineering rather than method. Prior surveys either run no experiments, cover one modality or one paradigm, or do not dissect components.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **分类法**:重建类(图像重建:AE、VAE、MemAE、Constrained AE、AE-U、DAE、f-AnoGAN、GANomaly、AnoDDPM、AutoDDPM 等;特征重建:FAE 等)、自监督类(一阶段:CutPaste、FPI、NSA、PII、AnatPaste 等合成异常分类/分割;两阶段:先在合成异常上训练再去掉头、在表示上拟合 Gaussian density estimator)、特征参照类(知识蒸馏、特征建模,如 ReContrast、PANDA、MSC)。**统一实现**:所有重建法尽量共用同一个 AE(64×64 输入、4 个编码块、latent 16);所有 SSL 法共用 ResNet18 特征提取器(224×224 输入)。另把「冻结的 ImageNet ResNet18/ResNet152 + 一类分类器」作为无特殊设计的对照。**指标**:图像级 AUC 与 AP;像素级 AP 与数据集级最优 Dice(在测试集最佳阈值处计算,规避阈值选择);刻意不用像素级 AUC(对假阳性不敏感)。每个实验 3 个随机种子。
<!-- EN --> **Taxonomy**: reconstruction (image reconstruction: AE, VAE, MemAE, Constrained AE, AE-U, DAE, f-AnoGAN, GANomaly, AnoDDPM, AutoDDPM…; feature reconstruction: FAE…), self-supervised (one-stage: synthetic-anomaly classification/segmentation such as CutPaste, FPI, NSA, PII, AnatPaste; two-stage: train on synthetic anomalies, drop the head, fit a Gaussian density estimator on the representation) and feature-reference methods (knowledge distillation, feature modelling — ReContrast, PANDA, MSC). **Unified implementation**: all reconstruction methods share one AE where possible (64×64 input, four encoder blocks, latent 16); all SSL methods share a ResNet18 extractor (224×224). A frozen ImageNet ResNet18/ResNet152 + one-class classifier serves as a no-frills control. **Metrics**: image-level AUC and AP; pixel-level AP and dataset-wise best Dice (at the optimal test operating point, avoiding threshold selection); pixel AUC is deliberately excluded (insensitive to false positives). Three random seeds per experiment.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **图像级分类(6 个)**:RSNA 肺炎胸片(训练 3851 正常;测试 1000+1000)、VinDr-CXR(14 类异常;训练 4000;测试 1000+1000)、Brain Tumor MRI(训练 1000;测试 600 正常 + 300 胶质瘤 + 300 脑膜瘤)、LAG 青光眼眼底(训练 1500;测试 811+811)、ISIC2018(痣为正常,其余 6 类为异常;训练 6705;测试 909+603)、Camelyon16(沿用 Bao et al. 的处理版本,40× patch;训练 5088;测试 1120 正常 + 1113 异常)。**像素级分割(1 个)**:BraTS2021 FLAIR,1051 例取 4211 张正常轴位切片训练,200 例取 828 正常 + 1948 肿瘤切片测试。作者区分两类异常:胸片/MRI/眼底是**局部异常**(异常图包含健康版本 + 局部病灶),Camelyon16 与 ISIC2018 是**全局语义异常**(整体属于不同类别)。由于训练时无异常,不设验证集。
<!-- EN --> **Image-level classification (6)**: RSNA pneumonia CXR (train 3851 normal; test 1000+1000), VinDr-CXR (14 anomaly classes; train 4000; test 1000+1000), Brain Tumor MRI (train 1000; test 600 normal + 300 glioma + 300 meningioma), LAG glaucoma fundus (train 1500; test 811+811), ISIC2018 (nevus normal vs six other classes; train 6705; test 909+603), Camelyon16 (Bao et al.'s processed version, 40× patches; train 5088; test 1120 normal + 1113 abnormal). **Pixel-level segmentation (1)**: BraTS2021 FLAIR — 4211 normal axial slices from 1051 scans for training, 828 normal + 1948 tumour slices from 200 scans for testing. Two anomaly types are distinguished: CXR/MRI/fundus have **local anomalies** (a healthy image plus a lesion), whereas Camelyon16 and ISIC2018 have **global semantic anomalies** (a different category altogether). No validation set, since anomalies are unavailable at training.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **基线**:朴素 AE 在 Hyper-Kvasir、OCT2017 上近乎满分——这两个数据集不适合作 AD 基准;在 7 个数据集上 AE 也是稳定的基线,作者强烈建议所有论文都报告 AE。 <!-- EN --> **Baseline**: a plain AE is near-perfect on Hyper-Kvasir and OCT2017 — these datasets are unsuitable AD benchmarks; AE is also a stable baseline across the seven datasets, and the authors urge every paper to report it.
- <!-- ZH --> **ImageNet 权重**:冻结的 ImageNet ResNet18 做两阶段特征 + 一类分类器,几乎在所有数据集上进前三,胜过多数训练流程更复杂的 SSL 方法;PANDA、MSC 这类「适配 ImageNet 特征」的方法没有显著提升。不用 ImageNet 权重时,重建类比 SSL 类更稳健。 <!-- EN --> **ImageNet weights**: a frozen ImageNet ResNet18 with a two-stage one-class classifier lands top-3 on nearly all datasets, beating most SSL methods with more complex training; adaptation methods like PANDA and MSC give no significant gain. Without ImageNet weights, reconstruction methods are more robust than SSL.
- <!-- ZH --> **病理(Camelyon16)**:不用 ImageNet 权重时,多数 AE/GAN 方法基本失效,只有 AE-U 与 DAE AUC 超过 51%;DDPM 类方法突出——AutoDDPM **80.7%**、AnoDDPM-Simplex **70.6%** AUC。作者认为病理异常与细胞形态密切相关,高保真重建在此反而有利。 <!-- EN --> **Pathology (Camelyon16)**: without ImageNet weights most AE/GAN methods fail outright, with only AE-U and DAE above 51% AUC; DDPM methods stand out — AutoDDPM **80.7%** and AnoDDPM-Simplex **70.6%** AUC. The authors argue pathology anomalies are tied to cellular morphology, so high-fidelity reconstruction helps here.
- <!-- ZH --> **latent 大小**:对局部异常,极小 latent 大幅提升——latent 从 128 降到 4,RSNA AUC +12.4%、AP +10.0%,VinDr-CXR AUC +12.7%,LAG AUC +7.3%;脑 MRI 的最优 latent 更大(约 32/16);ISIC2018(全局异常)反而越大越好。MemAE 在医学图像上失败,AE latent 上的 OC-SVM 接近随机(如 Camelyon16 AUC 0.49),说明正常/异常 latent 不可分。 <!-- EN --> **Latent size**: for local anomalies a tiny latent helps a lot — 128 → 4 gives RSNA +12.4% AUC / +10.0% AP, VinDr-CXR +12.7% AUC, LAG +7.3% AUC; brain MRI prefers larger latents (≈32/16); ISIC2018 (global anomalies) improves with larger latents. MemAE fails on medical images, and OC-SVM on AE latents is near chance (e.g. Camelyon16 AUC 0.49), showing normal/abnormal latents are inseparable.
- <!-- ZH --> **距离函数**:perceptual loss(AE-PL)在 6 个数据集上达到或接近 SOTA,部分数据集比 L2 高 20% 以上 AUC,但在 ISIC2018 上比 L2 低 6.1%;SSIM 在 RSNA 上 +10% 以上、在 LAG 上 −10% 以上。没有一个距离函数在 7 个数据集上全面胜过 L2。 <!-- EN --> **Distance function**: perceptual loss (AE-PL) is SOTA or close on six datasets and >20% AUC above L2 on some, yet 6.1% worse on ISIC2018; SSIM gains >10% on RSNA but loses >10% on LAG. No distance beats L2 on all seven datasets.
- <!-- ZH --> **分割**:重建类显著优于 SSL 类,朴素 AE 在 BraTS2021 上胜过所有一阶段 SSL 分割法;DAE 最好,领先第二名超过 10% Dice;**简单强度阈值法排第二**,超过 DDPM 类方法。AnoDDPM 用 Simplex 噪声比 Gaussian 噪声在 BraTS 上高 30% 以上 Dice。 <!-- EN --> **Segmentation**: reconstruction beats SSL clearly; a plain AE beats every one-stage SSL segmenter on BraTS2021; DAE is best, >10% Dice above second place; **simple intensity thresholding ranks second**, ahead of DDPM methods. Simplex noise beats Gaussian noise in AnoDDPM by >30% Dice on BraTS.
- <!-- ZH --> **SSL 内部**:合成异常越逼真越好(两阶段 AnatPaste 比 CutPaste 在 RSNA 上 AUC +20.2%、VinDr-CXR +14.0%);同一合成方法下两阶段通常优于一阶段(AnatPaste:RSNA +11.5% AUC、Brain Tumor +16.0% AUC)。 <!-- EN --> **Within SSL**: more realistic synthetic anomalies help (two-stage AnatPaste over CutPaste: +20.2% AUC on RSNA, +14.0% on VinDr-CXR); with the same synthesis, two-stage usually beats one-stage (AnatPaste: +11.5% AUC on RSNA, +16.0% on Brain Tumor).
- <!-- ZH --> **重建质量 ≠ 检测性能**:AnoDDPM-Gaussian 重建质量最高却整体最差;AE-PL 重建明显失真却很有竞争力——关键是正常与异常残差分布可分,而非整体重建好坏。 <!-- EN --> **Reconstruction quality ≠ detection**: AnoDDPM-Gaussian reconstructs best yet performs worst overall; AE-PL reconstructs with visible distortion yet is highly competitive — what matters is separable normal vs anomalous residual distributions, not overall fidelity.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 覆盖重建、自监督、特征参照三大范式 30 种方法的医学 AD 统一基准,跨 5 种模态并同时评测分类与分割。 <!-- EN --> A unified medical-AD benchmark of 30 methods across three paradigms and five modalities, evaluating both classification and segmentation.
- <!-- ZH --> **同范式统一网络结构**,让比较落在方法本身而非工程细节;并把冻结 ImageNet 特征、强度阈值这类「朴素对照」纳入基准。 <!-- EN --> **Unified architectures within each paradigm**, so comparisons isolate the method rather than engineering; and naive controls (frozen ImageNet features, intensity thresholding) are included in the benchmark.
- <!-- ZH --> 首次系统拆解关键组件(latent 限制、距离函数、梯度分数、噪声类型、SSL 阶段数、合成逼真度),并提炼出「局部 vs 全局异常」这一解释框架。 <!-- EN --> First systematic dissection of key components (latent restriction, distance function, gradient scores, noise type, SSL stages, synthesis realism), distilled into a "local vs global anomaly" explanatory frame.
- <!-- ZH --> 数据重划分与代码全部公开。 <!-- EN --> Dataset re-partitions and code are fully released.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 只用 2D(MRI 也切片处理),3D 与体数据 AD 未覆盖;像素级只有 BraTS2021 一个数据集、以显著肿瘤为主。 <!-- EN --> 2D only (MRI is sliced); 3D/volumetric AD is not covered; pixel-level evaluation has only BraTS2021, dominated by conspicuous tumours.
- <!-- ZH --> 统一的小 AE(64×64 输入)和 ResNet18 牺牲了各方法的原始最佳配置;病理只用了 Camelyon16 一个预处理版本,且不含病理/医学 foundation model。 <!-- EN --> The unified small AE (64×64 input) and ResNet18 sacrifice each method's original best configuration; pathology is represented only by one processed Camelyon16 version, and no pathology/medical foundation models are included.
- <!-- ZH --> 最优 Dice 在测试集上选阈值,是乐观上界;作者也指出 Dice 偏爱过度分割的方法,现有指标未必反映临床效用,特征空间分数缺乏校准。 <!-- EN --> Best Dice picks the threshold on the test set — an optimistic upper bound; the authors also note Dice favours over-segmenting methods, current metrics may not reflect clinical utility, and feature-space scores lack calibration.
- <!-- ZH --> 最优 latent 大小等关键配置仍只能靠看测试结果来找,没有给出无监督的选择准则。 <!-- EN --> Key configurations such as optimal latent size are still found by looking at test results; no unsupervised selection criterion is given.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是 detect 一环在**跨模态医学图像**上的方法学基准,与病理专用的 `pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025` 互补(后者也引用本文作为最近的医学 AD 基准)。对 **Plan A / KidneyAnomalyBench** 最有价值的是它的**基准卫生**原则:(1) **先证明数据集不平凡**——朴素 AE 在 Hyper-Kvasir 上满分这一发现,意味着我们的 benchmark 必须报告朴素基线(AE、冻结特征 + 一类分类器、乃至「强度/细胞计数阈值」这种非学习方法),否则无法说明任务有难度;对肾脏组学而言,对应的朴素对照可能是「单基因表达阈值」或「细胞类型比例偏移」;(2) **局部 vs 全局异常**——肾脏病变多为局部(一个肾小球硬化、一片小管损伤),而作者显示局部异常需要强限制的表示、全局异常相反,KidneyAnomalyBench 应按这两类分别分层;(3) **冻结的预训练特征很强**——这暗示把 UNI2-h / Nicheformer 等 foundation model 冻结嵌入 + 一类分类器作为默认强基线,是合理的起点;(4) **阈值与校准**——作者批评的「测试集最优 Dice」与「特征空间分数不可校准」,正是 benchmark 需要回避的,我们应在专家验证集上固定阈值,并报告校准指标。对 **Plan B** 的启示在于:如果 agent 在分析中声称「方法 X 检出异常优于基线」,verifier 应检查它是否与 AE / 强度阈值这类朴素基线比较过——这是可以写进组学/影像特定失败分类的一个具体条目(「缺失朴素基线」)。
<!-- EN --> This is the detect-stage methods benchmark for **cross-modality medical images**, complementing the pathology-specific `pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025` (which cites this paper as the most recent medical-AD benchmark). For **Plan A / KidneyAnomalyBench** its biggest value is **benchmark hygiene**: (1) **prove the dataset is non-trivial first** — a plain AE acing Hyper-Kvasir means our benchmark must report naive baselines (AE, frozen features + one-class classifier, even non-learned "intensity/cell-count thresholding"), or it cannot show the task is hard; for kidney omics the naive controls might be single-gene expression thresholds or cell-type-proportion shifts; (2) **local vs global anomalies** — kidney lesions are mostly local (one sclerotic glomerulus, a patch of tubular injury), and the paper shows local anomalies need tightly constrained representations while global ones do the opposite, so KidneyAnomalyBench should stratify by these two types; (3) **frozen pretrained features are strong** — suggesting frozen foundation-model embeddings (UNI2-h, Nicheformer) + a one-class classifier as the default strong baseline; (4) **thresholds and calibration** — the "best Dice on the test set" and "uncalibrated feature-space scores" the authors criticize are exactly what the benchmark must avoid; we should fix thresholds on the expert-validated split and report calibration. For **Plan B**: when an agent claims "method X detects anomalies better than baseline," the verifier should check that it was compared against naive baselines like AE or thresholding — a concrete entry for the omics/imaging-specific failure taxonomy ("missing naive baseline").
