## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> MVTec AD 是第一个面向真实工业检测、多物体、多缺陷、**带像素级真值**的无监督异常检测数据集:15 个类别(5 纹理 + 10 物体)、5,354 张高分辨率图像、73 种缺陷、1,888 个手工标注的异常区域;训练集只含无缺陷图像。论文同时给出 AnoGAN、L2/SSIM 自编码器、CNN 特征字典、GMM 纹理模型、variation model 的初始基准,结论是没有一种方法在所有类别上都好。它此后成为 PaDiM、PatchCore、DRÆM、CFLOW-AD 等几乎所有视觉 AD 方法的标准评测集。
<!-- EN --> MVTec AD is the first unsupervised anomaly-detection dataset aimed at real industrial inspection with multiple objects, multiple defect types and **pixel-precise ground truth**: 15 categories (5 textures + 10 objects), 5,354 high-resolution images, 73 defect types and 1,888 hand-annotated anomalous regions, with defect-free images only in training. The paper also benchmarks AnoGAN, L2/SSIM autoencoders, a CNN feature dictionary, a GMM texture model and a variation model, concluding that no method works well on every category. It became the standard benchmark for virtually all visual AD methods since, including PaDiM, PatchCore, DRÆM and CFLOW-AD.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 当时的异常 / 新奇检测评测大多是"把 MNIST、CIFAR-10、ImageNet 中任意几类当离群类",离群样本与训练分布差异巨大,无法说明方法在**与训练数据极接近、只在小局部有细微偏差**的真实异常上表现如何;而少数能做分割评测的数据集(NanoTWICE 只有 45 张单一纹理、DAGM 2007 是人工合成纹理且只有椭圆粗标)规模小、多样性低。作者认为缺少一个像 ImageNet 之于分类那样的 AD 数据集。
<!-- EN --> Evaluation of anomaly/novelty detection mostly relabelled arbitrary classes of MNIST, CIFAR-10 or ImageNet as outliers — outliers far from the training distribution — which says nothing about real anomalies that are **very close to the training data and differ only subtly in small regions**; the few datasets supporting segmentation (NanoTWICE: 45 images of one texture; DAGM 2007: synthetic textures with coarse ellipse labels) were small and low in diversity. The authors argue AD lacked a dataset playing the role ImageNet plays for classification.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **采集**:2048×2048 工业 RGB 传感器 + 两个双远心镜头(放大倍率 1:5 和 1:1),高度受控光照(部分类别有意改变光照以增加变异),裁剪到 700²–1024²;grid、screw、zipper 只提供灰度图。部分物体粗略对齐(toothbrush、capsule、pill),部分随机旋转摆放(metal nut、screw、hazelnut)。缺陷是**人工制造**的,目标是逼真地模拟生产中会出现的缺陷:表面缺陷(划痕、凹痕)、结构缺陷(部件变形)、缺失部件等。**基准协议**:各方法输出单通道异常图;作者提出**只用无缺陷验证图像确定阈值**的方法 —— 对每类设定一个最小缺陷面积,在验证集上逐步提高阈值,直到最大连通"异常"区域面积刚好低于该面积,用此阈值评估。**指标**:图像分类分别报告无缺陷和缺陷图的正确率;分割报告该阈值下的 per-region overlap(PRO)以及与阈值无关的像素级 ROC AUC。**基线**:AnoGAN(潜空间搜索 300 次迭代)、L2 与 SSIM 卷积自编码器(纹理 128×128 patch、物体 256×256)、CNN 特征字典(ResNet-18 avgpool + PCA + k-means,Napoletano 等)、基于 GMM 的纹理检测模型(HALCON 实现)、variation model(逐像素均值/方差,需要形状匹配对齐)。深度方法通过增强扩到每类 10,000 个训练 patch。
<!-- EN --> **Acquisition**: a 2048×2048 industrial RGB sensor with two bilateral telecentric lenses (1:5 and 1:1 magnification) under highly controlled illumination (deliberately varied for some classes), cropped to 700²–1024²; grid, screw and zipper are grey-scale only. Some objects are roughly aligned (toothbrush, capsule, pill), others randomly rotated (metal nut, screw, hazelnut). Defects were **manually produced** to realistically mimic production defects: surface defects (scratches, dents), structural defects (distorted parts), missing parts, etc. **Benchmark protocol**: every method outputs a one-channel anomaly map; the authors propose setting the threshold **from anomaly-free validation images only** — per category, a minimum defect area is defined and the threshold raised on the validation set until the largest connected "anomalous" region falls just below that area. **Metrics**: per-image classification accuracy for defect-free and defective images separately; for segmentation, per-region overlap (PRO) at that threshold plus threshold-free pixel ROC AUC. **Baselines**: AnoGAN (300 iterations of latent search), L2 and SSIM convolutional autoencoders (128×128 texture patches; 256×256 objects), a CNN feature dictionary (ResNet-18 avgpool + PCA + k-means, after Napoletano et al.), a GMM-based texture inspection model (HALCON implementation) and a variation model (per-pixel mean/variance, needs shape-based alignment). Deep methods are augmented to 10,000 training patches per category.

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> 这是**数据集/基准**而非方法;所评测的基线覆盖了 2019 年的两大范式 —— **reconstruction/generative**(AnoGAN、L2/SSIM-AE)与 **feature-embedding**(预训练 CNN 特征 + 聚类),外加经典的 **density / one-class**(GMM 纹理模型、逐像素高斯 variation model)。**承接**:Schlegl 等 AnoGAN(论文明确以其为 GAN 基线)、Bergmann 等 SSIM-AE、Napoletano 等 CNN 特征字典。**影响**:此后每一篇主流视觉 AD 论文都以它为主评测集 —— PaDiM、PatchCore、DRÆM、CFLOW-AD 的主结果全部在 MVTec AD 上;PaDiM 还据此构造了非对齐版本 Rd-MVTec AD;到 PatchCore 时图像级 AUROC 已达 99%+,基本饱和。PRO 指标也被沿用为标准定位指标。
<!-- EN --> A **dataset/benchmark**, not a method; its baselines span the two main 2019 paradigms — **reconstruction/generative** (AnoGAN, L2/SSIM-AE) and **feature-embedding** (pretrained CNN features + clustering) — plus classical **density / one-class** models (GMM texture model, per-pixel Gaussian variation model). **Builds on**: Schlegl et al.'s AnoGAN (explicitly the GAN baseline), Bergmann et al.'s SSIM-AE, Napoletano et al.'s CNN feature dictionary. **Led to**: it is the main benchmark of every mainstream visual AD paper since — PaDiM, PatchCore, DRÆM and CFLOW-AD all report headline results on it; PaDiM derives a misaligned variant (Rd-MVTec AD) from it; by PatchCore image AUROC exceeds 99%, i.e. the benchmark is essentially saturated. The PRO metric also became the standard localization metric.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **规模**:3,629 张训练/验证(全部无缺陷)+ 1,725 张测试(467 无缺陷 + 1,258 有缺陷);15 类各有 60–391 张训练图,toothbrush 最少(60)、hazelnut 最多(391);每类 1–8 个缺陷组,共 73 种;共 1,888 个缺陷区域。**纹理**:carpet、grid(规则),leather、tile、wood(随机)。**物体**:bottle、cable、capsule、hazelnut、metal nut、pill、screw、toothbrush、transistor、zipper。**许可**:CC BY-NC-SA 4.0(禁止商用,来自 MVTec 官网)。
<!-- EN --> **Scale**: 3,629 training/validation images (all defect-free) + 1,725 test images (467 defect-free + 1,258 defective); 60–391 training images per category (toothbrush fewest at 60, hazelnut most at 391); 1–8 defect groups per category, 73 in total; 1,888 defect regions overall. **Textures**: carpet, grid (regular), leather, tile, wood (random). **Objects**: bottle, cable, capsule, hazelnut, metal nut, pill, screw, toothbrush, transistor, zipper. **License**: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial; per the MVTec website).

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **没有赢家**:没有任何方法在所有物体与纹理类上都表现好。纹理类无明显胜者,只看 ROC AUC 时 CNN 特征字典最稳定;物体类上 L2 / SSIM 自编码器最好,哪一个更好取决于具体物体。 <!-- EN --> **No winner**: no method performs consistently well across all object and texture classes. On textures there is no clear winner, with the CNN feature dictionary most consistent by ROC AUC; on objects the L2 / SSIM autoencoders are best, which one depending on the object.
- <!-- ZH --> **高 AUC ≠ 好分割**:像素 ROC AUC 高时,用无缺陷验证集估出的阈值下的 PRO 仍可能很低(例如 bottle 上四种深度方法 PRO 只有 0.05–0.22,而 ROC AUC 为 0.78–0.93)—— 说明**仅用正常样本定阈值**本身就是难题。 <!-- EN --> **High AUC ≠ good segmentation**: even with high pixel ROC AUC, PRO at the threshold estimated from defect-free validation images can be very low (e.g., on bottle the four deep methods reach PRO of only 0.05–0.22 while ROC AUC is 0.78–0.93) — showing that **setting thresholds from normal samples alone** is itself a hard problem.
- <!-- ZH --> **失败模式**:AnoGAN 出现 mode collapse,对形状/朝向多变的物体几乎无法重建,carpet 完全失败;自编码器在高频纹理(tile、zipper)上重建模糊、假阳性多;CNN 特征字典不含空间位置信息,物体类上退化;GMM 纹理模型只处理灰度,漏掉颜色缺陷;variation model 需要精确对齐,capsule 上随机位置的印字总被判为缺陷。 <!-- EN --> **Failure modes**: AnoGAN suffers mode collapse, barely reconstructing objects with variable shape/orientation and failing completely on carpet; autoencoders blur high-frequency textures (tile, zipper) with many false positives; the CNN feature dictionary lacks spatial information and degrades on objects; the GMM texture model is grey-scale only and misses colour defects; the variation model needs exact alignment, so the randomly placed imprint on capsule is always flagged as a defect.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 第一个多类别、多缺陷、像素级真值、面向真实应用的无监督 AD 数据集。 <!-- EN --> The first multi-category, multi-defect, pixel-annotated, application-oriented unsupervised AD dataset.
- <!-- ZH --> **只用无缺陷验证图像确定阈值**的评测协议,以及 PRO 指标的使用 —— 后者成为定位的标准指标。 <!-- EN --> A thresholding protocol **using defect-free validation images only**, and use of the PRO metric — which became the standard localization metric.
- <!-- ZH --> 跨范式的初始基准,揭示了每类方法的具体失败模式。 <!-- EN --> A cross-paradigm initial benchmark that exposes concrete failure modes of each method family.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 缺陷是人工制造的,采集条件高度受控(远心镜头、可控光照),与真实产线的变异性有差距;每类训练图较少(60–391)。 <!-- EN --> Defects are manually produced and acquisition is highly controlled (telecentric lenses, controlled light), unlike real production variability; few training images per class (60–391).
- <!-- ZH --> 只有外观 / 结构类局部缺陷,没有"逻辑异常"(组件数量、排列错误)—— 这在后续 MVTec LOCO 中才被补上(本文未涉及)。 <!-- EN --> Only local appearance/structural defects; no "logical anomalies" (wrong count/arrangement of parts) — addressed later by MVTec LOCO (not part of this paper).
- <!-- ZH --> 后来的方法把图像级 AUROC 推到 99%+,基准已近饱和,区分力下降;而 pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 显示 MVTec 上的好表现不能迁移到病理(工业平均 ROC-AUC 0.888 vs 真实病理 0.645)。 <!-- EN --> Later methods push image AUROC beyond 99%, so the benchmark is near-saturated and discriminates less; and pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 shows MVTec success does not transfer to pathology (industrial mean ROC-AUC 0.888 vs real pathology 0.645).
- <!-- ZH --> 非商用许可,限制了工业用途。 <!-- EN --> Non-commercial license limits industrial use.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> MVTec AD 对 Plan A 的意义是**KidneyAnomalyBench 的设计模板与反面教材**。**可以直接借鉴的**:(1) "训练集只有正常、测试集正常 + 异常、异常带区域级真值"的结构 —— 对应"健康参考训练、95 例活检测试、专家标注的病变区域(肾小球硬化、间质纤维化、小管萎缩、炎症浸润)作为区域级真值";(2) **只用正常验证集定阈值**的协议 —— 肾脏数据里同样没有可用于调参的已知异常,论文揭示的"AUC 高但阈值下分割差"在组学中会更严重,因此 KidneyAnomalyBench 应同时报告阈值无关指标(AUROC/AP)与固定协议阈值下的区域重叠;(3) **按类别分别报告**:MVTec 的"没有方法在所有类别上都好"对应到肾脏就是按解剖单元 / 细胞类型 / 病变类型分别报告,而不是给一个全局 AUC。**哪些假设会破**:(1) MVTec 的"正常"是严格受控的单一产品,而健康肾本身就异质(皮质/髓质、小管节段、年龄相关改变),且"健康供体"并不完全健康;(2) MVTec 的异常是**离散、局部**的缺陷,肾病是**连续、弥散**的状态谱,二值像素 mask 不足以表达,需要分级标签(例如 IFTA 程度)或连续损伤评分(damage-scores-cellgenomics-2026 的 PDS/HDS 可作为连续伪真值);(3) MVTec 几乎没有"技术伪影 vs 真异常"的混淆,而组学中 ambient RNA、doublet、分割错误、批次效应都是会被检测为"异常"的伪影 —— KidneyAnomalyBench 应专门设一类**伪影型异常**并要求方法把它们与疾病区分开,这正是 Plan A 跨模态一致性的评测出口;(4) 像素级 mask 在 snRNA/snATAC 中不存在,只在 Xenium/H&E 空间数据上可近似。**仓库里已有的同类思想**:pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 把 MVTec 的 Hazelnut 与 Tile 作为工业参照,与 Camelyon16、Crohn 结肠、小鼠肾小球对比,并直接量化了工业→病理的性能断崖;medianomaly-media-2025 是医学影像版的统一 AD 基准(7 个数据集、30 种方法);nih-kidney-ai-challenge-2026 是肾脏方向可能的外部基准出口。
<!-- EN --> For Plan A, MVTec AD is **both the design template and a cautionary tale for KidneyAnomalyBench**. **What to borrow**: (1) the structure "normal-only training, normal + anomalous test, region-level ground truth for anomalies" — i.e., healthy-reference training, the 95 biopsies as test, expert-annotated lesions (glomerulosclerosis, interstitial fibrosis, tubular atrophy, inflammatory infiltrate) as region-level truth; (2) the **threshold-from-normal-validation-only** protocol — kidney data also lack known anomalies for tuning, and the paper's "high AUC but poor thresholded segmentation" will be worse in omics, so KidneyAnomalyBench should report both threshold-free metrics (AUROC/AP) and region overlap at a fixed-protocol threshold; (3) **per-category reporting** — MVTec's "no method wins every category" translates into reporting per anatomical unit / cell type / lesion type rather than one global AUC. **What breaks**: (1) MVTec's "normal" is a tightly controlled single product, whereas healthy kidney is itself heterogeneous (cortex/medulla, tubular segments, age-related change) and "healthy donors" are not fully healthy; (2) MVTec anomalies are **discrete, local** defects, while kidney disease is a **continuous, diffuse** spectrum that binary pixel masks cannot express — graded labels (e.g., IFTA severity) or continuous damage scores are needed (the PDS/HDS of damage-scores-cellgenomics-2026 could serve as continuous pseudo-truth); (3) MVTec has almost no "technical artifact vs true anomaly" confusion, whereas in omics ambient RNA, doublets, segmentation errors and batch effects all register as "anomalies" — KidneyAnomalyBench should include an explicit **artifact-type anomaly** class and require methods to separate it from disease, which is precisely where Plan A's cross-modal agreement gets evaluated; (4) pixel masks do not exist for snRNA/snATAC and can only be approximated on Xenium/H&E spatial data. **Existing repo papers with the same idea**: pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 uses MVTec Hazelnut and Tile as industrial references against Camelyon16, Crohn's colon and mouse glomeruli and directly quantifies the industrial→pathology performance cliff; medianomaly-media-2025 is the medical-imaging counterpart unified AD benchmark (7 datasets, 30 methods); nih-kidney-ai-challenge-2026 is a possible external benchmark outlet on the kidney side.
