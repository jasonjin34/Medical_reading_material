## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> PatchCore 把所有正常训练图像的**中层、局部邻域聚合的 patch 特征**存进一个 memory bank,用 **greedy coreset 子采样**把它压缩到 1–25% 而几乎不掉性能,测试时以"最远的那个 patch 到其最近正常邻居的距离"作为图像异常分数;在 MVTec AD 上图像级 AUROC 99.1%(集成可达 99.6%),把 PaDiM 的误差减半以上。
<!-- EN --> PatchCore stores **mid-level, neighbourhood-aggregated patch features** of all normal training images in a memory bank, shrinks it to 1–25% with **greedy coreset subsampling** at almost no loss, and scores a test image by the distance of its most anomalous patch to that patch's nearest normal neighbour — 99.1% image AUROC on MVTec AD (up to 99.6% with an ensemble), more than halving PaDiM's error.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 工业视觉检测的 **cold-start** 问题:只用正常(nominal)样本拟合模型,同时要在很多不同品类上自动工作良好。当时最强的方法(SPADE、PaDiM)都是"ImageNet 预训练特征 + 离群检测",但有两个缺陷:(1) 深层特征过度偏向 ImageNet 分类任务,与工业缺陷关系不大;(2) 测试时可用的"正常上下文"有限 —— SPADE 依赖图像级 kNN 预筛,PaDiM 只把每个 patch 与同位置的统计量比较,需要图像对齐。PatchCore 想同时做到:最大化测试时可用的正常信息、降低 ImageNet 偏置、保持推理速度。
<!-- EN --> The **cold-start** problem in industrial inspection: fit using nominal images only, while working well across many product categories automatically. The best methods (SPADE, PaDiM) combined ImageNet features with an outlier detector but had two flaws: (1) deep features are biased toward ImageNet classification and overlap little with industrial defects; (2) the nominal context usable at test time is limited — SPADE relies on an image-level kNN preselection, and PaDiM compares each patch only with same-position statistics, requiring alignment. PatchCore aims to maximize nominal information at test time, reduce ImageNet bias, and keep inference fast.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **(1) Locally aware patch features**:取 ImageNet 预训练 WideResNet-50 的**中层**(第 2、3 个 block,j∈[2,3]),既避免浅层太泛、也避免深层太偏 ImageNet。每个位置的特征用 p×p 邻域(默认 p=3)做 adaptive average pooling 聚合,扩大感受野、对小空间偏移鲁棒而不降分辨率;第 3 层特征双线性上采样后与第 2 层拼接。所有正常图像的全部 patch 特征的并集即 memory bank M。**(2) Coreset 子采样**:M 很大,随机子采样会丢失特征空间覆盖。采用 minimax facility location coreset —— 找子集 M_C 使 M 中任一点到 M_C 的最近距离最大值最小 —— 用迭代贪心近似(每步加入离当前 coreset 最远的点),并先用 Johnson–Lindenstrauss 随机投影降维以加速选择。PatchCore-n% 表示 bank 保留 n%。**(3) 打分**:测试图像每个 patch 在 M 中找最近邻,取距离最大的那个 patch 的距离 s*;再按其最近邻在 bank 中自身的"稀有程度"重加权(若 m* 离自己的 b 个邻居都远,说明它本身是罕见正常样本,则上调分数)。异常图 = 各 patch 分数按空间位置排回、双线性上采样、σ=4 高斯平滑。图像无需同尺寸,不需对齐,不需在目标数据上训练。
<!-- EN --> **(1) Locally aware patch features**: take **mid-level** features from an ImageNet-pretrained WideResNet-50 (blocks 2 and 3, j ∈ [2,3]) — avoiding both too-generic shallow and too-ImageNet-biased deep features. Each position's feature is aggregated over a p×p neighbourhood (default p = 3) by adaptive average pooling, enlarging the receptive field and adding robustness to small shifts without losing resolution; block-3 features are bilinearly upsampled and concatenated with block 2. The union of all patch features of all nominal images is the memory bank M. **(2) Coreset subsampling**: M is huge and random subsampling loses feature-space coverage, so a minimax facility-location coreset is used — the subset M_C minimizing the maximum distance from any point in M to M_C — approximated greedily (add the point farthest from the current coreset each step), with Johnson–Lindenstrauss random projections to speed up selection. PatchCore-n% keeps n% of the bank. **(3) Scoring**: each test patch finds its nearest neighbour in M; the image score uses the largest such distance s*, reweighted by how rare that neighbour m* is within the bank (if m* is far from its own b nearest neighbours, it is itself a rare nominal sample, so the score is raised). The anomaly map places patch scores back spatially, upsamples bilinearly and smooths with σ = 4. Images need not share a shape, need no alignment, and no training on the target data is required.

## 方法谱系 / Method lineage
<!-- ZH --> **范式:feature-embedding & memory bank**(非参数 kNN 版本,是这一范式的代表作)。**承接**:Bergman 等 2020(DN2,预训练特征上的 kNN)、SPADE(多层特征 memory bank + kNN 分割)、PaDiM(逐位置高斯 + Mahalanobis);作者明确说其组件"与 SPADE 和 PaDiM 最相关",并把 PaDiM 的"每个 patch 只用同位置统计"换成"所有 patch 共享一个 bank"。coreset 思想来自主动学习(Sener & Savarese)与 GAN 训练中的 coreset 选择。**影响**:成为工业 AD 的事实标准基线,并被大量迁移到医学 / 病理 —— pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 把它列入 feature distribution 家族直接评测;virchow-2024 的待读里也提出在冻结 Virchow 嵌入上做 PatchCore。它与 CFLOW-AD(同一时期、同类预训练特征、换成 flow 密度)形成"非参数 kNN vs 参数化密度"的对照。
<!-- EN --> **Paradigm: feature-embedding & memory bank** (the non-parametric kNN version, and the paradigm's flagship). **Builds on**: Bergman et al. 2020 (DN2, kNN on pretrained features), SPADE (multi-layer feature memory bank + kNN segmentation), PaDiM (per-position Gaussian + Mahalanobis); the authors state its components are "most related to SPADE and PaDiM" and replace PaDiM's same-position-only statistics with one bank shared by all patches. The coreset idea comes from active learning (Sener & Savarese) and coreset selection in GAN training. **Led to**: the de-facto standard industrial AD baseline, widely ported to medicine/pathology — pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 benchmarks it within the feature-distribution family, and virchow-2024's follow-ups propose PatchCore on frozen Virchow embeddings. With CFLOW-AD (same era, same pretrained features, flow density instead) it forms the "non-parametric kNN vs parametric density" contrast.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **MVTec AD**:15 个子集,5354 张图,其中测试 1725 张;缩放到 256×256 并中心裁剪 224×224,不做数据增强(作者认为增强需要"哪些变换保持类别"的先验)。**Magnetic Tile Defects (MTD)**:925 张无缺陷 + 392 张缺陷磁瓦图,光照和尺寸不一,20% 无缺陷图用于测试。**mini ShanghaiTech Campus (mSTC)**:STC 每 5 帧取 1 帧,12 个场景,256×256。
<!-- EN --> **MVTec AD**: 15 sub-datasets, 5354 images, 1725 of them test; resized to 256×256 and centre-cropped to 224×224 with no augmentation (augmentation would need prior knowledge of class-preserving transforms). **Magnetic Tile Defects (MTD)**: 925 defect-free + 392 defective tiles with varied illumination and size; 20% of defect-free images held out for test. **mini ShanghaiTech Campus (mSTC)**: every fifth STC frame, 12 scenes, 256×256.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **图像级检测(MVTec AUROC)**:PatchCore-25% **99.1%**、-10% 99.0%、-1% 99.0%,对比 PaDiM(任务特定 backbone)97.9%、MahalanobisAD 95.8%、PaDiM 95.3%、DifferNet 94.9%、Patch SVDD 92.1%、SPADE 85.5%。误差从 2.1% 降到 0.9%(降 57%);F1 最优阈值下 1725 张中只错 42 张,三分之一的类别完全正确。 <!-- EN --> **Image detection (MVTec AUROC)**: PatchCore-25% **99.1%**, -10% 99.0%, -1% 99.0%, vs PaDiM (task-specific backbone) 97.9%, MahalanobisAD 95.8%, PaDiM 95.3%, DifferNet 94.9%, Patch SVDD 92.1%, SPADE 85.5%. Error drops from 2.1% to 0.9% (−57%); at the F1-optimal threshold only 42 of 1725 images are misclassified and a third of the classes are solved perfectly.
- <!-- ZH --> **分割**:像素级 AUROC 98.1%(PaDiM 97.5%、SPADE 96.0%);PRO 93.5%(PaDiM 92.1%、SPADE 91.7%)。 <!-- EN --> **Segmentation**: pixel AUROC 98.1% (PaDiM 97.5%, SPADE 96.0%); PRO 93.5% (PaDiM 92.1%, SPADE 91.7%).
- <!-- ZH --> **更大配置**:PatchCore-1% 用 DenseNet-201 & ResNeXt-101 & WRN-101 集成、320 分辨率,图像 AUROC **99.6%**、像素 AUROC 98.2%、PRO 94.9%。 <!-- EN --> **Larger setups**: PatchCore-1% with a DenseNet-201 & ResNeXt-101 & WRN-101 ensemble at 320 px reaches **99.6%** image AUROC, 98.2% pixel AUROC, 94.9% PRO.
- <!-- ZH --> **推理时间(每图,GPU)**:PatchCore-100% 0.6 s (99.1/98.0/93.3),-10% 0.22 s,-1% 0.17 s (99.0/98.0/93.1);SPADE 0.66 s (85.3/96.6/91.5),PaDiM 0.19 s (95.4/97.3/91.8);PatchCore-100% + IVFPQ 近似最近邻 0.2 s 但图像 AUROC 掉到 98.0。 <!-- EN --> **Inference time (per image, GPU)**: PatchCore-100% 0.6 s (99.1/98.0/93.3), -10% 0.22 s, -1% 0.17 s (99.0/98.0/93.1); SPADE 0.66 s (85.3/96.6/91.5), PaDiM 0.19 s (95.4/97.3/91.8); PatchCore-100% + IVFPQ approximate NN 0.2 s but image AUROC falls to 98.0.
- <!-- ZH --> **消融**:邻域大小 p=3 在局部性与全局上下文之间最优;第 2+3 层最好;coreset 明显优于随机子采样和学习代理点;不子采样时只有 <30% 的 bank 条目在测试时被用到,coreset 到 1% 后升至近 95%;改用 stride 2 / 3 降低 bank 大小时图像 AUROC 降到 97.6% / 96.8%。 <!-- EN --> **Ablations**: neighbourhood p = 3 is the optimum between locality and global context; blocks 2+3 are best; coreset clearly beats random subsampling and learned proxies; without subsampling <30% of bank entries are ever used at test time, rising to ~95% after a 1% coreset; shrinking the bank via stride 2 / 3 drops image AUROC to 97.6% / 96.8%.
- <!-- ZH --> **其他基准与少样本**:mSTC 像素 AUROC 91.8%(PaDiM 91.2%);MTD 图像 AUROC 97.9%(DifferNet 97.7%);只用五分之一正常训练数据即可追平此前 SOTA。 <!-- EN --> **Other benchmarks & low-shot**: mSTC pixel AUROC 91.8% (PaDiM 91.2%); MTD image AUROC 97.9% (DifferNet 97.7%); one fifth of the nominal training data already matches the previous state of the art.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **中层 + 局部邻域聚合**的 patch 特征:在分辨率、上下文与 ImageNet 偏置之间取平衡。 <!-- EN --> **Mid-level, neighbourhood-aggregated** patch features balancing resolution, context and ImageNet bias.
- <!-- ZH --> **所有 patch 共享的 memory bank**:摆脱 PaDiM 的位置绑定,不需对齐、不限输入尺寸。 <!-- EN --> **One memory bank shared by all patches**: removes PaDiM's positional binding — no alignment, no fixed input shape.
- <!-- ZH --> **Greedy coreset 子采样**:把 bank 压缩两个数量级而性能基本不变,是 kNN 方法可扩展的关键 —— 这也是本文对我们最可迁移的一点。 <!-- EN --> **Greedy coreset subsampling**: shrinks the bank by two orders of magnitude at near-constant performance — the key to scaling kNN methods, and the most transferable idea for us.
- <!-- ZH --> 基于邻居稀有度的分数重加权;高样本效率。 <!-- EN --> Neighbour-rarity score reweighting; high sample efficiency.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 作者自陈:适用性受限于预训练特征的可迁移性;与特征自适应结合留作未来工作。 <!-- EN --> Self-stated: applicability is bounded by the transferability of the pretrained features; combining with feature adaptation is left to future work.
- <!-- ZH --> **"memory bank 污染"**:kNN 天然假设 bank 里全是干净的正常样本;训练集里混入少量异常时,异常 patch 会在 bank 里找到"正常"近邻 —— 论文未讨论这一点。 <!-- EN --> **Bank contamination**: kNN implicitly assumes the bank is clean; a few anomalies in the training set give anomalous test patches "nominal" neighbours — not discussed in the paper.
- <!-- ZH --> 图像分数取最大 patch 距离,对单个噪声 patch 敏感;没有校准过的阈值或 FPR 保证。 <!-- EN --> The max-patch image score is sensitive to a single noisy patch; no calibrated threshold or FPR guarantee.
- <!-- ZH --> 评测几乎全在 MVTec,而 MVTec 已接近饱和(99%+),对真实场景的区分力有限。 <!-- EN --> Evaluation is almost entirely MVTec, which is near-saturated (99%+) and discriminates little for real-world settings.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> PatchCore 是伪影注入课题中**最值得直接移植的经典 AD 方法**,原因是它的三个部件在组学里都有干净的对应。**映射**:memory bank = 健康参考中所有细胞 / spot 的 embedding(scVI、scGPT/Geneformer 类基础模型、或 Nicheformer 这类空间感知 embedding);patch 的"局部邻域聚合" = 对 Xenium/Visium 的空间邻居做 pooling(即 niche 特征);测试分数 = 细胞到最近健康邻居的距离;图像级分数 = 一块活检 / 一个解剖区域中最异常细胞(或 top-k 平均)的分数。H&E 端可以原样使用:UNI2-h / Virchow 的 patch embedding + PatchCore,这正是 virchow-2024 待读里写的方案。**coreset 是关键**:健康图谱动辄数十万到百万细胞,coreset 既解决 kNN 的规模问题,又保证稀有健康细胞类型(如足细胞、肾小球旁细胞)不被随机子采样丢掉 —— 等价于"覆盖率优先的健康参考压缩"。**哪些假设会破**:(1) **bank 污染**:健康供体也有年龄相关的局灶损伤、ambient RNA 污染和 doublet,这些会进入 bank 并"掩护"测试集里的同类异常;(2) **batch effect**:新活检若与参考来自不同批次 / 平台,最近邻距离整体抬升,所有细胞都像异常 —— 需要先做 scArches 式映射或在 bank 中纳入多批次正常样本;(3) **连续疾病态**:kNN 距离给出的是"离健康多远",不区分方向,因此 injured PT 与 doublet 可能拿到相同分数;(4) **没有像素 mask**,PatchCore 赖以报告的像素 AUROC / PRO 在组学里要换成按细胞的专家标签或已知真值的合成扰动。伪影注入课题的跨模态一致性正好补 (2)(3):同一个细胞/区域在 RNA、ATAC、Xenium、H&E 四个 bank 里**都**远离健康邻居,才更可能是疾病而非技术伪影。**仓库里已有的同类思想**:pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 直接评测 PatchCore 于小鼠肾小球等病理数据;histo-nejmai-2024 用病理基础模型 CTransPath 特征 + 改进 kNN 打分,是 PatchCore 思想在病理的近亲;spatial-natcommun-2024(STANDS)也用了 memory bank,但那是为了在 GAN 重建中抑制 mode collapse,与 PatchCore 的"正常特征库 + kNN"用途不同。对 Agent 可信度课题:kNN 距离没有校准,验证器应要求 agent 报告阈值来源(例如用 conformal-scrna-bioinformatics-2025 式的 conformal p 值)。
<!-- EN --> PatchCore is **the classic AD method most worth porting directly** in the artifact-injection topic, because each of its three components has a clean omics analogue. **Mapping**: memory bank = embeddings of all cells/spots in the healthy reference (scVI, scGPT/Geneformer-style foundation models, or spatially aware embeddings like Nicheformer); "local neighbourhood aggregation" of patches = pooling over spatial neighbours in Xenium/Visium (i.e., niche features); test score = distance of a cell to its nearest healthy neighbour; image-level score = the score of the most anomalous cell (or top-k mean) in a biopsy or anatomical region. On the H&E side it ports unchanged: UNI2-h / Virchow patch embeddings + PatchCore, exactly what virchow-2024's follow-ups propose. **The coreset is the key piece**: healthy atlases hold hundreds of thousands to millions of cells; a coreset fixes kNN's scaling problem while guaranteeing that rare healthy cell types (podocytes, juxtaglomerular cells) are not dropped as they would be under random subsampling — i.e., coverage-first compression of the healthy reference. **What breaks**: (1) **bank contamination** — healthy donors carry age-related focal injury, ambient-RNA contamination and doublets, which enter the bank and "cover" similar anomalies in the test set; (2) **batch effects** — if a new biopsy comes from a different batch/platform than the reference, nearest-neighbour distances rise globally and everything looks anomalous, so scArches-style mapping or multi-batch normals in the bank are needed first; (3) **continuous disease states** — kNN distance says "how far from healthy" but not in which direction, so an injured PT cell and a doublet can score alike; (4) **no pixel masks** — the pixel AUROC / PRO PatchCore reports must become per-cell expert labels or synthetic perturbations with known ground truth. The artifact-injection topic's cross-modal agreement directly addresses (2) and (3): a cell/region that is far from healthy neighbours in **all** of the RNA, ATAC, Xenium and H&E banks is more likely disease than technical artifact. **Existing repo papers with the same idea**: pathology-ad-bench-mlhealth-2025 benchmarks PatchCore on pathology data including mouse glomeruli; histo-nejmai-2024 scores CTransPath foundation-model features with a modified kNN — a close pathology relative of PatchCore; spatial-natcommun-2024 (STANDS) also has a memory bank, but to curb mode collapse inside GAN reconstruction, not as PatchCore's normal-feature bank for kNN. For the agent-trustworthiness topic: kNN distances are uncalibrated, so a verifier should require the agent to state where its threshold comes from (e.g., conformal p-values as in conformal-scrna-bioinformatics-2025).
