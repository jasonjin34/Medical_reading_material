## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> 作者把 snRNA+snATAC 多组学与 CosMx 高通量单细胞空间成像(6,000 与 1,000 探针)结合,在人类肾病中找到一类 **VCAM1+ICAM1+ 炎症性近端小管(PT)细胞**:它们是 KPMP「adaptive PT」的一个亚群,表达趋化因子、促纤维化与衰老相关基因,特异地定位在纤维化 niche;表观上由 **HNF4α 网络崩溃 + AP-1/NF-κB 激活**锁定;在小鼠中用 AP-1 抑制剂 T5224 或衰老清除剂 ABT-263 能减少这类细胞并减轻炎症和纤维化。
<!-- EN --> Combining snRNA+snATAC multiome with CosMx high-plex single-cell spatial imaging (6,000- and 1,000-plex), the authors identify a **VCAM1+ICAM1+ inflammatory proximal-tubule (PT) cell** in human kidney disease: a subset of KPMP "adaptive PT" cells that expresses chemokines, pro-fibrotic and senescence-associated genes and localizes specifically to the fibrotic niche; it is epigenetically imprinted by **HNF4α-network collapse plus AP-1/NF-κB activation**; in mice the AP-1 inhibitor T5224 or the senolytic ABT-263 depletes these cells and reduces inflammation and fibrosis.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 人类肾病中已描述了表达 VCAM1 的「adaptive」PT 状态,小鼠中也有 Havcr1+Vcam1+「failed repair」PT 细胞,但(1) failed repair 表型与人类肾病的关系、(2) 它在组织中的空间位置、(3) 驱动它的转录调控程序,都不清楚 —— 因此也不知道它是不是一个可干预的靶点。
<!-- EN --> Human kidney disease has a described VCAM1-expressing "adaptive" PT state, and mice have Havcr1+Vcam1+ "failed-repair" PT cells, but it was unclear (1) how failed repair relates to human disease, (2) where it sits spatially, and (3) which transcriptional programs drive it — hence whether it is a tractable target.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **人类图谱**:肿瘤肾切除标本的非肿瘤极,5 例输尿管梗阻(UUO,尿路上皮癌导致)vs 7 例无梗阻的肾细胞癌对照;为减少批次,把 3–5 位患者的核混成 6 个 pool 做 10x Multiome,用 SNP 反卷积分配供者(RNA 与 ATAC 文库的供者一致率 98.68%,并用 SNP 芯片验证),跨供者 SNP 还能检出普通阈值漏掉的双细胞。**投射到 KPMP**:验证聚类、确认 inflammatory 亚群在其他病因中也存在。**轨迹与跨物种**:RNA velocity + 伪时间;与小鼠 IRI、可逆 UUO(R-UUO)时间序列比较。**衰老签名**:照射诱导衰老的 RPTEC 做 bulk RNA-seq。**空间**:CosMx 6,000-plex(UUO 5 例、对照 2 例)与 1,000-plex(微小病变 3 例、IgA 肾病 6 例、慢性肾盂肾炎 4 例);25 µm 半径共定位富集、到细胞质心距离的转录本富集、niche 划分;多重免疫荧光(PanCK、VCAM1、ICAM1、FAP、CD68)验证。**调控**:配对 RNA/ATAC 构建 TF–CRE–靶基因网络、motif footprint;TNF 刺激 RPTEC 后做 JUN 与 NF-κB1 的 CUT&RUN。**干预**:小鼠单侧 IRI 后第 3 天起每日灌胃 T5224 10 mg/kg × 2 周;R-UUO 模型中给 BCL2/w/xL 抑制剂 ABT-263。
<!-- EN --> **Human atlas**: non-tumour poles of tumour nephrectomies — 5 patients with ureteric obstruction (UUO, from urothelial carcinoma) vs 7 renal-cell-carcinoma controls without obstruction; to limit batch effects, nuclei from 3–5 patients were pooled into 6 libraries for 10x Multiome and donors assigned by SNP deconvolution (98.68% donor agreement between RNA and ATAC libraries, validated by SNP arrays); cross-donor SNPs also catch doublets missed by standard thresholds. **Projection onto KPMP** validates clusters and confirms the inflammatory subset in other aetiologies. **Trajectory and cross-species**: RNA velocity + pseudotime; comparison with mouse IRI and reversible-UUO (R-UUO) time courses. **Senescence signature**: bulk RNA-seq of irradiation-induced senescent RPTECs. **Spatial**: CosMx 6,000-plex (5 UUO, 2 controls) and 1,000-plex (minimal change disease n=3, IgA nephropathy n=6, chronic pyelonephritis n=4); co-localization enrichment within 25 µm, transcript enrichment by distance from centroids, niche partitioning; multiplex IF (PanCK, VCAM1, ICAM1, FAP, CD68) for validation. **Regulation**: paired RNA/ATAC TF–CRE–target networks and motif footprinting; CUT&RUN for JUN and NF-κB1 in TNF-stimulated RPTECs. **Intervention**: daily gavage of T5224 10 mg/kg for 2 weeks starting day 3 after unilateral IRI in mice; the BCL2/w/xL inhibitor ABT-263 in the R-UUO model.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **状态谱系**:PT 细胞分为健康(S1–S3)、损伤(HAVCR1+VCAM1+,对应 KPMP adaptive PT)与炎症(额外表达 CCL2、CXCL1、TGM2、ICAM1 等)三态;炎症态几乎只见于梗阻肾,在 KPMP 的 adaptive PT 中也能找到 ICAM1+ 亚群。KPMP CKD 患者中,**炎症 PT 比例与活化巨噬细胞/肌成纤维细胞比例紧密相关,而总 adaptive 比例则不显著相关**。 <!-- EN --> **State spectrum**: PT cells split into healthy (S1–S3), injured (HAVCR1+VCAM1+, matching KPMP adaptive PT) and inflammatory (additionally CCL2, CXCL1, TGM2, ICAM1, …); the inflammatory state is almost exclusive to obstructed kidneys and an ICAM1+ subset is also found within KPMP adaptive PT. In KPMP CKD patients, **inflammatory-PT proportion correlates closely with activated macrophages/myofibroblasts, whereas total adaptive proportion does not significantly**.
- <!-- ZH --> **「不可回头点」**:RNA velocity 提示早期损伤细胞仍可能回到健康态,而更晚期的细胞只流向炎症态;小鼠 IRI 与 R-UUO 中损伤 PT 在恢复期逐渐减少,而炎症 PT 持续存在更久;炎症 PT 的照射衰老签名评分明显更高。 <!-- EN --> **"Point of no return"**: RNA velocity suggests early injured cells may still revert to healthy, while more advanced cells flow only towards the inflammatory state; in mouse IRI and R-UUO, injured PT declines during recovery while inflammatory PT persists longer; inflammatory PT scores much higher on the irradiation-senescence signature.
- <!-- ZH --> **空间定位**:CosMx 与多重 IF 均显示只有炎症 PT(VCAM1+ICAM1+)与单核细胞、单核–巨噬过渡细胞、树突细胞和肌成纤维细胞共定位于纤维化 niche,损伤 PT 则分散分布;同一小管内细胞状态很少混杂;ICAM1 位于炎症 PT 的管腔膜,可拴住尿腔中的髓系细胞。在 IgA 肾病等活检中,炎症 PT 比例与肌成纤维细胞、单核细胞、cDC 比例及纤维化严重度正相关,与 eGFR 负相关。 <!-- EN --> **Spatial localization**: both CosMx and multiplex IF show that only inflammatory PT (VCAM1+ICAM1+) co-localizes with monocytes, transitioning monocyte–macrophages, dendritic cells and myofibroblasts in the fibrotic niche, while injured PT is diffusely scattered; cell states rarely intermix within one tubule; ICAM1 sits on the luminal membrane of inflammatory PT and may tether myeloid cells in the urinary space. In biopsies (e.g. IgA nephropathy), inflammatory-PT proportion correlates positively with myofibroblasts, monocytes, cDCs and fibrosis severity and inversely with eGFR.
- <!-- ZH --> **信号**:炎症 PT 是纤维化 niche 中趋化因子转录本的主要来源(如 CCL2–CCR2),并表达 PDGF、TGF、FGF 受体的多种配体;同时上调 FAS、TWEAK、TNF/TRAIL 受体,提示双向和自分泌的前馈回路。 <!-- EN --> **Signalling**: inflammatory PT is the main source of chemokine transcripts in the fibrotic niche (e.g. CCL2–CCR2) and expresses multiple ligands for PDGF, TGF and FGF receptors; it also up-regulates FAS, TWEAK and TNF/TRAIL receptors, suggesting bidirectional and autocrine feed-forward loops.
- <!-- ZH --> **调控**:下调的 PT 功能基因(CUBN、SLC34A1 等)由 HNF1α/HNF4α 主导的 TF 模块 A 控制,HNF4α 疑为主调控因子并自我调控其位点 —— 持续损伤打断这一自调控环,导致 PT 身份不可逆丢失;小鼠 IRI 中 HNF4α 靶基因 4 小时内下调,恢复的细胞 48 小时内回升,而炎症 PT 直到 6 周仍维持低水平。炎症程序由 AP-1(约 20% 可及 CRE 含其 motif)与 NF-κB1(晚期 CRE 更富集)驱动,CUT&RUN 证实 JUN 结合 CDKN1A、HDAC9、HAVCR1、VCAM1、ICAM1、PDGFA 附近的 CRE。 <!-- EN --> **Regulation**: down-regulated PT function genes (CUBN, SLC34A1, …) are controlled by a TF module A led by HNF1α/HNF4α; HNF4α appears to be the master regulator and auto-regulates its own locus — sustained injury breaks this loop, causing irreversible loss of PT identity; in mouse IRI, HNF4α targets drop within 4 h and recover within 48 h in cells that recover, but stay low in inflammatory PT through 6 weeks. The inflammatory program is driven by AP-1 (motifs in ~20% of accessible CREs) and NF-κB1 (enriched in late CREs); CUT&RUN confirms JUN binding at CREs near CDKN1A, HDAC9, HAVCR1, VCAM1, ICAM1 and PDGFA.
- <!-- ZH --> **逆转**:T5224 减轻 IRI 后的肾重量下降、炎症(IBA1)与纤维化(PSR),降低 KIM1+VCAM1+ 炎症 PT 比例及 Icam1、Vcam1、Pdgfa 表达;ABT-263 在 R-UUO 中显著降低炎症 PT 基因表达,并(经 bulk 反卷积)降低炎症 PT 比例。 <!-- EN --> **Reversion**: T5224 attenuates post-IRI loss of kidney mass, inflammation (IBA1) and fibrosis (PSR), and lowers the KIM1+VCAM1+ inflammatory-PT fraction and Icam1, Vcam1, Pdgfa expression; ABT-263 in R-UUO markedly reduces inflammatory-PT genes and (by bulk deconvolution) the inflammatory-PT fraction.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把小鼠「failed repair」概念落到人类肾病上,并用 ICAM1 把它从更宽泛的 adaptive/injured PT 中区分出来(人类中 VCAM1 两态共有,ICAM1 特异)。 <!-- EN --> Translates the mouse "failed repair" concept to human disease and uses ICAM1 to separate it from the broader adaptive/injured PT (in humans VCAM1 marks both, ICAM1 is specific).
- <!-- ZH --> 在单细胞分辨率空间数据中把这一状态定位到纤维化 niche,并给出可在活检中使用的抗体面板(VCAM1/ICAM1)。 <!-- EN --> Localizes the state to the fibrotic niche at single-cell spatial resolution and provides a biopsy-usable antibody panel (VCAM1/ICAM1).
- <!-- ZH --> 从「状态 → TF 程序 → 药理干预」完整走通:HNF4α 崩溃 + AP-1/NF-κB,AP-1 抑制与衰老清除在小鼠中有效。 <!-- EN --> Goes the full way "state → TF program → pharmacological intervention": HNF4α collapse + AP-1/NF-κB, with AP-1 inhibition and senolysis effective in mice.
- <!-- ZH --> 混池 + SNP 反卷积的 Multiome 设计,同时降低批次并检出跨供者双细胞。 <!-- EN --> A pooled Multiome design with SNP deconvolution that both reduces batch effects and detects cross-donor doublets.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 人类样本来自肿瘤肾切除的非肿瘤部分,UUO 作为损伤范式;样本量小(5 vs 7),对照有轻度肾小球硬化(队列平均 71 岁)。 <!-- EN --> Human tissue is the non-tumour part of tumour nephrectomies with UUO as the injury paradigm; small n (5 vs 7), and controls have mild age-related glomerulosclerosis (mean age 71).
- <!-- ZH --> 「不可回头点」主要来自 RNA velocity 与小鼠时间序列的推断,人类中无纵向证据;作者也指出需要早期活检来判断炎症态是起因还是继发于浸润白细胞。 <!-- EN --> The "point of no return" rests on RNA velocity and mouse time courses; there is no longitudinal human evidence, and the authors note early biopsies are needed to tell whether the inflammatory state initiates or follows leukocyte infiltration.
- <!-- ZH --> 干预只在小鼠中,T5224 与 ABT-263 均为全身给药,非细胞特异;ABT-263 对炎症 PT 比例的影响来自 bulk 反卷积。小鼠中 VCAM1 的特异性与人类不同。 <!-- EN --> Interventions are mouse-only and systemic, not cell-specific; ABT-263's effect on inflammatory-PT fraction comes from bulk deconvolution. VCAM1 specificity differs between mouse and human.
- <!-- ZH --> snRNA-seq 的细胞比例受解离偏差影响(作者自陈),CosMx 用于部分弥补。 <!-- EN --> snRNA-seq proportions are subject to dissociation bias (acknowledged), partly compensated by CosMx.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是本仓库里**肾脏方向最完整的 detect → model → revert 范例**:**detect** —— 在单细胞与空间数据中识别出一个疾病特异、偏离健康 PT 的状态(VCAM1+ICAM1+);**model** —— 用 TF–CRE 网络解释它为何被锁定(HNF4α 自调控环断裂 + AP-1/NF-κB);**revert** —— 用 AP-1 抑制剂与衰老清除剂在小鼠中把它拉回去,并减轻纤维化。对 **Plan A** 的具体价值:**(1) 专家可验证的正样本** —— VCAM1+ICAM1+ 炎症 PT 与纤维化 niche 是 KidneyAnomalyBench 理想的「真实疾病异常」类别,且有 IF 抗体面板作为与转录组正交的蛋白层验证(跨模态一致性);**(2) 异常应分级** —— 「可逆的损伤 PT」与「不可逆的炎症 PT」都偏离健康,但临床意义不同,基准应区分「偏离程度」与「是否可逆」;**(3) 伪影风险** —— 炎症 PT 恰好贴着巨噬细胞与肌成纤维细胞,在 Xenium/CosMx 上极易被转录本溢出污染(见 `xenium-split-natmethods-2026`、`resolvi-natmethods-2026`),所以它也是检验「去伪影会不会抹掉真异常」的最佳测试例;**(4) 数据** —— 6K 与 1K CosMx 数据(含 IgA 肾病活检)和 Multiome 全部公开,可作为外部验证集。对 **revert**:HNF4α/AP-1 给出了明确的、可在虚拟组织中模拟的扰动靶点。对 **Plan B**:本文的结论强度不一 —— 有 CUT&RUN 与小鼠干预支持的(AP-1)、只有相关性的(KPMP 比例相关)、只来自推断的(RNA velocity 的「不可回头点」)—— 很适合用来构建 claim-to-evidence 校验中「证据类型分级」的标注样例。
<!-- EN --> This is the repo's **most complete kidney detect → model → revert exemplar**: **detect** — identify a disease-specific state departing from healthy PT (VCAM1+ICAM1+) in single-cell and spatial data; **model** — explain via TF–CRE networks why it is locked in (broken HNF4α auto-regulatory loop + AP-1/NF-κB); **revert** — pull it back in mice with an AP-1 inhibitor and a senolytic, reducing fibrosis. Concrete value for **Plan A**: **(1) Expert-verifiable positives** — VCAM1+ICAM1+ inflammatory PT and the fibrotic niche are an ideal "genuine disease anomaly" class for KidneyAnomalyBench, with an IF antibody panel as an orthogonal protein-level check (cross-modal agreement); **(2) Anomalies should be graded** — "reversible injured PT" and "irreversible inflammatory PT" both depart from healthy but mean different things, so the benchmark should separate "degree of deviation" from "reversibility"; **(3) Artifact risk** — inflammatory PT sits right next to macrophages and myofibroblasts and is highly exposed to transcript spillover on Xenium/CosMx (see `xenium-split-natmethods-2026`, `resolvi-natmethods-2026`), making it the best test case for "does de-artifacting erase real anomalies?"; **(4) Data** — the 6K and 1K CosMx (incl. IgA-nephropathy biopsies) and Multiome data are all public, usable as an external validation set. For **revert**: HNF4α/AP-1 are explicit perturbation targets that a virtual tissue could simulate. For **Plan B**: claim strength varies — some backed by CUT&RUN and mouse intervention (AP-1), some purely correlational (KPMP proportion correlations), some inferred only (the RNA-velocity "point of no return") — ideal for building annotated examples of evidence-type grading in claim-to-evidence verification.
