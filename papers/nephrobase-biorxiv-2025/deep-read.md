## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Susztak 组从零预训练了首个肾脏专用单细胞基础模型 Nephrobase Cell+(1B 与 500M 两个版本):约 39.5M 个细胞/核、4,319 个样本、四个物种(人、小鼠、大鼠、猪)、scRNA / snRNA / snATAC / CosMx / Xenium 多模态,约 1000 亿 token;encoder-decoder Transformer + MoE + 对抗去批次;在留出的人(KPMP 2025 Q2)与小鼠肾数据上,聚类、批次整合、跨物种对齐与「零样本」注释均优于 Geneformer、scGPT、UCE、PCA 与 autoencoder。
<!-- EN --> The Susztak lab pretrains from scratch the first kidney-specific single-cell foundation model, Nephrobase Cell+ (1B and 500M variants): ~39.5M cells/nuclei, 4,319 samples, four species (human, mouse, rat, pig), scRNA / snRNA / snATAC / CosMx / Xenium, ~100B tokens; an encoder-decoder transformer with MoE and adversarial batch removal. On held-out human (KPMP 2025 Q2) and mouse kidney data it beats Geneformer, scGPT, UCE, PCA and an autoencoder on clustering, batch integration, cross-species alignment and "zero-shot" annotation.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 通用单细胞基础模型大量涌现,但没有肾脏专用模型,也不清楚「器官专用」能否胜过「通用」。肾的细胞结构复杂、微环境动态,大规模单细胞 + 空间数据整合时批次效应、跨模态差异与跨物种泛化都是难点(KPMP 等图谱项目的老问题)。
<!-- EN --> General single-cell foundation models abound, but there is no kidney-specific model, and it is unclear whether organ-focused models beat generalist ones. The kidney's complex architecture and dynamic microenvironments make large-scale single-cell + spatial integration hard: batch effects, cross-modality variation and cross-species generalisation (long-standing issues for atlases like KPMP).

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **输入/编码**:细胞×基因矩阵;每个基因由身份、归一化表达值(sum-log 归一化)及可选元数据/扰动信息组成 token,通过 cross-attention 嵌入;固定的 32,768 个直系同源基因特征空间。**架构**:encoder 自注意力、decoder 交叉注意力;RMSNorm;稀疏 top-k 路由的 Mixture-of-Experts(含 shared expert 与负载均衡损失)。**目标**:掩码生成式预训练,重建头用 ZINB 似然;分类头用 focal loss(按有效样本数做类别加权,缺失标签样本跳过);Elastic Cell Similarity 正则(防止嵌入坍缩,目标余弦相似度阈值如 0.5);监督对比损失(同标签拉近);带梯度反转层的 domain / assay 对抗判别器(assay 对抗权重随 epoch 递增)。**注意**:模型训练使用了细胞类型标签(监督对比 + 分类头),因此文中的「零样本注释」是指在留出数据上不再微调、直接用已训练的分类头,而非无监督意义上的零样本。**扰动**:随机抽 5,000 个细胞,将目标基因表达加倍,看预测的差异表达并做 GSEA。
<!-- EN --> **Input/encoding**: a cell×gene matrix; each gene token combines identity, normalised expression (sum-log normalisation) and optional metadata/perturbation, embedded via cross-attention; a fixed 32,768 orthologous-gene feature space. **Architecture**: encoder self-attention, decoder cross-attention; RMSNorm; sparse top-k Mixture-of-Experts (with a shared expert and load-balancing loss). **Objectives**: masked generative pretraining with a ZINB reconstruction head; a focal-loss classification head (effective-number class weights, missing labels skipped); Elastic Cell Similarity regulariser (prevents embedding collapse, target cosine threshold e.g. 0.5); supervised contrastive loss (pulls same-label cells together); domain/assay adversarial discriminators with gradient reversal (assay weight ramps up over epochs). **Note**: training uses cell-type labels (supervised contrastive + classification head), so the paper's "zero-shot annotation" means applying the trained head to held-out data without further fine-tuning — not zero-shot in the unsupervised sense. **Perturbation**: sample 5,000 cells, double a target gene's expression, rank predicted DE genes and run GSEA.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 预训练语料:4,319 个样本、约 39.5M 个谱 —— 人 25.2M、小鼠 12.5M、大鼠 1.4M、猪 0.293M;约 30M 来自肾组织,约 10M 来自免疫/外周(含健康与 CKD 患者 PBMC);也包括胎肾与类器官。Assay 构成:scRNA ~48.7%、snRNA ~25.4%、CosMx ~7.7%、snATAC ~6.2%、Xenium ~5.5%。细胞类型以 PT(27.4%)、T 细胞(19.1%)、内皮(8.8%)为主。来源:GEO、SRA、HCA、CELLxGENE、KPMP 及大量自产数据(CosMx ~3M、Xenium ~2M、sn/sc/snATAC ~3M)。评测:留出的 KPMP 2025 Q2 人肾数据、一个小鼠发育模型数据、一个人鼠混合肾数据集。自产数据 GEO 号在 v1 中尚为占位符(***)。
<!-- EN --> Pretraining corpus: 4,319 samples, ~39.5M profiles — human 25.2M, mouse 12.5M, rat 1.4M, pig 0.293M; ~30M from kidney tissue, ~10M immune/peripheral (incl. PBMCs from healthy and CKD donors); also fetal kidney and organoids. Assays: scRNA ~48.7%, snRNA ~25.4%, CosMx ~7.7%, snATAC ~6.2%, Xenium ~5.5%. Cell types dominated by PT (27.4%), T cells (19.1%), endothelium (8.8%). Sources: GEO, SRA, HCA, CELLxGENE, KPMP plus substantial in-house data (CosMx ~3M, Xenium ~2M, sn/sc/snATAC ~3M). Evaluation: held-out KPMP 2025 Q2 human kidney data, a mouse developmental dataset, and a mixed human–mouse kidney dataset. In v1 the GEO accession for the in-house data is still a placeholder (***).

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **人肾(KPMP 留出)**:KMeans ARI 1B 模型 0.82(scGPT 0.40、autoencoder 0.55、Geneformer 0.22),NMI 0.78(scGPT 0.48),silhouette ~0.68;cLISI 1.00,iLISI ~0.17–0.18,kBET 0.25–0.28(scGPT 0.09);总整合分 0.71(1B)/0.70(500M),所有方法中最高。 <!-- EN --> **Human kidney (held-out KPMP)**: KMeans ARI 0.82 for 1B (scGPT 0.40, autoencoder 0.55, Geneformer 0.22), NMI 0.78 (scGPT 0.48), silhouette ~0.68; cLISI 1.00, iLISI ~0.17–0.18, kBET 0.25–0.28 (scGPT 0.09); total integration score 0.71 (1B) / 0.70 (500M), highest of all methods.
- <!-- ZH --> **小鼠**:ARI ~0.70,cLISI 1.0,总分 0.69–0.68,优于 UCE、PCA、scGPT。 <!-- EN --> **Mouse**: ARI ~0.70, cLISI 1.0, total 0.69–0.68, above UCE, PCA, scGPT.
- <!-- ZH --> **跨物种**:500M 模型 NMI 0.75 / ARI 0.72(Geneformer 0.44/0.22,scGPT 0.62/0.43);1B 模型 NMI 0.73 / ARI 0.57;iLISI 0.01,graph connectivity 与 PCR ~0.94;总分 0.63(500M)/0.61(1B),Geneformer 0.50、UCE 0.51。即较小的 500M 模型在跨物种上略好。 <!-- EN --> **Cross-species**: 500M NMI 0.75 / ARI 0.72 (Geneformer 0.44/0.22, scGPT 0.62/0.43); 1B NMI 0.73 / ARI 0.57; iLISI 0.01, graph connectivity and PCR ~0.94; total 0.63 (500M) / 0.61 (1B) vs Geneformer 0.50, UCE 0.51 — the smaller 500M model is slightly better cross-species.
- <!-- ZH --> **注释**:人与小鼠主要谱系 >90% 正确(如 PT),误差主要在相近亚型(远曲小管 vs 连接小管)之间;与「更简单方法」的比较「未展示」。 <!-- EN --> **Annotation**: >90% correct for major lineages (e.g. PT) in human and mouse, errors mostly between related subtypes (distal vs connecting tubule); comparison against simpler methods is "not shown".
- <!-- ZH --> **in silico 扰动**:CCL2、VCAM1 加倍富集免疫黏附/趋化通路;GDF15 富集生长调控与 MAPK;SOX4 富集肾形态发生与氧化磷酸化 —— 仅为定性 GO 结果,无实验或基线验证。 <!-- EN --> **In silico perturbation**: doubling CCL2 and VCAM1 enriches immune adhesion/chemotaxis; GDF15 growth control and MAPK; SOX4 kidney morphogenesis and oxidative phosphorylation — qualitative GO results only, no experimental or baseline validation.

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 第一个肾专用、跨四物种、跨五种 assay 的单细胞基础模型,规模达 1B 参数。 <!-- EN --> The first kidney-specific single-cell foundation model spanning four species and five assays, up to 1B parameters.
- <!-- ZH --> 把 ZINB 重建、MoE、监督对比、ECS 与对抗去批次组合在一个 encoder-decoder 中。 <!-- EN --> Combines ZINB reconstruction, MoE, supervised contrastive learning, ECS and adversarial batch removal in one encoder-decoder.
- <!-- ZH --> 在 KPMP 留出数据上提供了与通用基础模型的直接对比,初步回答「器官专用 vs 通用」。 <!-- EN --> Gives a direct comparison against generalist foundation models on held-out KPMP data, a first answer to "organ-specific vs generalist".

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> 预印本,未同行评审;未见公开代码/权重链接,自产数据 GEO 号为占位符 —— 目前不可复现。 <!-- EN --> Preprint, not peer reviewed; no public code/weights link found and the in-house GEO accession is a placeholder — not currently reproducible.
- <!-- ZH --> **评测口径存疑**:训练用了细胞类型标签与监督对比损失,所谓「零样本」注释与聚类优势部分来自监督信号,与 Geneformer/scGPT 的无监督零样本嵌入不完全可比;文中把低 iLISI 解释为「批次分离少」、把 kBET「越低越好」,与 scib 惯例不一致,需要读者自行核对。 <!-- EN --> **Evaluation caveats**: training uses cell-type labels and supervised contrastive loss, so the "zero-shot" annotation and clustering advantages partly reflect supervision and are not fully comparable to unsupervised zero-shot embeddings from Geneformer/scGPT; the paper reads low iLISI as "minimal batch separation" and kBET as "lower is better", which departs from usual scib conventions — readers should verify.
- <!-- ZH --> 缺少 HVG + scVI/Harmony 这类强传统基线(只比了 PCA 与 autoencoder);与简单方法的注释比较「未展示」。 <!-- EN --> Missing strong conventional baselines like HVG + scVI/Harmony (only PCA and an autoencoder); annotation comparison against simpler methods "not shown".
- <!-- ZH --> 扰动实验只是「表达加倍 + GSEA」的定性展示,没有对照线性/加性基线或实验验证;作者自陈只能捕获相关性。 <!-- EN --> The perturbation experiment is a qualitative "double expression + GSEA" demo with no linear/additive baseline or experimental validation; the authors concede correlation only.
- <!-- ZH --> 固定 32,768 基因空间;蛋白/影像等模态未纳入;训练成本高,普通实验室难以重训。 <!-- EN --> Fixed 32,768-gene space; proteomics/imaging not included; heavy training cost limits retraining by typical labs.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是课题 1 最直接的「模型层」候选:同为肾、同样覆盖 snRNA + snATAC + Xenium(+ CosMx),且语料包含 KPMP 与 CKD 样本 —— 若能拿到权重,它的嵌入是构建「健康肾流形」并在上面做异常打分的现成骨干,其对抗去批次也正对准我们多中心、多协议的批次问题。但三点必须在课题 1 中自己验证,不能直接采信:**(1) 去批次可能「过头」** —— 对抗去 assay 信号会同时抹掉 assay 特异可见的真实状态(`kidney-multimodal-genomebiol-2026` 显示 WFDC2⁺ aTAL、Norn 只在部分协议中可见),对异常检测而言这正是要保留的信号;**(2) 监督训练会把嵌入拉向已知细胞类型**,新的/中间的疾病状态可能被吸附到最近的已知类别,从而降低异常可检出性 —— 需要用 KidneyAnomalyBench 的已知阳性状态检验;**(3) 必须补上 HVG/scVI 基线与零样本口径**(参见 `scfm-zeroshot-genomebiol-2025`)。对 revert 阶段,它的「表达加倍 + GSEA」扰动正是 `linear-baselines-natmethods-2025` 所批评的、缺乏基线的扰动 claim。对课题 2,这篇是一个现成的 claim-to-evidence 练习对象:「远超所有现有模型」「>90% 零样本准确率」等 claim 与其证据(监督训练、缺失基线、指标解读)之间存在可被 verifier 系统性标注的落差。
<!-- EN --> This is Topic 1's most direct "model-layer" candidate: same organ, same snRNA + snATAC + Xenium (+ CosMx), with KPMP and CKD samples in the corpus — if weights become available, its embeddings are a ready backbone for building a "healthy kidney manifold" and scoring anomalies on it, and its adversarial batch removal targets exactly our multicentre, multi-protocol batch problem. But three things must be verified in Topic 1 rather than taken on trust: **(1) batch removal may overshoot** — adversarially removing assay signal also erases real states visible only in some assays (`kidney-multimodal-genomebiol-2026` shows WFDC2⁺ aTAL and Norn are protocol-specific), which is precisely the signal anomaly detection must keep; **(2) supervised training pulls embeddings toward known cell types**, so novel/intermediate disease states may be absorbed into the nearest known class, lowering anomaly detectability — test with KidneyAnomalyBench's known-positive states; **(3) HVG/scVI baselines and a true zero-shot protocol must be added** (see `scfm-zeroshot-genomebiol-2025`). For the revert stage, its "double expression + GSEA" perturbation is exactly the baseline-free perturbation claim criticised by `linear-baselines-natmethods-2025`. For Topic 2, the paper is a ready claim-to-evidence exercise: claims like "far surpassing all existing models" and ">90% zero-shot accuracy" versus their evidence (supervised training, missing baselines, metric interpretation) show gaps a verifier should systematically flag.
