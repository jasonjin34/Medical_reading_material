## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> Edison Scientific / FutureHouse 的 Kosmos 是面向「数据驱动发现」的 AI scientist:给定开放式目标与数据集,最长运行 12 小时,用一个**结构化 world model** 在并行的数据分析 agent 与文献检索 agent 之间共享信息,单次运行平均约 200 次 agent rollout、写约 42,000 行代码、读约 1,500 篇论文,报告中每句话都引用代码 notebook 或原始文献;独立科学家核验 102 条陈述,79.4% 正确(数据分析类 85.5%、文献类 82.1%、解释综合类仅 57.9%)。
<!-- EN --> Kosmos (Edison Scientific / FutureHouse) is an AI scientist for data-driven discovery: given an open-ended objective and a dataset it runs for up to 12 hours, sharing information between parallel data-analysis and literature-search agents through a **structured world model** — on average ~200 agent rollouts, ~42,000 lines of code and ~1,500 papers per run — and every report statement cites a code notebook or primary literature. Independent scientists checked 102 statements: 79.4% accurate (data-analysis 85.5%, literature 82.1%, interpretive synthesis only 57.9%).

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 现有自动科研 agent 能执行的动作数有限,超过一定步数就失去连贯性,发现的深度因此受限:Robin 的 agent 间上下文共享有限且偏向疗法开发;Sakana AI Scientist 局限于机器学习;Google Co-Scientist 只推理不做实验或数据分析;Virtual Lab 缺乏探索性数据分析能力。Kosmos 要回答:能否让 agent 在一个目标下**连贯地运行数百次 rollout**,对高维数据做大规模探索,并让结论可追溯?
<!-- EN --> Current research agents can take only so many actions before losing coherence, capping the depth of findings: Robin shares little context between agents and targets therapeutics; Sakana's AI Scientist is limited to ML; Google's Co-Scientist reasons but does not run or analyse experiments; the Virtual Lab lacks exploratory data analysis. Kosmos asks whether an agent can **coherently sustain hundreds of rollouts** toward one objective, explore high-dimensional data at scale, and keep conclusions traceable.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **循环**:科学家提供研究目标与数据集;每个 cycle 中 Kosmos 并行执行最多 10 个文献检索与数据分析任务(分别调用 Edison 的通用数据分析 agent 与文献检索 agent),把任务输出摘要写入 world model;再查询 world model 提出下一轮任务。这种上下文管理让它能同时探索多条研究路线,运行轮数是已有系统的 8 倍。完成后综合出 3–4 份科学报告,每条陈述与图都引用一篇文献或一个 Jupyter notebook。**评估**:(1) 从 3 份代表性报告抽 102 条陈述,标注为数据分析 / 文献 / 解释三类,由专家在**看不到原始代码与引文**的情况下独立复现或查证,判为 Supported / Refuted(Unsure 经澄清后重判);(2) 按读论文 15 分钟、做一个 notebook 分析 2 小时、每月 174 工时估算等效专家时间;(3) 请合作学术组在第 5、10、20 cycle 评估等效研究时间、有价值发现数、新颖性与推理深度。
<!-- EN --> **Loop**: a scientist supplies an objective and dataset; each cycle Kosmos runs up to ten literature-search and data-analysis tasks in parallel (via Edison's general data-analysis and literature agents), writes task-output summaries into the world model, then queries it to propose the next cycle's tasks. This context management lets it pursue many lines at once and run eight times as many iterations as prior systems. At the end it synthesizes three or four reports, each statement and figure citing a paper or a Jupyter notebook. **Evaluation**: (1) 102 statements from three representative reports, labelled as data-analysis / literature / interpretation, independently reproduced or checked by experts **without access to the original code or citations**, judged Supported/Refuted (Unsure re-judged after clarification); (2) expert-equivalent time estimated at 15 min per paper, 2 h per notebook analysis and 174 work-hours per month; (3) collaborating academic groups rated expert-equivalent time, valuable findings, novelty and reasoning depth at cycles 5, 10 and 20.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> 七个 discovery 各用不同数据与领域:(1) 小鼠脑低温神经保护的非靶向代谢组(对照 6、低温 6、常温 5);(2) 钙钛矿太阳能电池制备环境数据;(3) 神经元连接组;(4) 血浆蛋白组 GWAS + 心肌 T1 弛豫 GWAS(公开数据,Mendelian randomization);(5) 胰岛 10x multiome(GSE124742、GSE164875)+ DIAMANTE T2D GWAS;(6) AD 患者 tau 阳性/阴性神经元的激光显微切割蛋白组(按伪时间排序);(7) 6–28 月龄小鼠内嗅皮层与对照皮层的单核转录组。部分数据来自未发表或「准备中」的手稿。
<!-- EN --> Each of the seven discoveries uses a different data type and field: (1) untargeted brain metabolomics of hypothermic neuroprotection in mice (control 6, hypothermic 6, normothermic 5); (2) perovskite solar-cell fabrication environment data; (3) neuronal connectomics; (4) plasma-proteome GWAS + myocardial T1 GWAS (public, Mendelian randomization); (5) islet 10x multiome (GSE124742, GSE164875) + DIAMANTE T2D GWAS; (6) laser-capture proteomics of tau-positive/negative neurons in Alzheimer's disease ordered on pseudotime; (7) single-nucleus transcriptomes of entorhinal vs control cortex in mice aged 6–28 months. Several datasets come from unpublished or in-preparation manuscripts.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **规模**:单次运行平均 166 次数据分析 rollout 与 36 次文献 rollout,约 42,000 行代码、1,500 篇全文;代码生成量是 Robin 的 9.8 倍。 <!-- EN --> **Scale**: per run, on average 166 data-analysis and 36 literature rollouts, ~42,000 lines of code and 1,500 full papers; 9.8× Robin's code generation.
- <!-- ZH --> **陈述准确率**(102 条):总体 79.4%;数据分析类 85.5% 可复现、文献类 82.1% 有原始来源支撑、解释/综合类仅 57.9% 正确。 <!-- EN --> **Statement accuracy** (102 statements): 79.4% overall; 85.5% of data-analysis statements reproducible, 82.1% of literature statements supported by primary sources, only 57.9% of interpretive/synthesis statements accurate.
- <!-- ZH --> **等效工作量**:按任务计数估算每次运行约 4.1 个专家月(n = 6);合作组估计 20-cycle 运行的发现需要他们 6.14 个月(n = 7),且从 cycle 5 到 20 约翻倍;有价值发现数随 cycle 线性增长(测到 20),cycle 20 的发现被评为中到完全新颖、推理深度中到高。 <!-- EN --> **Equivalent effort**: task counting estimates ~4.1 expert-months per run (n = 6); collaborators estimate a 20-cycle run's findings would take them 6.14 months (n = 7), roughly doubling from cycle 5 to 20; valuable findings scale linearly with cycles (tested to 20), and cycle-20 findings are rated moderate-to-complete novelty and moderate-to-high reasoning depth.
- <!-- ZH --> **七个发现**:3 个独立重现了运行时未接触过的预印本或未发表结果(低温下脑内核苷酸代谢为主要改变通路;退火湿度决定钙钛矿性能;神经元连接度呈对数正态分布);2 个为已有发现补充新证据(MR 分析提示高循环 SOD2 可能因果性地减少心肌纤维化;T2D 保护性变异 rs9379084 对 SSR1 的顺式调控);1 个自主提出新分析方法(用分段回归在 AD 伪时间上定位 ECM 通路下降的断点,Davies 检验 p = 0.017);1 个新的临床相关发现(衰老中内嗅皮层神经元易损机制,涉及 flippase Atp10a 下降与小胶质激活,并在独立数据与人类 Braak 分期中验证)。 <!-- EN --> **Seven discoveries**: three independently reproduce preprinted or unpublished results not accessed at runtime (nucleotide metabolism as the dominant pathway altered in hypothermic brain; annealing humidity as the critical determinant of perovskite performance; log-normal neuronal connectivity); two add new support to existing findings (Mendelian randomization suggesting high circulating SOD2 may causally reduce myocardial fibrosis; cis-regulation of SSR1 by the protective T2D variant rs9379084); one devises a new analysis (segmented regression locating a breakpoint in ECM-pathway decline along Alzheimer's pseudotime, Davies test p = 0.017); one makes a novel clinically relevant finding (entorhinal-neuron vulnerability in ageing involving declining flippase Atp10a and microglial activation, validated in an independent dataset and human Braak stages).

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> **结构化 world model 作为多 agent 的共享上下文**,把连贯运行从几十步推到数百次 rollout,是本文的核心工程贡献。 <!-- EN --> **A structured world model as shared context for many agents**, pushing coherent operation from tens of steps to hundreds of rollouts — the core engineering contribution.
- <!-- ZH --> **全陈述可追溯**:报告里每句话都链接到产生它的 notebook 或文献,使独立复核成为可能。 <!-- EN --> **Every statement traceable**: each report sentence links to the notebook or paper that produced it, enabling independent re-checking.
- <!-- ZH --> **按陈述类型分层的准确率评估**(数据 / 文献 / 解释),并由看不到原始证据的专家独立复现——评估 AI 科学家时少见的严格做法。 <!-- EN --> **Accuracy evaluation stratified by statement type** (data / literature / interpretation), with experts reproducing blind to the original evidence — an unusually rigorous way to evaluate an AI scientist.
- <!-- ZH --> 跨代谢组、材料、连接组、统计遗传、蛋白组、转录组六类数据的发现展示。 <!-- EN --> Discoveries demonstrated across metabolomics, materials, connectomics, statistical genetics, proteomics and transcriptomics.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **解释类陈述只有 57.9% 正确**,作者归因于倾向把统计显著混同为科学重要;还会发明统计上合理但概念晦涩的指标;独立评估者指出它常给出过强的结论。 <!-- EN --> **Interpretive statements are only 57.9% accurate**, attributed to conflating statistical significance with scientific value; it also invents statistically sound but conceptually obscure metrics; evaluators note it tends to make overly strong claims.
- <!-- ZH --> **没有自动化方法判断一条 claim 是否准确、新颖、重要**:一份报告 3–4 个发现叙事,每个约 25 条 claim、基于 8–9 条轨迹,筛选全靠领域专家,规模化识别有价值发现仍是瓶颈。 <!-- EN --> **No automated way to judge whether a claim is accurate, novel and significant**: a report has 3–4 discovery narratives of ~25 claims each, built on 8–9 trajectories, and triage relies entirely on domain experts — identifying valuable discoveries at scale remains a bottleneck.
- <!-- ZH --> **随机性**:多次独立运行不一定收敛到相同发现;研究方向对目标措辞敏感;输入数据的预处理会显著改变结果焦点。 <!-- EN --> **Stochasticity**: independent runs may not converge on the same discoveries; directions are sensitive to objective phrasing; input preprocessing markedly changes the focus of results.
- <!-- ZH --> 技术限制:数据集上限约 5GB;不擅长原始数据(图像、原始测序文件);不能自主获取外部公共数据做参照或正交验证;中途不能人机交互。准确率评估只抽了 3 份报告、102 条陈述。 <!-- EN --> Technical limits: datasets up to ~5 GB; weak on raw data (images, raw sequencing files); cannot fetch external public data for reference or orthogonal validation; no mid-run human interaction. The accuracy audit sampled only 102 statements from three reports.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> Kosmos 几乎就是 **课题 2 的研究对象原型**:一个分析组学数据、输出带引用结论的 agent,而它的作者自己承认三件事——解释类陈述只有约 58% 正确、多次运行不一定收敛、没有自动化方法判断 claim 的正确性。这三点分别对应课题 2 的三根支柱:**(1) claim-to-evidence verification**——Kosmos 已经把每条陈述链接到 notebook 或文献,天然提供了「claim–证据」对,我们的 verifier 可以直接在这种结构上工作,并沿用它的「数据 / 文献 / 解释」三分法分别报告;**(2) calibrated verifier**——57.9% vs 85.5% 的差距说明 verifier 必须按 claim 类型校准,解释类 claim 需要更低的先验置信度;**(3) rerun stability**——「独立运行不收敛」与「对措辞和预处理敏感」需要被量化,例如对同一肾脏数据集多次运行,统计每个发现的复现率。它的 Discovery 5(胰岛 10x multiome + GWAS)与 Discovery 7(单核转录组)正好是肾脏 snRNA/snATAC 数据的同类分析,可以作为构造**组学特定失败分类**的真实样本(把统计显著当作生物学重要、发明晦涩指标、不做外部数据的正交验证)。对 **课题 1** 也有一条线:Kosmos 不擅长原始图像且不能调用外部参考数据,而课题 1 的跨模态一致性恰好依赖 H&E 原图与 KPMP/HuBMAP 外部参考——这正是此类 agent 目前做不好的地方,说明课题 1 的检测流程应以专门工具的形式提供给 agent,而不是期望 agent 自己写出来。
<!-- EN --> Kosmos is almost the **prototype object of study for Topic 2**: an agent that analyses omics data and outputs cited conclusions, whose authors concede three things — interpretive statements are only ~58% accurate, independent runs may not converge, and there is no automated way to judge claim correctness. These map onto Topic 2's three pillars: **(1) claim-to-evidence verification** — Kosmos already links each statement to a notebook or paper, naturally yielding claim–evidence pairs our verifier can operate on, reporting separately along its data / literature / interpretation split; **(2) calibrated verifier** — the 57.9% vs 85.5% gap means calibration must be per claim type, with lower prior confidence for interpretive claims; **(3) rerun stability** — "runs don't converge" and "sensitive to phrasing and preprocessing" need quantifying, e.g. rerun on the same kidney dataset and measure each finding's reproduction rate. Its Discovery 5 (islet 10x multiome + GWAS) and Discovery 7 (single-nucleus transcriptomics) are the same kind of analysis as kidney snRNA/snATAC data, providing real cases for an **omics-specific failure taxonomy** (treating significance as biological importance, inventing obscure metrics, skipping orthogonal validation on external data). There is also a thread to **Topic 1**: Kosmos is weak on raw images and cannot pull external reference data, whereas Topic 1's cross-modal agreement depends on raw H&E and KPMP/HuBMAP references — exactly where such agents fail today, so Topic 1's detection pipeline should be exposed to agents as dedicated tools rather than expecting agents to write it themselves.
