## 一句话 / One-liner
<!-- ZH --> DrugCLIP 把虚拟筛选从"逐对打分的回归问题"改写成"**稠密检索**问题":用对比学习把蛋白口袋和分子编码到同一个向量空间,分子库可以**预先编码**好,筛选时只需算一次口袋向量再做余弦相似度检索,复杂度从 O(M×N) 降到 O(M+N),比对接快到 Science 摘要所说的"最多 1,000 万倍";在 DUD-E 与 LIT-PCBA 上超过对接与各类深度学习基线,湿实验上 NET(去甲肾上腺素转运体)命中率 15%、两个抑制剂解出了与靶点的复合物结构,TRIP12(无 holo 结构、无已知小分子配体的靶点)仅用 AlphaFold2 预测结构就拿到 17.5% 命中率;最后把约 1 万个人类蛋白 × 5 亿化合物的筛选结果做成开放数据库 GenomeScreenDB。
<!-- EN --> DrugCLIP recasts virtual screening from pairwise-scoring regression into a **dense-retrieval** problem: contrastive learning embeds protein pockets and molecules into one vector space, the compound library can be **pre-encoded**, and screening needs only one pocket embedding plus cosine-similarity retrieval, taking complexity from O(M×N) to O(M+N) — up to 10 million times faster than docking per the Science abstract. It beats docking and deep-learning baselines on DUD-E and LIT-PCBA; in the wet lab it reached a 15% hit rate for NET (norepinephrine transporter) with two inhibitors' complex structures solved, and a 17.5% hit rate for TRIP12 — a target with no holo structure and no known small-molecule binders — using only AlphaFold2-predicted structures. The screening results for ~10,000 human proteins against 500 million compounds are released as the open database GenomeScreenDB.

## 研究问题 / Problem
<!-- ZH --> 人类基因组约 2 万个编码蛋白中,只有大约 10% 有 FDA 批准药物或文献记载的小分子配体(预印本的说法是约 90% 缺少靶向干预手段),剩下的"未成药基因组"是机会也是难题。要系统地筛完所有靶点,实验高通量筛选不现实,只能靠虚拟筛选,但两条现有路线都卡住了:**分子对接**每个蛋白-配体对要几秒到几分钟,论文举的例子是用 10,000 个 CPU 核对单一靶点筛 10 亿分子要两周,放到基因组尺度完全不可行;**回归式深度学习**则受三个问题困扰——不同实验条件下的亲和力标签本身不一致(合并 IC50/Ki 会引入大量噪声)、训练集与真实筛选场景之间存在分布偏移(真实库里无活性分子的比例远高于精选训练集)、百万参数模型的推理速度本身就是瓶颈。论文要同时解决速度与鲁棒性。
<!-- EN --> Of the ~20,000 protein-coding genes in the human genome, only about 10% have FDA-approved drugs or documented small-molecule binders (the preprint puts it as ~90% lacking targeted intervention), leaving the "undrugged genome" as both opportunity and problem. Screening every target experimentally is impractical, so virtual screening is the only route — but both existing routes stall. **Molecular docking** needs seconds to minutes per protein–ligand pair; the paper's example is a campaign that took two weeks on 10,000 CPU cores to screen 1 billion molecules against a single target, hopeless at genome scale. **Regression-style deep learning** suffers from three problems: affinity labels from heterogeneous experimental conditions are mutually inconsistent (merging IC50/Ki values injects substantial noise), there is a distribution shift between training sets and real screening (real libraries hold far higher proportions of inactives than curated training sets), and inference speed of million-parameter models is itself a bottleneck. The paper targets speed and robustness at once.

## 方法 / Method
<!-- ZH --> **核心设计**:两个独立的编码器——分子编码器与口袋编码器——用对比损失训练,使"口袋与其真实 binder"这对正样本的相似度最大、"口袋与结合其他靶点的分子"这类负样本的相似度最小;筛选时用向量相似度(余弦)代表结合概率。这样做的两个直接好处:标签只需要"结不结合"的相对关系,绕开了亲和力数值的噪声;分子库可以离线预编码,线上只算一个口袋向量。**两阶段训练**:(1) **预训练**——分子编码器用现成的 Uni-Mol 初始化并冻结,口袋编码器随机初始化,通过对比学习向分子编码器对齐(作者称之为 contrastive distillation)。为了拿到足够多的预训练数据,作者提出 **ProFSA(Protein Fragment-Surrounding Alignment)**:从只有蛋白的结构里切出短肽片段当"伪配体",把它周围的区域当"伪口袋",经过片段切分、末端校正、邻居移除、口袋检测等一系列操作,在 PDB 上造出 **550 万** 对伪口袋-配体样本。预训练后的口袋编码器在口袋匹配、口袋性质预测等下游任务上,即便零样本也能超过不少有监督模型以及物理/知识驱动的模型。(2) **微调**——用实验测定的蛋白-配体复合物结构,对两个编码器一起用对比损失更新。**推理**:候选库先用训练好的分子编码器批量预编码;给定一个口袋,口袋编码器把它转成向量,再用余弦相似度取 top 配体。**面向 AlphaFold 结构的改造**:直接用 AF2 预测结构 + Fpocket 找最近口袋会显著掉分(DUD-E 上 EF1% 从 27.4% 掉到 13.3%),于是作者提出 **GenPack(Generation-Packing)**:先用 Fpocket 在 AF2 结构上检测初始口袋,去掉侧链,用一个以口袋 backbone 为条件的分子生成模型造分子(这些分子不一定可合成,目的只是把口袋定位得更准、把口袋构象诱导到更合适的状态),然后重新装回侧链,用物理力场精修整体构象。**基因组尺度流程**:对每个 AF2 预测结构,用同源结构比对(TM-align)、口袋检测软件(Fpocket)和生成式 AI 模型共同定义口袋;检索完成后再用一个 CADD 的 cluster-docking 流程,为每个口袋挑出化学多样且物理合理的分子。
<!-- EN --> **Core design**: two separate encoders — one for molecules, one for pockets — trained with a contrastive loss that maximizes similarity for positive pairs (a pocket and its true binder) and minimizes it for negatives (the pocket and molecules binding other targets); at screening time vector similarity (cosine) stands in for binding probability. Two immediate benefits: labels only need the relative "binds or not" relation, sidestepping affinity-value noise; and the library can be pre-encoded offline, leaving only one pocket embedding to compute online. **Two-stage training**: (1) **pretraining** — the molecule encoder is initialized from Uni-Mol and frozen while a randomly initialized pocket encoder is aligned to it by contrastive learning (the authors call this contrastive distillation). To get enough pretraining data they introduce **ProFSA (Protein Fragment-Surrounding Alignment)**: short peptide fragments cut from protein-only structures serve as pseudo-ligands and their surroundings as pseudo-pockets, and after fragment segmentation, terminal correction, neighbor removal and pocket detection this yields **5.5 million** pseudo pocket–ligand pairs from the PDB. The pretrained pocket encoder outperforms many supervised as well as physical and knowledge-based models on downstream tasks such as pocket matching and pocket property prediction, even zero-shot. (2) **Fine-tuning** — both encoders are updated with a contrastive loss on experimentally determined protein–ligand complex structures. **Inference**: the candidate library is pre-encoded in batch by the trained molecule encoder; for a given pocket the pocket encoder produces one vector and cosine similarity retrieves the top ligands. **Adapting to AlphaFold structures**: using AF2 structures directly with Fpocket's nearest pocket drops performance sharply (DUD-E EF1% from 27.4% to 13.3%), so the authors propose **GenPack (Generation-Packing)**: detect initial pockets with Fpocket on the AF2 structure, strip side chains, run a generative model conditioned on the pocket backbone to create molecules (not necessarily synthesizable — their purpose is to localize the pocket more precisely and induce a more suitable pocket conformation), then reintroduce side chains and refine with physical force fields. **Genome-scale pipeline**: pockets for each AF2 structure are defined jointly by homology alignment (TM-align), pocket-detection software (Fpocket) and generative AI models; after retrieval, a CADD cluster-docking pipeline selects chemically diverse and physically proper molecules for each pocket.

## 数据 / Data
<!-- ZH --> **预训练**:PDB 上用 ProFSA 造出的 550 万对伪口袋-配体。**微调**:实验测定的蛋白-配体复合物结构(预印本用的是 BioLiP2 数据库)。**in silico 基准**:DUD-E(含 102 个靶点的 22,886 个活性化合物)与 LIT-PCBA(约 8,000 个活性分子、以及数量级更大的非活性分子集合)。基线包括物理对接软件 Glide-SP、AutoDock Vina、Surflex,以及回归式机器学习模型 NNScore、RFScore、Pafnucy、OnionNet、PLANET、Gnina、BigBind。**湿实验靶点**(预印本):5HT2AR(5-羟色胺受体 2A,抗抑郁新靶点;因其结构在训练集中,作者专门训了一个定制模型,并把 90% 同源的实验结构从训练集剔除以防数据泄漏)与 NET(去甲肾上腺素转运体,抑郁与 ADHD 的成熟靶点,但其复合物结构到 2024 年才解出,因此是对泛化能力更强的考验)。5HT2AR 筛的是 ChemDiv 的 1,648,137 个现货分子。**基因组尺度筛选**:AlphaFold2 预测的全部人类蛋白,约 1 万个蛋白、2 万多个口袋,库为 ZINC 与 Enamine REAL 的 5 亿类药分子;整个万亿级打分在**单节点 8 张 A100 上 24 小时**完成,最终产出 200 多万个潜在 hit 及其复合物结构与打分。
<!-- EN --> **Pretraining**: 5.5 million pseudo pocket–ligand pairs built from the PDB with ProFSA. **Fine-tuning**: experimentally determined protein–ligand complex structures (the preprint draws these from the BioLiP2 database). **In-silico benchmarks**: DUD-E (22,886 actives across 102 targets) and LIT-PCBA (~8,000 actives plus an order-of-magnitude larger inactive set). Baselines include the physics-based docking programs Glide-SP, AutoDock Vina and Surflex, and the regression-style ML models NNScore, RFScore, Pafnucy, OnionNet, PLANET, Gnina and BigBind. **Wet-lab targets** (preprint): 5HT2AR (serotonin receptor 2A, an emerging antidepressant target; since its structure was in the training data the authors trained a customized model and excluded experimental structures at 90% homology to prevent leakage) and NET (norepinephrine transporter, an established target for depression and ADHD whose complex structures were only solved in 2024, making it a sterner generalization test). For 5HT2AR they screened 1,648,137 in-stock ChemDiv molecules. **Genome-scale screen**: all human proteins predicted by AlphaFold2 — ~10,000 proteins and over 20,000 pockets — against 500 million drug-like molecules from ZINC and Enamine REAL; the whole trillion-scale campaign finished in **24 hours on a single node with 8 A100 GPUs**, producing over 2 million potential hits with complex structures and docking scores.

## 主要结果 / Key results
- <!-- ZH --> **速度**:Science 摘要给的是"比对接最多快 1,000 万倍"(预印本的表述是比传统对接快约 10 万倍、比现有机器学习方法快约 100 倍——引用时注意版本差异)。具体数字:在 LIT-PCBA 上借助预编码加速,DrugCLIP 用 **38 秒**完成筛选,而 Glide 对接要 3 天、Uni-Dock 要 22 小时、机器学习模型 PLANET 要 3 小时。复杂度上 DrugCLIP 是 O(M+N)(M 个靶点、N 个化合物),而多数现有方法是 O(M×N)——这是基因组尺度筛选能成立的根本原因。 <!-- EN --> **Speed**: the Science abstract says up to 10 million times faster than docking (the preprint says ~100,000× faster than conventional docking and ~100× faster than existing ML methods — mind the version difference when citing). Concretely, on LIT-PCBA with pre-encoding DrugCLIP finishes screening in **38 seconds**, against 3 days for Glide docking, 22 hours for Uni-Dock and 3 hours for the ML model PLANET. Its complexity is O(M+N) for M targets and N compounds, where most methods are O(M×N) — the fundamental reason genome-scale screening becomes feasible.
- <!-- ZH --> **in silico 基准**:在 DUD-E 与 LIT-PCBA 上,以 EF1%(前 1% 的富集因子,衡量召回能力)计,DrugCLIP 相对所有基线都有大幅提升(具体每个基线的数值在 Fig. 2A/B 的图里,正文未逐一列出)。泛化性测试方面,即便调高测试靶点与训练数据之间的序列同源性阈值,表现仍然稳健;对口袋侧链构象误差(RMSD 0–3 Å)也比 Vina 更鲁棒。 <!-- EN --> **In-silico benchmarks**: on DUD-E and LIT-PCBA, measured by EF1% (enrichment factor in the top 1%, a recall metric), DrugCLIP improves substantially over every baseline (per-baseline values live in Fig. 2A/B rather than the text). It stays robust when the sequence-identity cutoff between test targets and training data is raised, and is more robust than Vina to pocket side-chain conformational error (RMSD 0–3 Å).
- <!-- ZH --> **NET(更难的泛化测试,预印本)**:104 个 DrugCLIP 挑出的分子在 10 µM 下做放射性配体转运实验,**16 个**抑制率超过 60%;Science 摘要给出的命中率是 **15%**,其中 12 个分子的亲和力优于已上市的 bupropion。两个抑制剂与 NET 的复合物结构被解出(预印本写的是 Cryo-EM:0086-0043 与 Y510-9709,IC50 分别为 1.14 µM 与 0.31 µM,均优于常用抗抑郁药 bupropion 的 1.5 µM)。化学上的新意在于:以往 NET 抑制剂通常靠脂肪族氮原子与 ASP75 形成盐桥,而这次筛出的几个 hit 用的是带正电的芳香族氮——结构分析显示芳香环确实形成了更有利的相互作用(0086-0043 的异喹啉环与 PHE72 形成 T 形 π-π 相互作用)。 <!-- EN --> **NET (the harder generalization test, preprint)**: 104 DrugCLIP-selected molecules were assayed at 10 µM by radio-ligand transport, and **16** inhibited by more than 60%; the Science abstract reports a **15%** hit rate, with 12 molecules binding better than the marketed drug bupropion. Two inhibitors' complexes with NET were solved (the preprint says by cryo-EM: 0086-0043 and Y510-9709, with IC50 values of 1.14 µM and 0.31 µM, both better than the widely used antidepressant bupropion at 1.5 µM). The chemical novelty: previous NET inhibitors typically rely on an aliphatic nitrogen forming a salt bridge with ASP75, whereas several hits here use a positively charged aromatic nitrogen — and structural analysis shows the aromatic rings do form more favourable interactions (0086-0043's isoquinoline ring makes a T-shaped π–π interaction with PHE72).
- <!-- ZH --> **TRIP12(Science 版新增的关键结果)**:对这个**既没有 holo 结构、也没有已知小分子配体**的靶点,仅用 AlphaFold2 预测结构就达到 **17.5%** 命中率——这是"后 AlphaFold 时代"叙事里最硬的一块证据。(预印本中无此实验。) <!-- EN --> **TRIP12 (new in the Science version)**: for a target with **neither a holo structure nor known small-molecule binders**, a **17.5%** hit rate using only AlphaFold2-predicted structures — the hardest piece of evidence in the "post-AlphaFold era" narrative. (This experiment is not in the preprint.)
- <!-- ZH --> **5HT2AR(预印本)**:78 个化合物先用钙流实验在 10 µM 下初筛,8 个为阳性激动剂(活性至少为血清素的 10%);[³H]-ketanserin 竞争结合实验中 6 个 Ki < 10 µM;这 6 个在测 β-arrestin2 招募的 NanoBit 实验里 EC50 全部 < 1 µM。最高亲和力的 V008-4481 亲和力 21.0 nM、EC50 60.3 nM、Emax 35.8%,且其吲哚环、苯基哌嗪部分、线性尾部分别结合正位、extended、side-extended 三个区域(同时结合这三处的分子此前很少见);另一个 V006-3328 具有 5HT2AR 配体中罕见的分支拓扑。 <!-- EN --> **5HT2AR (preprint)**: 78 compounds were primary-screened by calcium flux at 10 µM, 8 scoring as positive agonists (at least 10% of serotonin's activity); six had Ki < 10 µM in [³H]-ketanserin competitive binding; all six reached EC50 < 1 µM in NanoBit assays of β-arrestin2 recruitment. The highest-affinity V008-4481 has 21.0 nM affinity, 60.3 nM EC50 and 35.8% Emax, and binds the orthosteric, extended and side-extended regions with its indole ring, phenylpiperazine moiety and linear tail respectively (molecules engaging all three are rarely seen); another, V006-3328, has a branched topology uncommon among 5HT2AR binders.
- <!-- ZH --> **AlphaFold 结构的代价与 GenPack 的修复**:把 DUD-E 的实验结构换成 AF2 预测结构 + Fpocket 口袋后,EF1% 从 27.4% 跌到 13.3%;加上 GenPack 后回升到 19.5%,同时 docking 成功率从 19.1% 提升到 38.7%。换句话说,**AF2 结构能用,但不白给,仍不及 holo 结构**。 <!-- EN --> **The cost of AlphaFold structures and GenPack's repair**: replacing DUD-E's experimental structures with AF2 predictions plus Fpocket pockets drops EF1% from 27.4% to 13.3%; GenPack brings it back to 19.5% and raises the docking success rate from 19.1% to 38.7%. In other words, **AF2 structures are usable but not free, and still short of holo structures**.
- <!-- ZH --> **GenomeScreenDB**:覆盖约 1 万个人类蛋白、2 万多个口袋,含 200 多万个高置信蛋白-配体复合物结构及打分,完全开放访问。预印本给的覆盖口径是 **9,908 个靶点**,是 ChEMBL 34 覆盖的 4,810 个人类蛋白的两倍多(UniProt 收录 20,436 个经审阅的人类蛋白),且 ChEMBL 里并非每个靶点都有高亲和力小分子配体——有些只有肽或抗体配体,或只有低质量实验的模糊结果。论文给了几个示例说明其价值:JNK3(经典激酶靶点,筛出的分子与铰链区 MET149 的主链形成氢键)、SLC45A2(SLC 超家族、药理学研究很少,分子都结合在对蛋白稳定性重要的 L374 附近)、OR6A2(嗅觉受体家族,在巨噬细胞中表达、感知血液辛醛并促进动脉粥样硬化斑块形成,预测分子贴合其正位口袋,可作为抗动脉粥样硬化的潜在抑制剂)、Sestrin-2(感知亮氨酸、促进癌细胞耐药,预测分子结合在亮氨酸所在的同一口袋)。 <!-- EN --> **GenomeScreenDB**: ~10,000 human proteins, over 20,000 pockets, more than 2 million high-confidence protein–ligand complex structures with scores, fully open. The preprint's coverage figure is **9,908 targets**, more than twice the 4,810 human proteins covered by ChEMBL 34 (UniProt lists 20,436 reviewed human proteins) — and not every ChEMBL target even has a high-affinity small-molecule binder; some have only peptide or antibody binders, or only vague results from low-quality assays. The paper illustrates its value with examples: JNK3 (a classical kinase target, where the identified molecules hydrogen-bond to the backbone of MET149 in the hinge region), SLC45A2 (an SLC-superfamily member with little pharmacology, where all database molecules bind near L374, a site important for protein stability), OR6A2 (an olfactory receptor expressed in macrophages that senses blood octanal and promotes atherosclerotic plaque formation, where predicted molecules fit the orthosteric pocket as potential anti-atherosclerosis inhibitors) and Sestrin-2 (a leucine sensor promoting cancer drug resistance, where predicted molecules bind the same pocket as leucine).

## 创新点 / Contributions
- <!-- ZH --> 把虚拟筛选重新表述为稠密检索任务,从而(a)把算法复杂度从 O(M×N) 降到 O(M+N),(b)用对比学习的相对标签绕开亲和力数值噪声,(c)顺带缓解了训练集与真实库之间活性/非活性比例的分布偏移。 <!-- EN --> Reformulates virtual screening as dense retrieval, which (a) takes complexity from O(M×N) to O(M+N), (b) uses contrastive learning's relative labels to sidestep affinity-value noise, and (c) incidentally eases the active/inactive distribution shift between training sets and real libraries.
- <!-- ZH --> ProFSA:用"蛋白内部的肽片段 + 其周边"自动造出 550 万对伪口袋-配体,解决了对比预训练的数据量问题——一个不需要任何额外实验数据的自监督构造。 <!-- EN --> ProFSA: automatically builds 5.5 million pseudo pocket–ligand pairs from peptide fragments inside proteins and their surroundings, solving the data-volume problem for contrastive pretraining — a self-supervised construction needing no extra experimental data.
- <!-- ZH --> GenPack:面对 AF2 结构缺乏 holo 构象的问题,用"生成分子诱导口袋构象 → 重装侧链 → 力场精修"这条非常规路线把筛选能力部分补回来。 <!-- EN --> GenPack: faced with AF2 structures lacking holo conformations, it recovers part of the screening power by an unconventional route — generate molecules to induce the pocket conformation, repack side chains, refine with force fields.
- <!-- ZH --> 第一次做到万亿级打分、覆盖近半个人类基因组的筛选,并把结果作为开放数据库释放,把"基因组尺度虚拟筛选"从设想变成可查的资源。 <!-- EN --> The first screening campaign with more than 10 trillion protein–ligand scoring operations, covering nearly half the human genome, released as an open database — turning genome-scale virtual screening from an aspiration into a queryable resource.
- <!-- ZH --> 湿实验不只验证"能找到 binder",还找到了化学机制上新颖的分子(NET 上用芳香族氮代替传统的脂肪族氮盐桥),并用结构确认。 <!-- EN --> The wet lab validates more than "it finds binders": it finds mechanistically novel chemistry (aromatic nitrogen replacing the conventional aliphatic-nitrogen salt bridge at NET), confirmed structurally.

## 局限 / Limitations
- <!-- ZH --> **口袋必须先给定**。整个方法的输入是一个口袋,而不是一个蛋白;基因组尺度流程里口袋是由 TM-align 同源比对 + Fpocket + 生成模型凑出来的,这一步的错误会直接传导到检索结果,论文没有给出口袋定义错误率对最终命中率影响的量化。 <!-- EN --> **The pocket must be given up front.** The method's input is a pocket, not a protein; in the genome-scale pipeline pockets are assembled from TM-align homology alignment plus Fpocket plus generative models, and errors there propagate straight into retrieval, with no quantification of how pocket-definition error affects final hit rates.
- <!-- ZH --> **AF2 结构有明确代价**:EF1% 27.4% → 13.3%,GenPack 也只补回到 19.5%,离 holo 结构还有距离。所以"覆盖整个基因组"与"在每个靶点上都可靠"是两件事,TRIP12 的 17.5% 是单个成功案例,不能当作 AF2-only 筛选的期望命中率。 <!-- EN --> **AF2 structures carry a measurable cost**: EF1% 27.4% → 13.3%, with GenPack recovering only to 19.5%, still short of holo structures. "Covering the whole genome" and "being reliable on every target" are different claims; TRIP12's 17.5% is one success, not the expected hit rate of AF2-only screening.
- <!-- ZH --> **检索给的是"结合概率"的代理量,不是亲和力**:余弦相似度没有物理单位,也不给结合位姿;预印本里真正要挑分子送实验时,仍然要叠加对接打分、类药性与化学多样性这些额外判据(5HT2AR 的 78 个化合物就是这么挑的),基因组尺度流程也要再跑一遍 cluster-docking。换句话说 **DrugCLIP 是一个极快的预筛器,不是对接的替代品**。 <!-- EN --> **Retrieval yields a proxy for binding probability, not affinity**: cosine similarity has no physical units and gives no binding pose. When the preprint actually picks molecules for assay it layers on docking score, drug-likeness and chemical diversity (that is how the 78 5HT2AR compounds were chosen), and the genome-scale pipeline still re-runs cluster-docking. **DrugCLIP is an extremely fast prefilter, not a replacement for docking.**
- <!-- ZH --> **训练数据的覆盖决定能力边界**:微调依赖已有的实验复合物结构,因此对训练集中代表不足的口袋类型(论文自己指出 5HT2AR 在训练集里、要专门去同源以防泄漏)表现如何,需要更系统的检验;预印本中"调高同源阈值仍稳健"的实验是向这个方向的回答,但只在 DUD-E 上做。 <!-- EN --> **Training-data coverage bounds the capability**: fine-tuning relies on existing experimental complex structures, so performance on pocket types underrepresented there needs more systematic testing (the authors themselves note 5HT2AR was in the training set and had to be de-homologized against leakage); the preprint's identity-cutoff experiment addresses this but only on DUD-E.
- <!-- ZH --> **命中率的分母是人挑过的**:NET 的 15%(或预印本的 104 测 16 中)是在"从 top 结果里按类药性等条件挑出来送实验"之后算的,所以这个命中率衡量的是"整条流程(模型 + 人工筛选)",不是模型单独的精度。 <!-- EN --> **The denominator of the hit rate is human-filtered**: NET's 15% (or the preprint's 16 of 104) is computed after candidates were picked from the top results by drug-likeness and similar criteria, so it measures the whole pipeline (model plus human selection), not the model's accuracy alone.
- <!-- ZH --> 预印本未经同行评审,且与 Science 最终版在靶点(5HT2AR vs TRIP12)与加速倍数(10 万倍 vs 1,000 万倍)上都有差异;本文的数字已标注来源,但要引用关键数字时应以 Science 正式版为准。 <!-- EN --> The preprint is not peer-reviewed and differs from the final Science version in targets (5HT2AR vs TRIP12) and speedup (100,000× vs 10,000,000×); sources are flagged above, but key numbers should be cited from the published Science version.

## 与本研究方向的关系 / Relation to our direction
<!-- ZH --> 这是 Sikander 给 **"Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening"**(Hayat Lab 的硕士/HiWi 课题:LLM agent + 分子基础模型 + docking + active learning,给定蛋白结构自主规划、执行、改进筛选,应用方向心脏疾病)指定的 **key paper 2**。它在 pipeline 里的位置很清楚:**agent 的快速预筛层**。**(1) 为什么适合做工具**:O(M+N) 的复杂度和"库可预编码"意味着一次性付出编码成本之后,**每个新口袋的查询几乎免费**——这对 agent 恰好是最重要的性质,因为 agent 的工作方式就是反复试探(换口袋、换 top-k、换库子集),而对接式工具每次试探都要重新付全额算力。一个合理的分工是:DrugCLIP 从几亿分子里秒级取出几万个候选 → `adaptiveflow-natbiotech-2026` 的 ATG-VS 对接这几万个 → `competitive-docking-npjdrugdiscov-2026` 的 AF3 竞争对接给最后几十个排序。**(2) agent 要做的决策**:哪个口袋(同一个蛋白往往有多个 Fpocket 候选,GenomeScreenDB 的 2 万口袋 / 1 万蛋白就说明这个比例)、用 holo 还是 AF2 结构(有 holo 一定用 holo——27.4% vs 13.3% 就是代价)、AF2 结构要不要先跑 GenPack、top-k 截断取多少(这是检索召回与下游对接预算之间的权衡,完全是 agent 该算的账)、是否要为特定靶点训定制模型(论文为 5HT2AR 就这么做了,但同时必须做去同源处理防泄漏——这一步 agent 很容易遗漏)。**(3) 一个合理的第一个里程碑**:把 DrugCLIP(或等价的检索式预筛)和一个对接后端包成两个工具,让 agent 在固定的总算力预算下自己决定"预筛取多少 top-k 送对接",与人工设定的固定 k 做对照。评测指标用最终对接 top-N 的平均打分或已知活性分子的富集(DUD-E/LIT-PCBA 可直接当 ground truth,不需要湿实验)。这比一上来就做全自主安全得多,而且有现成基准。**(4) 对课题"心脏疾病"方向的直接可用性**:GenomeScreenDB 已经预先算好了约 1 万个人类蛋白的筛选结果并开放,所以心脏相关靶点很可能已经在库里——课题开头可以直接查库,而不是从零起筛;论文自己举的 OR6A2 例子(巨噬细胞感知辛醛、促进动脉粥样硬化斑块)就是一个心血管方向的现成切入点。**(5) 要当心的坑**:余弦相似度**不是**亲和力,把它当奖励信号去做 active learning 很危险(模型会学会生成"看起来像 binder"的向量);agent 必须在流程里保留一层物理检查(对接位姿、PoseBusters 式的合理性检验、ADME)。另外 DrugCLIP 的能力边界由训练集的口袋覆盖决定,agent 面对一个新口袋时无法从相似度分数本身看出"这个口袋我没见过"——**检索式方法缺少校准过的不确定性**,这正好与我们在 agent 可信度方向关心的问题重合:需要一个外部 verifier 来判断"这个预测该不该信",而不是让模型自己的分数说话。
<!-- EN --> This is **key paper 2** for the project Sikander assigned, **"Autonomous scientific orchestration of virtual screening"** (a Hayat Lab Master's/HiWi topic: LLM agents + molecular foundation models + docking + active learning, autonomously planning, executing and improving screening campaigns from protein structures, applied to heart disease). Its place in the pipeline is clear: **the agent's fast prefilter**. **(1) Why it makes a good tool**: O(M+N) complexity and a pre-encodable library mean that after a one-off encoding cost **each new pocket query is nearly free** — exactly the property that matters most to an agent, whose mode of work is repeated probing (different pockets, different top-k, different library subsets), where a docking-style tool pays full compute for every probe. A sensible division of labour: DrugCLIP pulls tens of thousands of candidates from hundreds of millions in seconds → ATG-VS from `adaptiveflow-natbiotech-2026` docks those tens of thousands → AF3 competitive docking from `competitive-docking-npjdrugdiscov-2026` ranks the final few dozen. **(2) The decisions for an agent**: which pocket (one protein often has several Fpocket candidates — GenomeScreenDB's 20,000 pockets across 10,000 proteins shows the ratio), holo or AF2 structure (always holo when available — 27.4% vs 13.3% is the price), whether to run GenPack first on AF2 structures, where to cut top-k (a trade-off between retrieval recall and downstream docking budget, precisely the accounting an agent should do), and whether to train a target-specific model (the paper did for 5HT2AR, but that requires de-homologizing against leakage — a step an agent would easily skip). **(3) A sensible first milestone**: wrap DrugCLIP (or an equivalent retrieval prefilter) and a docking backend as two tools, and let the agent decide, under a fixed total compute budget, how many top-k candidates to pass from prefilter to docking, against a human-fixed k as control. Score it by the mean docking score of the final top-N, or enrichment of known actives (DUD-E/LIT-PCBA serve directly as ground truth, no wet lab needed). Far safer than starting fully autonomous, and the benchmarks already exist. **(4) Direct usability for the project's heart-disease focus**: GenomeScreenDB already holds precomputed screening results for ~10,000 human proteins, openly, so cardiac targets are quite likely already in it — the project can start by querying rather than screening from scratch; the paper's own OR6A2 example (macrophage octanal sensing promoting atherosclerotic plaques) is a ready cardiovascular entry point. **(5) Pitfalls to watch**: cosine similarity is **not** affinity, and using it as a reward signal for active learning is dangerous (the model learns to produce vectors that merely look like binders); the agent must keep a layer of physical checking in the loop (docking poses, PoseBusters-style plausibility, ADME). And DrugCLIP's capability is bounded by its training set's pocket coverage, yet an agent facing a new pocket cannot tell from the similarity score itself that the pocket is unfamiliar — **retrieval methods lack calibrated uncertainty**, which overlaps exactly with what the agent-trustworthiness direction cares about: an external verifier must judge whether a prediction is trustworthy, rather than letting the model's own score speak.
