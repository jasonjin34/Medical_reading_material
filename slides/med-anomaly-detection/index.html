Anomaly Detection
in Medical Imaging

Three tensions and the road ahead
Presenter: Jin Er  ·  2026  ·  Medical Reading Material
Motivation

Why it's hard — and the one idea

  • Clinical need: screening, rare & long-tail disease, triage, opportunistic findings [Fernando et al., ACM CSUR 2021]
  • The label wall: exhaustive abnormal labels are impossible — anomalies are diverse & unseen [Cai et al., MedIA 2025]
  • Anatomy + heterogeneity + safety make supervised, closed-set models brittle [Baur et al., MedIA 2021]
  • → the one idea: learn "normal" only, flag deviation (unsupervised / zero-/few-shot) [Schlegl et al., IPMI 2017]
Setup

How the task is framed

  • Train on normal → a "normal prior"; score = deviation (residual · feature distance · prompt similarity)
  • Granularity: image-level detection vs pixel-level localization / segmentation
  • Metrics: AUROC · AP/AUPRC · Dice/IoU — metric choice turns out to matter [Cai et al., MedIA 2025]

Tension ②

Fidelity vs sensitivity

The noise paradox — the central deployment pain point

Tension ② · The paradox

Erase the lesion, or keep the anatomy?

To erase pathology you inject high noise — but high noise destroys healthy anatomy → pseudo-healthy image drifts from the patient → false positives spike, specificity drops. [Bercea et al., MICCAI 2024]

Too little noise

pathology survives → false negatives

Too much noise

healthy tissue destroyed → false positives

Tension ② · Responses

Guidance & latent restoration

  • THOR: temporal harmonization — implicit guidance re-integrates healthy tissue, cuts false positives [Bercea et al., MICCAI 2024]
  • Latent + rectified flow for 3D volumes → context, lower compute [Frontiers Radiol. 2024]
  • ↳ partial fixes — specificity stays open

Scarcity & generalization

Tension ③ · Self-supervision

Manufacture the anomalies you lack

  • Synthesize disease-like perturbations [Li et al., CVPR 2021] [Tan et al., MICCAI 2021]
  • Turns AD into a proxy classification / segmentation task — no real anomalies [Tan et al., 2020]
  • Pro: strong localization · Con: synthetic ≠ real distribution

The road ahead

Three directions — closing the loop

① Efficient generative

SDE → latent flow matching / rectified flows; distillation + compression [Lipman et al., ICLR 2023]

② Beyond pixels

Reasoning / knowledge-grounded, interpretable, agentic AD [Jin et al., AAAI 2025]

③ Trustworthy FMs

Privacy (unlearning), safety, compliance [Bourtoule et al., IEEE S&P 2021]

Summary

Roadmap & takeaways

Near-term

Standardized benchmarks · latent-diffusion/flow baselines · FM adapters

Long-term

Reasoning-grounded, trustworthy, 3D-native systems

  • Medical AD = learn-normal, flag-deviation; two engines: diffusion reconstruction & VLM adaptation
  • Real bottlenecks: specificity, 3D compute, trust, privacy — not raw accuracy
  • Frontier = efficient generative + reasoning-grounded + trustworthy
