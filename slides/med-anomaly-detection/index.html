Motivation

Why it's hard — and the one idea

  • Clinical need: screening, rare & long-tail disease, triage, opportunistic findings
  • The label wall: exhaustive abnormal annotations are impossible — anomalies are diverse and unseen
  • Anatomy + heterogeneity + safety make supervised, closed-set models brittle
  • → The unifying idea: learn "normal" only, then flag deviation (unsupervised / zero-/few-shot)

How the task is framed

Tension ① · Reconstruction

Reconstruct-and-compare: AE → GAN

  • Assumption: a normal-trained generator can't rebuild pathology → residual = anomaly
  • Lineage: AnoGAN → f-AnoGAN (adversarial pseudo-healthy generation)
  • Failure modes: mode collapse, low fidelity, and the "identity shortcut" — the network copies the anomaly straight through
  • → the field needed a stronger, higher-coverage generative prior

Tension ① · Reconstruction

Diffusion becomes the backbone

  • DDPM: forward noising + learned reverse denoising → better mode coverage & fidelity than GAN
  • AnoDDPM: partial diffusion + multi-scale Simplex noise → control anomaly size (Gaussian noise erases low-freq, misses large lesions)
  • AutoDDPM: automatic mask–stitch–resample → seamless healthy in-painting

The noise paradox — the central deployment pain point

Tension ② · The paradox

Erase the lesion, or keep the anatomy?

To fully erase pathology you must inject high noise — but high noise irreversibly destroys healthy anatomical detail → the pseudo-healthy image drifts from the patient → false positives spike, specificity drops.

Too little noise

pathology survives → false negatives

Too much noise

healthy tissue destroyed → false positives

Tension ③ · Self-supervision

Manufacture the anomalies you lack

  • Synthesize disease-like perturbations (intensity / morphology — CutPaste / FPI / Poisson-blend styles)
  • Turns AD into a proxy classification / segmentation task — no real anomalies needed
  • Pro: strong localization   ·   Con: synthetic ≠ real pathology distribution

The road ahead

Three directions — closing the loop

① Efficient generative

Diffusion SDE → latent flow matching / rectified flows; step distillation + compression (answers specificity + 3D)

② Beyond pixels

Reasoning / knowledge-grounded, interpretable, agentic AD — rationales, not just heatmaps (answers trust)

③ Trustworthy FMs

Privacy (machine unlearning), safety / behavior control, regulatory compliance (answers privacy)

The road ahead19
Summary

Roadmap & takeaways

Near-term

Standardized benchmarks · latent-diffusion / flow baselines · FM adapters

Long-term

Reasoning-grounded, trustworthy, 3D-native systems

  • Medical AD = learn-normal, flag-deviation; two engines: diffusion reconstruction & VLM adaptation
  • The real bottlenecks are specificity, 3D compute, trust, privacy — not raw accuracy
  • Frontier = efficient generative + reasoning-grounded + trustworthy
