1You can't label every disease1:30
Full script

Start with why the field exists. Disease is rare, diverse, and long-tailed — most scans are normal, and you can't enumerate every abnormality to label it. Supervised, per-disease models are brittle and don't scale. So the whole field makes one move: learn what "normal" looks like, and flag anything that deviates. That idea spans every modality — chest X-ray, brain MRI, retina, dermoscopy, pathology.

中文口播

先说为什么会有这个领域。疾病是稀有、多样、长尾的 —— 大多数扫描都是正常的,你不可能把每一种异常都标注出来。按病种做的监督模型又脆又不可扩展。所以整个领域只做一件事:学会"正常"长什么样,再把偏离的地方标出来。这个思路适用于所有模态 —— 胸片、脑 MRI、视网膜、皮肤镜、病理。

Cue
  • Disease = rare·diverse·long-tailed → can't label all
  • Supervised per-disease doesn't scale
  • The one idea: learn normal, flag deviation
  • Spans 7 datasets / 5 modalities
2How the task is framed1:30
Full script

Quick vocabulary. You train only on normal data to build a "normal prior"; the anomaly score is the deviation from it. Two granularities: image-level detection — is this scan abnormal — and pixel-level localization — where. Metrics: AUROC, AP, Dice. Keep one thing in your pocket: the metric matters as much as the model. Pipeline: input → normal prior → deviation → anomaly map.

中文口播

快速过一下术语。你只用正常数据训练,建一个"正常先验",异常分数就是对它的偏离。两种粒度:图像级的检测 —— 这张扫描是否异常;像素级的定位 —— 异常在哪。指标是 AUROC、AP、Dice。记住一点:选哪个指标和选哪个模型一样重要。流程:输入 → 正常先验 → 偏离 → 异常图。

Cue
  • Train on normal → prior → score = deviation
  • Detection (image) vs localization (pixel)
  • Metric choice matters (foreshadow)
  • input→prior→deviation→map
3One "normal" model, four clinical jobs1:30
Full script

Why should a clinician care? One normal-only model does four jobs: screening and triage on normal-heavy data; lesion localization with zero annotations — the deviation map is already pixel-level; OOD and quality control, a gatekeeper before any downstream model; and catching rare or novel disease never in the training set. No positive examples needed.

中文口播

临床上为什么要在意?一个只学正常的模型能干四件事:在以正常为主的数据上做筛查和分诊;零标注地定位病灶 —— 偏离图本身就是像素级的;做 OOD / 质控,当下游模型的守门员;以及抓住训练里从没见过的罕见或新型疾病。全程不需要任何阳性样本。

Cue
  • Screening / triage (label-free)
  • Localization without annotations
  • OOD / quality-control gatekeeper
  • Rare & novel — no positives needed
4Three tensions the field is resolving1:00
Full script

The spine of the talk. I group the field into three tensions it's actively resolving. One — how to represent "normal": reconstruct and read the residual, or embed and measure distance. Two — fidelity vs sensitivity, really the specificity problem. Three — scarcity and generalization: almost no abnormal data, must work across modalities. Methods are answers; the gaps are future work.

中文口播

这是整场的主线。我把领域归成三道张力。一 —— 怎么表示"正常":重建后读残差,还是嵌入后量距离。二 —— 保真度 vs 敏感度,本质是特异性问题。三 —— 稀缺与泛化:几乎没有异常数据,还得跨模态。现有方法是对这些张力的回答;没解决的部分就是未来工作。

Cue
  • ① Represent normal — reconstruct vs embed
  • ② Fidelity vs sensitivity — specificity
  • ③ Scarcity & generalization
  • Methods = answers · gaps = future

6Reconstruct-and-compare: AE → GAN1:40
Full script

The first answer is reconstruction. Train a generator on healthy scans only; it can't rebuild pathology it's never seen, so the residual is the anomaly. Lineage: AnoGAN → f-AnoGAN. Failure modes: mode collapse, blur, and the "identity shortcut" — the net copies the lesion through and the residual goes flat. The AE family — AE, VAE, VQ-VAE, MemAE — differ in how they bottleneck normal. A stronger generative prior fixes this.

中文口播

第一个答案是重建。只用健康扫描训练一个生成器;它画不出没见过的病灶,所以残差(输入减重建)就是异常。谱系:AnoGAN → f-AnoGAN。失败模式:模式坍缩、模糊、还有"identity shortcut" —— 网络把病灶原样复制过去,残差就变平了。AE 一家(AE、VAE、VQ-VAE、MemAE)区别在于怎么"卡住"正常。这些弱点正是更强的生成先验要修的。

Cue
  • Normal generator can't rebuild pathology → residual = anomaly
  • AnoGAN → f-AnoGAN
  • Fails: mode collapse · blur · identity shortcut
  • AE family = ways to bottleneck normal
7Diffusion becomes the backbone1:50
Full script

Diffusion is now the dominant reconstruction backbone — better coverage and fidelity than GANs. AnoDDPM: Simplex noise controls the anomaly scale. AutoDDPM: automatic mask–stitch–resample. Latest: Synomaly, multi-stage diffusion (MedIA 2025). Figure: Simplex noise repairs the lesion and exposes it in the residual; plain Gaussian fails.

中文口播

扩散现在是主流的重建骨干 —— 覆盖度和保真度都比 GAN 好。AnoDDPM:用 Simplex 噪声控制异常尺度。AutoDDPM:自动 mask–stitch–resample。最新:Synomaly 多阶段扩散(MedIA 2025)。看图:Simplex 噪声把病灶修好、在残差里干净地暴露;普通高斯噪声做不到。

Cue
  • Diffusion > GAN (coverage·fidelity)
  • AnoDDPM = Simplex controls size
  • AutoDDPM = mask–stitch–resample
  • Latest: Synomaly ('25)
  • Fig: Simplex exposes · Gaussian fails
8Don't reconstruct — embed & compare1:40
Full script

The other answer: don't generate — embed and compare. Frozen features + a memory bank of normal patches + nearest-neighbour distance. PatchCore, from industry, is a deceptively strong baseline. In medicine ImageNet features are already strong; the field moves to DINOv2 foundation features — AnomalyDINO, medical DINO-DPMM (MICCAI '25). Picture: normal features cluster; an anomaly sits far from all of them.

中文口播

另一个答案:根本不生成 —— 嵌入并比较。冻结特征 + 正常 patch 的 memory bank + 最近邻距离打分。PatchCore 来自工业界,是个被低估的强基线。医学里 ImageNet 特征已经很强,领域正转向 DINOv2 基础模型特征 —— AnomalyDINO、医学 DINO-DPMM(MICCAI '25)。一句话:正常特征聚成一团,异常离所有正常都很远。

Cue
  • No reconstruction — features + memory bank + kNN
  • PatchCore ('22, industrial, strong)
  • → DINOv2: AnomalyDINO · DINO-DPMM ('25)
  • Anomaly = far from normal cluster

10Why specificity decides deployment1:50
Full script

This tension decides whether a tool ships. A balanced AUROC of 0.95 sounds great and is not usable. At 1% prevalence, a few-percent false-positive rate × a huge normal population buries the true lesions. Clinicians get alert fatigue and abandon the tool — and "flag every anomaly" is hard for regulators. Specificity is the clinical bar for every method.

中文口播

这道张力真正决定能不能落地。平衡集上 AUROC 0.95 听着很好,但不可用。在 1% 患病率下,几个百分点的假阳率乘以海量正常人群,把那点真病灶淹没。医生疲于虚警,最后弃用 —— 而且"把所有异常都报出来"很难过监管。特异性是每个方法的临床门槛。

Cue
  • AUROC 0.95 ≠ usable
  • 1% prevalence: few% FP × many normals → alarms bury lesions
  • → alert fatigue → abandoned; hard for regulators
  • Specificity = bar for ALL
11Erase the lesion, or keep the anatomy?1:40
Full script

Reconstruction's own trap — the noise paradox. To erase a lesion you inject noise and denoise back to "healthy". Too little noise → the lesion survives → false negatives. Too much → healthy anatomy destroyed, pseudo-healthy scan drifts → false positives. Tuning the noise level can't win both. That's the core reconstruction pain.

中文口播

重建自己的陷阱 —— 噪声悖论。要擦掉病灶,你注入噪声再去噪回"健康"。太小 → 病灶还在 → 漏检。太大 → 健康解剖被破坏,伪健康图偏离病人 → 假阳。调噪声两头都讨不到好。这就是重建的核心痛点。

Cue
  • Erase by add-noise → denoise
  • Too little → lesion survives (FN)
  • Too much → anatomy destroyed (FP)
  • Can't satisfy both
12Guidance & latent restoration2:00
Full script

The response — stop tuning noise, add guidance. THOR (Bercea et al., MICCAI 2024): intermediate anomaly maps as implicit guidance → preserves healthy detail while erasing the lesion → fewer FP. cDDPM (2025): conditions the denoiser on a latent of the input, adapting to the patient without copying unhealthy structure. Latent + rectified flow brings 3D within compute reach. Partial fixes — specificity improved, not solved.

中文口播

领域怎么应对 —— 不再调噪声,改加引导。THOR(Bercea 等,MICCAI 2024):用中间异常图做隐式引导 → 保留健康区细节又擦掉病灶 → 减少假阳。cDDPM(2025):把输入的 latent 作为条件给去噪器,适应病人又不复制不健康结构。latent + rectified flow 把 3D 拉进算力可行范围。都是部分修复 —— 特异性改善但没解决。

Cue
  • Guidance instead of noise-tuning
  • THOR ('24): implicit guidance → cuts FP
  • cDDPM ('25): condition on input latent
  • Latent + rectified flow → 3D
  • Partial — specificity still open
13Feature / VLM: no paradox, specificity stays open1:30
Full script

The other half — embedding and VLM — don't reconstruct, so no noise paradox. But specificity is still unsolved differently: a distance score has no obvious clinical operating point, and thresholds drift across scanners and sites. Takeaway: specificity is a bar for all methods; the noise paradox is only reconstruction's special case.

中文口播

领域的另一半 —— 嵌入和 VLM —— 不做重建,所以没有噪声悖论。但特异性依然没解决,只是换了种方式:距离分数没有明显的临床工作点,阈值还会跨机器、跨中心漂移。结论:特异性是所有方法的门槛;噪声悖论只是重建派的特例。

Cue
  • Embed/VLM → no noise paradox
  • But no operating point; thresholds drift across sites
  • Takeaway: specificity universal; paradox = recon-only

15Manufacture the anomalies you lack1:40
Full script

First answer to scarcity: manufacture the anomalies you lack. CutPaste, FPI, PII (2020–21) synthesize "what's wrong" by cut-paste / Poisson blending → a proxy segmentation task, no real anomalies. Weakness: synthetic ≠ real — the model learns paste artifacts and doesn't transfer. Latest: ART-ASyn synthesizes realistic textures at anatomically plausible locations (chest X-ray).

中文口播

对稀缺的第一个答案:自己造你缺的异常。CutPaste、FPI、PII(2020–21)用剪贴 / 泊松融合合成"哪里不对" → 变成不需要真异常的代理分割任务。弱点:合成 ≠ 真实 —— 模型学到拼贴痕迹,不迁移。最新:ART-ASyn 在解剖上合理的位置合成真实纹理(胸片)。

Cue
  • Synthesize defects → proxy segmentation
  • CutPaste / FPI / PII (2020–21)
  • Weakness: synthetic ≠ real
  • Latest: ART-ASyn (CXR, '25)
16Borrow a foundation model — MVFA-AD1:50
Full script

Second answer: borrow a foundation model. Zero-shot CLIP collapses on medical images — its features are global and semantic, but medical anomalies are local and subtle. MVFA-AD (CVPR 2024 highlight) keeps CLIP frozen, inserts lightweight adapters at multiple encoder levels, and aligns patch features to text prompts — so it can localize, not just classify. One backbone spans brain MRI, liver CT, retinal OCT.

中文口播

第二个答案:借基础模型。零样本 CLIP 在医学图像上直接崩 —— 它的特征是全局、语义的,而医学异常是局部、细微的。MVFA-AD(CVPR 2024 highlight)保持 CLIP 冻结,在多个编码器层插入轻量 adapter,把 patch 特征和文本 prompt 对齐 —— 于是它能定位,不只是分类。一个骨干覆盖脑 MRI、肝 CT、视网膜 OCT。

Cue
  • Zero-shot CLIP fails (global vs local)
  • MVFA: frozen CLIP + multi-level adapters + pixel V–L align
  • Localizes (AS), not just classifies (AC)
  • One backbone → MRI · CT · OCT
17MediCLIP → MadCLIP & the FM trend1:30
Full script

Push to the few-shot, no-real-anomaly extreme. MediCLIP: synthetic tasks + learnable prompts + adapters, from a handful of normals. Latest: MadCLIP beats MVFA cross-dataset (MICCAI 2025). Trend: pathology/radiology foundation models as default backbones. Honest aside — VLM is also a "represent normal" method; I file it under scarcity because that's mainly what it solves.

中文口播

推到少样本、没有真异常的极端。MediCLIP:合成任务 + 可学 prompt + adapter,从少量正常样本就能做。最新:MadCLIP 跨数据集超过 MVFA(MICCAI 2025)。趋势:病理 / 放射基础模型成为默认骨干。一句实话 —— VLM 其实也是"表示正常"的方法;我归到稀缺这一支,因为它主要解决的是这个。

Cue
  • MediCLIP: few-shot · synthetic + prompts
  • MadCLIP > MVFA cross-dataset ('25)
  • Trend: pathology/radiology FMs
  • VLM also ①; filed under ③

20Don't chase SOTA — a tuned AE is a strong baseline1:50
Full script

Real image-level AUCs from MedIAnomaly. Three things. A tuned AE stays competitive — Brain Tumor 85.9, BraTS 82.6. The scoring metric matters as much as the model — RSNA AE-L2 67.5 → SSIM 80.9. And no single winner — the best flips across datasets: FAE-SSIM, ImageNet features, AnatPaste on ISIC, AutoDDPM on Camelyon16. Don't chase the leaderboard — pick by modality, data regime, constraints.

中文口播

这些是 MedIAnomaly 里真实的图像级 AUC。三点。调好的朴素 AE 依然能打 —— Brain Tumor 85.9、BraTS 82.6。打分的度量和模型一样重要 —— RSNA 上 AE 用 L2 是 67.5,换 SSIM 跳到 80.9。而且没有通吃赢家 —— 最好的随数据集翻转:FAE-SSIM、ImageNet 特征、ISIC 上 AnatPaste、Camelyon16 上 AutoDDPM。别追排行榜 —— 按模态、数据规模、约束选。

21The bug is in the evaluation, not the model1:30
Full script

The sharper point: the bug is in the evaluation, not the model. The field lacks a fair, comprehensive evaluation, which muddies conclusions. A plain AE hits near-perfect on popular datasets — Hyper-Kvasir 99.5, OCT2017 96.4 — so those datasets can't tell methods apart, and much "SOTA" gain is an evaluation artifact. Takeaway: don't be led by leaderboards; invest in specificity and generalization.

中文口播

更尖的一点:病灶在评测,不在模型。领域缺乏公平、全面的评测,导致结论含糊。一个朴素 AE 在常见数据集上就近乎满分 —— Hyper-Kvasir 99.5、OCT2017 96.4 —— 说明这些数据集区分不出方法,很多"SOTA"提升只是评测假象。结论:别被排行榜带节奏;把力气投到特异性和泛化上。

Cue
  • Bug in evaluation, not model
  • AE near-perfect: Hyper-Kvasir 99.5 · OCT2017 96.4
  • → can't discriminate; "SOTA" = artifact
  • Invest in ② specificity · ③ generalization

23A heatmap is not a diagnosis1:40
Full script

Barrier one — interpretability. Mostly a blank, and not because it's easy: it's genuinely hard for AD. A heatmap is indirect evidence, not a diagnosis — no lesion name, uncalibrated score, saliency can be unfaithful (some maps barely change even when the model is randomized — Adebayo's sanity checks). What would help: treat the map as a lead, test faithfulness, calibrate into a clinical confidence, move to reasoning-grounded explanations. And interpretability is increasingly a regulatory requirement.

中文口播

第一道坎 —— 可解释。它大部分是空白,但不是因为容易:对 AD 来说是真的难。热图是间接证据、不是诊断 —— 没有病名、分数没校准、saliency 还可能不忠实(有些图哪怕把模型随机化都几乎不变 —— Adebayo 的 sanity check)。怎么办:把图当线索,测 faithfulness,把分数校准成有临床意义的置信度,转向 reasoning-grounded 的解释。而且可解释性正越来越是一个监管要求。

Cue
  • Heatmap = indirect, not diagnosis (no name · uncalibrated · unfaithful) — Adebayo
  • Genuinely hard for AD (score relative)
  • Fix: faithfulness · calibrate · reasoning-grounded
  • Regulatory requirement (GDPR / EU AI Act)
24Don't fake 3D by stacking 2D slices1:30
Full script

Barrier two — 3D. Clinicians read volumes, but most methods retreat to 2D slice-by-slice → loses volumetric context, normal distribution harder to learn — a 3D model beats its 2D counterpart, Dice 31.4 vs 25.8. Full 3D generation is compute-bound. What would help: state the 2D cost honestly, or move to latent space — reconstruction there is ~10× faster → 3D feasible. 3D UAD is now its own area with a dedicated benchmark.

中文口播

第二道坎 —— 3D。医生读的是体积,但大多数方法退回 2D 逐层做 → 丢体积上下文、正常分布更难学 —— 3D 模型胜过它的 2D 版本,Dice 31.4 对 25.8。完整 3D 生成又受算力限制。怎么办:要么老实说清 2D 的代价,要么走 latent 空间 —— 那里重建大约快 10 倍 → 3D 可行。3D UAD 现在已是有专门 benchmark 的独立方向。

Cue
  • Clinicians read volumes; methods retreat to 2D
  • 3D beats 2D: Dice 31.4 vs 25.8
  • 3D generation compute-bound
  • Fix: latent ~10× faster → 3D feasible
  • 3D UAD = its own area
25A high AUROC is not "deployable"1:30
Full script

Barrier three — trust. A high balanced-set AUROC is not "deployable". At 1% prevalence even 95% specificity buries ~10 real lesions under ~50 false alarms — five to one. Add cross-site domain shift, scarce pixel-level labels, privacy and governance.
