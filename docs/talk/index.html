Motivation · why this matters

You can't label every disease

  • Disease is rare, diverse, and long-tailed — most cases are normal
  • You can't enumerate every possible abnormality to label it
  • Anatomy + heterogeneity + clinical safety make supervised models brittle
  • → The one idea: learn "normal", flag the deviation

The spine of this talk

Three tensions the field is resolving

① Represent "normal"

Rebuild the scan and read the residual — or embed it and measure feature distance.

input → reconstruction → anomaly map
e.g. on brain MRI, the residual highlights a tumour a normal-trained model can't reconstruct

② Fidelity vs sensitivity

In reconstruction methods, the noise paradox: erase the lesion without distorting healthy anatomy.

too much noise → false positives
e.g. over-noising also alters healthy anatomy → the pseudo-healthy scan drifts from the patient → false positives

③ Scarcity & generalization

Almost no abnormal data, and it must work across many modalities.

one model → MRI · CT · OCT
e.g. MVFA-AD adapts one CLIP backbone to brain MRI, liver CT and retinal OCT
Existing methods = answers to these tensions · the unresolved parts = future work

Tension ① · Reconstruction

Reconstruct-and-compare: AE → GAN

  • Train a generator on healthy scans only → at test time it can't rebuild disease → the residual = anomaly
  • AE family: AE · VAE · VQ-VAE · MemAE · context / denoising AE — differ in how they bottleneck "normal"
  • GAN family: AnoGAN → f-AnoGAN (adversarial pseudo-healthy generation)
  • The residual metric matters: L1/L2 < SSIM < perceptual (LPIPS)
  • Failure modes: mode collapse, blur, the "identity shortcut" (lesion copied straight through) → motivates a stronger prior: diffusion (next)

Tension ① · The other answer

Don't reconstruct — embed & compare

  • Skip generation: pass patches through a frozen encoder, store healthy embeddings in a memory bank
  • Score a test patch by its kNN / one-class distance to the healthy bank — far = anomaly
  • No generation → fast, stable, sharp localization; robust across modalities
  • ↳ these are the "boringly strong" baselines new methods must beat

Tension ②

Fidelity vs sensitivity

The noise paradox — the central deployment pain point

Tension ② · The paradox

Erase the lesion, or keep the anatomy?

To erase pathology you inject high noise — but that destroys healthy anatomy → the pseudo-healthy image drifts from the patient → false positives spike, specificity drops.

Too little noise

pathology survives the reconstruction → false negatives (missed disease)

Too much noise

healthy tissue is destroyed & re-hallucinated → false positives (over-calling)

↳ In medicine a false positive means an unnecessary biopsy or callback — so specificity, not just detection, decides clinical value.

Sources — [7] Bercea, MICCAI 2024 (THOR)
Tension ② · Responses

Guidance & latent restoration

  • THOR: guidance re-integrates healthy tissue → cuts false positives
  • Latent + rectified flow for 3D → context, lower compute
  • ↳ partial fixes — specificity stays open

Tension ③ · Self-supervision

Manufacture the anomalies you lack

The road ahead

Three directions — closing the loop

① Efficient generative

Diffusion SDE → latent flow matching / rectified flows; step distillation + compression → high-fidelity restoration at 3D scale.

answers: specificity + 3D compute

② Beyond pixels

From residual/embedding to reasoning: a VLM extracts anatomical facts → rules/logic → auditable natural-language rationales, agentic diagnosis.

answers: interpretability & trust

③ Trustworthy FMs

Privacy via machine unlearning, behavioural safety control, regulatory compliance — prerequisites for deployment.

answers: privacy & safety

Summary

Roadmap & takeaways

Near-term

Standardized benchmarks · latent-diffusion/flow baselines · FM adapters

Long-term

Reasoning-grounded, trustworthy, 3D-native systems

  • Medical AD = learn-normal, flag-deviation; two engines: diffusion & VLM adaptation
  • Real bottlenecks: specificity, 3D, trust, privacy — not raw accuracy
  • Frontier = efficient generative + reasoning-grounded + trustworthy
