# KPMP · Kidney Precision Medicine Project

> The NIH consortium behind Lake 2023: research biopsies from AKI and CKD patients, profiled with single-cell, spatial and imaging assays.

**Species** human · **Assays** scRNA · snRNA · multiome · spatial · **Access** processed open, raw controlled

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Scale | consortium; growing |
| People / samples | AKI and CKD patients with research biopsies, plus reference tissue |
| Labels | atlas annotations (Lake 2023) |
| Licence | see the KPMP repository |

## What was measured

Single-cell and single-nucleus RNA, multiome, spatial transcriptomics, imaging.

## People and samples

KPMP aims to enrol 1,000–1,200 participants with research biopsies (El-Achkar 2024).

## Known issues

- Processed data are open in the Atlas Explorer and repository; raw sequencing is controlled-access.
