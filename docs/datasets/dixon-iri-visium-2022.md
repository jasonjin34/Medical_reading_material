# Dixon 2022 · Spatial time course of mouse kidney injury

> Visium spatial maps across the same kind of injury and repair course as Kirita 2020: the spatial check for what a model predicts from single-nucleus data.

**Species** mouse · **Assays** spatial · **Access** open

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Scale | 5 Visium sections |
| People / samples | one female mouse per time point: sham, 4 h, 12 h, 2 d, 6 weeks (GEO sample names) |
| Labels | time after ischaemia-reperfusion injury |
| Licence | GEO: not stated |
| Paper | Dixon EE et al. J Am Soc Nephrol 2022;33:279–289. doi:10.1681/ASN.2021081150 |

## What was measured

10x Visium spatial gene expression across the injury and repair time course, analysed with Giotto and SPOTlight; the authors report injured proximal tubule cells with increasing macrophage and lymphocyte interactions up to 6 weeks (GEO summary).

## Known issues

- One section per time point, so no replicate: usable as orthogonal evidence, not as a test set with statistics.
- Female mice and a different ischaemia time from Kirita 2020, so the two are not paired animals.
