# Balzer 2026 · ZSF1 rat DKD under ACE inhibition

> A second rodent DKD model with an ACE-inhibitor arm: checks whether a hypothesis about RAS blockade holds across species models (mouse Wu 2022, rat here).

**Species** rat · **Assays** scRNA · **Access** open

## What was measured

Single-cell RNA (enzymatic dissociation, 10x 3′ v3.1) of ZSF1 rat kidneys: lean, obese, and obese treated with enalapril; the paper reports cell-type-resolved drug effects of renin–angiotensin–aldosterone blockade.

## Known issues

- Only 3 treated rats.
- Enzymatic dissociation at 37 °C (dissociation stress); ambient RNA was removed before release.
