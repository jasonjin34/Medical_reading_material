# Kirita 2020 · Mouse kidney injury and repair time course

> The standard single-nucleus time course of mouse kidney injury and repair; it introduced the failed-repair proximal tubule state.

**Species** mouse · **Assays** snRNA · **Access** open

## What was measured

Single-nucleus RNA of mouse kidneys at six time points after IRI. The authors describe a proinflammatory, profibrotic proximal tubule state that fails to repair (FR-PTC) and detect it in other injury models, the ageing rat kidney and human allografts by deconvolution (GEO summary).

## Known issues

- A disease course, not an intervention: there is no treated arm that reverses the injury.
