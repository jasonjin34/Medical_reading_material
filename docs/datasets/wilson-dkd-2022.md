# Wilson 2022 · Diabetic kidney disease, RNA and chromatin

> A small DKD cohort from another lab (Humphreys): one of the held-out cohorts for checking whether a DKD hypothesis replicates.

**Species** human · **Assays** snRNA · ATAC · **Access** open

|---|---|
| Scale | snRNA 39,176 nuclei; snATAC 68,458 nuclei (paper); CELLxGENE snATAC has 37,747 nuclei from 8 donors |

## What was measured

Single-nucleus RNA and single-nucleus ATAC of kidneys from controls and people with DKD; the paper reports a VCAM1+ injured proximal tubule state that is more frequent in DKD.

## Known issues

- Only 5–7 DKD donors, with large differences between donors.
- Shares donors with earlier series: GSE131882 (Wilson et al., PNAS 2019) and GSE151302 (Muto et al., Nat Commun 2021). Controls 1–5 are the Muto 2021 donors; DKD 1–3 snRNA are the PNAS 2019 diabetic samples.
