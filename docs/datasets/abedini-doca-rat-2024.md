# Abedini 2024 · Rat hypertensive kidney injury under MR antagonists

> Rat kidneys under finerenone or spironolactone: a measured answer for hypotheses about mineralocorticoid-receptor blockade, a drug class that clinical trials have shown to protect the kidney in CKD with type 2 diabetes.

**Species** rat · **Assays** snRNA · ATAC · **Access** open

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Scale | 22 snRNA samples (310,218 nuclei); 9 snATAC samples (53,298 nuclei) |
| People / samples | snRNA: sham 5, DOCA 5, +finerenone 5, +spironolactone 5, +amiloride 2 (one DOCA, one finerenone and one spironolactone sample at 3 weeks, the rest at 6 weeks); snATAC 2/2/2/2/1; male Sprague-Dawley rats |
| Labels | treatment arm and cell type per nucleus; time point only in the GEO sample records |
| Licence | GEO: not stated |
| Paper | Abedini A et al. J Clin Invest 2024;134:e157165. doi:10.1172/JCI157165 |

## What was measured

Single-nucleus RNA and ATAC of rat kidneys: control, DOCA-salt, DOCA-salt + finerenone, DOCA-salt + spironolactone, plus an amiloride arm.

## Known issues

- Rat, not human; 2–5 samples per arm, and only one sample per DOCA arm at 3 weeks; the GEO overall_design text is out of date.
