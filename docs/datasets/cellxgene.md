# CZ CELLxGENE · single-cell portal

> The Chan Zuckerberg portal that serves curated datasets as standard h5ad files with harmonised metadata; the easiest way to get Lake 2023 and mBDRC.

**Species** human · **Assays** scRNA · snRNA · multiome · **Access** open

## What was measured

Hosts processed RNA (and some ATAC) datasets; no raw reads.

## Known issues

- cellxgene versions can differ from the GEO release (Lake 2023 has versions 1.0 and 1.5 with different cell numbers), and author labels such as `state.l2` may only be in the GEO metadata.
