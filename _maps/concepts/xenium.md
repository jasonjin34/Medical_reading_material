# Xenium / Xenium

> A 10x Genomics imaging-based platform that measures a targeted panel of transcripts in situ at subcellular resolution with single-cell segmentation.
> 一种基于成像的 10x Genomics 平台,在亚细胞分辨率下原位测量目标转录本组合,并进行单细胞分割。
