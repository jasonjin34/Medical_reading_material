# Doublets / 双细胞 (doublet)

> A technical artifact where two cells or nuclei are captured under one barcode and analysed as a single cell, producing a chimeric expression profile that can masquerade as a novel cell state.
> 两个细胞或细胞核被同一条形码捕获、当作一个细胞分析的技术假象,产生嵌合表达谱,可能被误认为一种新的细胞状态。
