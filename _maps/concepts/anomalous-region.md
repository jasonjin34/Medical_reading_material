# Anomalous tissue regions / 异常组织区域

> A spatial region of a tissue whose molecular profile deviates from healthy reference tissue, typically because of disease and detected without predefined markers.
> 组织中分子特征偏离健康参考组织的空间区域,通常由疾病导致,可在无预定义标记物的情况下被检出。
