# Hallucination / 幻觉

> A model output stated with confidence but unsupported by its sources; in evidence-citing systems it must be measured alongside accuracy, together with what the model silently omitted.
> 模型自信给出、却无来源支撑的输出;在会引用证据的系统里,它必须与准确率并列度量,并连同「模型悄悄漏掉了什么」一起评估。
