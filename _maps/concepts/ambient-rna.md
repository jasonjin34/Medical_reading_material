# Ambient RNA / 环境 RNA

> Free-floating transcripts released by lysed cells that get counted into other droplets, adding a background signal that makes unrelated cells look alike.
> 破裂细胞释放的游离转录本被计入其他液滴,形成背景信号,使本不相关的细胞看起来彼此相似。
